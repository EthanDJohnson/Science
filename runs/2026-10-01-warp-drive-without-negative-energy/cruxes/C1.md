## Response to the verdicts

### C1-0 (physics, survives)

1. **"The DEC-based support does not reach the NEC claim."** *Conceded.* Eichmair–Huang–Lee–Schoen (E ≥ |P|) and Beig–Chruściel rigidity rule out a superluminal coaster only for matter that obeys the DEC. Matter that obeys the NEC but breaks the DEC is ruled out only by Olum [D-59], Gao–Wald [D-60] and VBL [D-61].
   - The coasting bullet under (A) becomes supporting evidence for DEC matter. It is not part of the NEC core.
   - The NEC wording of (A) now rests on the time-advance theorems, and on the linear delay calculation in point 5 below.
2. **"Beig–Chruściel's abstract speaks of asymptotically null Killing fields."** *Conceded as unverified.* The spacelike (v > c) case was checked only for its kinematics (M-DIALECTICIAN-04). Nobody in this run read it in the body of the paper.
   - It drops to [unverified body text]. Point 1 already removes it from the core, so the core loses nothing.
3. **"'The payload rides with the shell' is a choice, not forced."** *Conceded on wording, and the core is strengthened.* The refuter's Part 2 solves the 1+1 geodesic with Killing energy E = 1.
   - A free payload drifts at v = X inside the shell. It leaves at v = 0 relative to the exterior.
   - Riding with the shell (E = √(1 − X²) = 0.9992 at X = 0.04) is also a bound geodesic.
   - Neither path gives net transport [calc: runs/2026-10-01-warp-drive-without-negative-energy/calc/falsifier-C1-0_linear_delay.py].
   - (B) is reworded: "the shift gives a free payload no net velocity relative to the exterior. All transport comes from moving the whole mass."
4. **"Gao–Wald Theorem 1 is a far-points result."** *Already conceded in C1.* The finite region is covered instead by Olum's local definition, given the generic condition, and by the linear delay integral (point 5).
5. **What C1 gains: the non-generic loophole closes at first order.** The refuter's linear calculation writes the one-way delay as Δt = 2∫dλ∫T_kk(x′)/|x − x′| d³x′.
   - Under the NEC every term is ≥ 0 for every null ray, generic or not, with a positive retarded kernel.
   - Over the full search of two-stream, zero-net-momentum shells (f₁ = 0.01–0.99, stream speeds up to 0.999 c), at M = 4.511e27 kg, the minimum forward delay is **+219.8 ns at D = 1e3 m** and **+425.6 ns at D = 1e5 m**. There is no advance [calc: runs/2026-10-01-warp-drive-without-negative-energy/calc/falsifier-C1-0_linear_delay.py].
   - The model's sources are thin shells, which are distributional. So the result also covers, at first order, C6's branch (b) "distributional sources", provided the sheets obey the NEC.
   - The refuter's beyond-first-order argument is a continuity sketch, not a proof: add positive-T_kk matter, and a strict advance on a non-generic ray would become a generic one, which contradicts Gao–Wald. I carry it as a sketch.

### C1-1 (evidence, survives)

1. **"'Olum, Gao–Wald and VBL require NEC failure before any time advance' is too broad."** Two parts, two answers.
   - **NEC versus WEC: rebutted, in C1's favour.** Olum states his result for the WEC. His proof uses only "R_ab K^a K^b ≥ 0" (Olum p. 5, quoted in C1-1), which is the NEC contraction. So the NEC reading C1 uses is what the proof actually establishes.
   - **Omitted hypotheses: conceded.** C1 left out Olum's "S contains no singularities" and Gao–Wald's null geodesic completeness. They are added to C1's evidence against.
   - **The horizons point is partly rebutted.** Horizons form where the shift exceeds the lapse [D-05], but a Killing horizon is neither a curvature singularity nor null incompleteness. Fell–Heisenberg's horizons [D-05] arise in metrics that already fail the WEC. So they do not open a NEC-satisfying escape. They do sit inside C6 branch (b) as a hypothesis to watch.
2. **"Gao–Wald's second theorem is AdS-type."** *Conceded.* Only Theorem 1 is cited for the asymptotically flat setting.
3. **"Lentz understated."** *Accepted.* Celmaster & Rubin (preprint) report a negative Eulerian density [D-30], which is stronger than an all-observer failure. It is added to (A) with its preprint label.
4. **"Missing counterexample candidate, Garattini–Zatrimaylov."** *Accepted.* It is excluded three ways:
   - its background is de Sitter, not asymptotically flat;
   - its speed is comoving, the expansion speed, not faster than local light;
   - its NEC and WEC hold only "up to a total divergence term" [D-75].
   It goes on C1's list of checked candidates.
5. **What C1 gains: the reopened non-generic branch is mostly closed by Olum's own text.** Olum writes: "The generic condition holds whenever there is any normal matter or any transverse tidal force anywhere on P" (Olum p. 4, quoted in C1-1).
   - This is the "separate argument" that C1's evidence against called sketched, not proved (M-DECOMPOSER-10). Olum needs the generic condition only somewhere on the path, and any crossing of matter with ε + p > 0 supplies it.
   - The residue is narrower than C1 first stated. It is a fastest path that crosses no matter with T_kk > 0, has no transverse Weyl tidal force anywhere, and is still advanced; or a path through a singular or incomplete region.
   - The M-DECOMPOSER-10 example (radial Schwarzschild rays) is Shapiro-delayed, not advanced. That residue is now C6's burden, not a hole in C1.

### C1-2 (scale, survives)

1. **"The 8.8× margin is profile-specific; cite the design-space floor of ≈ 3.4×."** *Conceded.* The robust margin is the scanned-family floor of 3.38, at C = 0.01 and R₁ = 0.5 m. The published-profile 8.8× is not a floor.

   - Thin-shell analytic check: β_int = C/2 against caps of 0.07–0.09 C gives a required/achievable ratio of 5.56–7.14 [calc: runs/2026-10-01-warp-drive-without-negative-energy/calc/crux-C1_margins.py].
2. **"21–80× holds for the fixed geometry at D = 1e3 m only."** *Conceded.* It is a property of the published geometry, not a design floor.

3. **"β_NEC at the published geometry, 0.0248 against 0.0239."** *Accepted.* The refuter's two-line sample is an upper bound, which can only widen the margin. C1 keeps 0.0239 (M-CONSTRAINTS-13).
4. **"The 0.210 threshold is the interior light speed, not a one-way advance."** *Conceded on wording.* C1 said "a forward light advance needs β > 0.210". It should say "an interior forward light speed above c needs β > 0.210, a necessary but not sufficient condition".
5. **"Shapes not scanned"** (non-spherical walls, anisotropic stress, tailored shift). *Conceded as open.* A factor of ≥ 3.4 has to be closed before any shape could give even interior superluminality.

   - The propulsion bill ratio equals the mass ratio, 4.5e22 [calc: runs/2026-10-01-warp-drive-without-negative-energy/calc/falsifier-C1-2_payload_gain.py].

- Added to the evidence against: Olum's no-singularity hypothesis, Gao–Wald's completeness hypothesis, unscanned non-spherical or anisotropic shapes, and the strong-field non-generic vacuum residue.

## Deciding observation

### C1 against C6 (direct contradiction of C1's FTL half)

- **Deciding calculation:** take any construction offered under C6's branches (a)–(e). Run an interval-certified, all-observer NEC test, Le's Warpax S-lemma method [D-73], including Israel surface terms for any sheet. Then compute the one-way arrival time between two exterior rest points against light through the asymptotic exterior.
- **Which result favours which:**
  - a certified pointwise NEC pass together with a strictly positive advance favours C6 and refutes C1;
  - a certified NEC failure, or a non-positive advance, favours C1.
- **The theory version of the same test:** prove, or find a counterexample to, the statement that a fastest null path which crosses no matter with T_kk > 0 and meets no transverse Weyl curvature cannot be advanced.
  - That is the only residue Olum's p. 4 remark (C1-1) leaves open.
  - Such a path lies in vacuum along principal null directions, so the natural test case is a Petrov type-D vacuum exterior. The run's M-DECOMPOSER-10 example there is delayed.
- **Precision needed:**
  - an advance resolved against the linear delay floor, of order +220 ns for this mass at D = 1e3 m [calc: runs/2026-10-01-warp-drive-without-negative-energy/calc/falsifier-C1-0_linear_delay.py], to better than a few per cent;
  - NEC margins certified by interval arithmetic, not sampled.
- **Who and when:** any GR or numerical-relativity group, now; Le's toolkit exists [D-73]. No date is known.

### C1 against C4 (C4 says the shell can beat a rocket; C1 says the shell does nothing a massive vehicle cannot)

- **Deciding calculation:** a nonlinear numerical-relativity evolution of a compact elastic shell [D-74], pushed by a modelled exhaust, run **twice**: once with the NEC-capped interior shift and once with zero shift. Measure in each run the payload's proper acceleration as a fraction κ of the shell's acceleration.
- **Which result favours which:**
  - **κ significantly below 1:** the payload needs a contact force, as in Le's construction [D-72]. C4 fails and C1 stands.
  - **κ near 1 but the same with and without the shift:** the shielding is a property of a massive compact shell, not of warp geometry. C1's core stands, but I concede in advance that its "ordinary massive vehicle" wording must narrow to "nothing the same shell without shift cannot do", since a rocket does not shield g-forces.
  - **κ near 1 only with the shift:** this favours C4 and refutes C1's (B).
- **Precision needed:**
  - linear κ is 0.670 at R = 20 m and 1.340 at R = 10 m for M = 4.511e27 kg [calc: runs/2026-10-01-warp-drive-without-negative-energy/calc/crux-C1_margins.py];
  - a correction that depends on the shift can be at most of order β_crit ≈ 0.024. That is my order-of-magnitude assumption, not a computed number;
  - so κ must be resolved to about 1% to see the difference between the two runs.
- **Who and when:** a numerical-relativity group, now in principle. No result is expected on any known timeline.

### C1 against C2 (compatible: C2 is a candidate instance of C1's subluminal half)

- **Deciding calculation:** an independent Einstein–matter solve of the shifted shell, with a stated equation of state [D-74], certified energy-condition tests and a gauge-invariant cavity survey.
- **Which result favours which:**
  - **The shell exists and its only invariant difference from the unshifted shell is the Sagnac flux** (6.86 ns at β = 0.02 [calc: runs/2026-10-01-warp-drive-without-negative-energy/calc/lens-decomposer_transit_time.py]), with P_ADM = 0. Both C2 and C1 hold, and C2 is an instance of C1(B).
  - **The shell gives a payload net transport, proper time or momentum beyond the unshifted shell.** C1(B) fails.
  - **No solution exists** (Barzegar Error 18 [D-31]). C2 fails and C1(B) holds vacuously.
- **Precision needed:**
  - cavity invariants at the 1-ns level against 6.86 ns;
  - P_ADM resolved to well below the per-stream wall momentum of 6.8e34 kg m/s at β = 0.02 (M-MECHANIST-03).
- **Who and when:** any numerical-relativity group, now [D-31].

### C1 against C3 (compatible: C3 is the momentum-emission route C1(B) already names)

- **Which result favours which:**

  - **Le's construction beats the photon-rocket mass ratio for the whole mass** (1.0833 at 0.04 c). C3's cost floor fails, and C1(B)'s "ordinary massive vehicle" bill would need revisiting.
- **Precision needed:** the momentum balance to better than about 1% of γMv.
- **A cheaper step:** fix Le's L by reading arXiv:2606.22531v4 [D-38].
- **Who and when:** reading, now; the numerical evolution, any group, now.

- **Which result favours which:**

  - **A non-Sagnac cavity invariant appears with no transport.** C7's reframe fails and C1 stands.

### C1 against C8 (C8 says even negative energy cannot hasten arrival)

- **Deciding calculation:** a launch-from-rest Alcubierre-type bubble whose (NEC-violating) source lies entirely in the causal future of the launch event at A. Compute whether the payload reaches B before light through the exterior. Alternatively, extend Krasnikov's globally-hyperbolic argument to the brief's travel-time definition.

  - **No advance even with negative energy.** C8 holds. C1's statement stays true, but it is not the binding obstacle. I concede that in that case C8 is the sharper answer to "what blocks FTL".
- **Precision needed:** the sign of the arrival-time difference against light. This is a qualitative result needing a converged evolution, not a percentage.
- **Who and when:** theorists or numerical-relativity groups, now. No date is known.
