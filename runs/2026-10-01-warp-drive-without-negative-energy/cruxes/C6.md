## Response to the verdicts

**Summary of what I concede.** All three verdicts say "weakened", and they are right. The heading's "is possible" is not supported, and I withdraw it. C6 survives only in a narrowed form:

> No published non-perturbative theorem excludes a one-way advance over exterior light, between rest points of an asymptotically flat exterior, with the NEC holding pointwise and distributionally, when (b) the spacetime is null-incomplete with non-negative mass, or (c) the fastest path fails the generic condition along its whole length. No example exists in either branch. The only explicit strong-field test of (c), made for this crux, shows a delay.

Read this way, C6 is not a rival option that gives a way to travel faster than light. It is a statement about how much of C1's faster-than-light half has actually been proven. The point-by-point responses follow.

### 1. Heading against body (C6-0 §3, C6-1 net, C6-2 net)
**Conceded in full.** The heading lists four hypotheses whose breaking makes faster-than-light travel "possible". My own body concedes that (a) is outside the brief and that (d) and (e) remain under Olum and Gao–Wald, and it builds nothing (TRL 0). "Not yet excluded by proof" is the most the evidence supports. I also accept C6-0's wording fix: "pointwise" should read "pointwise or distributional", because the brief counts sheet and singular contributions.

### 2. Branch (a), asymptotic flatness (C6-1 §4, C6-2 §1)
**Conceded.**
- The brief requires an asymptotically flat exterior.
- Garattini–Zatrimaylov's bubble moves "at a velocity equal to the speed of the expansion of the universe", and its energy conditions hold only "up to a total divergence term that averages to zero" [D-75]. That is recession, not an advance, and the conditions are not pointwise.
- C6-2's scale point stands [calc: runs/2026-10-01-warp-drive-without-negative-energy/calc/falsifier-C6-2_loophole_scale.py]. Superluminal recession across a vacuum region of radius d sets in exactly where 2GM/(c²d) = 1. A 10 m region needs 6.7e27 kg (SI) of vacuum energy. Under the NEC, forming such a region from asymptotically flat space needs a singularity (Farhi–Guth, spherical case), which returns the question to branch (b).
- I withdraw (a) as an "example".

### 3. Branch (b), null incompleteness and distributional sources (C6-0 §2, C6-1 §3, C6-2 §2)
**Conceded, except for one residue.**
- Negative-mass Schwarzschild is the one explicit escape. It is vacuum at every regular point and null-incomplete, and it does advance light:
  - −30.75 m at D = 1e4 m, for M = −1 m and r₀ = 10 m [calc: runs/2026-10-01-warp-drive-without-negative-energy/calc/falsifier-C6-0_incompleteness.py];
  - −101.4 µs (SI) at b = 1e5 m, for m = −1000 m [calc: runs/2026-10-01-warp-drive-without-negative-energy/calc/falsifier-C6-1_negmass_advance.py].
- But its Komar mass is M < 0 on every sphere. Under the brief's distributional reading that is negative energy, so this example works against C6 rather than for it. My statement that "no construction exists in any branch" was also wrong in the letter: this spacetime is a construction, just an excluded one.
- C6-2's Planck-thickness point is accepted. At the Fuchs mass a δ-sheet needs a wall 1.7e-73 to 6.9e-73 m (SI) thick even at Planck density. Any wall at least a Planck length thick is a smooth source, so Olum's "S contains no singularities" hypothesis applies again. In practice that reduces (b) to (c).
- **Residue I keep:** a naked singularity carrying non-negative mass. Penrose–Sorkin–Woolgar decline to cover nakedly singular spacetimes (quoted in C6-1), so only the weak cosmic censorship conjecture excludes this case, and that conjecture is labelled as such. No example exists.

### 4. Branch (c), the generic condition (C6-0 §1, C6-1 §2, C6-2 §3)
**Mostly conceded. One technical rebuttal, and a new calculation that cuts against me.**
- **Conceded:**
  - The "pending" argument is already in Olum's text: "The generic condition holds whenever there is any normal matter or any transverse tidal force anywhere on P" (C6-1).
  - In the weak field, (c) is closed with no generic condition at all. C6-0's identity ∇⊥²Δt = −8πΣ makes Δt superharmonic whenever Σ = ∫T_kk dv ≥ 0. Its counter-streaming shell shows an advance only at κ = 1.5–2.0, which is 3–4× past the NEC threshold [calc: runs/2026-10-01-warp-drive-without-negative-energy/calc/falsifier-C6-0_linear_censorship.py]. VBL give the same result.
  - At 1e12 kg (SI) and 10 m, the compactness is 1.5e-16, so every buildable source lies in that regime (C6-2).
- **Rebuttal, which applies equally to my own proposed "Proof for (c)":**
  - Crossing matter does not by itself restore the generic condition. It does so only where T_kk > 0. Matter that saturates the NEC along the path direction (ρ + p_k = 0) leaves R_ab k^a k^b = 0.
  - Example: a static spherical configuration with g_tt·g_rr = −1, which forces p_r = −ρ.
    - The radial null direction is then Ricci-silent.
    - It is a principal null direction of the Weyl tensor, so it feels no transverse tide.
    - The core is de Sitter, which is conformally flat.
    - So the radial ray through the centre is non-generic along its whole length while passing through matter with ρ > 0.
  - The NEC holds if ρ′ ≤ 0, because ρ + p_⊥ = −rρ′/2.
  - So C6-2's "one crossing of a positive-energy wall is enough" and my "R_kk = 8π(ε + p_r) > 0" are both false for NEC-saturating matter. The non-generic strong-field class is larger than "vacuum paths that touch no matter".
- **New calculation: that larger class gives no advance in the case I computed** [calc: runs/2026-10-01-warp-drive-without-negative-energy/calc/crux-C6_nongeneric_strongfield.py].
  - **Set-up:** de Sitter core to R₁ = 10 m, C¹ fall-off to vacuum at R₂ = 20 m, compactness 2M/R₂ = 0.1–0.7 (minimum f from 0.889 to 0.226, no horizon).
    - The NEC holds: ρ + p_r = 0, and min(ρ + p_⊥) = 0. The WEC holds too.
    - The DEC fails transversally: min(ρ − |p_⊥|) runs from −4.5e-5 to −3.1e-4 m⁻².
  - **Comparison:** static rest points A and B at r = D on opposite sides. I compare the non-generic radial ray with every lensed null geodesic from A to B.

  | 2M/R₂ | D (m) | radial T − 2D (m) | exterior ring T − 2D (m) | radial − exterior (m) | (ns, SI) |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | 0.1 | 1e3 | +18.56 | +15.47 | +3.09 | +10.3 |
  | 0.1 | 1e5 | +36.99 | +24.43 | +12.55 | +41.9 |
  | 0.5 | 1e4 | +159.38 | +83.86 | +75.52 | +252 |
  | 0.7 | 1e3 | +192.36 | +84.01 | +108.35 | +361 |
  | 0.7 | 1e5 | +321.70 | +144.14 | +177.56 | +592 |

  - **Result:** in all 12 cases the non-generic ray arrives after the light that passes through the vacuum exterior, and the margin grows with compactness. The radial ray passes the flat-space check exactly.
  - **Precision caveat:**
    - The off-axis integrator's flat-space check is logged as FAIL. It is off by +3.1e-3 m in time and 3.1e-5 rad in sweep at b = 100 m, D = 1e4 m.
    - The exterior margins (≥ 3.09 m) are about 1000× larger than that error.
    - The radial ray also seems to arrive 0.005–0.033 m before an inner lensed ray through the core. That would make it a *local* fastest path, as the absence of focusing along it would suggest. But the margin is the same size as the integrator error, so I do not rely on it.
  - **Net:** in an explicit strong-field family that breaks Olum's generic condition along the whole path, nothing beats the exterior light. The run's shell family points the same way: the advance threshold sits 21–80× above the NEC threshold. Branch (c) stays open only as a gap in what has been proven: one spherically symmetric static family is not a theorem.

### 5. Branch (d), stationarity (C6-1 §1, C6-1 corrections)
**Conceded.** Olum, Gao–Wald and VBL do not assume stationarity. C6-0's linear identity carries the u-dependence. Breaking stationarity escapes only Beig–Chruściel's coasting no-go, which says nothing about a time advance. Stationarity should not have been in the heading.

### 6. Branch (e), DEC-only violation (C6-1 corrections)
**Conceded.** The WEC implies the NEC, so Olum and Gao–Wald still apply. The DEC is not a hypothesis of any time-advance theorem, so (e) does not belong in the heading's list.

### 7. Gao–Wald's K′ ≫ K caveat counted as evidence (C6-2 corrections)
**Conceded.** It weakens a no-go theorem and supplies no construction. It supports only the narrowed "not excluded" reading.

### 8. Krasnikov (my own "evidence against")
This is unchanged and still binding on every branch that keeps global hyperbolicity [new: Krasnikov, PRD 57, 4760 (1998), ACCESS abstract, as quoted in C6-1]. Only branch (b), with a naked singularity, breaks global hyperbolicity, which narrows the residue further.

## Deciding observation

**Main crux (C6 against C1, the null).** The deciding test is a non-perturbative calculation: a theorem, or a certified counterexample, for asymptotically flat spacetimes in which the NEC holds pointwise and distributionally, the ADM mass is non-negative, and the candidate fastest path is non-generic along its whole length. That includes NEC-saturating matter with T_kk = 0, as in this crux's model, and not only vacuum.
- **Route to a theorem:** extend Gao–Wald's Theorem 1 by Galloway's remark (its conclusion holds if the spacetime "fails to contain a null line") together with a null-splitting theorem for non-vacuum NEC spacetimes. Only the vacuum, asymptotically simple corollary was quoted in this run (C6-1).
- **Result that favours C1:** a theorem saying that a non-generic null line forces flatness, or no advance, in the NEC class. C6 then reduces to the naked-singularity residue of (b), which rests on cosmic censorship.
- **Result that favours C6:** an explicit metric, such as a non-spherical or rotating extension of the p_r = −ρ family, whose radial or principal-null-aligned ray arrives at an exterior rest point before every exterior ray, with an interval-certified NEC pass (Le's Warpax method [D-73]).
- **Precision:** a margin resolved to well below the 3–178 m (10–592 ns, SI) delays found here. Millimetre-level light-time accuracy in geometric units is enough.
- **Who and when:** mathematical-relativity groups (null splitting) or any numerical GR group. No timeline is stated in the dossier.

**Against each other rival:**
- **C2 (valid positive-energy coaster).** C2 and C6 can hold together; they part only if a shift shell produces an advance.
  - Test: an interval-certified one-way light-time calculation, between exterior rest points, through the shifted shell at β up to the corrected NEC cap β_crit ≈ 0.0239.
  - An advance at or below the cap favours C6. No advance leaves C2 a subluminal coaster and removes the shell route from C6.
  - The run's numbers already favour C2 over C6 here: a forward advance needs β > 0.210 in the strong-field family and β ≥ 0.55 in linear theory.
- **C3 (start and stop only by emitting momentum).** This tests branch (d).
  - Test: a numerical-relativity evolution of a radiating, accelerating NEC shell (Le-type [D-72]) that tracks both the Bondi balance and the one-way arrival time of light against exterior rays during the acceleration.
  - Any advance during the non-stationary phase favours C6. A delay throughout, with emission of at least γMv, favours C3.
- **C4 (inductive dragging, no felt g-forces).** Use the same pushed-shell evolution with two diagnostics:
  - the payload's proper acceleration against the shell's, which decides C4;
  - the arrival time against exterior light, which decides C6.
  - C4 can be true while C6 is false. Only an advance favours C6.
- **C7 (shift is counterflow frame dragging, capped near 0.07·C).**
  - Test: nonlinear solves of shifted shells up to the Buchdahl compactness 8/9, finding β_max(C, Δ/R₂).
  - If β_max stays below the advance threshold (0.21 strong-field, 0.55 linear) at every allowed C, as C7's estimates suggest (≲ 0.04–0.15 at 8/9), C7 holds and the shift route to C6 is closed.
  - A β_max above the advance threshold at an NEC-clean C would reopen it.
- **C8 (causal access binds, not the energy sign).**
  - Test: check whether Krasnikov's "reasonable assumptions" (global hyperbolicity) hold in C6's only surviving residue, the naked singularity of branch (b).
  - A launch-from-rest construction inside A's causal future that hastens arrival through a non-globally-hyperbolic, NEC-satisfying, non-negative-mass spacetime favours C6.
  - If Krasnikov's argument extends to that case, or the construction needs pre-placed matter, C8 holds.
  - Theorists could do this now. No experiment applies to any of these tests.
