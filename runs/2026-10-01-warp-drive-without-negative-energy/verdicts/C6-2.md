# Verdict on C6 (refuter 2, angle: scale)
claim: Faster-than-light travel without pointwise negative energy is possible by breaking a hypothesis of the time-advance theorems: asymptotic flatness, null completeness, the generic condition, or stationarity.
status: final
verdict: weakened
basis: calculation
narrowed: Only two theorem hypotheses, null completeness/smoothness (b) and the generic condition on the fastest path (c), are not yet closed by a general proof, and realising either one needs a wall thinner than the Planck length (infinite density) or a fastest path that crosses no matter and no transverse tidal force anywhere; so these are formal loopholes with no realisable faster-than-light route, not a demonstrated possibility.

## Strongest attack

The claim says faster-than-light (FTL) travel "is possible" through any of four broken hypotheses. Sized for engineering, each branch either drops out or needs a source that no physical matter can supply. The candidate's own text already concedes (d) and (e): both "remain under Olum and Gao–Wald". The scale attack takes care of the other three.

1. **(a) Breaking asymptotic flatness (the de Sitter bubble) needs a region that is its own horizon, and that region can't be created from flat space with positive energy.**
   - In a vacuum-energy region of Hubble rate H, the mass inside areal radius d has compactness 2GM/(c²d) = H²d²/c² (symbolic, [calc: runs/2026-10-01-warp-drive-without-negative-energy/calc/falsifier-C6-2_loophole_scale.py]). So recession reaches c (H d = c) exactly where the region's compactness reaches 1, at its own horizon.
   - With the observed Λ = 1.1056e-52 m⁻² (SI), recession reaches c only at d = c/H = 1.65e26 m (1.74e10 ly).
   - A local bubble that makes recession superluminal across its own size has, from the calc:

     | Bubble radius d | Vacuum energy density (SI) | Mass-energy inside |
     |---|---|---|
     | 10 m | 1.4e41 J/m³ (7.0e6 × nuclear density) | 6.7e27 kg |
     | 1 AU | 6.5e20 J/m³ | 1.0e38 kg = 5.1e7 M_sun |
     | 4.37 ly | 8.5e9 J/m³ | 2.8e43 kg = 1.4e13 M_sun |

     In every row R_s/d = 1.000. The 10 m case needs 4.3e22 times the energy density of a record 1e23 W/cm² laser focus (3.3e18 J/m³).
   - Farhi & Guth show that, under the NEC, such a bubble formed in asymptotically flat space "must have merged from an initial singularity" (quote below). Breaking hypothesis (a) locally therefore needs either NEC violation or a singularity, which is branch (b).
   - The Garattini–Zatrimaylov bubble moves "at a velocity equal to the speed of the expansion of the universe", so it rides the Hubble flow. It is not a one-way advance over local light, and its energy conditions hold only "up to a total divergence term".
2. **(b) Distributional sources and null incompleteness need infinite density; any wall that physical matter could make is smooth, and the smooth theorems cover it.**
   - At the published Fuchs mass (M = 4.49e27 kg), the surface density at R = 10–20 m is σ = 0.89–3.6e24 kg/m² (SI).
   - Made of nuclear-density matter, that wall would be 3.9e6–1.6e7 m thick, a million times the bubble's size. The published 10 m wall already averages 6.7e5 times nuclear density.
   - The δ-function limit needs ρ → ∞. Even at Planck density the wall would be 1.7e-73 to 6.9e-73 m thick, which is 1e-38 of the Planck length, where classical GR (the theorems' own setting) no longer applies.
   - Any wall at least a Planck length thick is a smooth, non-singular source. That is exactly the "S contains no singularities" hypothesis of Olum's theorem, which then applies again.
3. **(c) The generic-condition escape requires the fastest path to touch no matter, which a payload-carrying vehicle can't manage.**
   - For a perfect fluid, R_ab k^a k^b = 8π(ρ + p)(u·k)² > 0 (geometric units) wherever ρ + p > 0 [calc].
   - Olum states that the generic condition "holds whenever there is any normal matter or any transverse tidal force anywhere on P" (quote below). One crossing of a positive-energy wall is therefore enough to restore his hypothesis.
   - A payload carried inside a cavity bounded by a matter wall follows a path from outside, through the cavity, to outside. That path crosses the wall's worldtube, or the wall crosses it when the bubble forms and dissolves.
   - What is left is a path through pure vacuum, aligned with a principal null direction all the way and free of transverse tidal force. M-DECOMPOSER-10 shows such paths exist (radial Schwarzschild), but the same check finds them Shapiro-delayed, not advanced.
   - Any engineered source short of planetary mass lies deep in the linear regime: 1e12 kg at 10 m has compactness 1.5e-16 [calc]. There the Visser–Bassett–Liberati result (dossier D-61) makes NEC-satisfying sources delay light, never advance it.
   - Leaving the linear regime at 10 m takes M ≳ 6.7e26 kg (compactness 0.1) [calc], which is Fuchs-class mass. Even then no advance is exhibited.

Net result:
- Branch (a) is outside the brief's definition and needs a horizon-sized region that requires a singularity to form.
- Branches (d) and (e) are closed by the candidate's own admission.
- Branch (b) needs a sub-Planck, infinite-density wall.
- Branch (c) survives only for a path that touches no matter at all, and has no exhibited advance.

The core claim "is possible" is therefore not supported. What survives is "not yet excluded by proof", for (b) and (c) only, in idealised forms that can't be built.

## In principle vs. in practice

- **In principle:**
  - (a) fails the brief's asymptotically flat, pointwise definition, and a local version needs a horizon-sized vacuum region whose formation needs a singularity (Farhi–Guth; spherical case proven, non-spherical case open).
  - (d) and (e) are closed by Olum and Gao–Wald, as the candidate itself says.
  - (b) and (c) are not closed by a general proof. No metric in either branch shows a pointwise NEC pass together with a one-way advance. So "possible" overstates them: they are unexcluded, not shown possible.
- **In practice:**
  - (b) needs a wall thinner than 1e-72 m, or infinite density.
  - (c) needs a payload path that crosses no matter, which no cavity-and-wall vehicle provides; any engineered source far below about 1e26 kg at 10 m has compactness ≪ 1 (1e12 kg gives 1.5e-16), which is the linear regime, where NEC sources only delay light.
  - (a) needs ≥ 6.7e27 kg of vacuum energy at 10 m, or 5e7 M_sun at 1 AU.
  - TRL 0 in every branch, as the candidate concedes.

## Corrections to details

- (a) is presented as "an example" of FTL by breaking asymptotic flatness. By its own abstract, though, the Garattini–Zatrimaylov bubble co-moves with the expansion. Its "faster than light" is recession beyond 1.65e26 m, not an advance, so it is not an instance of the claim even in principle.
- The decisive test for (c) is mis-specified: it should also cover vacuum paths threading gaps in a non-enclosing wall. A cavity fully enclosed in spacetime is already settled by Olum's sentence quoted above, without a new proof.
- The candidate counts Gao–Wald's caveat (that K′ may be much larger than K) as evidence for the claim. It weakens a no-go theorem; it supplies no construction.

## What survives and remaining doubt

- **What survives:** a narrow, formal statement. The null-completeness/smoothness and generic-condition hypotheses have not been shown unnecessary in general. A spacetime with a matter-free, everywhere non-generic fastest path, or with a genuinely singular source, is not excluded by any theorem in this dossier.
- **Remaining doubt:**
  - Farhi–Guth is a theorem only in spherical symmetry; their non-spherical condition is left open ("We do not know if this condition can ever be met").
  - Whether a non-enclosing wall (toroidal or open) could steer a vacuum path, non-generic everywhere, to an advance is not settled by any calculation here. The linear-regime delay result covers only weak fields.
  - So the verdict is "weakened" rather than "refuted": the attack removes the "possible" reading and every buildable form, but it does not prove the two idealised loopholes empty.
