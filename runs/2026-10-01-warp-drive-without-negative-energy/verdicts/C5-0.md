## Strongest attack

Units: geometric (G = c = 1, lengths in m) unless marked SI; mass conversion M = E_geo·c²/G = E_geo × 1.3466e27 kg/m.

**1. Lentz's own ansatz puts his soliton inside an exact divergence identity, so "compact" and "everywhere non-negative with E_tot > 0" contradict each other.** Lentz's metric is N = 1, h_ij = δ_ij, N_i = ∂_iφ (his eqs. 1, 3, 12, quoted below). With his eq. 6, 16πE = K² − K_ijK^ij, and his eq. 7 with N_i = ∂_iφ reduces symbolically to (∇²φ)² − φ_ijφ_ij = ∂_iF_i, F_i = φ_i∇²φ − φ_jφ_ij (residual 0) [calc: runs/2026-10-01-warp-drive-without-negative-energy/calc/falsifier-C5-0_lentz_energy.py, part A]. The momentum constraint gives J_i ≡ 0 for a gradient shift (same calc), so E is the rest-frame energy density of the source, not just one observer's reading. Lentz describes his configuration as "compact", with "net hyperbolic source … zero" and sources "organized to terminate the stray branches of the wave cone". So φ has compact support, and ∫E d³x = 0 exactly. Then "everywhere non-negative" forces E ≡ 0, which contradicts his own E_tot ~ (few)×10⁻¹ M_sun v_s² > 0 [D-30a]. If E is not identically zero, it is negative somewhere, and since J = 0 that is a WEC failure of the source itself.

**2. The non-smooth source does not open the identity; it puts the compensating energy on the kink planes.** The math checks show:
- a kink in the shift (continuous N, jump in ∂N) adds no surface term (M-CONSTRAINTS-11, a refutation that strengthens the identity);
- a kink in φ (a jump in N) puts a distributional sheet on the plane that cancels the bulk exactly, with no leftover δ² (M-DIALECTICIAN-02).

I modelled Lentz's own non-smooth geometry, the "bi-lateral" ansatz φ = ψ(|x| + |y|, z) with kinks on x = 0 and y = 0 (the planes his reply names). In each quadrant, 16πE = 4 det Hess ψ, which reproduces his eq. 17 form exactly (symbolic residual 0). The bulk integral, excluding the planes, as an "everywhere-positive bulk" claim would, is
  ∫_bulk E d³x = −(1/2π) ∫ N_z(0, 0, z)² dz ≤ 0,
and it is strictly negative whenever the axial shift is nonzero. Lentz's speed assignment v_s = N_z(0, 0) makes it nonzero by construction. The sheets on x = 0 and y = 0 and the line x = y = 0 carry exactly +(1/2π)∫N_z(0, 0, z)²dz; the total is 0 to ≤ 7e-16 in three test potentials, and a direct 3D grid confirms the bulk (−0.18906 against −0.18908). So in the non-smooth geometry Lentz appeals to, the smooth bulk, where he claims E ≥ 0, has a strictly negative integral. The "C⁰ escape" moves positive energy onto the kink planes and leaves negative energy behind in the bulk. At the reference case (v_s = 10 c, central plateau L = 200 m), the bulk deficit is at least 3.18e3 m (geo) = −4.29e30 kg = −2.2 M_sun.

**3. The faster-than-light half fails on Lentz's own terms.** In Lentz's metric, ds² = −dt² + Σ(dx^i − N^i dt)², a payload comoving at v_s is timelike only if |v_s − N_z| < 1, and Lentz assigns v_s = N_z(0, 0). For v_s > 1, N_iN^i > 1 in the central region (100 at v_s = 10) [calc, part E]. Lentz states that the DEC holds "so long as the magnitude of the shift vector is less than unity in all domains", and that above that "the soliton begins to form horizons". So even granting the energy claim, the hyper-fast branch is the one where his own source fails the DEC: it is an unphysical source, in the brief's terms. The "superluminal" claim is an assigned |N| > N = 1 (a shift exceeding the lapse), not a demonstrated travel-time advance.

**4. The theorem does not need smoothness or a "single fastest path".** Santiago–Schuster–Visser treat the zero-vorticity class by name, Lentz included. Natário's Theorem 1.7 (WEC-or-SEC) and SSV's NEC argument assume N = 1, flat slices and localization, and nothing about a fastest causal path; Lentz's soliton meets all three [D-41, D-58].

**5. The only remaining escape takes the solution outside the question.** A φ that does not decay (a hyperbolic wake reaching infinity) breaks the identity's falloff (shift ~ r^(−a) with a > 1/2, M-CONSTRAINTS-03). But it also breaks asymptotic flatness, which the brief's definition of "faster than light" (arrival time measured in an asymptotically flat exterior) needs, and it contradicts Lentz's own "compact" description.

## In principle vs. in practice

- **In principle:** refuted. A compact gradient-shift soliton with N = 1 and flat slices has ∫E = 0 exactly, smooth or kinked, with J = 0. So any non-flat example either has negative rest-frame energy somewhere (a WEC failure) or E ≡ 0, and then SSV/Natário apply. In Lentz's bilateral non-smooth geometry the bulk is strictly negative, at ≥ 2.2 M_sun at the reference case. Above v_s = 1 his own DEC condition fails as well.
- **In practice:** moot. The construction does not exist as a non-negative-energy solution, and no Maxwell–plasma source solving its own equations has been exhibited [D-06]. Even the claimed cost (20–50 M_sun of organized plasma, 3.6e48–8.9e48 J SI, M-ENGINEER-14) is ~28 orders of magnitude above a world-year of energy.

## Corrections to details

- The candidate says the sheet "carries the negative part only when [g′]g(0) > 0". In Lentz's own bilateral |x| + |y| geometry the sheets and the axis line carry the *positive* part, +(1/2π)∫N_z(0, 0, z)²dz, and the smooth bulk carries the negative part. So the C⁰ planes do not hide negative energy; they hold the positive energy that the bulk lacks.
- The decisive test's far-field criterion ("faster than r^(−1/2)") is about the shift's decay; Lentz's own description (compact, zero net source, cone branches terminated) already puts him in the localized case, so the test is decided by his paper.

## What survives and remaining doubt

Nothing of the core survives. Remaining doubts:
- The bilateral model φ = ψ(|x| + |y|, z) is my reading of Lentz's "bi-lateral s-projection"; it reproduces his eq. 17 exactly, but his figure configuration was not rebuilt. Attack 1 (the general identity) does not depend on that model.
- A non-decaying hyperbolic wake would evade the identity, but only by leaving asymptotic flatness, which the brief's definition of faster than light requires, and by contradicting Lentz's own "compact" claim.
- The all-observer NEC counts for the smooth proxy are unverified (M-CONSTRAINTS-14); I do not rely on them.
