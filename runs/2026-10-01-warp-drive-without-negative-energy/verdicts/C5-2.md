## Strongest attack

From the scale angle, the attack has three parts. Each uses Lentz's own numbers, and each on its own defeats one conjunct of the core claim.

1. **The energy Lentz quotes cannot be positive, and the size of the inconsistency is a factor of hundreds to thousands, not a small correction.**
   - Lentz's metric has unit lapse and flat slices, h_ij = δ_ij. On flat slices the ADM mass surface integral, (1/16π)∮(∂_j h_ij − ∂_i h_jj) dS^i, vanishes identically, so M_ADM = 0 exactly [calc: runs/2026-10-01-warp-drive-without-negative-energy/calc/falsifier-C5-2_lentz_scale.py, section 4].
   - Lentz himself sets J_i = 0 (his eq. 24). With J ≡ 0, the initial-data dominant energy condition ρ ≥ |J| reduces to his own claim that ρ_E ≥ 0. The positive-mass theorem with rigidity, which the brief lists among the no-go results, then gives one of two outcomes: M_ADM > 0, or the data are Minkowski data with ρ ≡ 0. Since M_ADM = 0, ρ ≡ 0. In integral form, this is the divergence identity ∫ρ_E = 0 (M-CONSTRAINTS-03, M-DIALECTICIAN-01).
   - The scale shows how far off the claim is. Lentz quotes E_tot ≈ (few)×10⁻¹ M_sun·v_s² at R = 100 m and w = 1 m, which is 20–50 M_sun at v_s = 10 (2–5 M_sun if you take the linear-in-v_s form printed in the CQG text).
     - If that energy were positive and gravitated, its Schwarzschild radius would be 59–148 km (5.9–14.8 km for the linear form). That is r_s/R = 590–1480 (59–148), or 665–1661 times the Buchdahl bound 2GM/(c²R) = 8/9.
     - In general, 2GE/(c⁴R) = 2C v_s²(R/w) (geometric units). Lentz's own form factor, C ≈ 0.030–0.074, gives 2GE/(c⁴R) ≈ 5.9 already at v_s = 1. Below its own gravitational radius at v_s = 10, a thin-wall soliton is impossible: that needs R/w < 0.07–0.17, so the wall would have to be 6–15 times thicker than the payload radius [calc: same file, sections 2–3].
   - So the flat-slice ansatz, which carries zero gravitating mass, is consistent only if the 20–50 M_sun of positive Eulerian energy is cancelled by an equal amount of negative energy (bulk or sheet).
     - With J ≡ 0, ρ_E is the rest-frame density (M-CONSTRAINTS-04), so that negative part is a WEC violation of the source itself.
     - The other reading, that the positive energy is real, contradicts the metric the energy was computed from by three orders of magnitude.

2. **The "because" clause is refuted by the run's math checks.**
   - The C⁰ escape needs a non-smooth source to add a surface term that the divergence identity misses. It adds none.
   - For a continuous, piecewise-C¹, curl-free shift, a kink in ∂X gives [V·n] = (X·n)(n·a) − (X·n)(a·n) = 0. The worked tent example gives bulk pieces +1.5 and −1.5, total 0 (M-CONSTRAINTS-11, log: `PASS 0 == 0: symbolic` …, `piece totals [1.50000000000000, -1.50000000000000]  total 0`).
   - A kink in φ itself moves an exactly equal and opposite amount into an Eulerian sheet on the kink plane (M-DIALECTICIAN-02; verified, sum ≤ 2e-30).
   - Non-smoothness therefore relocates the negative energy; it does not remove it.

3. **"Faster than light" and "Einstein–Maxwell–plasma" are not jointly established even on Lentz's own terms.**
   - Lentz fixes the soliton speed as the central shift: "Propagation of the soliton is taken to be uniform with speed consistent with shift vector in the central region, vs =Nz(0, 0)."
   - So any v_s > 1 has |N| > 1 in the central region. His dominant-energy-condition claim covers only |N| < 1, and he states that horizons form above it.
   - A perfect-fluid plasma with |p| ≤ ρ_m, plus Maxwell fields (his eq. 26), satisfies the DEC by construction. In the superluminal regime, therefore, neither the claimed source class nor a payload-carrying causal structure is shown.
   - His "superluminal" is at most the brief's second sense (shift exceeds lapse). No travel-time advance is computed.

## In principle vs. in practice

- **In principle: refuted.**
  - As a localized, asymptotically flat solution with unit lapse and flat slices, it cannot have ρ_E ≥ 0 everywhere unless ρ_E ≡ 0. This follows from M_ADM = 0 plus positive-mass rigidity, or equivalently from the divergence identity, and non-smoothness does not change it.
  - The only exits are those C5 itself lists:
    - a non-decaying φ wake, which is outside asymptotic flatness and so outside the brief's definition;
    - a jump in the shift itself, where ρ_E contains an undefined δ² term (M-CONSTRAINTS-11).
  - Neither yields an admissible positive-energy faster-than-light payload transport.
- **In practice: TRL 1, or lower, since the configuration is not a consistent solution.**
  - Even taking Lentz's energy at face value: 3.6e48–8.9e48 J, which is 41.6–42.0 orders of magnitude above NIF's 8.6 MJ and 27.8–28.2 above a world-year (5.9e20 J).
  - The wall would sit at 1e9 times nuclear density, with 1e19 T-equivalent fields: about 9 orders above the QED critical field. Matter there pair-produces and is not a classical "plasma", so the source the claim names cannot exist in that regime.
  - No experiment at any foreseeable scale could assemble it.

## Corrections to details

- **Linear versus quadratic in v_s.**
  - C5 says Lentz's number is "of order (few)×10⁻¹ M_sun v_s²". The arXiv/CQG text prints it linear, "M⊙vs" (p. 6) and "M⊙vs/c" (p. 12), while its own eq. 23 is quadratic in v_s.
  - The 20–50 M_sun figure follows eq. 23 and the 2022 proceedings. The printed form gives 2–5 M_sun at v_s = 10.
  - The conclusion is unchanged either way: r_s/R = 59–148 even for the smaller figure.
- **"C of order unity" understates the inconsistency.** Lentz's own 0.2–0.5 M_sun at v_s = 1 implies C ≈ 0.03–0.07. With C = 1, eq. 23 would give 677 M_sun at v_s = 10.

## What survives and remaining doubt

- **Survives:** the narrow mathematical fact that Lentz's potential obeys a hyperbolic equation whose characteristics are cones (M-DIALECTICIAN-03), and that some sign-definite Eulerian density can be arranged locally. Neither is the core claim.
- **Doubt 1, the positive-mass rigidity step.** It assumes the low-regularity version of the theorem applies to piecewise-continuous K_ij. The direct divergence identity (M-CONSTRAINTS-11) covers the C⁰ case without that assumption, so the conclusion does not rest on the theorem.
- **Doubt 2, a non-decaying hyperbolic wake.** This could evade localization, but it would also make the spacetime non-asymptotically-flat and the energy infinite. That moves the claim to C6's territory; it does not rescue C5.
- **Doubt 3, the hoop and Buchdahl comparisons.** These are illustrations of scale. The binding statement is the exact one: M_ADM = 0, so the integral of ρ_E is 0.
