## Strongest attack
I attacked the two places where C1 is weakest, by its own account.

1. **The non-generic, finite-region gap in half (A).** Olum and Gao–Wald both assume the null generic condition. M-DECOMPOSER-10 refuted the run's lemma that "a non-generic path gains nothing", so C1's faster-than-light half could fail on a fine-tuned ray: one on which T_kk = 0 and the tidal terms vanish, threading a counter-streaming warp shell whose currents are tuned to cut the forward delay. That is the attack configuration.
   - I computed the one-way light delay through such a shell, the two-stream construction behind the 2024 shell's interior shift (C7, M-ENGINEER-05). This was done in linear theory, where no generic condition is needed.
   - I searched every split of mass-energy between the shells (f₁ from 0.01 to 0.99) and every stream speed up to 0.999 c, at zero net momentum.
2. **The "payload rides with the shell" clause in half (B).** I asked whether a free payload in the shift region picks up a net velocity relative to the exterior. That would be something the same shell without a shift cannot do.

Neither attack succeeds.

## Evidence
**Linear theory needs no generic condition** [calc: runs/2026-10-01-warp-drive-without-negative-energy/calc/falsifier-C1-0_linear_delay.py]. Units are geometric (G = c = 1, m), with results converted to SI ns.
- For any null k, h_ab k^a k^b equals h̄_ab k^a k^b. The one-way delay is therefore Δt = 2∫dλ∫T_kk(x′)/|x−x′| d³x′. Under the NEC every term is non-negative, for any ray, generic or not.
- For dust streams, T_kk = ε(1 ∓ u)². This vanishes only for null dust moving along the ray, and such dust cannot have zero net momentum.
- Results for the run's shell mass, M = 4.511e27 kg (M_geo = 3.35 m, R₁ = 10 m, R₂ = 20 m):
  - minimum +x delay over the whole parameter search: **+219.8 ns at D = 1e3 m** and **+425.6 ns at D = 1e5 m**. Both are positive; there is no advance.
  - delay at β = 0.02: +x 234.00 ns, −x 237.70 ns. At β = 0.04: +x 234.67 ns, −x 242.06 ns. The same shell with no currents gives +235.0 ns.
  - In linear theory the counter-streams barely change the forward delay at fixed mass. They mainly add a fore–aft asymmetry, which is the Sagnac-type trace C1 describes.
- This is the Visser–Bassett–Liberati result [D-61], extended here to the warp shell at hand. It closes the non-generic loophole at first order for every source motion: the retarded kernel is positive, so a source pattern moving faster than light is covered too.
- **Beyond first order:** suppose a non-generic ray gave a *strict* advance. Adding a small amount of positive-T_kk matter makes every crossing ray generic, keeps the NEC, and by continuity keeps the strict advance. That would contradict Gao–Wald. This is a sketch, not a proof (see remaining doubt).

**Payload kinematics** (same calc, Part 2). Take the 1+1 metric ds² = −dt² + (dx − X dt)², with X constant inside the shell and zero outside.
- A payload arriving from rest at infinity has Killing energy E = 1. Solving the geodesic constraint gives interior velocities v = X or v = (X³ − X)/(X² + 1). The physical branch is **v = X**: the payload drifts with the Eulerian flow.
- On leaving, E = 1 and X = 0 force v = 0. **The shift gives the payload no net velocity relative to the exterior.**
- A payload at rest relative to the shell has E = √(1−X²), which is 0.9992 at X = 0.04. So "riding with the shell" is an available bound geodesic. Either way, all net transport comes from moving the whole mass M, as C1 says.

## In principle vs. in practice
- **In principle:** under classical GR with the brief's asymptotically flat exterior, the faster-than-light half stands:
  - closed at first order for any source by the positive-kernel delay (my calc; VBL);
  - closed non-perturbatively by Olum and Gao–Wald, given the generic condition;
  - closed for unit-lapse metrics by SSV.
  The subluminal half follows from conservation of ADM momentum and the shell's P_ADM = 0. Only a nonlinear, non-generic, NEC-saturated configuration remains formally open, and it is measure-zero and fine-tuned.
- **In practice:** even the subluminal positive-energy shell needs about 4.5e27 kg (about 2.4 M_J) inside 20 m. Starting and stopping it at 0.04 c needs about 3.4e43 J of ideal photon exhaust. Nothing here is near buildable. No experiment probes the faster-than-light half.

## Corrections to details
- **The DEC-based support does not reach the NEC claim.** C1 says coasting faster than light is "also closed under the DEC" via E ≥ |P| (Eichmair–Huang–Lee–Schoen) and Beig–Chruściel rigidity. Those results need the DEC, a stronger premise than the NEC. They exclude coasting faster than light only for DEC-satisfying matter. Matter that obeys the NEC but breaks the DEC (for example ρ < 0 with p > |ρ|) is excluded by Olum, Gao–Wald and VBL, not by these theorems. The NEC wording of the core rests on the time-advance theorems alone.
- **Beig–Chruściel's abstract speaks of asymptotically null Killing fields.** The spacelike (v > c) case C1 cites must come from the paper's body, which I did not re-read. M-DIALECTICIAN-04 verified only the kinematics.
- **"The payload rides with the shell" is a choice, not forced.** A free payload entering from rest drifts at the Eulerian velocity X inside and leaves at rest (Part 2). Neither path gives net transport, so the core is unaffected.
- **Gao–Wald's first theorem is a "far points" result.** By itself it does not cover a finite trip between nearby exterior points. C1 already concedes this. Olum's local definition and the linear delay integral (Part 1) cover the finite region.

## What survives and remaining doubt
Both halves of C1 survive a physics attack.
- With the NEC, every light or payload path through any warp-type source is delayed:
  - at first order, for any source and without the generic condition;
  - non-perturbatively, under the generic condition.
- The positive-energy shell's shift gives a free payload no net velocity, no proper-time gain over the unshifted shell (identical g₀₀, run calc) and no momentum saving.

Remaining doubt:
- The nonlinear, non-generic case rests on a perturbation sketch, not a theorem.
- My linear model uses thin shells of dust streams and ignores the stresses that hold the walls up. Under the NEC those stresses only add T_kk ≥ 0, so they can only lengthen the delay.
- Whether the 2024 shell is an exact solution at all [D-31] bears on whether positive-energy subluminal shells exist. It does not bear on what they can do.
