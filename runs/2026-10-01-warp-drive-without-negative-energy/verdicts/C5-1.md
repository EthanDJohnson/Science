# Verdict on C5 (refuter 1, angle: evidence)
claim: Lentz's Einstein–Maxwell–plasma soliton moves a payload faster than light with non-negative energy, because its non-smooth source evades the unit-lapse no-go theorems.
status: final
verdict: refuted
basis: cited-evidence
narrowed: none

## Strongest attack
The claim has two parts: a factual one (Lentz's soliton has non-negative energy) and a mechanism (the non-smoothness of the source evades the unit-lapse no-go theorems). The literature defeats both, and the mechanism also fails a calculation.

1. **Lentz's own geometry has negative Eulerian energy, so it fails the WEC even for the observers he chose.** Celmaster & Rubin (arXiv:2511.18251, Nov 2025, preprint) compute T_μν directly from Lentz's soliton geometry and find regions of negative Eulerian energy density. Their analysis keeps the distributional terms on the non-smooth planes, so it engages the C⁰ premise head-on. They also find several derivation errors. A modified Lentz geometry that repairs those errors still violates the WEC in the Eulerian frame. They state outright that the non-smoothness loophole does not rescue the drive: their modified drives satisfy the theorem's rapid-falloff condition, "but not the on-off condition or the differentiability condition. However, despite these possible no-go loopholes, the Lentz and modified Lentz warp drives violate the WEC". The C5 heading names exactly this escape, through non-smoothness. The only direct recomputation of the metric says it does not happen.
2. **The peer-reviewed no-go paper names this metric and has no fastest-path premise.** Santiago, Schuster & Visser (PRD 105, 064038, 2022) put the Lentz/Fell–Heisenberg zero-vorticity drive inside their generic Natário class. They conclude that "all physically reasonable warp drives will violate the null energy condition". Their summary verdict is that "none of the recent claims of positive-mass physical warp drives survive careful inspection of the proffered arguments".
3. **The premise of Lentz's reply is false.** Lentz's 2022 proceedings argue that the divergence argument fails because his source is "only continuous" on x = 0 and y = 0, and that "a requirement of the divergence theorem is that the total divergence be at least first order smooth everywhere". That is not a requirement. The divergence theorem needs only a continuous, piecewise-C¹ flux. My calculation builds a φ with exactly Lentz's regularity: C², with the third derivative jumping on both planes, so ρ_E is C⁰ and no smoother. The quadrant integrals of 16πρ_E are −2.28, −2.55, −2.55 and +7.38 (geometric units), and their sum is 0 to 30 digits. A rougher case, with a jump in φ_xx so that ρ_E itself is discontinuous, also sums to 0. This agrees with M-CONSTRAINTS-11 and M-DIALECTICIAN-02: a kink adds no surface term, and a kink in φ moves an exactly compensating amount into a sheet. So ∫ρ_E = 0 holds at Lentz's regularity. A ρ_E that is non-negative everywhere must then vanish almost everywhere, which means a flat metric, not a soliton.
4. **The same class concedes the failure.** Fell & Heisenberg, whose drive SSV treat as the same zero-vorticity construction, concede that for the type-I tensor "ρ + p_i < 0 for some i ∈ {1,2,3} in compact regions" [D-05].
5. **Even on Lentz's own terms, the "moves a payload faster than light" half is not delivered.**
   - The soliton speed is "assigned to be consistent with the shift vector", not derived from the source dynamics [examiner Finding 2].
   - No full solution of the Maxwell–plasma system is exhibited [D-06].
   - Lentz himself lists "the dominant energy condition, horizons, and the identification of a creation mechanism" as "Remaining challenges to autonomous superluminal travel" (arXiv:2201.00652).
   - By his own account the DEC holds only while N_iN^i < 1 [D-06]. In the superluminal regime the source would therefore be "unphysical" in the brief's sense, even if its energy were positive.

## In principle vs. in practice
- **In principle:** refuted as stated.
  - As a localized, asymptotically flat, unit-lapse, flat-slice metric, the soliton cannot have ∫ρ_E ≠ 0 at Lentz's regularity, by the calculation and M-CONSTRAINTS-11.
  - Its actual geometry has negative-ρ_E regions (Celmaster & Rubin).
  - SSV prove NEC failure for the class.
  - The one logically open route is a non-decaying φ wake from the hyperbolic equation (M-DIALECTICIAN-03), which the candidate itself calls "the surviving escape". It is not Lentz's mechanism, which is non-smoothness. It is not shown for his solution. And Celmaster & Rubin report that the repaired versions satisfy rapid falloff. A φ that does not decay would also put the spacetime outside the brief's asymptotically flat definition. That route belongs to C6, not C5.
- **In practice:** TRL 1 at best, with no consistent source.
  - No Maxwell–plasma solution is exhibited, the speed is assigned, and no creation mechanism exists (Lentz's own words).
  - The DEC fails above N_iN^i = 1 [D-06].
  - The quoted cost of 20–50 M_sun (4e31–1e32 kg, 3.6e48–8.9e48 J) would be moot even if the sign issue were fixed.

## Corrections to details
- **"Positive-energy" framing of the cost estimate.** Lentz's E_tot ~ C v_s²R²/w ("(few)×10⁻¹ M_sun v_s²") cannot be a non-zero total of ρ_E. For a localized curl-free unit-lapse shift, ∫ρ_E = 0 (my calc; M-CONSTRAINTS-03 and -11). Nor can it be an ADM mass (Beig–Chruściel, dialectician S3). The 20–50 M_sun build cost therefore has no defined physical meaning. Read it at most as a positive-part magnitude E₊.
- **"Unit-lapse no-go theorems" in the plural.** SSV's NEC result (§7.4), as Celmaster & Rubin read it, needs the "on-off condition" rather than rapid falloff. Lentz's eternal soliton does not satisfy the on-off condition, so his metric sits outside that particular proof. This is irrelevant to the verdict, because the direct computation finds negative ρ_E anyway. But the candidate's "evades the theorems" is partly true as a statement about proof hypotheses, and false as a statement about the energy.
- **Celmaster & Rubin is a preprint.** Its status is accurately flagged in the dossier [D-30]. As of this search, no published Lentz reply to it was found.

## What survives and remaining doubt
- **Nothing of the core survives.** The non-smooth source does not evade the divergence identity. Lentz's actual metric has negative Eulerian density, which is the rest-frame density here (J ≡ 0), so it is a WEC failure for all observers. Lentz himself concedes that the DEC and horizons remain unsolved.
- **Residual doubt:**
  1. The only direct recomputation of Lentz's specific metric is a preprint (Celmaster & Rubin) that has not been peer reviewed. The class-level arguments (SSV, peer-reviewed; the divergence identity, verified here and in the math checks) do not depend on it.
  2. A non-localized, non-decaying hyperbolic wake is not excluded by the identity. It would be a different construction outside asymptotic flatness (C6's territory), not Lentz's soliton as published.
- I chose `refuted` rather than `weakened` because the narrowed residue, a hypothetical non-decaying wake, is not the C5 claim. Lentz's soliton is the claim's subject, and non-smoothness is its stated mechanism, and both fail.
