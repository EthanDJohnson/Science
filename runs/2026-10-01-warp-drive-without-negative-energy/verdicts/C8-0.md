# Verdict on C8 (refuter 0, angle: physics)
claim: The binding obstacle to faster-than-light warp travel is causal access to the bubble's front wall, not the energy sign; operationally, travellers cannot hasten arrival even with negative energy.
status: final
verdict: weakened
basis: calculation
narrowed: Under global hyperbolicity and Krasnikov's "utter causality", a traveller cannot hasten a first one-way arrival into an unprepared region by any geometry he builds, whatever its energy sign. But structure laid inside his own causal future on the outbound leg does let him hasten the return (or any later trip), and that structure needs NEC/WEC violation, so for round trips and repeat routes the energy sign, not causal access, is the binding obstacle.

## Strongest attack
C8 has two parts: (i) the binding obstacle is causal access, *not* the energy sign; (ii) "operationally, travellers cannot hasten arrival even with negative energy". The source C8 leans on for (ii), Krasnikov 1998, itself refutes the general form of both parts.

1. **The infrastructure a traveller builds inside his own causal future does give a time advance, on the return leg.** In Krasnikov's Example 5 the metric is altered only in the region {x < t} ∩ {0 < x < D}, inside the future light cone of the departure event S. Outside it the metric is exactly flat (k = 1). I recomputed the light cones of this metric. Inside the tube (k = δ − 1), the vector l_I = −(1 − δ)∂_t − ∂_x is null and future-directed (g(∂_t, l_I) = −δ/2 < 0). Along it, a photon heading home *loses* coordinate time (1 − δ)D over a return of length D. With D = 4.37 ly and δ = 0.01, the traveller reaches α Cen at t ≈ 4.37 yr and is home at t ≈ 0.0437 yr. A light signal sent home from α Cen when he leaves would arrive at 8.74 yr, so the return leg arrives 8.70 yr early in the brief's own travel-time sense: before light through the undisturbed (flat) exterior. Nothing was pre-placed by anyone else, so C8's description of the Krasnikov tube as "pre-placed infrastructure" is wrong. The traveller lays the tube himself, within his own J⁺, on the way out.
2. **That advance needs negative energy, so the energy sign binds again.** For the 4D steady-state tube (Everett–Roman form) I computed T_ab from the metric with `gr_tensors`. In the wall the NEC fails (T_ab k^a k^b = −9.6e-2 m⁻² geo at ρ = 9 m for k = ∂_t + ∂_y). The static-observer energy density is negative (T_tt = −0.20 m⁻² geo at ρ = 9 m). The all-observer NEC minimum is −0.32 m⁻² geo at ρ = 8.7 m. The violation persists when the wall is twice as steep (σ = 4 m⁻¹) and when the tilt is changed (δ = 0.5). The flat control (δ = 2, so k ≡ 1) gives zero violations. Krasnikov and Everett–Roman say the same in their own words (quoted below), and the quantum-inequality cost is about 10³² galaxy masses of negative energy.
3. **So what binds depends on the trip, and in the general case it is the energy sign.** Causal access is a one-time cost, paid by a single trip at just under c. After that, every return or repeat trip along the route is limited only by whether the NEC/WEC-violating structure can exist and be sustained. C8's own mechanism, the outbound traveller seeding the route, is what Everett & Roman call a "superluminal subway". Once it is accepted, the claim "not the energy sign" fails for every leg except the first.

What does **not** fall is the one-way core. Krasnikov's Proposition 1 holds for globally hyperbolic spacetimes that diverge only inside J⁺(S). The geometric reason: generators of ∂J⁺(S) lie in the unaltered region's closure, so they are the original null geodesics. Under that proposition the first arrival at an unprepared destination cannot be hastened, whatever the energy sign. Everett–Roman restate it: "the time for a one-way trip to a distant star cannot be shortened". C8's examples (the gauge dependence of |β| > N; the comoving horizon at 99.45 m, M-EXAMINER-02; the eternal bubble's advance being a property of a metric already laid along the path) are consistent with this and were verified by the math checks.

## In principle vs. in practice
- **In principle:**
  - The one-way, first-arrival bar stands under established GR plus two assumptions: global hyperbolicity and "utter causality" (no influence outside J⁺). Krasnikov calls the latter "just a physical postulate" (p. 6).
  - Round-trip advances are allowed causally. They are barred only if NEC/WEC violation of the required size is barred, so in that case the energy sign is the operative obstacle.
  - Krasnikov's Example 3 gets an advance without WEC violation by breaking global hyperbolicity. C8 already lists that exception.
- **In practice:** both obstacles are absolute for any foreseeable technology. The tube's quantum-inequality cost is about 10³² galaxy masses, and the Alcubierre QI cost is 6.3e63 kg (M-CONSTRAINTS-09). Causal access is the cheap constraint by comparison: one trip at just under light speed.

## Corrections to details
- "Pre-placed infrastructure along the route (a Krasnikov tube)": the tube is laid by the traveller on the outbound leg, inside J⁺(departure). It is not pre-placed by a third party.
- Citing Krasnikov's abstract for "cannot hasten" without its next sentence misreads the source. The same abstract asserts the round-trip advance.
- The comoving-horizon radius 99.45 m (v = 10, R = 100 m, σ = 2 m⁻¹) and T_H = 1.3e-3 K are verified (M-EXAMINER-02, M-CONSTRAINTS-09). No correction is needed.

## What survives and remaining doubt
- **What survives:**
  - The reframe is right that |β| > N alone is gauge-dependent and does not mean a time advance.
  - A bubble's front wall is causally inaccessible from its centre (Everett–Roman).
  - In a globally hyperbolic spacetime, the first one-way arrival cannot be hastened by matter the traveller controls, negative or not.
- **What fails:**
  - "Not the energy sign" as a general statement.
  - "Travellers cannot hasten arrival" as a general statement: return and repeat trips over a self-built route can be hastened, and the price is NEC/WEC violation.
- **Remaining doubts:**
  - Krasnikov's Proposition 1 is proved in his appendix under his divergence definition. I did not re-derive it.
  - My NEC scan is a steady-state planar-cylinder tube with a tanh wall, not the full time-dependent construction. Everett–Roman's full analysis reaches the same sign.
  - Whether the brief's travel-time definition counts a return-leg advance as "faster than light" is a matter of reading. On the brief's wording (two points at rest in the exterior, arrival before light through the undisturbed exterior), it does.
