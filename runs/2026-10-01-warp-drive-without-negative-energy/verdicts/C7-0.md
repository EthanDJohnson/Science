## Strongest attack
The physics attack goes after the reframed question's own variable, the interior shift β. Units are geometric (G = c = 1) for metric work and SI for numbers.

**The attack: the cavity shift is pure gauge for thick walls too, not only thin ones.**
- C7 says only that "on a thin shell the shift is exactly gauge". But any stationary metric keeps its Killing vector ∂_t under a time-independent resynchronization t = t′ + F(x⃗). That changes g_0i by g_00 ∂_iF.
- Take F = βx/(N² − β²) in the cavity and blend it smoothly to F = 0 across the wall. The cavity metric then becomes exactly diag(−(N² − β²), N²/(N² − β²), 1, 1): static, with zero shift. The shift has been moved entirely into the wall [calc: runs/2026-10-01-warp-drive-without-negative-energy/calc/falsifier-C7-0_gauge_sagnac.py, item 1: "g'_0x = 0 ; g'_00 = -N**2 + beta**2 ; g'_xx = N**2/(N**2 - beta**2)"].
- So the cavity value of β is not an observable, even for the thick 2024 wall. Within the family "uniform shift inside, zero outside", a blended F can set the cavity β to any value.
- If the reframe's "real question", shift per unit compactness, meant the coordinate β, it would be ill-posed.

**Why the attack does not defeat the core.**
- The Sagnac one-form ω = g_0i dx^i/(−g_00) changes only by an exact form, ω′ = ω − dF [same calc, item 2: "omega' - omega + dF (should be 0,0,0): [0, 0, 0]"]. So its loop integrals (holonomy) are invariant.
- Their curl B_g = dω vanishes in the cavity and is confined to the wall (M-IDEALIZER-05, verified).
- The invariant content of the "warp effect" is therefore a gravitomagnetic flux threading the wall. That flux is sourced by momentum currents whose linear-order net integral is exactly zero (M-DIALECTICIAN-08, M-MECHANIST-01, M-IDEALIZER-05, all verified). This is exactly C7's "solenoid" picture.
- C7 already names this holonomy, the Sagnac co-minus-counter time, as the gauge-invariant separator. Fuchs et al. make the same move: "Since the photon travel time is a measurable quantity, the shift-vector modification of the shell metric cannot be reduced to a coordinate transformation" [Fuchs et al. 2024, arXiv:2405.02709 p. 25, fetched]. They describe the effect as "a linear frame dragging inside the shell" (p. 27).
- Within a fixed construction (Fuchs eq. 26: g_0x = −S(r)β, g_00 held fixed), β is in one-to-one correspondence with that holonomy. So "shift per unit compactness" is a legitimate stand-in once it is read as "Sagnac holonomy per unit compactness, for a given wall profile". This sharpens the wording; it does not narrow the core.

**The "zero net momentum" clause is stronger than C7 itself claims.**
- The exterior of the shifted shell is exactly static Schwarzschild, because S(r) = 0 for r > R₂ by construction. So the ADM momentum is exactly zero at every β, to all orders (M-MECHANIST-02, verified).
- C7's caveat "second-order net momentum unchecked" applies only to the slice integral of the matter's momentum density. That integral is not a conserved quantity beyond linear order. The physically relevant net momentum, P_ADM, is already settled at zero.

**The rocket-cost half.**
- With P_ADM = 0 and the exterior a plain Schwarzschild exterior of mass M_ADM, changing the vehicle's velocity requires γM_ADM·v from outside, or emitted momentum (Bondi). No interior rearrangement changes that.
- The "no" answer to the cost question follows from ADM/Bondi momentum conservation, not from a model.

## In principle vs. in practice
- **In principle:** the reframe is consistent with classical GR.
  - The effect is a stationary gravitomagnetic holonomy sourced by positive-energy, zero-net-momentum wall currents.
  - It is bounded by the DEC and the energy conditions at about 0.07·C in the rebuild (M-CONSTRAINTS-13), and it vanishes for thin walls.
  - It gives no propulsion: P_ADM = 0, and start and stop costs γM_ADM·v.
- **In practice:** the effect has no use for propulsion at any scale, so practice adds nothing beyond C2's build gaps (mass 22 orders above the ISS).

## Corrections to details
- **Counterflow energy floor.** C7 pairs "each sign carries 6.8e34 kg m/s" with "E_flow ≥ 2cP = 2.42e43 J = 0.060 Mc²". These come from two different models:
  - the 2.42e43 J uses P = 4.04e34 kg m/s from the two-thin-layer model;
  - for the rebuild profile, whose per-sign momentum C7 quotes, the floor is 2cP = 4.05e43 J = 0.100 M_ADM c², with a whole-mass counterflow of 0.100 c.
  - The order of magnitude and the conclusion are unchanged.
- **"A payload at rest relative to the shell gains only a clock deficit of 3.4e-4."**
  - Relative to the plain shell it gains nothing. The construction holds g_00 fixed, so a shell-static clock runs at √(−g_00) = 0.7611 whatever β is.
  - The 3.4e-4 is the Killing-to-Eulerian ratio, and the Eulerian observer is gauge-dependent (item 1 above). This strengthens the reframe: the shift has no local cavity effect.
- **Prediction "g_00 ... in the cavity unchanged".** In the 2024 construction and the rebuild, this holds by construction (the input fixes g_00), so it cannot test the reframe. The discriminating version is: "in a self-consistent solve at fixed matter, the Killing redshift of the cavity changes only at O(β²), through the energy of the counterflow."
- **"Second-order net momentum unchecked"** (evidence against). This is moot for the conserved quantity: P_ADM = 0 exactly, because the exterior is static Schwarzschild. Only the non-conserved slice integral ∫T_0i dV is open beyond linear order.
- **Wording of the reframed question.**
  - The cavity "shift" is gauge for any wall thickness, not only for thin shells.
  - The invariant is the Sagnac holonomy through the wall, ∮g_0i dx^i/(−g_00).
  - The question should read "holonomy (or the β of a fixed construction) per unit compactness".

## What survives and remaining doubt
- **Survives:**
  - the identification of the positive-energy "warp effect" as a wall-confined gravitomagnetic flux (translational frame dragging, measurable only by a Sagnac-type holonomy), sourced by counter-streaming currents with zero net momentum, with P_ADM = 0 exactly;
  - the reframed questions: how much holonomy per unit compactness, and whether anything beats the γM_ADM·v propulsion bill. The second is answered "no" by momentum conservation.
- **Remaining doubt:**
  - The scaling law β_max ∝ C·Δ/R₂ and the cap of about 0.07·C come from linear models used at compactness 0.33–0.67, plus one nonlinear rebuild, sampled rather than certified.
  - Whether the Fuchs shell is a self-consistent solution with a real matter model is contested [D-31].
  - Neither doubt touches the core identification.
