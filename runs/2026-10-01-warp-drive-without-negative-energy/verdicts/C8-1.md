# Verdict on C8 (refuter 1, angle: evidence)
claim: The binding obstacle to faster-than-light warp travel is causal access to the bubble's front wall, not the energy sign; operationally, travellers cannot hasten arrival even with negative energy.
status: final
verdict: weakened
basis: cited-evidence
narrowed: In a globally hyperbolic spacetime, and with matter whose influence stays inside the launch event's causal future, a traveller cannot reach a destination before light on a first one-way trip launched on demand, whatever the energy sign, so on-demand superluminal bubbles fail on causal grounds; but round trips and routes prepared in advance can beat light, and for those the binding obstacle is again NEC/WEC violation.

## Strongest attack

The sources C8 leans on (Krasnikov 1998; Everett & Roman 1997 [D-13]) support only the **one-way, first-flight** part of the claim. They contradict both general parts of the heading.

1. **"Travellers cannot hasten arrival even with negative energy" is false for the return arrival, in Krasnikov's own paper.** C8 quotes the first sentence of Krasnikov's abstract and leaves out the next one, which reverses the operational conclusion for round trips. In §4, Krasnikov states that "even in globally hyperbolic spacetimes there is nothing to prevent an astronaut from modifying the metric so as to move R [the return event] closer to S." Everett & Roman's abstract says the same: "the time for a round trip, as measured by clocks on Earth, can be made arbitrarily short." A traveller *arriving back at Earth* is a traveller arriving at a destination, and that arrival can be hastened. To α Cen (4.37 ly), with the outbound leg at 0.99c, the Earth-clock round trip is 4.414 yr against 8.740 yr for light: an advance of 4.33 yr, made entirely by matter the traveller placed inside its own causal future [calc: runs/2026-10-01-warp-drive-without-negative-energy/calc/falsifier-C8-1_roundtrip.py].

2. **When causal access is solved, the energy sign is what still binds.** Everett & Roman built the Krasnikov tube precisely because it "does not suffer from the first difficulty" (causal access to the wall). They found it still needs "unphysically thin layers of negative energy density, as well as large total negative energies, and therefore probably cannot be realized in practice." Krasnikov (p. 12–13) says creating such a space machine "requires violation of the weak energy condition," and cites a quantum-inequality estimate of about 10³² M_galaxy of negative energy. Pre-placed devices allow "a regular spaceship service" (Krasnikov p. 10), and for every flight after the first, Olum [D-59], Gao–Wald [D-60] and Visser–Bassett–Liberati [D-61] make NEC/WEC violation necessary. So "not the energy sign" fails for the round trip, for pre-built routes, and for the brief's own definition (d) of travel between two points at rest in the exterior. In each of those, causal access can be removed and the energy condition cannot.

3. **The literature on exactly the operational question puts the barrier in the matter's energy conditions.** Low 1999 (CQG 16, 543) formalises "constructing" a warp drive from a compact region in the initial-value problem. His conclusion is conditional on the absence of exotic matter. He defines exotic as matter failing the DEC or symmetric-hyperbolic evolution, and the step that blocks construction is that superluminal signalling by matter "is only possible if the matter violates the dominant energy condition." Krasnikov's energy-free Proposition 1 instead rests on his divergence condition, that the pilot's activity changes the spacetime only inside J⁺(S). Krasnikov himself calls this condition "tentative to some extent," and in his p. 2 setup he excludes tachyons and "all other means for changing the metric with violating what we call below 'utter causality'." So "even with negative energy" holds only for negative energy carried by causally propagating fields, such as semiclassical QFT. It does not hold for exotic matter in general, and it is not what Low's theorem says.

4. **Internal inconsistency in the argument.** The argument says "The theorems answer no, whatever the energy sign." Of the theorems the run cites, only Krasnikov's Proposition 1 is energy-free. Gao–Wald assumes the NEC [D-60], Olum assumes the WEC [D-59], and Visser–Bassett–Liberati assumes the NEC [D-61]. The source lens itself (EXAMINER R1) answers "no, under Gao–Wald (NEC, …) and Krasnikov (global hyperbolicity)". The candidate also concedes "Pre-placed infrastructure along the route (a Krasnikov tube) is the real alternative." That alternative hastens arrival only with negative energy, which contradicts the heading's "cannot hasten arrival even with negative energy" unless the heading is limited to one-way first flights.

## In principle vs. in practice

- **In principle:**
  - The narrowed claim holds under global hyperbolicity and Krasnikov's divergence ("utter causality") assumption, with no energy condition needed. That rules out on-demand superluminal Alcubierre or Natário bubbles on a first one-way trip.
  - Round trips (Krasnikov tube) and pre-placed routes are not barred causally. They are barred by the energy conditions: Olum, Gao–Wald, VBL and Low.
  - Exotic matter with superluminal characteristics, or a breakdown of global hyperbolicity, would void Proposition 1. Krasnikov's p. 12 says that creating compactly generated Cauchy horizons itself needs WEC violation, so negative energy is the enabler there as well.
- **In practice:** every route left open needs macroscopic negative energy. Everett & Roman say "probably cannot be realized in practice", and the quantum-inequality estimate is about 10³² M_galaxy for the 4-D tube. So in practice, the energy sign is the binding obstacle for any trip a causal argument does not already forbid.

## Corrections to details

- The argument's sentence "The theorems answer no, whatever the energy sign" should read: "Krasnikov's Proposition 1 answers no for a first one-way flight whatever the energy sign. Gao–Wald, Olum and VBL answer no only under the NEC/WEC."
- Krasnikov is quoted selectively. The very next sentence of his abstract allows hastened round trips.
- "Fell–Heisenberg concede that horizons prevent transport across the light barrier" [D-05] concerns pushing a configuration from subluminal to superluminal. It is a statement about horizon formation, not about launch with negative energy, and it does not support the "even with negative energy" clause.
- The Everett & Roman "front wall" disconnection applies to the superluminal Alcubierre geometry once it exists. It does not by itself show that negative-energy matter in J⁺(launch) cannot build such a bubble. Krasnikov's Proposition 1 shows only that such a bubble gives no first-flight advance.

## What survives and remaining doubt

- **What survives:** the one-way, first-flight operational no-go, independent of energy sign under global hyperbolicity and utter causality. It is well sourced in Krasnikov's Proposition 1 and Everett & Roman. It usefully shows that the eternal-bubble advance (definition (d), M-EXAMINER-01) is not a launchable drive.
- **What fails:** the ranking "causal access, not the energy sign" as a general statement, and "cannot hasten arrival even with negative energy". Round-trip and pre-built-route travel can be hastened in globally hyperbolic spacetimes, and NEC/WEC violation is then the obstacle that binds.
- **Remaining doubt:**
  - Whether exotic but causally propagating negative-energy sources, the only kind semiclassical QFT supplies, fully respect Krasnikov's divergence condition is plausible but unproven here.
  - Low's DEC-based route and Krasnikov's geometric route are two different formalisations of "operational", and neither is the brief's definition (d).
- **Why weakened rather than refuted:** the one-way first-flight core is sound and sourced. Only the general ranking and the "even with negative energy" universality fail.
