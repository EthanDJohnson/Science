## Strongest attack
I looked in the literature for evidence against each of C7's three load-bearing parts.

1. **The mechanism is not counter-streaming currents, or carries net momentum.** The primary source contradicts neither. It describes exactly C7's picture.
2. **The cavity is not "frame-dragged" in the invariant sense.** This is the strongest attack I found.
   - In a cavity with constant lapse N and shift b, the static Killing observers are geodesic (ξ·ξ = −N² + b² is constant). Their Killing covector is closed, d(ξ♭) = 0, so it has no twist. They are orbits of the asymptotic time translation [calc: runs/2026-10-01-warp-drive-without-negative-energy/calc/falsifier-C7-1_counterflow_floor.py].
   - Only the slicing-dependent Eulerian observers "move", at b/N = 0.0263 c relative to the static observers. Le (2026) defines frame dragging as the change in "the comparison between the cavity's inertial axes and those at infinity". In the stationary translational case nothing comparable happens inside the cavity.
   - What is gauge-invariant is a holonomy (a Sagnac or clock-synchronization gap) on loops that thread the wall, where B_g lives (M-IDEALIZER-05).
   - So "internal frame dragging" is, strictly, a misnomer for a wall-threading gravitomagnetic holonomy.
   - This does not defeat the core:
     - C7's own argument already says B_g vanishes in the cavity and that there is no gyroscope precession.
     - The term is the source's own: Fuchs et al. call the Sagnac-type transit difference "a Lense-Thirring effect … creating a linear frame dragging".
     - Their analogy is a loop holonomy (Kerr photons "with or against the rotation").
   - The correction is to wording, not substance.
3. **The positive-energy shell is not a valid solution, so there is no "positive-energy warp effect" to reframe.**
   - Barzegar, Buchert & Vigneron (preprint) say the 2024 construction "is not a solution to Einstein's equations" (Error 18), and that "the shift vector is a choice of coordinate system".
   - The run's rebuild computes T_μν from G_μν, so the energy-condition statements refer to the T the metric actually requires. The shift's coordinate character is precisely C7's claim (exactly gauge on a thin shell).
   - This critique weakens the 2024 paper, not the reframe. C7 already lists D-31 as evidence against.
4. **Other routes to a positive-energy warp effect exist, so "the" effect is too general.**
   - Buchert & Frackowiak 2026 (Universe 12, 132) only propose future "physical warp fields … that obey energy conditions" within tilted perfect-fluid flows. They build no example with a vacuum payload region.
   - The zero-vorticity class fails the NEC (C1).
   - The Bolívar–Abellán–Vasilev shells are static and carry no shift.
   - I found no constructed positive-energy warp spacetime whose effect is something other than a shift on a massive shell.
5. **I checked one attack and rejected it.** Le's abstract says "nonnegative isotropic pressure cannot support a static spherical shell … with two free vacuum faces". That does not apply here, because Fuchs et al. use anisotropic pressure: "for a stable shell the pressures P can not be assumed as isotropic … diag(ρ,P1,P2,P3)".

- **In principle:** the reframe is consistent with established GR.
  - The run's calculations and the primary source agree that the shift is sourced by a zero-net-momentum circulation.
