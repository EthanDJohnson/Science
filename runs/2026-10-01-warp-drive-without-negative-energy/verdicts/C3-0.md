# Verdict on C3 (refuter 0, angle: physics)
claim: A positive-energy shell starts and stops only by emitting momentum (photon or gravitational-wave exhaust, e.g. Le's radiative steering), at no less than photon-rocket cost for its whole mass.
status: final
verdict: weakened
basis: calculation
narrowed: An isolated, self-contained positive-energy shell can start and stop only by ejecting exhaust (null exhaust, photons or gravitational waves, being optimal), so its initial-to-final mass ratio for shell plus payload is at least (1 + v)/(1 − v); a shell pushed from outside escapes both the exhaust requirement and the photon-rocket energy bill, but still pays about 4.5e22 times the payload-alone bill.

## Strongest attack
The heading states two universals: "only by emitting momentum" and "at no less than photon-rocket cost". The argument behind them rests on one premise: "For an isolated, asymptotically flat system, Bondi/ADM 4-momentum changes only through flux at null infinity." That premise supports the mass-ratio floor for an **isolated, self-contained** vehicle and nothing more. The heading drops the qualifier, and without it both universals fail.

1. **"Only by emitting momentum" fails for a non-isolated shell.** A shell that is pushed from outside receives momentum and emits none. Examples are a beamed-light sail reflecting a laser from a home station, a mass driver or tether reacting against a planet, or a gravity assist. The momentum ends up in the station's planet, not at null infinity, so no Bondi flux is needed. The shell's own mass ratio is then exactly 1 [calc: runs/2026-10-01-warp-drive-without-negative-energy/calc/falsifier-C3-0_external_push.py].

2. **"No less than photon-rocket cost" is false as an energy bill, even for some isolated vehicles.** A photon rocket minimises the mass ratio, but it maximises the energy spent per unit of momentum (E = pc). At 0.04 c, start plus stop, with M = 4.511e27 kg (SI) [calc: same file]:
   - **Ideal photon rocket:** 3.38e43 J of exhaust; mass ratio 1.0833. This matches the candidate and M-MECHANIST-08.
   - **Perfect-mirror sail, single bounce:** the exact relativistic integral, dE_beam = Mc²(1 + β)γ³dβ/2, gives 8.28e42 J per leg. That is 1.66e43 J for both legs, **0.49 of the photon-rocket figure**, with shell mass ratio 1.
   - **Mass driver or N-bounce photon recycling:** the floor is the kinetic energy, 2(γ − 1)Mc² = 6.49e41 J. That is **1/52 of the photon-rocket bill** (the ratio tends to 2/v as v → 0), with shell mass ratio 1.
   - **Isolated vehicle that pushes off a massive reaction mass k·M:** k = 1 needs 1.30e42 J (0.038 of the photon bill), and k = 1000 needs 6.50e41 J. The mass ratio, however, rises to 2.0 and 1001 per leg. Every case keeps the mass ratio ≥ e^(rapidity) = 1.0408 per leg. So the photon-rocket floor is a floor on mass ratio for isolated vehicles, not a floor on cost.
   - **At the corrected cap β = 0.0239:** the photon rocket costs 1.99e43 J, against a kinetic floor of 2.32e41 J, a factor of 85.7.

3. **Internal inconsistency.** C3's own in-principle verdict reads "allowed by conservation at the 'start and stop' level, with external propulsion counted". Counting external propulsion contradicts "only by emitting momentum" and the photon-rocket floor in the same candidate. The slate's exclusivity line also says C3 and C4 "can all hold at once", but C4's premise is a shell "pushed externally", which C3's heading forbids.

## In principle vs. in practice
- **In principle:**
  - The "only by emitting momentum" universal fails once the shell may exchange momentum with external bodies (sail, mass driver, tether, gravity assist). None of these needs negative energy, and the shell keeps its rest mass.
  - The photon-rocket energy floor fails by a factor of 2 (single-bounce sail) up to 52 (kinetic floor) at 0.04 c.
  - For an isolated, self-contained shell the core holds as a theorem of 4-momentum conservation: exhaust with E ≥ |p|c gives m_i/m_f ≥ γ(1 + v) per leg. Null exhaust is optimal, and the GW-rocket variant obeys the same bound.
- **In practice:** C3's "eliminated" conclusion survives intact. At the lowest floor, 2KE = 6.49e41 J = 1.1e21 world-years (SI; 592.2 EJ/yr). That is about 1.7 orders below the candidate's 5.7e22 world-years, but still about 21 orders above one world-year. The 4.5e22 ratio of the shell bill to the payload-alone bill holds for every mechanism, because every bill scales with M. The external-push options also need infrastructure (a laser or a reaction body at **both** ends for start and stop) that this mass makes absurd.

## Corrections to details
- "3.38e43 J of exhaust … for the shell plus payload": correct as a photon-rocket figure (verified), but it is not a floor on energy. The energy floor is 6.5e41 J (start plus stop, no braking recovery). The prediction "the propulsion bill stays ≈ 4.5e22× the payload-alone bill" holds for any mechanism.
- "If false: … a start-and-stop construction keeps the energy conditions without any exhaust": an externally pushed shell already does this trivially. Neither the sail nor the mass driver touches the shell's energy conditions beyond ordinary positive-energy contact stresses (not computed here). So this falsifier tests only the isolated case, and should say so.
- The exclusivity line, "C2, C3 and C4 can all hold at once", conflicts with C3's unqualified "only": C4 assumes an external push.

## What survives and remaining doubt
- **What survives:** for an isolated, asymptotically flat, self-contained shell, start plus stop needs exhaust and a mass ratio ≥ (1 + v)/(1 − v) for the whole mass. That is 1.0833 at 0.04 c, and 1.049 at β = 0.0239 if v = β. Le's e^(3L) = 1.27 is consistent with this floor.
- **Doubts:**
  - The isolated-case floor uses the special-relativistic 4-momentum balance plus the Bondi flux statement; no primary-source theorem specific to warp shells was found (D-69). Wisdom-type "swimming" in a curved **background** would be another external-field exchange; it does not touch the isolated case.
  - My sail and mass-driver figures ignore the pusher's own losses and the shell's self-gravity. That makes them floors, which is all the attack needs.
  - Whether the trip speed v equals the interior shift β is a C1/C2 matter, and I take no position on it.
