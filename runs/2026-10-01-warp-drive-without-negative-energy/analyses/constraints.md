# Analysis: constraints (Kant: audit of the conditions any valid answer must satisfy)
status: final

## Method applied
Constraint audit. For every metric in scope, compute T_μν = G_μν/8π from the metric itself (geometric units G = c = 1 unless marked SI), test NEC/WEC/SEC/DEC for all observers with the Hawking–Ellis type over the whole wall region, integrate E₋/E₊ on a slice, check grid convergence, and set each candidate against the theorems (Natário/SSV, Olum, Gao–Wald, positive mass, Buchdahl/Andréasson), quantum inequalities, momentum conservation and horizons, stating where each bound's assumptions hold.

## Findings
1. **Alcubierre fails NEC, WEC, SEC and DEC for all observers across the wall, and its Eulerian density is negative at every wall point.** Paper-like case (R = 1 m, σ = 8 m⁻¹, v = 1; geometric units): 364/364 wall points violate all four conditions; Hawking–Ellis type IV at 342 points and type I at 22; worst sampled NEC minimum −1.33 m⁻². Reference case (R = 100 m, Δ = 2/σ = 1 m, v = 10): NEC fails at 340/364 points, WEC and DEC at 364/364 (type IV at 188, type I at 176). Eulerian slice energy E = −0.22334 m (geo) = −3.01e26 kg at the paper-like case and −5.5556e4 m = −6.72e48 J = −7.48e31 kg = −37.6 M_sun at the reference case, with E₊ = 0; n vs 1.5n agree to 4e-9; matches the closed form −v²R²/(18Δ) = −5.5556e4 m and D-28 [calc: runs/2026-10-01-warp-drive-without-negative-energy/calc/lens-constraints_unitlapse.py] [D-02] [D-28]. Symbolic Natário/irrotational Einstein tensors timed out in that script (>400 s) and were redone numerically (Finding 2 onward).
2. **Natário zero-expansion drive (built as X = v curl((f/2)(−y, x, 0)), θ ≡ 0 to 1e-5 m⁻¹ numerically) fails all four conditions for all observers, and its Eulerian density is ≤ 0 everywhere.** Paper-like case (R = 1 m, Δ = 0.25 m, v = 1): 312/312 wall points violate NEC, WEC, SEC and DEC (robust and strict verdicts agree; 0 unconverged); type IV at 230, type I at 82; worst NEC −11.2 m⁻². Eulerian energy E = −3.288 m (geo) = −4.43e27 kg = −2.23e-3 M_sun, E₊ = 0. Reference case (R = 100 m, Δ = 1 m, v = 10): E = −4.4455e8 m = −5.38e52 J = −5.99e35 kg = −3.0e5 M_sun, E₊ ≈ 0 (≤ 5e-24 m, round-off), converged to 4e-9; E/(v²R⁴/Δ³) = −0.04446, reproducing D-34's fit (−0.0445) independently and 8.0e3× the Alcubierre value at the same R, Δ, v. Brief premise 9 ("Natário needs negative energy like Alcubierre") holds, and the NEC failure is not removed by θ = 0: Natário's own claim concerns only the expansion form of the SEC [D-02] [D-34] [calc: runs/2026-10-01-warp-drive-without-negative-energy/calc/lens-constraints_natario_irrot.py].
3. **A smooth zero-vorticity drive (X = v∇(ζ f), the Lentz / Fell–Heisenberg / Rodal class) has Eulerian density of both signs and total Eulerian energy zero to numerical precision; it fails the NEC for all observers.** Paper-like case: Eulerian ρ negative at 172/312 wall points; E₋ = −2.8576 m, E₊ = +2.8577 m, E_total/|E₋| = 6.9e-6 (→ 0 with refinement: 1.4e-5 → 6.9e-6 from n to 1.5n); all 312 points type I; NEC fails at 288/312, WEC at 304, DEC at 312. Reference case: E₋ = −1.667e7 m = −2.25e34 kg = −1.13e4 M_sun with E₊ = −E₋ to 3e-7. This is the exact divergence identity: θ² − ∂_iX_j∂_jX_i = ∂_i(X_iθ) − ∂_j(X_i∂_iX_j), and for curl-free X, K_ijK^ij = ∂_iX_j∂_jX_i, so for any localized smooth irrotational shift ∫ρ_E d³x = 0 exactly. A positive-definite Eulerian density (Lentz's claim) therefore forces either ρ_E ≡ 0 (flat space), a shift that is not localized (surface term at infinity), or distributional sheets — the C⁰ loophole Lentz's reply invokes [D-06] [D-30] [D-36] [calc: runs/2026-10-01-warp-drive-without-negative-energy/calc/lens-constraints_natario_irrot.py]. It reproduces Rodal's "net energy consistent with zero" (D-36) as an identity, not a numerical accident.
4. **Finite-difference caveat at the superluminal reference case.** At v = 10 the scan of the numeric tool marks 148–280 of 312 wall points "vacuum within FD error" and many unconverged, with the counts moving with step size (NAT NEC failures 32 → 58 → 104 → 164 as h goes 0.04/σ → 0.005/σ; identical for the case scaled by 1/100, so it is not coordinate round-off). Cause: with |β| = 10 the coordinate-basis T_ab is ~10⁴× larger than its Eulerian-frame components, so the tetrad projection cancels. Point counts at v = 10 from that scan are lower bounds only; Finding 5 replaces them [calc: runs/2026-10-01-warp-drive-without-negative-energy/calc/lens-constraints_fdcheck.py].
5. **Exact speed scaling removes the FD problem and shows the unit-lapse violations at every speed, subluminal included.** For a rigidly translating N = 1, flat-slice drive, the Eulerian-frame tensor is exactly T(v) = v²A + vB (ρ and S_ij ∝ v², momentum density J_i ∝ v). A and B were extracted from FD frames at v = 1 and 1/2 (A leaks into T_0i at ≤ 7e-8; the v = 2 prediction matches a direct FD frame to a median 2e-9–1.5e-7), then T(v) classified exactly. Results over 312 wall points:
   - Natário, both geometries: NEC, WEC, SEC and DEC fail at 312/312 points for every v in {0.1, 0.5, 1, 2, 10}; Eulerian ρ < 0 at 312/312. Reference case v = 10: worst sampled NEC −2.15e4 m⁻² (= −2.6e48 J/m³ via c⁴/G); type IV at 52 points, type I at 260.
   - Zero-vorticity drive: B ≡ 0 to noise, because J_i = (∂_jK_ij − ∂_iθ)/8π vanishes identically when K_ij = ∂_i∂_jΦ. So T ∝ v², every point is type I with the Eulerian observer as rest frame, and the violation pattern is speed-independent: NEC fails at 288/312 (paper-like) and 284/312 (reference), WEC at 304 and 300, DEC at 312/312, at v = 0.1 as at v = 10. Reference v = 10 worst NEC −1.22e5 m⁻².
   The unit-lapse failure is thus not a superluminal effect: it holds at v = 0.1c, as the Santiago–Schuster–Visser theorem (speed-independent; N = 1, flat slices) requires [D-41] [D-58] [calc: runs/2026-10-01-warp-drive-without-negative-energy/calc/lens-constraints_vscaling.py].
6. **The rebuilt 2024 warp shell passes all four conditions for all observers at β_warp = 0.02, and fails the NEC at β_warp = 0.04.** Published M = 4.49e27 kg, R₁ = 10 m, R₂ = 20 m, rebuilt by `warp_shell.py` (Rb = 0, default smoothing spans, our choice). Dense scan of the shift-transition region (850 points, r = 10.05–19.95 m, 25 angles, h = 0.05 m, 0 unconverged):
   - β = 0.02: 0 violations of NEC/WEC/SEC/DEC, all type I, worst NEC margin +2.2e-5 m⁻²;
   - β = 0.04 (the speed of the paper's Table 1): NEC, WEC, SEC fail at 100/850 points and DEC at 104, with 108 type IV points; worst NEC −8.0e-5 m⁻² (geometric) at r = 12 m perpendicular to the shift, where ρ_E = 1.11e-4 m⁻² = 1.35e40 J/m³, so the violation is 70% of the local density, not noise;
   - β = 0.08: 264/850; β = 0.15: 544/850.
   The coarse whole-wall scan (416 points, r = 9–24 m, h = 0.1 and 0.05 m) agrees: β = 0.04 gives 34–38 NEC failures. Its only failures at β = 0 and 0.02 sit in the smoothing tail at r ≈ 24 m with |T| ~ 1e-12 m⁻², eight orders below the shell density and inside FD noise: artefacts, not physics (the exact type-I test of the zero-shift shell passes). Mechanism: in the 2×2 (t, x) block the NEC along k = (1, ±1) and the type-I condition both reduce to |J_x| ≤ (ρ + p_x)/2, and J ∝ β, so the shift's own momentum density sets a cap. Fuchs et al. say the same qualitatively ("the shift adds momentum flux, so its magnitude has an upper limit"; D-08, D-64) [calc: runs/2026-10-01-warp-drive-without-negative-energy/calc/lens-constraints_shell.py].
7. **The shift changes neither the shell's ADM 4-momentum nor its slice energy.** The exterior (r ≥ R₂) is exact Schwarzschild with S = 0, so K_ij = 0 there: P_ADM = 0 and M_ADM = 4.511e27 kg (2.376 M_J) for every β_warp. Eulerian slice integral at β = 0: E₊ = 5.040e27 kg = 1.117 M_ADM (proper mass exceeds ADM mass by the binding energy); E₋ = −2e18 kg, which is quadrature noise (4e-10 of E₊); grid n vs 1.5n agree to 4e-4. At β = 0.02, E changes by −0.04%. So the warp shell and the plain shell carry identical conserved charges: (M_ADM, P = 0) in the shell frame, and after a boost (γM, γMv), the same as any shell of that mass coasting at v [calc: runs/2026-10-01-warp-drive-without-negative-energy/calc/lens-constraints_shell.py] [D-08].
8. **No travel-time advance is available to the shell inside its energy conditions.** Light along the shift axis through the centre, L = 2000 m each side: the interior static lapse is e^a = 0.761, so interior light runs at β + √(β² + e^{2a}) = 0.802c forward and 0.722c backward at β = 0.04. One-way delay against flat space is +264.6 ns forward and +278.3 ns backward (difference 13.7 ns, independent of L from 1000 to 4000 m; the delays themselves grow with L as the Schwarzschild exterior's logarithmic Shapiro term). Interior light beats c only for β > (1 − e^{2a})/2 = 0.210, and even at β = 0.30 the forward trip is still delayed by +228.9 ns. That is 5–10× above the NEC cap of Finding 6. This is the Gao–Wald / Penrose–Sorkin–Woolgar content in numbers: positive mass delays light, and the shift that might overcome it costs the NEC first [D-60] [D-62] [calc: runs/2026-10-01-warp-drive-without-negative-energy/calc/lens-constraints_shell.py].
9. **The positive-energy shift cap, by bisection: β_crit = 0.0244 ± 0.0002 for the published shell, roughly 0.07 × compactness.** The scan uses 630 points over the transition region, robust verdicts, and h = 0.1, 0.05 and 0.025 m, which give identical counts. NEC failures: 0 at β = 0.020, 3 at 0.025, 22 at 0.030, 69/630 at 0.040. Varying the shell:
   - compactness: β_crit/(2GM/c²R₂) = 0.082, 0.079, 0.073, 0.066 at compactness 0.083, 0.167, 0.333, 0.500;
   - transition buffer Rb = 1 m and 2 m: β_crit = 0.016 and 0.010;
   - smoothing span 0.5 m and 2 m: β_crit = 0.025 and 0.022;
   - scale invariance: scaling to R₁ = 100 m, R₂ = 200 m, M = 4.49e28 kg gives the same β_crit = 0.0244, as it must (T ∝ 1/L² at fixed compactness).
   Extrapolating the ratio (falling with compactness) to the Andréasson/Buchdahl ceiling 2GM/c²R₂ < 8/9 (M < 1.197e28 kg = 6.31 M_J at R₂ = 20 m) gives a cap of roughly β ≲ 0.05–0.06 for this profile family (our extrapolation, not computed past compactness 0.5). The published 0.02 sits just under the cap; the 0.04 used for the time-delay table is above it. The cap is on the internal shift, not on the vehicle's speed: the shell itself can be boosted to any v < c, because that is a coordinate change of a static solution [D-27] [D-63] [D-64] [calc: runs/2026-10-01-warp-drive-without-negative-energy/calc/lens-constraints_shellcap.py].

10. **Independent confirmation of the zero-vorticity structure and of the shell's low-speed pass.** Le's observer-robust preprint states the same structure we found in Finding 5: [new: Le, arXiv:2602.18023v6 abstract, ACCESS full-text (abstract page via fetch_text), "On flat unit-lapse slices, the momentum constraint relates Eulerian momentum to shift vorticity." and "The ideal irrotational Rodal profile has zero Eulerian momentum and is everywhere Type I; Type-IV regions are detected in the other sampled walls."]. The paper's time-delay table is at a higher speed than its energy-condition check: [new: Fuchs et al., arXiv:2405.02709 p. 26, ACCESS full-text, "Comparison of time delay between different warp models and the matter shell for vwarp = 0.04 c."]. At that speed our rebuild violates the NEC (Finding 6).
11. **Quantum inequalities and their domain, Alcubierre reference case (R = 100 m, v = 10).**
   - At Δ = 1 m the wall's peak Eulerian density is ρ_E = −0.9947 m⁻² = −1.20e44 J/m³, at r = R on the transverse axis. The curvature radius there is r_c = 0.115 m. `precision_check` at 50 digits shows no float64 error.
   - The Ford–Roman bound, sampling over τ₀ = 0.1 r_c, is −1.4e-64 m⁻² (−1.7e-20 J/m³). The wall exceeds it by a factor of 7.1e63.
   - The scaling is exact (ρ ∝ σ^2.0000, r_c ∝ σ^−1.0000). The free-field QI therefore allows at most Δ_QI = 1.19e-32 m = 733 L_P = 73 v L_P, consistent with Pfenning–Ford's 10² v L_P [D-29] [D-67]. There E = −v²R²/(18Δ) = −4.7e36 m (geo) = −6.3e63 kg = −3.2e33 M_sun.
   - **Validity domain:** at Δ_QI the curvature radius scales to r_c ≈ 0.115 m × (1.19e-32 m / 1 m) ≈ 1.4e-33 m ≈ 85 L_P. The QI-limited wall therefore sits within two orders of the Planck scale, where semiclassical gravity (and the flat-space QI it uses) is no longer reliably applicable. The QI argument thus does not show that a Planck-thin wall is allowed. It shows that no wall inside the semiclassical domain is allowed.
   - **Horizons:** the v = 10 bubble has horizons on its axis where v(1 − f) = 1 (|ξ| = 99.45 m), with surface gravity κ = 3.6 m⁻¹, giving T_H = 1.3e-3 K. So the Everett–Roman control obstruction and the Finazzi–Liberati–Barceló exponential growth of the renormalized stress tensor both apply [D-13] [D-71] [calc: runs/2026-10-01-warp-drive-without-negative-energy/calc/lens-constraints_qi_horizon_trip.py].
12. **Lentz's C⁰ reply does not open the identity.** The divergence theorem for V = Xθ − (X·∇)X needs only V continuous and piecewise C¹. A C⁰ Eulerian density means K_ij ∈ C⁰, so X ∈ C¹, so V is continuous, and ∫ρ_E = 0 still holds for a localized irrotational shift. A nonzero bulk total needs a jump in ∂X (K discontinuous across a surface). The jump then contributes a surface term −(1/16π)∮[V]·dS, and through ∂_tK = −v∂_ζK it puts δ-function stresses on that surface. Those sheets need their own Israel-junction energy-condition test, which neither Lentz nor this lens has done. Since J ≡ 0 in this class (Finding 5), the Eulerian density is the matter's rest-frame density, so a negative bulk ρ_E anywhere is a WEC failure of the source itself, not an observer artefact [D-30] (analytic, plus [calc: runs/2026-10-01-warp-drive-without-negative-energy/calc/lens-constraints_natario_irrot.py], where the divergence-form and direct totals agree).
13. **Start, stop and self-propulsion are fixed by conservation, whatever the metric.**
   - For an isolated asymptotically flat system the Bondi/ADM 4-momentum changes only through flux at null infinity. A shell at rest therefore reaches v only by emitting momentum γMv.
   - Published shell (M_ADM = 4.511e27 kg), per leg: 3.30e34 kg·m/s at β = 0.0244 and 5.41e34 kg·m/s at 0.04. An ideal photon rocket, which is the floor for any radiative scheme, has start-and-stop mass ratio (1 + β)/(1 − β) = 1.050 at 0.0244 c, which radiates 2.15e26 kg = 1.93e43 J. A bare 1e5 kg payload at the same speed radiates 5.0e3 kg.
   - Le's radiative steering (m_f/m_i = e^(−3L)) is this photon rocket wearing a warp-style cavity, not an exemption [D-38] [D-69] [D-72].
   - "Self-propelled" with only gravitational-wave emission is bounded the same way: GW momentum is also flux at null infinity, and no source shows a GW recoil of order γMv from a warp shell.
   - The interior clock runs at lapse/γ = 0.761 of exterior time at any shell speed ≤ 0.1 c. A rocket matches that slowing at 0.649 c. On the α Cen trip at 0.0244 c this means 179 yr outside and 136 yr inside [calc: runs/2026-10-01-warp-drive-without-negative-energy/calc/lens-constraints_qi_horizon_trip.py].
14. **The surviving shell is still strained by the conditions the energy conditions don't cover (premise 6).**
   - Densities: peak ε = 1.38e40 J/m³ (ρ = 1.53e23 kg/m³, 6.7e5× nuclear saturation, whose value is itself unsourced [D-22]).
   - Stresses: peak tangential pressure p_t = 3.87e39 Pa, and a tangential tension of −1.2e38 Pa near R₁. Both sit about 27.6 orders above the 1 TPa static record [D-44].
   - Sound speed: max |p|/ε = 0.596 < 1, so the DEC (and hence subluminal sound) holds at β = 0.
   - Stability: not computed by any source. Le reports growing modes in self-similar shells [D-72], and Buchert & Frackowiak report generic instability in the Natário class [D-15].
   - Source consistency: Barzegar et al. Error 18 is unanswered [D-31]. Our rebuild takes T_μν = G_μν/8π from the metric, so it shows only that the required stress-energy meets the energy conditions. It does not show that a named matter model with its own equations of motion produces it.

### C. Validity domain of the models the dossier relies on
- **Classical GR with T = G/8π** (all tables): valid for every geometry here at curvature radii ≫ L_P. The reference walls (r_c = 0.115 m for Alcubierre) are inside that domain. The QI-limited walls (r_c ≈ 1.4e-33 m) are not.
- **Free-field flat-space QI** (Pfenning–Ford, Ford–Roman): valid for a minimally coupled free field and sampling τ₀ ≪ r_c. It is not a theorem for interacting or non-minimally coupled fields, and not for states sustained over times longer than r_c.
- **The `warp_shell.py` rebuild**: it uses our smoothing spans (the paper prints only the ratio) and Rb = 0. β_crit moves by about 10% with the span and by a factor 2.4 with Rb = 2 m (F9), so the cap is family-specific. The pass at β = 0.02 survives every variant tested.
- **FD energy-condition scans**: reliable where the step-halving estimate converges. At |β| ≫ 1 the tetrad projection cancels catastrophically (F4). We replaced those scans with exact speed scaling (F5). Pointwise scans sample the space; they are not interval certificates.
- **The question's regime** (macroscopic payload, R ≥ 10 m) lies inside classical GR for all candidates. It lies outside semiclassical control only for the QI-limited superluminal wall.

## What would change my mind
- An all-observer, convergence-checked (or interval-certified) NEC pass of a metric that is asymptotically flat, has a localized warp region, and gives a measured one-way light advance against the undisturbed exterior. That would break Olum and Gao–Wald assumptions. I'd look for null-geodesic incompleteness or failure of the generic condition.
- A smooth shift profile, or a thick transition with L_s ≈ R, that pushes β_crit well above 0.1 × compactness. That would weaken the reframe [CONSTRAINTS-E] quantitatively, not the null.
- A Fuchs-group reply showing the published shell uses a different shift profile, smoothing or buffer such that 0.04 c also passes. That would move β_crit, not the qualitative cap.
- A rigorous QI for interacting fields allowing macroscopic, sustained negative energy density. That would revive [CONSTRAINTS-C] in principle.
- An explicit Lentz solution with a non-localized shift that still meets the brief's asymptotic-flatness definition, plus junction sheets passing the energy conditions.

## Assumptions I relied on
- Classical GR with T_μν = G_μν/8π (G = c = 1, metres; SI via c⁴/G = 1.21e44 N and c²/G = 1.35e27 kg/m). Signature (−,+,+,+).
- **Profiles.** Tanh top-hat with wall convention Δ = 2/σ (D-28) for Alcubierre, Natário and the zero-vorticity proxy. The Natário and zero-vorticity drives are built from my own constructions (curl and gradient of the top-hat), not the authors' exact profiles. The Lentz and Fell–Heisenberg verdicts rest on the class results and the identity, not on their published potentials.
- **Sampling.** Pointwise energy conditions are tested on wall samples: 312–850 points per case, two azimuths, angles from 0.02 to π − 0.02 rad. Type I is tested exactly. Other types use sampled boosts up to 0.99c and 64 null directions. "Everywhere" claims hold only to that sampling.
- **The shell.** It is the toolkit's rebuild (`warp_shell.py`) with our smoothing span and Rb = 0, in the shell's comoving frame. β is the covariant interior g_0x magnitude, as in the paper's eqs. 26–28.
- **Momentum.** Conservation uses the isolated-system Bondi/ADM balance. The photon rocket is treated as the ideal floor for radiative propulsion.
- **QI and horizons.** The QI uses Ford–Roman with τ₀ = 0.1 r_c, where r_c is the peak Eulerian-frame Riemann component at the wall. The horizon temperature uses the 1+1 axis reduction.
