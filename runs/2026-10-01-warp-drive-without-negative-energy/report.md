# Warp drives without negative energy: report
*Run 2026-10-01-warp-drive-without-negative-energy · 2026-10-02 · depth deep · question: "Can any warp-drive spacetime move a payload faster than light, or at all, without negative energy? Weigh the Alcubierre and Natário metrics against the positive-energy and subluminal solutions proposed since 2020 (Lentz, Fell–Heisenberg, Bobrick–Martire, and the 2024 constant-velocity "physical warp drive"), checking each one's energy conditions by calculation. For whatever survives, what would it take to build in mass, energy and technology, and what is the nearest experiment that could test any part of it?"*

## Bottom line
No. Under established physics no warp spacetime carries a payload faster than light without negative energy, meaning a violation of the null energy condition (non-negative energy along every light ray). For the Alcubierre, Natário, Lentz and Fell–Heisenberg family this is a theorem, confirmed here by direct calculation; for massive positive-energy shells the allowed internal "shift" stays 3 to 80 times below what even interior superluminality needs. Confidence about 94%. The 2024 positive-energy shell does pass all four energy conditions at its published setting, but it is a coasting Jupiter-mass shell, not a drive: it moves only as any massive vehicle does, and its one invariant "warp" signature is a 7-nanosecond light-timing asymmetry. Building it lies 22–28 orders of magnitude beyond demonstrated capability.

## Why, candidate by candidate

**C1 (null, 0.94).** *For.* (A) Faster than light. The Santiago–Schuster–Visser theorem (unit lapse, flat slices, localized shift) covers Alcubierre, Natário and the zero-vorticity drives by name [D-41]. Computed from the metric for all observers: Alcubierre fails NEC/WEC/SEC/DEC at 364/364 paper-like and 340–364/364 reference wall points; Natário fails all four at 312/312 for every v from 0.1 to 10; the zero-vorticity proxy fails the NEC at 284–288/312 (lens-constraints_unitlapse, _vscaling logs; pointwise signs verified M-CONSTRAINTS-01/02/03, counts unverified M-CONSTRAINTS-14). For lapse ≠ 1 shells, outside SSV, the deciding theorems are Olum 1998 (assumptions: classical GR, no singularities, generic condition somewhere on the path, which "holds whenever there is any normal matter or any transverse tidal force anywhere on P"), Gao–Wald Theorem 1 (NEC, null generic condition, null completeness; far points only) and Visser–Bassett–Liberati (linear, no generic condition). The worked shell family agrees: NEC fails at β_crit = 0.0239 (M-CONSTRAINTS-13) while interior forward light exceeds c only above β = 0.2103 (M-CONSTRAINTS-07); a design scan over cavity 0.5–16 m and C = 0.01–0.85 never brings the ratio below 3.38 (C1-2); a net advance at D = 1e3 m needs 21–80× the NEC threshold (M-DECOMPOSER-09) and none exists up to β = 0.95 at half or full mass (lens-decomposer_thresholds log). Three independent calculations close the non-generic loophole in the regimes that matter: the first-order delay kernel is positive for any ray and any source motion, minimum forward delay +219.8 ns at D = 1e3 m (falsifier-C1-0); linear censorship shows an advance only at 3–4× the NEC threshold (falsifier-C6-0); and the C6 advocate's own strong-field NEC-saturating family (p_r = −ρ, non-generic along the whole radial ray) is delayed by +3.09 to +177.56 m of light time in all 12 cases (crux-C6_nongeneric_strongfield log). (B) Moving at all. The shell exterior is exact Schwarzschild, so P_ADM = 0 and M_ADM = 4.5114e27 kg at every β (M-MECHANIST-02); a free payload drifts with the Eulerian flow and leaves at rest (falsifier-C1-0 Part 2); the shift's only invariant trace is a co-minus-counter light asymmetry of 6.86 ns at β = 0.02 (M-DECOMPOSER-05), about a forward delay of +267.88 ns; the lapse 0.761 is present at β = 0. *Against.* The lemma that closed the non-generic branch was refuted (M-DECOMPOSER-10), so the strong-field residue is an unproven negative (kept C1 just under 0.95). Olum's no-singularity and Gao–Wald's completeness hypotheses leave a naked-singularity loophole excluded only by cosmic censorship [conjecture]. All scans are samples. Per the C1 advocate's advance concession, (B) should read "nothing the same shell without shift cannot do": the partial g-shielding of C4 belongs to any compact mass. *Decisive test:* an interval-certified pointwise NEC pass plus a computed one-way advance between exterior rest points, for any metric (would collapse C1); or a null-splitting extension of Gao–Wald to non-vacuum NEC spacetimes (would close C6 and raise C1 above 0.95). Headline mover (2×): either result.

**C3 (0.90).** *For.* Bondi/ADM momentum changes only by flux (Le p. 5; Nerz); isolated start plus stop at 0.04 c needs mass ratio (1 + v)/(1 − v) = 1.0833, exhaust 3.38e43 J (M-MECHANIST-08); a GW rocket radiates ≥ 7.69%; Le's DEC-satisfying burns cost e^(3L) = 1.27, and C3-1 read Le's L as the exterior rapidity path length, confirming M-ENGINEER-08. *Against.* "Only by emitting" and "photon-rocket cost" fail for pushed shells (sail 0.49×, kinetic floor 6.49e41 J, 52× lower) (falsifier-C3-0/-1/-2; conceded). Le's construction is a single-author preprint with growing normal modes [D-72]. *Decisive test:* a numerical-relativity burn of a radiating shell tracking Bondi balance against exterior rapidity; a whole-mass ratio below e^L would refute it (and conservation).

**C7 (0.85).** *For.* Net wall momentum is exactly zero at linear order (M-DIALECTICIAN-08) and P_ADM = 0 to all orders; the cavity shift is pure gauge for any wall thickness (falsifier-C7-0), the invariant being the Sagnac holonomy through the wall; Fuchs et al. themselves describe "a circulation pattern" and "linear frame dragging". *Against.* "Frame dragging" is a misnomer (cavity Killing observers are geodesic and twist-free); the 0.07·C cap is profile-specific, and the counterflow floor is 0.100 Mc² for the rebuild profile (conceded). If no matter model sustains any holonomy, the reframe is vacuous and collapses into C1(B). *Decisive test:* an Einstein–matter solve with an elastic counterflowing wall, certifying the energy conditions and computing holonomy, P_ADM and cavity redshift.

**C2 (0.70).** *For.* Rebuild dense scan: 850 points at β = 0.02, 0 violations, all Hawking–Ellis type I (exact tests), worst NEC +2.228e-5 m⁻² geo (lens-constraints_shell log), reproduced by M-CONSTRAINTS-06 (+2.0e-5), M-DECOMPOSER-08 and C2-0 (β_crit 0.02383); the zero-shift shell passes the exact DEC with max|p|/ε = 0.597 (M-ENGINEER-02); the tail beyond R₂ is exact zero-shift Schwarzschild, all conditions holding with max|p|/ε ≤ 0.116, identical at β = 0 and 0.02 (crux-C2_tail log). The paper's "0.04 c" is a comparison speed only; its energy-condition claim is at 0.02. *Decisive test:* an interval-certified Warpax scan of the Warp Factory metric over r ∈ [R₁ − 1, R₂ + 15] m at β = 0 and 0.02 (certified pass → ~0.9; failure or shift-induced tail Type IV → ~0.3), then an elastic-EOS solve. *Nearest experiment:* none reaches warp scale; translational gravitomagnetism is confirmed only in the weak field (LLR ~0.1%, disputed; Jupiter 20%); a rotor beside GINGERINO is 5.1 orders short.

**C8 (0.65).** *For.* Krasnikov's Proposition 1 (globally hyperbolic, divergence only inside J⁺(S)) and Everett–Roman: "the time for a one-way trip to a distant star cannot be shortened"; |β| > N is gauge-dependent, and the comoving horizon at r = 99.45 m (M-EXAMINER-02) makes an on-demand bubble uncontrollable. *Against.* The next sentence of Krasnikov's abstract grants round trips: a tube laid on the outbound leg gives a return-leg advance of 8.696 yr at δ = 0.01 (falsifier-C8-0; crux reproduced), needs NEC violation (NEC min −0.32 m⁻² geo in the wall), and costs 5.6e41–3.4e74 kg of negative energy, 37–70 orders above the causal-access leg. **C4 (0.60).** *For.* LBK 1999 (full text): a free body inside an accelerated shell accelerates "in sympathy but less rapidly", reduced by the interior g₀₀ ratio, complete only at b → 0 (extremal black hole); Pfister et al. 2005: dragging factor 1 only at R = 2M, outside the DEC region (Arnfinnsson–Grøn, full text). Pfister's κ(C) is paywalled. *Decisive test:* numerical relativity of a pushed elastic shell at C ≈ 0.33, F to ±0.045, at β = 0 and 0.02.

**C6 (0.05).** The advocate withdrew "is possible". Branch (a) is outside the brief (de Sitter, comoving, averaged conditions); (d) and (e) are not hypotheses of Olum, Gao–Wald or VBL; (b)'s only example, negative-mass Schwarzschild, advances light (−30.75 m at D = 1e4 m) but carries Komar mass M < 0 in its singularity, and a δ-sheet at the Fuchs mass needs a wall 1.7e-73 m thick; (c) is closed at weak field and delayed in the explicit strong-field family. What survives is a proof gap, true but not a route.

## Assumptions every surviving answer shares
Classical GR plus semiclassical gravity as the admissible frame (modified gravity and negative active mass [D-81] excluded as speculative); an asymptotically flat exterior and the brief's travel-time definition; that the toolkit rebuild (smoothing spans, Rb = 0, TOV profile, none printed in the paper) represents the Fuchs construction; that sampled energy-condition scans on two to 25 lines stand in for certificates; that Olum's generic condition is met wherever matter with T_kk > 0 is crossed and the strong-field residue stays empty; 4-momentum conservation for isolated systems; linear-gravity estimates used at C = 0.33–0.67 good to about a factor of 2.

## What would change this verdict
1. **Certified energy-condition scan plus matter-model solve of the Warp Factory shell** (Le's Warpax or the Helmerich group; feasible now, no date). A certified pass with an elastic EOS sustaining β ≈ 0.02 lifts C2 to ~0.9 and confirms C7's object; Barzegar's Error 18 confirmed independently drops C2 to ~0.2 and empties C7, leaving C1(B) vacuously true.
2. **A theorem or certified counterexample for the strong-field non-generic class** (mathematical-relativity groups via Galloway's null-line remark; now). A theorem pushes C1 above 0.95 and C6 to ≤ 0.02; a certified NEC pass with a computed one-way advance collapses C1 and makes C6 the answer.
3. **Numerical relativity of a pushed elastic shell at C ≈ 0.33, β = 0 and 0.02** (groups like Clough et al.; no timeline). F ≈ 0.6–0.76 independent of β confirms narrowed C4 and C1(B); F ≥ 0.92 collapses C4 into C7; a β-dependent F would be the first warp-specific payload effect and weaken C1(B).
