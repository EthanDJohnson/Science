## Refuted
- None. No load-bearing claim failed.
- Corrections that change no finding or candidate answer:
  - M-01: the overall sign of the F3 momentum-density formula is reversed for the lens's stated (dx + β dt) convention. The correct form is j_x = +(β₀/16π)[f″ sin²θ + f′(1 + cos²θ)/r] for g_tx = +β₀f. Only magnitudes and the zero total are used downstream.
  - M-09: γ is dropped (83.07 rather than 83.14 yr at 0.04 c).
  - M-11 and M-08: F7 and F10 use different mass conventions (4.511e27 kg is the final mass in F7 and the initial mass in F10).
## Unverified
- **Toolkit outputs used as inputs:** M_ADM = 4.511e27 kg, centre lapse 0.761 and the smoothed density profile (F2). They come from `warp_shell.py`; I used them but did not re-derive them. The examiner's M-EXAMINER-06 bound puts the lapse between the thin-shell values 0.574 (at R₁) and 0.816 (at R₂), and 0.761 lies in that range. The shell's DEC with zero shift (max \|p\|/ε = 0.60) was not checked here; the constraints lens owns it.
- **Linear-theory scope:** M-01, 03, 04, 05, 07 and 10 are verified as statements inside linear, flat-slice (or harmonic-gauge) gravity. The shell has compactness 0.33–0.67, so whether they hold quantitatively in the nonlinear solution is not settled by any check here. The lens labels them as scale estimates, and this matters most for MECHANIST-C (the 0.054 c ceiling and κ). The ceiling also depends on the toolkit's smoothing span, which is the toolkit's choice and is not printed in the paper.
- **κ coefficient beyond its model (F9):** the pushing agent's stresses and retardation are not included. Lynden-Bell, Bičák & Katz discuss how these affect induced interior accelerations, so 4GM/(c²R) is verified only under the lens's stated simplifications.
- **Not mathematical, or quoted:** the "cubic" reading of Le's e^(−3L) depends on L being the rapidity (a source question; only e^(−3L) < e^(−L) for L > 0 was checked). Also not checked: the Sperhake kick value, GP-B and LAGEOS precisions, and world energy (592.2 EJ/yr, used only arithmetically). The F4 claim that matter is positive wherever the momentum sits is consistent with the M-05 grid (the ratio is finite everywhere in R₁ < r < R₂) but was not tested separately.
## Formalizable
- M-MECHANIST-01 (radial part): for f ∈ C²[0, ∞) with f′ of compact support in (0, ∞), ∫₀^∞ (r²f″ + 2rf′) dr = 0. The angular parts: ∫_{S²} sin²θ dΩ = 8π/3 and ∫_{S²} (1 + cos²θ) dΩ = 16π/3.
- M-MECHANIST-04: for M > 0, 0 < R₁ < R₂, p₁ = p₂ = p, 0 ≤ p ≤ E_k and E₁ + E₂ ≤ M: 4p(1/R₁ − 1/R₂) ≤ 2M/R₁ − 2M/R₂.
- M-MECHANIST-06: for 0 ≤ b < N, with metric block [[−N² + b², b], [b, 1]], the vectors u_p = (1/N, −b/N) and u_s = (1/√(N² − b²), 0) satisfy −g(u_s, u_p) = 1/√(1 − b²/N²).
- M-MECHANIST-08 / 11: for b ∈ (0, 1), γ(1 + b) = √((1 + b)/(1 − b)) = e^{atanh b}; for L > 0, e^{−3L} < e^{−L}.
