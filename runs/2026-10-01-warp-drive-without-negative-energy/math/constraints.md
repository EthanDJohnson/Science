## Refuted
- **M-CONSTRAINTS-13 (β_crit):** the claim β_crit = 0.0244 ± 0.0002 (F9) is too high, and its error bar too small. With dense sampling (0.01 m) on the axis perpendicular to the shift, the NEC fails already at β = 0.02425 (min −2.0e-6 m⁻² at r = 12.17 m). Bisection gives β_crit = 0.02386 ± 0.00001, an upper bound for the shell as a whole, since other points could be worse. The correct statements are: β_crit ≈ 0.0239 (published profile, toolkit rebuild, Rb = 0, default smoothing); β_crit/C ≈ 0.072; the frame-drag cap β_crit/N_in ≈ 0.0314 c (not 0.032 c); the momentum per leg at β_crit is ≈ 3.23e34 kg m/s. Findings F9, F10 and F13 and candidates CONSTRAINTS-B, CONSTRAINTS-E and CONSTRAINTS-F carry these numbers. No verdict changes: 0.02 still passes (min NEC +2.0e-5 m⁻²), and 0.025, 0.03 and 0.04 still fail. F10's prediction that the test fails at v_s ≥ 0.025 still holds.
- **M-CONSTRAINTS-08 (extrapolated cap):** F9 and CONSTRAINTS-B and E say the cap at the Buchdahl ceiling is "roughly 0.05–0.06". Extrapolating the lens's own falling ratio gives β ≈ 0.042–0.046. Only a ratio frozen at 0.066 gives 0.059. The correct statement: β ≲ 0.04–0.06 at C = 8/9, with 0.06 the generous end. The qualitative cap, and "below about 0.06", stand.
- **M-CONSTRAINTS-11 (C⁰ loophole mechanics):** F12 says a nonzero bulk total needs a jump in ∂X, which contributes a surface term −(1/16π)∮[V]·dS. For a continuous shift that surface term is identically zero, because [V·n] = (X·n)(n·a) − (X·n)(a·n) = 0 when [∂_iX_j] = n_ia_j. The tent example has kinks and gives bulk pieces ±1.5, with total 0. So ∫ρ_E = 0 holds for every continuous, piecewise-C¹, localized curl-free shift. A nonzero bulk total needs a jump in X itself, where K carries a δ and ρ_E = (θ² − K·K)/16π a δ² that is not defined. This strengthens CONSTRAINTS-D, and F12's conclusion, that the C⁰ reply does not open the identity, stands. F12's step "C⁰ ρ_E ⇒ K ∈ C⁰" is a non sequitur, but nothing needs it. The δ-function stresses from the evolution term at a kink in K (F12) were not checked here.

## Formalizable
- M-CONSTRAINTS-03: for X ∈ C²(ℝ³, ℝ³), (∇·X)² − ∂_iX_j∂_jX_i = ∂_i(X_i∇·X) − ∂_j(X_i∂_iX_j). If additionally curl X = 0, then Σ_ij (½(∂_iX_j + ∂_jX_i))² = ∂_iX_j∂_jX_i. Hence for curl-free X ∈ C²_c (compact support), ∫_ℝ³ [(∇·X)² − ‖sym ∇X‖²] d³x = 0.
- M-CONSTRAINTS-11: for unit n ∈ ℝ³ and a, X ∈ ℝ³, with J = n aᵀ, (X·n) tr J − Xᵀ J n = 0. Consequently the identity above extends to continuous, piecewise-C¹, compactly supported curl-free X, with interfaces on smooth surfaces.
- M-CONSTRAINTS-04: for Φ ∈ C³, ∂_j∂_i∂_jΦ − ∂_i∇²Φ = 0.
- M-CONSTRAINTS-05: for real ρ, p, J, the matrix diag(−1, 1)·[[ρ, −J], [−J, p]] has real distinct eigenvalues iff (ρ + p)² > 4J². For ρ + p > 0 and |J| < (ρ + p)/2, the eigenvector of the lower eigenvalue, (1, w), has |w| < 1. And kᵀTk = ρ + p ∓ 2J for k = (1, ±1).
- M-CONSTRAINTS-07: for A ∈ (0, 1) and β ≥ 0, √(β² + A) + β > 1 ⇔ β > (1 − A)/2.
