## Refuted
- **M-DECOMPOSER-09 (minor arithmetic, in F7).**
  - The lens's own table gives advance-to-NEC threshold factors of 21, 70 and 80, not "8 to 80". The corrected range is about 21 to 80.
  - "Above the lowest-mass row", beta_NEC/C runs 0.090, 0.084, 0.079 and 0.074, so the range is 0.074–0.090, not 0.074–0.084. The 0.01 M row (beta_NEC = 3e-4) has a bisection resolution of ±6e-5, which puts its ratio anywhere in 0.072–0.108.
  - Extrapolated to C = 8/9, the cap is then ≲ 0.08, not ≲ 0.07.
  - The qualitative conclusions stand: the advance threshold lies above the NEC threshold at every mass, and the cap is roughly linear in compactness. DECOMPOSER-B's "≲ 0.07 at the Buchdahl limit" should read "≲ 0.08", and the cap stays profile-specific.
- **M-DECOMPOSER-10 (the lemma used to eliminate DECOMPOSER-D, and ledger P5).**
  - A null geodesic can fail the generic condition everywhere in vacuum, with no transverse tidal force, and still not be Minkowski. Radial null geodesics in Schwarzschild are the counterexample: the generic tensor is identically zero (they run along the principal null directions), the Kretschmann scalar is 48M²/r⁶ ≠ 0, and they are Shapiro-delayed.
  - Corrected form: failure of the generic condition means only that the optical tidal matrix R_acbd k^c k^d vanishes, modulo terms along k, on the path. It does not make the region flat.
  - So the clause "a non-generic path ... is locally Minkowski and gains nothing" does not establish that such a path gains nothing. That needs a separate argument, for example that Olum's theorem needs the generic condition only somewhere on the path, which matter crossings supply through R_ab k^a k^b > 0.
  - What depends on it: the elimination of DECOMPOSER-D (the non-generic-path branch) and the P5 row of the assumption ledger. The worked shell family itself is unaffected: its axial ray crosses matter, where R_ab k^a k^b = 8π(ε + p_r) > 0.

## Formalizable
- **M-DECOMPOSER-01:** let T be a real symmetric bilinear form on R^{1,3}. If T(u,u) ≥ 0 for every u with η(u,u) < 0, then T(k,k) ≥ 0 for every k with η(k,k) = 0. The converse is false.
- **M-DECOMPOSER-02:** for real E and a vector P in R³ with E ≥ |P|: if E > 0 then |P|/E ≤ 1, with equality iff E = |P|; and E = 0 implies P = 0.
- **M-DECOMPOSER-03:** for 0 < A < 1 and 0 ≤ b < 1: b + sqrt(b² + A) > 1 ⟺ b > (1 - A)/2.
- **M-DECOMPOSER-10:** for the Schwarzschild metric with r > 2M and k = (1/f, 1, 0, 0), the tensor k_[e R_a]cd[b k_f] k^c k^d vanishes identically, while R_abcd R^abcd = 48M²/r⁶ ≠ 0. This is a finite symbolic computation.
