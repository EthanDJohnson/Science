## Formalizable
- M-EXAMINER-02: for real v ∈ (0, 1) and f ∈ [0, 1], −1 + v²(1 − f)² < 0; for v > 1 the zero in f ∈ [0, 1] is unique, at f = 1 − 1/v.
- M-EXAMINER-04: for N ∈ (0, 1) and b ∈ [0, N), 2R(1/(N + b) − 1) < 0 ⇔ b > 1 − N; and 2R(1/(N − b) − 1/(N + b)) = 4Rb/(N² − b²).
- M-EXAMINER-05: for L > 0 and b ≥ 0, L − L/(1 + b) = Lb/(1 + b).
- M-EXAMINER-06: if Φ ∈ C¹[R₁, R₂] and Φ' > 0 on [R₁, R₂], then Φ(R₁) < Φ(R₂). The physics content is the sign condition m + 4πr³p_r > 0.
