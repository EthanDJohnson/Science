## Unverified
- No claim row is unverified. These steps that findings rest on were not re-derived because they are literature or quoted premises, not the lens's mathematics: the Beig–Chruściel theorem (p^μ = 0 for a spacelike or null asymptotic Killing vector) and its decay hypotheses, and the positive-energy-theorem rigidity step (p = 0 with the DEC ⇒ Minkowski) behind S2 (i) and DIALECTICIAN-A. "P_ADM = 0 in Fuchs's frame" follows from the quoted static, zero-shift exterior (K_ij = 0); I did not check it separately. Lentz's operator ∂_x² + ∂_y² − (2/v_h²)∂_z² is the lens's reading of D-06; M-03 checks only its characteristics.
- Notes on scope, none of them refutations: (M-02) whether the bulk or the sheet carries the negative part depends on the sign of g(0)[g′]; only "bulk + sheet = 0" is general. (M-06, M-07) 3.3 µs and 4.5–4.6 ns are in Killing (asymptotic) time; a static cavity observer measures 2.54 µs and 3.47–3.51 ns.
## Formalizable
- M-DIALECTICIAN-01: for φ ∈ C³(ℝ³), (Δφ)² − Σ_ij(∂_i∂_jφ)² = Σ_i ∂_i(∂_iφ Δφ − Σ_j ∂_jφ ∂_i∂_jφ); hence for φ ∈ C³_c(ℝ³) the integral is 0.
- M-DIALECTICIAN-02: for φ ∈ C⁰_c(ℝ³), smooth on each side of the plane x = 0 with a jump [∂_xφ] across it, the distributional expression (Δφ)² − Σ(∂_i∂_jφ)² is well defined (the δ² terms cancel), equals the bulk value plus 2[∂_xφ](∂_y² + ∂_z²)φ δ(x), and has total integral 0. If φ ∈ C¹ with only ∂_x²φ jumping, the bulk integral alone is 0.
- M-DIALECTICIAN-03: the characteristic set of ∂_x² + ∂_y² − (2/v²)∂_z² (v > 0) is the cone x² + y² = (v²/2)z².
- M-DIALECTICIAN-04: in Minkowski space, A = ∂_t + v∂_x has η(A, A) = v² − 1, which is ≥ 0 iff v ≥ 1; and p = λA with η(p, p) = −M² gives |p⃗| = Mv/√(1 − v²).
- M-DIALECTICIAN-07: L/(α − b) − L/(α + b) = 2Lb/(α² − b²) for 0 < b < α.
