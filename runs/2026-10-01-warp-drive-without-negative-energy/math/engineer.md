## Refuted
- M-ENGINEER-05 (F9; ENGINEER-C): the shift formula β = (4GP/c³)(1/R1 − 1/R2) is right, but the energy bound counts only one of the two counterflowing streams.
  - Corrected: E_flow ≥ 2cP = 2.42e43 J = 2.7e26 kg-equivalent = 0.060 Mc², not 1.2e43 J (0.03 Mc²).
  - The matching counterflow speed, if the whole mass carries it, is 0.060c, not 0.03c.
  - The "4e30 kg at 10 km/s" and "2e26 kg at β = 1e-6" are per stream; the totals are 8.1e30 kg and 4.0e26 kg.
  - Consequence: none for the conclusions. The published 4.49e27 kg still exceeds the corrected minimum by about 17×. ENGINEER-C's law β ~ C·u/c and its "needs near-black-hole compactness and relativistic currents" survive; the required flowing mass is twice what F9 states.
- M-ENGINEER-06 (F11; engineering limits, "Power and heat"): GM²/R̄ = 9.05e43 J = 0.22 Mc² is computed correctly, but it is not the binding energy of this shell.
  - Corrected: 3.91e43 J = 0.096 Mc² (Newtonian, uniform shell); 4.75e43 J = 0.118 Mc² = 8.1e22 world-years (GR proper-mass deficit).
  - Consequence: none for the conclusions. "About 23 orders above world energy" stands (22.9).
- M-ENGINEER-15 (engineering limits, "Cost"): 4.51e27 kg is 6.9e23 years of world mass-energy (6.6 t/yr), not "about 1e23 years". F6 (23.8 orders) already states it correctly. Wording only.
