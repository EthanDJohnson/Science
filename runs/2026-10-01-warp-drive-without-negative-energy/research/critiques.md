## Claims
- [RC-01] CLAIM: Santiago, Schuster & Visser (PRD 105, 064038, 2022) critique the three 2020-21 positive-energy claims (Lentz; Bobrick-Martire; Fell-Heisenberg): those papers only examine the comoving Eulerian observers, whereas the WEC needs all timelike observers; within the unit-lapse/flat-slice (Natario-type) framework all "physically reasonable" warp drives violate the WEC, SEC and DEC, and under plausible subsidiary conditions the NEC.
  SOURCE: Santiago, Schuster, Visser 2022, "Generic warp drives violate the null energy condition", PRD 105 064038, arXiv:2105.03079 (arXiv preprint of the published paper)
  QUOTE: "These claims are at best incomplete, since the arguments as presented only assert but do not prove the existence of one set of timelike observers, the co-moving Eulerian observers, who see relatively “nice” physics. While these particular observers might arguably see a positive energy density, the WEC requires all timelike observers to see positive energy density." ... "within the framework adopted by those three papers all physically reasonable warp drives will certainly violate the WEC, and both the strong and dominant energy conditions. Under plausible subsidiary conditions the null energy condition is also violated."
  ACCESS: full-text
  STATUS: peer-reviewed
  CONFIDENCE: high

- [RC-02] CLAIM: Santiago et al. list the prior-known results: Alcubierre violates the WEC and (later shown) the NEC; Natario zero-expansion violates the WEC; they strengthen this to show Natario zero-expansion also violates SEC and NEC, and generic Natario drives (incl. the variants of refs 1-3) violate WEC and, under plausible subsidiary conditions, NEC. Their scope is the Natario class (unit lapse, flat slices), not arbitrary lapse/curved slices.
  SOURCE: arXiv:2105.03079 (as above), Section 1
  QUOTE: "Alcubierre warp drives have long been known to violate the WEC [9], and have more recently been shown to also violate the null energy condition (NEC) [29]. • Natário zero-expansion warp drives have long been known to violate the WEC [10]. • Generic Natário warp drives are known to violate the dominant energy condition (DEC) [15], and have also been shown to violate either the strong energy condition (SEC) or the WEC [10]."
  ACCESS: full-text
  STATUS: peer-reviewed
  CONFIDENCE: high

- [RC-03] CLAIM: Celmaster & Rubin (Nov 2025, preprint) report that Lentz's 2020/21 claim of non-negative energy density is incorrect: direct computation in the Eulerian frame gives negative-energy regions; the derivation has several errors; a modified Lentz geometry that respects his stated equalities/inequalities still violates the WEC even for Eulerian observers. They conclude no Natario-type warp drive has been demonstrated to satisfy the WEC.
  SOURCE: Celmaster & Rubin 2025, "Violations of the Weak Energy Condition for Lentz Warp Drives", arXiv:2511.18251v1 (preprint, not peer-reviewed as of my search)
  QUOTE: "We demonstrate that Lentz’s claim is incorrect. We begin with a direct calculation of the energy-momentum tensor of Lentz’s warp drive in a Eulerian reference frame, and show that there are spacetime regions where the energy density is negative. We then examine the theoretical basis of Lentz’s investigation and identify several derivation errors." ... "Lentz’s demonstration is incorrect. Hence, the paradox is resolved: no Natario warp-drive has been demonstrated to comply with the WEC."
  ACCESS: full-text
  STATUS: preprint
  CONFIDENCE: medium

- [RC-04] CLAIM: Celmaster & Rubin stress that Lentz's source functions give singular (delta-function / vertex) regions, and that the WEC has no "singular-line exclusion": the integrated energy, with unambiguous treatment of delta functions, is what matters. This bears directly on hidden premise 1 (distributional sheet contributions at kinks).
  SOURCE: arXiv:2511.18251, Sec. 6
  QUOTE: "We cannot ignore the singular regions just because of the delta functions. The WEC does not have a ‘singular-line exclusion.’ In fact, as we shall discuss further in the context of “no-go theorems,” the T term is essential for computing the total energy that is the integrated energy-density. Even though the energy density is ambiguous (owing to the options for completion of the singular planes), the integrated value (with unambiguous treatment of δ functions) is not."
  ACCESS: full-text
  STATUS: preprint
  CONFIDENCE: medium

- [RC-05] CLAIM: Barzegar et al. (Feb 2026, preprint) catalogue "Errors" in the "physical warp drive" papers: they say the Fuchs et al. 2024 construction "is not a solution to Einstein's equations" because the TOV equations are not actually solved (their Appendix B); that Fuchs et al.'s "constant velocity warp drive" has no meaning unless contravariant and covariant 4-velocity components are distinguished (Error 13); that Fuchs et al. is vague on whether the metric is asymptotically flat; and that the "Galilean transformation to a constant-velocity comoving frame" yields a time-independent metric by dropping time derivatives (the same mistake as in Bobrick-Martire). These are a preprint's claims against the 2024 shell, not settled; authors' reply not found yet.
  SOURCE: Barzegar et al. 2026, "General formalism, classification, and demystification of the current warp-drive spacetimes", arXiv:2602.16495 (preprint)
  QUOTE: "Error 18. The construction proposed by Fuchs et al. [28] is not a solution to Einstein’s equations; indeed, as we showed in Appendix B, the authors do not solve the Tolman–Oppenheimer–Volkoff (TOV) equations resulting from the Einstein equation in the given context. Hence, the algorithm in Fuchs et al. [28, Figure 3] for “metric creation” is flawed."
  ACCESS: full-text
  STATUS: preprint
  CONFIDENCE: medium

- [RC-06] CLAIM: Lentz's published reply (16th Marcel Grossmann proceedings, 2022) does not concede; it argues (i) the Santiago et al. divergence-plus-negative-definite decomposition E = (1/16π)(∂_i(N^i ∂_j N^j − N^j ∂_j N^i) − ½ ω_i ω^i) cannot be integrated with the divergence theorem over all space because his Eulerian density is only continuous (not first-order smooth) at the planes x=0 and y=0, so a "patchwork" divergence theorem (Dray & Hellaby) is needed; and (ii) the no-go proofs assume a limit (collapsing the soliton interior to a point to get one fastest causal path) that his soliton "cannot undergo ... without being destroyed", so he says they do not apply. Celmaster & Rubin (RC-03/04) later computed negative Eulerian density and delta-function contributions at those same singular planes and say the reply's premise fails.
  SOURCE: Lentz 2022, "Hyper-fast positive energy warp drives", Proc. 16th Marcel Grossmann Meeting (World Scientific, doi:10.1142/9789811269776_0061), arXiv:2201.00652
  QUOTE: "This argument does not hold in the case of Lentz 2021 as the Eulerian energy density of the example positive energy soliton is smooth save for the boundaries x = 0 and y = 0 where stress-energy sources are only continuous, while a requirement of the divergence theorem is that the total divergence be at least first order smooth everywhere." ... "The example positive energy warp drive of Lentz 2021 cannot undergo this limit without being destroyed. This drive therefore does not meet the pre-conditions of the proofs and exists outside their scope, implying that the proofs are not applicable."
  ACCESS: full-text
  STATUS: peer-reviewed (proceedings); author's own defence
  CONFIDENCE: high (that he says this); the argument's validity is contested by RC-03

- [RC-11] CLAIM: Bolívar, Abellán & Vasilev (Aug 2026, preprint) prove a static boundary obstruction: in the unit-lapse flat-slice radial Painlevé-Gullstrand class an empty flat cavity surrounded by a regular nonnegative-density wall has Type-I source p_r = -ρ and p_perp = -ρ - rρ'/2, so a density that rises out of the cavity violates the transverse NEC/WEC; but once the unit-lapse restriction is released an "incomplete-beta family" of regular hollow shells with flat cavities and Schwarzschild exteriors satisfies NEC/WEC/SEC/DEC on an explicit compactness interval. They state the result is "a static boundary theorem and construction, not ... a transport result". This is the critique-literature statement of why the 2021 positive-energy claims (unit lapse) fail and why a nonconstant lapse (the Bobrick-Martire / Fuchs shell route) is the loophole.
  SOURCE: Bolívar, Abellán, Vasilev 2026, "Boundary Obstructions and Lapse Freedom in Static Spherical Hollow Cores", arXiv:2608.15000 (preprint)
  QUOTE: "A regular nonnegative density that rises out of the cavity must therefore violate the transverse null and weak energy conditions." ; "The result is a static boundary theorem and construction, not a general hollow-shell existence theorem, a transport result, or an experimental feasibility claim."
  ACCESS: abstract
  STATUS: preprint
  CONFIDENCE: medium

- [RC-12] CLAIM: Control/causality objection (Everett & Roman 1997): in Alcubierre's superluminal bubble an observer at the centre is causally separated from the outer wall, so cannot create or control the bubble. Scope: superluminal (v > c) bubbles only; it does not apply to a subluminal shell, which has no horizon.
  SOURCE: Everett & Roman 1997, "A superluminal subway: the Krasnikov tube", PRD 56, 2100, arXiv:gr-qc/9702049
  QUOTE: "The “warp drive” metric recently presented by Alcubierre has the problem that an observer at the center of the warp bubble is causally separated from the outer edge of the bubble wall. Hence such an observer can neither create a warp bubble on demand nor control one once it has been created."
  ACCESS: full-text
  STATUS: peer-reviewed
  CONFIDENCE: high

- [RC-13] CLAIM: Semiclassical instability (Finazzi, Liberati & Barceló 2009, PRD 79, 124017): for superluminal bubbles the renormalized stress-energy tensor of a quantum field is predicted to grow exponentially in time at the front wall, and an observer at the centre would generically see a thermal Hawking-like flux; the flux is extremely high if the exotic matter comes from a field obeying quantum inequalities. Scope: superluminal bubbles (those with horizons); it says nothing about a subluminal shell without a horizon. Coutant, Finazzi, Liberati & Parentani (PRD 85, 064020, 2012) found the instability persists with ultraviolet Lorentz-violating dispersion (abstract only seen).
  SOURCE: Finazzi, Liberati, Barceló 2009, "Semiclassical instability of dynamical warp drives", PRD 79 124017, arXiv:0904.0141
  QUOTE: "an observer located at the center of a superluminal warp-drive bubble would generically experience a thermal flux of Hawking particles. On the other side, such Hawking flux will be generically extremely high if the exotic matter supporting the warp drive has its origin in a quantum field satisfying some form of Quantum Inequalities. Most of all, we find that the RSET will exponentially grow in time close to, and on, the front wall of the superluminal bubble."
  ACCESS: abstract
  STATUS: peer-reviewed
  CONFIDENCE: high

Relevance to critiques: evolution of an Alcubierre-type bubble is not stable by itself; only the failure signal is computed. Details of the waveform left to the frontier facet.
  SOURCE: Clough, Dietrich, Khan 2024, "What no one has seen before: gravitational waveforms from warp drive collapse", arXiv:2406.02466
  QUOTE: "Whilst there are numerous practical barriers to their implementation in real life, including a requirement for negative energy, computationally, one can simulate their evolution in time given an equation of state describing the matter. In this work, we study the signatures arising from a warp drive "containment failure", assuming a stiff equation of state for the fluid."
