# Dossier: The neutron lifetime puzzle: proton-counting beam (~888 s) versus UCN storage (~878 s)
status: final

Units: SI throughout (lifetimes in s, rates in s⁻¹, fields in T or µT). Particle masses and energies are in eV, keV or MeV (natural units, ħ = c = 1, where the source uses them). λ = gA/gV, Vud, Vus and branching ratios are dimensionless. Sign convention: λ is negative in the sources that use it; "gA" in CMS 2018, Berezhiani and lattice papers means |λ|.

Inputs: brief.md; research/theory.md, quantitative.md, engineering.md, critiques.md, frontier.md; all five *.check.md files. No prior runs carried (brief: none bear on this question). No user-supplied data.

## 1. Established

- [D-01] In the SM the total radiative-inclusive neutron decay rate obeys the master formula 1/τn = (G_µ²|Vud|²/2π³) m_e⁵ (1 + 3gA²)(1 + RC) f. With Vud, τn and gA free, CMS 2018 give |Vud|² τn (1 + 3gA²) = 4908.6(1.9) s, the error dominated by radiative corrections; with superallowed Vud they give τn(1 + 3gA²) = 5172.0(1.1) s. (refs: RT-01, RQ-12, RT-07) peer-reviewed, verified.
- [D-02] Later evaluations change the constant only at the 10⁻⁴ level: Gorchtein & Seng write |Vud|² = 5024.7 s / [τn(1 + 3λ²)(1 + ΔR^V)] with recommended ΔR^V = 0.02479(21); Tan gives 5024.46(30) s with outer correction δ'_R = 0.014902(2), and Dubbers et al. use f = 1.6887(2). The theory (RC) uncertainty is of order 10⁻⁴ relative, far below the ~1.1% gap, so radiative-correction uncertainty cannot by itself produce the puzzle. (refs: RT-03, RT-13, RQ-12) review / peer-reviewed, verified.
- [D-03] The pion-induced radiative correction (Cirigliano et al. 2022) shifts the relation between QCD gA and measured λ at the percent level but "does not impact the extraction of V_ud from experiments"; it matters for the lattice-gA comparison (2.7σ shift against CalLat19, ~1σ against FLAG21), not for τ_β. (refs: RT-10, RQ-15) peer-reviewed, verified.
- [D-04] SM effects that could make a proton-counting beam differ from the total rate are far too small: bound-state decay n → H + ν̄ is predicted at ~4×10⁻⁶ branching; radiative decay n → p e ν̄ γ (branching 0.00335 ± 0.00005 ± 0.00015 for 14–782 keV photons) still yields a proton and an electron, so it is counted by both beam types and is inside the master-formula total. Neither approaches 1.1% (bound state is ~2750× smaller). (refs: RT-11, RT-02, RT-21) peer-reviewed, verified. [Corrected: RT-11's "factor ~250" is unexplained arithmetic and is dropped; see §7.]
- [D-05] The Zeeman energy |µn|B is ~277 neV at 4.6 T and ~302 neV at 5 T, ~4×10⁻¹³ of the decay Q-value (0.782 MeV), so no SM field dependence of the decay rate in the proton trap is relevant; only a near-degenerate level (e.g. n–n′ with Δm ~ 100–300 neV) could make a strong field matter. (refs: RT-21, RT-08) own calculation of the theory facet [calc: runs/2026-09-30-neutron-lifetime-beam-bottle/calc/theory_scales.py], constants from memory; UNCHECKED by the source checker (arithmetic judged plausible).
- [D-06] The two method classes measure different quantities: a proton-counting beam measures τn / Br(n → p + anything) (Fornal–Grinstein eq. 1), an electron-counting beam measures the rate of decays with an electron, and a storage experiment measures total disappearance (all decays plus any unidentified loss). In the SM Br(n → p + anything) = 100%. (refs: RT-05, brief definitions) peer-reviewed, verified.
- [D-07] The NIST BL1 current value (Yue 2013) is a re-analysis of the SAME 2005 beam data (Nico 2005) with a re-measured ⁶Li fluence-monitor efficiency (Alpha-Gamma device), a +1.4 s correction; the 2005 and 2013 values must not be averaged as independent. (refs: RE-05, RE-06) peer-reviewed, verified.
- [D-08] BL1 extracts τn from the slope of proton rate / (α + t monitor rate) versus trap length (3–10 electrodes), so the electrostatic end effect cancels; four collimation/filter configurations were used; series intercepts differ by up to 13%. Its proton-trap corrections are large (trap nonlinearity −5.3 s, absorption by ⁶Li +5.4 s). (refs: RE-18) peer-reviewed (arXiv text), verified.
- [D-09] J-PARC counts decay electrons in a TPC and normalises with ³He(n,p)³H captures in the same gas (σ = 5333 ± 7 b), with Geant4-simulated efficiencies; it needs no ⁶Li deposit and has no strong trapping field. (refs: RE-01, RE-02, RE-20) preprint, verified.
- [D-10] UCNτ (LANL) stores UCN in a wall-free magneto-gravitational trap with in-situ counting; its holding times were blinded (factor 0.99986–1.00171), four independent analyses (A–D) were run per year, and in-situ "dagger" detector, variable-height barrier and four detector geometries probe uncleaned/heated UCN and rate effects. Its largest systematic is the event-definition choice, then residual uncleaned or heated (marginally trapped) UCN. (refs: RE-03, RE-04, RE-09, RF-06) peer-reviewed, verified.
- [D-11] After unblinding UCNτ found an analysis bias in 2020 (seven runs wrongly excluded); re-including them shifted the lifetime of a 659-run subset by 1.0 s (equal to that subset's 1.0 s statistical error). (refs: RE-09) peer-reviewed, verified.
- [D-12] Gravitrap (PNPI) extrapolates in trap size (geometry), reducing dependence on the UCN–wall loss model. (refs: RE-07) peer-reviewed, verified (table and method); the "20–40 s → ~2 s" error-reduction figure is dropped as unverified (§7).
- [D-13] Space-based lifetimes (Lunar Prospector) are not competitive: temperature-model choice shifts τ by 28.7 ± 15.5 s and composition-map choice by 10.3 ± 12.2 s; they cannot arbitrate a 10 s gap. (refs: RF-10) peer-reviewed, verified.
- [D-14] Lattice-QCD gA (ETM: 1.250(24), ~2% precision) is far less precise than measured λ (~0.04% for PERKEO III, ~0.1% for the PDG average) and cannot arbitrate. (refs: RQ-17, RQ-01, RQ-03) peer-reviewed, abstract. [Checker: the "~20×" ratio holds only against the PDG error; against PERKEO III it is ~50×.]
- [D-15] The β-asymmetry (A) data are mutually consistent (PDG 2024 A = −0.11958 ± 0.00021, S = 1.2, CL 0.261); the PDG λ scale factor of 2.7 comes from including the proton-recoil (a-coefficient) inputs aSPECT and aCORN. (refs: RQ-03, RQ-04) review, verified; attribution of the scale factor is the researcher's inference, supported by the A-only χ².
- [D-16] Given the measured λ, the bottle lifetime is compatible with superallowed Vud and first-row unitarity while the beam lifetime "suggests a significant discrepancy with both" (Gorchtein & Seng 2023). (refs: RT-04) review; quote not re-grepped by the checker (unverifiable, low risk).
- [D-17] Charge exchange of trapped decay protons with residual H₂ does occur in a BL1-type trap (Caylor et al. 2025); the NIST group concludes the BL1 result "is unlikely to have been significantly affected". The paper itself estimates ~0.3% proton-loss probability for H₂ with large uncertainty because H₂ is not in thermal equilibrium (checker note). (refs: RE-19, RF-05) peer-reviewed, verified.

## 3. Contested or conflicting

- [D-70] **The central conflict.** Proton-counting beam 887.7 ± 1.2 ± 1.9 s (D-20; essentially one experiment plus unverified Sussex–ILL) vs storage: UCNτ 877.82 ± 0.22 +0.20/−0.17 s (D-27), PDG bottle average 878.4 ± 0.5 s (D-31); quoted tensions 4.6σ (Fuwa, beam vs PDG bottles), >4σ (ORNL), 4.0σ after adding J-PARC to the beams (D-26). The statistician recomputes. (refs: RE-05, RE-03, RE-10, RF-08, RC-10)
- [D-71] **Is J-PARC a clean discriminator?** For: 877.2 s sits with the bottles, 2.3σ from proton beams, and its normalisation is independent of BL1's ⁶Li deposit (D-23, D-09). Against: its own four configurations disagree (χ²/DOF = 15.8/3, cause undetermined; 50 kPa/new-SFC point 14–17 s above the others), the background from gas-scattered neutrons is 4–5× the MC prediction (D-24, D-25), and Desai (EPJA 62, 154, 2026; single author, abstract only) argues pressure-dependent detector effects plausibly explain the variation, size and sign unknown. No J-PARC reply found. (refs: RE-01, RF-02, RC-10, RC-09, RF-09)
- [D-72] **Proton-trap residual-gas / dead-layer systematic.** Serebrov et al. 2021 (PRD 103, 074010; the competing Gravitrap group): charge exchange on residual gas, "even the presence of only H₂", is "the most probable explanation of the neutron anomaly", and the dead-layer correction needs more analysis; but they did not compute a final correction and say their large low-temperature corrections "do not manifest" in the data. Byrne et al. 2019/2022 (Sussex–ILL group; abstracts only) find significant losses in Sussex–ILL data. Against: Wietfeldt et al. 2023 (PRD 107, 118501; abstract only): "None of the issues raised lead us to alter the value"; Caylor et al. 2025 measured H₂ charge exchange in the NIST trap and conclude BL1 "is unlikely to have been significantly affected" (with ~0.3% loss probability and large uncertainty per the checker); J-PARC calls charge exchange "considered negligible". Unresolved on paper; BL2 targets it. (refs: RC-18, RC-20, RC-08, RC-19, RE-19, RF-05, RE-12)
- [D-73] **Material vs magnetic bottles.** Gravitrap 881.5 ± 0.7 ± 0.6 s vs UCNτ 877.82 s; ORNL groups material 880.0 ± 0.7 s vs magnetic 877.8 ± 0.2 s, a ~3σ "second anomaly" (authors' grouping). - [D-75] **Neutron vs superallowed Vud and the Cabibbo-angle anomaly.** Superallowed Vud gives a 2.3–2.4σ first-row deficit (1.8σ with the lattice γW box); neutron Vud with bottle τ shows none (Δ_u^n = −0.00037(174)) and sits ~0.7σ above superallowed. ## 4. Constraints: theorems, bounds, no-go results

No theorem-level result applies; all constraints are SM perturbation theory with lattice/dispersive inputs, or model-dependent bounds on speculative physics.

- [D-82] **Exotic (proton-less) branch bound from λ and Vud.** BR_X < 0.27% (one-sided 95% CL; CMS 2018) and < 0.28% (95% CL; Dubbers 2019, from < 0.92% on PDG-2018 data; < 0.14% without pre-2000 λ). Assumptions: SM V−A with real couplings; λ from β-asymmetry data of 2018–2019 (gA = 1.2755(11) in CMS); superallowed Vud with 2018-era ΔR^V; bottle τ = true τn; no Fierz term. Applies equally to n → n′ and n → H ν̄ (CMS). Domain: fails if the a-coefficient λ (aSPECT) is right (D-74) or if Vud shifts. Needs recomputation with D-40–D-48 (constraints lens). (refs: RT-02, RT-12, RC-07)
- [D-83] **Fierz loophole.** A Fierz term b = 1.44% would allow the needed dark branch but is in tension with the experimental b limit; superallowed b_F ≤ 0.0033 (90% CL), aSPECT b = −0.0098 ± 0.0193. (refs: RT-12, RC-07, RQ-10, RQ-20)
- [D-84] **Dark-decay kinematic window (Fornal–Grinstein).** Assumes ⁹Be stability (937.900 MeV < M_f) and neutron decay kinematics (M_f < 939.565 MeV). n → χγ with χ dark matter (stable against χ → p e ν̄): 937.900 < m_χ < 938.783 MeV, 0.782 < E_γ < 1.664 MeV; if χ is not DM, E_γ extends to 0. n → χ e⁺e⁻: 937.900 < m_χ < 938.543 MeV, 2m_e ≤ E_ee < 1.665 MeV. n → χφ: 937.900 < m_χ + m_φ < 939.565 MeV, both stable if |m_χ − m_φ| < 938.783 MeV. (refs: RT-05, RT-06, RC-17)
- [D-85] **n → χγ excluded** as the explanation over the whole DM window E_γ = 0.782–1.664 MeV (LANL, D-60). Does not cover the non-DM case with E_γ → 0 or the invisible χφ channel. (refs: RC-05, RT-05)
- [D-86] **n → χ e⁺e⁻ excluded** as dominant for 32/37.5–644 keV (D-61); ~5% of the mass range with E_ee < 32 keV remains. (refs: RC-06, RC-06b)
- [D-87] **Invisible channels untested in the laboratory.** n → χφ and n → χχχ produce no visible particle, so the LANL γ and UCNA/PERKEO e⁺e⁻ searches do not constrain them; only λ/Vud consistency (D-82) and neutron-star bounds (D-88) apply. This is the researchers' inference from the channels searched (unverifiable as a sourced statement). (refs: RC-06b, RC-16)
- [D-88] **Neutron-star bound on dark decay.** Assumes a stable, feebly interacting dark fermion with m ≈ m_n in beta equilibrium in the star and standard nuclear EOS: M_max ≈ 0.7 M☉ (< 0.8 M☉ for Fornal–Grinstein Models 1–2) vs observed ~2 M☉ (> 1 M☉ for all well-measured stars); McKeen: particles must be heavier than 1.2 GeV or strongly self-repulsive. Model-dependent: evaded by the additions in D-77. In-medium n → χφ is "quite slow" (Harris 2026, bulk viscosity reduced at most 2–3×), a merger probe, not an exclusion. (refs: RT-15, RT-16, RC-03, RC-15, RF-13)
- [D-89] **Strong-field n → n′ (Berezhiani) excluded for Δm > 10 neV** by the SNS regeneration search at 6.6 T (p < 2.5×10⁻⁸); the model needs Δm ~ 100–300 neV to resonate at the 4.6–5 T trap field and P_nn′ ~ 10⁻² (worked example θ0 = 10⁻³, Δm = 280 neV). Separately, UCN anomalous-loss limits reinterpreted as n → n′ exclude θ0 ≳ 10⁻³ for Δm ≲ 60 neV. Assumptions: the model's field-profile and Landau–Zener treatment; whether all field-profile or attenuation variants are closed was not established. (refs: RC-02, RC-12, RT-17, RT-08)
- [D-90] **Weak-field (mirror-field B′) n–n′ anomalies excluded** in 99.98% of the B′ direction sphere at 95% CL (PSI 2026, D-63); peV-splitting limits τ_nn′ ~ 20 s (D-64, preprint). These test different parameter regions from D-89, not the NIST-trap mechanism. (refs: RC-01, RC-14, RF-07)
- [D-91] **Astrophysical n–n′ bounds are model-dependent.** Pulsar limits exclude 10⁵ yr < τ_ε < 10¹⁵ yr for mass-degenerate n–n′ (Berezhiani et al. 2021; abstract; unchecked range); NS cooling gives τ_nn′ > O(10) s only as "an indicative order of magnitude estimate", and can be relaxed via mirror millicharge (Goldman et al. 2022). (refs: RC-13)
- [D-92] **¹¹Be ceiling.** Dark-decay branching in ¹¹Be must be < 2.0×10⁻⁴ (kinematics; D-65). (refs: RC-17)
- [D-93] **Excited-neutron hypothesis (Koch–Hummel) constrained** by UCNτ holding-time (20–4000 s) and loading-time (150, 300 s) data (Blatnik et al., preprint; new 95% exclusion region, size to be read from its Fig. 4) and by Nab's Dalitz-plot paper (PRC 113, 035501, 2026; numerical limit not read).
