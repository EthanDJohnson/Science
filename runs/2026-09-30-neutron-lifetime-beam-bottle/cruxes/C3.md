## Response to the verdicts

All three verdicts return **weakened**, with narrowed forms that largely agree with one another. I accept the narrowed form. C3 survives only as a conditional bet with these conditions:
- the invisible channels n → χφ, χχχ or χA′;
- a dark sector repulsive enough to hold 2 M☉ stars;
- aSPECT's a-coefficient λ is right, so the β-asymmetry (A) group shares a ≈ 0.7% λ error, or a Fierz term b ≈ −0.016 exists;
- J-PARC 2024 reads about 2σ low.

On current data C3 is not the leading explanation. C1 fits the same data set better, by Δχ² = 15.2 with equal parameter counts (calc §2 below). The point-by-point answers follow.

### Verdict C3-0 (magnitude)

1. **J-PARC reads −1.12 s, not the +9.65 s C3 needs (2.24σ; 1.74σ inflated).** Conceded as a real ~2σ count against C3.
   - Partial mitigation only. J-PARC is a preprint whose four run conditions disagree (χ² 15.8/3) [D-23, D-24]. Its 50 kPa/new-SFC point, 884.8 ± 2.4 s, is the one closest to C3 [D-24]. Desai argues for a pressure-dependent effect of unknown sign [D-71].
   - The linear-in-pressure extrapolation to 896.9 ± 5.3 s rests on a single point [candidates C1, M-DIALECTICIAN-02], so I do not claim it as support.
   - Net: a likelihood ratio of about 4.5–12 : 1 against C3 [M-STATISTICIAN-06, M-CONSTRAINTS-11]. That stands.
2. **Each A-route λ implies a branch fraction near zero, and only aSPECT carries the gap.** Conceded as fact.
   - Rebuttal of the framing. The λ data already contain a 3.49σ internal conflict, PERKEO III against aSPECT 2024, so at least one side carries an unidentified error [calc: runs/2026-09-30-neutron-lifetime-beam-bottle/calc/crux-C3_deciding.py, §1].
   - C1 must also discard a measurement: aSPECT, 3.48σ from C1's fit (calc §2). The λ axis therefore costs every candidate something. It is not a cost unique to C3.
   - Concession on the relative size of that cost:
     - C1 drops one experiment.
     - C3 drops three: PERKEO III, PERKEO II and UCNA. Their internal χ² is 1.27/2 and their mean τ_β is 878.79 ± 0.74 s (calc §2).
     - PERKEO III alone must be off by 14.7 of its own errors from C3's needed λ [verdict C3-0], or 17.2 of them from aSPECT's value (calc §1).
3. **Joint fits: C3 gives 18.56/2 (PERKEO III) and 15.29/2 (world λ); "no branch" gives 0.10/2.** Conceded.
   - My own like-for-like comparison uses all eight inputs (storage, beam, J-PARC and five τ_β values) with two free parameters per model:
     - C1: χ² = 13.90/6 (p = 0.031);
     - C3: χ² = 29.10/6 (p = 5.8×10⁻⁵), with a best-fit Br_X of only 0.21% (calc §2).
   - In the route-choice form, each model drops the λ route it declares wrong:
     - C1: 1.65/4;
     - C3: 5.96/1 (calc §2).
   - C3 is disfavoured on every accounting.
4. **The branch carries only 16–29% of the gap on any A-route λ.** Conceded. An invisible branch of 0.2–0.3% that is not the dominant cause is not C3. That scenario would be C1 plus a sub-dominant branch.
5. **The Vud escape is closed.** Conceded without reservation [M-CONSTRAINTS-04, M-EXAMINER-07].
6. **Corrections (the "9.5–10σ" is a point distance; the fair figure is 4.26σ).** Accepted. This lowers the slate's figure but does not change the conclusion.

### Verdict C3-1 (evidence)

1. **J-PARC.** Same answer as C3-0 point 1: conceded at 2.24σ (1.74σ inflated).
2. **UCNA, PERKEO II and PERKEO III each exclude C3's λ (3.4σ, 3.0σ and 4.4σ). UCNA plus PERKEO II without PERKEO III sit 3.5σ from the beam.** Conceded. This is the strongest point against C3.
   - UCNA uses a different laboratory, a stored-UCN source and a different spectrometer. For C3 to hold, UCNA would have to share a ≈ 0.7% error with the ILL instruments.
   - I cannot name a common A-route systematic of that size, and no source in the dossier proposes one.
   - My only rebuttal is structural: the aSPECT–PERKEO conflict is real and unexplained [D-42, D-74; M-STATISTICIAN-10], and Nab exists to resolve it [D-107].
3. **Joint fits: 19.55/4 (A group), 29.2/6 (all λ), 5.92/2 (aSPECT only, p = 0.052).** Conceded. I reproduce them independently: 29.10/6 and 5.96/1 (calc §2).
4. **The other variants are dead:**
   - χγ is excluded in the dark-matter window;
   - χe⁺e⁻ is open only below 32 keV;
   - single-χ mixing gives τ_H 1.1–17× below its bound;
   - the minimal neutron-star model collapses stars to 0.69–0.70 M☉.

   Conceded. C3 now means the invisible channels with a repulsive dark sector [D-77, D-87, D-98; M-CONSTRAINTS-08, -09].
5. **Dubbers 2019: Br_X < 0.28%.** Conceded as conditional. The bound assumes the A-route λ and no Fierz term [D-82], which is exactly the premise in dispute. It adds no evidence independent of point 2.
6. **Correction: C3 needs |λ| = 1.2681 ± 0.0018, not aSPECT's 1.2668.** Accepted. C3's Nab prediction is corrected to |λ| = 1.2681 ± 0.0018. aSPECT sits 0.41σ from that value [verdict C3-1 log], so it is compatible with it.

### Verdict C3-2 (bounds)

1. **World λ, PDG-style scaling (S = 1.85): Br_X = 0.081 ± 0.145%; the needed value is 6.95σ away.** Partly rebutted.
   - A scale-factor average assumes one common true value with uniformly underestimated errors. C3's premise is that the λ data fall into two modes (A and a) and that one mode is wrong.
   - Averaging the modes and then scaling the error answers a different question. The result is dominated by PERKEO III, so it largely restates the A route.
   - The fair measure is the discrete route choice in C3-2's own point 5 and in calc §2: C3 on the a route gives χ² 5.96/1 (2.4σ), against C1's 1.65/4 on the A route.
   - I do not claim that this rescues C3. I claim only that 6.95σ overstates the tension, and that about 2.4σ conditional on the a route, plus the cost of discarding the A group, is the fair statement.
2. **J-PARC as a direct Br_X bound: −0.13 ± 0.50%.** Conceded. The correction is fair: J-PARC is a direct branch measurement under C3, not merely a "tension".
3. **Non-proton combination: 7.2–7.3σ (4.4–4.7σ with the λ error doubled).** Same answer as point 1. The number inherits the averaging choice. With the aSPECT-only route, aSPECT and J-PARC disagree at 2.3σ (1.9σ scaled). That is conceded as the residual cost of the escape.
4. **C3 pattern χ² 15.1/2 against C1's 0.39/2.** Conceded. It agrees with my Δχ² = 15.2 (calc §2).
5. **The a-route escape needs all three A experiments wrong by ≈ 0.7%.** Conceded. See C3-1 point 2.
6. **The Fierz escape C-b needs ε_T = 3.07×10⁻³, 2.36× the LHC 90% bound, or ε_S at 7.8× its bound.** Conceded as a strain.
   - The verdict itself notes that the LHC bound assumes heavy mediators (SMEFT), so a light tensor mediator is not excluded [verdict C3-2, González-Alonso et al. quote].
   - C-b therefore remains a second, weaker escape, not a primary route.
7. **Vud does not rescue C3.** Conceded.
8. **Neutron stars.** The cost is conceded: minimal χ gives 0.69–0.70 M☉ [M-CONSTRAINTS-09].
   - Rebuttal, which the verdict accepts: published constructions evade the bound [verdict C3-2: Grinstein, Kouvaris & Nielsen 2019; Bastero-Gil et al. 2024 preprint; D-77].
   - The self-scattering tension at σ/m ≈ 1.3–4 cm²/g applies only at the strong end, where the Born approximation fails, and it vanishes near m_V/g ≈ 100 MeV [M-CONSTRAINTS-10].

### What C3 still has, stated fairly

These points are not claimed as evidence *for* a branch:
- The invisible channels have no laboratory search [D-87].
- The aSPECT–PERKEO split is real and unexplained.
- On the a route C3 closes first-row unitarity at +0.1σ [M-CONSTRAINTS-04].
- C3 is the only candidate that makes a sharp, three-way, parameter-free prediction, fixed by the observed gap: LiNA ≈ 888 s, a UCNProBe in-trap β-rate against storage difference of ≈ +9.65 s, and Nab |λ| ≈ 1.2681.

What it cannot do:
- explain the material–magnetic split of 2.21 s (M-STATISTICIAN-09); it predicts 0 s, and the split is left to C7;
- survive a PERKEO-like Nab result.

## Deciding observation

The main calculation is [calc: runs/2026-09-30-neutron-lifetime-beam-bottle/calc/crux-C3_deciding.py] §3. C3's τ_β prediction is 887.97 ± 2.04 s from the beam alone, or 888.44 ± 1.72 s from the beam plus aSPECT. The storage pole is 878.32 ± 0.43 s.

- **C3 against C1 (proton-beam systematic): LiNA, the J-PARC solenoid-TPC electron-counting beam.**
  - Who and when: J-PARC (Mishima); ~1 s target; commissioned Feb 2024; background expected to fall ×50; no result date [D-102].
  - Predictions: C3 ≈ 888 s, flat in gas pressure once the background is cut. C1 ≈ 878 s.
  - At σ = 1 s:
    - 888 s sits 8.9σ from the storage pole, which would be decisive for C3;
    - 878 s sits 4.2σ from C3 on the beam error alone (5.1σ with aSPECT folded in), and 6.8σ if BL2 has meanwhile confirmed 888 ± 1 s.
  - At σ = 0.5 s the figures are 14.6σ and 4.6–8.7σ.
  - Co-decisive: Nab's a (ORNL SNS; Δλ/|λ| = 0.04%, i.e. σλ ≈ 0.00051; no a value yet [D-107]).
    - A PERKEO-like λ (1.27641) sits 4.45σ from C3's needed 1.2681 ± 0.0018 and would refute C3's λ route.
    - A value near 1.2681 sits 10.9σ from the A route that C1 relies on.
- **C3 against C2 (common storage loss).** C2 and C3 predict the same lifetime numbers:
  - BL2 ≈ 888 s;
  - LiNA ≈ 888 s;
  - UCNProBe β rate ≈ 888 s against storage ≈ 878 s;
  - an aSPECT-like Nab.

  They differ only in whether the extra storage disappearance is universal.
  - Under C3 it is a fixed particle-physics rate, 1.24×10⁻⁵ s⁻¹, the same in every trap and for every UCN energy band, wall and field setting.
  - Under C2 it is trap physics. C2's own required losses already differ by 4.76×10⁻⁶ s⁻¹ between Gravitrap and UCNτ, a 3.67 s split in τ.
  - The deciding observation is a comparison of two wall-free magnetic traps of different geometry at sub-second precision: τSPECT (Mainz/PSI, < 0.3 s; data taking in 2025 per a search summary; no τn yet [D-103]) against UCNτ(+) (0.30 s [D-27]; UCNτ+ well below 0.2 s, no date [D-105]).
  - The difference would carry σ ≈ 0.42 s. A C2-like, trap-specific split of 3.7 s would show at 8.6σ. C3 predicts 0 ± 0.42 s.
  - A second route is a storage-lifetime scan against stored-UCN energy band inside one trap: C3 predicts no dependence.
  - If the loss proves universal, C2 collapses into a bulk disappearance, which is C3 (the dialectician's point in C2's entry). Under C3 the material–magnetic split must also vanish as errors are re-assessed.
- **C3 against C5 (electron-always X⁺ branch): LiNA.**
  - C5 predicts ≈ 878 s (every decay yields an electron). C3 predicts ≈ 888 s.
  - The significances are those given for C1: 8.9σ for an 888 s reading, 4.2σ for an 878 s reading, at σ = 1 s.
  - UCNProBe separates them equally: C5 predicts a β-rate against storage difference D = 0, C3 predicts D ≈ +9.65 s.
  - Nab cannot separate them, since both need an aSPECT-like λ.
- **C3 against C6 (excited neutron n\*): UCNProBe, the in-bottle β rate against storage lifetime in one trap.**
  - Who and when: LANL, Krivos and Morris; 1–2 s target (search summary); no date [D-106].
  - Under C6 stored UCN have de-excited, so D = 0. Under C3, D = +9.65 s.
  - At σ_D = 1.5 s: a reading of 9.65 s sits 6.4σ from 0; a reading of 0 sits 3.8σ from C3, or 5.2σ if BL2 fixes the beam value to 1 s.
  - At σ_D = 1 s the figures are 9.7σ and 4.2–6.5σ.
  - Nab co-decides here too: C6 keeps the A route, so an aSPECT-like λ sits 10.9σ from C6's need.
- **C3 against C7 (null): UCNProBe's in-trap difference D.**
  - Trap-common systematics cancel within one apparatus, so the between-experiment heavy-tail scatter that C7 invokes cannot fake a 10 s class-structured difference.
  - C7 predicts β rate ≈ storage. I take D ≲ 4 s as C7's upper edge; that edge is my reading of the matrix entry "≈ storage, 878–882 s", not a number the C7 lens computed.
  - A reading of D = 9.65 s sits 3.8σ above C7's upper edge at σ_D = 1.5 s, and 5.7σ at 1 s.
  - Taken together, LiNA and BL2 both at ≈ 888 s against storage at 878 s would be the class pattern that C7 denies.
- **C3 against C8 (R-strong, a proton-detection cause): LiNA, conditional on Nab.**
  - Both predict an aSPECT-like Nab. R-strong predicts LiNA ≈ 878 s; C3 predicts ≈ 888 s. The separation is the same as for C1: 8.9σ against 4.2σ at σ = 1 s.
  - If Nab is PERKEO-like, C3 is refuted (4.45σ) and C8 collapses to C1.

**Single most informative observation for C3:** LiNA's electron-counting lifetime at ~1 s, which separates C3 from C1, C5 and C8 at once, read together with Nab's a at 0.04%. UCNProBe is the cleanest test against C6 and C7, and trap-universality (τSPECT against UCNτ) is the only test against C2.
