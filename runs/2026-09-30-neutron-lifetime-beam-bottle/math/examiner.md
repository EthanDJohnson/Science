## Refuted
- M-EXAMINER-11: the precision needed to "settle" ignores the uncertainty of the value being tested against. Corrected: n = Δ/√(σ_new² + σ_pole²). Against the UCNτ pole the lens's numbers barely change (1.96 s for 5σ, 3.28 s for 3σ). Against the BL1 pole, which is what a result reading ≈878 s must beat to exclude a proton-less decay (candidate C) or a common bottle loss (D), 3σ needs σ_new ≤ 2.41 s and 5σ is impossible at any σ_new: the ceiling is Δ/σ_BL1 = 4.40σ. Example: LiNA at 878 ± 1 s would be 3.9σ from BL1, not ≥5σ. A 5σ decision therefore also needs the proton-counting side's error below 1.98 s (BL2/BL3), or a test against a different pole. Depends: lens table A ("settle"), premise 11, the distinguishing tests of EXAMINER-A and EXAMINER-C. Their direction holds; the stated significance does not. The statistician's check (M-STATISTICIAN-14) reached the same floor independently.
- M-EXAMINER-12: 4.8×10⁻⁶ s⁻¹ is the Gravitrap vs UCNτ rate (3.68 s), not the material-class vs magnetic-class rate (2.85×10⁻⁶ s⁻¹ from the class means; 4.06×10⁻⁶ from the modern subset; 3.9×10⁻⁶ for the "≈3 s" the lens quotes next to it). Correct form: 2.9–4.1×10⁻⁶ s⁻¹ (3–4.4× smaller than the 1.27×10⁻⁵ s⁻¹ gap rate). Depends: F7, hardest question D2, EXAMINER-B. The corrected rate is smaller, so the "only" in F7 becomes stronger, and no conclusion changes.
- M-EXAMINER-10: the PERKEO II τ_β error is 1.63 s, not 1.35 s (the 0.0008 component of λ's error was dropped). No finding or candidate rests on PERKEO II.

## Formalizable
- M-EXAMINER-01: for reals x_i and weights w_i > 0 partitioned into classes c, Σ_i w_i(x_i − m)² = Σ_c Σ_{i∈c} w_i(x_i − m_c)² + Σ_c W_c(m_c − m)², where m_c and m are the weighted means and W_c = Σ_{i∈c} w_i; for two single-point classes the between term equals (x₁ − x₂)²/(σ₁² + σ₂²).
- M-EXAMINER-08: for p ∈ [0, 1] and integer k ≥ 1, p ≤ 1 − (1 − p)^k ≤ kp.
- M-EXAMINER-11: for Δ, σ, σ_P > 0, Δ/√(σ² + σ_P²) < Δ/σ_P; hence Δ/√(σ² + σ_P²) ≥ n is impossible whenever σ_P ≥ Δ/n.
- M-EXAMINER-05: for T(λ, V) = K/(V²(1 + 3λ²)) with K, V > 0, ∂ln T/∂λ = −6λ/(1 + 3λ²) and ∂ln T/∂V = −2/V.
