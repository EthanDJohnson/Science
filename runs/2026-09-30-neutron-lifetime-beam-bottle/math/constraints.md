## Refuted
- M-CONSTRAINTS-08: the n → χγ rate in the single-χ mixing model is ΔΓ = g_n²e²θ²m_n(1 − x²)³/(128π), not g_n²e²/(8π)(…). That follows from Γ = μ_T²k³/π with μ_T = θg_n e/(4m_n) and k = m_n(1 − x²)/2, and the M1 formula reproduces the Σ⁰ lifetime (7.41 vs 7.4×10⁻²⁰ s). With the correct prefactor, a 1.113% branch needs θ = 2.7×10⁻¹⁰ (m_χ = 937.993 MeV) to 6.5×10⁻¹⁰ (938.7 MeV), 4× the lens's values. McKeen's formula as quoted then gives τ_H = 0.06–0.9×10²⁹ s, 16× shorter than the lens's 0.94–14×10²⁹ s and below the quoted ≳10²⁹ s bound across the window. Affected: F11 and K7 ("marginal, right at the bound" should read "below the bound by 1.1–17×", given McKeen's formula and θ convention, which I did not check against the source). This strengthens, and does not weaken, the case against the single-χ-mixing variants of CONSTRAINTS-C with m_χ < m_H. The lens's caveat still applies: the τ_H bound partly relies on the n → χγ search. No candidate status depends on K7 alone.
## Unverified
- M-CONSTRAINTS-09 (escape threshold): the χ-repulsion value needed to restore 2 M☉ depends strongly on the neutron EOS. My stiffer toy neutron EOS (3.55 M☉ pure) reaches 1.90 M☉ already at m_V/g = 100 MeV and 2.5 M☉ at 60 MeV, where the lens's toy (2.36 M☉ pure) gives 1.62 and 1.90. So "m_V/g ≲ ~50 ± 10 MeV" is specific to the lens's EOS. A realistic EOS could place it anywhere from about 50 to 100 MeV. Consequence for F12: at m_V/g ≈ 100 MeV the Born σ/m falls to ≈0.16 cm²/g, so "strained if χ is the dominant dark matter" is less secure than stated. The free-χ collapse (the K8 kill of minimal dark decay) is unaffected.
- M-CONSTRAINTS-13 (Landau–Zener probabilities): P_trap = 0.0043 (4.6 T, BL1) and P = 0.167 (6.6 T SNS solenoid) need field profiles the analysis does not give. The 70× verdict uses only the SNS limit and the needed 1.11%, and it stands. The 10⁶× figure uses the unverified 0.167.
- M-CONSTRAINTS-14: the χ² ledger (F16) was not re-derived.
- Not checked as math because they are quoted from the literature: McKeen's τ_H formula and its θ convention, the ≳10²⁹ s bound, Yeh et al.'s ΔY_p per 5 s, and AME2020 mass excesses (entered by me from memory; they reproduce the three printed thresholds).

- M-CONSTRAINTS-05: for g, b > 0 and all s ≥ 0, g/√(s² + b²) ≤ g/b, with equality iff s = 0 (the BL1-error cap).
- M-CONSTRAINTS-03: for 0 < τ_t ≤ τ_β, Br = 1 − τ_t/τ_β lies in [0, 1), and Br ≤ (τ_β − τ_t)/τ_t.
- M-CONSTRAINTS-12: for 0 < a < b, 1/a − 1/b = (b − a)/(ab) and (b − a)/b² < 1/a − 1/b < (b − a)/a².
