## Formalizable
- M-STATISTICIAN-04: for reals x_i and positive weights w_i partitioned into classes c, Σ_i w_i(x_i − m)² = Σ_c Σ_{i∈c} w_i(x_i − m_c)² + Σ_c W_c(m_c − m)², where m_c and m are the weighted means and W_c = Σ_{i∈c} w_i.
- M-STATISTICIAN-03: for p ∈ [0, 1] and integer k ≥ 1, p ≤ 1 − (1 − p)^k ≤ kp.
- M-STATISTICIAN-13: for p ∈ (0, 1/e], 0 < −e p ln p ≤ 1, with equality only at p = 1/e.
- M-STATISTICIAN-07: for a positive-definite 2×2 covariance with correlation r, the GLS mean tends to the inverse-variance mean as r → 0.
