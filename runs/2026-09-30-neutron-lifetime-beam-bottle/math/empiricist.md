## Refuted
- M-EMPIRICIST-11: F13 quotes the PERKEO II-based τ_β error as ±1.40 s, which uses only the −0.0011 systematic side of λ. The correct propagation of 0.0008 (stat) ⊕ 0.00105 (sys) gives 880.29 ± 1.63 s. Only F13 depends on it, and its qualitative reading survives. The value came to the lens from U-01, so U-01's calc should be corrected at the source.

## Formalizable
- M-EMPIRICIST-02: for reals x_i and positive weights w_i partitioned into classes c, Σ_i w_i(x_i − m)² = Σ_c Σ_{i∈c} w_i(x_i − m_c)² + Σ_c W_c(m_c − m)², where m_c and m are the weighted means and W_c = Σ_{i∈c} w_i.
- M-EMPIRICIST-03: for W₁, W₂ > 0 and reals m₁, m₂, with M = (W₁m₁ + W₂m₂)/(W₁ + W₂), W₁(m₁ − M)² + W₂(m₂ − M)² = (m₁ − m₂)²/(1/W₁ + 1/W₂).
- M-EMPIRICIST-07: for z ≥ 0, 2∫_z^∞ (2√2)⁻¹(1 + t²/2)^(−3/2) dt = 1 − z/√(2 + z²).
- M-EMPIRICIST-10: for integer n ≥ 0, ∫₀¹ p(1−p)ⁿ dp / ∫₀¹ (1−p)ⁿ dp = 1/(n + 2).
