## Refuted
- M-MECHANIST-13: candidate [MECHANIST-G] says an SM field dependence of the decay rate is "10¹³ too small". The corrected shortfall is 1.14e-2 / (|µn|·4.6 T / Q) = 3.2×10¹⁰, or 6.4×10⁹ once the Q⁵ phase-space lever is included. That matches O1-M8's "10 orders of magnitude". Only the stated factor in [MECHANIST-G] is affected; the elimination of the SM field dependence (M8, G) is unchanged.
## Unverified
- None of the 13 rows is left unverified. These parts rest on inputs or premises this check did not establish:
  - M-MECHANIST-04 and -05: the cross sections are assumptions the lens labels as such (H⁺+H₂ 1e-20–1e-19 m²; UCN–gas 20/160/0.8 b). Only the arithmetic that uses them is verified.
  - M-MECHANIST-09 and -10: the n→n′ numbers depend on the field profile. θ0 ranges 1.0–2.2e-3 across plausible BL1 solenoid geometries, and the −9 s example at 260 neV is not reproduced in magnitude (my −19 to −25 s). The actual BL1 field map was not available.
  - M-MECHANIST-11: the premise that each SNS passage converts about as much as the NIST trap passage is not derived. A quick Landau–Zener estimate for a 6.6 T ramp that crosses 4.64 T (ε = 0.465 neV, B′ ≈ 5 T/m, v ≈ 1000 m/s) gives 2πΓ ≈ 7, which means near-adiabatic conversion per crossing. Whether that raises or cancels the regeneration signal depends on how the SNS field ramps up and down around the absorber, which I did not model. The ×5×10³ exclusion factor is therefore arithmetic on the lens's premise. The exclusion itself rests on the source (D-62, D-89).
  - M-MECHANIST-12: the neutron outer correction δR′ = 0.014902 and f = 1.6887 are literature values. They are used only to cross-check K against the D-44 calibration, and the two agree to 3×10⁻⁵.
