## Refuted
- M-DECOMPOSER-01 (minor, numerical): BL1 carries 82.3% of the inverse-variance weight in the proton-counting class, not 85%, and Sussex–ILL carries 17.7%, not "15%" (ledger L2). 85% is closer to a stat-only weighting (86.2%), which is not the combination the lens used. The class mean 887.97 ± 2.04 s and χ² 0.08/1 are correct. Dependents: the wording of F1 and ledger L2 only. No tension, candidate answer or conclusion changes, since every downstream number uses the correct mean and error.
## Unverified
- None of the 12 claims. Caveats that are left to other checks: all input values (D-20–D-30, D-40–D-45) and the lens's symmetrisation of asymmetric errors are taken as stated. The ΔR^V error (0.18 s) and the Vud error (0.58 s) are added in quadrature in M-07 to M-11 as the lens does. Because ΔR^V cancels when paired (M-07), this double-counts the RC part slightly and is conservative by less than 0.05 s.
- Interpretive notes, not refutations: (i) M-05: the J-PARC likelihood ratios 18 and 5.8 are simple point-hypothesis ratios that ignore the poles' own errors. With BL1's 2.25 s and UCNτ's 0.29 s included they are about 10 and 4.5 (11 and 4.9 with Gaussian normalisation). (ii) M-11: the Fierz τ_β error assumes no b–λ correlation. (iii) M-10: the 18σ applies to Nab's error alone, and it is 13σ against the A route's combined error.
## Formalizable
- M-DECOMPOSER-04: for reals x_i and positive weights w_i partitioned into classes c, Σ_i w_i(x_i − m)² = Σ_c Σ_{i∈c} w_i(x_i − m_c)² + Σ_c W_c(m_c − m)², where m and m_c are the weighted means and W_c = Σ_{i∈c} w_i.
- M-DECOMPOSER-01: for x₁, x₂ real and s₁, s₂ > 0, (x₁/s₁² + x₂/s₂²)/(1/s₁² + 1/s₂²) = x₁ + (x₂ − x₁)s₁²/(s₁² + s₂²), and the two-point χ² equals (x₁ − x₂)²/(s₁² + s₂²).
- M-DECOMPOSER-07: for positive K₀, K_n, Ft, λ and ΔR > −1, the function τ(ΔR) = K_n / ((K₀/(Ft(1+ΔR)))(1+3λ²)(1+ΔR)) is constant in ΔR.
- M-DECOMPOSER-10: for Δ, n, s_f, d > 0 with Δ/n > s_f, Δ/√(d²σ² + s_f²) ≥ n ⇔ 0 ≤ σ ≤ √((Δ/n)² − s_f²)/d.
