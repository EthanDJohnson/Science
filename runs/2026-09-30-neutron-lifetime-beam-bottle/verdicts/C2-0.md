# Verdict on C2 (refuter 0, angle: magnitude)
claim: An unidentified loss of about 1.2×10⁻⁵ s⁻¹ common to material and magnetic UCN traps shortens every storage result; the proton-beam value near 888 s is the true lifetime.
status: final
verdict: weakened
basis: calculation
narrowed: The storage results can be short and 888 s true only if some so-far unnamed disappearance channel, exactly constant in time, acts at about 1.3×10⁻⁵ s⁻¹ in magnetic traps and about 1.0×10⁻⁵ s⁻¹ in material traps. It would lie outside every loss line in UCNτ's budget, which together carry at most 4% of it, and J-PARC would also need its own separate error of about −10.8 s.

## Strongest attack
The attack is on size. C2 is not refuted outright, for two reasons. A loss at a constant rate cannot be told apart from β decay in a single-exponential storage fit (M-MECHANIST-06, verified). And the needed rate, about 1.2×10⁻⁵ s⁻¹, is within about 1σ of every storage class once the correlated BL1 error (±2.9×10⁻⁶ s⁻¹) is counted. So an effect nobody has named can always be made to fit. Three size failures still narrow the core claim sharply.

1. **Every loss channel anyone has named fails, by factors of 56 to 145, in the one trap that dominates storage.** In UCNτ, a trap with no walls, the only processes that remove UCN at a constant rate are depolarisation and residual-gas upscattering. Escape of marginally trapped or heated UCN depends on time, and UCNτ bounds it in situ. C2 needs a correction of +10.15 s (proton-beam mean) or +9.88 s (BL1).
   - Against depolarisation, the gap is 145× its budget line.
   - Against uncleaned UCN it is 92×, and against heated UCN 127×.
   - Against the gas correction it is 56× even at 3σ above the applied value.
   - All loss-type lines summed linearly at their limits give 0.44 s, which is **4.3% of the gap**. The gap is 50.7× UCNτ's whole +0.20 s systematic budget.
   - In rates: the needed 1.302×10⁻⁵ s⁻¹ is 130× the depolarisation bound. It is 91× the gas rate UCNτ measured run by run, which is about 600σ in pressure given UCNτ's 15% pressure uncertainty, if the cross sections are held fixed.

   The brief requires a systematic explanation to name "the specific effect" and "the size it would need against the published budget". None of the sub-hypotheses C2 names (B-mag, B-gas, B-mat) reaches even 2% of the gap in UCNτ. B-mat cannot act at all in a trap with no walls. That leaves B-pop, or a bulk disappearance with no mechanism behind it. The brief (line 105 of the dialectician's lens) counts a bulk disappearance as new physics, not a systematic.
2. **The loss is not the same in every trap.** On independent errors, the rates the classes need are:
   - material bottles: 9.81 ± 0.63 ×10⁻⁶ s⁻¹;
   - magnetic traps: 1.2665 ± 0.037 ×10⁻⁵ s⁻¹.

   Experiment by experiment, the needed rate runs from 6.6×10⁻⁶ s⁻¹ (Steyerl 2012) to 1.27×10⁻⁵ s⁻¹ (UCNτ), a factor of 1.9. A single common rate fits the seven storage results with χ² = 23.46 for 6 degrees of freedom (p = 0.0028). The error bars in the table below compare a mechanism with rates of 1.0×10⁻⁵ and 1.27×10⁻⁵ s⁻¹ against one at a common 1.2×10⁻⁵ s⁻¹. To be fair, this scatter does not count against C2 alone: a common τ (the C1 analogue) gives χ² = 23.33 for 6. So this point narrows the word "common" but does not separate C2 from C1.
3. **C2's agent contributes nothing to the electron-counting beam.** J-PARC stores no UCN. It sits −10.77 s from 888 s, and C2's loss supplies 0% of that, leaving a 2.24σ residual that needs a separate error. In a two-parameter fit to the four classes (proton beam, J-PARC, material, magnetic), C2 reaches χ² = 13.70 against 8.72 for the C1 structure. That is Δχ² = 4.98, a likelihood ratio of 12:1 against C2, and J-PARC pulls C2's own best-fit τ down to 886.0 s. This reproduces M-STATISTICIAN-06 and M-CONSTRAINTS-11 independently.

C2 therefore needs a much larger hidden effect than C1 does. C1 needs a hidden effect about 4.4× BL1's quoted uncertainty (M-CONSTRAINTS-12). C2 needs one about 50× UCNτ's systematic budget, and also about 10× Gravitrap's budget in a second technique that works quite differently. The two errors would have to point the same way, and J-PARC would still need an unrelated error on top.

## Corrections to details
- "About 1.2×10⁻⁵ s⁻¹ common": this holds only as an average across the storage experiments (the fitted 1.19×10⁻⁵ s⁻¹). The classes need 0.98×10⁻⁵ s⁻¹ (material) and 1.27×10⁻⁵ s⁻¹ (magnetic). The difference is (2.86 ± 0.73)×10⁻⁶ s⁻¹: 3.9σ on unscaled independent errors, or 2.95σ once the material class's scatter is scaled (M-STATISTICIAN-09). So the mechanism would have to be about 23% weaker in material traps. The candidate says 38% weaker, but that figure uses Gravitrap alone.
- The candidate gives the gas shortfall as "≈ 90×". That matches the applied correction (91×). Measured against the budget line at 3σ it is 56×, and against the pressure uncertainty it is about 600σ.
- C2's best-fit τ, once J-PARC is included, is 886.0 s, not 888 s.

## What survives and remaining doubt
Two things survive:
- **Degeneracy.** A strictly constant-rate disappearance cannot be distinguished from decay by any single storage experiment, and no published external audit of UCNτ's loss budget exists.
- **Beam-side support.** The a-route λ (aSPECT) supports the beam value.

What does not survive is C2 read as an experimental systematic in the brief's sense, meaning a named effect of the needed size. Every named effect falls short by one to two orders of magnitude in UCNτ. What remains is a constant loss with no mechanism behind it and trap-dependent strength, together with a separate J-PARC error. That is a much narrower claim, and it sits close to relabelled new physics, as the dialectician argues.

It is weakened rather than refuted because the J-PARC tension is only 2.2σ. The 12:1 likelihood ratio is suggestive, not decisive, and falls to 4.5–5:1 with inflated errors. And constant-rate degeneracy means the budgets cannot exclude, in principle, a channel that was never modelled. UCNProBe's in-trap β rate would settle it: C2 predicts β-rate ≈ 888 s against storage ≈ 878 s in the same trap.
