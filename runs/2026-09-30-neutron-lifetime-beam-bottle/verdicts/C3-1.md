# Verdict on C3 (refuter 1, angle: evidence)
claim: A proton-less, electron-less dark-decay branch of about 1.1% (invisible n → χφ, χχχ or χA′) makes beams measure τ_β while bottles measure the true total lifetime (speculative).
status: final
verdict: weakened
basis: cited-evidence
narrowed: A ~1.1% invisible dark branch survives only in its non-minimal form (invisible χφ/χχχ/χA′ with enough dark repulsion to hold 2 M☉ stars), and only if the three electron-asymmetry λ experiments (PERKEO II, PERKEO III, UCNA) are wrong together by ≈ 0.7% in λ (aSPECT's a-route λ right) or a Fierz term b ≈ −0.016 exists, and J-PARC 2024 reads ~2σ low; on current evidence it is disfavoured, not excluded.

Units: SI (lifetimes in s); λ, b and branching ratios dimensionless; masses in M☉.

## Strongest attack
C3's claim that the gap *is* a dark branch runs into two independent experimental classes that measure τ_β without counting protons. Both disagree with the ≈ 888 s that C3 requires.

1. **Electron-counting beam.** J-PARC counts decay electrons, so under C3 it has to read τ_β ≈ 888 s (C3's own shift-by-class line: "proton beam and J-PARC both +9.7 s"). The collaboration reports the opposite: [new: Fuwa et al., arXiv:2412.19519 (preprint), ACCESS abstract, "This result is consistent with bottle method measurements but exhibits a 2.3$\sigma$ tension with the average value obtained from the proton-detection-based beam method."] The J-PARC value sits 2.24σ below C3's prediction [calc: runs/2026-09-30-neutron-lifetime-beam-bottle/calc/falsifier-C3-1_lambda_experiments.py]. That falls to 1.74σ if J-PARC's internal scatter is used to inflate its error [M-CONSTRAINTS-11 verified]. So this line counts against C3 but is not decisive.

2. **The SM route through λ is not a single-experiment problem.** The candidate calls the λ route its "weakest link" and escapes it by assuming aSPECT's a-coefficient λ is right. This escape is much costlier than the slate suggests. C3 needs |λ| = 1.26812 ± 0.00179 (τ_β equal to the proton-beam value, 887.97 ± 2.04 s). Each electron-detecting β-asymmetry experiment excludes that value on its own [calc: runs/2026-09-30-neutron-lifetime-beam-bottle/calc/falsifier-C3-1_lambda_experiments.py]:
   - **PERKEO III** (ILL, pulsed cold beam): 4.4σ.
   - **UCNA** (LANL, stored UCN, a different neutron source and a different spectrometer): 3.4σ.
   - **PERKEO II** (ILL, continuous beam): 3.0σ.
   - **UCNA + PERKEO II without PERKEO III**, which is the experiment aSPECT is usually set against: τ_β = 879.46 ± 1.34 s, 3.5σ below the beam, with χ² 0.91/1 between them.

   The A-route experiments therefore agree with one another, and C3 needs all of them to share a ≈ 0.7% λ error of the same sign. The a-coefficient family is not unanimous either. aCORN, the other a-type measurement, gives |λ| 1.8σ *above* the needed value. C3's escape therefore rests on aSPECT alone, which gives Br_X = 1.26 ± 0.35%.

3. **The joint fit.** In a two-parameter C3 world (τn from storage; τ_β from the proton beam, J-PARC and λ):
   - with the A group: χ² = 19.55/4, p = 6.1×10⁻⁴, and the best-fit branch is only 0.17%, not 1.1%;
   - with all five λ inputs: χ² = 29.2/6, p = 5.6×10⁻⁵, best-fit Br_X 0.21%;
   - only when the A group is dropped and aSPECT kept does C3 fit acceptably: χ² 5.92/2, p = 0.052, Br_X 0.97% [calc: runs/2026-09-30-neutron-lifetime-beam-bottle/calc/falsifier-C3-1_lambda_experiments.py].

   The published exclusion rests on exactly this λ route: [new: Dubbers et al., PLB 791, 6 (2019), arXiv:1812.00626, ACCESS full-text, "Our analysis, based on all neutron decay dat a, excludes such an explanation, cf. Fig. 2, and lowers the bound on the dark branching ratio from BRX < 0.92% (95% C.L.), based on the data of PDG -2018, to BR X < 0.28%"].

4. **The variants the slate still lists are dead.** In the minimal model (free χ), neutron stars collapse to M_max ≈ 0.69–0.70 M☉ [M-CONSTRAINTS-09 verified]. The χγ and χe⁺e⁻ channels are excluded over almost the whole window [D-85, D-86]. Single-χ mixing gives τ_H below its bound by 1.1–17× [M-CONSTRAINTS-08 refuted the lens in the direction that hurts C3]. What remains is the invisible channels with an added repulsive dark sector [D-77, D-98].

Why this is not a refutation:
- The aSPECT–PERKEO III disagreement is a genuine, unresolved 3.5σ conflict in the data [M-STATISTICIAN-10; new: Broussard et al. 2025, arXiv:2511.16678, ACCESS full-text via frontier RF-11, "disagree by about 3σ"].
- Beck et al.'s combined fit with a Fierz term gives τ_β = 893.7 ± 4.2 s, 1.26σ from BL1 [M-DECOMPOSER-11 verified; new: Beck et al. 2024, arXiv:2308.16170, ACCESS abstract, "we derive a limit of b = -0.0098(193)"]. That opens a second escape (C-b), 1.6σ from Saul 2020's b.
- J-PARC is a preprint with an internal χ² of 15.8/3 [D-23, D-24].
- No laboratory search reaches the invisible channels [D-87].

The evidence does not exclude the core. It confines it to a narrow corner that requires several independent experiments to be wrong at once.

## Size against the discrepancy   (anomaly questions)
Reference gap: 9.65 ± 2.08 s (1.09% of τ, 1.24×10⁻⁵ s⁻¹).
- **Proton beam:** C3 gives +9.7 s (long). It carries the full gap by construction ✓.
- **J-PARC:** C3 predicts +9.7 s over storage, i.e. ≈ 888 s. Observed 877.2 s, 2.24σ against (1.74σ scaled).
- **Bottles, material and magnetic:** 0 shift. C3 cannot produce the 2.2 s material–magnetic split.
- **SM τ_β:**
  - C3 needs it to equal the beam value. The A group gives 878.79 ± 0.74 s (4.23σ against).
  - Only aSPECT (889.48 ± 3.14 s) complies.
  - Even the A route without PERKEO III sits at 3.49σ.
  - The C3 fit with the A group wants Br_X = 0.17%, about 15% of the needed branch.
- **Space-based measurements:** uninformative at ±14 s.

C3 carries the whole gap in the proton beam, but only by being inconsistent with the two other ways of measuring τ_β.

## Corrections to details
- "A route Br_X = 0.077 ± 0.106%" uses UCNτ alone. Against the all-storage 878.32 s it is 0.020 ± 0.112% (calc above). This makes no material difference.
- "Nab gives |λ| ≈ 1.2668". That is aSPECT's value. C3's own requirement (τ_β = 887.97 s) is |λ| = 1.2681 ± 0.0018.
- The slate lists the escape as "the a-coefficient (aSPECT) λ is right". This understates what C3 must give up. The escape needs UCNA (LANL, UCN) as well as PERKEO II and III (ILL) to be wrong, each at ≥ 3σ, and aCORN, the other a-type result, disagrees with the needed λ at 1.8σ.

## What survives and remaining doubt
What survives is a narrow, non-minimal invisible branch that stands or falls with aSPECT's λ, or with a Fierz term of b ≈ −0.016. Its best joint fit (p ≈ 0.05) needs the whole A group discarded. The live doubts:
- The λ conflict is real and unexplained. If Nab finds an aSPECT-like λ, the λ-route objection above evaporates.
- J-PARC is an unpublished preprint with internal scatter, so its 2.2σ could shrink.
- PERKEO II and PERKEO III come from one group with related methods, so their errors may be partly correlated. That is why the UCNA-only 3.3σ, from an independent lab, a UCN source and its own spectrometer, is the cleanest single figure.
- The in-medium dark-decay and neutron-star escapes are model-dependent and were not re-examined here.

The deciding measurements are the ones C3 names:
- **LiNA** at ~1 s. A reading of ≈ 878 s would add a second, independent electron-counting contradiction.
- **Nab's a** at 0.04%.
- **UCNProBe.**
