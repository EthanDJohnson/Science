# Verdict on C2 (refuter 2, angle: bounds)
claim: An unidentified loss of about 1.2×10⁻⁵ s⁻¹ common to material and magnetic UCN traps shortens every storage result; the proton-beam value near 888 s is the true lifetime.
status: final
verdict: weakened
basis: calculation
narrowed: A storage-only loss (material ≈ 1.0×10⁻⁵ s⁻¹, magnetic ≈ 1.3×10⁻⁵ s⁻¹) with τn ≈ 888 s stays viable only if three things all hold: the a-coefficient λ (aSPECT, |λ| ≈ 1.268) is right, PERKEO III, UCNA and PERKEO II share a bias of about 0.6%, and J-PARC 2024 carries its own error of about −10.5 s. With the A-route or PDG-averaged λ, the storage-free data put 888 s 3.3–4.2σ away.

## Strongest attack
C2 says the storage results are the biased ones. So it must agree with every determination of τ that never stores a UCN. Two of these exist:
- the J-PARC electron-counting beam;
- the SM master formula τ_β(λ, Vud).

If the storage results are dropped altogether, these two alone locate τ. They exclude the proton-beam value unless λ comes from the proton-recoil a coefficient. [calc: runs/2026-09-30-neutron-lifetime-beam-bottle/calc/falsifier-C2-2_bounds.py]

  J-PARC's own inconsistency matters little. Inflating its statistical error by S = √(15.8/3) leaves the proton beam 4.22σ from the A route and 3.31σ from the PDG λ route. The SM routes alone give 4.20σ (A route) and 3.19σ (PDG λ).
- **Rival worlds fitted to the same storage-free data.**
  - *C2 world:* one τ fitted to {proton beam, J-PARC, SM}, with storage left free.
  - *C1 world:* one τ fitted to {storage, J-PARC, SM}, with the beam left free.

  | a route | 5.15 | — | 9.10 | 0.14 (C2 favoured) |

  The whole case therefore turns on λ.
- **What λ C2 needs.** τ_β = 887.97 s needs |λ| = 1.26821. That is 14.7σ from PERKEO III on PERKEO III's own λ error, and PERKEO III agrees with UCNA and PERKEO II. With PERKEO III's λ, BL1 gives a first-row sum 4.6σ below unitarity [M-CONSTRAINTS-04, verified]. So C2 is not refuted, because the aSPECT λ is a real, published, peer-reviewed measurement. But C2 does need the three β-asymmetry experiments to share an unexplained ≈ 0.6% error, on top of its own unidentified storage loss and an independent J-PARC artefact of ≈ −10.5 s [M-MECHANIST-07, M-DIALECTICIAN-07, verified]. That makes three independent failures in three unrelated techniques.
- **Physical bound on a "common" agent.**
  - The loss channels open in a wall-free magneto-gravitational trap are depolarisation (it causes loss only where just one spin state is trapped), marginally trapped UCN and residual gas.
  - The loss channels open in a material bottle are wall absorption and upscattering, and gas.
  - The only channel common to both is residual-gas upscattering. UCNτ measured it at +0.11 ± 0.06 s, ≈ 90× short of what is needed [M-MECHANIST-06, verified].
  - Any other agent acting identically on walled and wall-free traps is a bulk disappearance of the neutron. That would make 878 s the true total lifetime and 888 s only τ_β: it is C3, and it contradicts C2's own clause that "888 s is the true lifetime".

  C2 is therefore coherent only as two or more separate agents whose rates happen to be similar. It is not one common loss.

## What survives and remaining doubt
The core survives only through the a route. If aSPECT's λ is right and the three A-coefficient experiments are wrong, then:
- the SM prediction (887.2–889.6 s) sits with the proton beam;
- J-PARC is the lone 2.2σ dissenter (1.7σ once its internal scatter is inflated);
- C2 is even mildly favoured over C1 on the storage-free data (profile LR 0.14 against C1).

So I cannot call C2 refuted. The λ tension (≈ 3.5σ) is unresolved, and there is no published external critique of UCNτ's marginal-UCN budget.

C2 is still weaker than its heading says:
- It needs four independent failures: an unidentified storage loss (≈ 50× UCNτ's systematic), a coincidentally similar material-bottle loss, a shared ≈ 0.6% bias in PERKEO III, UCNA and PERKEO II, and a −10.5 s J-PARC artefact.
- A genuinely common agent turns into C3 (bulk disappearance), which contradicts "888 s is the true lifetime".

Nab's λ at 0.04% decides the bound. An A-like |λ| ≈ 1.276 would push C2 to ≥ 4σ on storage-free data alone. An aSPECT-like |λ| ≈ 1.268 would rescue it. UCNProBe is the direct test.
