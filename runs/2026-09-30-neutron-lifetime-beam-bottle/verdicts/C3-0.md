# Verdict on C3 (refuter 0, angle: magnitude)
claim: A proton-less, electron-less dark-decay branch of about 1.1% (invisible n → χφ, χχχ or χA′) makes beams measure τ_β while bottles measure the true total lifetime (speculative).
status: final
verdict: weakened
basis: calculation
narrowed: An invisible branch can carry the whole ~1.1% gap only if the a-coefficient λ (aSPECT) is right and the A-coefficient λ values (PERKEO III, UCNA, PERKEO II) are all wrong, with J-PARC's electron count about 2σ low; with the world λ it can carry at most about 30% of the gap (Br_X ≈ 0.3%).

Units: SI (lifetimes in s); λ = |gA/gV|, Vud and Br_X dimensionless. Vud = 0.97361(32) with the GS2023 inner radiative correction (a consistent pairing, U-01).

## Strongest attack
C3 fits the proton-beam class exactly by construction: one free number, Br_X = 1 − 878.32/887.97 = 1.087% (1.113% for BL1 against UCNτ). The magnitude test asks whether that same branch size also fits the other two classes that measure τ_β under C3. The first is the electron-counting beam (J-PARC 2024). The second is the Standard-Model τ_β from λ and Vud. An invisible decay yields neither a proton nor an electron, so both classes must read ≈ 888 s, 9.65 s above storage. Neither does.

1. **J-PARC (electron counting).** C3 predicts a shift of +9.65 s against storage. J-PARC observes −1.12 s. That is −0.12 ± 0.45 of the shift C3 needs, 2.24σ below C3's τ_β (1.74σ with J-PARC's error inflated for its internal χ²/DOF = 15.8/3, M-CONSTRAINTS-11).
2. **SM τ_β from λ.** The branch each λ measurement implies, as a fraction of the gap:
   - PERKEO III: +0.02, so Br_X = 0.020 ± 0.112%;
   - UCNA 2018: −0.07;
   - PERKEO II: +0.21;
   - aCORN: −0.36;
   - aSPECT 2024: +1.17, the only measurement that carries the gap.

   The |λ| C3 needs is 1.2682 ± 0.0018. PERKEO III's 1.27641(56) is 14.7 times its own error away from that value, and 4.3σ once the needed value's own uncertainty is included.
3. **Joint fit of the τ_β class under C3** (τ_β free, fitted to the proton beam, J-PARC and the SM τ_β from λ):
   - with PERKEO III: χ² = 18.56/2, 3.9σ;
   - with the S-scaled world λ: χ² = 15.29/2, 3.5σ;
   - with aSPECT: χ² = 5.90/2, 1.9σ.

   On the same data, "no branch" (τ_β = τn, proton beam biased) gives χ² = 0.10/2 (PERKEO III) and 0.38/2 (world λ).
4. **How much of the gap can the branch carry?** If Br_X is fitted to every τ_β-class datum instead of the proton beam alone, the best fit is Br_X = 0.18% with PERKEO III and 0.31% with the world λ. That is 16–29% of the gap (17–29% with J-PARC inflated). The proton beam is then still 3.1–3.7σ high, so a proton-beam systematic, C1's mechanism, would have to carry most of the gap. An invisible branch that is not the dominant cause is not C3.
5. **The Vud escape is closed.** Holding PERKEO III's λ and asking Vud to absorb the gap makes the first-row CKM sum 0.98842(251), −4.6σ (M-CONSTRAINTS-04, verified).

The only magnitude-consistent world for C3 is the one the candidate itself names as its weakest link: the a-coefficient (aSPECT) λ is right and the A-coefficient λ values are wrong. That is a live, unresolved ~3σ dispute that Nab is built to settle. I therefore narrow C3 and do not refute it.

## Corrections to details
- The slate's "needed value 9.5–10σ away" (A route, PERKEO III) treats the needed Br_X = 1.087% as exact. Including the proton beam's ±2.04 s, the uncertainty of the needed value itself, gives 4.26σ between PERKEO III's τ_β and the proton beam under C3 (calc §9; M-STATISTICIAN-11 lists 4.25). The "9.5σ" is a distance from a point value, not the tension between the data. The conclusion against the A route still holds.
- Likewise the λ-space Δχ² = 252.8 in calc §4 fixes λ at the needed value. Use the joint fits in calc §5, 3.5–3.9σ, as the fair measure.

## What survives and remaining doubt
- **What survives:** an invisible ~1.1% branch stays numerically consistent (χ² = 5.9/2, 1.9σ; 3.8/2 with J-PARC's error inflated) if the a-coefficient λ from aSPECT is right and PERKEO III, UCNA and PERKEO II are all biased low in τ_β by ~9 s-equivalent. That requires PERKEO III's λ to be off by ≈ 15 of its own quoted errors.
- **Where C3 fails the test:** on the world λ or any A-coefficient λ it can carry only 16–29% of the gap, and it is then not the dominant cause.
- **Doubts:**
  - J-PARC 2024 is a preprint with unexplained scatter between run conditions [D-24, D-71], so it alone weighs only ~2σ.
  - The λ dispute between aSPECT and PERKEO III is unresolved. Nab's a at 0.04% (or the Fierz sub-hypothesis C-b, b ≈ −0.016) decides whether the narrowed core lives.
  - I did not assess the neutron-star escape, which is a separate bounds question. Minimal free χ collapses neutron stars to ≈ 0.7 M☉ (M-CONSTRAINTS-09, verified), so C3 also needs the χ–χ repulsion sub-hypothesis.
