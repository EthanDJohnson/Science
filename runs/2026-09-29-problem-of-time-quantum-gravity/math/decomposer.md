## Refuted
- M-DECOMPOSER-10: Finding 4 says time dilation sourced by a superposed geometry is "20–30 orders of magnitude below clock resolution (Q-05: 7.6e-21)". The lens's own shifts put it 17.9 orders below (QGEM, 9.2e-39; 17.6–18.1 using exact rather than dipole distances) and 10.0 orders below (1 g delocalised by 1 µm at 1 mm, 7.4e-31). The corrected statement is "10–18 orders of magnitude below". Only the coupling λω ≈ 2.3e-60 lies further down, about 40 orders below. The qualitative conclusion is unaffected: clock-rate measurements cannot reach it. This holds for Finding 4, Q9.4 and the distinguishing test in DECOMPOSER-A (new physics only when the source is superposed), but any text that repeats "20–30 orders" should be corrected.

## Unverified
- M-DECOMPOSER-06: the specific Part 2 numbers (non-Hermiticity 0.19–0.30 rad/s, generator variation 2.0 rad/s, conditional norm 0.971–1.029, generator = H_AS to 3e-15) depend on model data the analysis does not state: A's dimension and Hamiltonian, H_S, X_A, how A's clock states are built, and the initial state. An independent model of the same type (M-DECOMPOSER-04/05) reproduces the qualitative result and the same order of magnitude (0.24–0.31 rad/s, 1.16 rad/s, 0.981–1.012 at g = 0.3 rad/s), but not the values. How big the numbers are is model-dependent and should not be quoted as general.

## Formalizable
- M-DECOMPOSER-02: let d ≥ 2, let c_k be real with c_k − c_0 = k (k = 0..d−1), and let t_j = 2πj/d. The vectors (1/√d)Σ_k e^{−i c_k t_j}|k⟩ are orthonormal. Hence for any Ψ in C^d ⊗ C^n and any projectors Π_a, Π_b, (P_{t_i}⊗Π_b)(P_{t_j}⊗Π_a)Ψ = 0 for i ≠ j.
- M-DECOMPOSER-04: let H_B = diag(b_1..b_m) on C^m, let H be Hermitian on C^n, and let Ψ ∈ ker(H_B⊗1 + 1⊗H). Then for every real t, (⟨t|⊗1)Ψ = e^{−iHt}(⟨0|⊗1)Ψ, where ⟨t| = m^{−1/2}Σ_k e^{+i b_k t}⟨k|, and its norm does not depend on t.
- M-DECOMPOSER-05 (existence): there exist a finite-dimensional A⊗B⊗S, a Hermitian J = H_A + H_B + H_S + g V_{AS} with g ≠ 0, and Ψ ∈ ker J such that the norm of (⟨t|_A⊗1)Ψ is non-constant in t (witness: the explicit 3×6×2 model in M-DECOMPOSER-04.py).
- M-DECOMPOSER-08: for real θ, abs((1 + e^{iθ})/2) = abs(cos(θ/2)), and 1 − cos(θ/2) ≤ θ²/8.
- M-DECOMPOSER-09: for 0 < x < d, x/d² − x²/d³ ≤ 1/d − 1/(d+x) ≤ x/d², and 1/(d−x) − 1/d ≥ x/d².
