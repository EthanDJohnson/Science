## Unverified
None among the listed claims. Not taken as math claims:
- The Agullo–Ashtekar, Varadarajan and Torre–Varadarajan statements (F6, F8), which are literature quotes.
- The interpretive steps: that the F4 identity shows GPP and HSL are "one dynamics", and C's prediction (a) by analogy.

Two caveats for downstream use:
- F4 is a pure rewriting. Any exponent of the form aω² equals σ²ω²/2 for σ = √(2a), and the factor √3 is fixed by that match. Its physical content therefore rests on whether GPP's decoherence really arises as Gaussian clock-reading error, which the lens itself did not confirm.
- F1's "\|⟨t1\|t2⟩\|²/d²" presumes unnormalised clock states (norm² = d). With normalised states the same quantity is \|⟨t1\|t2⟩\|². The b ≠ a part vanishes for any H_S, not only for a spin flip, because P_b P_a = δ_ab P_a.
## Formalizable
- M-DIALECTICIAN-01: for d ≥ 2 and real x not in 2πℤ, \|Σ_{j=0}^{d−1} e^{ijx}\|² = sin²(dx/2)/sin²(x/2). For orthogonal projectors P_a, P_b on ℂ² and vectors u, v ∈ ℂ^d, (\|v⟩⟨v\|⊗P_b)(\|u⟩⟨u\|⊗P_a) = ⟨v\|u⟩ \|v⟩⟨u\| ⊗ δ_ab P_a.
- M-DIALECTICIAN-03: for σ > 0 and real Δ, ∫_ℝ (2πσ²)^(−1/2) e^(−x²/(2σ²)) e^(−iΔx) dx = e^(−σ²Δ²/2).
- M-DIALECTICIAN-04: for t, T, ω > 0, 3(t(T/t)^(1/3))²ω²/2 = (3/2)t^(4/3)T^(2/3)ω².
- M-DIALECTICIAN-06: max_{x∈ℝ}(2cos x − cos 2x) = 3/2, attained at x = ±π/3 + 2πℤ. If X, Y are iid N(0, σ²), then E[cos(a + Ω(X − Y))] = e^(−Ω²σ²)cos a. Hence 1.5e^(−y²) > 1 ⇔ y² < ln(3/2).
- M-DIALECTICIAN-07: for a Hermitian matrix H and real λ with 1 ∉ λ·spec(H), H(1 − λH)^(−1) is Hermitian and equals Σ_n λⁿH^(n+1) for \|λ\|‖H‖ < 1. The scalar equation ε + E − λεE = 0 has the unique solution ε = −E/(1 − λE).
