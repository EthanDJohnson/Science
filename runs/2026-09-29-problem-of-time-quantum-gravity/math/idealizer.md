## Refuted
- M-IDEALIZER-15: F12's parenthetical says the dossier's 0.23/0.57 rad [Q-10] comes from G m²/d with d = 450 µm. That formula gives 0.141 rad (1 s) and 0.352 rad (2.5 s), not 0.23/0.57. The dossier numbers are reproduced by Gm²t(1/(d−Δx) − 1/(d+Δx))/ħ with d = 450 µm and Δx = 250 µm, that is 1/(200 µm) − 1/(700 µm): 0.226 and 0.565 rad. Corrected wording: "the dossier's 0.23/0.57 rad uses the near-minus-far separations 200 µm and 700 µm; F12 uses 200 µm and 450 µm; same order." Nothing else changes. F12's own numbers (0.176/0.440 rad) are verified (M-IDEALIZER-12), and IDEALIZER-C and IDEALIZER-F rest on the order of magnitude, which holds.
## Unverified
- M-IDEALIZER-09 (sub-claim only): the WKB-vs-Airy error numbers 4.5e-2, 1.1e-2 and 3.3e-3 at 1, 3 and 10 widths. The lens does not define the error measure. Pointwise relative error gives 0.046, 0.0126 and 0.0257; the leading asymptotic amplitude correction 5/(72ζ) gives 0.104, 0.020 and 0.0033. Neither measure reproduces all three numbers. The load-bearing parts of F9 (ℓ = (2M²g)^(−1/3), Δt ∝ M^(−1/3)) are verified.
- Coefficient-level details that the checks show are not universal (the row verdicts stand): the 0.45 g/ω_clock non-unitarity coefficient and the 0.14g–0.6g detuning in F4 depend on the random V (0.34–0.93 and 0.04g–0.16g in M-IDEALIZER-04). Only the linear scaling is robust. F10's clock dependence rests on the modelling assumption that the chosen clock variable absorbs all of E_S. The arithmetic is verified, but that assumption is not derived from a full two-variable BO expansion.
- Not checked: m_P = 2.65e19 GeV in F13 is a literature value from [Q-17]. It is 2.17× the standard Planck energy 1.22e19 GeV (M-IDEALIZER-13), so the source check should confirm the convention.
## Formalizable
- M-IDEALIZER-01: for finite-dimensional Hermitian H_C = diag(E_n) and H_S, and any Ψ ∈ ker(H_C⊗1 + 1⊗H_S), the map t ↦ Σ_n e^{iE_n t}(⟨n|⊗1)Ψ equals e^{−iH_S t} applied to its t = 0 value.
- M-IDEALIZER-02 (naive part): for orthogonal projectors P₀P₁ = 0 and any operators C₁, C₂, (C₁⊗P₀)(C₂⊗P₁)(C₁⊗P₀) = 0.
- M-IDEALIZER-03: ker(H_C⊗1 + 1⊗H_S − λH_C⊗H_S) for commuting diagonal H_C, H_S consists of pairs (c, s) with c = −s/(1−λs), λs ≠ 1.
- M-IDEALIZER-05: for |t⟩ = d^{−1/2}Σ_{n=0}^{d−1} e^{−int}|n⟩, |⟨0|t⟩| = |sin(dt/2)/(d sin(t/2))|, and (d/2π)∫₀^{2π}|t⟩⟨t|dt = 1_d.
- M-IDEALIZER-06: for a positive diagonal density matrix ρ = diag(p_n) and H = diag(E_n) with distinct E_n, −ln ρ = βH + α·1 for some real α, β iff ρ = e^{−βH}/Tr e^{−βH}. For n = 2 this always holds.
- M-IDEALIZER-07: expansion of −v(√(2M(Me₀−E)) − M√(2e₀)) in 1/M equals E + E²/(4e₀M) + O(M⁻²).
- M-IDEALIZER-09: Ai(x(2M²g)^{1/3}) solves ψ'' = 2M²gxψ.
- M-IDEALIZER-14: for a random variable c ~ N(0, σ²), |E[e^{iλcΔt}]| = exp(−(λΔσt)²/2).
