## Unverified
No claim is unverified as a whole. Some parts of verified claims could not be re-derived:
- M-CONSTRAINTS-02 (part): the turning-point counts (φ 17, H 35; SEC violated at 18 of 40 points) depend on initial data F2 does not state. My own data give the same qualitative pattern (φ 17 and 18 turns, H 38 turns), and nothing rests on the exact counts.
- M-CONSTRAINTS-08 (part): the misalignments 3.4e-3, 3.3e-2 and 0.16, and the fit residuals of 1.3%, 14% and 75%, come from an unstated random non-Gibbs admixture. My independent instance gives values of the same order that grow with ε (9e-3, 8.7e-2 and 0.33 at ε = 0.01, 0.1 and 0.5). The "1–75%" range quoted in CONSTRAINTS-H is instance-dependent and should be read as illustrative.
- Not listed as claims, because they are not derived mathematics: the D2 bounds (Q-20, Q-21), the Hořava |β| bound (quoted) and the GPP and Pikovski formulas themselves (source quotes). Only the arithmetic done with them was checked.
- M-CONSTRAINTS-01 caveat: "strictly monotonic" in F1 fails in two saturated cases where York time is constant: flat de Sitter (NEC saturated, k = 0) and the Einstein static universe (SEC saturated, H = 0). Any matter with ρ+p > 0 restores strictness, so no candidate is affected.

- M-CONSTRAINTS-07: for Hermitian H_S and real λ with 1 − λH_S invertible, H_S(1 − λH_S)⁻¹ is Hermitian and has eigenvalues e/(1 − λe) for the eigenvalues e of H_S.
- M-CONSTRAINTS-08: for Hermitian H and β real, −log(e^{−βH}/tr e^{−βH}) = βH + log(tr e^{−βH})·I.
- M-CONSTRAINTS-01: for real x ≥ 0 and real h, −(4π/3)x − h² ≤ 0; for y ≥ 0 and k ≤ 0, −4πy + k/A² ≤ 0.
- M-CONSTRAINTS-03: for Ω_m, K > 0, f(a) = Ω_m a⁻³ − K a⁻² + c has a unique critical point on a > 0, at a* = 3Ω_m/(2K). It is a minimum, with f(a*) − c = −4K³/(27Ω_m²).
