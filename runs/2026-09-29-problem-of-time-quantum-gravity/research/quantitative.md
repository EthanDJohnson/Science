- [RQ-01] CLAIM: The Gran Sasso underground germanium-detector search for the spontaneous radiation predicted by gravity-related (Diosi-Penrose, DP) collapse gives, with 0.95 probability, R0 > 0.54e-10 m (0.54 Angstrom), where R0 is the size of the nuclear mass-density smearing (a free parameter in Diosi's version). The parameter-free Penrose version (R0 ~ 0.05e-10 m, from a crystal-lattice estimate at liquid-nitrogen temperature) lies more than one order of magnitude below this limit and is ruled out. Scope: the DP model with smeared mass density and the radiation-emission signature; Diosi's version with free R0 above 0.54e-10 m survives. Collapse needs an external time in its stochastic equation, so this bears on the "modify QM" positions only.

- [RQ-11] CLAIM: Numerical check of a finite-dimensional Page-Wootters clock (my calculation, dimensionless, hbar = 1): a d-level clock with H_C = -eps diag(0..d-1), qubit system H_S = diag(0, omega), physical states in the null space of H_C x 1 + 1 x H_S. For omega = j eps with integer j <= d-1 the conditional states <t_n|Psi> reproduce exp(-i H_S t_n) with fidelity 1.000000 at every clock tick t_n = 2 pi n/(d eps) (checked d = 4, 8, 16, 32). For omega/eps non-integer the constraint has no exact solution: the best approximate state has residual |C Psi|/|Psi| = |eta| eps/sqrt(2) (eta = distance of omega/eps from the nearest integer; e.g. 0.1768 eps for omega/eps = 1.25, 0.3536 eps for 1.5) and the minimum conditional-state fidelity over one period drops to 0.69 (d = 4) and 0.52 (d = 32) for omega/eps = 1.25, and to 0.146 (d = 4) and 0.0024 (d = 32) for omega/eps = 1.5. - [RQ-12] CLAIM: Zych et al. (2011) proposed the proper-time-witness interferometer: a "clock" particle with internal degrees of freedom in a spatial superposition at different gravitational potentials loses interference visibility to the extent that the clock reading reveals path information, so visibility decreases by the which-way information stored in the proper-time difference. This is the cleanest observational bridge between QM (complementarity) and GR (proper time) at low energy, needs no quantum gravity, and is not yet realised; it is the low-energy analogue of the Pikovski effect (RQ-04). Scope: theory proposal within QM plus weak-field GR; a positive result would confirm proper-time coupling to internal Hamiltonians in superposition but not distinguish among quantum-gravity positions.
