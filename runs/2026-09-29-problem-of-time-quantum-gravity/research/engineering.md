- [RE-02] CLAIM: Moreva et al. (2014) built the only laboratory illustration of the Page-Wootters mechanism so far: a two-photon polarization-entangled state in which one photon is a clock (polarization rotated by a birefringent plate in proportion to time spent crossing it) and the other the system; an "observer" mode conditioning on the clock reproduces evolution, a "super-observer" mode measuring only global properties shows the joint state is static. Scale: two photons, 2x2-dimensional, ideal clock with no back-reaction; it demonstrates the conditional-state bookkeeping (a demonstration of standard QM correlations, per the authors an "illustration"), not quantum gravity, and does not test Kuchar's two-time objection or non-ideal clocks.
  SOURCE: Moreva, Brida, Gramegna, Giovannetti, Maccone, Genovese 2014, Time from quantum entanglement: an experimental illustration, Phys. Rev. A 89, 052122 (arXiv:1310.4691), https://arxiv.org/pdf/1310.4691
  QUOTE: "We implement this mechanism using an entangled state of the polarization of two photons, one of which is used as a clock to gauge the evolution of the second: an “internal” observer that becomes correlated with the clock photon sees the other system evolve, while an “external” observer that only observes global properties of the two photons can prove it is static."
  ACCESS: full-text
  STATUS: peer-reviewed
  CONFIDENCE: high

- [RE-05] CLAIM: Donadi et al. - [RE-09] CLAIM: Oppenheim et al.
