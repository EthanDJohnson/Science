# Verdict on C3 (refuter 0, angle: consistency, backed by evidence)
verdict: weakened
basis: calculation

## Strongest attack
C3 makes two quantitative or structural claims beyond its core: a universal correction law, "(E/m_P)² with E the matter energy", and a stated cost, "gives up exact unitarity". Both fail as stated. The core claim survives: Schrödinger time emerges approximately from a Wheeler–DeWitt state in the heavy-gravity (WKB/BO) limit, and holds only where geometry is nearly classical.

**1. The correction law (E/m_P)² contradicts C3's own toy model and its own source (internal inconsistency, confirmed by calculation).**
- **Toy model M2.** C3 cites the toy model (IDEALIZER F7, verified M-IDEALIZER-07). Its exact series gives a phase rate ω_n = E_n + E_n²/(4e₀M). So the relative correction is E_S/(2Mv²) = E_S/(4K_heavy), where K_heavy is the kinetic energy of the heavy (gravitational) variable. It is not a function of E/√M alone. At fixed M, which is the lens's stand-in for m_P², lowering the background energy e₀ from 50 to 0.5 raises the correction from 1.25e-6 to 1.25e-4 (model units, ħ = 1) [calc: runs/2026-09-29-problem-of-time-quantum-gravity/calc/falsifier-C3-0_correction_scaling.py, part 1]. The lens wrote this itself: "phase error per unit time = E_S × (E_S / 4K_heavy)". It then relabelled the result "(E/m_P)²" without a derivation. The math checks verified only the arithmetic of (E/m_P)² (M-CONSTRAINTS-10, M-IDEALIZER-13), not that it is the right scaling.
- **The source's own formula scales the other way with energy.** Kiefer–Kramer is the paper behind [Q-17]/[F-03]. Their eq. (14) makes the correction proportional to (H/m_P)²/k³. Expanded, C_k² − 1 = −247.68 (H/m_P)²/k³ (dimensionless, k in the paper's compact-space units) [calc: same file, part 2]. At fixed H the correction **falls** as k⁻³ with mode energy. A law (E/m_P)² with E ∝ k would **rise** as k². The two agree only at E ≈ H, the horizon-crossing scale. The (E/m_P)² phrasing comes from the Gravity Research Foundation essay's heuristic "One expects ..." [Q-17], not from the derived equation.
- **The laboratory number 4.5e-57 is not a prediction.** In SI, for the Sr transition (E_S = 2.844e-19 J), (E/m_P)² = 4.49e-57. This equals E_S/E_grav with E_grav = (3π/2)c⁴L/G only when the background length is L = ħc/E_S = 1.11e-7 m, the transition's own reduced wavelength. That is not a gravitational background scale. Other choices give different values: 5.0e-54 for L = 1e-10 m, 5.0e-64 for L = 1 m, 7.8e-71 for L = R_Earth, and 3.4e-89 for the Hubble volume (E_grav = c⁵/(2GH₀) = 8.3e69 J) [calc: same file, part 3]. C3 lists the "(E/m_P)²" correction under predictions. That prediction is therefore ill-defined outside inflationary horizon-scale modes. Its laboratory values rest on a hidden choice of background scale spanning about 35 orders of magnitude.

**2. "Gives up exact unitarity" is not a forced cost (cited evidence).** Kiefer, the originator of the Kiefer–Singh expansion that [D-09] quotes for "a gravitationally induced violation of unitarity", later showed that the violation can be removed at arbitrary order. Chataignier reports perturbative unitarity with a gauge-fixed inner product. The non-unitarity depends on the scheme: it comes from using the naive L² inner product on the matter modes, as Kiefer–Kramer themselves note. So C3 overstates what it gives up. The candidate's own evidence-against list also omits this reply.

**Attacks considered and not pressed.**
- Non-uniqueness of semiclassical time [D-21] and the Giulini–Kiefer anomaly condition [K-04] are real. C3 already concedes both, so they do not defeat it as stated.
- The parenthetical identifying Hartle's generalized QM with the WKB/BO limit is loose: they are different frameworks that share a classical-spacetime limit. This is not decisive.

## In principle vs. in practice
- **In principle:** the core survives. None of the attacks touches the emergence of an approximate Schrödinger time at leading order, which is verified in M2 (M-IDEALIZER-07) and matches [D-09].
- **In practice:** C3's own "untestable for decades" conclusion is unaffected, or strengthened. The attack changes the size of the laboratory correction but does not make it observable. The one quasi-observable regime (CMB, (H/m_P)²/k³) is the regime where the scaling is derived, and there it is a bound, H ≲ 4e17 GeV, not a detection [Q-19].

## What survives and remaining doubt
**Narrower form that survives.** QM's parameter time is the leading-order WKB/BO time of a Wheeler–DeWitt state. It is valid where the gravitational background is nearly classical. Its first corrections have relative size E_S/(4K_heavy), the matter energy over the kinetic energy of the heavy gravitational background, and not a universal (E/m_P)². For cosmological perturbations this becomes (H/m_P)²·k⁻³, which is (H/m_P)² only at horizon crossing.

**Costs, restated.**
- **Given up:** a unique time (routes differ at O(1/m_P²) [D-21]).
- **Given up:** time where geometry is not classical.
- **Contingent:** an anomaly-free full theory [K-04].
- **Not given up:** exact unitarity is not necessarily lost. Whether it survives depends on the inner product and on keeping backreaction (Kiefer–Wichmann 2018; Chataignier 2023). C3 should list the violation as a feature of the naive L² scheme, not as a price of the position.

**Remaining doubt.**
- Whether the Kiefer–Wichmann restoration and Chataignier's perturbative unitarity hold beyond minisuperspace (field theory with local constraints) is not established here. I saw only their abstracts.
- My lab E_grav = (3π/2)c⁴L/G is a dimensional stand-in: a static lab has no well-defined "heavy kinetic energy". That is the point of the attack (the number is background-dependent), not a competing prediction.
- The verdict is weakened, not refuted. The central claim stands, and the errors lie in the stated scaling law and in one listed cost.
