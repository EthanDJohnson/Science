# Verdict on C1 (refuter 0, angle: consistency, supported by evidence)
verdict: weakened
basis: calculation

## Strongest attack
C1 has three clauses:
- (i) QM already runs on GR proper times on any fixed classical spacetime;
- (ii) GR's time is already relational, so the genuine problem arises only when geometry is quantum (superposed);
- (iii) "its first observable form is a superposition of proper times, i.e. the gravity-mediated-entanglement phase."

Clause (iii) fails as stated. Clause (i) needs narrowing. Clause (ii) survives, but on a different reason from the one C1 gives.

**Attack 1 (decisive, against iii): the GME/QGEM phase carries no problem-of-time content.**

a. **The standard prediction of the phase uses exactly the time the question calls "universal and absolute."** Bose et al. 2017 evolve every branch with one external time τ, taking |Ψ(t = 0)⟩ to |Ψ(t = τ)⟩, with φ ~ Gm₁m₂τ/(ħd) (quote below).
   - The Christodoulou–Rovelli "superposed proper times" reading gives the same number by an algebraic identity: mc²·(τGm/(c²r))/ħ ≡ Gm²τ/(ħr).
   - My calculation reproduces both readings to 50-digit precision (residual ≤ 1.3e-51 rad, which is rounding).
   - An observed phase therefore cannot separate "absolute Newtonian time plus a branch-dependent potential" from "superposed proper times with no common time."

b. **The part of the phase that depends on which time runs in each branch is unobservably small.** Swapping lab time τ for branch proper time τ(1 − Φ_b/c²) changes the phase by at most 1.2e-38 rad (τ = 1 s) and 2.9e-38 rad (τ = 2.5 s), in SI. That is a fraction 3.7e-38 of a phase of about 0.3 rad. It is 37 orders of magnitude below the 0.23/0.57 rad signal.

c. **By the run's own slate, the outcome does not discriminate among problem-of-time positions.**
   - candidates.md says, under C2: "If gravity is quantized, gravity-mediated entanglement is present (shared with C3–C5)."
   - C6's Gambini–Porto–Pullin exponent for the branch energy split (ω₁₂ = 0.226 rad/s) is 1.6e-59 (1 s) and 2.9e-59 (2.5 s), dimensionless.
   - So C2–C6 predict the same witness to within about 1e-59. QGEM separates only C7 (classical or collapsing gravity) from the rest. That tests hidden premise 7 ("must gravity be quantized?"), not any of facets 1–7 of the problem of time.

d. **Internal inconsistency with C1's own reasoning.**
   - C1 cites Regge–Teitelboim: for asymptotically flat, isolated systems "the Hamiltonian does not vanish."
   - The DECOMPOSER finding C1 draws on (F7) states the reframe canonically: "time exists when there is a classical, external reference (boundary, background, or heavy classical degrees of freedom) and is frozen only when the quantum system is the whole closed universe."
   - A tabletop QGEM experiment is exactly such an isolated subsystem, with classical lab clocks and Earth as its external reference. By C1's own argument the frozen formalism (facet 1) is absent there, and none of facets 2–7 is exercised.
   - C1 therefore names as the "first observable form of the genuine problem of time" an experiment in which, on its own account, that problem does not arise.
   - At most, GME is the first observable form of *superposed gravitational sources*, which is a different claim.

e. **The literature disputes even the quantum-geometry reading.** Martín-Martínez & Perche (PRD 2023) show the BMV entanglement "could be accounted for without appealing to quantum degrees of freedom of the gravitational field." C1 lists only the withdrawn Anastopoulos–Hu preprint [D-23] against it. This peer-reviewed result is stronger.

**Attack 2 (narrowing, against i): "any fixed classical spacetime" is too broad.**
- Kay, Radzikowski & Wald prove that the quantum theory of a linear scalar field breaks down on spacetimes with a compactly generated Cauchy horizon.
- QM "running on GR's proper times" is established only for globally hyperbolic backgrounds, where a Cauchy foliation exists.
- The run's own Gödel calculations fall outside that class.
- C1's Torre–Varadarajan reply (generalized unitarity) is fair, but it too presupposes global hyperbolicity.

**Attack 3 (reason, against ii): the facets come from treating the whole system as closed, not from superposition.**
- The brief's required toy model, a finite-dimensional Page–Wootters clock, has no geometry. Yet it shows the frozen formalism, clock choice and Kuchař's two-time problem.
- So the facets arise whenever the whole system, clocks included, is quantum and subject to a Hamiltonian constraint with no external reference. That is DECOMPOSER F7's criterion, and it is met in quantum cosmology.
- A "superposed geometry" inside a lab with external clocks does not meet it.
- The locus C1 names (superposed geometry) is therefore neither necessary (the Page–Wootters model has none) nor sufficient (the QGEM lab has it but also has an external clock).

## In principle vs. in practice
- **In principle:** clauses (i) and (ii) are consistent, provided (i) is restricted to globally hyperbolic backgrounds. Clause (iii) is a category error. The GME phase is a superposed-*source* effect, fully described with a single external time. Any dependence on how the problem of time is resolved enters at a relative 1e-38 or below.
- **In practice:**
  - A QGEM witness within about 20 years would test quantized versus classical or collapsing gravity (C7 versus the rest), and even that is model-dependent (Martín-Martínez & Perche).
  - It would not bear on facets 1–7.
  - C1's refutation test, excess visibility loss for a clock in superposed heights on a classical background, remains valid and well posed.

## What survives and remaining doubt
- **What survives:** the narrower reframe.
  - On globally hyperbolic classical backgrounds QM clocks already read GR proper time; this is measured (Sr 1 mm redshift, D-14).
  - "Absolute vs relative" and "curvature" misdescribe the conflict.
  - The genuine problem of time arises when the whole system, clocks included, is treated quantum-mechanically under a Hamiltonian constraint without an external classical reference, as in quantum cosmology and a closed universe.
- **What must be dropped:**
  - "its first observable form is ... the gravity-mediated-entanglement phase." The most GME could show is that gravitational sources can be superposed, which is a precondition for, not an instance of, the problem of time.
  - A defensible replacement: no observation within reach probes facets 1–7 directly. The candidate probes (WDW/semiclassical-time CMB corrections, Q-19) give only H ≲ 4e17 GeV and are disputed.
- **Remaining doubt:** Christodoulou–Rovelli would reply that in a generally covariant description the phase *is* a proper-time difference, and that the Newtonian external τ is a gauge artefact. That is true as an interpretation. But both descriptions give the same number to about 1e-38. The observation therefore cannot count as evidence *for* the problem of time as against a background-time account, and that is what "observable form" requires.
