# Verdict on C4 (refuter 0, angle: strongest available: consistency, supported by evidence)
verdict: weakened
basis: cited-evidence

## Strongest attack
C4's headline is a general claim. Any of the four listed added clocks (unimodular 4-volume, Brown–Kuchař dust, CMC/York, Hořava foliation) "removes the frozen formalism and global-time problem by supplying an ideal clock with a constraint linear in its momentum". Checked branch by branch, the claim holds cleanly only for dust. Two branches fail on the mechanism C4 names, and a third fails on global time.

**1. Hořava: the frozen formalism is not removed (cited evidence).**
- In a closed universe, projectable/Hořava gravity keeps a global first-class Hamiltonian constraint that generates time reparametrizations. Its Hamiltonian vanishes on shell.
- Hořava therefore supplies a preferred *foliation* but no *clock variable* whose constraint is linear in its momentum. Facet 1 survives in its global (zero-mode) form, just as in closed-universe GR.
- C4's "Gives up" list for Hořava names only "a preferred foliation and Lorentz invariance". It does not say that facet 1 stays open. That is a hidden cost: the branch fails to deliver the benefit C4 claims for it.

**2. Unimodular time: only one global constraint becomes linear in the clock momentum (calculation plus cited evidence).**
- In Henneaux–Teitelboim form, the local constraints 𝓗(x) + Λ(x)√q(x) = 0 are each linear in the local momentum Λ(x). But the constraint ∂_iΛ = 0 makes the individual τ(x) pure gauge, so the only clock is the global T = ∫τ.
- My lattice toy shows the consequence explicitly. N local Hamiltonian constraints reduce to one Schrödinger equation in T plus N − 1 residual constraints R_k = h_k/v_k − h_1/v_1. These residuals:
  - have zero Poisson bracket with T;
  - are quadratic in the gravitational momenta and contain no clock momentum;
  - generate nontrivial gauge flow on the gravitational variables.
- In the continuum limit this leaves infinitely many Wheeler–DeWitt-type constraints 𝓗(x)/√q(x) = const, which stay frozen with respect to the unimodular clock.
- This is the formal content of Kuchař's 1991 hypertime objection (quoted below). The originators of the proposal themselves doubt it gives enough observables beyond minisuperspace (Unruh–Wald, quoted below).
- So "removes the frozen formalism" holds only for the spatial zero mode (minisuperspace). Facets 1 (local), 5 and 6 remain open in the field theory.
- C4 lists Kuchař's critique under "evidence against" but still claims facet 1 "resolved" in its headline and facet summary. That is an internal inconsistency.

**3. CMC/York: the global-time claim fails even with physically reasonable matter and classical GR (cited evidence).**
- Isenberg and Rendall construct maximal globally hyperbolic Einstein–*dust* solutions that have a CMC Cauchy surface but are not covered by a CMC foliation. CMC time is therefore not a global time in general, before any quantization.
- Bartnik (1988, CMP 117, 615) is the standard earlier reference for cosmological spacetimes lacking CMC Cauchy surfaces. I could not fetch its text (403), so I rely only on Isenberg–Rendall's abstract.
- C4's own prediction already concedes a York turning point in closed ΛCDM (t* = 120.5, 79.9 and 51.2 Gyr for Ω_k = −0.001, −0.01 and −0.05; M-CONSTRAINTS-03 verified). Isenberg–Rendall add that the failure is generic, not special to recollapse.

**4. Dust: survives the attack in C4's own qualified form.**
- Brown–Kuchař dust does give a local constraint linear in the dust-time momentum at each point [D-10].
- Its limits are ones C4 already lists: factor ordering is an "overwhelming if", vacuum problems are unresolved, and it is one clock among many.
- The "facet 4 holds only until dust worldlines cross" point is a dossier inference with no quote [F-02]. I do not rely on it.

## Evidence
- **Hořava.** Donnelly & Jacobson 2011, PRD 84, 104019 (arXiv:1106.2131), abstract via fetch_text: "In a closed universe the Hamiltonian is a sum of generators of gauge symmetries, the foliation-preserving diffeomorphisms, and vanishes on shell. The scalar constraint is second class, except for a global, first-class part that generates time reparametrizations." Mukohyama 2009, PRD 80, 064005, abstract via lit_search: "the Hamiltonian constraint is not a local equation satisfied at each spatial point but an equation integrated over a whole space."
- **Unimodular (calculation).** [calc: runs/2026-09-29-problem-of-time-quantum-gravity/calc/falsifier-C4-0_unimodular_residual.py] (sympy, model units with ħ = 1, dimensionless, N = 3 sites). Output:
  - Local constraints are each linear in L_i: degrees [1, 1, 1].
  - {D_1, τ_1} = 1 (the individual τ_i are gauge) and {D_i, T} = 0.
  - Residuals R_2 = −p1²/(2q1²) + p2²/(2q2²) − U(q2)/q2² + U(q1)/q1², and likewise R_3.
  - {R_k, T} = 0; the residuals have degree 2 in p and contain no L or Λ.
  - {R_k, q_1} = p1/q1² ≠ 0, and {R_2, R_3} = 0 (first class).
  - "3 local Hamiltonian constraints -> 1 Schrodinger equation in T + 2 residual WDW-type constraints."
  - The toy is ultralocal (no spatial derivatives), so it tests the counting and the clock-independence of the residuals, not the full hypersurface-deformation algebra.
- **Unimodular (Kuchař).** Kuchař 1991, PRD 43, 3332, abstract via fetch_text: "The constraint system of parametrized unimodular gravity implies that the cosmological time labels only equivalence classes formed by hypersurfaces separated by a zero four-volume, while individual spacelike hypersurfaces within an equivalence class are physically irrelevant. As a result, unless complemented by a hypertime variable, cosmological time does not uniquely set the conditions for measuring geometric variables".
- **Unimodular (the proposers).** Unruh & Wald 1989, PRD 40, 2598, abstract via fetch_text: "A specific proposal considered in detail yields a theory which corresponds at the classical level to general relativity with an arbitrary, unspecified cosmological constant. In minisuperspace models, this proposal yields a quantum theory with satisfactory interpretive properties, although it is unlikely that this theory will admit sufficiently many observables for general spacetimes."
- **CMC.** Isenberg & Rendall 1998, CQG 15, 3679 (arXiv:gr-qc/9710053), abstract via fetch_text: "We show that there exist maximal globally hyperbolic solutions of the Einstein-dust equations which admit a constant mean curvature Cauchy surface, but are not covered by a constant mean curvature foliation."
- **Math checks used, not contested.** M-CONSTRAINTS-03 (York turning point), M-CONSTRAINTS-11 (ΔΛΔV4 bound, valid under its assumed pairing), M-CONSTRAINTS-02 (T4 monotonic in three FRW models, which are minisuperspace only, so it bears only on the zero mode) and M-IDEALIZER-05.

## In principle vs. in practice
Not a feasibility question. The in-principle issue is whether each branch delivers the claimed resolution:
- **Dust:** yes, classically and formally, for facets 1 and 4 while the dust frame is regular.
- **Unimodular:** only for the global mode.
- **CMC:** only on spacetimes that admit a CMC foliation.
- **Hořava:** no, for facet 1 in a closed universe.

In practice the branches are empirically equivalent to GR with Λ at accessible scales, apart from Hořava's |β| ≲ 1e-15 tuning, as C4 itself says.

## What survives and remaining doubt
- **The narrower form that survives.** A matter clock with a local constraint linear in its momentum (Brown–Kuchař dust, or similar deparametrizing fields) removes the frozen formalism formally, at the classical and reduced-phase-space level, with the costs C4 lists: extra matter, a privileged frame, factor-ordering ambiguity and a postulated clock.
  - A CMC/York time does the same only on spacetimes that admit a CMC foliation, which is not all globally hyperbolic Einstein–dust spacetimes.
  - Unimodular time removes the frozen formalism only for the spatial zero mode (minisuperspace). Beyond that it needs Kuchař's hypertime, and facets 1 (local), 5 and 6 remain open.
  - Hořava should be moved out of the "removes the frozen formalism" list. In a closed universe its global Hamiltonian constraint still generates time reparametrizations, so it supplies a preferred foliation, not a clock.
- **C4's "costs" list is incomplete.** Beyond the costs it states, the position gives up locality of the time variable (unimodular), global existence of the slicing (CMC) and the frozen-formalism benefit itself (Hořava).
- **Why weakened, not refuted.** The dust branch, and C4's general structure (added structure → deparametrized Schrödinger evolution, with a postulated clock), survive. C4 also already flags Kuchař's hypertime objection and the York turning point, so the defect is overstatement in the headline and facet summary, not a false core.
- **Remaining doubts.**
  - My unimodular toy is ultralocal. A full treatment with the hypersurface-deformation algebra might change how the residual constraints close, but not their number or their independence of T.
  - I could not access Bartnik's full text.
  - Whether a Hořava extension with an added global clock (e.g. unimodular-Hořava) would repair branch 1 was not investigated.
