# Verdict on C7 (refuter 0, angle: evidence, backed by a calculation; consistency secondary)
verdict: weakened
basis: cited-evidence

## Strongest attack
C7 bundles two mechanisms under one premise, that geometry never stays superposed, and one prediction: no gravity-mediated entanglement (GIE), with "Entanglement eliminates DP and postquantum classical gravity" as its decisive test. The Diósi–Penrose (DP) branch fails on both counts.

1. **DP with free R0 predicts GIE.** A peer-reviewed analysis (Trillo & Navascués, PRD 111, L121101, 2025) shows that DP dynamics entangle two masses in standard GIE geometries whenever the separation is below a limiting distance d_c ∝ R0. In some configurations the entanglement survives for more than a day. The opposing paper (Feng, Vedral & Marletto 2025, preprint) does not deny the entanglement. It argues that DP is nonlocal and so escapes the witness theorem. Both sides therefore agree that DP can produce GIE. That contradicts C7's claim of "no gravity-mediated entanglement predicted" and its decisive-test logic that "entanglement eliminates DP".
2. **The claim that the QGEM decoherence time does not depend on R0 fails inside DP's allowed window.** C7 says τ_DP = 5.8–11.6 ms "independent of the free R0". That holds only for R0 ≪ R = 0.88 µm. In my calculation τ_DP rises from 11.6 ms at R0 = 4e-10 m to 0.30 s at 1e-5 m, 1.07 s at 3e-5 m and 8.1 s at 1e-4 m (SI, C7's own convention). At R0 = 1e-4 m, 88% of the coherence remains after the 1 s QGEM time, and the entangling phase accrued within one τ_DP is 1.8 rad. With the doubled E_G convention, τ_DP = 4.05 s and about 78% of the coherence remains at 1 s. So across part of DP's allowed range, a QGEM test would see entanglement even if DP is true. A null result does not uniquely promote C7, and a positive result does not eliminate DP.
3. **"Geometry never stays superposed" is not guaranteed by DP with free R0.** The DP collaboration's own analysis (Figurato et al., NJP 26, 113004, 2024) finds that DP "does not guarantee classicality". The premise C7 needs for its avoidance of the problem of time is not a theorem of the model it names.
4. **Minor points:**
   - C7 quotes the older lower bound, R0 > 0.54e-10 m. Figurato et al. give "R0 ≳ 4 Å" (4e-10 m) as the current bound, and propose an upper bound R0 ≲ 10^6 Å (1e-4 m).
   - For postquantum classical gravity (PQCG), "presupposed foliation" is overstated. The covariant path-integral formulation claims that diffeomorphism invariance can be imposed. The theory still presupposes a single classical spacetime, which carries its own time-orientation and chronology assumptions (the Gödel point, M-CONSTRAINTS-04, still applies).

## What survives and remaining doubt
- **What survives:** a narrower C7.
  - **Main branch, postquantum classical gravity with a non-delta kernel.** Gravity is strictly classical, so no GIE is possible under local operations and classical communication (LOCC). Time is the classical spacetime's time, facets 1–5 are removed by assumption, and the price is a stochastic, non-unitary quantum sector. The decisive test for this branch stands: any GIE refutes it.
  - **DP only for R0 ≲ 1 µm (SI).** There τ_DP ≈ 12–30 ms for the QGEM mass, far shorter than the 1 s GIE time, so a QGEM test effectively sees no entanglement. For R0 between about 1e-5 and 1e-4 m, DP allows observable GIE and is not separated from the quantum-geometry positions by an entanglement witness alone. Separating it would need the time-dependence of decoherence, the d_c ∝ R0 cutoff, or spontaneous heating.
- **What changes in C7:**
  - The position must drop "geometry never stays superposed" as a property of DP with free R0.
  - It must drop the unconditional "no gravity-mediated entanglement predicted" for the DP branch.
  - Its decisive test must be restated as: GIE refutes PQCG and small-R0 DP only.
  - It should update the DP bound to R0 ≳ 4e-10 m.
- **What this does not show:** the attack does not touch C7's cost ledger (non-unitarity, stochasticity), its stance on the problem of time, or its PQCG core.
- **Remaining doubt:**
  - The result that DP produces GIE is a single peer-reviewed paper, and a preprint disputes its interpretation, though not its dynamics.
  - My τ_DP scan uses a continuum sphere with Gaussian smearing and neglects lattice granularity. That is appropriate for R0 ≳ lattice spacing, and C7 itself treats the granular term as negligible.
  - The upper bound R0 ≲ 1e-4 m is described by its own authors as "to some degree arbitrary". If a stricter macro-objectification requirement forced R0 ≲ 1 µm, DP would return to the no-GIE regime in QGEM. By the same authors' finding, though, it would then still fail to guarantee classicality for all macroscopic systems.
  - For these reasons I judge C7 weakened, not refuted: its PQCG branch and its framing survive.
