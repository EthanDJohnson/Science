## Summary: 47 claims checked, 24 flagged (all weak, none contradicted outright); the 7 that the source checks weaken or contradict are listed first.

Report claims the source checks weaken or contradict:
- **Row 19 (C7, Loup lapse)**: the numbers reproduce (98×, 1.37×), but RS-12 is *contradicted* on "no published critique". Bobrick–Martire 2021 App. A.1 shows the Loup lapse metric reduces to Alcubierre, so the lapse yields no real energy reduction. The report omits this rebuttal.
- **Row 7 and 8 (thick-wall floor v²R/12 = 1.12e30 kg; the headline 1e28–1e30 kg range)**: RQ-11 marks the floor *unverifiable* (its derivation does not follow from Pfenning–Ford Eq. 28). The headline "smallest known budget" and 10^26.2 / 10^61.3 depend on it.
- **Row 26 (flattened floor 1.11e28 kg, α_X = 101)**: the RS-13 link between "two orders" and α_X ≈ 100 is unverifiable (the researcher's inference).
- **Row 13 (antimatter 16 ng, 10^17.1)**: the Symmetry page was not openable (403); 16 ng is the verdict's own sum from a search summary (C8-0.check Q10).
- **Row 37 (squeezed light)**: C5-0.check Q14, Q11 and Q13 unverifiable; Q9 (Sopova–Ford) misattributed; RQ-14 status-wrong (Maclay–Davis is peer-reviewed, Found. Phys. 49, 797).
- **Row 31 (C4 δ-sheet cancellation)**: C4-0.check Q15 misattributed and the kink-plane/δ² reading is labelled the verdict's inference. The core SSV and Fell–Heisenberg quotes are verified.
- **Row 21 ("~8e3× Natário penalty")**: does not match the tabulated 4.91e36 / 1.62e32 = 3.0e4; the compact/dipole multipliers are unchecked internal calcs.
