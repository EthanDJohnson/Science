# Source check: theory (part 2 of 3: RT-07-RT-12)
status: final
| Claim | Verdict | Note |
|---|---|---|
| RT-07 | verified | Ford-Roman 1999 (arXiv gr-qc/9901074, full text): quote matches; abstract says negative pulse must be followed by positive pulse with separation inversely proportional to amplitude; conjecture proven for "massless quantize[d]" delta-function pulses; the paper's Sec. 4 proves it in 4D (Lorentzian sampling) and extends to 2D and 4D. The "no steady-state negative energy" sentence is the researcher's inference, not stated in the source. Journal ref (PRD 60, 104018) not independently re-checked. |
| RT-08 | verified | Everett-Roman (arXiv gr-qc/9702049, full text): abstract wording matches both quotes (observer at centre "can neither create a warp bubble on demand nor control one"; single tube has no CTCs, two non-overlapping tubes give a time machine; unphysically thin negative-energy layers). Krasnikov's original was for two spacetime dimensions; Everett-Roman generalize to 4D. INSPIRE confirms PRD 56, 2100. |
| RT-09 | verified | INSPIRE abstract of Everett 1996, PRD 53, 7365, matches the quote ("We verify this conjecture by exhibiting a simple modification..."). Abstract-only, as the claim admits. The statement that chronology protection is a conjecture is general background, not supported by a fetched source here; the claim that CTCs depend on producing the bubble at will is the researcher's gloss. |
| RT-10 | verified | Hiscock (arXiv gr-qc/9707024, full text): Eq. (24) is exactly <rho> ~ -(f')^2/(48 pi) [f-(1-1/v0)]^-2; stress-energy regular for v<1 and diverging for apparent v>1 at the horizon; text says "If the instability is not an artifact of working in two dimensions" backreaction would preclude v>c. The divergence is attributed to a mismatch between the Hawking temperature of the horizon and the asymptotically empty (Boulware-like) state. The "Hawking-like flux" wording is a fair paraphrase. |
| RT-11 | verified | Finazzi-Liberati-Barcelo (arXiv 0904.0141, full text): abstract text matches the quote, including "created out of an initially flat spacetime". The 1+1-dimension restriction ("restricting attention to warp drives in 1 + 1 dimensions") is confirmed; the claim of "extremely high" flux and extension to 3+1 is the authors' expectation ("we expect our results to be valid also in 3 + 1 dimensions"), not a 4D calculation. The claim's scope caveats are accurate. Boulware-like state is discussed in the paper. Journal ref (PRD 79, 124017) not independently re-checked. |
| RT-12 | verified | VBL (arXiv gr-qc/9810026, full text): linearized gravity around Minkowski; NEC makes light cones contract; Shapiro delay never an advance; the paper itself says the strong-field case is "rather tricky". Barcelo et al. (arXiv 2307.11472): Gao-Wald (CQG 17, 4999, 2000) challenge confirmed, "both papers are correct" in distinct paradigms; "non-geometric paradigm" accommodates VBL. INSPIRE confirms Gen. Rel. Grav. 56, 116 (2024) and CQG 17, 4999. The closing sentence about the Olum form being a theorem is the researcher's synthesis. |
