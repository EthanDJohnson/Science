| RT-17 | verified | Synthesis, no new source; each ingredient traces to verified claims. Olum (gr-qc/9805003) abstract: "assuming the generic condition) I prove that superluminal travel requires weak-energy-condition violation", and the flat-space-in-odd-coordinates example ("appears to allow superluminal travel, but in fact is just flat space") confirmed. The linear-in-v NEC term and "violated for all v" were verified under RT-03 in check-1. Two caveats: (1) Olum's theorem concludes WEC violation at some point of the path P, and P is a null geodesic so the violation is of R_ab K^a K^b ≥ 0 (NEC-type); the claim's "WEC/NEC" is a fair reading but the paper's own wording is WEC. (2) "requires ... no conjugate points" is slightly off: Olum states "Since there are no conjugate points, θ is well-defined", which follows from P being the earliest-arriving path, not as a separate hypothesis; the stated assumptions are the superluminal-travel definition plus the generic condition. The final inference (positive-energy claims must be subluminal or hide violation for other observers) is the researcher's synthesis, consistent with the Santiago abstract, and is labelled as such. |
