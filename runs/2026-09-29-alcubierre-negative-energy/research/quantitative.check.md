## Summary
6 verified, 0 unverifiable, 0 contradicted, 0 misattributed, 0 status-wrong. Most notable issue: RQ-05's "σ ≈ 2/Δ" is not from Lobo and Visser (they use Δ = 1/σ); an O(1) convention factor, not an error of magnitude.
