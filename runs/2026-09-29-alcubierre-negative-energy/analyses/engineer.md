# Analysis: engineer (Archimedes)

## Method applied
For each option, compare what the reference case (R = 100 m, v = 10c, Δ = 1 m or Δ at the QI limit) requires with the best demonstrated value of the same quantity (total energy, energy density, wall thickness or fabrication tolerance, power, time, cost). Compute log10(required/demonstrated) in Python, state the scaling law, identify which parameter changes shrink the gap, then rate TRL of the key component and name the next milestone.

## Findings
1. **Largest demonstrated parallel-plate Casimir configuration (the "demonstrated" anchor for total negative energy).** Bressi et al. 2002 measured the force between parallel chromium-coated surfaces over 0.5–3.0 µm with an area of 1.2 × 1.2 mm². [new: Bressi et al. 2002 PRL 88 041804, arXiv:quant-ph/0203002, full-text, "The two opposing surfaces, on which the Casimir force is studied , form a capacitor with an area of 1.2 × 1.2 mm 2."] and [new: same, abstract, "The scaling of the force with the distance between the surfaces was tested in the 0.5 - 3.0 $\mu$m range, and the related force coefficient was determined at the 15% precision level."] Ideal-plate energy at the 0.5 µm end is computed in [calc: runs/2026-09-29-alcubierre-negative-energy/calc/lens-engineer_gaps.py].
2. **Smallest probed Casimir gap (the anchor for negative energy density).** Ederth 2000 measured Casimir forces in the 20–100 nm range between template-stripped gold surfaces. [new: Ederth 2000, Phys. Rev. A 62 062104, title via lit_search (Crossref), "Template-stripped gold surfaces with 0.4-nm rms roughness suitable for force measurements: Application to the Casimir force in the 20–100-nm range"]. I use the ideal-conductor density at 20 nm as an *upper bound* on the demonstrated negative energy density; real gold at 20 nm is below the ideal value because 20 nm is below the plasma wavelength.
3. **Highest demonstrated (positive) energy densities, as a ceiling on any engineered stress-energy.** Quark–gluon plasma at the LHC: about 14 GeV/fm³ (Bjorken estimate, central Pb–Pb at 2.76 TeV, τ₀ = 1 fm/c) [new: arXiv:1304.1452 and Braun-Munzinger & Stachel review via WebSearch, search-summary, SUMMARY "For central Pb–Pb collisions at √s_NN = 2.76 TeV this yields an energy density of about 14 GeV/fm³"]. Record laser intensity 1.1e23 W/cm² (CoReLS, Optica 8, 630, 2021) [new: Optica 8(5) 630 via WebSearch, search-summary, SUMMARY "The highest laser pulse intensity is ... 1.1 x 10^23 W/cm2 ... CoReLS ... published in ... Optica on 6 May 2021"]. These bracket what any lab has ever concentrated, of either sign, for ~1e-23 s and ~2e-14 s respectively.
4. **Humanity's energy supply.** World primary energy use in 2024 was 592 EJ = 5.92e20 J/yr, i.e. a mean power of about 1.9e13 W [new: Energy Institute Statistical Review 2025 via dieselnet/Forbes, search-summary, SUMMARY "In 2024 global energy consumption rose 2% from its 2023 level to 592 exajoules"].
5. **Direct warp-field detection attempts.** The only lab "warp-field" experiment (White–Juday interferometer, NASA Eagleworks) is inconclusive and was argued incapable of resolving the claimed signal. [new: Lee & Cleaver 2014, arXiv:1407.7772, full-text (abstract page), "the spacetime distortions resulting from the experimentally obtainable electric field of a parallel plate capacitor configuration cannot be detected by the White-Juday Warp Field Interferometer"]. With White et al. 2021 being numerics only [D-11], [D-68], no lab has produced or detected any warp metric: TRL 1 for every warp geometry.
6. **Does lab negative energy gravitate? Not yet measured.** The Archimedes experiment aims to weigh the vacuum-energy contribution to a layered superconductor's transition energy [new: Avino et al. 2020, MDPI Physics 2, 1, abstract, "the Archimedes experiment, devoted to measuring the debated interaction of quantum vacuum fluctuations and gravity. The method is essentially the weighing of the transition energy of a layered superconductor"]. The 2024 status is a thermal-noise-limited prototype balance, no vacuum-weight result [new: Calloni et al., EPJ Plus 2024, search-summary, SUMMARY "the research group built a prototype balance with sensitivity in the frequency range between 20 and 100 millihertz, compatible with thermal noise"]. So the gravitational coupling of engineered vacuum energy is itself at TRL ~2–3.
7. **Natário's zero-expansion drive costs more, not less, at thin walls (ours).** With the same tanh profile, the Eulerian negative energy of Natário's drive (shift β = ∇×[(v/2) f(r)(0, −z, y)], exactly v x̂ inside, ∇·β = 0 checked) scales as |E_nat| ≈ 0.0056 v²R⁴σ³ (log-log slope 2.97 in σ), against Alcubierre's |E_alc| = v²R²σ/36 (slope 1.00; matches the PF 1D integral to 4 digits). The ratio is ≈ 0.8 (R/Δ_PF)²: 5× at R/Δ = 2, 821× at R/Δ = 32, and an extrapolated **8e3× at R = 100 m, Δ = 1 m (6.0e35 kg at v = 10c vs 7.5e31 kg)** [calc: runs/2026-09-29-alcubierre-negative-energy/calc/lens-engineer_natario_vs_alcubierre.py]. Engineering reading: a divergence-free shift must return the interior "flux" (area πR² at speed v) through the wall (area ~2πRΔ), so the wall shift is ~vR/(2Δ), K ~ vR/Δ², ρ ~ v²R²/Δ⁴ and E ~ v²R⁴/Δ³. This conflicts with the dossier's "same v²R²σ scaling" [D-04] except when Δ ~ R, and is consistent in direction with Rodal's 35× larger curvature invariants [U-10]. Grid convergence: relative change < 1e-5 on 1.5× refinement.
8. **Thick-wall floor verified (ours).** For a flat interior of radius R and a wall of thickness D, the minimum of −(v²/12)∫r²f′²dr is attained by f′ = −C/r², C = R(R+D)/D, giving E_min = −(v²/12)R(R+D)/D; numeric and closed form agree to 6 digits for D/R = 0.1, 1, 10 [calc: same file]. This confirms D-19's formula, which the dossier marked as not re-derived.
9. **Demonstrated anchors in numbers (ours, ideal-plate formulas).** Bressi's plates at 0.5 µm hold an ideal Casimir energy of 5.0e-15 J = 5.5e-32 kg; the ideal density at Ederth's 20 nm gap is 2.7e3 J/m³. A 15 dB squeezed vacuum beam (1064 nm, ~100 MHz bandwidth, 0.01 mm² spot) has a negative-energy dip of order 3e-12 J/m³ lasting ~9e-16 s per half-cycle; this is an order-of-magnitude estimate, not a measurement, and it sits 10 orders inside the Ford–Roman bound for that duration. World energy use is 5.9e20 J/yr, whose mass-equivalent is only 6.6e3 kg/yr [calc: runs/2026-09-29-alcubierre-negative-energy/calc/lens-engineer_gaps.py].
10. **Total-energy gaps at the reference case (R = 100 m, v = 10c)** relative to the Bressi Casimir energy: Alcubierre Δ = 1 m 10^63.1 (7.5e31 kg, 38 M_sun); QI wall 10^94.9 (4.6e63 kg); thick-wall floor 10^61.3 (1.1e30 kg); Bobrick–Martire flattening (α_X = 1 + v² = 101) and optimal profile 10^60.6 (2.5e29 kg); Natário with the same profile 10^67.0; Van Den Broeck 10^61.4 (as published, v-independent) to 10^63.4 (if ∝ v², Bobrick–Martire's expectation [U-05]). Against a full year of world energy the same options are 10^25.6 to 10^59.8 short. Every geometric trick buys at most ~3 of the ~61–95 orders [calc: same].
11. **Energy-density gaps.** The peak Alcubierre wall density (v = 10c, Δ = 1 m) is 1.2e44 J/m³: 10^40.6 above ideal 20 nm Casimir, 10^55.6 above squeezed light, and 10^7.7 above even the LHC quark–gluon plasma (a positive, 1e-23 s density). At the QI wall it is 4.6e107 J/m³ (10^104 above Casimir); Van Den Broeck's peak is 5.9e110 J/m³ (10^107) [calc: same].
12. **Casimir cannot be separated from its plates (ours).** Per cell, the plates' rest energy exceeds the Casimir deficit by 10^17.8 (gold, a = t = 20 nm), 10^23.3 (Bressi-like 0.5 µm gap, 1 µm plate) and even 10^11.1 for a one-atom (0.3 nm) plate at a 1 nm gap using the (over-optimistic) ideal formula. Any stack of plates therefore has a *net positive* averaged energy density (+5e19 to +9e20 J/m³). The negative energy exists only inside the gap, on scales of the gap, bracketed by positive matter; Hidden Premise 4 fails by 11 or more orders before any scaling question arises [calc: same].
13. **Positive-energy shells hit self-gravity before engineering.** A Lentz-type shell carrying the required 7.5e31 kg of positive energy at R = 100 m has Schwarzschild radius 1.1e5 m, 1.1e3 times the bubble radius, so it would collapse; its shell density (5.4e43 J/m³) is 10^7.4 above QGP, sustained rather than for 1e-23 s. The Fuchs et al. subluminal shell (4.49e27 kg between 10 and 20 m) has 2GM/c²R₂ = 0.33 and a mean density 1.4e40 J/m³, 10^3.8 above QGP, held static [calc: same]. (This is a physics limit, recorded because it is decisive for Lentz's claimed budget.)
14. **Wall thickness vs fabrication.** Δ_QI = 1.6e-32 m at 10c is 10^21.8 below atomic spacing (1e-10 m) and 10^12 below the shortest length probed at the LHC (~1.5e-20 m, our ħc/E estimate for ~13 TeV). Van Den Broeck's ~10 L_P curvature radius is 10^23.8 below atomic scale; its 3e-15 m neck is nuclear-sized [calc: same].
15. **Duration, power and cost.** The trip lasts 1.4e7 s; the squeezed-light negative-energy interval is ~9e-16 s, a 10^22.2 gap. Assembling 6.7e48 J in one year needs 2.1e41 W, 10^28.1 times world power and 10^14.7 times the Sun's luminosity; at $0.05/kWh the energy alone costs ~$9e40 [calc: same].
16. **Which parameters shrink the gap, and how far they can go.** |E| ∝ v²R²/Δ at fixed wall (elasticities +2, +2, −1); at the QI wall ∝ vR²; on the thick-wall floor ∝ v²R. Dropping to v = 1c saves 2 orders, R = 1 m saves 4, a thick wall saves ≤ 1.8 at R = 100 m. Combined (v = 1c, R = 1 m, D = R): 2.2e26 kg, still 10^57.6 above Casimir and 10^22.5 above a world-year. A 1 mm probe at 1c needs 2.2e23 kg (a few lunar masses). Inverting: all world energy to 2100 (4.4e22 J) could supply a thick-wall bubble of radius only 4.4e-21 m at 1c; the Sun's full rest energy supplies R ≈ 18 km at 1c or 180 m at 10c on the thick-wall floor, but only ~2e-15 m at the QI wall [calc: same]. No parameter choice brings any superluminal bubble of material size within 19 orders of demonstrated capability.
17. **The only direct lab observation of sub-vacuum fluctuations is subcycle and self-compensating.** Riek et al. 2017 resolved squeezed vacuum in the time domain by electro-optic sampling with few-femtosecond pulses [new: Riek et al. 2017, Nature 541, 376, arXiv:1611.06773, abstract, "Subcycle intervals with noise level significantly below the pure quantum vacuum are found. Enhanced fluctuations in adjacent time segments manifest generation of highly correlated quantum radiation as a consequence of the uncertainty principle."]. That is the lab face of the quantum-interest pattern [D-56]: femtosecond sub-vacuum intervals paid for by adjacent excess. It demonstrates the sign, not a usable energy; no absolute J/m³ was given in the abstract.
18. **Reframe benchmark: sub-light relativistic travel needs no negative energy.** An ideal photon rocket at 1 g (accelerate to midpoint, then brake) reaches Alpha Centauri (4.37 ly) in 3.58 yr ship time, 6.00 yr Earth time, peak 0.952c, mass ratio 40.4 and 3.5e18 J per kg of payload [calc: `python3 .claude/skills/conundrum/scripts/rocket_tools.py trip --distance "4.37 ly" --accel "1 g0" --ve c`]. For a 1e5 kg ship that is 3.5e23 J, about 600 world-years of energy (10^2.8), roughly 25 orders closer to demonstrated capability than the cheapest superluminal warp at the reference case. It is not superluminal in the brief's exterior-frame sense.

### Next milestone experiments (smallest demonstration that raises the TRL or kills the option)
- **Casimir supply:** weigh a change in vacuum energy (Archimedes-type superconductor transition, Finding 6). A null at the predicted level would undercut every vacuum-energy source; a positive result raises "negative energy gravitates as predicted" to TRL 3. A second milestone: any geometry in which the *net* energy (plates included) of a macroscopic volume is negative; Finding 12 predicts this fails by ≥ 11 orders.
- **Squeezed vacuum:** an absolute calibration (J/m³ and duration) of the sub-vacuum intervals in Riek-type electro-optic sampling, compared with the Ford–Roman bound. A measured violation of the free-field QI would reopen the density question; satisfaction confirms the ≥ 22-order duration gap.
- **Thick-wall / Bobrick–Martire / Natário:** a calculation, not an experiment: full-observer energy conditions (Le 2026 matrix test, [D-69]) plus QI along every timelike worldline, at the reference case. My Natário scaling (Finding 7) should be reproduced independently with gr_tensors `from_adm`.
- **Van Den Broeck:** recompute region-II energy at v = 10c with high precision to settle the v⁰ vs v² dispute [U-05]. This moves its gap by 2 orders.
- **Lentz:** an independent reproduction (e.g. Warp Factory-type numerics) of the full stress tensor and all-observer WEC/NEC; also check whether a positive 38 M_sun shell at R = 100 m is inside its own horizon (Finding 13 says yes by 10^3).
- **Any warp option:** the first tabletop detection of a shift-vector (frame-dragging-like) signature from an engineered stress-energy; current interferometers cannot see even the capacitor-scale signal (Finding 5).
- **Reframe (sub-light):** laser-sail and antimatter-production milestones; outside this brief.

## What would change my mind
- A measurement showing a macroscopic volume with net negative energy (including its source) or showing that Casimir/vacuum energy gravitates differently from prediction (Archimedes).
- A peer-reviewed, independently reproduced superluminal solution satisfying the NEC and WEC for all observers with a total mass whose Schwarzschild radius is smaller than the bubble.
- An error in my Natário calculation (e.g. a Natário profile choice that avoids the R⁴/Δ³ return-flow penalty while keeping the interior flat), which would move ENGINEER-C from eliminated to strained.
- Evidence that Van Den Broeck's region II is genuinely v-independent and survives Bobrick–Martire's objection, which would keep its budget at ~1 M_sun at 10c (it would not change the null).

- World energy 592 EJ/yr (search summary) and $0.05/kWh are round anchors; conclusions do not depend on them at better than 10 orders.
