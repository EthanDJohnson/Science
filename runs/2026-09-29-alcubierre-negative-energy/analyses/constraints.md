# Analysis: constraints (Kant — audit the conditions any valid answer must satisfy)

## Method applied
List every constraint a warp-drive negative-energy option must pass (energy conditions and the theorems that use them; Ford–Roman quantum inequalities with their assumptions; conservation/thermodynamics; horizons, control and causality; semiclassical stability; tides), then compute what each candidate in the dossier requires with `gr_tensors.py` and small scripts, checking convergence (n vs 1.5n) and float64 precision. Each candidate is classed violates / strained / consistent only where the bound's assumptions hold in the regime.

## Findings
1. **Alcubierre budget reproduced.** The toolkit's 3D Eulerian slice integral (R = 1 m, σ = 8 m⁻¹, v = 1; geometric) gives E = −0.22334 m at n = 64 and n = 96 (converged to 5 digits), equal to the 1D formula −(v²/12)∫f′²r²dr = −0.22334 m; the positive part is exactly 0 (Eulerian ρ ≤ 0 everywhere) [calc: runs/2026-09-29-alcubierre-negative-energy/calc/lens-constraints_alcubierre.py]. Reference case R = 100 m, Δ_PF = 1 m (σ = 2.000 m⁻¹): E = −5.556e2 m = **−7.48e29 kg = −0.38 M_sun** at v = 1c and **−5.556e4 m = −7.48e31 kg = −37.6 M_sun = −3.9e4 M_J** at v = 10c (1D grid n and 1.5n agree to 6 digits), confirming D-16/D-17 and E = −v²R²/(18Δ).
2. **Every energy condition fails in the Alcubierre wall at every speed tested, and the matter is mostly Hawking–Ellis type IV.** Scan of 108 points covering the whole wall (R = 1, σ = 8; scale-free): v = 0.1c → NEC, WEC, SEC, DEC violated at 108/108 points, all type IV; v = 1c → all four violated at 108/108, 93 type IV + 15 type I; v = 10c → NEC 100/108, WEC 108/108, SEC 100/108, DEC 108/108, 55 type IV + 53 type I [calc: …/lens-constraints_alcubierre.py]. Type IV (no causal eigenvector of T^a_b) has no classical-fluid or classical-field realisation; it arises because the momentum flux (O(v), from ∂K) outruns density and pressure (O(v²)) at low v. This confirms D-03/D-52 (NEC violation is not a v = c threshold effect) and adds that the required stress tensor is not of the type any known source (Casimir: type I, diag(−1,1,1,−3) [D-08]) produces.
3. **Pfenning–Ford QI wall limit reproduced from the curvature radius.** Peak Eulerian density at the wall is exactly ρ_pk = −v²/(32πΔ²) (tanh, PF convention; toolkit value −0.00995 v²/Δ² = −1/(32π)), and the minimum curvature radius (Eulerian frame, over equator and axis) is exactly r_c = 1.000 Δ/v at v = 1 and 10, σ = 8 and 32. Imposing the Ford–Roman bound |⟨ρ⟩| ≤ 3L_P²/(32π²τ₀⁴) with τ₀ = α r_c gives **Δ_max = 97.7 v L_P for α = 0.1** (1.58e-33 m at 1c; 1.58e-32 m at 10c), matching PF's "≈ 10² v L_P" [D-13]. At R = 100 m that forces **E = −4.7e62 kg (v = 1c) and −4.7e63 kg = −2.4e33 M_sun (v = 10c)** for a tanh wall, matching D-18. Loosening to α = 0.01 moves Δ_max up 100× and E down 100× (still −4.7e61 kg at 10c): the conclusion is insensitive to α at the 30-orders level. Check: the Lorentzian average of ρ along an actual Eulerian geodesic crossing the wall (R = 1, σ = 32, v = 10) equals 0.985 ρ_pk at τ₀ = 0.1 r_c and 0.998 ρ_pk at 0.01 r_c, so the "sampled ≈ peak" step of PF holds [calc: …/lens-constraints_alcubierre.py].
4. **Natário-class energy identity, checked numerically.** For unit lapse and flat slices the slice-integrated Eulerian energy equals −(1/32π)∫|∇×X|² d³x plus a boundary flux that vanishes for a compactly supported shift (our derivation from ρ = (K² − K_ijK^ij)/16π). Toolkit integrals (R = 1 m, σ = 8 m⁻¹, v = 1; n = 64 and 96 agree to 5 digits): Alcubierre E = −0.22334 m = −(1/32π)∫|∇×X|² exactly; **Natário zero-expansion (same f) E = −3.288 m, 14.7× Alcubierre**, pointwise ρ ∈ [−2.00, −4e-16] m⁻² (≤ 0 everywhere, peak 12.6× Alcubierre's); **irrotational potential-flow drive X = v∇((x−vt)f): E_tot = 0.0000 m with E_− = −2.858 m and E_+ = +2.858 m**, pointwise ρ ∈ [−4.34, +7.71] m⁻² [calc: runs/2026-09-29-alcubierre-negative-energy/calc/lens-constraints_natario_energy.py]. Consequences: (a) zero expansion does not reduce the requirement — it raises it, because the curl of a divergence-free shift contains r f″ terms (see finding 5 for scaling); this contradicts the dossier's "same v²R²σ scaling" reading of Lobo–Visser for thin walls [D-04] and agrees with Rodal's 35× curvature invariants [U-10]; (b) any compactly supported zero-vorticity drive (the class Lentz and Fell–Heisenberg work in, and Rodal 2026's irrotational drive) has **exactly zero** total Eulerian energy, so "Eulerian ρ ≥ 0 everywhere" forces ρ ≡ 0, i.e. no drive, unless the shift is non-smooth or does not decay (Minkowski exterior lost). This is Santiago–Schuster–Visser's argument [D-51] made quantitative, and it matches Rodal's reported net ≈ 0 (0.04%) [U-10]. (c) For an irrotational shift β = ∇φ the boundary flux is (1/16π)∮(∇φ∇²φ − (∇φ·∇)∇φ)·dS; for the Painlevé–Gullstrand shift √(2M/r) this equals M (our derivation), so a positive total Eulerian energy in this class is exactly the ADM mass of a Schwarzschild-like exterior — consistent with Fell–Heisenberg's non-Minkowski exterior [U-07] and their positive total 1.0e27 kg [D-26].
5. **Van Den Broeck region II reproduced; its "ten Planck length" curvature radius is not an invariant.** With VdB's own profile B = α(−(n−1)wⁿ + n w^{n−1}) + 1, n = 80 [new: Van Den Broeck 1999, arXiv gr-qc/9905084, full-text, "B = α (−(n − 1)wn + nwn−1) + 1, (12) ... let us choose n = 80"], α = 1e17, R̃ = Δ̃ = 1e-15 m, the 1D mpmath integral (checked against the toolkit's 3D slice integral at α = 3, n = 4: E_− −7.8295 vs −7.8297 m) gives **E_II,− = −1.38e30 kg (−0.69 M_sun) and E_II,+ = +4.87e30 kg (2.45 M_sun)**, net +3.49e30 kg, converged between 2e4 and 3e4 points; this matches D-20/D-21. Region II is ultrastatic in comoving coordinates (K_ij = 0), so **E_II is independent of v** (supporting Bobrick–Martire's observation that it lacks v dependence [U-05]). The orthonormal radial Ricci component reaches 3R_r̂r̂ = −3.8e33 m⁻², so ρ + p_r = 3R_r̂r̂/8π < 0: **radial NEC violated** (a flare-out neck, as for a wormhole throat). The invariant (orthonormal) curvature radius there is ≈ 1.6e-17 m ≈ 1e18 L_P, and the peak |ρ| is 1.8e32 m⁻² = 2.4e59 kg/m³ [calc: runs/2026-09-29-alcubierre-negative-energy/calc/lens-constraints_vdb.py]. VdB's paper states the minimum curvature radius is "about ten Planck lengths" computed after the transformation to "gµν = diag(−1, B 2, B 2, B 2)" [new: Van Den Broeck 1999, full-text, "The minimum curvature radius is determined by the largest component of the Riemann tensor"]; see finding 6 for our reconstruction of the discrepancy. Region IV (outer Alcubierre wall, R = 3e-15 m, QI-limited Δ = 97.7 v L_P, tanh): **E_IV = −4.3e29 kg at 1c and −4.3e30 kg (−2.1 M_sun) at 10c** (PF linear ramp ×1.5: −6.4e30 kg, matching D-22's 6.3e29 v_s kg).
6. **Van Den Broeck's QI pass depends on a non-invariant curvature measure (our reconstruction).** In region II the largest *orthonormal* sectional curvature is 1.88e33 m⁻² (at r = 1.426e-15 m, B = 1.32), so the invariant minimum curvature radius is **2.3e-17 m ≈ 1.4e18 L_P**; the toolkit's `curvature_at` gives the same 1.8824e33 m⁻² and 2.305e-17 m, and `precision_check` on ρ shows relative error 2e-14 with no sign flips. The largest *Cartesian all-lower coordinate* component B⁴|K| is 6.3e67 m⁻² (at the pocket edge, B = 1e17), giving "r_c" = 1.26e-34 m = **7.8 L_P — which reproduces VdB's "about ten Planck lengths"** [calc: runs/2026-09-29-alcubierre-negative-energy/calc/lens-constraints_vdb_curvature.py]. With τ₀ = 0.1 r_c the Ford–Roman bound is −1.3e95 kg/m³ using the coordinate value (QI "satisfied", as VdB and D-24 report) but **−1.2e26 kg/m³ using the invariant value, against a required peak |ρ| = 2.4e59 kg/m³: violated by ≈ 2e33**. The throat (minimum of the area radius B·r) sits at r = 1.456e-15 m with area radius 1.46e-15 m, where ρ + p_r = −7.6e30 m⁻² (NEC violated, flare-out). That VdB used the coordinate component is our inference from the numerical match (7.8 vs "about ten" L_P), not a statement in the paper; if correct, VdB's n = 80 geometry fails the same free-field QI that sets the Alcubierre wall, and a region II that did satisfy it would need invariant curvature radii of O(10) L_P — outside semiclassical validity.
7. **Irrotational drive: Eulerian-positive points still violate the NEC.** Scan of 11 wall points (R = 1, σ = 8, v = 1, full 4D Einstein tensor): all Hawking–Ellis type I; NEC, WEC, SEC and DEC violated at 11/11, **including all 6 points where the Eulerian density is positive** (e.g. ρ_Eul = +3.50 m⁻² with sampled ρ+p minimum −33.7 m⁻² and some observer measuring −1.66e3 m⁻²) [calc: runs/2026-09-29-alcubierre-negative-energy/calc/lens-constraints_irrot_ec.py]. This is the Santiago et al. counterexample [D-06] realised in an explicit zero-vorticity drive, and the type-I result agrees with Rodal 2026's "globally type I" irrotational drive [D-64].
