# Analysis: constraints (Kant — audit the conditions any valid answer must satisfy)

## Method applied
List every constraint a warp-drive negative-energy option must pass (energy conditions and the theorems that use them; Ford–Roman quantum inequalities with their assumptions; conservation/thermodynamics; horizons, control and causality; semiclassical stability; tides), then compute what each candidate in the dossier requires with `gr_tensors.py` and small scripts, checking convergence (n vs 1.5n) and float64 precision. Each candidate is classed violates / strained / consistent only where the bound's assumptions hold in the regime.

19. **General form of the QI wall limit: Planck-scale curvature is forced, for any design.** If the wall's peak |ρ| is a fixed fraction of its curvature, |ρ_pk| = κ_ρ/r_c² (Einstein's equation makes this generic), the Ford–Roman bound with τ₀ = α r_c becomes **r_c ≤ √(3/(32π²κ_ρ)) L_P/α²** (our derivation), independent of R and v. Sampling times are then τ₀ = α r_c ≈ 10 L_P and 2.4 L_P. ### C. Validity domains of the models the dossier relies on
- **Classical GR with prescribed metric ("metric engineering").** Valid for any metric; it says nothing about whether the implied T_μν can be sourced. All energy numbers above are of this kind.
- **Eulerian slice budget.** Slicing-dependent: changes of lapse (finding 16), flattening, or irrotational shifts (finding 4) change E_Eul without removing NEC violation. It is not an invariant of the spacetime.
- **Ford–Roman QI.** Free fields in Minkowski; transferred to the wall with τ₀ = α r_c. In this regime τ₀ ≈ 2–10 L_P at the limit (finding 19), so the transfer is at the edge of its domain. It is fully in its domain for the lab sources (Casimir, squeezed light: r_c of lab spacetime ~1e11 m).
- **Semiclassical gravity.** Needs r_c ≫ L_P and small fluctuations of ⟨T_μν⟩. QI-limited walls: r_c ≈ 24–98 L_P (marginal); VdB region II r_c = 2.3e-17 m (inside); reference Δ = 1 m walls (deep inside).
- **Hiscock / Finazzi instability.** 2D reductions with a single free field; 4D behaviour unknown [D-61]. Our e-fold counts assume the 2D growth rate κ carries over.
- **Test-particle and tidal calculations.** No backreaction; stationary bubble; no acceleration phase.
- **Lobo–Visser ship bound.** Linearised, v ≪ 1; not valid at 10c.
