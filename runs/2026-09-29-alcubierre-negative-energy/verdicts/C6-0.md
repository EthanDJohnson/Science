# Verdict on C6 (refuter 0, angle: evidence, mainly the reframe's own sources, backed by a physics calculation and an internal inconsistency)
verdict: weakened
basis: cited-evidence

## Strongest attack
C6 makes four claims:
1. At 10c, 90–97% of the Eulerian negative energy lies where no bubble-comoving timelike source can exist.
2. The ship cannot create, steer or stop the bubble.
3. So the only coherent option is a route pre-laid by earlier subluminal agents.
4. That route "shortens only Earth-timed round trips". The obstacle is "causal, not quantitative".

Claims 1–3 survive my attack; I reproduced them. The headline ranking fails, and so does the closing restriction. The overlap is also overstated about twofold.

**A. "Shortens only Earth-timed round trips" is contradicted by the source C6 cites for the control problem (cited evidence).**
- The round-trip-only property belongs to the Krasnikov tube. That is a metric changed *only in the causal future of the traveller's own launch*.
- Everett & Roman, the source behind [D-58], say plainly that a *pre-laid Alcubierre bubble* gives a superluminal *one-way* journey to a passenger who boards it:
  > "The foregoing discussion does not mean that Alcubierre bubbles, if it were possible to create them, could not be used as a means of superluminal travel. It only means that the actions required to change the metric and create the bubble must be taken beforehand by some observer whose forward light cone contains the entire trajectory of the bubble. Suppose space has been warped to create a bubble travelling from the Earth to some distant star, e. g., Deneb, at superluminal speed. A spaceship appropriately located with respect to the bubble trajectory could then choose to enter the bubble, rather like a passenger catching a passing trolley car, and thus make the superluminal journey."

  [Everett & Roman 1997, arXiv gr-qc/9702049, p. 3, ACCESS: full-text via fetch_text]
- They confine the one-way limit to Krasnikov's construction: "the time for a one-way trip to a distant star is limited by all the usual restrictions of special relativity, but the time for a round trip may be made arbitrarily short. In four dimensions this entails the creation of a "tube" during the outbound flight of the spaceship" (p. 18).
- Krasnikov's own limit is also about a traveller who "can partly control the geometry of his world": "under some reasonable assumptions in globally hyperbolic spacetimes the traveller cannot hasten reaching the destination" [Krasnikov 1998, PRD 57 4760, ACCESS: abstract via lit_search]. It limits a self-built route. It does not limit a passenger on a track that someone else laid earlier.
- **Consequence.** Once a pre-laid Alcubierre track exists, a passenger leaving Earth at t₀ arrives at t₀ + D/v on Earth-frame clocks: a one-way superluminal trip. What is not shortened is the *first* arrival of anything from Earth, because the builders travel subluminally. C6 carries [D-59]'s Krasnikov-tube property over to all "route infrastructure". That is a misreading of its own sources.

**B. "Causal, not quantitative" contradicts C6's own content and the numbers (internal inconsistency plus calculation).**
- C6 says the causal obstacle has a coherent workaround, pre-laying. It also concedes, under evidence against, that "A pre-laid route still needs QI-thin walls [D-59]".
- An obstacle with a workaround cannot be the binding one while an obstacle without a workaround remains. Once the track is pre-laid, the binding constraint is again the quantitative one: the supply, the quantum inequality (QI) and the energy.

  - So the quantitative obstacle is necessary and sufficient to rule out the drive. The causal obstacle is an *additional*, independent one that applies at v > 1. It is not the binding one "instead of" the quantitative.

**C. The causally inaccessible share is about half of what the phrasing implies (calculation).**
- The 90–97% region (f < 1 − 1/v) splits equally between the front and rear walls, because the Eulerian angular weight (y² + z²)/r² is even in x.
- A photon sent backward from the ship's centre crosses the rear horizon and leaves through the rear wall: at v = 10c it reaches ξ = −120 m, f = 0, by t = 101 m (geometric). A forward photon stalls at f = 0.900, the front horizon.
- So the rear 48.6% (tanh), or 45% (linear, Bobrick–Martire optimal), lies in the ship's causal future. No comoving timelike worldline can hold it, but the ship could in principle feed it with a steady rearward outflow, whose stationary pattern sits in the bubble frame like an exhaust plume.
- Only the front half, 45–48.6% at 10c, lies strictly where "the ship cannot" act. Even so, one causally unreachable front wall is enough to block creating the bubble on demand. That part of C6 stands.

## In principle vs. in practice
- **In principle (GR + semiclassical QFT).**
  - The causal facts survive:
    - The horizon at f = 1 − 1/v exists for every v > 1.
    - No comoving timelike source exists at f < 1 − 1/v.
    - The front wall is outside the ship's causal future, so the ship cannot create the bubble on demand [D-58].
  - A pre-laid track is causally coherent and allows **one-way** superluminal passenger trips after it is built. That goes beyond Earth-timed round trips (Everett & Roman p. 3).
  - The track does not remove the supply problem. It still needs the full wall stress-energy, at the free-field QI thickness about −4.7e63 kg at 10c, and closed timelike curves become possible with multiple tracks or tubes [D-60]. Chronology protection is a conjecture.
- **In practice (2100).**
  - Pre-laying needs subluminal agents spread along the whole 4.37 ly route, with timed exotic sources at every point. That is TRL 1 or below.
  - The binding constraint in practice is the same quantitative gap as in C1: 60–95 orders of magnitude in energy. It is not causality.

## What survives and remaining doubt
- **What survives, in narrower form:** "At v > c, in addition to (not instead of) the quantitative supply problem, a horizon at f = 1 − 1/v places 90–97% of the Eulerian E_− (at 10c) outside any comoving timelike source."
  - About half of that, the front wall, is outside the ship's causal future. So the bubble cannot be created, steered or stopped from inside, and superluminal use requires a track laid in advance by earlier subluminal agents.
  - Such a track shortens one-way trips for passengers who depart after it is built. It does not speed up the first arrival from Earth. Only self-built Krasnikov tubes are limited to Earth-timed round trips.
- **The decisive test in C6** (the comoving fraction for Natário and Loup-lapse metrics) is not affected by my attack. I did not run it.
  - For Natário the wall shift deviates from v e_x by about vR/Δ, which would raise the fraction, not lower it (my estimate, not computed).
  - A lapse above 1 in the wall could move the threshold. That is untested.
- **Remaining doubts:**
  - Whether a steady rearward outflow could in fact reproduce the rear-wall stress tensor. The constraints lens reports it to be mostly Hawking–Ellis type IV, with no timelike eigenvector. So point C is a causal-reach correction, not a demonstrated source.
  - Whether a passenger can board a passing 10c bubble and leave it at the destination, given the tidal forces in the wall and the rear-only exit. I did not compute this, and it may narrow the "one-way" point in practice.
