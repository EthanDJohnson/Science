## Summary
22 rows: 17 verified, 1 misattributed, 3 unverifiable, 0 contradicted. All 17 verbatim quotes are exact. The most serious problem is Q15. The verdict's key step, that Fell–Heisenberg's positive total is an "ADM mass" boundary flux in SSV's eq. 7.16/7.17, is not in any of the three sources: SSV's 7.17 assumes strong fall-off, and FH attribute the absence of negative energy only to "non-truncation of the gravitational field". The verdict labels the delta-squared stresses and the kink-plane argument as its own inference. Several framing readings are also the verdict's own: that "net shift zero" means localized, and that SSV's thin-wall appendix applies to Lentz. The journal references (Q7, Q16, Q22) were not confirmed from the arXiv pages fetched.
