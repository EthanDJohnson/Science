status: final
Scope: only what the verdict says its sources say. All quotes are from Van Den Broeck, "A 'warp drive' with more reasonable total energy requirements", CQG 16 (1999) 3973, arXiv gr-qc/9905084, checked with fetch_text.py on the v1, v3, v4 and v5 PDFs and the arXiv abstract page. The verdict's calculations (mpmath curvatures, violation factors 2e33 and 7e32, the 3.7e-32 m threshold) were not checked. [D-24], [D-35], the C3 claim text, and the Bobrick–Martire B(0)²v² objection are cited without quoted wording and were not checked.

## Summary
Eleven rows verified, one unverifiable (Q10, the wording that v5 "matches" the published CQG text), none contradicted or misattributed. The central claim holds: v1, v3 and v4 use the Planck-scale numbers, v5 uses the femtometre numbers, and v5's QI section still prints the Planck-scale values (6.6e93 and 9.2e94 kg/m³, r_c = 1.4e-34 m). The one point to carry forward is that the "reason for the rescaling" and "v5 matches the published paper" are the verdict's inferences.
