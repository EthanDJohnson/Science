# Verdict on C4 (refuter 0, angle: physics, a calculation on Lentz's own construction, backed by cited evidence)
verdict: refuted
basis: calculation

## Strongest attack
C4 claims that Lentz's plasma soliton and Fell–Heisenberg's Eulerian-positive subclass "remove the need for negative energy altogether". Both constructions sit inside the generic Natário class: unit lapse, flat slices, and a gradient shift N_i = ∂_iφ. Santiago–Schuster–Visser (SSV) place them there explicitly. Within that class, neither claim survives.

**1. Lentz: the positive total he books is cancelled exactly by energy on his kink planes (calculation).**
- **His set-up, verbatim.** "hypersurfaces parameterized by ﬂat metrics under Cartesian coordinates hij = δij"; "a shift vector potential function ... Ni = ∂iφ"; φ and ρ "parameterized in the (x, y) coordinates by the l1 norm s = |x| + |y|"; and "The net shift vector of the soliton is found to be zero". The last point means the shift is localized.
- **The identity.** For a gradient shift, 16πE = (∇²φ)² − |∂∂φ|² = ∂_i(∂_iφ ∇²φ − ∂_jφ ∂_i∂_jφ). This is SSV eq. 4.4 with zero vorticity. Its integral over a slice is zero for a localized shift.
- **Lentz's reply** [D-40] is that the identity "does not hold" because his E is non-smooth at x = 0 and y = 0. I tested that reply directly.
  - With φ = g(z, |x| + |y|), the shift component N_x = sign(x) g_s jumps across the whole x = 0 plane wherever g_s ≠ 0. N_y does the same across y = 0.
  - Expanding Lentz's own eq. 7 splits the Eulerian density into three parts:
    - a smooth part, E_ac = (1/4π)(g_ss g_zz − g_zs²);
    - sheets on x = 0 and on y = 0, each (1/4π) g_s(g_ss + g_zz) δ(x) (and likewise δ(y));
    - a line on the z-axis, (1/2π) g_s² δ(x)δ(y), which is never negative.
  - For three test potentials, the smooth part plus sheets plus line sums to zero, to a relative 1e-5 or better [calc: runs/2026-09-29-alcubierre-negative-energy/calc/falsifier-C4-0_lentz_sheets.py].
  - Any nonzero smooth-part total is therefore balanced exactly by the kink-plane terms.
- **Consequence.** Lentz reports a positive E_tot ~ C v²R²/w, (few) × 0.1 M_sun at R = 100 m and w = 1 m [U-06]. That is the smooth part E_ac. His non-smoothness removes nothing. It relocates an equal and opposite amount of energy onto the x = 0 and y = 0 planes and the axis. The line term is ≥ 0, so if E_ac ≥ 0 everywhere, as he claims, the sheets must carry negative Eulerian energy of at least −E_tot. The WEC then fails even for the Eulerian observers that the positivity claim is built on.
- **The only way to avoid the sheets is to smooth the planes.** The shift is then smooth and localized, SSV's eq. 7.14 (∫ρ d³x = 0) applies directly, and "positive energy density anywhere" forces "negative energy density somewhere else".

**2. Lentz's l1 construction is not a valid thin-shell spacetime.**
- The shift component g_{0x} is discontinuous across x = 0, so the metric itself is discontinuous there.
- SSV spell out what that means: "A discontinuity in the metric leads to delta functions in the Christoﬀel symbols, squares of delta functions in the Riemann tensor". The junction formalism needs the metric to be C¹⁻.
- The Eulerian density E happens to contain only δ × (bounded) products, so it is well defined. The spatial stresses T_ij contain K K_ij and (K²)_ij terms (SSV eq. 4.9), and with K_xx ∋ 2g_s δ(x) these give δ(x)². So the stresses the plasma must supply are undefined on the very planes where Lentz places his escape.
- This is my inference from SSV eq. 4.9 and the explicit form of N_x. It is not a published result.

**3. Fell–Heisenberg: the positive total is an ADM mass, the NEC still fails, and "superluminal" is used in a weaker sense.**
- **Why the total is positive.**
  - Their exterior is "Schwarzschild-like". They write: "This non-truncation of the gravitational ﬁeld outside the warping region is likely the reason why negative energies are absent".
  - So their positive total is the boundary flux in SSV's eq. 7.16/7.17. It is not evidence that no exotic stress is needed.
- **The NEC fails anyway.** SSV's NEC proof does not need a zero ADM mass: "It is, therefore, proven that any warp drive in the generic Natário form will inevitably violate the NEC as it moves through spacetime. Note that this proof for NEC violations uses only the fact that warp drive contributions are suﬃciently localized — without any need to assume zero ADM mass or to invoke an integration by parts."
- **Their "superluminal" is not the brief's.** Fell–Heisenberg define "a shift vector ﬁeld satisfying N·N > N² is called superluminal due to the fact g(∂t,∂t) > 0", and they take the shift "to be time-independent". That criterion makes a region an ergoregion. It is met, for example, inside the horizon of Schwarzschild in Painlevé–Gullstrand form. It does not show that a bubble carries a ship between two points at rest in the exterior faster than light, which is the brief's definition. Their 1.26c central shift is exactly this N·N > 1 statement.
- **They expect a black hole.** For their own example they write: "More than likely, any attempt to construct this speciﬁc conﬁguration will form a black hole."

**Attacks I tried that failed or that I did not rely on:**
- **Celmaster–Rubin 2025** [D-40]. lit_search found no record. I did not use it.

## In principle vs. in practice
- **In principle (classical GR):**
  - Lentz's claim fails on his own terms. Eulerian energy integrates to exactly zero for his localized gradient shift, including the distributional kink terms. His booked positive E_tot is therefore matched by negative Eulerian energy on the x = 0 and y = 0 planes, and his discontinuous shift makes the stresses ill-defined there.
  - Fell–Heisenberg avoid the zero-integral argument only by carrying an ADM mass, and SSV's NEC proof covers that case.
  - Neither drive removes the need for NEC/WEC violation. Olum's WEC theorem [D-50] stands, and no counterexample has been shown.
- **In practice (by 2100):**
  - Even granting positivity, Lentz's budget at R = 100 m, w = 1 m and 10c is of order tens of M_sun.
  - The candidate's own figure, +7.5e31 kg, has 2GM/c² = 1.1e5 m ≈ 1.1e3 R. The bubble would sit inside its own Schwarzschild radius (C4's self-gravity entry).
  - Fell–Heisenberg themselves expect their 1.0e27 kg example to collapse to a black hole.
  - TRL 0–1. No mechanism exists to assemble or steer the plasma source, which lies beyond the ship's horizon.

## What survives and remaining doubt
- **What survives.** Only the narrow mathematical statement that the smooth part of the Eulerian density can be made non-negative away from a set of measure zero (Lentz), or everywhere once an ADM mass is allowed (Fell–Heisenberg). Neither version gives NEC/WEC satisfaction for all observers, a well-defined source, or superluminal transport in the brief's sense.
- **Remaining doubts:**
  - I did not reproduce Lentz's actual rhomboid source, but the identity holds for every g(z, |x| + |y|).
  - The claim that the stresses contain δ² is my inference from SSV eq. 4.9 and has not been computed explicitly.
  - SSV's NEC proof is general. I did not run a Le-type matrix-inequality test on Fell–Heisenberg's specific configuration, whose length unit is also not stated [U-07].
  - The Celmaster–Rubin preprint could not be retrieved.
