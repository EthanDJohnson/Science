## Strongest attack
C1 is a categorical impossibility claim: "No option supplies or avoids the negative energy ... within established physics (GR + semiclassical QFT)". Its **supply half** rests on one premise, stated in its argument: "The only demonstrated sources are free fields, and the proven quantum inequality (QI) binds them." From that premise it forces the wall to Δ ≤ 97.7 v L_P and gets −4.7e63 kg. The premise is false for established physics, and the literature says so directly.

1. **The QIs are not merely unproven for interacting fields. They are known to be false.** Olum & Graham (2003, Phys. Lett. B 554, 175) show that a renormalized, self-consistent interacting QFT (a domain wall coupled to a scalar) has *static* negative energy density, which violates the QIs and the AWEC. They add that "the quantum inequalities are simply not correct in the case of interacting fields". C1 lists this possibility only as an "evidence against" hedge ("proven only for free fields", [U-09]). It is actually a demonstrated counterexample, so the free-field QI cannot bind "every" established-physics source.
2. **The main demonstrated source, Casimir, is not bound by the free-field QI either.** The same paper says: "the Casimir effect violates the averaged weak energy condition and the quantum inequalities". C1's statement that the demonstrated sources are free fields bound by the proven QI is therefore wrong about Casimir, which is the source C5 tests. Casimir is killed by plate mass (C5), not by the QI.
3. **The fallback bound is a conjecture under the brief's rules.** Once the QI is gone, the remaining in-principle bar is the ANEC. Olum & Graham note that their system obeys the ANEC, "which is sufficient to rule out superluminal travel". However:
   - The ANEC is proven only in Minkowski space (C1's own citation of Faulkner et al. 2016).
   - In curved spacetime it "is violated by quantum fields" (Graham & Olum 2007).
   - The achronal ANEC is violated too, and "can be made as large as desired", for a conformally coupled scalar on a fixed conformally flat background (Urban & Olum 2010).
   - What survives is only the *self-consistent* achronal ANEC, which Graham & Olum present as a condition "free of known violations". That is a conjecture. The brief allows it only when it is labelled as one, and conjectures cannot carry an "established physics" verdict.
4. **The density itself is within reach of known QFT scales (calculation).** The Δ = 1 m, 10c wall needs a peak of |ρ| = 1.2e44 J/m³ (C5's figure). An interacting-field static negative density of the form −ε(Mc²)⁴/(ħc)³ reaches that at Mc² ≈ 87–870 GeV for ε = 1e-1 to 1e-5, the electroweak scale [calc: runs/2026-09-29-alcubierre-negative-energy/calc/falsifier-C1-0_checks.py]. So the required *density* is not beyond established QFT. What is missing is a configuration that is net-negative over a 1 m wall made of about 1e18 Compton-length layers, while its positive-energy cores are kept out of the budget. No theorem of established physics forbids that configuration; only the conjectured self-consistent achronal ANEC and the empirical pattern of net positivity (Casimir plates, domain-wall cores) argue against it.

**Net effect.** C1's in-principle "no" survives as "no known option, and excluded if the self-consistent achronal ANEC (a conjecture) holds". It does not survive as "excluded by established physics". C1's own "Status of the verdict" half-concedes this, but its headline claim and its supply argument do not. Its falsification clause ("a proven QEI permits metre-scale walls") is also mis-specified. For interacting fields the relevant outcome is not a looser QEI but *no QEI at all*, and that is already the literature's position.

## Evidence
- **Olum & Graham 2003**, "Static negative energies near a domain wall", Phys. Lett. B 554, 175, arXiv gr-qc/0205134. ACCESS: full-text via fetch_text.
  - Abstract: "This system provides a simple, explicit example of violation of the averaged weak energy condition and the quantum inequalities by interacting quantum fields. Unlike idealized systems with boundary conditions or external background fields, this calculation is implemented precisely in renormalized quantum field theory with the energy necessary to support the background field included self-consistently."
  - p. 2: "Since the negative energy density in the Casimir effect is static, it can be averaged for arbitrarily long times. Thus the Casimir effect violates the averaged weak energy condition and the quantum inequalities."
  - p. 5: "the averaged weak energy condition and the quantum inequalities are simply not correct in the case of interacting fields, so the failure of attempts to prove them is not due merely to technical reasons. The present system does, however, satisfy the averaged null energy condition ... which is sufficient to rule out superluminal travel and the construction of time machines."
- **Graham & Olum 2007**, "Achronal averaged null energy condition", Phys. Rev. D 76, 064001, arXiv 0705.3193. ACCESS: abstract. "This condition is sufficient to prove many important theorems in general relativity, but it is violated by quantum fields in curved spacetime. However there is a weaker condition, which is free of known violations, requiring only that there is no self-consistent space-time in semiclassical gravity in which ANEC is violated on a complete, achronal null geodesic."
- **Urban & Olum 2010**, "Averaged null energy condition violation in a conformally flat spacetime", Phys. Rev. D 81, 024039, arXiv 0910.5925. ACCESS: abstract. "The violation is dependent on the quantum state and can be made as large as desired. ... Since all geodesics in conformally flat spacetimes are achronal, the achronal averaged null energy condition is likewise violated."

## In principle vs. in practice
- **In principle (GR + semiclassical QFT):**
  - The *need* for NEC/WEC violation at v > c is solidly theorem-backed (Olum 1998; Santiago–Schuster–Visser 2022) and survives.
  - The *supply* exclusion does not follow from established physics. The free-field QI demonstrably does not govern interacting fields or Casimir systems (Olum & Graham 2003).
  - The only bound left that would exclude a superluminal bubble is the self-consistent achronal ANEC, which is a conjecture ("free of known violations", not proven). The ANEC and achronal ANEC on fixed curved backgrounds are violated (Urban & Olum 2010).
  - Correct status: "not shown possible; excluded conditional on a conjecture". It is not "impossible within established physics".
- **In practice (by 2100):** C1 survives intact.
  - Even with the QI dropped entirely, the GR floor for any profile at R = 100 m and 10c is 1.12e30 kg (0.56 M_sun) of negative energy, 10^26.2 world-years of primary energy.
  - Every demonstrated negative-energy system is net positive once its source is included (C5).
  - Horizons block creation and steering [D-58], and no self-accelerating solution is known [D-62].
  - TRL ≤ 1 for the negative-energy supply at warp densities and scales is consistent with the evidence. I found no counter-case.

## What survives and remaining doubt
- **Surviving form of C1:**
  - (a) Every superluminal Alcubierre-type metric needs NEC/WEC-violating stress-energy (theorem-backed).
  - (b) No known established-physics source supplies it at the reference scale. The free-field QI excludes free-field and squeezed-state sources (walls ≤ 1e2 v L_P → about 1e63 kg). Casimir and interacting-field sources escape the QI but are net positive once their sources are included. Complete exclusion rests on the *conjectured* self-consistent achronal ANEC.
  - (c) Nothing is buildable by 2100: the in-practice half stands by at least 26 orders of magnitude in energy.
- **Remaining doubt:**
  - Whether a superluminal Alcubierre bubble necessarily contains a complete *achronal* null geodesic with negative ANEC integral, so that the Graham–Olum conjecture applies, is my inference plus Olum–Graham's one-line statement, not a checked theorem for this metric.
  - The ε scaling in the calculation is dimensional, not taken from Olum–Graham's numbers.
