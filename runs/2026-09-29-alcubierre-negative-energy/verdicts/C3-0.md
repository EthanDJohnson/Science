# Verdict on C3 (refuter 0, angle: physics, with a supporting source-version check)
verdict: refuted
basis: calculation

## Strongest attack
C3 claims a specific configuration: α = 1e17, R̃ = Δ̃ = 1e-15 m and outer bubble R = 3e-15 m (Van Den Broeck's published eq. 7). It says this configuration gives about −5.7e30 kg at 10c **and** satisfies the free-field quantum inequality (QI). The QI part rests only on Van Den Broeck's "amply satisfied" [D-24]. **That check was not done for these parameters.** It is left over from earlier arXiv versions that used a Planck-scale pocket (α = 1e34, R̃ = Δ̃ = 1e-32 m). For the femtometre parameters C3 states, the QI is violated by about 2e33.

1. **The QI numbers in the paper belong to different parameters (source-version check).**
   - arXiv v1, v3 and v4 of gr-qc/9905084 all read: "α = 10 34, ˜∆ = 10 −32 m, ˜R = 10 −32 m R = 3 × 10−32 m. (7)"
   - v5, the version that matches CQG 16, 3973, reads: "α = 10 17, ˜∆ = 10 −15 m, ˜R = 10 −15 m, R = 3 × 10−15 m. (7)"
   - v5 still prints the old QI results: a density peak "at w = 0. 349", the curvature radius "rc,min = ... = ˜∆ 72. 5 = 1 . 4 × 10−34 m", and "about −6. 6 × 1093 kg/ m3 ... −9. 2 × 1094 kg/ m3. We conclude that the QI is amply satisfied."
   - This is internally inconsistent in v5 itself: with v5's own Δ̃ = 1e-15 m, Δ̃/72.5 = 1.4e-17 m, not 1.4e-34 m.
   - v5 explains why eq. 7 changed: "The value of R in (7) is roughly the largest that keeps |EIV| below a solar mass for vs ≃ 1". The parameters were rescaled, but the QI section was never redone.
2. **Independent recomputation (calculation).** I computed Van Den Broeck's eqs 11 and 19 and the conformally flat sectional curvatures directly with mpmath at 60 digits and 60,000 cells [calc: runs/2026-09-29-alcubierre-negative-energy/calc/falsifier-C3-0_vdb_qi.py].
   - **C3's stated parameters (α = 1e17, n = 80, R̃ = Δ̃ = 1e-15 m):**
     - The density peak is at w = 0.576, with ρ = −176/Δ̃² = −2.37e59 kg/m³ (SI). It is not at w = 0.349.
     - The largest orthonormal sectional curvature is 1.88e33 m⁻² (geometric), so r_c = 2.3e-17 m = Δ̃/43.4 = 1.4e18 L_P.
     - The Ford–Roman bound with τ₀ = 0.1 r_c is −1.18e26 kg/m³. **The QI is VIOLATED by a factor of 2.0e33.**
   - **v1–v4 parameters (α = 1e34, R̃ = Δ̃ = 1e-32 m):** my code gives the peak at w = 0.349 with ρ = −492/Δ̃² = −6.63e93 kg/m³, r_c = Δ̃/72.46 = 1.38e-34 m, and a bound of −9.21e94 kg/m³. These match the paper's printed values exactly, which confirms where they came from.
   - **Van Den Broeck's printed formulas with his stated Δ̃ = 1e-15 m:** LHS −6.6e59 kg/m³, RHS −9.2e26 kg/m³, violated by 7e32.
   - This agrees with the constraints lens [calc: runs/2026-09-29-alcubierre-negative-energy/calc/lens-constraints_vdb_curvature.py]: an invariant r_c of 2.3e-17 m, confirmed by toolkit `curvature_at` and `precision_check` (relative error 2e-14). It also settles the "unresolved density discrepancy" (6.6e93 vs 2.4e59 kg/m³) that C3 lists. The two figures are the same profile at two different scales. Neither is a coordinate-versus-orthonormal artefact of the femtometre case: Van Den Broeck's eq. 19 is already orthonormal.
3. **C3's decisive test gives the eliminating branch.** C3 says a largest orthonormal Riemann component of "About 1.9e33 m⁻² (geometric) eliminates the QI pass". The computed value is 1.882e33 m⁻².
4. **The QI and "few solar masses" cannot both hold at femtometre scale (scaling argument).**
   - For this profile family, the peak density goes as k_ρ/Δ̃² and r_c = Δ̃/k_c.
   - With τ₀ = 0.1 r_c, the QI passes only if Δ̃ < 3.7e-32 m (2.3e3 L_P), i.e. r_c < 32 L_P (k_ρ = 490, k_c = 72.5; same calc).
   - So no femtometre-scale region II passes. The QI forces the pocket neck down to the Planck scale.

**Attacks tried that did not bite:**
- **Region-II energies.** E_II,− = −1.38e30 kg (0.69 M_sun) and E_II,+ = +4.87e30 kg reproduce C3's and Van Den Broeck's numbers.
- **Invariance of E_II.** E_II is the same to four digits under (α, R̃, Δ̃) → (1e17·α, R̃/1e17, Δ̃/1e17) at a fixed pocket radius αR̃ = 100 m, as Van Den Broeck says ("these energies do not vary much with α if ˜R = ˜∆ and α ˜R = 100 m").
- **v-independence.** E_II does not depend on v, because region II is ultrastatic in comoving coordinates. I did not dispute this.

## In principle vs. in practice
- **In principle (GR + semiclassical QFT, with the QIs admissible "where their assumptions hold", per the brief):**
  - **As stated (femtometre neck, α = 1e17):** excluded by the free-field QI, which it violates by about 2e33. The −5.7e30 kg figure is therefore not a QI-consistent budget.
  - **Planck-scale variant (α ≈ 1e34, Δ̃ = R̃ ≈ 1e-32 m, i.e. Van Den Broeck's v1–v4):**
    - It formally passes the QI by a factor of 14.
    - This requires r_c = 8.5 L_P and a sampling time τ₀ = 0.85 L_P.
    - At that scale the curved-space QI (a flat-space bound applied over τ₀ ≪ r_c) and semiclassical gravity are not established. The brief admits the QIs only where their assumptions hold, so the "pass" is not an established-physics result.
    - Shrinking Δ̃ further to get a bigger margin pushes r_c below L_P (0.83 L_P at 1e-33 m).
  - The free-field QI therefore cannot be satisfied inside the regime where it is trusted, at any scale of this profile family: passing needs r_c < 32 L_P.
- **In practice:**
  - Either version needs about 1.4e30 kg (0.7 M_sun) of negative energy in region II, paired with about 4.9e30 kg of positive energy.
  - That energy has to be concentrated at densities of 1e59 to 1e94 kg/m³ (SI) on femtometre-to-Planck scales.
  - This is 10^61 or more times any demonstrated Casimir energy [D-35]. Nothing buildable this century approaches it.

## What survives and remaining doubt
- **What survives:** the geometric statement. A Van Den Broeck pocket with αR̃ = 100 m needs E_II,− ≈ −1.4e30 kg and E_II,+ ≈ +4.9e30 kg, independent of v and nearly independent of α when R̃ = Δ̃. That is far below the PF Alcubierre figure.
- **What does not survive:** "while satisfying the free-field quantum inequality" for the stated femtometre configuration. That configuration's own QI check was never performed in the published paper and fails by about 2e33.
- **The only QI-passing version** puts the neck at about 1e-32 m and the curvature at about 10 L_P or less. There the check is formal, not physical, and C3's own decisive test concedes this regime is "outside semiclassical validity".
- **Remaining doubts:**
  - τ₀ = 0.1 r_c is Van Den Broeck's choice. The bound scales as β⁻⁴, so the femtometre violation factor is 2e33 × (β/0.1)⁴.
    - That factor stays above 1 for every β > 5e-10.
    - The QI must hold for *all* sampling times in its range of validity (τ₀ ≪ r_c), so failing at τ₀ = 0.1 r_c (and equally at 0.01 r_c, by 2e29) is already a violation. Picking a tiny β does not rescue it.
    - Going the other way, β = 1 tightens the Planck-variant condition to r_c < 0.3 L_P.
  - The QI was checked only for Eulerian observers. Boosted observers generally make the bound harder to satisfy, not easier.
  - I did not examine Bobrick–Martire's B(0)²v² objection. It would only add to the energy.
  - At 10c, the outer wall adds E_IV of about −4.3e30 kg (C3's tanh figure) only in the femtometre version. In the Planck version E_IV is negligible, but the outer bubble (R ≈ 3e-32 m) is then barely thicker than its own QI-limited wall (about 100 v L_P ≈ 1.6e-32 m at 10c), so the "bubble" geometry itself degenerates.
