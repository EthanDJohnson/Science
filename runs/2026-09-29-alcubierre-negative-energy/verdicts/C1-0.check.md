## Summary
12 rows, all verified. One minor wording slip (Q4: a shortened quote inside quotation marks). One framing nuance (Q3: the paper's "not restricted by any quantum inequality" holds in its coupled-fields view of Casimir, and it also describes a view in which difference QIs apply). Nothing contradicted. The verdict's central source claims hold up, but Olum & Graham's system is a 2+1-dimensional toy model, so it does not show that a net-negative wall of warp scale can exist.
