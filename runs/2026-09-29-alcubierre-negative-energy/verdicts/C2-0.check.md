| Q4 | F&H 2005 abstract: "We present rigorous QEI bounds for a class of interacting quantum fields, namely the unitary, positive energy conformal field theories ... on two-dimensional Minkowski space." | Fewster & Hollands, math-ph/0412028 abstract (fetch_text) | verified | Exact wording, ellipsis only for "(with stress-energy tensor)". The verdict does not say so beyond the quote, and in the body it never says 4D. |
| Q5 | "Bostelmann–Fewster prove QIs in general interacting QFT" | arXiv:0812.4760 abstract and p. 3 | verified, with a caveat | The abstract says "We establish such inequalities in general (possibly interacting) quantum field theories". The verdict says "state-independent bounds cannot be expected", so it makes the distinction. |

The verdict's inference that the claim does not extend to warp bubbles is its own. |
