No claim was contradicted. The worst problems are provenance rather than numbers: RE-11 labels Riess 2025 as peer-reviewed while its arXiv page says "ApJ, submitted"; RE-20 marks Li 2025 as peer-reviewed while its arXiv page says "submitted"; RE-15's "22% smaller" has no support in the abstract; RE-03 repeats an internal inconsistency in the H0DN V20 label.
