# Verdict on C2 (refuter 1, angle: evidence)
claim: An early-universe analysis systematic (CMB likelihood, foregrounds or lensing anomaly; BAO reconstruction; BBN inputs) biases the inferred early-universe H0 low by most of the gap.
status: final
verdict: refuted
basis: cited-evidence
narrowed: none

Units: H0 in km/s/Mpc (astronomical units, not SI); ω_b dimensionless; BAO and α shifts in percent. "Most of the gap" means the true early-type value lies above the ≈ 70.23 km/s/Mpc midpoint of CMB-SPA 67.19 and the local reference 73.264, which is the boundary rule in candidates.md [calc: runs/2026-10-30-hubble-tension-h0-discrepancy/calc/falsifier-C2-1_evidence.py].

## Strongest attack
C2 names three routes: CMB foregrounds, likelihood or A_L; BAO reconstruction; BBN inputs. For "most of the gap", whichever route is responsible must move the early value by at least 3 km/s/Mpc (to above 70.2), and in the full version by about 6. The literature has run a published test aimed at each route, and each route's own published budget, or its own null test, holds it to a small fraction of that:

1. **Foregrounds and likelihood.** Foregrounds are much weaker in polarisation than in temperature, yet the polarisation-only Planck PR4 spectra still give a low H0: TE 68.49 ± 0.76, 6.3σ below 73.26 and 2.3σ below the midpoint [D-41; calc]. ACT DR6 states it gets "consistent results … from either temperature or polarization". Two independent likelihoods built from the same Planck data (CamSpec, and PR4 HiLLiPoP) both report foregrounds and systematics as negligible for ΛCDM parameters. Changing the likelihood moves P-ACT by 0.24 km/s/Mpc [D-41], which is 4% of the gap [calc].
2. **The lensing anomaly.** In PR4 the A_L anomaly is gone (A_L = 1.039 ± 0.052), yet H0 moved only from 67.36 to 67.64 (+0.28, 4.6% of the gap) [D-41; calc]. ACT DR6 reports "no evidence for excess lensing". Removing the A_L preference therefore does not raise H0 by anything like the amount C2 needs.
3. **Three CMB instruments.** SPT-3G has a different instrument, different sky and different foreground handling. On its own it gives 66.66 ± 0.60, "6.2 sigma away from local measurements from SH0ES". ACT alone gives 66.51 ± 0.64 [D-42]. Each would need its own same-sign bias of 10.6–11.0σ [math: M-MECHANIST-08].
4. **BAO reconstruction.** To reach 73.26, the BAO distance scale (equivalently r_d) must be off by a common 6.5% [math: M-MECHANIST-07]. DESI DR2's quoted fiducial-cosmology systematic on α_AP is 0.18%, and its Lyα theory systematic is 0.3%, so 6.5% is 22–36 times those budgets [calc]. A pipeline-specific reconstruction artefact is also ruled out by an independent survey with its own pipeline: SDSS DR16 + LUNA BBN gives 67.6 ± 1.0, consistent with DESI+BBN 68.51 ± 0.58.
5. **BBN inputs.** The deuterium-burning rates are the dominant BBN systematic. Schöneberg 2024 marginalises over "a wide range of nuclear rates" and still finds ω_b = 0.02218 ± 0.00055 (2.5%). The ω_b × 1.26 that C2 needs [math: M-MECHANIST-07] is 10.5σ of that error, which already includes the rate uncertainty. Even reaching only the midpoint would need ×1.09, which is 3.6σ [calc].
6. **The ceiling of the early-type class.** The highest early-type values, DESI+BBN at 68.51 and P-ACT+DESI DR2 at 68.43, sit 20–22% of the way across the gap [calc]. To carry "most of the gap", C2 needs independent analysis errors, in the same direction and each many times its own published budget, in three CMB pipelines, two BAO surveys and the BBN rate network at once. The candidate's own "weakest link" concedes this: the only element these probes share is the physics model, and an error there belongs to C3, not C2.

## Corrections to details
- C2(c) sets the needed ω_b × 1.26 against "≈ 1% D/H precision". The relevant error is ω_b with the nuclear rates marginalised: 0.02218 ± 0.00055 (2.5%, Schöneberg 2024). Against that error the requirement is 10.5σ, not about 26σ. The conclusion is unchanged.
- C2(c) calls the d(p,γ)³He rate claim "unverified". Schöneberg 2024 confirms that the D-burning rates drive the largest differences in ω_b, theory-based rates favouring a lower ω_b, but its rate-marginalised error is only 2.5%. The rate question is real, and too small for C2.
- C2's decisive test says "SPT-3G extended and Simons Observatory … (a ≥ 1 km/s/Mpc same-direction shift reopens C2)". A 1 km/s/Mpc shift is 16% of the gap, so it would reopen only a partial (C6-type) early contribution, not C2 as dominant. The bar for reopening C2 should be about 3 km/s/Mpc.

## What survives and remaining doubt
- A **partial** early-side contribution survives the evidence: of order 0.3–1.3 km/s/Mpc (5–22% of the gap). Its main components are the DESI–CMB Ω_m difference and the spread between likelihoods. Under the candidates' boundary rule such a contribution belongs to the null or mixed candidate (C6), not to C2.
- The only way to escape the attack is an error that is common to Planck, ACT, SPT, DESI, SDSS and BBN together. That means the shared ΛCDM physics model, which by C2's own admission is C3.
- Remaining doubt: Simons Observatory and SPT-3G extended data have not yet reported, so the cross-instrument agreement rests on current pipelines that share some foreground templates and the PR4 τ prior. Even so, the polarisation-only results and BAO+BBN, which use neither, already sit at ≤ 68.5. Choosing "refuted" over "weakened": the claim as stated ("most of the gap") fails on every named route, and the narrower form that survives is no longer a distinct C2 dominant cause.
