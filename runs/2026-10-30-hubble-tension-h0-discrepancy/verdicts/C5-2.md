# Verdict on C5 (refuter 2, angle: bounds)
claim: Local structure, a large underdensity or coherent bulk flow around us, makes the locally measured expansion rate exceed the global H0 by most of the gap.
status: final
verdict: refuted
basis: calculation
narrowed: none
## Strongest attack

Units: H0 in km/s/Mpc; distances in Mpc or h⁻¹ Mpc; velocities in km/s; magnitudes in mag; δ, f and fractions are dimensionless.

C5 needs a local excess ε = H_loc/H_glob − 1 = 9.04% (H0DN 73.264 against CMB-SPA 67.19). To be the *dominant* cause under the slate's boundary rule, it needs more than ε = 4.32%, which moves the true global H0 below the 70.227 midpoint. I tested this against four independent bounds that it has to meet all at once. All four are expressed in the same currency, the local H0 excess they allow [calc: runs/2026-10-30-hubble-tension-h0-discrepancy/calc/falsifier-C5-2_bounds.py]:

1. **The SN Ia Hubble diagram caps the void below the "dominant" threshold.** Kenworthy, Scolnic & Riess 2019 exclude |δ| > 20% at 4–5σ (δ < 27% at 5σ, D-11). Converted with Bernardeau's nonlinear outflow formula and f = Ω_m^0.55, |δ| = 0.20 gives ε = 3.56–3.66% (Ω_m = 0.30–0.315). That is 39–41% of the gap and 82–85% of the dominant threshold. A dominant share needs δ ≈ −0.235 to −0.241, and the whole gap needs δ ≈ −0.465 to −0.477. The dominant share lies between the 4σ and 5σ SN exclusions. The full gap needs about 2.3 times the 4–5σ-excluded contrast.
2. **The CosmicFlows-4 velocity field leaves at most about half, and only in an evidence-disfavoured profile does it reach most.** A field-level forward model of the CF4 Tully–Fisher distances, run by authors who include the leading void proponent (Stiskalek, Desmond & Banik 2025), prefers a void radius under 70 Mpc. It predicts a local H0 of 70.4 (Gaussian profile) or 70.2 (Maxwell–Boltzmann profile). Against a Planck background of 67.4 (my assumption for "background Planck cosmology"), that is 3.00 or 2.80 of the 5.86 km/s/Mpc gap: 51% and 48%. Only the exponential profile reaches 80% (72.1), and the authors report that profile "is disfavoured by the Bayesian evidence". The preferred models also give bulk-flow curves that "disagree with recent estimates from CosmicFlows-4". Even the most favourable empirical fit of the void model therefore sits at the boundary of "most". It does so in a beyond-ΛCDM growth scenario, and with flows that fail a second check.
3. **A bulk flow cannot supply a monopole.** A coherent dipole flow v averages to zero over an isotropic sky. For SNe uniform in volume over 0.023 < z < 0.15, I computed the leak ΔH/H = v⟨cosθ⟩⟨1/cz⟩:
   - at v = 400 km/s (CF4 measures "in excess of 380 km/s" at 150–200 h⁻¹ Mpc, which Watkins et al. put at "only about 0.023%" probability in ΛCDM), the leak is 0.33% for a strongly lopsided sky, 0.65% if every SN lay in one hemisphere, and 1.31% if every SN lay along the flow axis;
   - the full 9.04% needs 2,765 km/s with every SN along the flow, or 5,531 km/s for a one-hemisphere sample, which is 7 to 14 times the largest measured bulk flow.
   The "coherent bulk flow" branch of C5 is therefore excluded outright as a dominant cause. D-36 also finds that flow corrections *raise* local H0, by +0.6 (SN) and +1.4 (SBF).
4. **ΛCDM statistics, and the Hubble-diagram shape beyond the void edge.** Inside ΛCDM the needed void is a 20σ fluctuation (Wu & Huterer; Huterer & Wu: "δ ≈ −0.8 on a scale 120 h⁻¹ Mpc"). It is 84σ for a void that hides its edge beyond the Hubble-flow sample (math M-CONSTRAINTS-12, verified). If the void is pushed past z = 0.15 to hide its edge, a toy fit to the SN Hubble diagram with Pantheon+-like counts (assumed: 1,500 SNe, 0.14 mag each) shows the cost. With a 9.04% local excess and the edge at z_e = 0.15–0.3, a flat-ΛCDM fit costs Δχ² = 16–65 (sharp edge) or 110–162 (smooth edge). It absorbs the excess only by dragging Ω_m down to 0.199–0.222, or by moving to w = −1.06 to −1.90 in wCDM. The measured values point the other way. SN+BAO give Ω_m = 0.322 ± 0.012 (D-48), above DESI's 0.2975 ± 0.0086 (D-45), not about 0.09 below it.

Bounds 1 and 2 each limit local structure to at most about half the gap: the SN diagram at 4–5σ, CF4 through the preferred profiles. Bound 3 excludes the bulk-flow branch, and bound 4 excludes the ΛCDM-growth branch. The non-Copernican LTB branch (b) is tied to SNe at 0.023 < z < 0.15 and loses its preference with the full SN sample (D-77, D-97). It also has to meet bound 1, and the observer must sit within 61 Mpc of the centre (P = 8.6 × 10⁻⁴, M-CONSTRAINTS-12). No branch reaches "most of the gap" without breaking at least one independent bound.

## Corrections to details
- The candidate's "R ≳ 430 h⁻¹ Mpc, 84σ" figure is the ΛCDM-statistics bound. It does not apply to sub-hypothesis (c), where void growth is enhanced beyond ΛCDM (Milgromian, νHDM). The bound on that branch is empirical: the SN Hubble diagram and the CF4 velocity field, as above.
- The evidence-for line "empirical local-structure scatter 0.60% vs ≈ 0.42% predicted" is Kenworthy et al.'s *precision* on any void-induced H0 change (σ_H0 = 0.60%). It is not a measured excess scatter. The same abstract concludes "the distance ladder measurement is not affected by local density contrasts". It counts against C5, not for it.
- The candidate does not list Stiskalek, Desmond & Banik 2025, which is the most direct empirical test of the KBC void with distance tracers, by a team that includes its proponent.

## What survives and remaining doubt
- What survives: local structure can contribute *part* of the gap. At most about 2.8–3.0 km/s/Mpc (roughly half) comes from the CF4-preferred void profiles, and only in a beyond-ΛCDM growth scenario whose flows disagree with CF4 bulk-flow data. Inside ΛCDM the contribution is about 0.3–0.65 km/s/Mpc (D-37). A partial contribution belongs to the null (C6), not to C5 as the dominant cause.
- Doubts:
  - The Kenworthy bound assumes an LTB void with a sharp edge, and the 2019 sample has 1,295 SNe. A smooth profile with its edge beyond z ≈ 0.15 is tested only by my toy shape fit, which uses assumed SN counts and per-SN scatter and a simplified exterior-distance model. Its Δχ² values are indicative, not a likelihood analysis of real data.
  - In wCDM the toy's penalty drops to Δχ² ≈ 3–25, at the cost of w = −1.06 to −1.90. That joint fit with BAO was not run.
  - Stiskalek et al.'s 51% for the Gaussian profile sits on the "most" boundary. I treat the claim as refuted because the SN bound independently caps the dominant share at 4–5σ, and the CF4 fit's own flows fail the bulk-flow check. A future fit that passes both checks with more than 4.3% would reopen C5 in branch (c).
- Timescape (d) was not tested here. Its quoted abstract claims a better SN fit, not a resolution of the H0 gap.
