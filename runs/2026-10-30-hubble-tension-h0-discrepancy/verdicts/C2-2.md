# Verdict on C2 (refuter 2, angle: bounds)
claim: An early-universe analysis systematic (CMB likelihood, foregrounds or lensing anomaly; BAO reconstruction; BBN inputs) biases the inferred early-universe H0 low by most of the gap.
status: final
verdict: refuted
basis: calculation
narrowed: none

Units: H0 in km/s/Mpc, distances and r_d in Mpc, ages in Gyr; ω_i = Ω_i h² and Ω_m are dimensionless (astronomical units, not SI). σ is a Gaussian-equivalent significance.

## Strongest attack
C2 is an *analysis* systematic, so by construction the physics stays standard. The true universe is then flat ΛCDM with standard pre-recombination physics and H0 ≈ 73.26 (the full version) or at least ≈ 70.23 (the midpoint, the slate's own threshold for "dominant"). That world makes predictions for quantities that **no CMB likelihood, foreground model, BAO-reconstruction choice or BBN rate touches**:
- late-time Ω_m from the shape of the SN Hubble diagram, from DES 3x2pt and from the shape of the DESI BAO;
- the age of the Universe against globular clusters;
- r_d-free H0 values (uncalibrated BAO with the equality scale or lensing);
- the CMB's own ω_b against BBN deuterium, which are two independent measurements.

I computed every one of these with the run's verified background toolkit [calc: runs/2026-10-30-hubble-tension-h0-discrepancy/calc/falsifier-C2-2_bounds.py]. The core trap has two horns, and each fails an independent bound.

1. **Keep θ_* and bias ω_c (the only CMB-internal route).** At h = 0.73264 this needs ω_c −13.2%, which gives Ω_m = 0.2374. That is 7.0σ below DESI DR2's BAO-shape Ω_m (0.2975 ± 0.0086, D-45), 7.0σ below DES-SN5YR+DESI (0.322 ± 0.012, D-48) and 3.4σ below DES Y6 3x2pt (D-54). The same world predicts h·r_d = 110.99 Mpc against DESI's 101.54 ± 0.73, which is 12.9σ. So a CMB-side bias pushes Ω_m *away* from every late-time probe.
2. **Keep the late-time Ω_m (0.30–0.33) and accept H0 = 73.26.** The CMB would then have to have mismeasured ω_m by +11% to +25% and θ_* by +3.2% to +4.7%, which is 112σ to 164σ of 100θ_* = 1.0411 ± 0.0003 (D-40). θ_* comes from peak positions and agrees across Planck, ACT and SPT, and DESI is consistent with it (D-45). No foreground, likelihood or A_L residual moves peak positions by 3%.

Each of the other sub-routes runs into a bound of its own:
- **BAO side, sub-route (b).** With standard r_d and ω_b fixed, DESI DR2 BAO (Ω_m and h·r_d with correlation −0.92) disfavours h = 0.73264 against its best fit (h ≈ 0.6875) by Δχ² = 104, about 10σ. The published DESI+BBN value, 68.50 ± 0.58, gives 8.2σ. Adding the SN Ω_m and the GC age raises this to Δχ² = 113. The reason is that the standard-r_d curve at h = 0.733 runs almost parallel to the DESI degeneracy direction but is offset from it. Tilting Ω_m therefore does not rescue it, which corrects the candidate's "+4.7–5.2σ" (a marginal-Ω_m figure) upward.
- **BBN side, sub-route (c).** The ×1.26 in ω_b [math: M-MECHANIST-07] gives ω_b = 0.0273. That is 30σ above the D/H value (0.02165 ± 0.00019) and 23% above the independent CMB ω_b (0.02218). Cooke et al. state that BBN ω_b "agrees to within 2 sigma" of Planck. A BBN-rate error would break that agreement, but the two methods share no input.
- **Ages.** The full C2 world with late-time Ω_m = 0.30–0.33 is 12.49–12.89 Gyr old, 2.7σ to 3.9σ below the GC age 13.81 ± 0.34 Gyr (D-55).
- **r_d-free H0.** Nine values (D-49, D-50) do not use the CMB likelihood, BAO r_d or, for Zaborowski, BBN rates. They lie 0.7σ to 3.9σ below 73.26, with six of the nine at ≥ 2.7σ. They are correlated through DESI DR1, so I do not multiply them.

**Minimal "dominant" version (true H0 = 70.23).** C2 still fails several independent bounds at once:
- the θ_*-preserving CMB route gives Ω_m = 0.274, which is 2.8σ from DESI BAO, 4.0σ from DES-SN+DESI and 2.1σ from DES Y6;
- keeping the late-time Ω_m instead needs θ_* off by 42σ to 96σ;
- DESI BAO with standard r_d is off by 3.5σ (3.0σ against the published BAO+BBN value);
- all three CMB instruments must also share a same-sign bias of 5.0σ (Planck PR4), 5.8σ (ACT) and 6.2σ (SPT) to reach 70.23 (D-41 to D-43; 10.6–11.0σ to reach 73.26, M-MECHANIST-08).

These bounds come from data sets with no shared analysis pipeline. So the minimal version needs coordinated, same-sign errors in instruments and methods that share nothing but the physics model. If the physics model is what is wrong, the cause is C3 or C4, not an analysis systematic.

**Checking my own assumptions:**
- *Independence of the bounds.* I treat late-time Ω_m as independent of the early-side systematics C2 names. SN shape and DES 3x2pt do not use r_d or the CMB likelihood. The DESI BAO-shape Ω_m does not use r_d, though it shares the BAO-reconstruction step that sub-route (b) blames. I therefore lean on DES-SN and DES Y6 as well, and those give 3.4–7σ in the full version.
- *Correlations.* The D-48 Ω_m uses DESI DR1 BAO, so it is not fully independent of the DESI DR2 Ω_m, and I do not multiply them.
- *Toolkit approximations.* The z_* and z_d fitting formulas are calibrated at one point. The θ_* shifts I quote (1.2–4.7%) are 8 to 34 times larger than that calibration offset (0.14%), so the conclusion does not depend on it.
- *Ages.* The GC age is a preprint value (D-55), and the age bound is the weakest of these; I do not rest the verdict on it.

## Corrections to details
- Route (b) understates its own cost. Read jointly, DESI's (Ω_m, h·r_d) with correlation −0.92 exclude h = 0.733 with standard r_d at Δχ² ≈ 104 (about 10σ), not "+4.7–5.2σ". The marginal Ω_m figure ignores the h·r_d offset along a curve that is almost parallel to the degeneracy direction. The published DESI+BBN σ alone gives 8.2σ.
- Route (a): the ω_c −13.2% from my independent solve agrees with the corrected −13.0 to −13.3% (M-MECHANIST-06). The candidate's matrix entry "CMB Ω_m ≈ 0.238" is confirmed (0.2374). Its collateral cost, which C2 leaves unstated, is a 7σ conflict with SN-shape Ω_m and a 12.9σ conflict with DESI h·r_d.
- The C2 world has an age cost the candidate does not mention: 12.5–12.9 Gyr, 2.7–3.9σ below the GC age (preprint).

## What survives and remaining doubt
- **Not in doubt.** A small early-side analysis effect is real and of the size the candidate itself cites:
  - likelihood choice moves P-ACT by 0.24 km/s/Mpc (D-41);
  - the DESI–CMB Ω_m discrepancy, if it is a systematic, moves the early value from 67.19 to 68.5, about 21% of the gap.
  That is a sub-dominant contribution, which belongs to C6 (the null), not to C2.
- **Remaining doubt.** The r_d-free values cluster at 69–70.3 (Zaborowski 2026, D-49), which is consistent with the midpoint. So the midpoint version of C2 is excluded by a set of individually 2–4σ bounds rather than a single overwhelming one.
- **What would reopen C2.** It would need all of the following:
  - SO or SPT-3G Ext moving the CMB ΛCDM H0 up by at least 1–2 km/s/Mpc;
  - DESI final BAO with a revised reconstruction raising Ω_m toward 0.33 at fixed h·r_d;
  - r_d-free H0 converging on 72–73.
  Even then, the CMB route would still have to explain why a likelihood or foreground error mimics a −7% to −13% ω_c shift in three instruments with different foregrounds and scan strategies.
