# Verdict on C3 (refuter 0, angle: magnitude)
claim: Pre-recombination new physics, led by early dark energy, shrinks the sound horizon r_d so that ΛCDM-based early values read low; this carries most of the gap.
status: final
verdict: weakened
basis: calculation
narrowed: Pre-recombination new physics (axion-like EDE) shrinks r_d enough to carry roughly a third to two-thirds of the local–early gap (true H0 ≈ 69.3–71.4 km/s/Mpc in fits with no ladder prior); it carries more than half only in the profile-likelihood and eROSITA-including analyses, not in the CMB-centric Bayesian ones or the r_d-free H0 probes, and it never carries the whole gap.

Units: H0 in km/s/Mpc (astronomical units, not SI), f_EDE dimensionless, ages in Gyr, magnitudes in mag. All numbers below come from [calc: runs/2026-10-30-hubble-tension-h0-discrepancy/calc/falsifier-C3-0_magnitude.py] (log beside it) unless a source is named.

## Strongest attack

C3 defines its own threshold. "Dominant" means the true global H0 lies above the midpoint between CMB-SPA ΛCDM (67.19) and H0DN (73.264), which is **70.23**. The residual ladder offset must also be smaller than the shift driven by r_d. The attack measures C3 against that threshold, in three ways.

1. **The f_EDE needed sits at the CMB's 95% edge.** Reaching the midpoint takes f_EDE ≈ **0.065–0.072**.
   - The low end comes from the background mapping verified as M-CONSTRAINTS-07, at fixed Ω_m and z_c = 3500.
   - The high end comes from calibrating dH0/df ≈ 42 km/s/Mpc per unit f on the two full Boltzmann fits, Poulin and eROSITA.
   - The Bayesian 95% upper bounds without a ladder prior are 0.061 (Planck NPIPE) and 0.070 (SPT+ACT+Planck) [D-53, D-75]. These equal 0.85–1.07 times the threshold. So in the Bayesian frame, "most of the gap" lies at or beyond the 95% exclusion edge.
   - Planck NPIPE's own EDE interval, 66.9 < H0 < 69.5 at 95%, ends **0.73 km/s/Mpc below** the midpoint.
2. **Most fits without a ladder prior fall short.** Six full-fit results are given as H0 values. Only two of them put the true H0 above the midpoint:
   - ACT P-ACT EDE: 34.7% of the gap; P(H0 > 70.23) = 0.11.
   - ACT P-ACT-LB EDE: 44.6%; P = 0.24.
   - ACT modified recombination: 39.7%; P = 0.27.
   - ACT PMF: 31.4%; P = 0.01.
   - Poulin 2026 profile: 62.7%; P = 0.76.
   - eROSITA+ext: 69.3%; P = 0.80.

   Two further results are given as f_EDE posteriors that include DESI DR2:
   - SPT+ACT+Planck+DESI DR2: f = 0.055 maps to H0 ≈ 69.7, with P(f > threshold) = 0.16–0.22.
   - SPT+DESI: P = 0.49–0.55.

   In every ACT case the ladder residual (3.36–4.16 km/s/Mpc) is larger than the early shift (1.91–2.71). That breaks C3's second dominance condition as well.
3. **C3's own clean test points the wrong way.** H0 values that bypass r_d should read the true, high H0 if C3 holds, and C3 predicts ≥ 71. The three correlated DESI-DR1 Zaborowski values combine to 69.73 ± 0.80–1.32. That gives P(H0 > 70.23) = 0.27–0.35 and P(H0 ≥ 71) = 0.06–0.17. The older set (arXiv:2411.16677) gives P(H0 > 70.23) = 0.02–0.11. Only the broad equality-scale value (70.6 +3.7/−5.0) is neutral, at P = 0.46.

None of these is a ≥ 3σ exclusion, so the core is not refuted. Taken together, though, the measurements put the EDE share at about 31–69%, centred near half. "Carries most of the gap" holds only in a subset of analyses. It is not supported across them.

## Size against the discrepancy

The reference gap is H0DN − CMB-SPA = +6.07 km/s/Mpc (0.188 mag); against SH0ES it is +6.30. The signs below are those C3 predicts.

Bottom line: C3 can carry part of the gap. Its share falls on either side of 50% depending on the statistical framework (Bayesian or profile likelihood) and the data combination. It cannot carry the whole gap; no fit reaches 73.

## Corrections to details

- C3 mixes baselines. Its dominance threshold, 70.2, is set against H0DN (73.264), but its quoted shares are against SH0ES 73.49: "43%", "60%" and "67%". Against H0DN those shares are 44.6%, 62.7% and 69.3%. This does not change the conclusion.
- Measured from ACT's own ΛCDM baseline of 68.2 km/s/Mpc, the midpoint becomes 70.73. P(H0 > midpoint) then falls to 0.60 (Poulin) and 0.68 (eROSITA), and to 0.10 for ACT P-ACT-LB EDE.
- With the background mapping, closure at H0DN needs f = 0.124 (z_c = 3500, M-CONSTRAINTS-07). The calibration to full fits gives f ≈ 0.144. Both lie above every Bayesian 95% bound except P-ACT-LB (< 0.12) and SPT alone (< 0.12).
- Variant (e), electron-density fluctuations at 69.3, used a ladder prior (D-95). It should not be counted as a no-prior result. C3 already flags this.

## What survives and remaining doubt

The mechanism survives. EDE is the only class that moves the CMB, BAO+BBN and the inverse ladder together. With current data it lifts the inferred H0 by 2–4 km/s/Mpc without a ladder prior. The profile-likelihood result (Poulin et al.) and the eROSITA result put the share above half, at P ≈ 0.76–0.80. The Bayesian CMB-centric analyses and the r_d-free probes put it below half, at P ≈ 0.01–0.35.

Two disputes cannot be settled from the inputs:
- Whether the Bayesian bounds are inflated by prior-volume effects. Poulin et al. say they are.
- Whether the DESI-driven preference reflects a DESI–CMB Ω_m offset rather than r_d (D-53, D-83).

So I give `weakened`, not `refuted`. The magnitude does not support "most of the gap" across the analyses, but it does not exclude it at ≥ 3σ either. The deciding measurements are the ones C3 names: an anchor-free or r_d-free H0 at σ ≈ 1 km/s/Mpc landing at ≥ 71, and SO/SPT-3G detecting f_EDE ≳ 0.07 without a ladder prior. If either lands at ≤ 69.5, or gives f < 0.05, C3 falls to a partial contribution inside C6.
