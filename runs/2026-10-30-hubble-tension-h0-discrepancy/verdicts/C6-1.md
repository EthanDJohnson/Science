# Verdict on C6 (refuter 1, angle: evidence)
claim: No single dominant cause: comparable partial contributions (ladder-calibration levers, partial early-universe shifts) plus fluctuation and underestimated errors spread over several measurements produce the gap.
status: final
verdict: weakened
basis: cited-evidence
narrowed: The null survives only as a specific two-part hybrid: a ladder-side (SN-calibration, selection or peculiar-velocity) offset just under half the gap (about 2.3–3.0 km/s/Mpc), plus a real pre-recombination shift of about 2–3 km/s/Mpc that CMB-only data do not yet favour, plus at most a ~1σ fluctuation. The "underestimated errors spread over several measurements" and "pure fluctuation" variants fail against the published evidence.

Units: H0 in km/s/Mpc (astronomical units, not SI); magnitudes in mag; fractions dimensionless; σ is a Gaussian-equivalent significance. Gap reference (the slate's own): H0DN 73.264 ± 0.806 [D-22] minus CMB-SPA 67.19 ± 0.38 [D-44] = 6.074 km/s/Mpc. The boundary rule makes any cause carrying more than half the gap (3.037 km/s/Mpc, true H0 crossing 70.23) dominant [calc: runs/2026-10-30-hubble-tension-h0-discrepancy/calc/falsifier-C6-1_evidence.py].

## Strongest attack

The candidate's heading claims that the gap comes from partial contributions *spread over several measurements*, including diffuse underestimated errors. Published evidence on both sides speaks against that diffuse structure. Once the boundary rule is applied, what is left of C6 is a narrow hybrid that rests on an early-time component the CMB-only literature does not support.

1. **The early side shows no diffuse error, and its partial shift is not favoured by CMB data alone.**
   - Three CMB experiments with independent instruments agree (D-02, D-41–D-44).
   - CMB-only fits of the leading early-time model give upper limits, not detections:
     - ACT DR6: "models introduced to increase the Hubble constant ... are not favored by our data" (quoted below).
     - SPT+ACT+Planck: f_EDE < 0.070 at 95%, with the tension still at 3.6σ (quoted below).
     - Planck NPIPE: f_EDE < 0.061, H0 < 69.5 at 95% [D-53].
   - The only data that push f_EDE above zero are those that include DESI. The SPT authors call that shift "a manifestation of the discrepancy currently present between DESI and CMB experiments" (quoted below), and D-87 places that offset along Ω_m h³ = const, which does not by itself pull H0 toward 73.
   - The candidate's own "weakest link" adds that inflating the early-side errors fails (p < 0.01) [math: M-DECOMPOSER-06, verified].

2. **The ladder side's internal consistency rules out "errors spread over several measurements" there too.**
   - H0DN, the community combination across Cepheids, TRGB, JAGB, Miras, SBF, SN II and Tully–Fisher, concludes: "Local H0 is robustly determined, with first-rank indicators internally consistent within their uncertainties" (quoted below). Its analysis-variant centres scatter by only 0.400 km/s/Mpc [D-23].
   - JWST rejects the main diffuse photometric error, Cepheid crowding growing with distance, at 8.2σ (quoted below).
   - Bringing H0DN within 2σ of CMB-SPA by diffuse local error needs a local error of 3.01 km/s/Mpc (×3.74 the quoted error, 7.5× the variant spread), or a common unmodelled shift of 4.29 km/s/Mpc [calc: falsifier-C6-1_evidence.py; math: M-CONSTRAINTS-13, verified].
   - **Internal inconsistency.** A common ladder shift of 4.29 km/s/Mpc is 70.7% of the gap. Under the slate's own boundary rule ("several ladder-side effects that together carry most of the gap count as one method's systematic (C1)"), sub-hypothesis (b) is C1, not C6. The candidate concedes that "a diffuse null must in practice be a ladder null".
   - Within every class the χ² is normal (P5 within p = 0.58), and all the excess lies between classes (pooled χ² = 54.03/11) [math: M-EXAMINER-05, M-EXAMINER-06]. That is the signature of a break between groups, not of errors spread over many measurements.

3. **Pure fluctuation is excluded by the candidate's own numbers.** Gaussian p₂ = 3.6 × 10⁻¹⁰. Even under almost-Cauchy tails, the all-local excess is 4.3–4.5σ [math: M-STATISTICIAN-14, verified]. The candidate itself calls (c) "near-eliminated".

4. **The hybrid (a) survives, but only in a squeezed window.**
   - Without fluctuation, C6 rather than C1 needs an early-side shift b_E above 3.04 − ε. Without an early shift, it needs an upward fluctuation of 3.04 km/s/Mpc (3.41σ, P = 3.3 × 10⁻⁴).
   - With only the DESI BAO+BBN offset (b_E = 1.31), the fluctuation must be at least 1.73 km/s/Mpc (1.94σ, P = 0.026).
   - The window opens comfortably (ε ≥ 0.33–0.73, P = 0.21–0.36) only if the early shift sits at 2.3–2.7 km/s/Mpc. That is the Planck-NPIPE 95% upper edge or the ACT P-ACT-LB EDE posterior centre, and both are bounds, not detections [calc: falsifier-C6-1_evidence.py].
   - The published analyses that do report a non-zero EDE fraction put H0 at 71.0 (Poulin 2026) and 71.4 (eROSITA) [D-53, D-94]. These close 62.7% and 69.3% of the gap, so the boundary rule classes them as **C3, not C6** [calc; math: M-DECOMPOSER-05, verified].
   - So the early-side evidence falls either below what C6 needs (CMB-only upper limits) or above it (detections large enough to be C3). C6's early part sits in the gap between the two, where no analysis claims a detection.

The attack does not refute C6. Real evidence supports partial ladder levers of 1.9–2.9 km/s/Mpc each:
- Wojtak & Hjorth 70.6–71.5 [D-35];
- Percival 2026, −1.4 / −0.2 / −1.0 [D-88];
- Dong 2026, peculiar velocities 74.30 → 71.37 [D-82].

The between-pole values also support a mid-range outcome: one-step mean 71.27 ± 1.93 [math: M-DIALECTICIAN-07]; r_d-free values 69.2–70.3 [D-49]. And the historical Sandage–de Vaucouleurs analogue exists. None of this is contradicted. The attack shows that the null in its stated form, with diffuse errors spread over several measurements, is not supported. What survives is a specific conjunction: a ladder lever just under half the gap, plus an early-time shift of about 2–3 km/s/Mpc that is currently undetected in CMB-only data. Hence `weakened`, not `refuted`.

## Size against the discrepancy

Gap: 6.074 km/s/Mpc (H0DN − CMB-SPA), 0.188 mag. Sign convention: + means the measured value sits above the true global H0 for the local classes, and below it for the early classes.

| Measurement class | C6 shift needed (sign) | What the evidence allows |
|---|---|---|
| Cepheid-SN / anchor-calibrated ladders | + about 2.3–3.0 km/s/Mpc (just under half) | Contested levers of 1.9–2.9 each [D-35, D-82, D-88]; JWST excludes crowding (8.2σ); H0DN variant spread 0.40. Above 3.04 the candidate becomes C1 |
| TRGB/JAGB ladders | + smaller (CCHP ≥ 71.5 on the full host set predicted) | Consistent with D-27 (72.1–73.3) and Hoyt's +2 when extended [D-25] |
| CMB under ΛCDM | − about 2–3 km/s/Mpc (true H0 about 69.5–70.2) | CMB-only: f_EDE < 0.061–0.070, H0 < 69.5 (95%); "not favored" (ACT). Edge of the allowed region |
| BAO+BBN | − about 1–1.7 | 68.50 ± 0.58: needs the DESI–CMB Ω_m offset to be early-side physics, which SPT attributes to a DESI–CMB discrepancy |
| One-step (time delays, masers, sirens) | ≈ 0 (they measure the true value, about 69.5–70.2) | Mean 71.27 ± 1.93: consistent |
| Fluctuation share | + 0.3–1.7 km/s/Mpc (0.4–1.9σ) depending on the early share | Plausible only if the early share is ≥ 2.3 km/s/Mpc |

C6 can carry the whole gap only in the corner where the early shift is 2.3–3.0 km/s/Mpc and the ladder offset is 2.3–3.0 km/s/Mpc. If the early shift is smaller, as CMB-only data prefer, the ladder share exceeds half (C1) unless there is a 2–3.4σ fluctuation. If the early shift is larger, as in the pro-EDE detections, it becomes C3.

## Corrections to details

- In sub-hypothesis (a), "best fits closing 30–67%" is inconsistent with the slate's boundary rule. Fits closing more than 50% (H0 ≥ 70.23: Poulin 71.0 at 62.7%, eROSITA 71.4 at 69.3%) are C3 by construction. C6's early part must stay below 50%, i.e. H0_fit ≤ 70.2 [calc; math: M-DECOMPOSER-05].
- Sub-hypothesis (b), the common ladder shift of 4.3 km/s/Mpc (70.7% of the gap), is C1 under the boundary rule, not C6. Only its error-inflation reading belongs to the null, and the H0DN internal consistency and the 0.40 variant spread argue against that reading.
- The "partial early-universe shifts" from the DESI–CMB Ω_m offset (about 1.3 km/s/Mpc) are not shown to be an early-universe bias. D-87 places the offset along Ω_m h³ = const, and the SPT authors attribute the DESI-driven EDE preference to that discrepancy, not to pre-recombination physics.
- The "evidence for" bullet that physically no bound is violated holds only for the hybrid. The diffuse-error variant conflicts with the H0DN internal-consistency result.
