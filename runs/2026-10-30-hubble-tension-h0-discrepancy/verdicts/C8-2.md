# Verdict on C8 (refuter 2, angle: bounds)
claim: The ≥5σ significance rests on one team's Cepheid-calibrated SN ladder; the team-independent anomaly is a smaller ~4 km/s/Mpc local excess at only about 3–3.7σ.
status: final
verdict: weakened
basis: calculation
narrowed: No current local combination without SH0ES Cepheids reaches 5σ, but the team-independent local excess is 4.5 ± 1.2 km/s/Mpc (statistically indistinguishable from the SH0ES gap at 1.2–1.5σ) at 2.5–4.2σ depending on the early-universe reference, rising to 4.1–4.9σ for Cepheid-free sets that use SH0ES-side or H0DN TRGB, so "only ~3–3.7σ" is a reference-selected band and "smaller" is a point-estimate statement, not an established one.

Units: H0 in km/s/Mpc (astronomical units); magnitudes in mag; σ is a Gaussian-equivalent significance. All numbers below are from [calc: calc/falsifier-C8-2_bounds.py] (log beside it) unless another source is cited.

## Strongest attack
C8's heading has three quantitative parts: (i) the ≥5σ needs one team's Cepheid-calibrated SN ladder; (ii) without that ladder the anomaly is *smaller*, about 4 km/s/Mpc; (iii) its significance is *only* about 3–3.7σ. From the bounds angle I tested each part against every independent early-universe reference in the dossier, and against the Cepheid-free and team-variant local sets that C8's own construction leaves out.

1. **(iii) is a choice of reference, not a bound.** The candidate compares the non-SH0ES local mean (reproduced: 71.692 ± 1.141, matching M-EXAMINER-03) with CMB-SPA. That reference includes Planck. Against the CMB experiments that are independent of Planck, the same local mean gives ACT DR6 3.96σ, SPT-3G 3.90σ and SPT+ACT 4.15σ [D-42, D-43]. Against BAO+BBN it gives 2.49σ, against Planck PR4 3.23σ, and against the flat-ΛCDM inverse ladder 3.02σ. The honest band is therefore **2.5–4.2σ**. Its upper end lies outside C8's "only ~3–3.7σ", and it comes from the CMB pair (SPT+ACT) that the brief singles out as the check on Planck-specific systematics.
2. **(i) holds, but only just, and on "Cepheid", not on "one team".** Several Cepheid-free sets come close to 5σ:
   - SH0ES-side TRGB (Li 2026, 72.1–73.3 ± 1.8, 35 hosts [D-27]) put in place of CCHP's TRGB, plus SBF, TF and the one-step methods: 72.51 ± 1.11, which is 4.53σ above CMB-SPA and 4.92σ above SPT+ACT;
   - the same set without TF and with the shared NGC 4258 anchor treated by GLS: 4.1–4.5σ (SPA) and 4.5–4.9σ (SPT+ACT);
   - H0DN without Cepheids (V08, 72.509 ± 1.296 [D-23]) plus the one-step methods: 4.32σ (SPA) and 4.73σ (SPT+ACT). Applying the −0.2 to −0.4 km/s/Mpc corrected-Pantheon+ shift [D-20, D-22] still leaves 4.1–4.2σ (SPA) and 4.5–4.6σ (SPT+ACT).

   None reaches 5σ, so the literal claim that "≥5σ needs Cepheids" stands. But the distance between ~3σ and ≥5σ is set mostly by which TRGB analysis of the same calibrator hosts is used (CCHP's selection or SH0ES's) and by which CMB reference is used. Removing Cepheids alone does not set it.
3. **(ii) the anomaly is "smaller" only in its point estimate.** The team-independent gap to CMB-SPA is 4.50 ± 1.20 km/s/Mpc (0.141 mag); the SH0ES gap is 6.30 (0.195 mag). The two differ by 1.50σ of the team-independent error, and SH0ES sits 1.22σ from the rest of the local data (as in M-EXAMINER-03). The between-class χ² is 2.90 for 3 degrees of freedom (p = 0.41) [math: M-EMPIRICIST-06]. So the independent data neither show nor exclude a smaller anomaly. They are less precise. At the current non-SH0ES central value, 5σ against SPA needs σ_local ≤ 0.816 km/s/Mpc, against 1.141 now (a factor of 1.40). With SH0ES's central value and the non-SH0ES error, the significance would already be 5.24σ. With the rest's central value and SH0ES's error it would be 4.48σ. Of the 2.53σ drop from 6.27σ to 3.74σ, the shift of the central value therefore carries 59–71% and the loss of precision 29–41%, depending on which step is taken first. The shift that does most of the work is itself only a 1.2σ effect.

## Size against the discrepancy
C8 names no cause, so it moves no measurement. It re-weights them. With the non-SH0ES local mean set to 71.69 ± 1.14, the gap splits as follows:
- SH0ES against the rest: +1.80 ± 1.47 km/s/Mpc (0.06 mag, 1.22σ);
- the rest against CMB-SPA: +4.50 ± 1.20 km/s/Mpc (0.141 mag, 3.74σ);
- the rest against BAO+BBN: +3.19 (2.49σ);
- the rest against SPT+ACT: +5.10 (4.15σ).

Of the 0.195 mag SH0ES–CMB-SPA gap, about 72% (0.141 mag) is still there without SH0ES. C8 is right that the ≥5σ *significance* needs SH0ES Cepheids. It is not shown that most of the *size* of the gap does.

## Corrections to details
- "~3–3.7σ" should read "≈2.5–4.2σ for the CCHP-plus-one-step set depending on the early reference (3.2–3.7σ against Planck-containing CMB), and 4.1–4.9σ for Cepheid-free sets using SH0ES-side or H0DN TRGB".
- "~4 km/s/Mpc" against CMB-SPA is 4.5 ± 1.2 (4.0–4.5 depending on set). Against SPT+ACT it is 5.1.
- "local excess": the set includes strong-lensing time delays (z ≈ 0.3–0.7) and GW sirens, which are not local. "Late-universe excess" is the accurate term.
- "team-independent": TF (Cosmicflows-4) takes its zero points from Cepheid and TRGB hosts [D-30], and SBF and CCHP share the NGC 4258 anchor [D-24, D-29]. Dropping TF changes the result very little (71.56 ± 1.19, 3.50σ against SPA), so this is a labelling issue, not a numerical one.
- H0DN without Cepheids alone is 3.94σ against CMB-SPA [math: M-STATISTICIAN-01], already above the stated 3.7σ ceiling. The candidate lists it in its argument without noticing this.

## What survives and remaining doubt
These parts survive:
- No current local combination that excludes SH0ES Cepheids reaches 5σ against any early reference. The highest I found is 4.92σ, and it uses the SH0ES team's own TRGB analysis against SPT+ACT.
- Taken alone, the CCHP-plus-one-step data give a 2.5–4.2σ excess.
- The ≥5σ headline therefore does depend on the SH0ES Cepheid-SN ladder.

These parts fail:
- the claim that the team-independent anomaly is "smaller", which the data support at only 1.2–1.5σ;
- the claim that it is "only" ~3–3.7σ: that band holds only against Planck-containing references and with CCHP's TRGB rather than the SH0ES-side TRGB.

Remaining doubt:
- The Li 2026 TRGB range is SH0ES-side and overlaps CCHP's data [D-25, D-27]. Using it brings back a team dependence, though not a Cepheid one.
- Which TRGB analysis is right is the open SH0ES–CCHP dispute [D-80].
- C8's decisive test is unchanged: a team-independent TRGB/JAGB ladder on ≥ 35 hosts, plus a non-Cepheid combination at σ ≤ 1.1 km/s/Mpc. A result near 72.5–73 would refute C8's "smaller" clause. A result near 70–71 would confirm it.
