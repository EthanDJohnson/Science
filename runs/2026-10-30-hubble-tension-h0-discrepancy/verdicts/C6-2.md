# Verdict on C6 (refuter 2, angle: bounds)
claim: No single dominant cause: comparable partial contributions (ladder-calibration levers, partial early-universe shifts) plus fluctuation and underestimated errors spread over several measurements produce the gap.
status: final
verdict: weakened
basis: calculation
narrowed: The gap can be shared without a dominant cause only as a two-part hybrid: a ladder-side systematic of about 2.3–3.0 km/s/Mpc, plus a genuine pre-recombination reduction of the sound horizon by about 2–5% (EDE-like or modified recombination, not extra dark radiation) shifting every r_d-calibrated route by 1.3–3.0 km/s/Mpc, with true H0 ≈ 69.4–70.6 km/s/Mpc. Fluctuation and diffuse underestimated errors can carry at most about 1 km/s/Mpc.

Units: H0 in km/s/Mpc, r_d in Mpc, ages in Gyr, magnitudes in mag (astronomical units, not SI). Gap reference: H0DN-corrected 73.264 ± 0.806 [D-22] minus CMB-SPA 67.19 ± 0.38 [D-44] = 6.074 km/s/Mpc. "Dominant" follows the slate's own boundary rule: more than half of the gap (3.037 km/s/Mpc), with several ladder-side effects counted as one cause (C1) [candidates.md, preamble].

## Strongest attack
C6 counts three kinds of ingredient as interchangeable: systematics, new physics, and fluctuation or underestimated error. The independent bounds say they are not interchangeable. Once each side's shift is capped at half the gap and every r_d-calibrated route has to agree, three things follow.

1. **C6 lives in a narrow window, and inside it the early side always needs a large coherent shift.**
   - Fluctuations are limited by the poles' own errors (0.806 and 0.38 km/s/Mpc), and neither side may carry more than half the gap. Under those two conditions the true H0 must lie in [69.43, 70.60] at Δχ² ≤ 1, or [68.62, 70.98] at Δχ² ≤ 4 [calc: runs/2026-10-30-hubble-tension-h0-discrepancy/calc/falsifier-C6-2_bounds.py].
   - Everywhere in the 2σ window the early side needs a coherent shift S_E ≥ 1.26 km/s/Mpc (21% of the gap). In the 1σ window it needs 2.2–3.0 km/s/Mpc (36–50%). The ladder side needs S_L of 2.3–3.0 km/s/Mpc (37–50%).
   - So "fluctuation and underestimated errors" can only be a minor term (F_L ≤ +0.7 km/s/Mpc at T ≥ 69.5). The two systematic or physical pieces carry about 85–100% of the gap.

2. **The early piece cannot be error or fluctuation. It must be a real reduction of r_d.**
   - Suppose the true H0 is 70.2 and r_d is standard. Then the routes calibrated by the r_d ruler sit at 7.9σ (CMB-SPA), 2.9σ (DESI BAO+BBN) and 4.6σ / 3.7σ (the two inverse-ladder variants). The summed χ² is 92.1 for 3 dof. At T = 69.5 it is 52.2 for 3 dof.
   - These are three different instruments or data types: CMB anisotropies, BBN-calibrated BAO, and BAO + uncalibrated SNe. A shift in all three together is the signature of a smaller r_d, not of errors spread across measurements. The early-error-inflation test fails at p < 0.01 [math: M-DECOMPOSER-06], which agrees.
   - The r_d needed, holding H0·r_d at the inverse-ladder value 67.43, is 144.2 Mpc (−1.9%) at the edge of the window, 142.6 Mpc (−3.0%) at T = 69.5 and 141.2 Mpc (−3.9%) at T = 70.2, against the standard 147.00 Mpc [calc: same file].
   - C6's own "partial early-universe shift" is therefore pre-recombination new physics, i.e. a partial C3. It is not a null-type effect.

3. **Bounds on that new physics decide which variants survive.**
   - **Extra dark radiation is excluded.** Using D-56's mapping (N_eff = 4.21 for a 7.0% cut, so 0.167 per 1%), the 1.9–3.9% cut needs N_eff = 3.36–3.70. That is 3.8–6.5σ above ACT DR6 N_eff = 2.86 ± 0.13, and 4.3–7.4σ above 2.89 ± 0.11 with He and D [D-52, D-74; calc: same file].
   - **EDE and modified recombination are allowed but not preferred.**
     - The C6 midpoint T = 70.2 sits +0.4σ from P-ACT-LB EDE (69.9 +0.8/−1.5), −0.7σ from Poulin 2026 (71.0 ± 1.1), −0.9σ from eROSITA EDE (71.4 ± 1.4) and +0.6σ from modified recombination (69.6 ± 1.0) [D-52, D-53, D-94].
     - It is +2.2σ above the primordial-magnetic-field value (69.1 ± 0.5) and +3.0σ above ACT-alone EDE (67.5 +0.9/−1.7).
     - It lies above the Planck NPIPE EDE 95% range of 66.9 < H0 < 69.5 [D-53].
     - SPT+ACT+Planck gives f_EDE < 0.070 (95%), and ACT DR6 reports that such models are "not favored by our data" [D-13, D-53].
   - So the early part C6 needs sits at the edge of the CMB-only EDE bounds, and is comfortable only when DESI, eROSITA or Pantheon+ are added.

4. **Two of C6's three named sub-hypotheses do not survive as a null.**
   - **(b), diffuse underestimated errors.** Against CMB-SPA it needs an unmodelled common ladder shift of 4.29 km/s/Mpc [calc: runs/2026-10-30-hubble-tension-h0-discrepancy/calc/lens-constraints_void_null.py; math: M-CONSTRAINTS-13]. That is 71% of the gap, on one side [calc: falsifier-C6-2_bounds.py, (F)]. By the slate's boundary rule that is a dominant ladder systematic (C1), not the null. C6's own weakest-link text concedes that "a diffuse null must in practice be a ladder null". This is an internal inconsistency in (b), not just a weak point.
   - **(c), pure fluctuation.** C6 itself calls it near-eliminated (Gaussian p₂ = 3.6 × 10⁻¹⁰; heavy-tailed all-local excess still 4.3–4.5σ) [math: M-STATISTICIAN-14, M-STATISTICIAN-15].
   - **(a), the comparable-parts hybrid,** is the only sub-hypothesis left. Its early part must be r_d physics of the EDE or recombination type.

**Why the verdict is weakened, not refuted.** The narrowed hybrid violates no independent bound I could test:
- the EDE and recombination posteriors reach 69.9–71.4 [D-52, D-53, D-94];
- at T = 70.2 the flat-ΛCDM age is 13.22 Gyr, −1.7σ from the globular-cluster age 13.81 ± 0.34 Gyr [D-55; calc];
- the r_d-free values 69.2–70.3 [D-49] and the one-step mean 71.27 ± 1.93 sit inside the window.

What fails is the wording "fluctuation and underestimated errors spread over several measurements" as a load-bearing part, and the reading of C6 as a null without new physics.

## Corrections to details
- C6's argument puts the true H0 at "≈ 69–71". Under the slate's own half-gap rule, with Gaussian pole errors, the 1σ window is 69.4–70.6. Near T = 69 the ladder must carry exactly 50% and the ladder pole must fluctuate by +1.5σ. Near T = 71 the CMB pole must fluctuate by −2σ.
- One of the early-side options C6 offers is the DESI–CMB Ω_m offset (≈ 1.3 km/s/Mpc, 21%). That is enough only at the very edge of the 2σ window (T ≈ 68.7, minimum S_E = 1.26). Even there it leaves the inverse ladder 2.3σ low and the ladder carrying exactly half the gap. In the bulk of the window the early part has to be r_d physics.
- The decisive test, an anchor-free aggregate at 70–71, cannot by itself tell the narrowed C6 hybrid from a "C1 + C3 at 50/50" reading, because they are the same thing. It also needs a CMB damping-tail or polarisation detection of the r_d reduction (f_EDE ≈ 0.05–0.09 or modified recombination) at ≥ 3σ without a ladder prior.
