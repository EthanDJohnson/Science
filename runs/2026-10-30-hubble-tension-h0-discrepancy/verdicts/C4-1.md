# Verdict on C4 (refuter 1, angle: evidence)
claim: Post-recombination new physics, either a modified expansion history H(z) or a low-redshift change in G_eff or standard-candle luminosity, is the dominant cause of the gap.
status: final
verdict: weakened
basis: cited-evidence
narrowed: Post-recombination new physics can still be the dominant cause only as an abrupt, low-redshift (z ≈ 0.01–0.023) step in SN Ia absolute magnitude that leaves the Cepheid-host volume untouched. Even that form is disfavoured at about 2–3σ by SN-free and G-independent H0 probes, and at about 3.3–4σ by red dwarfs when the step is driven by a ~11% change in G. A smooth change in H(z) cannot be the cause.

Units: H0 in km s⁻¹ Mpc⁻¹ (astronomical units, written km/s/Mpc); magnitudes in mag; times in Myr; σ is a Gaussian-equivalent significance.

## Strongest attack
C4 groups three variants under one heading. The literature hits each one with a different measurement, and in each case the measurement is one the variant cannot move.

1. **Variant (i), a smooth change in H(z), fails on data that do not assume a late-time model.**
   - The inverse distance ladder (BAO calibrated by the standard r_d, plus uncalibrated SNe Ia) gives 67.19 +0.66/−0.64 (cosmography) or 67.67 +0.69/−0.68 km/s/Mpc [D-48]. The SN-cosmology systematics move it by < 0.1 km/s/Mpc [D-07].
   - Any smooth H(z) leaves both the anchor ladder and this inverse ladder unchanged. The gap between the two, which (i) cannot touch, is 5.6–6.1 km/s/Mpc, or 5.0–5.8σ [calc: calc/falsifier-C4-1_evidence.py].
   - Efstathiou (2021) reached this conclusion with a dynamics-free inverse ladder (quoted below).
   - The one late-time model the data actually prefer, DESI DR2 w0wa, moves H0 *down* to 63.6–67.5 km/s/Mpc [D-51, D-12].
   - Variant (i) is therefore refuted as a dominant cause unless r_d is non-standard, and a non-standard r_d is C3, not C4.

2. **Variant (ii), an M_B or G_eff step between z ≈ 0.01 and 0.023, makes testable predictions, and current data disfavour it.**
   - (a) **The Hubble flow of the near-region hosts reads about 73, not 67.**
     - Under (ii), Cepheids at z < 0.01 are standard, so the Cepheid hosts' own Hubble flow should give the true H0, which is about 67.2–68.5 because (ii) leaves CMB and BAO untouched.
     - Kenworthy et al. 2022 (SH0ES co-authors) built a two-rung ladder that uses no SNe: Cepheid host distances and redshifts at cz < 3300 km/s. They got 73.1 +2.6/−2.3 km/s/Mpc, with every selection model between 71.8 and 77.0. That is 2.5σ above CMB-SPA and 1.9σ above BAO+BBN [calc].
     - Huang et al. 2025 (peer-reviewed) state that their two-rung result "rules out third-rung supernova systematics including the late-time transition in supernova absolute magnitude".
   - (b) **SN-free, G-independent probes sit at about 72, not at the true H0 that (ii) predicts.** I combined Kenworthy two-rung, masers (Stiskalek 2026), TDCOSMO-2026 and GWTC-5 sirens:
     - the mean is 72.01 ± 1.46 km/s/Mpc;
     - that is +4.82 ± 1.51 km/s/Mpc (3.2σ) above CMB-SPA and +3.51 ± 1.57 km/s/Mpc (2.2σ) above BAO+BBN;
     - against 67.19 the χ² is 11.27 for 4 degrees of freedom (p = 0.024). Against the ladder's 73.26 it is 1.05 (p = 0.90) [calc].
     - With Pesce 2020 masers in place of Stiskalek 2026, the offset is 3.6σ above CMB-SPA. Dropping the time delays leaves 2.8σ [calc].
   - (c) **Red dwarfs exclude the G-driven version.**
     - Sakstein & Desmond 2026 (preprint) test the original G-step model: ΔG/G0 = +0.11 at 130 Myr, which is the transition at z = 0.01 needed to restore concordance with L_SNIa ∝ G^1.46. They exclude it at 4.0σ (Δχ² = 16.41, which I reproduce as 4.05σ [calc]).
     - This is the same amplitude C4 needs: G_far/G_near = 0.89–0.91 [math: M-CONSTRAINTS-11, M-MECHANIST-12].

3. **Variant (iii), a screened fifth force acting on Cepheids, does not carry the gap even by its proposers' own account.** Desmond, Jain & Sakstein 2019 find that it "can alleviate (though not resolve)" the tension. The variant also has to explain why JAGB and TRGB agree with Cepheids in the same hosts: JAGB minus Cepheid is −0.03 ± 0.02 (stat) ± 0.05 (sys) mag [D-26], and the three-method JWST distance-limited sample gives 72.6 [D-28].

What remains is (ii) as a pure SN-luminosity step, not tied to G, at an older lookback (> 145 Myr). For that form the evidence against it is only 2–3σ. That leaves the core alive in a narrow form, so the verdict is weakened rather than refuted.

## Size against the discrepancy
The reference gap is H0DN 73.264 ± 0.806 minus CMB-SPA 67.19 ± 0.38 = +6.07 km/s/Mpc (0.188 mag); against BAO+BBN it is +4.99 km/s/Mpc.
- **(i) Smooth H(z).**
  - Shift in the ladder: 0. Shift in the inverse ladder: 0. CMB-inferred H0: up only with phantom w = −1.194, which needs Ω_m 0.266, 3.6σ below DESI [math: M-CONSTRAINTS-10].
  - The w0wa model that the data prefer shifts H0 the wrong way, by −0.5 to −3.6 km/s/Mpc [D-51].
  - Share of the ladder-vs-inverse-ladder gap it carries: 0 of 5.6–6.1 km/s/Mpc.
- **(ii) M_B/G step.**
  - On paper it carries all 0.15–0.19 mag, by brightening Hubble-flow SNe relative to calibrators.
  - It predicts that SN-free, G-independent probes read the true H0 (67.2–68.5). They read 72.0 ± 1.5, which is +3.5 to +4.8 km/s/Mpc, or 70–80% of the gap, on the ladder side. That is the opposite of what (ii) needs [calc].
- **(iii) Screened fifth force.**
  - It acts only on Cepheid-SN; TRGB/JAGB-SN are predicted lower.
  - Its proposers say it cannot resolve the gap. Matched-host JAGB/TRGB–Cepheid agreement at ≤ 0.05 mag [D-26, D-28] limits it to well below the 0.19 mag needed.

## Corrections to details
- C4 says "Huang 2025 claims to rule out an M_B transition (unverifiable)". The abstract has now been fetched and states exactly that. It should count as cited evidence against (ii), still subject to its peculiar-velocity modelling.
- C4's lookback window (144.5–333 Myr for z = 0.01–0.023 in SPA ΛCDM) begins somewhat later than the 130 Myr the G-step literature uses for z = 0.01, which follows from the higher H0 assumed there. Sakstein & Desmond tested 130 Myr. A step at the old end of C4's window (up to 333 Myr) leaves a weaker red-dwarf memory, and that weaker constraint is not quantified here.
- C4's matrix says time delays read "≈ 67–68" under (ii). In a G-step world the lens is at z ≈ 0.3–0.7, where G = G_far. The stellar-kinematics mass, M ∝ σ²R/G, used to break the mass-sheet degeneracy may then be biased by about 10%. So the time-delay prediction under the G form is not clean, and I report the combination without time delays as well: 2.8σ against CMB-SPA. This caveat is my own inference, not a published result.
- C4 treats SBF 73.8 [D-29] as contradicting (ii). SBF galaxies at 50–100 Mpc lie beyond the step, and their red-giant luminosities depend on G. So SBF is a clean test only of the pure-M_B form, not of the G form.

## What survives and remaining doubt
- **What survives:** a phenomenological SN Ia luminosity step at z ≈ 0.01–0.023 not driven by G. It has no known physical mechanism; physically it amounts to SN Ia evolution over about 200 Myr. It also escapes the red-dwarf bound if the change is placed earlier than about 145 Myr.
- **Against even that form:**
  - SN-free probes disfavour it at 2.2σ (BAO+BBN reference) to 3.2–3.6σ (CMB-SPA reference);
  - Kenworthy's and Huang's two-rung results find near-region expansion at about 73.
- **Why this is not stronger:** none of these probes is individually beyond 2.6σ. The maser value is preliminary and statistical-only, the time delays and sirens are preprints, and the two-rung results depend on modelling peculiar velocities at z < 0.01. A ≥ 5σ exclusion of (ii) must wait for the decisive test C4 itself names: TRGB- or geometric-distance M_B for SNe in 45–100 Mpc hosts, plus TDCOSMO and siren precision near 2%.
- **On the G-step exclusion:** it rests on one preprint with 8 red-dwarf binaries and stellar-model systematics. The authors' conservative test, a coherent 1% downward shift in predicted radii, lowers Δχ² at the benchmark from 16.41 to 10.92, which is about 3.3σ (√10.92), so the exclusion holds at more than 3σ even then. Quote (ACCESS full-text): "The likelihood- ratio statistics at the EGSM and OGSM benchmarks are then reduced from ∆χ 2 = 9.52 and ∆χ 2 = 16.41 to ∆χ2 = 5.72 and ∆χ2 = 10.92, respectively."
- **Verdict:** the core claim that post-recombination physics in general is the dominant cause does not stand. Only the narrow M_B-step form survives, and it is disfavoured. I chose weakened rather than refuted because the surviving form is excluded at only about 2–3σ.
