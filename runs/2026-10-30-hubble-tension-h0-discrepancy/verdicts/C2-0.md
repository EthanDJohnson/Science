# Verdict on C2 (refuter 0, angle: magnitude)
claim: An early-universe analysis systematic (CMB likelihood, foregrounds or lensing anomaly; BAO reconstruction; BBN inputs) biases the inferred early-universe H0 low by most of the gap.
status: final
verdict: refuted
basis: calculation
narrowed: none

Units: H0 in km/s/Mpc, r_d in Mpc, ω_i = Ω_i h² and Ω_m dimensionless (astronomical units, not SI). "σ" is each measurement's own quoted 1σ error. Dominance boundary, from candidates.md: the true H0 must cross the midpoint 70.23 between CMB-SPA 67.19 and H0DN 73.264.

## Strongest attack
C2 names **one** analysis systematic. But "the early-universe H0" is not one measurement. It is at least three classes that share no data:
- CMB anisotropies (Planck, ACT and SPT);
- BAO+BBN, which uses no CMB anisotropies;
- the inverse ladder, which uses BAO and SNe with r_d.

An analysis error in one class leaves the others unchanged, so each sub-hypothesis must also explain why the classes it cannot reach sit low. The numbers do not allow this:

1. **(a) A CMB-only systematic** (foregrounds, likelihood, A_L):
   - It leaves BAO+BBN at 68.50 ± 0.58. That value must then be a low fluctuation of 2.98σ even at the bare midpoint (one-sided p = 1.5×10⁻³), or 8.2σ at 73.26.
   - Its own size is out of reach. Each CMB experiment needs a same-sign shift of 2.6–3.7 km/s/Mpc just to reach the midpoint (5.0σ for Planck PR4, 5.8σ for ACT, 6.0σ for SPT). That is 9–11 times the largest published analysis-choice spread, which is 0.23–0.28 km/s/Mpc.
   - Its only internal route that reaches 73 is ω_c low by 13%. That route makes the true r_d 3.0% **larger**, so it moves the inverse ladder **down**, from 67.67 to 65.72 (the wrong sign).
   - The same route predicts h·r_d = 110.96 Mpc. DESI measures 101.54 ± 0.73, a 12.9σ miss. It also predicts Ω_m = 0.238, which is 7.0σ below both DESI (0.2975 ± 0.0086) and the SN value (0.322 ± 0.012).
   - The θ_* route needs a 58σ error in θ_*, and still gives Ω_m = 0.266: 3.6σ from DESI and 4.6σ from SNe.
2. **(b)/(c) A BAO-only or BBN-only systematic.** Either leaves CMB-SPA at 67.19 ± 0.38, which must then be a low fluctuation of 8.0σ even at the midpoint (p = 6.6×10⁻¹⁶). Within its own class:
   - (b) needs a 6.5% shift in the BAO/r_d scale (9σ). DESI's whole systematic budget adds only 1–9% to the error on α_iso, and its theory systematic for Lyα is 0.3%.
   - (c) needs ω_b ×1.26. That is about 30σ of the D/H precision and 37σ from the CMB's own ω_b of 0.02237 ± 0.00015, an independent measurement from the peak-height ratios.
3. **The only input all classes share** is the recombination and drag-epoch physics in the Boltzmann codes. Shrinking r_d by 6.5% through it needs z_d moved by about +110 (×1.108). That is a changed recombination history, which is C3, not an analysis systematic. Even ACT's fit with recombination left free (D-52) reaches only 69.6 ± 1.0, below the midpoint.
4. **Lensing-anomaly sign.** ACT and SPT do not show Planck's PR3 A_L excess, yet they sit **lower**: 66.59 ± 0.46 against PR4 67.64 ± 0.52. Removing an A_L-type Planck artefact cannot pull the CMB group up by 3–6 km/s/Mpc.

Every route therefore fails on size by an order of magnitude or more, on sign, or on a class it cannot reach. The candidate's own "weakest link" paragraph concedes the shared-physics point; the numbers above make it decisive.

No sub-hypothesis moves more than one early class upward. The class it leaves alone then sits 3.0σ (BAO+BBN) to 8.0σ (CMB-SPA) below even the midpoint. ## Corrections to details
- C2's sub-hypothesis (a) says the ω_c route "reaches 73.26". It does so for the CMB alone, but in the same cosmology it pushes the inverse ladder down to 65.7 and contradicts DESI h·r_d by 12.9σ and BAO/SN Ω_m by 7σ. The prediction matrix's "↑" for the inverse ladder under C2 holds only for r_d-shifting routes, not for the ω_c route.
- The prediction "the inverse ladder rises with a corrected r_d" needs r_d to fall by 6.5%, a Δz_d of about +110. That is a recombination-physics change (C3), not an analysis systematic.

## What survives and remaining doubt
- **What survives** is a sub-dominant early-side contribution, which belongs to C6, not C2. The DESI–CMB Ω_m offset (2.3–2.8σ) moves the early value from 67.19 to 68.50, about 21% of the gap. Likelihood and release choices add about 0.2–0.3 km/s/Mpc each.
- **Assumptions of the attack:**
  - flat ΛCDM;
  - the run's calibrated background toolkit, with the Hu–Sugiyama z_* fit and the Eisenstein–Hu z_d fit (r_d normalised to 147.09 Mpc);
  - σ(ω_c) taken as 0.0012;
  - Gaussian errors.
- **Effect of relaxing them:** a non-Gaussian tail or errors underestimated by ×2 would not close factors of 9–20. M-DECOMPOSER-06 shows that ×3 inflation of the early-side errors still fails at p < 0.01.
- **Remaining doubt:** an unknown common input, outside the recombination physics already bounded by ACT's free-recombination fit, that biases the CMB and BAO+BBN by the same amount. None is identified, and any such input that changes r_d is new physics (C3).
