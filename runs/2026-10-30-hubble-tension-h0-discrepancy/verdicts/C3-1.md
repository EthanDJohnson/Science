# Verdict on C3 (refuter 1, angle: evidence)
claim: Pre-recombination new physics, led by early dark energy, shrinks the sound horizon r_d so that ΛCDM-based early values read low; this carries most of the gap.
status: final
verdict: weakened
basis: cited-evidence
narrowed: Pre-recombination new physics (axion-like EDE first) can shrink r_d enough to carry a partial share of the gap, about 10–45% in full-CMB fits without a ladder prior, and above half only in combinations driven by DESI BAO that leave out SPT, high-ℓ Planck or Lyα data; it is not shown to be the dominant cause.

## Strongest attack

C3 sets its own test of "dominant": the true global H0 lies above the midpoint of the gap, ≈ 70.2–70.3 km/s/Mpc (SI-derived units, km s⁻¹ Mpc⁻¹, used throughout). The literature fits that use **no ladder prior** fall into three groups.

1. **The most complete CMB data fail the test outright.** The SPT collaboration's dedicated axion-EDE paper (Khalife et al. 2025, arXiv:2507.23355, full text) fits SPT-3G D1 + ACT DR6 + Planck ("CMB-SPA") and finds H0 = 68.03 +0.53/−1.1 and f_EDE < 0.070. That closes 13–14% of the gap. The authors conclude that the model "passes none" of their three metrics, and that "from CMB data alone, AEDE cannot be considered as a solution to the Hubble tension." This fit uses the same CMB data as the ΛCDM early value that defines the gap (67.19). SPT+ACT alone gives 67.25 +0.47/−1.1, a closure of 1%.
2. **Only DESI BAO pushes EDE toward the midpoint, and only below it with the full CMB.** CMB-SPA + DESI DR2 gives H0 = 69.81 +0.95/−1.2 (42–44% closure), with f_EDE = 0.055 +0.024/−0.047, which is 1.2σ from zero. The same authors attribute the shift to the DESI–CMB discrepancy rather than to H0: "this shift in parameters when adding the DESI dataset is a manifestation of the discrepancy currently present between DESI and CMB experiments in the concordance model ΛCDM." So the one route by which EDE rises toward the midpoint runs through a separate anomaly, in Ω_m and h·r_d, that has nothing to do with the ladder.
3. **An independent probe C3 does not address excludes the f_EDE it needs.** The Lyman-α forest (Goldstein, Hill, Iršič & Sherwin 2023, PRL 131, 201001, abstract) cuts the 95% bound from 0.07 to **f_EDE < 0.03** and gives H0 = 67.9 ± 0.4, with "similar results" from an independent high-resolution Lyα dataset. C3's own full-closure values f_EDE = 0.124–0.188 are 4.1–6.3 times that bound. Its partial-closure values, 0.06–0.07 and Poulin's 0.09, are 2–3 times it [calc: runs/2026-10-30-hubble-tension-h0-discrepancy/calc/falsifier-C3-1_evidence.py]. Neither the candidate nor the dossier mentions Lyα.

**Which fits clear the midpoint.** Only three no-ladder fits put their central H0 above it:
- Poulin 2026's profile likelihood, 71.0 ± 1.1. It uses Planck only at ℓ < 1000 and no SPT data [D-53, D-83].
- eROSITA cluster counts plus external data, 71.4 ± 1.4 [D-94].
- SPT + DESI, 70.9 +1.3/−1.9, which is DESI-driven as in point 2.

The full-CMB Bayesian fits fall short: ACT P-ACT-LB 69.9 and CMB-SPA + DESI 69.81 both sit just below the midpoint. The pure-CMB and Lyα fits fall far short. So "carries most of the gap" holds only on a selected subset of data choices.

**A misread source in C3's "evidence for".** C3 cites H0 World Cup II (Schöneberg, Poulin et al. 2026, arXiv:2607.13283, full text) as showing that group E is "strongly favored over ΛCDM". In that paper the model-comparison AIC is computed on the combined dataset: "AIC = χ²_min,A+B + 2N_param ... the minimum χ² obtained from the combined A+B dataset". Dataset B is the SH0ES calibration: "We adopt a Gaussian prior M_B = −19.253 ± 0.027 ... In the SH0ES collaboration results [199] this prior gives H0 = (73.04 ± 1.04) km/s/Mpc". The "strong preference" therefore partly measures how well EDE accommodates the ladder that is under test. It is not independent evidence that the early-universe value is biased. Even with B included, the same paper says group E leaves "approximately 3σ" and "no model fully resolves the Hubble tension in the full baseline analysis."

**C3's own prediction is not met.** C3 predicts that r_d-free H0 will rise to ≥ 71–73. The current DESI r_d-free values have centrals of 69.2, 70.3 and 69.6 [D-49], closures of 32–52%, all at or below the midpoint [calc]. Their errors of about 1.3–1.8 km/s/Mpc keep this from being decisive, since each is within about 1–1.5σ of 71. It points the same way as the rest of the evidence, but it is not a refutation by itself.

**Why weakened rather than refuted.** I was attacking from the evidence angle, and the attack does not reach "refuted" for three reasons:
- Profile-likelihood analyses show strong prior-volume effects in the Bayesian EDE bounds [D-75, D-83], which loosens points 1 and 2.
- The Lyα bound rests on one observable. Its own abstract says it is "driven by the preference of the Lyα forest data for n_s values lower than those required by EDE", so it is only as strong as the P1D modelling. I did not find or verify a later P1D re-analysis.
- eROSITA, an independent probe, does prefer f ≈ 0.10.

So a dominant early-universe share is disfavoured by most of the current data but not excluded. "Most of the gap" does not survive as the central reading. A partial share does.

## Size against the discrepancy

The gap is 6.30 km/s/Mpc (CMB-SPA ΛCDM 67.19 against SH0ES 73.49). C3's mechanism raises the early-type H0 (CMB, BAO+BBN, inverse ladder) and leaves the local ladder where it is. The sign is right. The sizes:

- **CMB-only, all three experiments:** +0.84 km/s/Mpc (13%).
- **With DESI:** +2.6 km/s/Mpc (42%).
- **With Lyα:** about +0.7 km/s/Mpc relative to 67.19, roughly 0 relative to its own Planck+BAO ΛCDM baseline.
- **Selected profile-likelihood or cluster combinations:** +3.8 to +4.2 km/s/Mpc (60–67%).
- **r_d-free H0, which C3 predicts should read the true value:** +2.0 to +3.1 km/s/Mpc (32–49%).

The fits on the full data carry less than half. Only fits that select which data to use carry more than half.

## Corrections to details

- [D-49] leaves open whether arXiv:2411.16677 is the "previous work" that Zaborowski 2026 updates. INSPIRE lists arXiv:2411.16677 as Zaborowski, E.A. et al., "A Sound Horizon-Free Measurement of H0 in DESI 2024", JCAP 06 (2025) 020 (lit_search, ACCESS: abstract). The low set (66.7–67.9) is therefore the same team's earlier DR1 analysis, and the current values are 69.2–70.3. C3's "r_d-free values 66.7–70.3" should read 69.2–70.3. This narrows the evidence against C3 slightly but does not reverse it.
- C3 lists World Cup II's "strongly favored over ΛCDM" as evidence for. Its AIC includes the SH0ES M_B prior (quoted above), so it is not ladder-independent support.
- The SPT residual for CMB-SPA EDE is Q_MPCL = 3.6σ at H0 = 68.03 +0.53/−1.1. The +DESI value is 69.81 +0.95/−1.2 at 2.6σ. D-53 quotes the σ values without the H0 central values, which are given here.
- The INSPIRE abstract for Strunk et al. (arXiv:2609.07834) describes a sample "spanning 0.1 < z < 0.8". [D-94] says that only 0.1 < z < 0.45 was used. I did not resolve this, and it does not affect the verdict.
