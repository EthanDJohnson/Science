# Verdict on C8 (refuter 1, angle: evidence)
claim: The ≥5σ significance rests on one team's Cepheid-calibrated SN ladder; the team-independent anomaly is a smaller ~4 km/s/Mpc local excess at only about 3–3.7σ.
status: final
verdict: survives
basis: cited-evidence
narrowed: none

Units: H0 in km/s/Mpc (astronomical units, not SI); σ is a two-sided Gaussian-equivalent significance. Every combination below treats errors as Gaussian, symmetrised and independent unless stated otherwise.

## Strongest attack

I attacked from the literature on three lines.

**(1) Does any route without SH0ES Cepheids reach ≥5σ?** If one did, the first clause would fail. The best candidate in the inputs was the H0DN entry "V08B 72.873 ± 0.874". Against CMB-SPA it gives 5.96σ [calc: runs/2026-10-30-hubble-tension-h0-discrepancy/calc/falsifier-C8-1_evidence.py]. The research file describes it as a "custom baseline with the same SN calibrators" (RE-03), which reads as if it might be Cepheid-free.

The H0DN paper rules that out. V08B keeps the Cepheids and only drops calibrators: "V08B Modified baseline for the no-Cepheid variant V08. This is identical to the baseline, except that SNe Ia for which we only have Cepheid distance estimates have been removed, leaving 35 SNe Ia calibrators" (Casertano et al., arXiv:2510.23823, p. 20, ACCESS: full-text, fetched with fetch_text). It also says custom baselines "are not variants per se" (p. 11). The genuinely Cepheid-free variant is V08, at 72.509 ± 1.296, which gives 3.94σ against CMB-SPA. The no-SN variant V13, at 73.434 ± 1.795, gives 3.40σ.

I also checked the newest late-universe combination. TDCOSMO-2026 builds non-H0DN combinations, and it states only that "The tension with Planck is 2.7σ or greater". Its no-SN combination is "in 3.7σ tension with Planck" (arXiv:2610.14881, pp. 1 and 4, ACCESS: full-text). No source I found puts a route free of SH0ES Cepheids at ≥5σ.

**(2) Does the "team-independent" ~4 km/s/Mpc at 3–3.7σ depend on which results the lens chose to include?**
- **Tully–Fisher.** The lens's non-SH0ES set includes Cosmicflows-4 Tully–Fisher. Its zero point comes from Cepheid/TRGB calibrations, so it is not team-independent (D-30). Dropping it gives 71.56 ± 1.19, which is +4.37 km/s/Mpc and 3.50σ above CMB-SPA.
- **CCHP JWST-only.** Replacing CCHP's headline value with its JWST-only value gives 70.84 ± 1.15, which is +3.65 km/s/Mpc and 3.00σ.
- **Effect on the claim.** Both choices stay inside the claimed band.
- **Leaning the other way.** Adding the independent-team Cepheid-only re-analysis of SH0ES data (Stiskalek et al. 2026, 71.1 ± 1.4) gives 71.36 ± 0.91 and 4.25σ. Those are still SH0ES data, so they are not team-independent, but this is the only route above 3.7σ.

**(3) Does C8 misread its sources?** One source is misread; see Corrections.

The attack fails on the core. The ≥5σ does require SH0ES Cepheids and SNe together. Every route without SH0ES Cepheid data sits at 2.7–3.9σ against CMB-SPA. Independent re-analyses of the SH0ES data themselves come out lower.

## Size against the discrepancy

- **The reference gap.** SH0ES minus CMB-SPA is +6.30 ± 1.00 km/s/Mpc (6.27σ).
- **C8's split.** About +2 km/s/Mpc is SH0ES-specific (SH0ES against the rest of the local data). The remaining +3.65 to +4.50 km/s/Mpc is shared by all non-SH0ES local routes. That shared part is 3.0–3.7σ against CMB-SPA, 2.5–3.2σ against Planck PR4 and 1.8–2.5σ against BAO+BBN.
- **Where 5σ comes from.** Only routes that carry the SH0ES Cepheid + Pantheon+ calibration reach it: SH0ES itself, and H0DN with its Cepheids (V08B, baseline).
- **Without the SN step.** Dropping the SN step (H0DN V13, 3.40σ; Stiskalek, 2.70σ) also takes the result below 5σ.

So the ≥5σ needs SH0ES Cepheids and SN Ia together, as C8 says.

## Corrections to details

1. **"CCHP itself states ≈ 3σ" is mis-attributed.** Freedman et al.'s 3.0σ is not a team-independent local measurement against the CMB. It is CCHP's re-evaluation of the SH0ES R22 value (74.03 − 2.00, with a 1.00 km/s/Mpc SN-calibration term added) against Planck. It supports "the ≥5σ is contested". It is not evidence for the size of the team-independent excess. CCHP's own TRGB value against CMB-SPA is 1.62σ.
2. **The upper end of 3.7σ is not a ceiling.** The most precise Cepheid-free combination in the literature, H0DN V08, is 3.94σ against CMB-SPA. Adding an independent-team analysis of SH0ES Cepheid data gives 4.25σ. "About 3–4σ" would be more accurate. Neither route is team-independent in its data, since V08 uses SH0ES-side TRGB and Pantheon+, so the core stands.
3. **"Rests on" describes precision, not a shifted value.** H0DN's custom-baseline comparison (V08B 72.87 against V08 72.51) attributes most of the no-Cepheid drop to losing 20 calibrators (σ 0.87 → 1.30), not to Cepheids pulling the value up. The SH0ES-side TRGB (72.1–73.3) and H0DN V08 (72.5) sit about 5 km/s/Mpc above CMB-SPA. So the claim that the team-independent excess is about 4 rather than 5–6 km/s/Mpc depends on weighting CCHP's lower TRGB value over the SH0ES-side TRGB analysis of overlapping data (D-26, D-27). That choice is consistent with C8's definition of "team-independent", but it is a choice.
4. **The lens set includes Tully–Fisher.** TF is calibrated through Cepheid/TRGB zero points (D-30), so it is not team-independent. Removing it moves the result from 3.74σ to 3.50σ and changes no conclusion.
5. **The significance depends on the reference.** The 3–3.7σ is against CMB-SPA. Against BAO+BBN the same set is only 1.8–2.5σ, so "anomaly at ~3–3.7σ" needs "vs CMB-SPA" attached.

## What survives and remaining doubt

The core survives the evidence attack. No published route that avoids SH0ES Cepheid data reaches 5σ: H0DN without Cepheids is 3.94σ, TDCOSMO's non-H0DN combinations are "2.7σ or greater" against Planck, and CCHP alone is 1.6σ. An independent-team analysis of the SH0ES Cepheids without SNe gives 2.8σ. The non-SH0ES local excess is about 3.6–4.5 km/s/Mpc at 3.0–3.7σ against CMB-SPA, robust to removing TF or swapping in CCHP's JWST-only value.

Remaining doubts:
- **"Team-independent" is not a clean partition.** NGC 4258 anchors almost every ladder (CCHP, SBF, masers, H0DN TRGB). Pantheon+ is shared. SH0ES-side and CCHP TRGB analyse overlapping data and disagree by about 2–4 km/s/Mpc.
- **The low significance is mostly a precision limit.** It is not evidence that SH0ES is biased. A team-independent full-sample TRGB/JAGB ladder at σ ≤ 0.77 km/s/Mpc that lands at 71.5 or above would by itself restore ≥5σ without SH0ES. No dated programme in the inputs delivers that.
