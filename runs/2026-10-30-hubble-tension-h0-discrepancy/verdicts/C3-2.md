# Verdict on C3 (refuter 2, angle: bounds)
claim: Pre-recombination new physics, led by early dark energy, shrinks the sound horizon r_d so that ΛCDM-based early values read low; this carries most of the gap.
status: final
verdict: weakened
basis: calculation
narrowed: Only axion-like (group-E) early dark energy at f_EDE ≈ 0.09–0.10 survives the independent bounds as a carrier of more than half the gap, and only under profile-likelihood or eROSITA readings with DESI DR2's low Ω_m. It raises the true global H0 to ≈ 70.2–71.4 km/s/Mpc (50–69% of the gap) and leaves a ≈ 2 km/s/Mpc ladder residual. Full closure to ≈ 73 is excluded at 3.6–4.4σ by the r_d-free, one-step and stellar-age bounds, and every other pre-recombination variant falls below the midpoint.

Units: H0 in km/s/Mpc, ages in Gyr (astronomical units, not SI). Gap reference: CMB-SPA 67.19 ± 0.38 [D-44] to H0DN 73.264 ± 0.806 [D-22]. "Most of the gap" means the true global H0 is above the 70.2 midpoint (the boundary rule in candidates.md).

## Strongest attack
C3 says the early-universe values are biased low by a non-standard r_d. If so, the true global H0 must show up, above 70.2, in every constraint that bypasses r_d and the distance ladder. These are:
- r_d-free BAO [D-49];
- the matter–radiation equality scale [D-50];
- one-step local probes (time delays, masers, sirens) [D-31–D-33];
- the stellar-age bound [D-55]. EDE changes H(z) only before recombination, so it leaves t0(H0, Ω_m) unchanged.

Inside the CMB itself, the model's own posteriors without a ladder prior must reach the midpoint too. I ran all of these tests [calc: runs/2026-10-30-hubble-tension-h0-discrepancy/calc/falsifier-C3-2_bounds.py]:

1. **Full closure is excluded.** Each r_d-free + one-step + age combination puts a true H0 of 73.26 at **3.6σ to 4.4σ** (sets A, D, E and F plus age: 69.64 ± 1.00, 69.99 ± 0.89, 69.27 ± 0.91, 68.89 ± 1.04). This bound does not depend on r_d, so EDE cannot escape it. The C3 table entries "≈ 71–73" for one-step methods and "≥ 71–73" for r_d-free shape only hold at the bottom of that range.
2. **The midpoint is not excluded, but it is not favoured either.** The same combinations give P(true H0 > 70.2) = 0.10 to 0.41. The most favourable r_d-free pair alone gives 0.54. C3's own best value, 71.0, sits +1.1σ to +2.0σ above these combinations. The independent bounds therefore leave room for a "most of the gap" EDE, but they lean against it.
3. **Every variant except EDE falls below the midpoint on its own fits:**
   - PMF 69.1 ± 0.5 gives P(>70.2) = 0.014 [D-52].
   - Electron-density fluctuations give 69.3 ± 0.5 even *with* a ladder prior: P = 0.036 [D-95].
   - ACT modified recombination 69.6 ± 1.0 gives P = 0.27 [D-52]. Varying m_e fails once DESI DR2 is added [D-76].
   - ΔN_eff needs 4.34–4.40 against 2.86 ± 0.13, which is 11–12σ [math: M-CONSTRAINTS-08, verified].

   "Pre-recombination new physics" as a class therefore does not carry most of the gap. Only the EDE (group E) branch can.
4. **Even EDE reaches the midpoint only under one statistical reading:**
   - The Bayesian CMB bounds put it below. Planck NPIPE gives 66.9 < H0 < 69.5 at 95% [D-53], so P(>70.2) < 0.025. ACT P-ACT-LB gives 69.9 +0.8/−1.5, P = 0.25. ACT alone gives 67.5, P = 0.001 [D-52].
   - Only the profile-likelihood result (71.0 ± 1.1, P = 0.77) [D-53] and the eROSITA-assisted result (71.4 ± 1.4, P = 0.80) [D-94] put the bulk of the probability above it.
   - The profile result uses Planck only at ℓ < 1000 and no SPT [D-83].
5. **BBN dependence.** The H0 World Cup II knockout round reports that the residual tension of every group-E model rises under PRIMAT BBN rates (quote below). So the 3σ residual it quotes is the optimistic end.

The attack does not refute C3. The bounds that do not use r_d leave the 70.2–71.4 window open at ~0.5–2σ. The strongest pro-EDE source (Poulin 2026, below) is a peer-reviewed profile analysis that finds an EDE model at H0 = 73 fitting better than ΛCDM once DESI is included. The core survives only in the narrowed form above.

## Corrections to details
- C3's predictions say one-step methods "converge on ≈ 71–73" and r_d-free H0 "rises to ≥ 71–73". The independent combinations exclude the upper end, 73.26, at 3.6–4.4σ (with age) or 2.4–3.4σ (r_d-free alone). Only the ≈ 70–71.5 end is consistent with the bounds. Read the prediction as ≈ 70–71.5.
- "r_d-free values 66.7–70.3 lie 1.9–3.5σ below SH0ES" in the evidence-against clause: against H0DN 73.26 with each value's upper error, I get 2.1–3.9σ. This is a minor change in the same direction.
- Variant (e), electron-density fluctuations, should not be listed as a carrier. Even with a ladder prior it reaches 69.3 ± 0.5 [D-95], below the midpoint (P = 0.036). Variant (d), PMF, likewise gives 69.1 ± 0.5 (P = 0.014). Neither can carry "most of the gap", so the class claim rests on EDE alone.
- The ages clause quotes only the 73.26 case (2.7–3.4σ). For C3's actual best value, 71.0, the age tension is 1.5–2.2σ. That is weaker against C3 than stated, and fair to note.

## What survives and remaining doubt
Survives: EDE at f ≈ 0.09–0.10 bringing the true H0 to ≈ 70.2–71.4. No independent bound I tested excludes this window:
- r_d-free + one-step + age combinations give P(H0 > 70.2) = 0.10–0.41;
- the profile and eROSITA fits give 0.77–0.80;
- the age tension is ≤ 2.2σ.

The claim "carries most of the gap" is therefore viable only in this narrow EDE form and on a knife-edge:
- it needs a frequentist/profile reading over the Bayesian CMB bounds (NPIPE caps H0 at 69.5 at 95%; ACT P-ACT-LB leaves only 25% of its mass above 70.2);
- it needs the DESI DR2 Ω_m, whose role the SPT authors attribute to the DESI–CMB discrepancy [D-83];
- it needs a residual ≈ 2.3 km/s/Mpc ladder offset.

Remaining doubts:
- The r_d-free and equality measurements are still too imprecise (σ ≈ 1–2 km/s/Mpc) to separate 69 from 71 decisively.
- I did not compute S8 under EDE against DES Y6 (0.789 ± 0.012); I found no verbatim source for the EDE-fit S8 value.
- The EDE-independence of the equality scale is taken from the candidate's table, not re-derived here.

The decisive tests named in C3 would close the question: f_EDE > 0 at ≥ 3σ in SO / SPT-3G without a ladder prior, and an r_d-free H0 at σ ≈ 1 km/s/Mpc.
