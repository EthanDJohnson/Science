# Verdict on C8 (refuter 0, angle: magnitude)
claim: The ≥5σ significance rests on one team's Cepheid-calibrated SN ladder; the team-independent anomaly is a smaller ~4 km/s/Mpc local excess at only about 3–3.7σ.
status: final
verdict: survives
basis: calculation
narrowed: none

Units: H0 in km s⁻¹ Mpc⁻¹ (written km/s/Mpc; astronomical units, not SI); magnitudes in mag; significances are Gaussian-equivalent σ. All errors are symmetrised totals (stat ⊕ sys) from the dossier IDs given.

## Strongest attack

From the magnitude angle, three attacks are possible. (1) Find a local combination that uses no SH0ES Cepheids and still reaches ≥ 5σ. That would refute the first clause. (2) Show that the "~4 km/s/Mpc at 3–3.7σ" figure is an artefact of the input choices: which CCHP value, whether TF (zero point from Cepheids) is kept, the shared NGC 4258 anchor, maser systematics, or the early-universe reference. (3) Show that the team-independent excess is not actually *smaller* than the SH0ES gap, that is, that the data cannot tell 4 km/s/Mpc from 6.3 km/s/Mpc.

Results:
- Attack (1) fails. No Cepheid-free or SH0ES-free combination in the dossier reaches 5σ. The highest is H0DN without Cepheids (D-23, 72.51 ± 1.30): 3.94σ against CMB-SPA. To reach 5σ at the non-SH0ES precision (σ = 1.14), the central value would have to be ≥ 73.20, which is essentially the SH0ES value.
- Attack (2) only blurs the edges. Over 12 variants the excess over CMB-SPA ranges from 3.99 to 5.20 km/s/Mpc (0.125–0.165 mag), at 2.88–3.94σ against CMB-SPA. The candidate's "~4, 3–3.7σ" sits inside that band. The only number outside it is the upper end, and only for H0DN-no-Cepheids (3.94σ), which is not team-independent. That variant still contains the SH0ES-side TRGB analyses that H0DN pools.
- Attack (3) is the strongest, but it hits the word "smaller", not the core. SH0ES minus the non-SH0ES mean is 1.80 ± 1.47 km/s/Mpc, only 1.22σ. The 95% interval on the non-SH0ES excess (2.14–6.86 km/s/Mpc) contains the full 6.30 km/s/Mpc gap, which lies 1.50σ above the central value. If the true local H0 were SH0ES's 73.49, the non-SH0ES set would be expected at 5.24σ. The observed 3.74σ falls short by 1.5σ (one-sided p = 0.067). So the team-independent excess is smaller only in central value, not as a measured difference.

The core claim is about significance: the ≥ 5σ needs SH0ES Cepheids, and without them about 3–3.7σ remains. It stands.

## Corrections to details

- "Smaller ~4 km/s/Mpc" holds only for the central value. The team-independent excess (4.50 ± 1.20 km/s/Mpc) is consistent with the full 6.30 km/s/Mpc SH0ES gap at 1.5σ. The SH0ES-vs-rest difference is 1.2σ, and the all-local χ² is 2.93 for 6 dof. The data do not show that the anomaly is smaller. They show that, without SH0ES, it is less significant. The candidate's own "evidence against" (the "SH0ES vs everything else" partition fits worst) points the same way.
- The 3–3.7σ range holds against CMB-SPA (and against 67.24 ± 0.35) only. Against early values that include BAO it is lower: 2.5σ vs BAO+BBN, 3.0σ vs DESI+CMB, and 2.8σ vs P-ACT-LB DR2 for the 6-class mean, and 1.8–3.3σ across variants. The heading should name its reference.
- Across defensible input choices the team-independent range is 2.9–3.9σ against CMB-SPA, not 3.2–3.7σ. Leaving out SBF gives 2.88σ. H0DN-no-Cepheids gives 3.94σ, but it is not team-independent.
- Against the 6-class mean, the size in magnitudes is 0.141 mag, a little above the 0.126–0.137 mag quoted from M-EMPIRICIST-12. That figure used 71.21–71.56. Over the variants the range is 0.125–0.165 mag.
- "≥5σ rests on SH0ES" also depends on the early reference. SH0ES alone reaches ≥ 5σ only against CMB-based values (5.2–6.3σ). Against BAO+BBN it is 4.55σ.
- Part of the "team-independent" set is not fully independent of the anchor or the Cepheid calibration. TF takes its zero point from Cepheids and TRGB [D-30], and CCHP, SBF and the masers share NGC 4258. Correcting for both lowers the excess slightly (3.40–3.50σ). This helps C8 rather than hurting it.

## What survives and remaining doubt

Both parts of the core survive the magnitude attack:
- No SH0ES-free or Cepheid-free combination reaches 5σ (maximum 3.94σ).
- The team-independent local excess is about 4–4.5 km/s/Mpc at about 3–3.7σ against the CMB.

The remaining doubt is about interpretation, not arithmetic. The current non-SH0ES precision (σ ≈ 1.1–1.3 km/s/Mpc) cannot tell C8's 70–72 from SH0ES's 73.5: the gap between them is only 1.2σ. So "the anomaly is smaller" is not yet a measured fact. To separate 71.7 from 73.5 at 3σ, a team-independent local H0 needs σ ≈ 0.6 km/s/Mpc (0.8%), tighter than the σ ≤ 1.1 km/s/Mpc in the candidate's decisive test (that bound is for 3σ against CMB-SPA, M-EMPIRICIST-09). M-EMPIRICIST-09 gives 0.60 km/s/Mpc as the requirement for 73 vs 70 at 5σ. For 73.5 vs 71.7 (a gap of 1.8 km/s/Mpc) at 3σ, 1.8/3 = 0.60 km/s/Mpc.
