## Response to the verdicts

Two verdicts say C8 survives (C8-0 magnitude, C8-1 evidence) and one says it is weakened (C8-2 bounds). All three agree on the first clause: no current combination without SH0ES-team ladder analyses reaches 5σ against any early reference. The highest values are 3.94σ (H0DN V08, no Cepheids, against CMB-SPA [math: M-STATISTICIAN-01]) and 4.92σ (Cepheid-free but with SH0ES-side TRGB, against SPT+ACT [C8-2]). The attacks hit the second and third clauses. I concede most of them and accept a narrowed core:

> **Narrowed core.** The ≥5σ headline requires the SH0ES team's Cepheid-SN ladder and a CMB-based early reference. Without SH0ES-team analyses, the late-universe excess is 4.0–4.5 ± 1.2 km/s/Mpc (0.125–0.141 mag). It sits at 2.5–4.2σ depending on the early reference: 3.0–3.7σ against Planck-containing CMB values, 2.5σ against BAO+BBN, 4.15σ against SPT+ACT. Whether it is *smaller* than SH0ES's 6.30 km/s/Mpc is not yet measured (1.2–1.5σ). The "only ~3σ" status reflects current precision, not a measured property.

### C8-0 (magnitude; survives)

1. **"Smaller" holds only for the central value.** Conceded in full. SH0ES minus the rest is 1.80 ± 1.47 km/s/Mpc, 1.22σ, and the all-local χ² is 2.93 for 6 degrees of freedom [C8-0]. I add a point that makes this worse for C8 today. With SH0ES's own error kept at 0.93 km/s/Mpc, even a *perfect* independent measurement at 71.69 would differ from SH0ES by at most 1.80/0.93 = 1.93σ (log [2]). Read as a comparison between two measurements, the "smaller" clause cannot reach 3σ until SH0ES's own error shrinks. It can be tested only as a point hypothesis ("is the true local H0 73.49?"). That needs σ_new ≤ 0.60 km/s/Mpc (0.83%) for 3σ (log [2], [3]), which agrees with C8-0's 0.60 km/s/Mpc.
2. **3–3.7σ holds only against CMB-SPA.** Conceded. Against BAO+BBN the excess is 2.49σ (log [1]). That cuts both ways. C8-0 also shows that SH0ES itself is only 4.55σ against BAO+BBN, and H0DN only 4.80σ. Against the one early reference that uses no CMB anisotropies, *no* local result reaches 5σ, SH0ES included. This supports the reframe: the ≥5σ needs SH0ES Cepheids *and* a CMB reference.
3. **The band is 2.9–3.9σ over input variants, not 3.2–3.7σ.** Conceded as a correction. The 3.94σ end is H0DN without Cepheids, which C8-0 itself notes is not team-independent.
4. **0.141 mag rather than 0.126–0.137 mag at the 6-class mean.** Conceded. The full range over variants is 0.125–0.165 mag [C8-0].
5. **Partial team-independence (TF zero point, shared NGC 4258).** Accepted. As C8-0 says, correcting for it lowers the excess to 3.40–3.50σ, which helps C8.

### C8-1 (evidence; survives)

1. **"CCHP itself states ≈ 3σ" is mis-attributed.** Conceded and withdrawn as evidence for the size of the team-independent excess. Freedman et al.'s 3.0σ is CCHP's re-evaluation of SH0ES R22 (72.03 with an added SN-calibration term) against Planck [C8-1, quoting arXiv:2408.06153 p. 48]. It remains evidence that the ≥5σ is contested, nothing more.
2. **3.7σ is not a ceiling (V08 3.94σ; with the Stiskalek re-analysis 4.25σ).** Partly conceded. Both routes use SH0ES-team data: V08 uses SH0ES-side TRGB and Pantheon+, and Stiskalek re-analyses the SH0ES Cepheid photometry [C8-1]. So they do not breach "team-independent ~3–3.7σ". They do breach the looser "Cepheid-free ≤ 3.7σ" reading, and the heading should say "about 3–4σ".
3. **"Rests on" describes precision, not a shifted value. The ~4 versus 5–6 km/s/Mpc split depends on weighting CCHP TRGB over SH0ES-side TRGB on overlapping hosts.** This is the strongest point against C8, and I concede it substantively. V08B (Cepheids kept, 35 calibrators) 72.87 against V08 (no Cepheids) 72.51 shows that dropping the Cepheids moves H0DN by only 0.36 km/s/Mpc but costs precision (σ 0.87 → 1.30) [C8-1, quoting arXiv:2510.23823 p. 20–21]. The lower central value of the "team-independent" set therefore comes mostly from CCHP's TRGB selection (70.39 [D-24]), against SH0ES-side TRGB at 72.1–73.3 [D-27], and not from removing Cepheids. Part of my rebuttal stands: C8's claim is about *team* dependence, and the SH0ES–CCHP split is exactly a team split on overlapping data [D-80]. The 2.5 km/s/Mpc difference is attributed by SH0ES to sample selection [D-28] and by CCHP's Hoyt et al. partly to SN photometry, with Pantheon+ shifting by +2 km/s/Mpc (3.1σ) [D-25]. Which TRGB analysis is right is unresolved, so the size of the team-independent excess is unresolved by the same amount. C8 should say this openly rather than present 4 km/s/Mpc as a measured residual.
4. **TF is not team-independent.** Conceded. Dropping it gives 71.56 ± 1.19 and 3.50σ, with no change of conclusion.
5. **The significance depends on the reference.** Conceded, as under C8-0 point 2.

### C8-2 (bounds; weakened)

1. **Against SPT+ACT the excess is 4.15σ, so "only 3–3.7σ" is reference-selected.** Partly conceded. Both the number (log [1]: 4.15σ) and the range 2.5–4.2σ are right. My rebuttal is that SPT+ACT 66.59 is the *lowest* CMB combination [D-43], while CMB-SPA, which contains it, is the brief's stated reference (candidates.md conventions). Picking SPT+ACT is as much a reference choice as picking CMB-SPA. The fair statement is the full band with each reference named, as in the narrowed core.
2. **Cepheid-free sets with SH0ES-side or H0DN TRGB reach 4.1–4.9σ. So the claim holds on "Cepheid", not on "one team".** I rebut the framing. C8-2's own calculation shows that no Cepheid-free set reaches 5σ: the maximum is 4.92σ, and only against SPT+ACT. The sets that get close all use SH0ES-team TRGB (Li 2026 [D-27]) or H0DN, which pools it [D-22]. That is what "rests on one team's ladder" predicts. Where C8-2 is right is the wording: the heading should read "one team's ladder analyses (Cepheid and SH0ES-side TRGB)", not only "Cepheid-calibrated SN ladder".
3. **"Smaller" holds at only 1.2–1.5σ.** Conceded, as under C8-0 point 1. I also accept C8-2's decomposition: 59–71% of the drop from 6.27σ to 3.74σ comes from the shift of the central value, which is itself only a 1.2σ effect.
4. **"Local excess" includes time delays and sirens, which are not local.** Conceded. "Late-universe excess" is the correct term.
5. **H0DN without Cepheids is already 3.94σ, above the stated ceiling.** Conceded, as under C8-1 point 2.

### A further concession the verdicts imply

C8's "only ~3–3.7σ" is a statement about the *current* precision of the non-SH0ES data, and it will expire. At σ_new = 0.60 km/s/Mpc, any team-independent value ≥ 70.74 km/s/Mpc is ≥ 5σ above CMB-SPA (log [5]). At C8's own predicted 70–72, a sub-1% team-independent result would therefore restore the "≈5σ" premise without SH0ES while leaving the "smaller" clause intact. The part of C8 that can survive better data is the central-value claim: the team-independent late-universe value lies *below* SH0ES and *above* the early values. The "only 3σ" clause cannot.

## Deciding observation

**The single observation:** a team-independent late-universe H0, at total σ ≤ 0.60 km/s/Mpc (0.83%), with no SH0ES-team Cepheid, TRGB or SN-calibrator analysis in it. Its centre is compared with SH0ES's 73.49 as a point hypothesis and with CMB-SPA 67.19 ± 0.38.
- **Sources:** a full ≥ 35-host TRGB/JAGB ladder run by CCHP or a third team on one SN photometric system, combined with anchor-free one-step methods.
- **C8 survives** if the value falls in 69.3–71.7 km/s/Mpc. That window means "smaller than SH0ES at 3σ" (≤ 71.69) and "excess over CMB-SPA real at 3σ" (≥ 69.32) (log [5]).
- **C8 fails** if the value is ≥ 72.5 or ≤ 68.5.

Against each rival:

- **vs C1 (ladder systematic, true H0 ≈ 67–68.5).**
  - Predictions: C1 says team-independent and anchor-free values converge to 67–68.5. C8 says 70–72.
  - Precision: separating 71.0 from 68.5 at 3σ needs σ ≤ 0.83 km/s/Mpc (1.17%); from 67.5 it needs σ ≤ 1.17 (log [3]).
  - A value ≤ 68.5 kills C8's "real excess" clause and favours C1. A value ≥ 69.3 at σ ≤ 0.6 favours C8 over C1's full-gap version.
  - Fastest arm: time delays. TDCOSMO is at 3.3% with 11 lenses [D-31]. Under an ASSUMED 1/√N scaling with no systematic floor, about 54 lenses reach σ ≈ 1.06 (log [6]). The review forecast is about 40 lenses for 1% (Treu 2026, dossier §6).
- **vs C3 (early-time new physics; ladder ≈ 73 essentially right).**
  - Predictions: C3 expects the anchor-free and team-independent ladders at ≈ 71–73, with at most a subdominant ladder offset. C8 expects them clearly below SH0ES.
  - The same observation decides between them. A value at 72.5–73.5 favours C3 (and refutes C8's "smaller" clause). A value ≤ 71.7 at σ ≤ 0.6 favours C8.
  - Precision: separating 71.0 from 73.0 at 3σ needs σ ≤ 0.67 km/s/Mpc (0.94%) (log [3]). With time delays alone that is about 135 lenses under the same no-floor assumption (log [6]).
  - The early-side test (SO / SPT-3G Ext detecting f_EDE > 0 without a ladder prior) bears on C3's cause, not on C8, which predicts nothing about the CMB.
- **vs C4 (late-time new physics; strongest variant (ii), an M_B / G_eff step).**
  - Predictions: C4(ii) says every stellar-candle ladder stays at ≈ 73 under any re-standardisation, team-independent TRGB included, while geometric one-step methods read ≈ 67–68 [candidates C4]. C8 says both arms read 70–72 and agree.
  - Deciding quantity: the split between a team-independent TRGB ladder and the anchor-free one-step aggregate. C4(ii) predicts about 5.5 km/s/Mpc; C8 predicts about 0.
  - Precision: 3σ on that split needs σ ≤ 1.83 km/s/Mpc on the difference, about 1.30 on each arm (log [4]). This is the cheapest of the crux tests.
  - C4(i) (smooth H(z)) is already pinned by the inverse ladder [D-48, D-70], and C8 does not dispute that.
- **vs C6 (no dominant cause, true H0 ≈ 69–71).** I concede that the local data cannot cleanly separate C8 from C6.
  - Predictions: both expect team-independent ladders at about 70–71.5 and both reject SH0ES's 73.5. C6 predicts the CCHP full-host set at ≥ 71.5 but < 73 and the one-step methods at 68–71; C8 predicts 70–72.
  - The separating observation lies on the early side. C6(a) expects a partial early-time shift of +1.5 to +3 km/s/Mpc (EDE f ≈ 0.05–0.09) or the DESI–CMB Ω_m offset to carry part of the gap. C8's prediction row puts CMB, BAO+BBN and the inverse ladder at 0.
  - SO / SPT-3G Ext ΛCDM-extended fits without a ladder prior that move the early value up by ≥ 1.5 km/s/Mpc favour C6. An early side that stays at 67–68.5 while a team-independent local value at σ ≤ 0.6 lands at 69.3–71.7 favours C8's reading: a single late-universe excess, with SH0ES's extra ≈ 2 km/s/Mpc being team-specific.
  - The dossier gives no release dates for SO or SPT-3G Ext (§6).
- **vs C7 (two calibrations; all anchor-calibrated ladders share one scale at 73.02 ± 0.78).**
  - Predictions: C7 says the anchor-calibrated ladders are mutually consistent across teams [math: M-EXAMINER-06]. It is falsified "if the anchor-calibrated ladders split from each other by more than their errors" [candidates C7]. C8 says the team-independent anchor-calibrated ladder sits ≥ 1.8 km/s/Mpc below SH0ES.
  - Deciding observation: the same full ≥ 35-host CCHP or third-team TRGB/JAGB ladder on one SN photometric system. A result ≈ 73 favours C7; a result ≤ 71.7 at σ ≤ 0.6 (a team split at ≥ 3σ against the 73.49 point) favours C8.
  - Today the split is CCHP 70.39 ± 1.94 against SH0ES-side TRGB 72.1–73.3 on overlapping hosts [D-24, D-27]. It is unresolved; C8-1 point 3 is conceded above.

**Who and when.**
- **Ladders:** CCHP or any third team with JWST TRGB/JAGB on the full SH0ES calibrator host list, on a common SN system (CSP or a re-calibrated Pantheon+). Hoyt et al. call for "independent analyses of low-redshift SNe" [D-80].
- **One-step methods:**
  - TDCOSMO and ELT time-delay follow-up;
  - LVK bright sirens: IR1 planned for mid-January 2027, O5 timeline "in discussion" [dossier §6];
  - Rubin and Roman lensed SNe.
- **Precision needed:** σ ≤ 0.60 km/s/Mpc (0.83%) for the C8, C3 and C7 comparisons; σ ≤ 0.83 for C1; about 1.3 per arm for C4(ii).
- **Dates:** the dossier gives no collaboration-stated H0 precision target or date for any of these programmes (§6). Gaia DR4 (2 Dec 2026) tests only the MW anchor [dossier §6]. H0DN without MW parallaxes is already 73.609 [D-23], so DR4 does not decide C8.
