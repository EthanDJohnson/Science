## Response to the verdicts

All three verdicts return `weakened`, and none returns `refuted`. I accept the narrowing they converge on. What survives is this:

> **C3 (narrowed).** Axion-like early dark energy (EDE) at f_EDE ≈ 0.07–0.10 shrinks r_d by about 4–5% against the CMB h·r_d. The true global H0 is then ≈ 70.2–71.4, so EDE carries 50–69% of the gap. A subdominant ladder offset of ≈ 1.9–3.0 km/s/Mpc (≈ 0.06–0.09 mag) remains. Full closure to ≈ 73 is not claimed.

That is the strongest version I can defend. It sits on the boundary with C6, and I say so below.

### Concessions (what the verdicts take away)

1. **Full closure is gone.** No fit without a ladder prior reaches 73: the best are 71.0 ± 1.1 [D-53] and 71.4 ± 1.4 [D-94]. C3-2's combinations of r_d-free, one-step and age data exclude 73.26 at 3.6–4.4σ, and that bound does not depend on r_d [calc: calc/falsifier-C3-2_bounds.py]. C3's predictions "one-step ≈ 71–73" and "r_d-free ≥ 71–73" should read **≈ 70–71.5**. I accept C3-2's correction.
2. **The class claim rests on EDE alone.** Each other variant falls below the midpoint on its own fits (C3-2):
   - PMF: 69.1 ± 0.5, P(>70.2) = 0.014 [D-52].
   - Electron-density fluctuations: 69.3 ± 0.5, even with a ladder prior [D-95].
   - Modified recombination: 69.6 ± 1.0, P = 0.27 [D-52]. Varying m_e fails once DESI DR2 is added [D-76].
   - ΔN_eff: 11–12σ [math: M-CONSTRAINTS-08].

   So "pre-recombination new physics, led by EDE" becomes "axion-like EDE (H0 World Cup II group E)". Variants (b)–(e) become at most partial contributors under C6.
3. **The most complete CMB data alone do not support it.** C3-1 quotes Khalife et al. 2025 (SPT): CMB-SPA EDE gives H0 = 68.03 +0.53/−1.1 and f_EDE < 0.070, which closes 13.8% of the gap. The authors write that "from CMB data alone, AEDE cannot be considered as a solution to the Hubble tension" [calc: crux-C3_deciding.py, block 1]. Planck NPIPE caps H0 at 69.5 (95%) [D-53]. I concede that the Bayesian CMB-only frame puts the EDE share well below half.
4. **World Cup II is not ladder-independent support.** C3-1 shows that its AIC is computed on CMB+BAO+SN data together with the SH0ES M_B prior. I withdraw "strongly favored over ΛCDM" as evidence for C3. That paper's ≈ 3σ residual after group E stands, and it rises further under PRIMAT BBN rates (C3-2, p. 44 quote).
5. **The r_d-free probes lean against C3, but not decisively.**
   - The current Zaborowski values combine to 69.73 ± 0.80–1.32 (C3-0). That gives P(H0 > 70.23) = 0.27–0.35 and puts 71.0 at 0.96–1.59σ above the combination [calc: crux-C3_deciding.py, block 5].
   - This is C3's own cleanest test, and it currently points below the boundary.
6. **The DESI route partly reflects the DESI–CMB offset.**
   - DESI's h·r_d (101.54 Mpc) is 2.81% above CMB-SPA's (98.77 Mpc) [D-44, D-45].
   - Under DESI h·r_d, reaching the midpoint needs r_d only −1.64%. Under CMB h·r_d it needs −4.32% [calc: crux-C3_deciding.py, block 2].
   - So the half of the gap that DESI-including fits gain is partly the 2.3–2.8σ Ω_m offset [D-87], not r_d physics. The SPT authors say the same [D-83].
7. **The Lyman-α bound is a new strike I cannot answer from the inputs.** Goldstein et al. 2023 give f_EDE < 0.03 (95%) with Planck+BAO+Lyα, as quoted in C3-1. C3 needs f ≈ 0.065–0.072 to reach the midpoint (C3-0), which is 2.2–2.4 times that bound. I can only add the limitations that C3-1 itself states:
   - the bound comes from one observable, the eBOSS P1D, from before DESI;
   - its abstract says the bound is "driven by the preference of the Lyα forest data for n_s values lower than those required by EDE", so it is only as strong as the P1D modelling.

   I found no re-analysis. This bound stands against C3 until a DESI-era P1D EDE analysis exists.

### Rebuttals (with calculation or source)

1. **C3-0's "second dominance condition" is not a separate strike.**
   - The early shift s and the ladder residual r always sum to the gap: s = H_true − 67.19 and r = 73.264 − H_true.
   - So s > r holds exactly when H_true > 70.227. The script asserts this identity for every fit [calc: crux-C3_deciding.py, block 1].
   - For the ACT fits, "ladder residual larger than the early shift" therefore restates "below the midpoint". It is one failure, not two.
   - At Poulin (71.0) the shift is 3.81 against a residual of 2.26. At eROSITA (71.4) it is 4.21 against 1.86. Both conditions hold there.
2. **The verdicts split by statistical framework, not by data quality.**
   - Every fit that clears the midpoint is a profile likelihood or includes independent cluster data: Poulin 71.0 ± 1.1, P(>70.23) = 0.76–0.77; eROSITA 71.4 ± 1.4, P = 0.80 (C3-0, C3-2).
   - Every Bayesian CMB-centric fit falls below it.
   - Poulin et al. (peer-reviewed, PRD 113 063519, quoted in C3-0 and C3-2) report "significant prior-volume effects in Bayesian analyses which do not include SH0ES". The dossier records this dispute as open [D-75, D-83].
   - The Bayesian upper bounds f < 0.061–0.070 sit at 0.85–1.07 times the dominance threshold (C3-0). They are therefore not an exclusion of the threshold at ≥ 3σ.
   - No verdict refutes the profile result. Each one names it as the reason it chose not to refute.
3. **eROSITA is the independent leg, and C3-1 only partly answers it.** Strunk et al. 2026 report f_EDE = 0.10 +0.04/−0.03 (≈ 3.2σ) and H0 = 71.4 ± 1.4 with no ladder prior [D-94], and the paper is accepted by A&A. It uses cluster counts, a probe outside both the CMB and BAO. It is the only no-ladder EDE preference not routed through DESI h·r_d alone, although its "external data" includes BAO. I keep it as the strongest single support. It is in direct conflict with the Lyα bound: f = 0.10 is 3.3 times the Lyα 95% limit of 0.03. One of the two must be wrong.
4. **The age bound at C3's actual value is weak.** C3-2 corrects the age clause in C3's favour. At H0 = 71.0 the tension with the globular-cluster (GC) age is 1.5–2.2σ, not 2.7–3.4σ [calc: calc/falsifier-C3-2_bounds.py]. I accept that number.
5. **The older r_d-free set is superseded.** C3-1 traces arXiv:2411.16677 to Zaborowski et al. (JCAP 06 (2025) 020), the same team's earlier analysis. The current values are therefore 69.2–70.3, not 66.7–70.3. This removes the 2.06–2.53σ entries (the 2411.16677 DESY5 value) and C3-2's set C, which uses 2411 Pantheon+. Set C feeds set F, the least-favourable combination (F+age: 68.89 ± 1.04, P(>70.2) = 0.103). That combination leans on a superseded value and should be dropped. Set E+age (69.27 ± 0.91, P = 0.153) uses current data, but it is pulled low by the pre-DESI BOSS equality value of 65.6 [D-50]. Set D+age (69.99 ± 0.89, P = 0.405) is the fairest figure [calc: calc/falsifier-C3-2_bounds.py]. Even so, the honest range stays P(>70.2) ≈ 0.15–0.41: still below even odds.

### Net position

C3 survives only as a knife-edge claim: the true global H0 is ≈ 70.2–71.4, driven by EDE at f ≈ 0.07–0.10. Weighed across the verdicts, the probability that the EDE share exceeds half is roughly:
- 0.76–0.80 in the profile and eROSITA readings;
- 0.01–0.35 in the Bayesian CMB readings and the r_d-free readings;
- below 0.05 if the Lyα bound holds.

The central reading of current data is that EDE carries less than half. C3 therefore stands behind C6 on present evidence, and the deciding tests below are what could reverse that.

## Deciding observation

Separations below are differences of predicted central values divided by the target σ, ignoring the poles' own errors (optimistic). Lens counts assume pure 1/√N scaling from TDCOSMO-2026's 11 lenses at ±2.35 km/s/Mpc, with no systematic floor (optimistic) [D-31; calc: crux-C3_deciding.py, block 3].

- **vs C1 (ladder systematic; true H0 67–68.5).**
  - Observation: an anchor-free, r_d-free global H0, from time delays, bright sirens or lensed SNe.
  - Predictions: C3 says 70.2–71.4 (central 71.0). C1 says 67–68.5.
  - Precision: separating 71.0 from C1's upper edge 68.5 at 3σ needs σ ≤ 0.83 km/s/Mpc (≈ 88 lenses). Separating it from C1's central 67.75 needs σ ≤ 1.08 (5σ: 0.50 and 0.65). Separating C3's lower edge 70.23 from 68.5 at 3σ needs σ ≤ 0.58 (≈ 181 lenses).
  - Reading: ≥ 70.2 favours C3. ≤ 68.5 favours C1 and kills C3.
  - A cheaper discriminant runs alongside it. C1 predicts f_EDE = 0 in the CMB. A detection of f_EDE > 0 at ≥ 3σ without a ladder prior, from SO or SPT-3G Ext, at f ≈ 0.07–0.09 needs σ(f) ≤ 0.023–0.030 [calc: block 4]. Such a detection favours C3; a bound f < 0.03 favours C1.
  - Who and when: TDCOSMO with JWST/ELT follow-up; LVK O5 ("in discussion"); Rubin/Roman lensed SNe. No collaboration H0 date was found [dossier §6]. The review forecast is ≈ 40 lenses for 1% [dossier §6].
- **vs C4 (late-time physics, mainly the M_B/G_eff step (ii)).**
  - Observation: the same anchor-free one-step H0.
  - Predictions: C4(ii) says ≈ 67–68 for geometric channels; C3 says ≈ 71.
  - Precision: 71.0 against 67.5 needs σ ≤ 1.17 km/s/Mpc at 3σ, or 0.70 at 5σ (≈ 44 or 124 lenses).
  - A second, independent discriminant: SNe Ia in hosts at 45–100 Mpc with JWST TRGB or geometric distances. C4(ii) needs a 0.15–0.19 mag M_B step there. C3 needs no step: at most a uniform ladder offset of ≈ 0.06–0.09 mag shared by calibrators and the flow. A step ≥ 0.15 mag favours C4. Absence of a step at ≤ 0.05 mag precision favours C3.
  - C4(i), a smooth H(z) change, cannot move H0·r_d [D-70]. C3 escapes that no-go by construction.
- **vs C6 (null; true H0 ≈ 69–71, partial EDE allowed). This is the real crux.**
  - The two differ only in whether the EDE-driven shift exceeds half the gap: true H0 above 70.23 and f_EDE above ≈ 0.065–0.072 (C3-0).
  - First observation, a true-H0 measurement: an r_d-free or anchor-free H0. Separating C3's central 71.0 from the 70.23 boundary needs σ ≤ 0.39 km/s/Mpc at 2σ, or 0.26 at 3σ. Separating it from C6's central ≈ 70.0 at 3σ needs σ ≤ 0.33. That is ≈ 400 lenses at 0.39, out of reach of every programme in the dossier.
  - Second observation, f_EDE itself: a CMB measurement with no ladder prior, analysed both with and without DESI, from SO / SPT-3G Ext. Telling C3's f = 0.09 (Poulin) from the threshold 0.068 needs σ(f) ≤ 0.011 at 2σ, or ≤ 0.0073 at 3σ [calc: block 4]. The Bayesian and profile analyses must agree to within that precision.
  - Reading:
    - f ≥ 0.07 at ≥ 3σ from zero, stable without DESI, favours C3.
    - f ≈ 0.03–0.06, or a preference that tracks the DESI–CMB Ω_m offset, favours C6.
    - A DESI-era Lyα P1D analysis confirming f < 0.03 kills C3 outright.
  - Forecast σ(f) for SO is not in the inputs, and no release dates were found [dossier §6].
- **vs C7 (reframe: the conflict is between M_B and r_d, not between expansion rates).**
  - C3 accepts C7's premise. C3 is one of the answers C7 leaves open (an r_d calibration error), so the two are compatible.
  - What separates them is whether the r_d error has its own signature beyond r_d. C3 (EDE) predicts f_EDE > 0, ω_c about +22% and damping-tail/TE residuals in the CMB **alone**. C7 needs only a calibration mismatch and predicts nothing inside the CMB.
  - Precision: a CMB-only (no BAO, no ladder) detection of f_EDE at ≥ 3σ needs σ(f) ≤ 0.023 at f = 0.07.
  - Reading: a detection favours C3 as the cause behind C7's frame. A null result while the r_d-free H0 sits ≥ 70.2 leaves C7's frame without C3's cause.
  - Who and when: SO / SPT-3G Ext-10k; dates not found.
- **vs C8 (reframe: ≥ 5σ rests on SH0ES; the team-independent excess is ≈ 4 km/s/Mpc at 3–3.7σ).**
  - C3 (narrowed) is largely compatible with C8: the non-SH0ES local mean of 71.2–71.7 sits on C3's 71.0.
  - Observation: a team-independent local or one-step H0 at ≤ 1.5% (≈ 1.07 km/s/Mpc).
  - Reading:
    - 70.2–72 is compatible with both.
    - Below 70.2 keeps C8 alive (it predicts 70–72) but fails C3.
    - ≥ 72.5 refutes C8's premise but also exceeds every no-ladder EDE fit (maximum 71.4). C3 would then need a larger r_d shift than any fit gives, so C3 survives only if f_EDE ≳ 0.12 is later allowed.
  - Precision: a σ ≤ 0.58 km/s/Mpc result separates C3's lower edge 70.23 from 68.5 at 3σ. Separating 70.23 from 72.5 at 3σ needs σ ≤ 0.76.
  - Who: CCHP or a third team running the full ≥ 35-host TRGB/JAGB ladder on one SN system, plus TDCOSMO. No dates were found [dossier §6].

**Single deciding observation.** Make an f_EDE measurement from SO / SPT-3G Ext CMB data with no ladder prior, with σ(f) ≲ 0.01. Pair it with an anchor-free, r_d-free H0 at σ ≲ 0.6 km/s/Mpc.
- C3 needs f ≥ 0.07 and H0 ≥ 70.2.
- f < 0.03 and H0 ≤ 68.5 favour C1 (or C4).
- An f between 0.03 and 0.065 with H0 of 69–70.2 favours C6.
