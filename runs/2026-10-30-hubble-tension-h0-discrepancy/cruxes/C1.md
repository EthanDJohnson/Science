## Response to the verdicts

All three verdicts say "weakened", and all three make the same core attack: an error confined to the SN Ia step cannot move the ladders that skip SNe Ia, and those ladders read 72.4–74. I concede the main point. I contest one narrowing, and I offer one rescue, with its price stated.

**1. Concession: the SN Ia step cannot be the main locus.** All three verdicts make this point.
- H0DN's own with- and without-SNe comparison gives an SN-step contribution of +0.002 ± 0.047 mag (nested) or ± 0.0595 mag (independent channels) [verdicts C1-0, C1-2; D-23]. The H0DN paper says "Replacing SNe Ia with galaxy-based indicators changes H0 by less than 0.1 km s−1 Mpc−1" (quoted in C1-1 and C1-2).
- My recomputation agrees with theirs. An SN-step-only bias that reaches truth 68.5 falls short by 3.06σ (nested) or 2.42σ (independent), and for truth 67.19 by 3.96σ or 3.12σ.
- On those truths, the uncorrelated SN-free set (SBF, TF, SNe II, time delays, masers, sirens) sits 72.69 ± 1.32, which is 3.18σ above 68.5 and 4.17σ above 67.19 [calc: crux-C1_rescue.py].
- So the heading's "most likely at the SN Ia calibration step" is lost as the dominant locus. Sub-hypotheses (a), (b), (d) and (g) can each be at most a minority share.
- Verdict C1-1 is also right that the SNe II ladder (de Jaeger 2022, 75.4) was missing from C1's evidence against. It points the wrong way for C1.

**2. Concession: the endpoint narrows to the BAO+BBN end, about 68–68.5. 67.19 is disfavoured.**
- I accept C1-2's calculation that the anchor-free and r_d-free arbiters average 68.49–70.26 ± 1.04–1.32. That puts truth 67.0 at +1.19 to +3.12σ and truth 68.5 at −0.01 to +1.69σ.
- Every route below also costs about 1σ more at 67.19 than at 68.5.
- C1's stated range "67–68.5" therefore becomes "≈ 68–68.5". Within C1 the 1.3 km/s/Mpc difference between CMB-SPA and BAO+BBN (1.9–2.2σ) [candidates C2 evidence for] is left open.

**3. Rebuttal: the narrowed "true H0 ≈ 69.5–70" in C1-0 (and "≈ 68–70" in C1-2) is not a version of C1.**
- C1's mechanism leaves the CMB, BAO+BBN and the inverse ladder untouched (prediction matrix row: "0 (66.5–67.6 correct)", "0 (68.5)", "0 (67.2–67.7)"). Under C1 the true H0 is therefore the early-universe value, 67.19–68.50 [D-44, D-45], by construction.
- A truth of 69.5–70 needs the early values to read 1.0–2.8 km/s/Mpc low as well. That is a second, early-side contribution, which is C6(a) (hybrid), not C1.
- So C1 stands or falls with truth ≈ 68–68.5, and C1-0's "what survives" belongs to C6. I accept the implication: if a calibrator-free H0 lands at 69.5–70, C1 loses to C6, not merely shrinks.

**4. Partial rebuttal: one ladder-side route reaches the SN-free tracers without a 9σ anchor error. It is the Hubble-flow velocity treatment (c), combined with a minority SN-step share.**
- All three verdicts close the "upstream" escape through the anchors (9.4σ), Cepheid photometry (5.9σ) and primary-indicator agreement. I concede those.
- C1-0 and C1-2 both leave open (c) as a *methodological* peculiar-velocity correction. Dong 2026: forward Pantheon+ 74.30 → 71.37, which is 0.0874 mag, or 46.5% of the gap [D-82].
- That correction reaches every Hubble-flow ladder tracer: SBF, TF, SNe II, and the SN Ia flow sample. It does not reach time delays or sirens, which are at cosmological distance.
- If (c) applies to every Hubble-flow tracer, the SN step needs only the remainder, which the H0DN SN-vs-no-SN comparison bounds:

- At truth 68.5 this two-part ladder systematic costs about 1–2σ everywhere, against 2.4–3.2σ for the SN-step-only form. It still counts as one method's systematic under the run's boundary rule (candidates.md conventions: "several ladder-side effects that together carry most of the gap count as one method's systematic (C1)").

**What this rescue costs. I state these plainly:**
- (i) It rests on one preprint's lever (Dong 2026), which is contested. The Laniakea flow model moves H0 *up* (+0.6 for SNe, +1.4 for SBF) [D-36], and Dong's backward model moves the other way, 60.83 → 63.25 [D-82]. So the lever's size and even its sign depend on the model.
- (ii) Applying the full SN-derived 0.087 mag to SBF, TF and SNe II is my assumption. Nobody has published it. Masers were left unshifted because Stiskalek already applies non-linear velocity corrections [D-32].
- (iii) H0DN, whose 0.03 < z < 0.10 variant gives 72.667 [D-23], has not adopted it.
- (iv) As a physical coherent flow it is excluded: it would be 8.7× the ΛCDM cosmic variance (C1-0) or the C5 void (C1-2). It survives only as a modelling error.

On this footing C1 is a live but minority-probability explanation. It is not the SN-step story its heading tells.

**5. Smaller points.**
- *C1-0's CCHP inconsistency.* A uniform SN-step shift would push CCHP to 62–66. This is conceded, and the rescue in point 4 makes it smaller: with the SN-step share at 0.059 mag, CCHP 70.39 → 68.51 [arithmetic: 70.39 / 10^(0.0586/5)], close to truth. The flow share is already partly inside CCHP's CSP Hubble flow, so the true correction may be different. Not computed.
- *C1-0's lever-sum correction.* C1-0 says (a)+(d) reaches only 0.125 mag (69.15), and that the "0.177 mag" sum mixes in (c). Both points are accepted.
- *C1-1 on GC ages.* TDCOSMO's ladder-free combination, which includes GC ages, sits 2.7σ above Planck. This is accepted. GC ages disfavour 73.26 [math: M-CONSTRAINTS-06], but they do not pick 68.5 over 70.
- *C1-1 on the one-step drift.* Accepted. The one-step mean of 71.27 ± 1.89–1.93 is 1.43–1.46σ above 68.5 and 0.57σ from the 70.2 midpoint. It does not yet favour C1 over C6.
- *What I keep.* None of the three verdicts reaches 5σ against the ladder-systematic class. The early side is internally consistent (CMB experiments χ² = 2.42/2) [candidates C2]. The r_d-free arbiters exclude 73.26 at 2.9–3.8σ (C1-2 §5). The GC age disfavours 73.26 at 2.7–3.4σ [math: M-CONSTRAINTS-06]. The base rate for single-team precision anomalies ending as new physics is 0 of 12 [math: M-EMPIRICIST-11]. These still place the bulk of the gap on the local side. The question is whether it is one ladder systematic (C1) or several partial causes (C6).

**Narrowed C1 I now defend.** A ladder-side methodological systematic carries ≥ 78% of the gap, and the true global H0 is ≈ 68–68.5 km/s/Mpc, the BAO+BBN end. The likeliest composition is a Hubble-flow peculiar-velocity treatment common to all Hubble-flow tracers (≈ 0.09 mag) plus a minority SN calibrator-vs-flow standardisation share (≈ 0.06 mag). On current data this costs a ≈ 1–2σ upward fluctuation of the SN-free probes. The SN-step-only form is disfavoured at 2.4–4σ and is withdrawn as the dominant locus.

## Deciding observation

The precision figures are from [calc: crux-C1_rescue.py], section 2, which gives the σ needed to separate two predictions by n σ (σ ≤ d/n).

**C1 vs C3 (pre-recombination new physics, EDE).**
- *Observation:* a global H0 that is anchor-free and r_d-free, from strong-lensing time delays, bright sirens or sound-horizon-free BAO.
- *Predictions:* C1 predicts ≈ 68–68.5. C3 predicts ≥ 71: its best no-ladder fits are 71.0–71.4 [D-53, D-94], and its full form is 73.
- *Precision:* separating 68.5 from 71.0 needs σ ≤ 0.83 km/s/Mpc (1.2%) at 3σ and ≤ 0.50 (0.7%) at 5σ. Separating 68.5 from 73.26 needs ≤ 1.59 / ≤ 0.95.
- *Current status:* TDCOSMO-2026 is at 3.3% [D-31], and the r_d-free values are at ≈ 1.3–2 [D-49]. Neither is there yet.
- *Who and when:* TDCOSMO (review forecast ≈ 40 lenses for 1%), LVK (IR1 planned mid-January 2027; O5 timeline "in discussion"), and DESI sound-horizon-free BAO (data to end-2028, search-summary) [dossier §6]. No collaboration-stated date for the needed precision was found.
- *Secondary discriminator:* SO / SPT-3G damping-tail detection of f_EDE > 0 at ≥ 3σ without a ladder prior favours C3. C1 predicts f_EDE consistent with 0. No dates were found.

**C1 vs C4 (late-time new physics; the live variant is (ii), an M_B / G_eff step).**
- *Why the calibrator-free H0 does not separate them:* C4(ii) also predicts that the one-step methods read ≈ 67–68 [candidates C4].
- *What separates them:* *what the SN Ia brightness difference tracks*. C1 predicts that any calibrator-vs-Hubble-flow M_B difference follows a host or flow-model property (colour/dust, environment, peculiar-velocity model) and vanishes once these are matched. C4(ii) predicts a 0.146–0.188 mag step that follows distance (between z ≈ 0.01 and 0.023) whatever the host.
- *Observation:* direct M_B for SNe Ia in hosts at ≈ 45–100 Mpc with JWST TRGB or geometric distances, compared with the < 40 Mpc calibrators on one photometric system.
- *Predictions:* C1 predicts a step < 0.06 mag after host matching. C4(ii) predicts 0.146–0.188.
- *Precision:* σ(ΔM_B) ≤ 0.049 mag for 3σ, or ≤ 0.029 mag for 5σ, on a 0.146 mag step.
- *Who and when:* SH0ES/CCHP JWST programmes, plus a velocity-corrected SN residual search at z ≈ 0.01 (ZTF DR2+) [candidates C4]. No dates are in the inputs.

**C1 vs C6 (no single dominant cause; hybrid with truth ≈ 69–71).**
- *Observation:* the same anchor-free (and preferably r_d-free) global H0.
- *Predictions:* C1 predicts ≈ 68–68.5. C6 predicts 70–71, with no class landing at 73 ± 1 [candidates C6].
- *Precision:* separating 68.5 from 70.5 needs σ ≤ 0.67 km/s/Mpc (0.95%) at 3σ and ≤ 0.40 (0.6%) at 5σ. If C1's truth were 67.19, σ ≤ 1.10 would suffice at 3σ.
- *Hardest discriminator:* this is the hardest rival to separate, because point 3 puts C1's truth only ≈ 2 km/s/Mpc below C6's centre.
- *Supporting within-ladder test:* an independent re-analysis that applies one forward peculiar-velocity model to SN Ia *and* SBF/TF/SNe II Hubble-flow samples. C1 predicts that all of them drop by ≈ 0.09 mag together and that one named variable carries the residual. C6 predicts partial, uncorrelated shifts (≤ 1–3 km/s/Mpc) with no single carrier.
- *Who and when:* time-delay and siren teams as above; the flow re-analysis by Dong et al., Stiskalek et al. or H0DN [D-82, D-32, D-23]. No dates were found.

**C1 vs C7 (reframe: the gap is M_B vs r_d, and the anchor-calibrated ladders share one scale).**
- *The question:* whether the anchor-calibrated ladders split.
- *Predictions:* C7 predicts that all anchor-calibrated ladders, SN-based and SN-free, stay on one scale (73.02 ± 0.78) [math: M-EXAMINER-06]. C1's narrowed form predicts that, *under a common flow treatment*, the SN-based ladders sit ≈ 0.059 mag (truth 68.5) below the SN-free ones.
- *Observation:* the same-anchor, same-flow-model SN-vs-no-SN ladder offset.
- *Precision:* σ ≤ 0.0195 mag for 3σ (≤ 0.012 for 5σ). Today it is +0.002 ± 0.047 mag [verdicts C1-0, C1-2], so it needs a ≈ 2.4× tighter SN-free channel. That means more SBF/SNe II calibrators on the JWST TRGB zero point.
- *Why the flow part is not tested here:* C7 is agnostic about it, because it moves both channels equally. So this test decides C1's SN share. C1's main share is decided only by the calibrator-free H0 above. That test favours C7's "M_B side" over r_d if it lands near 68.5, which is compatible with C1.
- *Who and when:* H0DN or a third team. No dates were found.

**C1 vs C8 (reframe: the ≥ 5σ rests on one team; the team-independent excess is ≈ 4 km/s/Mpc at ≈ 3–3.7σ).**
- *What separates them:* whether the team-independent, SN-Ia-free local excess is real. C8 accepts it as real at 70–72. C1 needs it to be a ≈ 1–2σ fluctuation plus the flow-treatment correction.
- *Observation:* a team-independent, non-Cepheid local H0 (TRGB/JAGB-SBF, SNe II, TF), with the Hubble-flow velocity model fixed blind before unblinding.
- *Predictions:* C1 predicts ≤ 69 (≈ 68.5 + fluctuation). C8 predicts 70–72.
- *Precision:* separating 68.5 from 71.0 needs σ ≤ 0.83 km/s/Mpc (1.2%) at 3σ. That is tighter than C8's own stated target of σ ≤ 1.1 [candidates C8].
- *Who and when:* CCHP or a third team on the full ≥ 35-host TRGB/JAGB set on one SN photometric system, plus SBF/SNe II [dossier §6]. No collaboration date was found.

**Single most decisive observation overall.**
- *Observation:* an anchor-free, r_d-free global H0 at σ ≤ 0.67 km/s/Mpc (≈ 1%).
- *What each result would mean:*
  - ≈ 68–68.5 favours C1 (and C4(ii); the M_B-step test above then separates those two);
  - 70–71 favours C6 or C8;
  - ≥ 71 with an SO/SPT-3G f_EDE detection favours C3.
- *Who and when:* TDCOSMO with ≈ 40–50 lenses [dossier §6; math: M-STATISTICIAN-12], LVK O5 sirens, and DESI sound-horizon-free BAO. No collaboration-stated date for this precision exists in the inputs.
