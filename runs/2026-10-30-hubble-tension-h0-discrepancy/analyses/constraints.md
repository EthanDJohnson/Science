# Analysis: constraints (Kant)
status: final

## Method applied
Audit of every condition a valid explanation of the local-vs-early H0 gap must satisfy: the inverse-distance-ladder (H0·r_d) and θ_* geometric constraints, the sound-horizon budget of each early-time variant at background level, the null-energy condition and stability for late-time H(z), M_B/G_eff physics, ΛCDM cosmic variance and Copernican bounds for a local void, and the error-budget inflation the null needs. Each candidate gets the magnitude it requires, set against the bound and that bound's assumptions. Units: H0 in km s⁻¹ Mpc⁻¹ (km/s/Mpc), distances in Mpc (cosmology units, not SI), magnitudes in mag, ages in Gyr.

## Findings

1. **Size of the gap that every candidate must close.** H0DN-corrected 73.264 ± 0.806 [D-22] minus CMB-SPA 67.19 ± 0.38 [D-44] = 6.07 km/s/Mpc, i.e. 9.0% of the early value or 0.188 mag in 5 log10 H0 (naive, uncorrelated 6.8σ). Against DESI DR2 BAO+BBN 68.50 ± 0.58 [D-45] the gap is 4.76 km/s/Mpc (7.0%, 0.146 mag, 4.8σ). With SH0ES 73.49 ± 0.93 [D-20] in place of H0DN it is 6.30 km/s/Mpc (0.195 mag, 6.3σ) against CMB-SPA. The target is therefore 0.15–0.20 mag (7–9.4%), depending on which early value is used [calc: runs/2026-10-30-hubble-tension-h0-discrepancy/calc/lens-constraints_geometry.py].
2. **Inverse-ladder budget (H0·r_d).** With DESI DR2's ΛCDM h·r_d = 101.54 ± 0.73 Mpc [D-45], H0 = 73.26 needs r_d = 138.6 ± 1.8 Mpc. That is 5.7% below the CMB-SPA r_d = 147.00 ± 0.24 Mpc [D-44] and 4.6σ from it. With the CMB-SPA's own h·r_d (0.6719 × 147.00 = 98.77 Mpc) it needs 134.8 ± 1.5 Mpc (an 8.3% reduction, 8.1σ). The Knox–Millea β = 29.54 ± 0.406 [D-03] gives 138.5 ± 2.4 Mpc (5.8%, 3.5σ). The 2.6% spread between the DESI and CMB versions is the DESI–CMB Ω_m discrepancy [D-87]: DESI's h·r_d with the CMB r_d already gives H0 = 69.07 [calc: .../lens-constraints_geometry.py].
3. **θ_* budget for a pre-recombination change with late-time ΛCDM kept.** At fixed Ω_m = 0.3175 (BAO and SNe fix Ω_m), keeping 100θ_* requires r_* to fall by 8.23% for H0 = 73.26 (8.51% for 73.49), and ω_c must rise by 22.5% (0.1203 → 0.1474). If ω_c is held fixed instead, r_* need only fall by 1.69%, but Ω_m then drops to 0.267: 3.5σ below DESI's 0.2975 ± 0.0086 and 9σ below CMB-SPA's 0.3175 ± 0.0055. At background level this reproduces the Jedamzik–Pogosian–Zhao squeeze [D-18, D-73]: an early-time fix must either raise ω_c by about 20% (raising S8 and ω_m h²) or lower Ω_m (conflicting with BAO). The integrators agree with scipy quad to 7e-11 (D_M) and 5e-10 (r_*) [calc: .../lens-constraints_geometry.py].
4. **A CMB analysis systematic within ΛCDM.** At fixed 100θ_* = 1.0411 and ω_b = 0.02237, H0 = 73.26 needs ω_c = 0.1045, which is −0.0155, or 13× Planck's σ(ω_c) ≈ 0.0012, and gives Ω_m = 0.238. Alternatively, at fixed ω_c it needs 100θ_* = 1.0585, which is 58σ. The baseline reproduces H0 = 67.35 (Planck 2018 67.36). Planck, ACT and SPT would have to share the shift although their instruments, sky and multipole ranges are largely independent [D-02]. BAO+BBN and the inverse ladder, which use no CMB anisotropies [D-08, D-48], would still sit at 67–68.5 [calc: .../lens-constraints_geometry.py].
5. **A BAO-side systematic.** Along DESI's h·r_d = 101.54 Mpc with a BBN ω_b, H0 rises with Ω_m (d ln H0/d ln Ω_m = 0.52). H0 = 73.26 needs Ω_m = 0.338, which is +4.7σ from DESI's 0.2975 ± 0.0086 for ω_b = 0.02218 and +5.2σ for ω_b = 0.02166 (the Cooke-type D/H value [D-46]). It also contradicts the CMB-SPA r_d. A BAO reconstruction or fiducial-cosmology bias would have to move Ω_m by 13% while BAO and the CMB agree on θ_* [D-45] [calc: .../lens-constraints_geometry.py].
6. **Ages.** CMB-SPA ΛCDM gives t0 = 13.804 Gyr, reproducing the quoted 13.805 [D-44]. Any candidate that makes the global H0 equal to 73.26 while late-time ΛCDM holds (Ω_m 0.2975–0.3175) gives t0 = 12.66–12.89 Gyr. That is 2.7–3.4σ below the globular-cluster t_U = 13.81 ± 0.25 ± 0.23 Gyr [D-55, D-79], with stat and sys added in quadrature. The source is quoted at "2σ" [D-55]. The difference comes from the error combination and from the H0 and Ω_m chosen, so the bound is best read as 2–3.4σ: a strain, not a violation. It binds early-time new physics. It does not bind ladder systematics, local structure or an M_B transition, all of which leave the global H0 near 67 [calc: .../lens-constraints_geometry.py].

7. **EDE at background level (axion-like n = 3 fluid).** I fix 100θ_* = 1.0411 and keep Ω_m = 0.3175 (BAO- and SN-like closure). Reaching H0 = 73.26 then needs f_EDE = 0.124 at z_c = 3500, 0.137 at z_c = 10⁴ and 0.188 at z_c = 1500, with ω_c rising to about 0.146 (+22%). The published 95% bounds without SH0ES are 0.061 (Planck NPIPE), 0.070 (SPT+ACT+Planck) and 0.12 (P-ACT-LB) [D-75]. So the needed f lies at or beyond every bound, and 1.8–2× the tightest one. With ω_c held fixed, f = 0.052 suffices, but Ω_m then falls to about 0.27, which BAO exclude (finding 3). Best case at the tightest bound (f = 0.06–0.07, Ω_m fixed): about 70–70.5 km/s/Mpc, which is half the gap. The full Boltzmann fits agree in size: 69.3–71.0 [D-52, D-53]. The crude damping diagnostic (θ_D/θ_*, fully ionised plasma, ratio only) moves by +2.8% at f = 0.12. That is about 9.5 times the shift produced by ΔN_eff = +0.13, the 1σ ACT N_eff error, which I use as a yardstick [calc: runs/2026-10-30-hubble-tension-h0-discrepancy/calc/lens-constraints_early.py].
8. **ΔN_eff.** H0 = 73.26 needs N_eff = 4.32 with z_eq fixed and 4.41 with Ω_m fixed. Against ACT DR6's 2.86 ± 0.13 [D-52, D-74] these are 11–12σ. Even the unphysical fixed-ω_c closure, which lets Ω_m fall to 0.26, needs N_eff = 3.56 (5.4σ). Free-streaming dark radiation is eliminated as the dominant cause. A self-interacting variant is bounded by N_idr < 0.134 [D-52] and cannot supply ΔN_eff ≈ 1.3 either [calc: .../lens-constraints_early.py].
9. **Varying m_e (z_* and z_drag ∝ m_e, σ_T ∝ m_e⁻²).** With Ω_m held at 0.3175, H0 = 73.26 needs δm_e/m_e = +6.25%, giving a damping shift of +3.5% (about 12 yardsticks). Along the near-symmetry direction ω_b, ω_c ∝ m_e², it needs only δm_e/m_e = +2.94%, and the damping shift falls to −1.1% (3.6 yardsticks), the smallest of any variant. That is consistent with varying m_e having won the H0-Olympics gold [D-17]. But the solution then has Ω_m = 0.282 and ω_b = 0.0237. That Ω_m is 1.8σ below DESI DR2 (0.2975 ± 0.0086) and lower still than the SNe prefer (D-48: 0.322 ± 0.012, 3.3σ). That ω_b is 7% above the BBN value (about 2.8σ against a 0.02218 ± 0.00055 BBN prior; [D-46] gives η10 = 5.931 ± 0.051, i.e. ω_b ≈ 0.0217). This reproduces at background level the Lee et al. 2026 statement that varying m_e "generically lowers Ω_m" and fails once DESI DR2 is added [D-76]. Low-z bounds on m_p/m_e do not bind if m_e returns to its present value by z ≲ 10, so the model must be time-dependent [calc: .../lens-constraints_early.py].

10. **Late-time H(z) with standard r_d: the null energy condition and the inverse ladder both bind.** At fixed θ_*, ω_b and ω_c, a constant w reaches H0 = 73.26 only for w = −1.194. That is phantom, ρ + p < 0, which violates the NEC for the dark-energy component. It gives Ω_m = 0.266, 3.7σ below DESI's 0.2975 ± 0.0086. With the same r_d it shifts BAO D_M by −4.9% at z = 0.295 and −3.4% at z = 0.51, against DESI's 0.7% precision on h·r_d [D-45]. It also bends the SN Hubble diagram by −0.10 mag between z = 0.05 and z ≥ 0.3, once the offset is removed. The DESI DR2 CPL best fit (w0, wa) = (−0.42, −1.75) at fixed θ_* gives H0 = 63.7, *down*, which reproduces 63.6 +1.6/−2.1 [D-51] and confirms D-12 [calc: runs/2026-10-30-hubble-tension-h0-discrepancy/calc/lens-constraints_late.py].
11. **Hockey-stick (late step in H²) transitions.** All of these need w_eff,min between −1.5 and −8.7, so all violate the NEC. A step at z_t = 0.01, below the Hubble-flow sample, leaves BAO intact (≤ 0.3%), but the ladder then infers only 68.3 km/s/Mpc from 0.023 < z < 0.15, so it does not explain the ladder. Steps at z_t = 0.05, 0.15 and 0.30 let the ladder infer 71.0, 72.9 and 73.1 respectively. But they shift 100θ_* by +4.4σ, +12σ and +22σ (Λ held fixed). They change BAO D_M at z = 0.295 by −1.5%, −4.1% and −6.5%. And they put a −0.08 to −0.14 mag step into the SN shape between z ≤ 0.15 and z ≥ 0.3. That is 2–3.5× the 0.04 mag low/high-z offset between SN compilations that is already debated [D-85]. This is the D-70 no-go reproduced: any late-time H(z) that moves the ladder value either does not reach 0.023 < z < 0.15 or breaks the SN shape and BAO. Escapes need a non-standard r_d or an M_B change [calc: .../lens-constraints_late.py].
12. **M_B/G_eff transition: the size and the sign are both model-dependent.** Hubble-flow SNe must be brighter than calibrators by 0.146–0.188 mag (a luminosity ratio of 1.14–1.19). The Chandrasekhar scaling used by Marra–Perivolaropoulos (L ∝ M_Ch ∝ G^−3/2) needs G_far/G_near = 0.89–0.91 [D-92]. But Wright & Li find that after light-curve standardisation "a weaker (stronger) gravity leads to intrinsically less (more) luminous SNe Ia after applying the same shape matching rescaling procedure" [new: Wright & Li 2018, PRD 97, 083505 (arXiv:1710.07018), full-text via fetch_text, "decreasing G leads to a decrease in the average rescaled intrinsic peak luminosity"]. On that model the needed G change has the opposite sign, G_far/G_near ≈ 1.09–1.12. The exponent is my rough reading of their Fig. 7 axes. The transition must fall between the calibrators (z ≲ 0.01, lookback ≤ 144 Myr, ≤ 44 Mpc) and the Hubble flow (z ≥ 0.023, lookback ≥ 333 Myr). That is a window of about 190 Myr, or 1.4% of cosmic time, in the very recent past [calc: .../lens-constraints_late.py]. A 10% step in G that recent would also have acted on the Sun and on every old star for nearly all of their lives. That is a further bound I did not compute (my inference).

13. **Local underdensity.** I take the EH98 no-wiggle P(k) normalised to the CMB-SPA σ8 = 0.8137 [D-44]; σ(R) is converged between 4000 and 6000 k-nodes to 4 digits. A local excess of ΔH/H = +9.04% needs a top-hat contrast δ = −0.51 in linear theory, or −0.46 with the Bernardeau nonlinear relation. In ΛCDM, σ_δ is 0.155, 0.065, 0.037, 0.012 and 0.006 for R = 50, 100, 150, 300 and 450 h⁻¹ Mpc, so the needed δ is 3.3σ, 7.8σ, 14σ, 42σ and 84σ. The top-hat σ(ΔH/H) at 150 h⁻¹ Mpc is 0.65% (0.44 km/s/Mpc), consistent with D-10/D-37 (0.31 km/s/Mpc for the SN-weighted geometry). The only "cheap" void (R = 50 h⁻¹ Mpc) has its edge at z ≈ 0.017. That is inside the calibrator/Hubble-flow gap, so it cannot move SNe at z > 0.023 at all. A void covering the whole Hubble-flow sample (R ≳ 430 h⁻¹ Mpc) is 84σ. One with its edge inside the sample would show a step in the SN Hubble diagram, which is excluded for |δ| > 0.2 at 4–5σ [D-11]. A Copernican bound adds to this: |v| < 370 km/s with ΔH = 6.07 km/s/Mpc puts us within 61 Mpc of the void centre, a random-position probability of 8.6×10⁻⁴ for R = 640 Mpc [calc: runs/2026-10-30-hubble-tension-h0-discrepancy/calc/lens-constraints_void_null.py].
14. **The null needs error budgets wrong by a factor of about 3–4.** For H0DN and CMB-SPA to agree at 2σ, the local error must grow from 0.806 to 3.01 km/s/Mpc (×3.7), or an unmodelled common shift of 4.3 km/s/Mpc must exist. Against BAO+BBN the factor is ×2.9 and the shift 2.8 km/s/Mpc. For SH0ES the factors are ×3.4 against CMB-SPA and ×2.6 against BAO+BBN. The internal variant spread of H0DN is only 0.40 km/s/Mpc [D-23]. But published, contested single levers are each about 1–3 km/s/Mpc: the CCHP sample/method gap of about 2.5–3 [D-80], SN two-population standardisation −1.6 to −2.5 [D-35], host peculiar velocities −2.9 [D-82], and Cepheid selection −1.4 to −2.6 [D-88]. So two or three partly additive effects could supply 4 km/s/Mpc without any one dominating. No physical law is violated [calc: .../lens-constraints_void_null.py].
15. **Local probes that use no Cepheids, as a test of the "SH0ES against everything else" reframe.** I combine them with symmetrised errors and treat them as independent, which is approximate because SBF and CCHP share NGC 4258. Time delays, masers and sirens (one-step only) give 71.27 ± 1.93: 2.1σ above CMB-SPA, 1.0σ below H0DN, χ² = 0.02/2. Adding SBF (no SNe, no Cepheids) gives 72.26 ± 1.51: 3.3σ above CMB-SPA, 2.3σ above BAO+BBN, 0.6σ below H0DN. Adding CCHP TRGB as well gives 71.56 ± 1.19: 3.5σ above CMB-SPA, 1.2σ below H0DN, χ² = 1.27/4. So removing every Cepheid-based result leaves a 3.3–3.5σ excess over the CMB. Any candidate confined to Cepheids or SNe must treat that excess as a fluctuation (one-sided p ≈ 5×10⁻⁴) or as a second, independent bias. This is the main strain on the ladder-systematic candidate. D-90 quotes 3.7σ for a no-SN combination against Planck, which agrees [calc: .../lens-constraints_void_null.py].
16. **Energy conditions, stability and causality, sorted by candidate.**
    - The axion-like EDE scalar and extra free-streaming radiation have −1 ≤ w ≤ 1, so they satisfy NEC, WEC and DEC.
    - SEC is violated by Λ itself, so it discriminates nothing here.
    - Phantom late-time dark energy (findings 10–11) violates NEC, WEC and DEC as a minimally coupled component. For a scalar field that means a ghost (vacuum instability), unless w_eff < −1 is effective: from dark-sector interactions or modified gravity. This is my inference from standard results and is not sourced in the dossier. The geometric BAO+SN constraint binds those effective models just the same, because it constrains H(z), not the stress tensor.
    - The DESI CPL fit crosses w = −1. A single minimally coupled field cannot do that without instability, which is again a standard result not sourced here.
    - None of the candidates involves closed timelike curves, horizons or causality violations. The binding constraints are observational-geometric (θ_*, H0·r_d, SN shape), statistical (cosmic variance) and the published parameter bounds.

### Validity domains of the models used
- **Background cosmology (`cosmo_background.py`):** homogeneous FRW with no perturbations. z_* and z_d are fits calibrated to CAMB for ΛCDM, and the dossier's tool notes say these fits ignore EDE, N_eff and varying constants. The EDE results use the effective-fluid form, with no perturbations and no damping or phase-shift effects. Real Boltzmann fits move H0 less per unit f, because the CMB shape also constrains ω_c and the perturbations. The f values in finding 7 are therefore lower bounds on what a full fit needs, and the 69–71 range in D-52/D-53 is consistent with that. Varying m_e enters only through the z_* scaling and σ_T. The question's regime (z ≲ 10⁴, linear background) lies inside the model's domain. Damping-tail and S8 effects lie outside it.
- **Damping diagnostic θ_D/θ_*:** fully ionised plasma, and it is a ratio only. It is uncalibrated against a Boltzmann code and serves only as a relative yardstick against ΔN_eff.
- **Void:** linear theory plus the Bernardeau approximation. Both are valid for |δ| ≲ 0.5, which sits at the edge of the needed contrast. EH98 no-wiggle P(k) is accurate to a few percent in σ(R). An LTB treatment with a non-top-hat profile would change the numbers by tens of percent, not the order of magnitude.
- **NEC argument:** GR with minimally coupled matter. It lapses for effective phantom behaviour from interactions or modified gravity. The geometric constraint K2 does not lapse.
- **Inverse ladder (K2):** FRW, standard r_d, and SN standardisation that does not evolve with z. A G_eff/M_B transition or a non-standard r_d escapes it by construction.
- **Semiclassical gravity and quantum inequalities** are irrelevant here. Every quantity is classical and macroscopic.

### Separation needed by a deciding measurement
For a measurement of the global H0 that uses neither r_d nor the local ladder (time delays, sirens, a sound-horizon-free BAO H0), "global 67.2" and "global 73.3" differ by 6.07 km/s/Mpc. Separating them needs σ ≤ 2.0 km/s/Mpc (2.8%) at 3σ and σ ≤ 1.2 km/s/Mpc (1.7%) at 5σ. TDCOSMO-2026 is at about 2.35 (3.3%) [D-31] and GWTC-5 at about 8.5 [D-33]. Sound-horizon-free DESI DR1 values are at 1.3–1.8 [D-49].

## What would change my mind
- An r_d-free global H0 (time delays, sirens, sound-horizon-free BAO) at σ ≲ 1.2 km/s/Mpc landing at ≥ 72.5 would move C (early-time) to surviving and push A and B toward eliminated. Landing at ≤ 68.5 would eliminate C and strengthen B.
- A full Boltzmann fit of EDE or varying m_e to Planck+ACT+SPT+DESI DR2+SNe, with no SH0ES prior, that reaches ≥ 72.5 with an acceptable S8 and age. The current best is 71.0 ± 1.1 (profile likelihood) and 71.4 ± 1.4 (eROSITA) [D-53, D-94]. That would lift C.
- An SN-free and Cepheid-free ladder (JWST TRGB → SBF or Hubble-flow masers) at 73 ± 1 would eliminate B and most of A for the ladder. Conversely, an independent re-analysis that confirms the Dong peculiar-velocity correction (−2.9) or the Wojtak–Hjorth standardisation (−2 to −2.5) and finds they add would make A or B dominant.
- A detected 0.15–0.19 mag M_B step between SN hosts at ≲ 44 Mpc and 45–100 Mpc with geometric distances would revive the M_B-transition variant of D.
- A demonstration that the SN low-/high-z offset [D-85] is ≥ 0.1 mag would reopen late-time H(z) through the K9 escape.
