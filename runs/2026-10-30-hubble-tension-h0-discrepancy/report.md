# The Hubble tension: report
*Run 2026-10-30-hubble-tension-h0-discrepancy · 2026-10-30 · depth deep · question: "Hubble tension"*

## Bottom line
The likeliest cause of the gap between the local Hubble constant (about 73 km/s/Mpc, from the distance ladder) and the early-universe value (67–68.5) is an error on the ladder side, at about 45 percent confidence. The main reason: the early value is reproduced by three independent telescopes and by a method using no microwave-background maps, while the 5-sigma claim rests on one team's ladder. But ladders that skip supernovae read just as high, so the error must sit in something every local method shares, or part of the gap is real. Pre-recombination new physics (about 20 percent) and a half-and-half mixture (about 20 percent) stay open. A 1-percent expansion-rate measurement using neither ladder nor sound horizon would decide it.

## Why, candidate by candidate

**C1 (0.44; split within it: unidentified or combined effects 0.24, Hubble-flow peculiar-velocity treatment 0.07, SN standardisation plus low-z compilation 0.07, calibrator/Cepheid selection 0.05, anchor/Cepheid physics/TRGB zero point 0.01).** Strongest for: the early side is one consistent structure (Planck PR4 67.64 ± 0.52, ACT 66.51 ± 0.64, SPT 66.66 ± 0.60; BAO+BBN 68.50 ± 0.58 without CMB anisotropies; inverse ladder 67.19–67.67), so a dominant early error must be physics, not analysis. The ≥ 5σ needs the SH0ES-team ladder (C8-0/1/2): without it the excess is 4.5 ± 1.2 at 2.5–4.2σ; no SH0ES R22 blinding statement was found; 0 of 12 resolved single-team precision anomalies ended as new physics (Wilson upper 0.24). Arbiters free of both calibrations (r_d-free 69.2–70.3 ± 1.3; one-step 71.27 ± 1.93) exclude 73.26 at 2.9–3.8σ, and the globular-cluster age disfavours it at 2.7–3.4σ. Every published lever (Wojtak–Hjorth, Percival, Dong, Hoyt's Pantheon+ shift) lowers H0 by 1–3. Strongest against (all three verdicts, conceded in crux C1): the SN Ia step is measured in situ by H0DN to add +0.002 ± 0.047 mag (73.50 with SNe vs 73.434 ± 1.795 without; calc/falsifier-C1-2_bounds.py.log), 3–4σ short of 0.15–0.19 mag, and SN-free probes (SBF, TF, SNe II 75.4, time delays, masers, sirens) give 72.4–72.6 ± 1.4, 2.8–2.9σ above 68.5. Anchor (9.4σ) and Cepheid-photometry (5.9–8.2σ) routes are closed. What survives is a ladder-wide effect: most plausibly a peculiar-velocity modelling error common to every Hubble-flow tracer (Dong 2026 preprint, 0.087 mag) plus a minority SN share, at the cost of a 1–2σ upward fluctuation of the SN-free probes; that lever rests on one contested preprint whose sign is model-dependent (Laniakea moves H0 the other way, +0.6 to +1.4). Current data cannot identify the effect: no named mechanism of the right size has an in-situ confirmation, so "unidentified" outranks each named effect. The advocate's narrowed truth, ≈ 68–68.5, is defensible; 67.2 is disfavoured. What would halve 0.44: a third-team SN-free ladder at ≤ 1.5 confirming ≥ 72.5, or an arbiter at ≥ 70.5. What would double its odds: an independent forward flow analysis lowering SN Ia, SBF and TF Hubble-flow H0 together by ≈ 0.09 mag.

**C3 (0.20; axion-like EDE 0.16, other variants 0.04).** For: the only class moving CMB, BAO+BBN and the inverse ladder together; the profile-likelihood fit f_EDE = 0.09 ± 0.03, H0 = 71.0 ± 1.1 (Poulin 2026, PRD) and eROSITA clusters plus external data f = 0.10, 3.2σ, 71.4 ± 1.4 (A&A), both without a ladder prior; the non-SH0ES local mean 71.7 ± 1.1 sits on that value. Against: CMB-only Bayesian bounds f < 0.061 (NPIPE, H0 < 69.5 at 95%) and f < 0.070 (SPT+ACT+Planck, H0 = 68.03 +0.53/−1.1, 3.6σ residual, "passes none" of SPT's metrics) while the midpoint needs f ≈ 0.065–0.072; Lyα forest f < 0.03 (Goldstein 2023, PRL, pre-DESI); SPT attributes the DESI-driven preference to the DESI–CMB Ω_m offset; r_d-free + one-step + age combinations give P(H0 > 70.2) = 0.15–0.41 and exclude full closure at 3.6–4.4σ; no no-prior fit reaches 73; World Cup II leaves ≈ 3σ even with the SH0ES prior; the advocate conceded C3 "stands behind C6 on present evidence". Other variants fall below the midpoint on their own fits (PMF 69.1 ± 0.5; modified recombination 69.6 ± 1.0; ΔN_eff needs 4.3–4.4 vs 2.86 ± 0.13, 11–12σ). What would double 0.20: a CMB-only f_EDE detection at ≥ 3σ (σ(f) ≲ 0.02) with the same sign in ACT and SPT. What would halve it: a DESI-era Lyα analysis confirming f < 0.03.

**C6 (0.20; two-part hybrid 0.19, diffuse errors 0.01, pure fluctuation < 0.005).** All three verdicts and the advocate narrow it to half ladder (2.3–3.0) plus a real 3–4% r_d cut (f ≈ 0.05–0.07), true H0 in a 1σ window 69.4–70.6. For: the cleanest arbiters (one-step + r_d-free, 70.25 ± 1.21) sit on the window; partial levers of 1.9–2.9 on the ladder and partial EDE at the CMB-bound edge (69.5–69.9) are each supported; no bound is violated. Against: diffuse errors need a 4.29 common ladder shift (71% of the gap, i.e. C1) or local errors ×3.7; fluctuation is 6.8σ Gaussian and still 4.3–4.5σ with almost-Cauchy tails; the balance window carries only 0.06–0.10 (flat early prior) to 0.08–0.26 (early shift capped at CMB bounds) of the arbiter posterior (calc/crux-C6_balance.py.log). C6 and C3 are set equal: C3 holds the positive fits, C6 the arbiters' central value. Its decisive test (σ ≤ 0.3) is beyond every programme in the dossier.

**C4 (0.03).** Smooth H(z) is refuted: BAO plus uncalibrated SNe fix H0·r_d (verified M-CONSTRAINTS-10; DESI w0wa lowers H0 to 63.6–67.5). The G-step is excluded by red-dwarf radii at 3.1–4.0σ (preprint) and by a 100–370 W/m² insolation change. The surviving SN-only luminosity step has no mechanism and is disfavoured 2–3.6σ by non-SN probes and by near-volume two-rung H0 (Kenworthy 73.1 +2.6/−2.3; Huang 73.4 ± 1.0, error budget unverified); the advocate would not defend it as likely.

**SH0ES–CCHP verdict.** The 2.5–3.1 difference (1.4–2.4σ for ρ = 0–0.8) splits about half–half between calibrator-subset selection (an 11-of-42 subset scatters by 0.84–1.31, so CCHP-JWST is a 1.9–3.0σ low draw; M-STATISTICIAN-09) and an unattributed method/SN-photometry component (Hoyt: Pantheon+ moves +2 at 3.1σ when extended, CSP-II +0.8). CCHP's 1.94 error is compatible with both camps; the same JWST hosts reanalysed by SH0ES give 72.1–73.3; no reply from CCHP was found. So the tension is 6.3 at 6.3σ with SH0ES's analysis, or ≈ 4.5 at 3–4σ without it; neither ladder analysis was blinded end to end.

**Early-versus-late verdict.** Late-time H(z) fails the inverse ladder; only an M_B step escapes and it is disfavoured. Of early-time models only axion-like EDE survives, closing 45–69% in the fits that favour it and 1–14% in CMB-only fits; none reaches 73.

**Reframes.** C7 is upheld as the right description: on published SN compilations the gap is 0.146–0.188 mag between the anchor-to-SN scale and r_d, and no H(z) fitting BAO+SN narrows it by more than ≈ 0.2σ; "mis-posed" is not upheld (partitions equivalent; r_d encodes a pre-recombination expansion rate). Its one new conditional is that the z ≲ 0.1 pin rests on legacy low-z SN cross-calibration (Nájera 2026 preprint, offset 0.065 ± 0.039 mag, 1.65σ). C8 is upheld on significance: "about 5σ" needs SH0ES-team analyses and a CMB reference (against BAO+BBN nothing reaches 5σ; SH0ES 4.55σ); "real" holds (3.1σ no-SN, 4.3–4.5σ heavy-tailed all-local); "smaller" is unmeasured.

## What would change this verdict
1. An anchor-free, r_d-free H0 at σ ≤ 0.8 (TDCOSMO with ≈ 40–50 lenses, review forecast ≈ 40 for 1%; LVK O5, timeline "in discussion"; DESI r_d-free BAO, data to end-2028): ≤ 69 raises C1 to ≈ 0.7; 69.4–70.6 moves C6 to ≈ 0.4; ≥ 71 moves C3 to ≈ 0.5. No collaboration date exists in the inputs.
2. A CMB-only f_EDE measurement from Simons Observatory or SPT-3G Ext at σ(f) ≲ 0.02 (no forecast or date found): f ≥ 0.07 at ≥ 3σ roughly doubles C3; f < 0.03 halves C3 and C6.
3. A team-independent ≥ 35-host TRGB/JAGB ladder on one SN photometric system, or a third-party forward peculiar-velocity analysis applied to SN Ia, SBF, TF and SNe II together (CCHP, H0DN, Stiskalek group; no date): ≥ 72.5 or no common drop halves C1; ≤ 71 with a common ≈ 0.09 mag drop doubles its odds. Gaia DR4 (2 Dec 2026) tests only the MW anchor (H0DN without MW parallaxes is already 73.61).
