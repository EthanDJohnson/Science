## Refuted
- M-STATISTICIAN-15: the Sellke bound numbers do not use the z values the lens itself computes.
  - **S1 (SH0ES vs CMB-SPA).** At z = 6.271, p2 = 3.59e-10, so the bound is BF_null >= 2.12e-8 and the odds against the null are at most 4.7e7 : 1. The lens has 1.8e-8 and 5.6e7 : 1 because it rounded p2 to 3e-10 (z = 6.3).
  - **S4 (H0DN without Cepheids).** At z = 3.94 the cap is 477 : 1 and the prior needed is 2.1e-3. The lens has 413 : 1 and 2.4e-3, which belong to z = 3.90, so its "at most 413 : 1" is not a valid upper bound.
  - **S5 (one-step).** At the Gaussian z = 1.88 the cap is 2.2 : 1 and the prior needed is about 0.31. The lens's 2.6 : 1 and 0.28 use the heavy-tail z = 2.0.
  - **S6 (SH0ES vs CCHP).** The cap is 1.3 : 1 at rho = 0, but 1.6, 2.1 and 5.0 : 1 at rho = 0.3, 0.5 and 0.8. The lens's "<= 1.4 : 1" therefore fails across the rho range it quotes itself (z = 1.44-2.36).
  - **What depends on it.** Finding 11 and table A, the "max odds" and "prior needed" columns for S1, S4, S5 and S6, and candidate STATISTICIAN-G (which quotes 5.6e7 : 1). No verdict changes: S1 stays overwhelming, S4 stays at a few hundred to one, S5 stays negligible, and S6 stays weak at no more than about 5 : 1. The Gaussian-prior Bayes factors (M-STATISTICIAN-07) are correct.

## Formalizable
- M-STATISTICIAN-09: for iid X_1..X_N with variance s^2 and 1 <= n < N, Var(mean(X_1..X_n) - mean(X_1..X_N)) = s^2 (1/n - 1/N).
- M-STATISTICIAN-02: for d, a, b > 0 and 0 <= rho < 1 with a^2 + b^2 - 2 rho a b > 0, the map rho -> d / sqrt(a^2 + b^2 - 2 rho a b) is strictly increasing.
- M-STATISTICIAN-07: for D real and s, tau > 0, the integral of N(D; delta, s^2) N(delta; 0, tau^2) d delta equals N(D; 0, s^2 + tau^2), and the ratio to N(D; 0, s^2) tends to 1 as tau -> 0.
- M-STATISTICIAN-05: for w1, w2 > 0, w1 (x1 - m)^2 + w2 (x2 - m)^2 = (x1 - x2)^2 / (1/w1 + 1/w2) with m = (w1 x1 + w2 x2)/(w1 + w2).
- M-STATISTICIAN-06: for p in [0, 1] and N >= 1, 1 - (1 - p)^N <= N p (Bernoulli), so Sidak is never more lenient than Bonferroni.
- M-STATISTICIAN-08: the Student-t density with nu degrees of freedom converges pointwise to the standard normal density as nu -> infinity.
