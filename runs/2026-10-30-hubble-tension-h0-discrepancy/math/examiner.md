## Refuted
- M-EXAMINER-04: the GLS line in F3 ("71.47 ± 1.26, 3.25σ above CMB-SPA") does not follow from the construction it states. Adding the shared 1.45% NGC 4258 anchor as a covariance between CCHP and SBF (C_ij = (0.0145 H_i)(0.0145 H_j), ρ = 0.23) gives 71.63 ± 1.21 km/s/Mpc, 3.51σ above CMB-SPA. Adding the anchor to the diagonals as well gives 71.67 ± 1.26, 3.40σ. Also letting the masers share the anchor gives 71.63 ± 1.25, 3.40σ. None of the 32 constructions scanned (shared sets {CCHP, SBF}, +TF, +masers; anchor 1.45%, 2.9% or 3.1%; with and without diagonal terms) reproduces 71.47; the closest is 71.56–71.57. Limit check: as the anchor error goes to 0 the GLS returns the uncorrelated 71.69 ± 1.14, which the lens also reports. Corrected form: about 71.6–71.7 ± 1.2–1.3, 3.4–3.5σ above CMB-SPA. Consequence: small, and in the direction of a stronger Cepheid-independent excess. F3's reading ("about 3σ" Cepheid-independent excess; "3.3–3.7σ" in EXAMINER-B and hardest question 1) should read 3.4–3.7σ. No candidate status changes. The RE-27 anchor value itself is not checked here.
- M-EXAMINER-08 (EDE P-ACT-LB residual only): H = 69.9 +0.8/−1.5 against SH0ES 73.49 ± 0.93. The lens says its tension() uses the facing-side error, which here is +0.8, and that gives (73.49 − 69.9)/√(0.8² + 0.93²) = 2.93σ. The quoted 2.43σ comes from the symmetrised error 1.15. Corrected: 2.93σ. Consequence: the residual range in F8 becomes 1.24–4.16σ, unchanged at its ends. EXAMINER-C ("strained") is reinforced, not weakened. The closure fractions (30–67%) are unaffected. Note, a convention rather than an error: closure is measured from CMB-SPA 67.19. Measured from ACT's own P-ACT-LB ΛCDM baseline (68.2, D-52), the same EDE fit closes 32%, not 43%.

## Formalizable
- M-EXAMINER-06 (decomposition): for weights W_k > 0 and group means m_k, Σ_k W_k (m_k − μ)² = (m_1 − m_2)²/(1/W_1 + 1/W_2) for two groups, with μ = (W_1 m_1 + W_2 m_2)/(W_1 + W_2). More generally, the total weighted sum of squares equals the within-group sum plus the between-group sum.
- M-EXAMINER-04 (GLS): for symmetric positive-definite C and the all-ones vector 1, the minimum-variance unbiased linear combination has weights C⁻¹1/(1ᵀC⁻¹1) and variance 1/(1ᵀC⁻¹1). For diagonal C it reduces to inverse-variance weighting.
- M-EXAMINER-01: for H_l, H_e > 0, 5 log10(H_l/H_e) and 1 − H_e/H_l are both positive iff H_l > H_e, both vanish at H_l = H_e, and 5 log10(1 + x)/x → 5/ln 10 as x → 0.
