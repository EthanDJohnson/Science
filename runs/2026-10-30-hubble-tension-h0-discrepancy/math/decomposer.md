## Refuted
- M-DECOMPOSER-10: F2 defines "best two-group split" by an exhaustive search. On that reading, local vs early-type is the best split only at sigma_c = 0.
  - **The counterexample.** At sigma_c = 0.5, 1.0, 1.5 and 2.0 km/s/Mpc the exhaustive best moves CCHP into the early group. From sigma_c = 1.5 it also moves the r_s-free BAO value into the local group. Those splits have chi2 = 8.359, 7.690, 7.082 and 6.225, which beats local/early by Delta chi2 = 0.08, 0.66, 1.16 and 1.92.
  - **Corrected form.** Among the named partitions (local/early, ladders/rest, r_s-using/not, SH0ES/rest), local/early stays the best at every sigma_c, and SH0ES/rest stays the worst. Once a common ladder offset is allowed, the exhaustive optimum puts CCHP with the early-type group, by less than 2 in chi2 out of 511 splits searched.
  - **What depends on it.** F8's sentence and Q2a's "settled" status hold only for the named partitions. Q2b ("CCHP cannot be placed") is reinforced rather than weakened. No candidate's status changes:
    - DECOMPOSER-D's "SH0ES vs everything else is the worst partition" still holds among the named splits, with Delta chi2 = 4.6 at sigma_c = 2;
    - DECOMPOSER-A and C are unaffected.

## Formalizable
- M-DECOMPOSER-02: for w1, w2 > 0 and m = (w1 x1 + w2 x2)/(w1 + w2), w1 (x1 - m)^2 + w2 (x2 - m)^2 = (x1 - x2)^2/(1/w1 + 1/w2).
- M-DECOMPOSER-06: for D > 0, k > 0 and 0 <= s < D/k, setting t = sqrt((D/k)^2 - s^2) gives D/sqrt(t^2 + s^2) = k, and t is the largest sigma_L with D/sqrt(sigma_L^2 + s^2) >= k.
- M-DECOMPOSER-08: let D be symmetric positive definite, A have full column rank, u lie in the column space of A, and c >= 0. Then the minimum over mu of (x - A mu)^T (D + c^2 u u^T)^-1 (x - A mu) does not depend on c. This is why "ladders vs rest" stays at 11.205 for every sigma_c (numerically observed in the log).
- M-DECOMPOSER-04: for 0 < b < a, 5 log10(a/b) <= (5/ln 10)(a - b)/b.
