## Refuted
- M-DIALECTICIAN-04: F3 says the SH0ES − CCHP-JWST gap of 4.68 km/s/Mpc is 2.55 (selection) plus 2.39 (residual), but those add to 4.94. The two parts use different SH0ES baselines: 2.55 is 73.75 − 71.2, while 4.68 is taken from 73.49. A consistent split is 2.29 + 2.39 (residual 0.074 mag), or 2.55 + 2.13 (0.066 mag). The conclusion "splits roughly in half" survives either way (49–55% selection). Affected: F3, the C-I synthesis, and candidate A's "1–2.5 km/s/Mpc" ladder-side share.
- M-DIALECTICIAN-13: the C-I synthesis and candidate A's "Why" call 1–2.5 km/s/Mpc "a third to a half of the 6 km/s/Mpc gap". It is 0.17–0.42 of it. Either the effect range should read 2–3 km/s/Mpc or the fraction should read "a sixth to two-fifths". This weakens, slightly, the weight candidate A (null) gives to the SH0ES–CCHP sample effect. It also leaves more of the gap for other partial causes, which is consistent with A's own sum.
- M-DIALECTICIAN-03 (minor): "p ≈ 0.01–0.02" fits only the 2.3σ end. At 2.9σ the two-sided p is 0.004. Corrected: p ≈ 0.004–0.02. This strengthens rather than weakens the lens's point that pure random selection is strained (F2, C-I "What yields", candidate B (b1)).

## Formalizable
- M-DIALECTICIAN-02: for iid X₁..X_N with variance s², Var((1/n)Σ_{i≤n} X_i − (1/N)Σ_{i≤N} X_i) = s²(1/n − 1/N) for 1 ≤ n ≤ N; for disjoint means of sizes n₁ and n₂ the variance is s²(1/n₁ + 1/n₂).
- M-DIALECTICIAN-07: for weights w_i = 1/σ_i², the weighted mean with all σ_i = s equals the arithmetic mean and has standard error s/√k.
- M-DIALECTICIAN-06: if 5 log10 H = M + c for a constant c, then H₁/H₂ = 10^{(M₁ − M₂)/5}, and H is strictly increasing in M.
