## Refuted
- M-MECHANIST-02 (crowding row only): the dossier records the JWST bound as HST − JWST = −0.01 ± 0.03 mag [D-78], and the lens rewrote it as "JWST − HST = −0.01". Crowding makes HST host Cepheids too bright, so the bias needed to raise H0 by the gap is HST − JWST = −0.188 mag. The measured offset already points slightly that way, and the separation is (0.188 − 0.01)/0.03 = 5.9σ, not 6.6σ. This affects finding 3, table B ("6.6σ") and MECHANIST-D ("6.6σ to ~68σ" becomes "5.9σ to ~68σ"). Crowding is still eliminated as the sole cause. If the primary source defines the offset the other way round, 6.6σ is correct; the dossier's sign gives 5.9σ.
- M-MECHANIST-06: the needed ω_c change is −13.0% (linearised in logs with the lens's own −0.62) or −13.3% (exact solve), about 13σ at the assumed 1%, not −13.9%/14σ. The lens read Δ ln ω_c = −0.1396 as a fractional change. This affects finding 5 and table B; MECHANIST-E is unchanged.
- M-MECHANIST-07 (ω_b factor): the lens's ×1.69 holds r_d's ω_b dependence alone, at fixed ω_cb. But BAO fixes Ω_m, so raising h raises ω_cb = Ω_m h², which shrinks r_d as well. Done self-consistently, the needed ω_b rise is ×1.26 (about +26%). That is still about 26× the ~1% D/H precision, so "excluded" in finding 5, table B and MECHANIST-E stands. The r_d −6.5% (9σ) part is verified.

## Formalizable
- M-MECHANIST-10: for i.i.d. X_1..X_N with variance s² and 1 ≤ n < N, Var(mean(X_1..X_N) − mean(X_1..X_n)) = s²(1/n − 1/N).
- M-MECHANIST-09: the inverse-variance weighted mean of x_i with weights 1/s_i² lies in [min x_i, max x_i], and its variance (Σ 1/s_i²)^−1 is at most min s_i².
- M-MECHANIST-01: for H_l, H_e > 0, 5 log10(c z/H_e) − 5 log10(c z/H_l) = 5 log10(H_l/H_e), independent of z > 0.

- M-MECHANIST-04: 0.188/0.17 > 1, so no Δf ∈ [0, 1] satisfies 0.17 Δf ≥ 0.188 (trivial).
