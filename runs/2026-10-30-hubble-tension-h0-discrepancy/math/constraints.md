## Unverified
- M-CONSTRAINTS-16: the hockey-stick model is not specified (step shape, width, which density is held fixed, the ladder-fit procedure), so no independent derivation is possible. Candidate D's "eliminated (H(z))" verdict also rests on M-CONSTRAINTS-10, which is verified, so D does not depend on this claim alone.
- M-CONSTRAINTS-17: the θ_D/θ_* diagnostic is undefined beyond "fully ionised plasma, ratio only". Its numbers (the 9.5× and 3.6× yardsticks, and the damping-tail prediction in candidate C) are unchecked and should be read as qualitative.
- Caveats on verified rows: M-CONSTRAINTS-04 holds only when θ_* is normalised to the model's own Planck best fit. With an absolute 100θ_* = 1.0411 and the HS96 z_* fit, the baseline becomes 68.03, not 67.35. The lens reports 67.35, so it either calibrated or used a closer z_* fit. M-CONSTRAINTS-10: the SN-shape term is +0.06 mag at z = 0.3 and +0.13 mag at z = 1.0 when matched at z = 0.05, and its sign as written (−0.10) depends on the convention.
## Formalizable
- M-CONSTRAINTS-13: for d > 2s_E > 0, with s' = √((d/2)² − s_E²), d/√(s'² + s_E²) = 2.
- M-CONSTRAINTS-05: if h^(1−2a) Ω^(−a) = K with 0 < a < 1/2 and K, Ω > 0, then d ln h / d ln Ω = a/(1 − 2a).
- M-CONSTRAINTS-12 (series part): (f/2)[1 − (1+δ)^(2/3)] = −fδ/3 + fδ²/18 + O(δ³) as δ → 0.
- M-CONSTRAINTS-14: the inverse-variance weighted mean of finitely many values lies in their convex hull, and its variance 1/Σw_i is no larger than the smallest single variance.
- M-CONSTRAINTS-15: for d, k, s > 0, d/s ≥ k ⇔ s ≤ d/k.
