## Refuted
- M-EMPIRICIST-12: the two pieces of the EMPIRICIST-B reframe cannot both hold, because magnitude gaps add along the chain: 0.1 + 0.13 = 0.23 mag exceeds the total SH0ES–CMB-SPA gap of 0.195 mag (and 3 + 4 = 7 km/s/Mpc exceeds 6.30). With the lens's own non-SH0ES local mean (71.21–71.56 km/s/Mpc), the corrected form is: SH0ES vs the rest of the local data ≈ 2 km/s/Mpc (0.06–0.07 mag), and the rest vs early ≈ 4 km/s/Mpc (0.13 mag, 6.0–6.5%). The "~3 km/s/Mpc (~0.1 mag)" figure is SH0ES vs CCHP alone (3.10 km/s/Mpc, 0.094 mag), not SH0ES vs the rest. Depends on it: candidate EMPIRICIST-B (its size statement only; its significance figures, 3.2–3.5σ and 6.27σ, are verified in M-02 to M-04).
- M-EMPIRICIST-14: "remove roughly half of the 0.195 mag gap" holds only at the low end of the stated 70–72 range (0.54 at 70, 0.48 at CCHP's 70.39). At the midpoint 71 it is 0.38. At the lens's own non-SH0ES mean (71.2–71.6) it is 0.30–0.35, and at 72 it is 0.23. Corrected form: a true local value of 70–72 removes about a quarter to a half of the gap, roughly a third at the non-SH0ES mean. Depends on it: candidate EMPIRICIST-A (size of the effect it claims to explain; it leaves a larger residual, of about 0.13 mag, for something else to explain).

## Formalizable
- M-EMPIRICIST-04: for s1, s2 > 0 and 0 ≤ c < min(s1², s2²), (s1²s2² − c²)/(s1² + s2² − 2c) ≥ s1²s2²/(s1² + s2²).
- M-EMPIRICIST-08: for 0 < p ≤ 1/e, −e p ln p ≤ 1, with equality only at p = 1/e.
- M-EMPIRICIST-11: the Wilson score upper bound at p̂ = 0 equals z²/(n + z²); for n = 12, z = 1.959964 it lies in (0.24, 0.25).
- M-EMPIRICIST-07: for N = 2, Σ (x_i − x̄_w)²/s_i² = (x1 − x2)²/(s1² + s2²), where x̄_w is the inverse-variance mean.
- M-EMPIRICIST-12: for a, b, c > 0, log(a/c) = log(a/b) + log(b/c); hence for any 67.19 < b < 73.49, 5 log10(73.49/b) + 5 log10(b/67.19) = 5 log10(73.49/67.19) < 0.23.
