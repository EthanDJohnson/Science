## Refuted
- **M-MECHANIST-08.** The lens says Chae-style calibration "removes the triple bias to ≤ +0.03", so that premise 2 "holds to ≲ 0.03 in γ for triples". This compares the calibrated estimators against truth (γ = 1) but E2a against its own f = 0 value. On the same yardstick the conclusions change.
  - **Triple-induced shifts (Δγ relative to each estimator's f = 0 row, f_in = 0.1/0.2/0.3):**
    - E1a +0.053/+0.095/+0.152
    - E1b +0.056/+0.074/+0.090
    - E1c +0.059/+0.073/+0.094
    - E2a +0.066/+0.110/+0.152
    - E3 +0.032/+0.056/+0.075
  - **Slope per unit residual fraction:** E1a and E2a 0.92, E1b 0.55, E1c 0.57, E3 0.45.
  - **What calibration actually does.** It cuts the triple sensitivity by about 40%; it does not remove it. The net ≤ +0.03 against truth is a cancellation: an eccentricity-gradient offset of −0.06 (E1b) or −0.09 (E1c) at f = 0 offsets the triple shift. M-06 independently confirms an offset of about −0.04 from the gradient alone.
  - **The verdict range is also wrong.** It says E1b/E1c lie "within −0.06 to +0.03", but E1c at f = 0 is −0.088.
  - **Corrected statement.** Calibration reduces the triple-induced shift to +0.06–0.09 at 5–16.5% residual triples. The mixture E3 is the least triple-sensitive estimator (+0.03–0.075). In this generator the "calibrated vs uncalibrated" axis matters, but less than the lens says. Tail truncation (E2a) and the full median (E1a) are equally sensitive.
  - **What depends on it:**
    - F6 and the verdict paragraph.
    - Candidate MECHANIST-F, whose reframe rests on "calibration removes it" and should read "reduces it by about 40%".
    - Candidate MECHANIST-A's "calibrated ≤ +0.03".
    - F9's gap budget. If the real calibration zone does not carry the same eccentricity offset, the calibrated triple share is up to +0.09, not +0.03. The extra needed from unbound pairs then falls from about +0.29 to about +0.23, i.e. about 15% unbound pairs at the linear slope, or about 12% with the convex response of M-09. This makes candidate A's missing link smaller, not larger.

## Formalizable
- M-MECHANIST-05: For γ, M_t, M_e, f > 0, (γ G M_t f/s)·s/(G M_e)/f = γ M_t/M_e. For k_l = k_c, (γ k_l f)/(k_c f) = γ.
- M-MECHANIST-10: For x > 0, y = x²/(1+x) is the unique positive solution of y·(1+√(1+4/y))/2 = x. Also K(y) = y·d/dy ln[(1+√(1+4/y))/2] → −1/2 as y → 0⁺ and → 0 as y → ∞.
- M-MECHANIST-09: If a fraction u ∈ [0, 1) of a mixture lies strictly above the median of the other component, with CDF F, then the mixture median equals F⁻¹(1/(2(1−u))), and it is convex in u whenever F⁻¹ is convex on [1/2, 1).
- M-MECHANIST-01/02: k/√(GM/(10s)) = √10·k/√(GM/s); (v s/d)/√(GM/s) = v s^{3/2}/(d√(GM)).
- Kepler self-test used in M-04 and M-06: the time average over mean anomaly of r = a(1 − e cos E) is a(1 + e²/2).
