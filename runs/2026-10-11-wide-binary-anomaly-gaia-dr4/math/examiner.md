## Refuted
- **M-EXAMINER-11 (minor, wording).** Finding 1 says the MOND prediction "spans a factor ~1.3 in (γ−1)". The lens's own numbers at g_N = 1e-10 m s^-2 give:
  - a factor ~6 in (γ−1) across simple vs standard ν (0.375 vs 0.061);
  - a factor 1.125 across simple vs chae2024;
  - a factor ~11 including the isolated case.
  
  The corrected statement is "a factor ~1.3 in γ (1.06 to 1.42)". Nothing downstream depends on the factor itself. The substantive point holds and is if anything stronger: "MOND-consistent" vs "MOND-excluded" is template-relative (P10, R3, cand E).
- **M-EXAMINER-12 (minor, quantitative).** R2 and candidate EXAMINER-A say a full-median γ of 1.3–1.5 (Chae's 1.37) needs the velocity-relevant f in the wide zone to be "≈ 0.2–0.3" higher than in the calibration zone.
  - **Corrected values.** Interpolating the lens's own test-zone table (calibration at f = 0.2) gives Δf ≈ 0.31 for 1.37, and 0.26–0.39 for 1.3–1.5. Calibrating at f = 0.1 instead gives 0.33 for 1.37.
  - **Effect.** This slightly weakens cand A: the separation dependence of f_multi it requires is larger than stated. Hardest question 1's threshold "rise by ≳0.2" should read "≳0.3 for 1.37".
## Unverified
- **M-EXAMINER-13.** The transfer rule "dγ_app/df ≈ 1" (±0.05 in f ↔ ±0.05 in γ) is model-dependent and not settled.
  - **The lens's table.** It is ≈ 1 only for f ≲ 0.3 and steepens to 1.5–1.9 above that.
  - **My toy model.** It has no Gaia noise, true masses, a different inner-mass and orbit model, and no RUWE rejection. It gives a steeper 1.8 between f = 0.1 and 0.3 (1.12 / 1.27 / 1.48 at f = 0.1 / 0.2 / 0.3, against the lens's 1.09 / 1.20 / 1.31).
  - **What agrees.** Both models agree on the sign and rough size of the zone contrast (M-07).
  - **What is open.** The precise slope depends on the inner-binary period distribution, the stability factor, noise and the photocentre model, which neither the lens nor I constrain.
  - **Effect on findings.** Finding 9's "Δγ ≈ 0.05–0.1 if the hidden f halves" scales with this slope, so it could be up to ~1.8× larger.

## Formalizable
- M-EXAMINER-01: For y > 0, the positive root x of x²/(1 + x^n)^(1/n) = y satisfies x/y = [(1 + √(1 + 4y^(−n)))/2]^(1/n) for n = 1 and n = 2. For n = 1, y·d/dy ln ν(y) = −2y^(−1)/(w(1 + w)) with w = √(1 + 4/y), and this tends to −1/2 as y → 0⁺ and to 0 as y → ∞.
- M-EXAMINER-02: For real ν, K, (1/2)∫₀^π ν(1 + K sin²θ/2) sinθ dθ = ν(1 + K/3).
- M-EXAMINER-04: For γ, M_t, M_e > 0, the map M_e ↦ γM_t/M_e is strictly decreasing and equals γ at M_e = M_t.
- M-EXAMINER-06: For differentiable Ω(R) > 0 with A = −(R/2)Ω′, d/dR(Ω²R) = Ω(Ω − 4A), and Ω² − d/dR(Ω²R) = 4AΩ.
- M-EXAMINER-10: For least squares of x(t) = c0 + c1 t + c2 t² with homoscedastic noise σ and N samples uniformly distributed (continuum limit) on [−T/2, T/2], sd(c1) = √(12/N) σ/T and sd(2c2)/sd(c1) = √60/T.
