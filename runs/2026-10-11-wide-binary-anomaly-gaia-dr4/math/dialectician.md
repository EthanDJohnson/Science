## Refuted
- **M-DIALECTICIAN-08.** F2 says the low-quantile shift caused by triples comes from the light-based mass deficit, a pure rescaling that a shape test cannot tell apart from gravity. That attribution is wrong.
  - **What the mass channel gives.** With the generator's stated population (q_in ~ U(0.1, 1), m_B/m_A ~ U(0.2, 1), L ∝ m^3.5), the deficit alone raises γ(0.3) by about +0.03 at f = 0.2 (+0.05 at f = 0.3). The lens reports +0.133 in the low bin and +0.096 in the high bin.
  - **What causes the rest.** About 70–80% of the shift comes from another channel, most plausibly the inner-binary photocentre velocity. That velocity is an added vector, not a rescaling.
  - **Calibration.** The mass deficit does not depend on separation, so calibrating at high g cancels it. The calibrated residual (1.034–1.040 at p = 0.3) is therefore separation-dependent velocity leverage, not mass deficit.
  - **Corrected form.** Uncalibrated, the mass-deficit term is about +0.01 to +0.05 for f = 0.1–0.3. Calibrated, it is about 0. The rest of the low-quantile shift is additive companion velocity whose leverage grows with s.
  - **Dependent claims:**
    - F2's sentences "The cause is … mass … pure rescaling" and "A shape test cannot tell this part from gravity, and a tail-cutting estimator does not remove it". Only the roughly 0.03 mass part fits that description.
    - DIALECTICIAN-C's "why": "F2 shows the mass-deficit channel shifts low quantiles by +0.13 uncalibrated and +0.03–0.04 calibrated, which tail truncation cannot remove". The +0.13 is mostly not mass deficit, and the calibrated +0.03–0.04 is not mass deficit at all.
    - C's predicted common-mode +0.03 to +0.08 survives only marginally from the mass term (about +0.03 at f = 0.2). It then leans on the f(e) term and the velocity term, which a shape test can in part see.
    - DIALECTICIAN-B's "one route left" (the mass-deficit channel absorbing a rescaling) is weaker still, which does not help B.
## Unverified
- **M-DIALECTICIAN-07 magnitudes.** The directions are verified. The specific S values (0.835 and 0.979) depend on noise and on the template's α(a) and were not reproduced: noise-free with α = 1.3 gives 0.735 and 0.947.
- **M-DIALECTICIAN-11.** "Until N reaches a few hundred" is a claim about prior dominance, not about σ ∝ N^−1/2. It is too vague to derive.
- **M-DIALECTICIAN-12.** Whether the gap exceeds 10σ depends on which gap is meant. The lens's own f = 0.2 numbers give only 4.7–6.6σ.
- **M-DIALECTICIAN-13.** The mass-split power and per-cell noise were not checked.
- **Generator outputs not re-derived.** The Monte Carlo outputs behind F1–F4 and F9 (the quantile profiles, the medians and the veto rungs) are generator outputs. Only the arithmetic done on them (M-05, M-06) and their analytic premises (M-01, M-07, M-08) were checked.
## Formalizable
- M-DIALECTICIAN-01: For a real random variable X with quantile function Q_X and c > 0, Q_{cX}(p) = c·Q_X(p) for all p ∈ (0, 1). Hence (Q_{cX}(p)/Q_X(p))² = c² is constant in p.
- M-DIALECTICIAN-02: For GM > 0, s > 0, k ≥ 0 and w > 0: (GM/s)(1 + w) − GM/(s(1 + k)) > 0, and (1 + k)(1 + w) > 1.
- M-DIALECTICIAN-04: For a < b < c, (b − a)/(c − a) + (c − b)/(c − a) = 1, and (b − a)/(c − a) is strictly increasing in b, with value 0 at b = a and 1 at b = c.
- M-DIALECTICIAN-06: The line through (t1, g1) and (t2, g2), with t1 ≠ t2, has intercept (g2·t1 − g1·t2)/(t1 − t2).
- M-DIALECTICIAN-08: For q ∈ [0, 1] and p ≥ 1, (1 + q^p)^(1/p) ≤ 1 + q (the ℓ^p norm of (1, q) is at most its ℓ^1 norm), with equality iff q = 0.
