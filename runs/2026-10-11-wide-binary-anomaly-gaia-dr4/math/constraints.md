## Refuted
- M-CONSTRAINTS-09: F11 and K7 say that faking γ = 1.37 with a full median "needs a true α ≈ 0.0 (0.0), i.e. uniform". That is wrong. A uniform eccentricity distribution (α = 0) gives only γ = 1.302 against an α = 1.2 template and 1.321 against α = 1.3 (M-09 log). The lens's own F6 medians agree: (0.5895/0.5125)² = 1.323. Reaching 1.37 needs α ≈ −0.15 (template 1.3) to −0.2 (template 1.2), a distribution that rises toward circular orbits. The required shift from Hwang+22's 1.17–1.32 is therefore larger than the "6–9σ" stated. The error runs in the lens's favour: the conclusion that eccentricity can't be the dominant cause gets stronger. Dependents: F11, K7, CONSTRAINTS-F (eliminated), and the K7 entry in CONSTRAINTS-D's "α ≈ 2 lowers only 9%" (true α = 2 gives 0.906–0.910, verified). No candidate's status changes. The other F11 numbers check out within MC noise (≈ ±0.004).
## Unverified
- M-CONSTRAINTS-10 (F12, K8): the hidden-companion mass model is underspecified, so I didn't re-derive it. Simple readings don't reproduce "mean 1.27, max 1.45". With q_in ~ U(0.1, 1) and L ∝ m^3.5, the per-component ratio (1+q)/(1+q^3.5)^(1/3.5) reaches 1.64 at q = 1, and for an equal-mass wide pair the total ratio reaches 1.35. The lens's mass of the other wide component, or its averaging, must differ. The downstream statements ("+4.6% at f = 0.2"; "separation-independent unless f_multi(s) is") stay unverified with it, but CONSTRAINTS-F's elimination rests on K14 (logic), not on these numbers.
- M-CONSTRAINTS-19 (F16, K5): the inner-binary table (1.238 → 1.364 from 1 to 10 kau; the DR4-flag column; truncated −0.03) comes from a crude model with assumed detection thresholds (0.1 mas RUWE-like, 0.05 mas acceleration), σ_logP = 2.3 dex and circular inner orbits. I didn't re-simulate it. Only its κ ∝ √s premise and its ratio arithmetic are verified (M-18). CONSTRAINTS-A's "+0.10 at 10 kau" and CONSTRAINTS-C's "strained" status rest on these unverified entries.
- M-CONSTRAINTS-02 domain note: the ceiling ṽ ≤ √(2ν_e) is proven only where the EF-linearised potential holds (g_N ≪ g_Ne ≈ 1.37e-10 m s⁻²). In the transition regime (g_N ~ g_Ne) the QUMOND potential is not homogeneous of degree −1. There ν(\|g_N\|) can exceed ν_e locally where the internal and external fields partly cancel, so "no bound pair exceeds 1.77 under any interpolating function" is not established there. The tail conclusion (F2, F22) for ṽ ≳ 2 is very likely unaffected, but I didn't check it.
- M-CONSTRAINTS-17: the "median log10 g_N(r)" column (−9.69, −10.29, −9.17) was not checked.
- Not checked at all: the F22 example (f ≈ 0.1 at κ ≈ 0.5–0.6 gives 1.04–1.05). Interpolating M-07 (f = 0.2, κ = 0.5 gives 1.070 truncated) makes it plausible, but I didn't run it.
## Formalizable
- M-CONSTRAINTS-02: for real ν > 0, K ∈ [−½, 0], w ∈ [0, 1] and u ∈ (0, 1]: 2ν(1 + Kw/2)u ≤ 2ν.
- M-CONSTRAINTS-01: for r = √(x²+y²+z²) ≠ 0, ∇²[−(ν/r)(1 + K(x²+y²)/(2r²))] = νK ∂²_z(−1/r); and (1/2)∫₀^π sin³θ dθ = 2/3.
- M-CONSTRAINTS-03: if F and g are C¹ vector fields on a closed ball B (minus a point carrying the same delta source in both divergences) with ∇·(g − F) = 0, then ∮_{∂B} g·n dA = ∮_{∂B} F·n dA. For a constant vector c, ∮_{S²} c·n dA = 0.
- M-CONSTRAINTS-04: ∫₀¹ √(1 − c²) dc = π/4; the median of √(1 − c²) for c ~ U(0, 1) is √3/2.
- M-CONSTRAINTS-11: with γ = (m/m0)² and sd(m) = 1/(2p√N), the delta method gives sd(γ) = γ/(p m √N).
- M-CONSTRAINTS-16: if ΔlnL = (γ − γ̂)²/(2σ²), then σ = \|γ − γ̂\|/√(2ΔlnL).
- M-CONSTRAINTS-18: Δv/√(GM/s) = Δv√s/√(GM) for G, M, s > 0.
