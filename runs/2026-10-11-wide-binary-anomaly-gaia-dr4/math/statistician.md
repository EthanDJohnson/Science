## Refuted
- M-STATISTICIAN-13: for p₂ = 0.037 (z = 2.09), 1/(−e p ln p) = 3.0:1, not 2.4:1, and the prior needed for P > 0.5 is 0.25, not ≈ 0.3. The value 2.4:1 belongs to p ≈ 0.05. This affects only the Chae 2025 row of Table 1; its "noise-level / fragile" verdict is unchanged.
- M-STATISTICIAN-14: under the lens's own model, P(F3 ∈ [0.97, 1.07]) is 0.73 under Newton and 0.14 under MOND, a likelihood ratio of about 5. The quoted 0.69 / 0.08 (ratio ≈ 8.6) are Table 3's probabilities for [0.95, 1.05] (0.685 / 0.084), attached to the wrong interval. Candidate STATISTICIAN-A's test is therefore about 40% weaker than stated. The direction is unchanged. Correct form: either quote [0.95, 1.05] with 0.685 / 0.084, or [0.97, 1.07] with 0.73 / 0.14.
- M-STATISTICIAN-15: these numbers are total biases at f_t = 0.2 that include the triple bias; they are not the increments the text attributes to the template or the mass. Independently, a thermal truth analysed with an α = 1.25 or 1.3 template shifts a pure-binary median by +0.040 or +0.050 in γ. On the lens's f_t = 0.2 baseline (1.11–1.15) that gives 1.15–1.20, which matches its own simulation's 1.177, so the template's contribution is about +0.03–0.07, not +0.18. A 10% mass underestimate multiplies γ_est by exactly 1/0.9 = 1.111: that is +0.12–0.13 on the uncalibrated median and +0.11 on the mixture, not +0.28 and +0.15. Those two figures are γ − 1 totals (1.277, 1.150 in the lens log). Depends on it: the "Unmodelled systematics" failure mode, candidate STATISTICIAN-E's quantitative support, and finding 9's sensitivity sentence wherever it is read as a shift. Where a refuter or judge uses "eccentricity template +0.18" as a size of systematic, it is overstated by about 3–4×.
## Unverified
- M-STATISTICIAN-12: the injection-recovery numbers are outputs of the run's generator and estimators (triples with photocentre wobble, Gaia noise, f_multi calibration, mixture fits). No independent derivation was built here; re-implementing the generator would duplicate the mechanist's calculation. The only independent piece, the thermal-template factor (M-15), agrees with the lens's simulated 1.177. Finding 10's inference that "calibration removes the triple bias when f_multi is separation-independent" therefore rests on unchecked simulation output.
## Formalizable
- M-STATISTICIAN-02: for p ∈ [0, 1] and real N ≥ 1, 1 − (1 − p)^N ≥ p, and 1 − (1 − p)^N = Np + O(p²).
- M-STATISTICIAN-04: for σ1, σ2 > 0 and ρ ∈ [−1, 1], σ1² + σ2² − 2ρσ1σ2 is strictly decreasing in ρ and equals (σ1 − σ2)² at ρ = 1; for the 2×2 equicorrelated covariance with equal variances s², (1ᵀC⁻¹1)⁻¹ = s²(1 + ρ)/2.
- M-STATISTICIAN-06: for p ∈ (0, 1/e), −e p ln p ∈ (0, 1) and is strictly increasing; with posterior Bπ/(Bπ + 1 − π), the prior giving posterior 1/2 is 1/(1 + B).
- M-STATISTICIAN-07: for Δ, σ1, σ2 > 0, Δ/√(σ1²/k + σ2²) → Δ/σ2 as k → ∞, and equals 5 at k = σ1²/(Δ²/25 − σ2²) when Δ > 5σ2.
- M-STATISTICIAN-08: the median of a log-uniform distribution on [a, b] is √(ab), and E[1/k] = (1/a − 1/b)/ln(b/a).
