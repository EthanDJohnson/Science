## Unverified
- M-DECOMPOSER-08: these are outputs of the run's generator (triples with photocentre wobble averaged over the baseline, L ∝ m^3.5, a_in log-uniform 0.1–300 au with a stability cut, DR3/DR4 noise). An independent re-derivation would mean rebuilding the generator's triple model, which is the mechanist's job; I did not do it. The arithmetic on these numbers (M-03) and the physics of which inner periods leak into a proper motion (M-07: fraction R = 3(sin x − x cos x)/x³ of the orbital speed, x = πT/P) are verified. So the claims that the triple bias grows with separation at fixed f_triple (F2) and that a_in = 5–300 au carries most of it (F10) are consistent with the checked physics, but their sizes (1.075 / 1.128; 1.039 vs 1.177) are unchecked. These are single-seed results, and the lens itself expects about ±0.02 jitter.
- M-DECOMPOSER-09: my independent Kepler MC gives 1.044 / 1.270 noise-free and 1.037 / 1.219 with a simple noise model (template α = 1.27). The lens reports 1.063 / 1.305. Noise in my model pulls the ratios toward 1, so the lens's higher values probably come from the generator's per-pair HTZ α(a) mixture, its mass-estimate scatter, or its bin selection, none of which I reproduced. These numbers support only the remark "consistent with [D-49]". They are not load-bearing, and the α = 2 value that F11 uses does reproduce (0.925 against 0.935).
## Formalizable
- M-DECOMPOSER-01: for γ_T, γ_C, b_T, b_C > 0, (γ_T/b_T)/(γ_C/b_C) = γ_T/γ_C when b_T = b_C. If γ_T = γ_C, the ratio equals k > 0 iff b_T/b_C = 1/k. If a random variable X > 0 has median m, then cX has median cm for c > 0.
- M-DECOMPOSER-07: for T, ω > 0, the least-squares slope of t ↦ A sin(ωt + φ) on [−T/2, T/2] is A cos φ · 12(2 sin(ωT/2) − ωT cos(ωT/2))/(ω²T³). Writing x = ωT/2, the ratio to Aω is R(x) = 3(sin x − x cos x)/x³, with R(x) → 1 as x → 0⁺ and R(x) → 0 as x → ∞.
- M-DECOMPOSER-06 (sub-claim): for n = 1 and x > 0, y = x²/(1 + x) solves y·ν₁(y) = x, where ν₁(y) = (1 + √(1 + 4/y))/2; and √y·ν₁(y) → 1 as y → 0⁺.
- M-DECOMPOSER-02: ln(a/b) + ln(b/c) = ln(a/c) for a, b, c > 0. This is trivial and is listed only for completeness.
