# Analysis: mechanist (causes and mechanisms)
status: final

## Method applied
For each candidate cause of the DR3 γ disagreement I write the causal chain (process, what it acts on, what sustains it, what ends it), classify it, evidence each link from the dossier or a calculation, and find the weakest link. The key new output is an independent injection-recovery reproduction of Boufourou's synthetic results using the run's generator and estimator calculators.

## Findings
1. Smoke test (Newtonian truth, f_triple = 0.2 input, N_low = 2583 pairs in log10 g_N ∈ [−11, −9.5]): the RUWE-like wobble cut removes about half of the triples (residual triple fraction 0.102 in the scoring bin). Recovered γ: E1a full-median vs pure template 1.059 ± 0.024; E1b Chae-style empirical calibration 0.983 ± 0.030; E1c template with f_multi calibrated at high g (f_multi,cal = 0.136) 0.959 ± 0.022; E2a threshold ṽ < 1.4 1.028 ± 0.016; E2b trimmed 1.050 ± 0.025; E3 mixture 0.994 ± 0.026; E4 simplified Bayes 1.072 ± 0.025 [calc: runs/2026-10-11-wide-binary-anomaly-gaia-dr4/calc/lens-mechanist_quick.py]. Superseded by the full grid below.
2. Mechanism scales, M_tot = 1.5 Msun [calc: runs/2026-10-11-wide-binary-anomaly-gaia-dr4/calc/lens-mechanist_scales.py]: v_c = 0.816 / 0.516 / 0.365 / 0.258 / 0.211 km/s at s = 2 / 5 / 10 / 20 / 30 kau. Any perturbation fixed in km/s grows in ṽ units as √s: a 0.1 km/s photocentre velocity is 0.12 v_c at 2 kau but 0.39 v_c at 20 kau; an unbound-pair dispersion of 0.5 km/s is 0.61 v_c at 2 kau and 1.94 v_c at 20 kau. This is the common physical reason every contamination channel (M1, M6, M7) is separation-dependent, and why a calibration at 0.2–2 kau (M2) cannot transfer exactly. Perspective term (v_sys = 40 km/s assumed): Δv_p/v_c = 0.15 at 20 kau and d = 100 pc, 0.30 at d = 50 pc, so uncorrected perspective is a first-order effect in the widest bins, consistent with Chae & Yoon saying it matters in the last bin (15–30 kau) [D-62]. Proper-motion noise (σ_μ = 0.02 mas/yr per star, G ≈ 13) is 0.05–0.10 v_c at 20 kau and 100–200 pc on DR3, falling ≈ 2.4× on DR4 [D-51]; an unmodelled per-axis noise of 0.05 (0.10) in ṽ units biases a median-based γ by only ≈ +1.3% (+5.3%) (crude quadrature estimate).
3. The "ended by" link for M1 already exists in DR3 at small scale: Gaia NSS solutions identify inner binaries inside wide pairs [new: Nagarajan et al. 2024, PASP (arXiv:2407.16760), abstract via lit_search, "we construct a sample of 14,791 gravitationally bound wide pairs in which one of the components is an unresolved binary with an astrometric orbital or acceleration solution. These systems are hierarchical triples, with inner binary separations of order $1$ au, and outer separations of $100$-$100,000$ au"]. Inner orbits of ~1 au have P ≈ 1 yr, i.e. they are the ones RUWE and NSS catch; the velocity-relevant long-period inner binaries (a_in ≳ 30 au, P ≳ 100 yr) are not in it, which is why DR4's 66-month baseline and acceleration solutions [D-12] matter, but only partially (P ≫ 66 months stays invisible). Whether the triple fraction itself depends on outer separation (premise 2) was not found in a quotable source [new: WebSearch, search-summary, SUMMARY "The search didn't turn up a direct measurement of how the fraction of wide-binary components that are close binaries varies with outer separation"].
4. **Injection-recovery grid (key output).** ~1e5 generated test pairs per catalogue, 8,085–9,958 pairs in the scoring bin log10 g_N ∈ [−11, −9.5] (g_N in m s^-2) after cuts, i.e. Banik-sized (8,611 [D-28]). The RUWE-like cut leaves about half the injected triples (residual 0.05 / 0.10 / 0.15–0.165 for input 0.1 / 0.2 / 0.3). Full table in Lens-specific outputs [calc: runs/2026-10-11-wide-binary-anomaly-gaia-dr4/calc/lens-mechanist_grid_g10.py, _g12.py, _g14.py]. Newtonian truth: the uncalibrated full median (E1a, pure Newtonian template) returns 0.989 / 1.042 / 1.084 / 1.141 for input f_triple = 0 / 0.1 / 0.2 / 0.3. **Boufourou's +0.08 to +0.13 full-median bias [D-50] is reproduced in sign and size** (+0.10 at 10% residual triples, +0.15 at 16.5%); my generator's triples are about twice as effective per unit residual fraction as his.
5. **Boufourou's robust-estimator immunity is NOT reproduced for tail truncation as such.** The truncated-likelihood estimator E2a (ṽ < 1.40, pure-binary model renormalised) returns 0.971 / 1.037 / 1.081 / 1.123 under Newton, essentially the same triple bias as the median; the trimmed mean E2b is worse (up to 1.165). Reason (mechanism): most residual triples in this generator carry kicks of 0.1–0.5 v_c (Finding 2), so they shift the CORE of the ṽ distribution below √2 rather than populating the tail, and cutting the tail does not remove them. The mixture likelihood (E3) is partly robust: 0.971 / 1.003 / 1.027 / 1.046 (+0.03 to +0.075 over its own f = 0 value). [calc: as Finding 4]
6. **What does remove the triple bias is calibration on Newtonian-regime pairs from the same pipeline, not the choice of median vs truncation.** Chae-style empirical calibration (E1b: γ = (median ṽ_low / median ṽ_cal)², calibration bin log10 g_N ∈ [−8.5, −7.0]) returns 0.938 / 0.994 / 1.012 / 1.028 under Newton; template median with f_multi calibrated at high g and carried over (E1c) returns 0.912 / 0.971 / 0.985 / 1.006. The f_multi calibrated at high g (0.10–0.17) matches the true residual triple fraction (0.05–0.165) to within a few per cent. So in this generator premise 2 (calibration transfers) holds to ≲ 0.03 in γ for triples, and the eccentricity gradient with separation [D-41] biases a calibrated median LOW by ≈ 0.06 at f = 0 (the calibration zone is more circular, so its median ṽ is higher). Boufourou's own remark that E2 returned 1.18 on Newtonian truth "without zone-matched noisy templates" [D-50] fits this reading: his E2 is a calibrated estimator. [calc: as Finding 4]
7. **No estimator family suppresses an injected boost.** At f_triple = 0: γ_inj = 1.2 → 1.148–1.208 across all seven estimators; γ_inj = 1.4 → 1.282–1.396 (the calibrated E1b/E1c are low by 0.10–0.12 at f = 0 because of the eccentricity-gradient offset in Finding 6). With triples, all families overshoot (γ_inj = 1.4, f = 0.2: E2a 1.478, E3 1.452, E1b 1.419). Premise 4's worry (robust or mixture estimators absorb a real boost into the triple component) is not borne out: the E3 mixture recovers 1.386–1.452 at γ_inj = 1.4 and 1.208–1.255 at 1.2. Boufourou's "all ≥ 1.26" for MOND truth [D-50] is reproduced. [calc: as Finding 4]

9. **Arithmetic of the Chae–Boufourou gap on the same catalogue.** Chae 2024b reports 1.37 (+0.10 −0.09 stat, +0.16 −0.09 syst) [D-22]; Boufourou gets 1.045–1.05 on Chae's 81,088 pairs after cutting to 41,760 [D-31]. In my reproduction no single mechanism at a plausible level opens a +0.3 gap between calibrated estimators: triples ≤ +0.03 after calibration, no RUWE cut +0.05, PM noise +0.03, a_in range +0.03, eccentricity truth +0.04. Getting +0.3 needs about 20% unbound or flyby pairs in the wide zone only (from the +1.5 per unit slope), or a 25% mass underestimate confined to wide pairs, or a combination. Neither size is supported by any dossier item [§6 gaps], though Pasquini's 3 of 12 non-bound RV-clean pairs at s > 13 kau [D-38] and the two above-escape systems in Chae et al. 2026 [D-26] point to the unbound channel. Uncalibrated medians (Boufourou's implied 1.12–1.20 on the same sample [D-31]) are the size my E1a gives.
10. **MOND target in the scoring bin** [calc: wide_binary_population.py gamma CLI]: γ_MOND(1e-10 m s^-2) = 1.3748 (BZ18 simple, Chae-form transition), 1.0608 (BZ18 standard). EF-limit anisotropy 1.354–1.560 around η = 1.4228 (simple) [D-02, D-43]. Full two-body QUMOND: 1.2–1.3 [D-45]. A boost of 1.06–1.42 is "MOND" depending on the interpolating function and the dynamics, so a measured intermediate γ ≈ 1.1–1.25 is not by itself evidence against a modified-gravity mechanism, or for one.
