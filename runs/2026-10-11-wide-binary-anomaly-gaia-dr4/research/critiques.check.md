## Most serious problem
RC-02 attributes the f_trip = 0.10 recalibration to the PSS sample, but the source gives it for the Chae 2023 ruwe<1.2 sample. The downstream reading, that Pittordis's preference for Newton rests on an uncalibrated triple fraction, is therefore less supported by this number than the claim implies.
