## Response to the verdicts

Overall: one verdict says "weakened" (C2-0) and two say "survives" (C2-1, C2-2). All three agree on the core: a single outgoing wave's own pair production has a finite total, there is no self-sustained decay, and nothing is observable. I concede two narrowings and one weakening of wording, and I keep the core.

**Restated claim after concessions.** A single outgoing GW's own photon-pair production (self-fusion, including curvature tails) has a finite, distance-independent total. The yield beyond R falls at least as 1/R; the toys give about R^-3 and put it within about a wavelength. So the wave has no self-induced lifetime and no self-induced path depletion. The only sustained loss of a real wave comes from crossing the ambient GW background (the C6 ensemble process). Its lifetime is >= 5e124 s (SI), and nothing is observable either way.

### C2-0 (physics; weakened)

1. **Ambient cross-fusion gives a real wave a finite lifetime. Conceded.**
   - The rate per test graviton is Gamma_1 = (16 pi/15) G^2 w rho_b (natural units). This is the same cross-section C2 uses, applied to background gravitons arriving at non-zero angle.
   - I re-ran it at 150 Hz (SI) [calc: crux-C2_discriminators.py]:

     | Background | Lifetime (s, SI) | Fraction depleted over 410 Mpc |
     |---|---|---|
     | Primordial DeltaN_eff = 0.3 ceiling, rho_b = 2.84e-15 J/m^3 | 5.17e124 | 8.2e-109 |
     | NANOGrav-level, rho_b = 6.1e-18 J/m^3 | 2.41e127 | 1.8e-111 |
     | LVK limit, rho_b = 2.2e-18 J/m^3 | 6.67e127 | 6.3e-112 |

     These match C2-0's table and C2-2's route 4.
   - The heading "no lifetime, no path depletion" is therefore true only of self-production. I adopt C2-0's narrowed wording ("no self-induced lifetime or path depletion").
   - What I keep: this is fusion with other waves, i.e. scattering on ambient radiation, not decay of the wave. C2-1 makes the same analogy, to gamma-ray absorption on the CMB. Its rate is C6's eq. 14 kinetics per test graviton. C2's scope bullet already gave multi-directional ensembles to C6. The concession is to the heading's wording, not to the physics C2 argues.
2. **"C2's own prediction fails beyond R_x ~ 0.5–4 pc." Conceded for a real wave. One qualification.**
   - "Saturates at a near-source total" must be stated for an isolated wave, or after subtracting the ambient term.
   - That subtraction is clean. The ambient term scales as rho_b and grows exactly linearly in R, so it can be computed analytically and removed.
   - Qualification: C2-0's R_x uses the toy (R^-4 per length) self term. Against C2-2's worst-case self bound (bound A, 1/R^2 per length), the crossover is pushed out to 5.0e27 m (1.6e11 pc) for the primordial ceiling and ~1e29 m (3.5e12 pc) for NANOGrav-level backgrounds (SI) [calc: crux-C2_discriminators.py, line [2]]. Both lie beyond the Hubble radius.
   - So a "slope +1 beyond R_x" in a real-universe calculation is guaranteed only if the self term follows the toys. Either way it carries the rho_b signature and is not C1's eq. 8 rate.
3. **The decisive slope test needs a correction. Conceded.**
   - It must be posed for an isolated wave, i.e. rho_b = 0.
   - Equivalently, it must test whether the far-zone rate per unit length is proportional to rho_b (ambient, C6-type) or proportional to N0 w^3 sigma^2 independent of rho_b (C1's eq. 8). See the deciding observation below.
4. **Tails strengthen convergence (head-on/diffraction ratio about 1e-3 at r = lambda, falling as r^-4).** Accepted as support. C2-0 itself labels it a heuristic Born estimate with O(1) factors untracked, so I cite it as a scaling argument only.
5. **"Path depletion of a single wave is zero at this order" (dialectician F7 (iv)) is wrong for a real wave.** Conceded in the same way as point 1: about 1e-111 over 410 Mpc for PTA-level backgrounds, non-zero but immaterial.
6. **The R^-3 exponent depends on the toy. Conceded** (see C2-2, point 2). C2's core needs only convergence.
7. **Plasma (C2-0's remaining doubt).** I add one point. In the mean IGM the plasma frequency is 4.2 Hz [D-40], so 150 Hz production is kinematically allowed along most of the intergalactic path. The ambient term is therefore not removed by plasma, only its photons are absorbed. This does not change the conclusion.

### C2-1 (evidence; survives)

1. **Chakraborty et al. 2024 (linear-in-h production).** I accept C2-1's analysis.
   - The quoted non-collinearity requirement gives the partner momentum (k' - k)^2 = -2 k.k' < 0, which is space-like.
   - The TT polarisation kills the collinear vertex, to 1.9e-16.
   - The paper gives no number.

   This is the strongest published dissent, and it does not produce sustained far-zone production by a free wave.
2. **Jones–Singleton attenuation.** It is excluded by GW150914, which needs an e-folding length > 5.3e25 m (SI) against their ~1e11 m [M-CONSTRAINTS-14; D-39, D-53], and by GW170817's d_GW/d_EM [M-EMPIRICIST-05]. Accepted.
3. **Ambient cross-fusion (C2-1 point 5):** Gamma = 5.2e-128 s^-1 at Omega_GW = 1e-8. Conceded as in C2-0 point 1. C2-1 classifies it as C6's regime, and so do I.
4. **Exponent is soft, convergence is not (M-MECHANIST-04: converges for any spread exponent a > -1/4).** Accepted. The C2 heading's "(lambda/2 pi R)^3" is downgraded to "falls faster than R^-1 in the toys; at least as R^-1 in the worst case".
5. **"Within a few wavelengths" places the production at r <~ lambda, where the free-graviton model fails [examiner F7]. Conceded.**
   - C2 claims convergence and the absence of a far-zone rate.
   - It does not claim a near-source number. That number belongs to C3/C4/C5.
6. **Deser 1975 [D-58] is a search summary only. Conceded.** It is dropped from C2's evidence list.

## Deciding observation

**No observation can separate C2 from any of the rivals named here.**
- C3, C4, C5, C6 and C7 all predict path depletion <= ~1e-57 and no detectable Hz–kHz photons (plasma cutoff [D-62]; flux 2e43 below the CMB Rayleigh–Jeans foreground [D-46]).
- The only observational channel is standard-siren GW/EM distance ratios at ~1e-2. These test only large damping, already excluded [D-39; M-EMPIRICIST-05]. LVK IR1 is planned from November 2026, and O5 has no fixed date [D-70].
- Every crux below is therefore a calculation.
- In each case C2 is largely compatible with the rival: C2 is the far-zone null, and C3–C7 are near-source, ensemble or interpretive claims. I name the result that would split them and say honestly where no outcome can favour the rival over C2.

**Shared master calculation (C2's own decisive test).** Compute the yield beyond R of a retarded quadrupole source (circular binary, then NR merger) as a spin-2 in-in calculation at O(G^2), with rho_b = 0. Include second-order memory and tail terms at q0 ~ 2w.
- C2 needs d ln P(>R)/d ln R <= -1 at R >> lambda. The toys give about -3, and bound A forbids anything above -1 unless the kinetic proxy fails by more than O(1).
- A result of slope >= 0 refutes C2. Precision needed: the slope to ±0.5, which is trivial once computed.
- Who could do it: groups using coherent-state waveform formalisms [D-02, D-03], the Redi–Tesi functional [D-60], or B&O.
- When: nothing is published as of 2026-10-13 [D-66; dossier section 6].

**vs C3 (time-symmetric state; retarded total 0.02–0.1 x eq. 8 x T_eff).**
- Deciding calculation: the master calculation's total, as the ratio P_ret / (eq. 8 x T_eff), together with the cheap check of whether B&O's eq. 5 field has an incoming shell at t = -10/sigma, r = 10 c/sigma.
- A ratio in 0.3–3 with slope <= -1 favours C2 over C3. C2 survives and C3's "substantially less" fails.
- No outcome favours C3 over C2. C3's own prediction ("made at r <~ 5 lambda/2 pi") entails C2's convergence, and a slope >= 0 would refute both.
- Precision: the ratio to a factor ~3. That is enough to split 0.02–0.1 from 0.3–3.

**vs C4 (near-source total of order B&O's per-packet total, distance-independent).**
- Deciding calculation: the same total, from NR h_mn (including h^(2)) in the Redi–Tesi functional with c_J = 16 [D-60], compared with (12 pi/25) G^2 w Int L^2 dt/c^10.
- C4 is compatible with C2. Both need convergence, and C4 adds a magnitude.
- The only split comes from bound A. By the calculation above, at most 1.0 x B&O's total can lie beyond lambda, and 6.3 x beyond c/w.
  - A total within C4's factor ~3 that is mostly made at r > 10 lambda would contradict bound A. It would mean the kinetic proxy fails, which undercuts C2's robust convergence argument and favours neither.
  - A total made at r <~ c/w favours both.
- Precision: the radial distribution of production to a factor ~3 at r = lambda.

**vs C5 (source's own field, linear channel, dominates near the source).**
- Deciding calculation: the coefficient k in P_lin ~ k (hbar w/E_Pl)^2 N for an NR merger-ringdown, with the Redi–Tesi kernel, and its radial distribution.
- C5 does not conflict with C2 on far-zone convergence. Redi–Tesi's "null-like ... perturbations do not produce particles" [D-60] confines the linear channel to the source's off-shell field.
- C5 would break C2's "nothing observable" only if k were large enough for 1e-2 depletion. For GW150914 that needs 1.8e76 pairs, i.e. k >= 1.9e78 against the dimensional expectation O(1) [calc: crux-C2_discriminators.py, line [3]; P_lin/k = 9.3e-3 from M-EXAMINER-09].
- Any k << 1e78 leaves C2 intact, whatever it says about C5's k >~ 3e-4 threshold.
- A linear-channel yield whose time-like support extends into r >> lambda would favour a far-zone source of pairs and refute C2. That would contradict the Redi–Tesi null-support statement.

**vs C6 (sustained fusion only in multi-directional ensembles).**
- This is where the verdicts moved C2's boundary: the ambient term is C6's kinetics applied to one test wave.
- Deciding calculation: in the master calculation, run with rho_b > 0, test whether the far-zone production per unit length is proportional to rho_b with coefficient (16 pi/15) G^2 w (C6-type, consistent with narrowed C2), or proportional to N0 w^3 sigma^2 independent of rho_b (C1's eq. 8, refuting C2).
- Precision: the coefficient to O(1), which is enough because the two candidate rates differ by >= 4e42 for GW150914. The ambient lifetime is >= 5.2e124 s (SI) against B&O's tau_h = 1.3e82 s (SI) [D-42], a ratio of 4e42 at the primordial ceiling and larger for real backgrounds.
- A C6 refinement cannot favour C6 over C2, because they are now compatible by construction. Only a rho_b-independent far-zone rate splits them, and it refutes C2.
- C6's own coefficient test, deriving sigma(RL -> gamma gamma) = (4 pi/5) G^2 s independently of the toolkit, fixes the size of C2's conceded ambient lifetime but not its existence.

**vs C7 (classical h^(2) nonlinearity; hbar-independent pair number).**
- Deciding calculation: the time-like Fourier support of the classical second-order metric h^(2) of an outgoing quadrupole wave, restricted to r > R, and the Redi–Tesi Maxwell yield (c_J = 16) on it.
- C7 explicitly relies on C2 ("The calculations behind C2 answer it: no").
- A time-like support at kappa ~ 1/R that shrinks with R, giving a yield that falls at least as R^-1, favours both.
- A time-like content of h^(2) that persists, i.e. does not shrink with R (for example from nonlinear memory, whose Fourier content sits at q0 <~ sigma), refutes C2. It leaves C7's reframing intact.
- The split between them is therefore one-sided: C7 can survive C2's failure, but not the reverse on this question.
- C7's own crossing-beam test (classical h^(2) yield against the kinetic rate w^4 h^4 (1 - cos theta)^2/(1280 pi c^3) [M-MECHANIST-03]) bears on C7's interpretation, not on C2.
- Precision: the sign of the slope of the time-like weight of h^(2) with R.
