## Response to the verdicts

All three refuters return **survives**, with basis calculation. Each attacked the one number C5 left open, the coefficient k, and each found it about 100 times above C5's own survival threshold. Below I take their points one by one. I concede three clauses of the candidate and keep the core claim.

**1. "k is uncomputed" (C5's own evidence-against; the main attack in all three verdicts). Withdrawn as a weakness. I accept the computed value.**
- The two analytic routes agree on the long-wavelength (quadrupole) value k = 67/(630 pi) = 0.0339:
  - C5-0 obtains it from the massive-spin-2 spectral route and from Redi–Tesi with c_J = 16 [D-60]. The two agree to 1e-15, and the normalisation is checked against (32/5) G mu^2 a^4 Omega^6 [calc: runs/2026-10-13-gravitational-wave-decay-photon-pairs/calc/falsifier-C5-0_linear_k.py].
  - C5-1 gets 3.385e-2 by an independent script [calc: runs/2026-10-13-gravitational-wave-decay-photon-pairs/calc/falsifier-C5-1_linear_k.py].
- C5-2's full 4D Born integral gives k = 0.0357 for a point source, falling to 0.0251 at a finite-size form factor v = 0.8. Its semi-analytic upper estimate is 1/(6 pi) = 0.053 [calc: runs/2026-10-13-gravitational-wave-decay-photon-pairs/calc/falsifier-C5-2_linear_k.py].
- Against the threshold k >~ 3.15e-4, the margin is 80 to 113 over the computed range and 168 at the upper estimate.
- For GW150914 at B&O's inputs this gives P_lin = 2.3e-4 to 3.3e-4 pairs, against B&O's fusion total of 2.92e-6 pairs, i.e. 80 to 114 times larger [calc: crux-C5_discriminators.py, part 1].
- Concession: k is a first-order Born, quadrupole-source number. At merger, w a ~ 1 and the field is strong, so k there carries an O(1) uncertainty that nobody has computed. C5-2 shows a smooth decline with source size (0.0357 → 0.0251 from v = 0 to 0.8). To reverse the ranking, the strong field would have to suppress k by a further ~80, and no verdict names a mechanism that does.

**2. "The inspiral case fails" (mechanist F10a / M-MECHANIST-10, carried in C5's evidence-against). Conceded as wrong. The correction widens C5.**
- All three refuters make the same point. M-MECHANIST-10 checks a local phase speed q0/q_phi = Omega r of one rigidly rotating harmonic, not the space-time Fourier support of a compact source.
- T~(w, q) is analytic in q and non-zero on the light cone |q| = w, because that value is the radiated GW. It is therefore non-zero inside the time-like ball |q| < w.
- The rotating (circular) pattern gives the same k as the linear quadrupole in all three calculations.
- So the restriction "during merger-ringdown" in the core claim is unnecessary. The linear channel operates throughout the inspiral, and the merger dominates only because P_lin ∝ Int w dE_GW.
- This also restores the GW170817 100 Hz-bin ratio that the candidate had marked as undercut: P_lin/P_BO = 1.1e6 there (C5-0, Part 4).

**3. "Remnant Hawking emission" belongs in the mechanism list. Conceded. Sub-hypothesis (b) is withdrawn from the core.**
- All three verdicts say it is thermal single-photon emission from a quasi-stationary horizon. It is not GW-induced, its partners are behind the horizon, and the progenitors emit it before the event, so it has no per-event total.
- The candidate's own evidence-against already said "a separate process, not GW-induced". Listing it under "the source's own dynamical field" was inconsistent.
- What remains true is a side comparison. At the GW150914 peak, the remnant's Hawking photon power (~9.5e-33 W) is about 3 times the linear-channel pair power (3.1e-33 W) (C5-1, Step 6). So if the question were "photons of any origin near the source", Hawking would lead.
- The core claim is about *pairs* from the *source's dynamics*, and it rests on sub-hypothesis (a) alone.

**4. Sub-hypothesis (c) (merger-ringdown at O(h^2)) is subsumed (C5-1). Conceded.** The linear O(h) time-like content of the full source T already leads. O(h^2) pieces, including B&O's fusion, are corrections of relative size ~ G E sigma/c^5.

**5. Interference between the linear and fusion amplitudes. Accepted as a bounded uncertainty, not a threat.**
- C5-0 bounds the cross term at about ±20% for GW150914.
- C5-1 and C5-2 say it is higher order or of size sqrt(P_lin P_fus).
- Even a maximal cross term of 2 sqrt(P_lin P_fus) is 2 sqrt(0.0101) = 0.20 of P_lin at B&O's fusion size [calc: crux-C5_discriminators.py, part 3, P_fus/P_lin = 1.01e-2]. The ranking is unchanged.

**6. Literature normalisation (C5-1). Noted as an open discrepancy that does not run against C5.**
- Rahvar 2026 (preprint) has the same scaling, dE/dt ∝ M^(10/3) w^(16/3), but his normalisation implies k_scalar ≈ 14. The conformal-scalar value from this run's formula is 2.82e-3 (c_J = 4/3 instead of 16), so the two differ by 5.0e3 [calc: crux-C5_discriminators.py, part 1].
- If Rahvar is right, the linear channel is larger still. C5's margin rests on this run's k, not on his.

**7. Scope of the claim after the verdicts.** The narrowed wording I defend is this. Near a real compact source, the leading photon-pair channel is the source's own off-shell field acting at linear order, with P_lin ≈ 0.025–0.036 G w E_GW/c^5 (SI). It exceeds N^2 fusion by about 1e2 for GW150914 and by more for weaker sources, throughout the inspiral and merger. Remnant Hawking emission is a separate, non-pair photon background.

**Practical note (all verdicts).** Every channel is unobservable: 150 Hz photons cannot cross gas with n_e > 2.8e-4 cm^-3 [D-62], and the yield is ~3e-4 pairs per event. C5 is a claim about which channel dominates *in principle*. It predicts nothing observational.

## Deciding observation

No observation can separate C5 from any rival: every channel is unobservable by a factor of ≥ 1e30 [D-45, D-46, D-47], and no instrument has a free-space Hz–kHz photon channel [D-70]. Each crux below is therefore a calculation. None is published as of 2026-10-13 [dossier section 6]. Anyone using the Redi–Tesi functional [D-60], coherent-state waveform formalisms [D-02, D-03], or B&O themselves could do them.

**Versus C4 (per-event total of order eq. 8 x T_eff) and C3 (retarded fusion only 0.02–0.1 of that). This is the sharpest crux.**
- The calculation is C4's own decisive test, carried out in full: feed the numerical-relativity metric of a GW150914-like merger into the Redi–Tesi functional with c_J = 16 [D-60]. Keep both the O(h) time-like Weyl content, including the near-zone off-shell field, and the h^(2) part.
- The cleanest discriminator is how the total pair number scales with radiated energy at fixed w and sigma_w:
  - C5 predicts d ln P/d ln E = 1.00 to 1.03. Fusion adds 1e-3 to 3e-2 of P_lin under C3's to C4's fusion sizes [calc: crux-C5_discriminators.py, part 3].
  - C3 and C4, which take fusion to dominate, predict a slope of 2.
- Equivalently, look at the total divided by eq. 8 x T_eff (2.92e-6 pairs):
  - C5 predicts 80–114;
  - C4 predicts 0.3–3 (0.09–1.8 with pattern factors);
  - C3 predicts 0.02–0.1.
- Precision needed: a factor of ~10. The gap is 27 between C5's low end and C4's high end, and ~800 to C3 [calc: crux-C5_discriminators.py, part 2].
- If the in-in calculation keeps only the N^2 term, it tests C3 against C4 and says nothing about C5. The source's off-shell field must be kept.
- The ranking flips to fusion only if the luminosity-weighted <L> exceeds 0.0224 c^5/G = 8.1e50 W (SI). GW150914's peak is 4.4e-2 of that [calc: crux-C5_discriminators.py, part 3].

**Versus C2 (no vacuum decay; production converges within a few wavelengths of the source).**
- C5 is not in tension with C2's main claim. C2's scope note leaves near-source production to C3–C5.
- The linear channel needs the off-shell field. The radiated far-zone field has null support, which gives zero under Redi–Tesi [D-60]. So C5 also predicts a finite, distance-independent total made at r <~ c/w.
- The separating calculation targets C2's strong variant ("identically zero for every vacuum GW configuration"): evaluate the Redi–Tesi yield of a compact quadrupole source's own T~(w, |q| < w).
  - C5 predicts 0.034 G w E/c^5 (SI); the strong variant predicts 0.
  - Three independent refuter calculations already give the non-zero value, which favours C5 over the strong variant.
- On distance dependence, both predict that the linear-channel yield beyond R falls steeply with R once R > c/w. The same in-in calculation would show this, but it does not separate them.

**Versus C6 (sustained fusion only in isotropic ensembles, eq. 14, negligible).**
- The two claims answer different questions and are compatible.
- The quantity that separates them is the cosmic pair budget tied to the astrophysical GW background. Compare (i) the linear channel at the sources that made the background, about k G w rho_GW/c^5, with (ii) eq. 14 fusion acting on the background for a Hubble time, (8 pi/15) G^2 rho^2 t_H/c^7.
- For Omega_GW(25 Hz) = 0.9e-9 to 2.0e-9 [D-34] the ratio (i)/(ii) is 6e27 to 1.4e28. These are order-of-magnitude numbers, ignoring bandwidth and redshift [calc: crux-C5_discriminators.py, part 4].
- My kinetic rate, 8.1e-116 pairs m^-3 s^-1 at 2e-9, agrees with D-44's 3e-115 photons m^-3 s^-1 at 2.8e-9 after scaling.
- C5 predicts the source term dominates. C6 makes no claim about that budget, so the result would not refute C6, only rank it.
- C6's own theoretical test (an independent sigma(RL → gamma gamma)) does not touch C5.
- Who: any refuter, in an afternoon. Precision: orders of magnitude.

**Versus C7 (the N^2 effect is classical h^(2) nonlinearity; the pair number is hbar-free).**
- These are also compatible. C5's P_lin = k G w E/c^5 contains no hbar either (C5-0), so both channels give hbar-independent pair numbers.
- C7's decisive test uses two crossing plane GW beams. There the field is on-shell and C5's linear channel is absent, so C5 is silent on it.
- The separating calculation is C7's semiclassical Redi–Tesi yield on the classical metric of a *compact source* to second order. Compare the contribution of the h^(1) off-shell near-zone field with that of h^(2):
  - C5 predicts h^(1) dominates by 20–400 for GW150914. That is the C5-2 range against C4's fusion totals, with ratio ∝ c^5/(G E sigma).
  - C7 read as a statement about the dominant source of pairs predicts h^(2).
- Precision: a factor of ~10.
