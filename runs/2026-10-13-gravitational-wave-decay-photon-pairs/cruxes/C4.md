## Response to the verdicts

All three verdicts are "weakened", and they make the same attack: the source's own linear, off-shell channel, with Redi–Tesi coefficient k ≈ 0.034, dominates the near-source pair total. **The size clause of C4 is conceded.** Below I take each point in turn and say what is kept.

### 1. "Another channel dominates": k_lin = 0.034 against C5's threshold of 3e-4 (C4-0, C4-1, C4-2). Conceded.

Three refuters computed the same coefficient by three routes:
- C4-0: Redi–Tesi eq. 6 integrated over the time-like ball, with a Gauss–Legendre cross-check against R–T eqs. 39/50 that agrees within a factor of 1.9.
- C4-1: R–T eq. 6 with c_J = 16, and independently the peer-reviewed Han–Lykken–Zhang width Gamma(spin-2 -> gamma gamma) = G m^3/10 fed into a spectral integral. Both give 8 pi/5, so k = 67/(630 pi).
- C4-2: the toolkit Born vertex, normalised so that the same T~ reproduces the quadrupole L_GW exactly (ratio 1.000000).

The agreement is exact between C4-1 and C4-2 (0.03385) and within a factor of 2 for C4-0 (0.017–0.068, an unresolved normalisation factor of 2).

I re-ran the arithmetic [calc: runs/2026-10-13-gravitational-wave-decay-photon-pairs/calc/crux-C4_channels.py]. The results are in SI and are pair numbers:
- G E w/c^5 = 9.287e-3. This matches M-EXAMINER-09's 9.27e-3.
- N_lin = 3.144e-4 pairs against P_BO = eq. 8 x T_eff = 2.928e-6 pairs, a ratio of 107.
- With the LVK value E = 3 Msun c^2: 4.716e-4 against 6.588e-6 pairs, a ratio of 72.

Fusion would have to exceed B&O's number by a factor of 72–107 just to equal the linear channel. Nothing in the slate points that way: C3's toys point the other way (0.02–0.1).

I have no rebuttal. The only escape would be strong-field suppression of the linear channel by more than 1e-2 at the merger, while the O(G^4) fusion term stays intact. All three refuters flag their merger k as uncertain by an O(1) factor (wa ~ 2v ~ 1). None of them, and nothing in the dossier, gives a mechanism for a 1e-2 suppression.

The inspiral cases are weak-field (k a ≈ 0.32 for GW170817 at 100 Hz, per C4-1), and there the ratio is about 1e3 (C4-1, C4-2) to about 1e6 per bin (C4-0). These are robust. So the clause "of the order of B&O's per-packet total" is false for the total. That also concedes the C5 sub-hypothesis (a) that C4's own "if false" line named.

### 2. The inspiral objection (mechanist F10a, M-MECHANIST-10) does not protect C4 (C4-0, C4-2). Conceded.

C4-2's argument is decisive. The same T~(w, k), which is polynomial in k, gives L_GW on the light cone and is non-zero inside it. M-MECHANIST-10 verified only a local wavenumber estimate for one harmonic.

### 3. The scaling predictions are wrong for the total (all three). Conceded with a restriction.

The leading total scales as k (G/c^5) Int w dE_GW (SI). It is linear in E and does not depend on sigma.

C4's laws hold only for the N^2 fusion piece:
- (r_m/l)^2 (w/sigma);
- w Int L_GW^2 dt;
- the Parseval identity (12 pi/25) G^2 w Int L^2 dt / c^10 [M-IDEALIZER-04];
- the chirp over-estimate of B&O's Fig. 2a recipe [M-DIALECTICIAN-04/05/06].

All of these remain verified statements about B&O's model. They are no longer statements about the physical total.

### 4. The fusion piece alone is not established at "order B&O" (C4-0, C4-1). Partly conceded; this is now C4's remaining claim.

M-EXAMINER-03 (verified scalar toy) gives retarded/time-symmetric whole-space ratios of 1/12 for a contact vertex and 0.020 for |M|^2 ∝ s^2. C4's case for an O(1) ratio rested on two things:
- the r < c/w share, which no toy includes (examiner F7; idealizer F6, F8);
- the idealizer's standing-wave proxy reproducing B&O to O(1) (R(>1) ≈ 1.1) [M-IDEALIZER-07/08].

That proxy is time-symmetric, so it supports C3's reading of B&O's state as much as it supports C4. I concede that the fusion share is unsettled between 0.02 and 3 of B&O (C4-1's own range). C4 is now a minority position within that range: it holds the upper end, 0.3–3.

### 5. The h^(1)–h^(2) interference "would itself exceed the pure fusion term by ~10" (C4-2, flagged by its author as uncomputed inference). Partly rebutted.

C4-1 states the opposite: there is no interference in the narrow-band limit, because the linear channel sits at q0 ≈ w and fusion at q0 ≈ 2w.

I bounded the interference by Cauchy–Schwarz on the q0 overlap. The model assumes:
- a Gaussian band for h^(1) (centre w, power width sigma);
- an h^(1)h^(1) piece at centre 2w with width sqrt(2) sigma.

The results [calc: runs/2026-10-13-gravitational-wave-decay-photon-pairs/calc/crux-C4_channels.py]:
- At GW150914's sigma/w = 0.21, the normalised amplitude overlap is O = 0.153. That bounds interference/fusion at <= 2 sqrt(N_lin/P_fus) O = 2.6–3.2, not ~10.
- At sigma/w = 0.1 the overlap is O = 2.3e-4, so the interference is negligible.

So at the merger the interference term is at most of the order of the fusion term, and in narrow-band inspiral bins it is negligible. Two caveats keep this a bound, not a value:
- it ignores the spatial-momentum and tensor structure, which can only reduce it further;
- a real merger spectrum has non-Gaussian tails, and the low-q0 (memory) part of h^(2) was not treated.

Either way, all three pieces lie below N_lin by more than 10.

### 6. What all three verdicts leave standing. Kept.

- **Finiteness and near-source localisation.**
  - Linear channel: support is set by the source's T~(K), within |k| < w.
  - Fusion: the outgoing share beyond kr ~ 1 falls as (kr)^-3 [M-IDEALIZER-08, M-EXAMINER-04, M-CONSTRAINTS-08].
- **Distance independence.** There is no rate sustained along the path, against C1.
- **Unobservability, now with the dominant channel included.**
  - About 3e-4 to 5e-4 pairs per GW150914-like event.
  - C4-1's cap gives at most about k ≈ 0.03 pairs per source.
  - The photons sit at Hz–kHz, below the plasma cutoff wherever n_e > 2.8e-4 cm^-3 [D-62].
  - The CMB foreground is about 2e43 times brighter, even at B&O's face-value flux [D-46].

### Revised claim (C4')

A real source makes a finite, distance-independent photon-pair total near itself. That total is dominated by the source's linear off-shell channel, N ≈ 0.034 G w E_GW/c^5 (SI): about 3e-4 pairs for GW150914, which is the C5(a) channel, now computed. B&O's eq. 8 x T_eff describes only the N^2 fusion sub-piece. C4' holds that this sub-piece is O(1) of B&O's number (0.3–3) once the r < c/w region is included. The total is unobservable either way.

## Deciding observation

No observation separates any of these candidates. Every channel's yield lies at least 1e30 below detectability [empiricist F10; D-45, D-46, D-47], and no instrument receives free Hz–kHz photons from outside the solar system [D-70]. Every decider below is therefore a calculation. None is published as of 2026-10-13 [D-66; dossier section 6].

### vs C5 (dominant channel). C4 has effectively lost this one.

- **Calculation:** feed numerical-relativity h_mn of a GW150914-like merger (h^(1) plus h^(2)) into the Redi–Tesi functional with c_J = 16 [D-60], split by q0 band (q0 ≈ w for linear, ≈ 2w for fusion). As C4-1 notes, this was C4's own decisive test, and it measures mainly the linear channel.
- **Outcomes:**
  - Original C4 needs a total <= 3 x P_BO ≈ 1e-5 pairs.
  - C5 and the three verdicts predict about 3e-4 pairs (k ≈ 0.034 within O(1)).
  - A result above 3e-5 pairs (k > 3e-3) decides for C5.
- **Precision:** a factor of 3 suffices.
- **Cheaper decider already done:** for the weak-field GW170817 bin, the quadrupole k is reliable and gives N_lin/P_BO ≈ 1e3 or more (C4-1, C4-2). That favours C5.
- **Who could do it:** NR groups, or Redi & Tesi, who already map T_S to the produced number (their eq. 34/39, quoted in C4-0).

### vs C3 (size of the fusion piece). This is C4's live crux.

- **Calculation:** a spin-2 in-in calculation at O(G^2) of the q0 ≈ 2w (N^2) pair number, for the retarded field of a quadrupole source with the source in the Hamiltonian. It must include r < c/w, because that is where about half the fusion yield is made (C4-0, citing M-EXAMINER-04/08). The calculation gives the ratio rho = P_fus,ret / (eq. 8 x T_eff).
- **Outcomes:**
  - C3 predicts rho = 0.02–0.1.
  - C4' predicts rho = 0.3–3.
  - rho < 0.15 favours C3; rho > 0.3 favours C4'.
- **Precision:** a factor of about 2 at the boundary.
- **Cheap precursor:** evaluate B&O's eq. 5 field at t = -10/sigma, r = 10 c/sigma. An incoming shell confirms C3's time-symmetric state identity and strongly favours C3.
- **Who could do it:** coherent-state waveform groups [D-02, D-03], or B&O themselves.

### vs C2 (no vacuum decay)

Compatible on everything C4' still asserts. C2's scope line does not deny near-source production, and both predict a convergent yield beyond R.

- **Calculation:** the same spin-2 in-in calculation, run for the slope d ln P(>R)/d ln R.
- **Outcomes:** both predict about -3, and at least below -1. A slope of +1 at R >> lambda would refute both, in favour of C1.
- **What would separate them:** only C2's named strong variant ("identically zero"). A non-zero near-source total, which the linear channel already supplies through the peer-reviewed HLZ coefficient (C4-1), decides against that variant and for C4'.

### vs C6 (ensembles)

These address different configurations, so there is no conflict on the headline.

- **Shared hinge:** whether a coherent multi-axis graviton state yields the kinetic (incoherent) overlap integral.
- **Calculation:** verify the unverified coherent/kinetic ratios 0.982 and 0.931 [M-IDEALIZER-14] analytically.
- **Outcomes:**
  - Ratios of about 1 support both C6's "coherence adds nothing" and C4's use of a kinetic-like per-packet fusion total.
  - A ratio far from 1 (by more than a factor of 3) would break C6's central claim and C4's O(1) transfer at the same time.

### vs C7 (classical h^(2) origin)

Compatible. Both channels are hbar-free in pair number: N_lin = k G w E/c^5 and P = (4/25) G^2 N^2 w^3 sigma (natural units) contain no hbar once N = E/(hbar w).

- **Calculation:** C7's own test. Compute the Redi–Tesi Maxwell yield on the classical O(h^2) metric for two crossing GW beams, and compare it with the kinetic two-beam rate w^4 h^4 (1 - cos theta)^2/(1280 pi c^3) (SI) [M-MECHANIST-03].
- **Outcomes:**
  - Equality within O(1) supports C7 and makes C4's fusion piece a semiclassical |W[h^(2)]|^2 quantity.
  - A leading-order mismatch would favour a genuinely quantum coherent-state effect. That would undercut C7, and leave C4' unaffected, since it does not depend on the origin.
- **Who could do it:** any refuter. It is a tree-level calculation.
