## Response to the verdicts

All three verdicts return "survives" with no narrowing. Each asks for corrections to qualifiers, and I take almost all of them. C6 restated with those concessions:

> Sustained vacuum fusion, meaning a production rate per volume that does not dilute away, needs non-collinear momentum content that does not dilute geometrically: an isotropic or multi-directional ensemble, or two waves crossing at a finite angle. For an isotropic, unpolarised, random-phase background, B&O's eq. 14 equals the incoherent kinetic rate (8 pi/15) G^2 rho_GW^2 (natural units). The value is exactly equal; the method is not. The rate is negligible at every T << M_Pl, and nothing grows collectively in any allowed background.

### Verdict C6-0 (physics)
1. **"Exactly" only for an unpolarised bath.** Conceded. A bath with net circular polarisation chi gives eq. 14 x (1 - chi^2), because only opposite-helicity pairs fuse [C6-0 A3]. That is 0.75 at chi = 0.5 and 0 at chi = 1. The claim now reads "isotropic, unpolarised". The factor is at most 1, so "negligibly small" is unaffected.
2. **"Coherence adds nothing" rested on the unverified ratios 0.982 and 0.931** [M-IDEALIZER-14]. Conceded as a support. I replace it with the verdict's structural argument:
   - in a random-phase ensemble, cross terms between distinct mode pairs average to zero;
   - coherent and chaotic statistics differ only for same-mode pairs, and those are exactly collinear, where the amplitude vanishes [D-13, D-55].
   This is an argument, not a computed ratio. Verdict C6-1 limits it further (Sawyer; see below).
3. **"Negligibly small" needs a domain.** Conceded: it holds today at every frequency (≤ 1.1e-66 of the GW energy converted per Hubble time, even at the Planck frequency) and for T ≲ 1e16 GeV (≤ 3.7e-12 per e-fold) [C6-0 B2, C2]. It fails only at T ≳ 0.1 M_Pl, outside the tree-level EFT.
4. **"Multi-directional ensembles" should not imply that randomness is needed.** Conceded, and the clause is broadened. Two coherent waves from two sources, crossing at a finite angle, also fuse throughout their overlap at the two-beam kinetic rate (SI) [M-MECHANIST-03]:

   w^4 h^4 (1 - cos theta)^2 / (1280 pi c^3)

   What the claim needs is non-collinear momentum content that does not dilute geometrically, not stochasticity. This makes C6 sharper against C2's strong variant (see the deciding observations).
5. **The "only" clause** was strengthened by the verdict, not refuted. For a single source the local kinetic yield beyond R falls at least as fast as R^-1, even with a fixed angular spread, from rho ∝ r^-2 alone. It falls as R^-3 for theta ∝ r^-1/2 and R^-5 for theta ∝ r^-1 [C6-0 D1]. I accept the verdict's caveat: this holds within a local kinetic description. A non-local coherent effect of a single wave is excluded here only by geometric dilution, not by a spin-2 in-in calculation.
6. **The ~1e-110 m^-3 s^-1 figure** corresponds to DeltaN_eff ≈ 0.1: 3.08e-110 pairs m^-3 s^-1 (SI) [C6-0 B1; D-71d]. The bandwidth caveat [D-44] stands.

### Verdict C6-1 (evidence)
1. **Sawyer's collective channel [D-06, D-68]** is the strongest published attack on "independent of phase coherence" and "scales as rho_GW^2".
   - **Conceded:** both statements are conditional, not unconditional. The collective Bose-stimulated growth rate is mu = 16 pi G n (natural units). It scales as G n, not G^2 n^2, and could in principle dominate in a dense cloud.
   - **Rebutted for every allowed background.**
     - I re-derived the verdict's identity independently: mu/H = 6 (rho_GW/rho_tot)(H/omega) [calc log line 2].
     - With H/omega ≤ 1 after horizon entry, the exponent accumulated over the radiation era is ≲ 6 x (rho_GW/rho_tot) ≈ 0.24 at DeltaN_eff = 0.3. Sawyer's break needs ~ln N ≈ 210–250 [C6-1 C].
     - Today mu/H0 ≤ 8e-14 [C6-1 B].
     - In the IGM the plasma detuning at 25 Hz, 2.0 rad/s, exceeds mu = 6.8e-43 s^-1 by ~42 orders of magnitude.
   - **Restated prediction:** the rate scales as rho_GW^2 and does not depend on phase coherence *while* mu/H << 1 and mu is below the medium dephasing rate. Every observationally allowed background meets both conditions.
2. **"The cross sections are not re-derived outside the toolkit."** This gap is closed, and I withdraw the line from "evidence against". C6-1 crossed the published unpolarised Compton cross section [D-01] s <-> t. That gives sigma_unpol = (2 pi/5) G^2 s and Gamma/V = (8 pi/15) G^2 rho^2 (natural units). Sawyer's printed amplitude magnitudes agree.
3. **"Three independent derivations agree to 1e-8"** shared one input. Conceded. The crossing route [C6-1 D1–D4] and C6-0's independent quadrature (4 pi/5 to 1e-10) [C6-0 A1] are the derivations that do not share it.
4. **Eq. 14 is incoherent by B&O's own construction.** C6-1 found that B&O's S8 density matrix is a P-representation whose weight depends only on |alpha|, i.e. it is phase-randomised. That supports C6's identification directly from the source text.
5. **Open, not answered: the squeezed primordial background.** A relic background has <a_k a_-k> ≠ 0. I have no calculation of whether these anomalous correlators change eq. 14 by O(1). The verdict's reason to expect not is that they feed only zero-total-momentum pairs. I do not claim it as shown. Any O(1) change would still leave "negligible" intact by > 60 orders of magnitude.

### Verdict C6-2 (scale)
1. **"Vanishes for a collimated background"** holds only for perfect collimation. Conceded. A beam of spread dth keeps a rate of about (dth^4/4)/(4/3) of the isotropic one: 1.9e-5 at 0.1 rad [C6-2 log 5]. C6-0's cone integral gives 3.1e-5 [C6-0 A4]. The prediction now reads "suppressed as the fourth power of the angular spread, zero only for exact collinearity".
2. **"Negligibly small" for T << M_Pl only.** Conceded (as in C6-0, point 3). The conversion fraction per Hubble time reaches 1 only at T = 0.42–0.89 M_Pl [C6-2], which is the graviton-decoupling regime, outside the EFT.
3. **"Exactly" is true of the value, not the method.** Conceded. B&O reach eq. 14 by substituting V w sigma^2 -> 40 pi^2/9 into eq. 8 [D-25]. The kinetic derivation lands on the same 8 pi/15 identically.
4. **The whole-universe figure** is reproduced: 1.38e-12 pairs per Hubble volume per Hubble time at DeltaN_eff = 0.3 (SI, spontaneous) [calc log line 3]. That is fewer than one pair in the observable universe.

### What the eq. 8 = eq. 14 identity says about single packets (new, bears on C3 and C4)
- Inverting the identity shows that B&O's per-packet rate is exactly the kinetic rate of the packet's N gravitons spread *isotropically* through a volume V_eff = 40 pi^2/(9 w sigma_w^2) (natural units) [calc log line 1].
- For GW150914 (B&O inputs [D-27], SI):
  - V_eff = 3.1e19 m^3;
  - equivalent radius R_eq = 1.96e6 m, which is 1.3 c/sigma_w, ≈ 0.98 lambda, and k R_eq = 6.2.
- So eq. 8 counts the whole packet as an isotropic graviton gas filling about one wavelength-sized ball around the source.
- That matches C3's time-symmetric shell at its focus, where incoming and outgoing momenta cross head-on. A retarded outgoing wave at k r ≈ 6 has nearly radial momenta and a theta^4-suppressed kinetic rate.
- This is a reading of an identity, not a calculation of either field. It takes no side between C3 and C4 by itself. It does mean, though, that C6's kinetic picture predicts a specific, cheap test for that pair (below).

### Remaining honest weaknesses
- Whether an ensemble rate counts as "decay of a GW in vacuum" in the brief's sense is a definitional matter.
- C6's practical content is a negligible number.
- "Sustained" is not "dominant". Per-event near-source yields exceed the sustained ensemble rate per volume once the merger rate density passes a very low threshold (SI) [calc log line 4]:
  - 5.1e-26 Gpc^-3 yr^-1 for GW150914-like events at eq. 8 x T_eff = 2.9e-6 pairs each, against the LVK-limited 1.6e-115 pairs m^-3 s^-1;
  - 8.9e-20 Gpc^-3 yr^-1 even against the DeltaN_eff = 0.3 ceiling.

  The dossier gives no merger-rate value, so I quote none. But even one merger per Hubble volume (1.08e79 m^3) per Hubble time (4.58e17 s) is a rate density of ~1.9e-13 Gpc^-3 yr^-1 (SI; hand arithmetic from C6-2's volume and time). GW150914 alone shows the real rate is at least that, and it is far above both thresholds. So C6 describes the only *sustained* channel, not the main one.

## Deciding observation

No observation can decide between C6 and any rival. The ensemble signal falls short of FIRAS by ≥ 1e71 [M-CONSTRAINTS-03], and PIXIE-class missions would improve on FIRAS by only ~3e3 in mu and ~1e4 in y [D-36]. Every discriminator below is therefore a calculation. None is published as of 2026-10-13 [dossier section 6]. C6 is compatible with C2–C5 and C7 as the slate states, so each test names where C6 and the rival could come apart.

- **vs C2 (null), against its strong variant only.** C6 is the "scope" exception that C2's main form already allows.
  - The test: a tree-level in-in (Schwinger–Keldysh) O(G^2) calculation of the photon number made in the overlap of two coherent, weak, non-collinear plane GW beams in flat vacuum (two sources). The overlap must be held long enough to read a rate per volume.
  - C6 predicts a non-zero rate per overlap 4-volume equal to the kinetic w^4 h^4 (1 - cos theta)^2/(1280 pi c^3) (SI) [M-MECHANIST-03], including the theta^4 limit.
  - C2's strong variant ("identically zero for every vacuum configuration") predicts zero.
  - Precision: any non-zero O(1) result decides against strong C2. Testing C6's "exactly" needs ~10% on the coefficient.
  - Who: any group using the coherent-state or in-in formalisms [D-02, D-03], or Redi–Tesi-type methods [D-60]. It is a cheap calculation.

- **vs C3 (time-symmetric state).**
  - The test: compute the kinetic integral Int (2 pi/5) G^2 T_mn T^mn d^4x (natural units; C6's second route [M-IDEALIZER-05]) over B&O's eq. 5 field and over the retarded field of the same quadrupole source, and compare each with the coherent in-in totals.
  - C6 predicts kinetic = coherent to O(1) for each field ("coherence adds nothing", now extended to single packets).
  - By the identity above, kinetic-on-the-time-symmetric-field should reproduce eq. 8 x T_eff, and kinetic-on-the-retarded-field should be well below it. Combined with C6, that is C3's 0.02–0.1.
  - If the coherent retarded total instead exceeds its kinetic integral by >> 3, C6's "coherence adds nothing" fails beyond ensembles. In that case C3's toy ratios, which are kinetic in character, lose their footing.
  - Precision: a factor ~3 separates the outcomes (C3 0.02–0.1 against C4 0.3–3).
  - Caveat: geometric optics is marginal at k r ~ 6, so the kinetic integral must be done with a Wigner-function, not a ray, stress tensor.

- **vs C4 (O(1) retarded total).**
  - The same pair of calculations decides. C4 and C6 are jointly consistent only if the retarded field's kinetic integral is itself within ~3 of eq. 8 x T_eff (times the 0.3–0.6 pattern factors).
  - If C6's equality holds and the retarded kinetic integral is ≤ 0.1 of eq. 8 x T_eff, C4 loses.
  - If the coherent retarded total is O(1) of eq. 8 x T_eff while the kinetic integral is ≤ 0.1, C4 stands and C6's "coherence adds nothing" is confined to random-phase ensembles.
  - Who: the groups named for C3/C4; C4's NR + Redi–Tesi test [D-60] supplies the coherent side.

- **vs C5 (source's own dynamical field).**
  - The test: apply the Redi–Tesi kernel (c_J = 16) [D-60] to an isotropic random-phase background of free on-shell waves, at O(h) in the amplitude and then at O(h^2) (the induced h^(2)).
  - C6 predicts zero at O(h), because free waves have only null support and the linear channel is absent. It predicts (8 pi/15) G^2 rho^2 at O(h^2).
  - C5's linear channel ∝ N needs off-shell time-like support, which only a source supplies.
  - A non-zero O(h) yield for a free background would mean a linear term ∝ rho_GW in the ensemble rate. That would contradict C6's rho^2 law and extend C5's mechanism to free waves.
  - Separately, C5's k (its own test) decides which channel dominates *per event*. C6 already concedes that per-event channels dominate cosmically above a merger rate of ~1.6e-29/k Gpc^-3 yr^-1 (SI) [calc log line 4].

- **vs C7 (classical h^(2) nonlinearity).**
  - The test: C7's own test, the Maxwell pair yield from the D-60 functional on the classical O(h^2) metric of two crossing beams, or of an isotropic random-phase background. Compare it with the kinetic two-beam rate and with 8 pi/15.
  - Equal to O(1): C6 and C7 both stand. C7 then supplies the classical reading and C6 the ensemble rate law; both are hbar-free in pair number, since (8 pi/15) G^2 rho_E^2/c^7 carries no hbar.
  - Unequal at leading order: C7 falls. C6 stands only if the full coherent-state (quantum) in-in calculation of the vs-C2 test still gives the kinetic rate.
  - Precision: ~10% on the coefficient; it is a cheap tree-level calculation.
