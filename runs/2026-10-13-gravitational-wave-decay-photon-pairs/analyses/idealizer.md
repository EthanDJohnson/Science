## Method applied
Build the most idealized model containing B&O's question (an isotropic, narrow-band coherent graviton shell with real-sign amplitudes, exactly B&O's S6 set-up), solve it in closed form, express the yield as a scaling law, then relax the idealizations one at a time (helicity phases, profile, outgoing vs time-symmetric field, incoherent bath, plasma, stimulation).

F3. **The invariant content of eq. 8 is a total, set by int L^2 dt.** For any narrow-band spectral profile, P = (3/(200 pi^4)) G^2 w^5 int C_f(K0)^2 dK0, and by Parseval this equals P = (12 pi/25) G^2 w int L_GW(t)^2 dt / c^10 (SI; L_GW the packet's luminosity profile), verified numerically to 1e-6 for top-hat and Gaussian profiles. F4. **Eq. 14 is the exact kinetic rate of an incoherent isotropic graviton bath, for any spectrum.** With the physical (sum-of-squares) cross section sigma(R L -> gamma gamma) = (4 pi/5) G^2 s (agrees with qft_second_order.sigma_total to 1e-15; same-helicity = 0), unpolarized average (2 pi/5) G^2 s, s = 2 w1 w2 (1 - cos), v_rel = 1 - cos, isotropic <(1-cos)^2> = 4/3: Gamma = (1/2) <n n sigma v> = (8 pi/15) G^2 rho^2 (natural units), = (8 pi/15) G^2 rho_E^2/c^7 in SI, hbar-free and independent of the spectral shape (checked with a two-line spectrum). So B&O's eq. 14 is right arithmetic AND the right quantity for a genuinely isotropic background (unlike eq. 8, it needs no real-sign coherence) [calc: lens-idealizer_coeff.py, Part 4]. Consistent with [D-25], [D-44].
F5. **A local rate density, and the plane-wave no-go, in one line.** Since T^{mu nu} = sum_i n_i k_i^mu k_i^nu / w_i for a gas of on-shell gravitons, T_{mu nu}T^{mu nu} = sum_ij n_i n_j w_i w_j (1 - cos_ij)^2, which is exactly the kinetic integrand. Hence, in geometric optics, Gamma(x) = (2 pi/5) G^2 T_{mu nu}T^{mu nu} (pairs per volume per time, natural units; reduces to eq. 14 for an isotropic bath). For a single plane wave T is null, T_{mu nu}T^{mu nu} = 0, so the rate vanishes identically: the collinear no-go [D-55] in idealized form. Any pair production needs a non-null (non-collinear) local stress, i.e. crossing or curved wavefronts [calc: runs/2026-10-13-gravitational-wave-decay-photon-pairs/calc/lens-idealizer_outgoing.py, docstring; Part 4 of lens-idealizer_coeff.py for the isotropic limit].
F6. **Where the yield is made: time-symmetric vs outgoing field (scalar multipole toy, l = 2).** Using Gamma = (2 pi/5) G^2 <T><T> with the exact cycle-averaged stress of (i) an outgoing wave h_2(kr)Y_2m and (ii) a standing wave j_2(kr)Y_22 (the time-symmetric, in + out, analogue of B&O's Pauli-Jordan field), and normalising to the same outgoing luminosity L: define f(x) = r^4 int dOmega TT / L^2, x = kr. (i) Outgoing wave: f ~ 0.25 x^-2.02 (m = 2) and 0.51 x^-2.10 (m = 0) at x >> 1, so the cumulative yield beyond r falls as (kr)^-3: R(>x0) = P(>r)/P_B&O = 0.069, 2.7e-4, 6e-8, 5e-14, 5e-19 at kr = 1, 2 pi, 100, 1e4, 4.7e5 (the last is 1 AU at 150 Hz). F10. **The resonant ULDM channel (eq. 17) needs head-on gravitons, which an outgoing wave does not contain.** Two gravitons with w1 + w2 <= 2m(1 + v^2/2) must have s = 2 w1 w2 (1 - cos theta) >= 4 m^2; with w1 w2 <= m^2 (1 + v^2/2)^2 this forces cos theta <= -1 + O(v^2), i.e. theta within ~2v of pi (2e-3 rad for v = 1e-3 c). An outgoing wave at kr >> 1 has local angular spread << 1 rad (F6), so its resonant yield is kinematically zero, not merely suppressed; head-on pairs exist only in a time-symmetric (incoming x outgoing) field like B&O's in-state, or in the source zone. The non-resonant scalar channel also gets no Bose factor there: final momenta ~ w >> m v lie outside the occupied ULDM phase space (analytic, this lens). Bears on [D-26], [D-50], [D-67], hidden premise 9.

F11. **Plasma closes the channel kinematically wherever w < w_p.** With a photon plasma mass m_gamma = w_p, g g -> gamma gamma needs s >= 4 w_p^2, but s <= (w1 + w2)^2 ~ 4 w^2; so for w < w_p the process is forbidden outright, not merely the photons absorbed. At 150 Hz this holds in the warm ISM (1.6 kHz), the VLISM, the solar wind, and in the fully ionised early universe at 1+z > ~1300 for photons of 150 Hz today [D-40], [D-62]; it is open in today's mean IGM (4.2 Hz). So every CMB-era (mu, y, BBN) use of the channel at astrophysical GW frequencies is shut by kinematics, before any rate is computed (analytic, this lens).
F12. **The binning premise (hidden premise 6).** Because the invariant yield is local in time, P = (12 pi/25) G^2 int w L^2 dt / c^10 (F3), splitting a slowly chirping signal into bins and summing the bins reproduces the same integral up to edge terms of relative order (bin duration x sigma)^-1. Binning is therefore neither a meaningful lower bound nor an upper bound; it is the same total, and the total is dominated by the last cycles (F8). Medium confidence: the local-in-time form is derived for a narrow band and extended to a slow chirp by a stationary-phase argument, not computed.

### Where reality departs from the model most
The time-symmetric field (I4). B&O's in-state contains an incoming half that converges onto the source region and meets the outgoing half head-on; that overlap (f -> const in F6) is what produces essentially all of the eq. 8 yield, and the kinetic proxy reproduces eq. 8's total to within 1% when the in x out overlap is used. A real source emits only the outgoing half. The departure hurts B&O decisively: the production that remains is confined to r <~ 1/w, which for a merger is the merger itself, and falls as (kr)^-3 (or faster) beyond. The second-largest departure, the real-sign sum (I1), is only a factor 2-3. All other relaxations change sizes that are already 1e-6 pairs per event or 1e-108 of a background per Hubble time.

## What would change my mind
- A coherent in-in (Schwinger-Keldysh) calculation with the retarded field of a compact source that shows a pair rate persisting at kr >> 1 (radial density falling slower than r^-3): that would revive a path-integrated effect, though at the sizes in F8-F9 still unobservable.
- A demonstration that B&O's S|0> in-state carries a purely outgoing field (no converging part); my F6 attribution of eq. 8 to the in x out overlap would then fail and the agreement of the standing-wave proxy (R = 1.008) would be a coincidence.
- A correct phased amplitude that makes the physical-pattern coefficient exceed the real-sign value (F7 says 0.2-0.6x).
- Evidence that the near-zone strong-field region enhances production by many orders of magnitude (it would change B's size, not the "no vacuum decay" verdict).
