# Analysis: mechanist (Aristotle: causes and mechanisms)
status: final

## Method applied
For each candidate mechanism by which a gravitational wave (GW) could put energy into photon (or scalar) pairs, I write the causal chain (producing process, what it acts on, what sustains it, what ends it), classify it by phenomenon and governing theory, evidence each link with [D-..] or [calc], and find the weakest link. Owned calculation: helicity amplitudes, their phases, and the physical coherent-state coefficient for realistic polarisation patterns.

## Findings
1. **Helicity amplitudes reproduced, with phases (natural units, CM frame, s = 4, unit 8 pi G s).** The toolkit's full Einstein-Maxwell tree amplitude (contact + t/u photon exchange + s-channel second-order metric) gives non-zero g g -> gamma gamma only for OPPOSITE physical helicities of the two gravitons (each helicity w.r.t. its own momentum). (+2,-2) -> photons (+1,-1) has |M| = 8 pi G u^2/s and -> (-1,+1) has |M| = 8 pi G t^2/s; (-2,+2) is the mirror. Same physical helicity (+2,+2) or (-2,-2) gives zero, numerically 5e-31 of the non-zero ones (a selection rule). The sum over final helicities equals (t^4 + u^4)/s^2 x (8 pi G)^2 to 1.9e-15 relative, matching [D-01] (Bjerrum-Bohr/Skobelev crossing) and B&O eq. 6 magnitudes. The phase is e^{i(pi + 4 phi)} for the (+2,-2) channel and its conjugate for (-2,+2), with phi the photon azimuth, which is what J_z = +-4 along the collision axis requires. [calc: runs/2026-10-13-gravitational-wave-decay-photon-pairs/calc/lens-mechanist_helicity.py]
2. **What B&O eq. 7 does.** The t^2 and u^2 amplitudes of ONE graviton helicity pair go to orthogonal final photon states, so they cannot interfere. They do interfere ACROSS initial helicity pairs: (+2,-2) -> (+1,-1) [u^2] and (-2,+2) -> (+1,-1) [t^2] land in the same final state. For two linearly polarised gravitons the cross term is +-2 t^2 u^2 x cos(4 chi), chi the relative orientation of their 'plus' axes; it does not depend on photon azimuth. Aligned polarisations (chi = 0, pi/2) reproduce B&O's real-sign value exactly: sum_final |M|^2/(8 pi G s)^2 = 0.18173 at theta = 1.1 rad, equal to (t^2+u^2)^2/(2 s^4). chi = pi/4 gives 0.10287, and the chi average gives 0.14230 = (t^4+u^4)/(2 s^4). Integrated over the CM photon angle, B&O's equal-sign square (14/15) exceeds the incoherent sum (4/5) by 7/6, and the anti-aligned case gives 2/3, i.e. 5/7 of B&O. So eq. 7 is not a double count: among states with |c_R| = |c_L| = 1/sqrt2 it is the maximal-constructive-interference case (realised by aligned linear polarisations), 7/6 above the polarisation-averaged value and 7/5 above the minimum. It is not an upper bound in general: a pure (+2,-2) collision (opposite circular, e.g. two gravitons leaving a circular binary along +z and -z) gives (t^4+u^4)/s^2, integrated 4/5, against B&O's 7/15 per collision, i.e. 12/7 times more; a pure (+2,+2) collision gives zero. The per-collision factor therefore ranges over [0, 12/7] x B&O depending on the helicity content of the two colliding directions. Hidden premise 2: a phase convention making all terms real and positive exists only for particular polarisation geometries; it is not a property of an "unpolarized" state. Mechanism: s = 2 w^2 (1 - cos theta) ~ w^2 theta^2, sigma ~ G^2 s, flux factor (1 - cos theta) ~ theta^2. This is the quantitative form of the collinear no-go [D-55] and of the plane-wave results [D-56] [D-57] [D-60]: a locally plane wave has nothing to fuse with. 7. **Which field the state carries (standard QFT, my inference from [D-10] [D-71a]).** B&O's eq. 5 field is the source convolved with the Pauli-Jordan (commutator) function Delta = Delta_ret - Delta_adv. That is a SOURCE-FREE solution: for t after the source it equals the retarded outgoing wave, and extended to t before the source it is minus the advanced field, a wave converging from past null infinity. Used as an in-state and evolved freely, S|0> is therefore a shell that converges onto the source position, focuses there to size ~ lambda, and re-expands. This is exactly the picture the toolkit attaches to its momentum-space profile (tool docstring, "time-symmetric field"). The non-collinear graviton pairs whose fusion makes eq. 8 overlap only in that focus. For a real source the focus region is replaced by the source itself (the binary, merging horizons, near zone r <~ lambda/2 pi), where the free-graviton model is not the right description. Hidden premise 1 fails, and this is the link that turns a converging-and-re-expanding wave into a "decaying" outgoing one. [D-10] [D-02] [D-03] [D-71a]; Pauli-Jordan decomposition is textbook (assumed, not quoted).
8. **Massive final states make it worse (ULDM, eq. 17).** Pair production of a scalar of mass m needs s = 4 w^2 sin^2(theta/2) > 4 m^2. In B&O's resonant window m <= w_s <~ m(1 + v^2/2) [D-26] this requires sin(theta/2) > m/w >~ 1 - v^2/2, i.e. NEARLY HEAD-ON gravitons (pi - theta <~ 2v ~ 2e-3 rad for v = 1e-3 c). An outgoing wave at r >> lambda has a direction spread theta(r) << 1 (finding 4), so the channel is kinematically closed beyond r ~ a few lambda. The Bose factor N_phi multiplies an amplitude that is zero there. Inference from standard kinematics [D-55].
9. **The N^2 factor is classical-field nonlinearity times a quantum vacuum response.** The amplitude for one pair is linear in the second-order metric h^(2) ~ G (h_c)^2 [D-16]. N^2 = |alpha|^4 is just (h_c)^4 in the yield, i.e. the classical field squared twice; no graviton quantum is involved. The pair itself comes from the photon vacuum (classically F = 0 stays F = 0 [D-17] [D-54]). The stimulated part (1 + 2 f_gamma) is hbar-free and is classical parametric scattering of the ambient (CMB) field by h^(2) (B&O's own statement, [D-54]). So the process is "photon vacuum response to the second-order classical metric": QFT in a fixed background, which puts it under the Redi-Tesi rule [D-60] applied to h^(2). Pairs need time-like Fourier support of h^(2). For an outgoing wave the oscillating (2w) part of h^(2) has 4-momentum k1 + k2 with q^2 = 2 w^2 (1 - cos theta_12) -> 0 as the wave becomes locally plane (finding 3). The non-oscillating (a-dagger a, k1 - k2) part has q^2 <= 0 and makes no pairs. [D-16] [D-17] [D-54] [D-60]; applying D-60 to h^(2) is my inference, as the dossier notes.
10. **Mundane competitors in the same region.** (a) The source's own dynamical spacetime (merger, ringdown, remnant Hawking emission) produces massless quanta at O(h^2) wherever the metric has time-like Fourier support [D-60] [D-52]. B&O's own eq. 13 puts the remnant's Hawking luminosity 1e2-1e3 times above their W_gamma [D-19] [D-43]. For a quasi-circular inspiral, the near-zone field oscillates at q0 = 2 Omega on spatial scales ~ a, so q0/|q| ~ v < 1 (space-like, no O(h^2) massless production); only merger and ringdown (v ~ c) have time-like support. This is my inference. (b) In a medium, GW energy goes to EM through background magnetic fields (Gertsenshtein, 1 -> 1 [D-05]) and to matter through viscous/collisionless absorption. These are linear in h, need no vacuum non-collinearity, and are the real "loss channels" of a GW, still negligible (assumed; no dossier size). (c) A plasma gives photons a mass w_p [D-40]. That closes vacuum-like g g -> gamma gamma below threshold where 2 w < 2 w_p, e.g. a 150 Hz GW in the warm ISM (w_p/2pi ~ 1.6 kHz) [D-62]. 1 -> 2 decay into massive photons stays forbidden, because the graviton is massless.

## What would change my mind
- A retarded (out-state or in-in) calculation showing the pair number beyond radius R grows linearly with R for an outgoing packet, i.e. a non-collinear component that does not decay with r (it would revive M1/MECHANIST-C).
