# Analysis: constraints (Kant: audit the conditions any valid answer must satisfy)
status: final

## Method applied
List every constraint that bears on GW -> gamma gamma in vacuum (kinematics, conservation, plane-wave no-go theorems, quantum-classical counting, plasma dispersion, spectral distortions, EBL, BBN photofission, LVK/PTA/N_eff energy budgets), compute what each B&O consequence requires against it, and classify each candidate as violates / strained / consistent, with the assumptions under which each constraint applies.

## Findings

F0. What B&O's Fig. 3 constrains (source read for this lens). Each source radiates W_gamma from its creation time onward, summed over all sources causally connected: [new: B&O v2 arXiv PDF p. 5, full-text, "Summing the energy radiated by all sources causally connected with the observation point, in the limit 1/κ(t− ta)≪1 andH(z)/κ≪1, the total deposited energy density reads (see SM SE for the derivation), ∆ργ(z,ωs) (1 +z) 4 ≈ WγΓ H2 0 F(z,ω),(19) where Γ = ˜nκ"]. Windows: "In the µ-regime, 105 ≲z≲z a≈3×10 6" and "In the range 10 3 ≲z≲z a≈10 5 ... y-distortion"; EBL bounds "for high frequency at low redshift (ω s ≳10 eV, z <zdec)"; photofission uses "requiringδ D <0.03" [new: same PDF pp. 5, 13, full-text]. No plasma correction appears in these passages (consistent with [D-62]).

F1. Plasma kinematics is a hard threshold, not just a propagation loss. A transverse photon in a cold plasma has effective mass hbar w_p, so g g -> gamma gamma needs s >= 4 w_p^2; two gravitons of frequency w have s <= 4 w^2, so production needs w > w_p (and theta >= 2 arcsin(w_p/w)). For the GW150914 band (35-250 Hz) production is kinematically FORBIDDEN in hot ISM (f_pe = 492 Hz at an assumed 3e-3 cm^-3), warm ISM (1.56 kHz) and the VLISM (2.54 kHz), and allowed in the mean IGM (f_pe = 4.2 Hz all-baryon, 1.5 Hz realistic) only for theta > 3.2 deg (150 Hz) [calc: runs/2026-10-13-gravitational-wave-decay-photon-pairs/calc/lens-constraints_plasma.py] [D-07] [D-40]. Combined with the far-zone invariant mass s ~ 4 w c/r (F4), production in the IGM stops altogether beyond r_max = c w/w_p^2 = 4.0e8 m (~200 lambda) at 150 Hz (3.2e9 m for the realistic IGM density) [same calc]. So "decay along the path" of a merger-band GW is kinematically zero in every real medium beyond ~1e9 m, independent of B&O's rate.

F2. Cosmic plasma voids the low-frequency end of the CMB windows. Minimum physical photon energy for production, hbar w_p(z) (fully ionised before recombination): 6.4e-10 eV at z = 1100, 1.7e-8 eV at z = 1e4, 5.5e-7 eV at z = 1e5, 9.1e-5 eV at z = 3e6 [calc: lens-constraints_plasma.py]. B&O's y window (1e3 < z < 1e5) therefore needs hbar w_s > 6.4e-10 to 5.5e-7 eV, and the mu window (1e5 < z < 3e6) hbar w_s > 5.5e-7 to 9.1e-5 eV; Fig. 3 starts at 1e-10 eV. Any part of the mu/y curves below these energies sits where the process is kinematically forbidden. For a fixed observed frequency f0 (primordial SGWB), production before recombination needs f0 > 4.2 Hz sqrt(1+z): f0 > 1.3 kHz at z = 1e5, > 6.0 kHz at z = 2e6; the LVK band (25 Hz) makes no photons at any z > 1100 [same calc]. Assumes cold unmagnetised plasma, all baryons ionised, helium recombination ignored (<~8% in n_e).

F3. Eq. 14 is exactly kinetic theory, so for an incoherent background it is the right quantity, and it is negligible. With qft_second_order's physical cross sections, sigma(R,L) = (4/5) pi G^2 s, sigma(R,R) = 0 (1.5e-31 roundoff), linear pairs (2/5) pi G^2 s; the isotropic Boltzmann average (1/2) n^2 <sigma (1 - cos)> gives Gamma/(G^2 rho^2) = 1.675516 at Gauss-Legendre n = 8, 12, 16, equal to 8 pi/15 to 6 digits [calc: runs/2026-10-13-gravitational-wave-decay-photon-pairs/calc/lens-constraints_eq14.py]. SI: pair rate 2.8e-109, 3.1e-110, 7.7e-111 m^-3 s^-1 for DeltaN_eff = 0.3, 0.1, 0.05 (rho_GW = 2.8e-15, 9.5e-16, 4.7e-16 J/m^3); LVK-limited 1.6e-115 m^-3 s^-1 [same calc] (agrees with [D-44]). Cosmic injection at DeltaN_eff = 0.3, plasma ignored (generous): Delta rho_gamma/rho_gamma <= 4.3e-79 (mu window, stimulated) and 1.6e-83 (y window), for any f0 from 25 Hz to 1e9 Hz; 3.8e-76 at f0 = 1e14 Hz; vs FIRAS ~6e-5 [D-08] the shortfall is >= 1e71. Verdict for eq. 14: right arithmetic, right quantity (incoherent kinetic rate); no constraint possible.

F4. Far-zone kinematics: production by one outgoing wave is confined to r ~ lambda. Pair production needs time-like total momentum q = k1 + k2 (s = q^2 > 0) [D-55]; an outgoing spherical wave at radius R has Fresnel angular spread theta^2 ~ 1/(w R), so s ~ 4 w c/R. In a toy (monopole kinematics, two-beam rate ~ s^2 as in qft_second_order), the pair yield of the region beyond R falls as Y ~ (w R)^-2.94 (local slopes -2.90, -2.95, -2.975 from w R = 20 to 160; 1.5x grids agree to 1.4e-4) [calc: runs/2026-10-13-gravitational-wave-decay-photon-pairs/calc/lens-constraints_farzone.py]. Expected asymptote -3. For GW150914 (lambda = 2.0e6 m at 150 Hz), the fraction of the near-zone yield made beyond R is (lambda/2 pi R)^3 = 4.0e-3 (R = lambda), 9.5e-18 (1 AU), 1.6e-59 (410 Mpc) [same calc]. So a freely propagating wave does not "keep fusing": the yield is a near-source total, and B&O's r-independent rate (D-14, D-21) cannot be carried along the path. Assumptions: scalar-monopole toy for the kinematics (the helicity structure only changes O(1) factors and the selection rule sigma(R,R) = 0, F3); vacuum (no plasma). This is the far-zone face of the plane-wave no-go (D-56, D-57, D-60: null-like perturbations do not produce particles).

F5. Path depletion is bounded by observation and is consistent with zero. GW150914 at D_L = 410 Mpc [D-30], detected with amplitude calibration <= 11.29% [D-39], requires an energy e-folding length L > 5.3e25 m = 1.7e3 Mpc [calc: lens-constraints_farzone.py]. B&O's face-value depletion over 410 Mpc is 3.3e-66 (vacuum) and 2.5e-57 (CMB-stimulated) [same calc; D-47]: consistent, by 56-65 orders of magnitude, so no observation tests it. The Jones-Singleton decay length <r> ~ 1e11 m [D-53] would give an amplitude factor exp(-6.3e13) at 410 Mpc and is excluded by the detection of GW150914 [same calc]. Assumes the GW150914 amplitude-distance consistency is limited by calibration (distance is not independently measured, so a uniform depletion would be degenerate with D_L; the bound is on an attenuation large enough to make the inferred source parameters inconsistent, a weaker but still ~exp(-1e13)-level exclusion of Jones-Singleton).

F8. hbar counting and the universal near-source cap. With N = E/(hbar w), eq. 8's pair rate is 3 G^2 E^2 w sigma^2/(25 pi c^10), exactly hbar-independent (sympy: d/dhbar = 0); eq. 11 = 2 hbar w x eq. 8 and eq. 12 = (3/(50 pi))(r_m/l)^2 hbar w^2 agree identically; the CMB-stimulated power 12 G^2 E^2 kT w sigma^2/(25 pi c^10) is hbar-free [calc: runs/2026-10-13-gravitational-wave-decay-photon-pairs/calc/lens-constraints_budget.py]. So the unstimulated photon power is a vacuum-seeded (spontaneous) quantum effect, linear in hbar, while the stimulated power is the classical amplification of an existing Rayleigh-Jeans field; there is no classical Einstein-Maxwell emission from F = 0 (consistent with D-17, D-54). Pairs per burst (rate x 1/sigma) = (3/(100 pi)) (r_m/l)^2 (w/sigma) [same calc]. A burst that is not inside its own Schwarzschild radius has r_m <= l (hoop bound, assumption), so a burst makes at most ~0.095 pairs at eps = sigma/w = 0.1, at any frequency and energy, before stimulation [same calc]. GW150914 at B&O inputs: 7.0e-7 pairs per burst (E = 2 Msun c^2), 1.6e-6 (E = 3), r_m/l = 3.9e-3 to 5.9e-3; x CMB occupation 7.6e8 -> 5.3e2 to 1.2e3 pairs [same calc] (reproduces D-42, D-43).

F9. Conversion fraction and the ceiling on any photon-based bound. Expressed with the GW luminosity, B&O's own formula gives W_gamma/L_GW = (6/(25 pi)) (L/L_P) (hbar w/E_P)^2, with L_P = c^5/G = 3.63e52 W and E_P = 1.22e28 eV [calc: lens-constraints_budget.py]. Values: GW150914 3.9e-85 to 5.8e-85; GW170817 at 100 Hz 4.3e-91 (4.9e-82 CMB-stimulated); BBH M_c = 28 Msun at 50 Hz 4.0e-88; galactic WD binary (assumed M_c = 0.3 Msun, 6.2 mHz) 1.6e-115; any merger at peak (L = 1e-3 L_P) at 150 Hz 2.0e-85 [same calc]. A photon-based bound on GW energy (FIRAS Delta rho/rho ~ 6e-5 [D-08]) adds anything beyond the gravitational DeltaN_eff bound (rho_GW/rho_gamma <= 0.068 at DeltaN_eff = 0.3 [D-32, D-33]) only if the conversion exceeds 8.8e-4. Near the source that needs hbar w > 1.3e27 eV = 0.11 E_P even at L = L_P (outside the graviton EFT), and is impossible with Rayleigh-Jeans stimulation (needs hbar w ~ 1e51-1e54 eV >> kT) [same calc]. So no corrected photon bound can beat the gravitational bound on the same GWs anywhere in B&O's Fig. 3 range (1e-10 to 1e10 eV). The N_eff comparison applies to GWs present at z >~ 1e3 (the mu and y windows). For post-recombination (EBL) sources the comparison is cruder but stronger: the near-source conversion at 10 eV is <= 5.1e-59 (L = 1e-3 L_P) or <= 2.6e-56 (hoop-limited) [calc: lens-constraints_budget.py, Part C], so even GWs carrying the whole critical density (7.7e-10 J/m^3 [calc: lens-constraints_eq14.py]) would deposit <= 2e-65 J/m^3, ~5e-52 of the CMB energy density (4.17e-14 J/m^3, same calc).

F10. B&O's eq. 19 overcount and its energy-conservation limit. The ratio of B&O's propagation-integrated photon energy per burst to the near-source total is X = sigma t_prop ~ eps w t_H = 6.6e21 at 1e-10 eV, 6.6e32 at 10 eV, 1.5e38 at 2.2 MeV, 6.6e41 at 1e10 eV (eps = 0.1, t_H = 4.36e17 s) [calc: lens-constraints_budget.py]. Bounds on sqrt(Gamma eps) E_rel (Delta rho proportional to Gamma E_rel^2) are therefore too strong by sqrt(X) ~ 1e11 to 1e21, before the plasma and kinematic cuts. At the top of Fig. 3 the integration also breaks energy conservation: for a maximal-luminosity source (L = 1e-3 L_P) B&O's integrated photon energy exceeds the burst's GW energy (C x X = 34) at 1e10 eV, and B&O's depletion time tau_h = 1/(sigma C) = 0.03 t_H there; for a hoop-limited burst C x X = 17 already at 1e9 eV [same calc]. Eq. 19 contains no depletion term (F6 quote), so any Fig. 3 point in that corner is internally inconsistent, independent of F4. Assumes t_prop ~ t_H; for z ~ 1e5 injection t_prop is shorter and X smaller by the same factor.

F11. Photofission and EBL windows need exotic sources whose own Hawking emission dominates. A binary radiating at the ISCO GW frequency w = 0.136 c^3/(G M) reaches hbar w = 10 eV at M = 3.6e18 kg, 2.2 MeV at 1.6e13 kg, 19.8 MeV at 1.8e12 kg, 1e10 eV at 3.6e9 kg; at 1e10 eV the components evaporate in 1.1e-6 t_H, so no such BH sources exist after BBN [calc: lens-constraints_budget.py]. For BH sources the ratio W_H/W_gamma is mass-independent: 341 (one 65 Msun BH, Page-type estimate) to 2.7e3 (two components), matching B&O's eq. 13 range [D-19, D-43]; the components' Hawking temperature (5.9 eV, 1.3 MeV, 12 MeV, 5.9 GeV respectively) is comparable to hbar w. So a photofission or EBL bound obtained through GW -> gamma gamma from BH binaries is always weaker than the evaporation bound on the same objects by >~ 1e2-1e3, even before X (F10). Non-BH high-frequency sources are hypothetical (D-64).

F12. Free-free absorption closes the IGM window for merger-band photons. Rybicki-Lightman free-free opacity (T = 1e4 K, g_ff = 14.6): absorption length at 150 Hz is 0.57 Mpc (all-baryon mean n_e = 2.2e-7 cm^-3) to 37 Mpc (realistic mean 2.75e-8 cm^-3); at 1 kHz 27-1.8e3 Mpc; hot halo gas (assumed 1e-4 cm^-3, 1e6 K) 2.2 kpc at 150 Hz [calc: lens-constraints_budget.py]. So photons made near GW150914 (410 Mpc) cannot reach the Milky Way even where they propagate (F1), on top of the ISM/solar-wind/ionosphere cutoffs [D-62]. Caveats: cold-plasma opacity without the refractive-index factor (small at f/f_pe ~ 36), uniform density (clumping raises absorption).

F13. ULDM resonant channel (eq. 17-18): kinematics and time-energy limits. Producing two non-relativistic phi (v = 1e-3) needs |k1 + k2| <= 2 m v, i.e. graviton momenta within 2.0e-3 rad of head-on (solid-angle fraction 1e-6); an outgoing wave supplies non-collinear pairs only within r <~ hbar c/m = lambda/2pi (2e14 m at m = 1e-21 eV, 2e15 m at 1e-22 eV) [calc: lens-constraints_budget.py]. The resonance width sigma = m v^2/2 needs 1/sigma = 1.3e12 s (1e-21 eV) to 1.3e13 s (1e-22 eV) to resolve, but a binary whose ISCO GW frequency sits on resonance (1.8e10 or 1.8e11 Msun) sweeps the band in 12-120 s: shortfall 1.1e11 [same calc]. Eq. 17's resonant coefficient therefore cannot apply to a chirping source; energy conservation is not at issue (W_phi ~ 8.6e13 W x x_MW [D-50] vs L_GW ~ 3.6e49 W). Assumes the quadrupole chirp rate at ISCO (order-of-magnitude there) and B&O's own sigma_w ~ m v^2/2 [D-26].

F14. A single plane wave carries only null momentum (gr_tensors). For the exact vacuum plane wave ds^2 = -dt^2 + dx^2 + dy^2 + dz^2 + H (dt - dz)^2 with H = A(u)(x^2 - y^2) + 2B(u)xy, the Einstein tensor vanishes and the Kretschmann scalar is 0 (64 nonzero Riemann components, R^x_txt = -A(t - z)): curvature without any scalar invariant [calc: runs/2026-10-13-gravitational-wave-decay-photon-pairs/calc/lens-constraints_ppwave.py]. The null-dust plane wave H = F(u)(x^2 + y^2), the exact analogue of the Isaacson effective stress tensor of a GW beam, has G_ab proportional to F k_a k_b with trace 0 and G_ab G^ab = 0; scan_energy_conditions over 32 points finds no NEC/WEC/SEC/DEC violation for the positive-energy sign (F < 0 in this convention; minima 1.5e-8, 4.8e-6 geometric) and violations at 17 of the 32 sampled points for the opposite (exotic) sign; Hawking-Ellis type II/III (null) [same calc]. Two crossing null beams give s = 2 w1 w2 (1 - cos theta): 0, 3.05e-4, 0.0304, 2, 4 for 0, 1, 10, 90, 180 deg [same calc]. So energy conditions, quantum inequalities and chronology impose nothing here (no exotic stress-energy anywhere: the GW, its second-order source and the photons all satisfy the classical energy conditions), and the only GR-side constraint that bites is the null support of a single plane wave, which is what the no-go theorems [D-56, D-57, D-60] use and what F4 quantifies for a spherical wave.

### 3. Validity domains of the models used
- Graviton EFT at tree level (O(G^2) rates): valid for hbar w << E_P; the largest frequency in play, 1e10 eV, is 8e-19 E_P. Inside.
- Second-order classical metric plus quantised photon field (B&O v2's semiclassical reading [D-16]): valid while the graviton occupation is huge (N0 = 3.6e78 for GW150914 [D-42]) and photon back-reaction is negligible (near-source conversion <= 1.5e-76 in the merger band, <= 5e-41 even at 1e10 eV, F9-F10). Inside, except B&O's own eq. 19 corner where their integrated conversion exceeds 1 (F10).
