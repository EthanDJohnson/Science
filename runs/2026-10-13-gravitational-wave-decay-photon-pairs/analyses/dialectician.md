## Findings
F1. **The 2 pi delta(0) -> T step defines a total, not a sustained rate.** In B&O's own model the in-out yield of one packet is finite: P = R x T_eff, with R the eq. 8 "rate" and T_eff = 2 pi J_f / I_f^4 = (4 pi/3)/sigma for the top hat (toolkit gw_coherent_fusion docstring; B&O S6 [D-14]). For GW150914 at B&O's inputs (E_rel = 2 Msun c^2, f = 150 Hz, sigma = 200 rad/s) [D-27], R = 1.397e-4 pairs/s (photon time tau = 3578 s, matching [D-42]). T_eff = 0.0209 s, so P = 2.9e-6 pairs per merger packet (Poisson probability of any pair 2.9e-6). B&O's own validity condition tau << 1/sigma [D-14, D-61] fails by tau sigma = 7.2e5. [calc: runs/2026-10-13-gravitational-wave-decay-photon-pairs/calc/lens-dialectician_rate_vs_total.py]
F2. **The rate read as sustained along the path over-counts by t_prop/T_eff.** If R held for the 4.22e16 s light-travel time to 410 Mpc (no cosmology), it would give 5.9e12 pairs, against P = 2.9e-6: an over-count of 2.0e18. Eq. 9 (N_gamma growing linearly in t) [D-22] and an eq. 19/S12-type integration over the propagation time [D-19, brief Q4] rest on that reading. [calc: same file, Part B]
F3. **hbar counting (K2).** With N = E/(hbar w), the eq. 8 pair rate in SI is R = 3 G^2 E^2 w sigma^2/(25 pi c^10), and d ln R/d ln hbar = 0 exactly. The photon power W = 2 R hbar w is linear in hbar. The Rayleigh-Jeans-stimulated power W x 2kT/(hbar w) = 12 G^2 E^2 kT w sigma^2/(25 pi c^10) is hbar-free. So the pair NUMBER is a functional of the classical wave alone (hbar^0), as for Bogoliubov |beta|^2 of a quantum test field on a classical background. The ENERGY vanishes as hbar -> 0, and the stimulated energy is classical. [calc: same file, Part A]
F4. **A chirp suppresses the model's own yield as 1/duration (K3).** P = J_f x I_ang, and a spectral phase psi(k) enters only J_f = int dK0 |C(K0)|^2 with C(K0) = int da f(a) f(K0 - a). For a Gaussian |f| with linear chirp psi = beta (k - w)^2 the closed form is J_chirp/J_TL = 1/sqrt(1 + 4 beta^2 sigma^4) = t_TL/t_rms exactly (numeric agreement to 1e-6 at beta sigma^2 = 1, 5, 25, 100). For a top hat it tends to 3 pi/(sigma t_c) (numeric ratio 0.84, 0.967, 0.993, 0.998 at sigma t_c = 10, 50, 200, 800). At fixed |spectrum|, the yield therefore scales as 1/duration: the energy density sets it, not the bandwidth. [calc: same file, Part C]
F5. **The "lower bound" from binning is reversed for coarse bins.** For a chirped top-hat bin with sigma_b t_b >> 1, the model's total is P = 4 pi^2 c_rate G^2 N^2 w^3 / t_b (natural units), independent of the bandwidth. B&O's recipe gives rate(sigma_b) x t_b = c_rate G^2 N^2 w^3 sigma_b^2 t_b, so Y_BO/P = (sigma_b t_b / 2 pi)^2. For a GW170817 bin at 100 Hz (eps = 0.1, t_eps = 0.60 s [D-48]): sigma_b t_b = 37.7, an over-estimate of 36. For a WD binary at 6.2 mHz, eps = 0.1 and an assumed t_eps ~ 1e13 s: 3.8e19. For the GW150914 merger (sigma dt = 1, transform-limited) the bin factor does not apply. Binning becomes a genuine lower bound only when the bins are transform-limited (sigma_b t_b <~ 2 pi). [calc: same file, Part D]
F6. **A luminosity-set source rate.** Read as a rate for a steadily radiating source, P/t = 4 pi^2 c_rate G^2 L^2 w / c^10 (SI), which depends on the instantaneous GW power L and not on the bandwidth. GW150914 peak (L = 3.6e49 W [D-29], 150 Hz): 1.4e-3 pairs/s, 2.8e-34 W of photons. GW170817 at 100 Hz (L = 1.8e44 W [D-48]): 2.3e-14 pairs/s. The GW150914 figure lies outside the sigma t >> 1 asymptote, so it is an order-of-magnitude figure, within ~10x of B&O's 1.4e-4 pairs/s. [calc: same file, Part E]
F7. **Where an outgoing wave's yield is made (scaling estimate, labelled).** Take the far-zone angular spread theta ~ 1/(k r) [D-55 inference, unsourced], with sigma_xs ~ G^2 s, s ~ w^2 theta^2, and a flux factor ~ theta^2. Then dR/dr ~ r^-6, and the fraction of the yield made beyond r is ~ (r0/r)^5 with r0 = lambda/2pi; numerical integration confirms the exponent (0.03124 vs 0.03125 at 2 r0). For GW150914 (r0 = 3.2e5 m) the fraction beyond B&O's "sphere of radius c tau" (1.07e12 m) [D-19] is ~2e-33, and beyond 410 Mpc ~1e-98. The yield is a property of the near zone and of the source while it radiates, not of the propagating wave. If D-55's spread fails, the exponent changes, but any power > 1 still converges at small r. ## Lens-specific outputs

### Contradiction K1: "GWs decay in vacuum at a rate" vs "a single (locally plane) wave cannot produce pairs"
- **Thesis (B&O).** The eq. 8 pair rate R = 3 G^2 N^2 w^3 sigma^2/(25 pi) is a "transition probability per unit time" [D-14, D-21]. Photons accumulate linearly, N_gamma = 2 R t (eq. 9) [D-22], and the wave depletes in tau_h = tau E_rel/w_s [F9]. W_gamma is integrated over the propagation sphere (S12) [F9], with flux "at the sphere of radius tau" [D-19].
- **Antithesis.** Exact plane GWs produce no massless quanta [D-56, D-57; D-51 except the collinear sector, where the amplitude vanishes, D-13]. B&O themselves concede "no particle production occurs for an individual plane-wave GW" [D-17]. Null-like metric perturbations do not produce particles (Redi-Tesi support q^2 > 0 only) [D-60]. 2 -> 2 fusion needs non-collinear momenta, s > 0 [D-55]. Far from the source a spherical wave is locally plane.
- **Where both hold.** The in-out quantity in B&O's model is a finite TOTAL per packet, P = R x T_eff with T_eff = (4 pi/3)/sigma [F1]. R is the average production during the time the packet's non-collinear components overlap, which is the near zone of the source, or the focus of the time-symmetric shell an in-state momentum profile describes (tool docstring). B&O's "rate" is right as a time average over T_eff and wrong as a rate along the path. The no-go is right for the locally plane far-zone wave and silent about the near zone, where the wave is not plane. Both claims hold with "rate" restricted to the interval T_eff (or to the duration of the source) and "no production" restricted to r >> lambda.
- **Who yields.** B&O's path-integrated readings (eq. 9 for t >> T_eff, tau_h depletion, S12, footnote 4) yield: they over-count by t_prop/T_eff = 2.0e18 for GW150914 [F2].
- **Prediction neither side made alone.** (i) The yield of a single wave saturates at P (2.9e-6 pairs for the GW150914 merger packet) and does not grow with distance. (ii) The fraction made beyond r scales as (lambda/(2 pi r))^5: ~2e-33 beyond B&O's c tau sphere and ~1e-98 beyond 410 Mpc [F7]. (iii) A genuine sustained vacuum rate exists only for ensembles with non-collinear momenta: an isotropic SGWB or crossing waves from different sources. Its rate is exactly eq. 14 [F10], so eq. 14 is the right quantity for a background even though eq. 8 is not the right quantity for a wave. (iv) Path depletion of a single wave is zero at this order, not 1e-66 [D-47].

### Contradiction K2: "a quantum process that vanishes as hbar -> 0" vs "semiclassical, and classical when stimulated"
- **Thesis.** B&O v1 called it "a quantum process forbidden classically and semi-classically", and the v2 Discussion says it "vanishes in the classical limit" [D-54].
- **Antithesis.** B&O v2 say it "admits a semiclassical description" at second order in the metric [D-16]. With stimulation "all the hbar disappear" [D-54]. The N^2 looks like classical h^4 nonlinearity (hidden premise 10).
- **Where both hold.** Count powers of hbar separately for number and energy [F3]. The pair number R = 3 G^2 E^2 w sigma^2/(25 pi c^10) has no hbar: it is a functional of the classical wave alone, like a Bogoliubov |beta|^2 for a quantum test field on a classical background. The energy W = 2 R hbar w is linear in hbar and vanishes classically. The stimulated energy in a Rayleigh-Jeans bath is hbar-free, which makes it classical scattering of the ambient field. The "N^2" is the n^2 of binary collisions [F10], equivalently the (h^2)^2 of a second-order metric squared. It is no coherence-specific quantum enhancement: a coherent packet and an incoherent gas give the same local rate per unit volume.
- **Who yields.** Neither side, once "quantum" is split into "spontaneous production from vacuum fluctuations of the photon field" (true) and "a quantum enhancement of the graviton state" (false). B&O's word "coherence" as the second lifting effect yields: coherence does not raise the local rate above the kinetic n^2 sigma v [F10].
- **Prediction.** (i) The same pair number must come from a Bogoliubov or Redi-Tesi-type yield of Maxwell on the classical O(h^2) metric, whose Fourier support is time-like q = k1 + k2 with k1, k2 non-collinear [D-60]. That one-loop formula, fed with the second-order metric, should reproduce P with no graviton quantisation. (ii) An isotropic incoherent background with the same rho gives the same eq. 14 rate as a coherent one (checked) [F10]. (iii) In the CMB the stimulated energy transfer is a classical exchange that already nets emission against absorption (B&O's 1 + 2 f_gamma) [F9], consistent with the Ai-Ellis-Pradler compensation [D-71c]. It is not a further quantum amplification.

### Contradiction K3: "binning into eps bins gives a lower bound" vs "the coherent chirp's own yield"
- **Thesis (B&O p. 3).** Neglecting coherence between bins can only lower the emissivity [F9].
- **Antithesis.** In B&O's own model, P = J_f x I_ang, and a chirp's spectral phase lowers J_f. At fixed |spectrum| the yield scales as 1/duration (Gaussian, exact: J_chirp/J_TL = t_TL/t_rms; top hat 3 pi/(sigma t_c)) [F4]. A bin with sigma_b = eps w and sweep time t_b >> 1/sigma_b is a chirped packet, not a transform-limited one.
- **Where both hold.** Binning is a lower bound relative to eq. 8 applied to the whole band, since sum_bins ~ c N^2 sigma^2 t/n^4 with n bins. It is also a lower bound on the true total when the bins are transform-limited (sigma_b t_b <~ 2 pi). It is an UPPER bound, an over-estimate by (sigma_b t_b/2 pi)^2, when B&O keep sigma_b = eps w for t_b > 1/(eps w), which is their own stated branch [F5, F9].
- **Prediction.** (i) The model's yield of a long signal depends on the instantaneous GW luminosity and not on the bandwidth: dP/dt ~ 4 pi^2 c_rate G^2 L^2 w/c^10 (SI) [F6]. (ii) B&O's Fig. 2a inspiral luminosities are too high, within their own model, by 36 for GW170817 at 100 Hz (eps = 0.1) and by up to ~4e19 for slowly chirping WD binaries [F5]. (iii) The merger panel (sigma dt ~ 1) is unaffected by this factor and is affected only by K1.

## What would change my mind
- A position-space calculation showing that a retarded outgoing packet's photon number keeps growing linearly with the radius of the counting sphere at r >> lambda. That would rescue B&O's path reading, kill A, and revive E.
- A demonstration that the far-zone non-collinearity does not fall as theta ~ 1/(k r). - A semiclassical Bogoliubov calculation of Maxwell on the O(h^2) metric that disagrees with the in-out P at leading order. That would undercut C's equivalence claim.
