## 1. Established

### 1a. Literature (peer-reviewed or textbook; verified)

- [D-01] Tree-level photon-graviton scattering: the massless helicity amplitudes have magnitudes 8 pi G s^2/t and 8 pi G u^2/t (each twice), giving dsigma(g gamma -> g gamma)/dt = 2 pi G^2 (s^4 + u^4)/(s^2 t^2) (natural units), stated to agree with Skobelev (1975). Crossing s <-> t gives magnitudes 8 pi G t^2/s and 8 pi G u^2/s for g g -> gamma gamma, the magnitudes B&O quote in their eq. 6 (the crossing step is the researcher's; phases and helicity labelling are not fixed by the source). Skobelev's original was not retrieved. (refs: RT-07) peer-reviewed; ACCESS full-text (arXiv version of PRD 91, 064008).
- [D-02] In pure gravity, tree amplitudes with all-plus or one-minus helicity (all-outgoing convention) vanish, by supersymmetric Ward identities, "because the supersymmetric partners couple quadratically". Extending this to Einstein-Maxwell amplitudes with external photons is the researcher's inference, cross-checked numerically in D-20. (refs: RT-08) textbook-or-review (Elvang & Huang, arXiv:1308.1697).
- [D-03] Exact plane gravitational waves produce no particles in the Green-function / DeWitt-Schwinger approach. Gibbons 1975 (abstract): a quantized massive scalar field on a c-number plane sandwich wave of arbitrary profile and polarisation; no particles; any reasonable regularisation of the vacuum <T_mu nu> vanishes; "a gravitational wave far from its source will propagate without hindrance by quantum effects". Garriga & Verdaguer 1991 confirmed it for scalars (via Khusnutdinov's account). Khusnutdinov 2026 (EPJ Plus 141, 532) extends it to massless scalar and Maxwell (vector) fields: the Green functions "coincide exactly with the DeWitt-Schwinger expansion, indicating no massless particles production". Scope: exact plane-fronted waves of infinite transverse extent, fixed classical background, free test fields, no back-reaction. Correction: the brief describes Gibbons as a result for a massless field; his abstract concerns a massive scalar, and the massless scalar and vector statements rest on Khusnutdinov. (refs: RT-10, RC-03, RF-16) peer-reviewed; Gibbons and Garriga-Verdaguer abstract or second-hand only.
- [D-04] Deser 1975: "Gravitational plane waves, like their electromagnetic and Yang-Mills counterparts, are undistorted by vacuum polarization effects to all loop orders, if no cosmological term is induced." This concerns the wave's own effective action, not pair production. (refs: RT-12) peer-reviewed; ACCESS abstract.
- [D-05] Garriga & Verdaguer 1991: for exact plane waves, the quantum scattering cross section equals the classical geodesic one, and the renormalised scalar stress tensor between scattering states diverges where classical test particles focus. This concerns probe particles crossing the wave, not production from vacuum. (refs: RT-13) peer-reviewed; ACCESS abstract.
- [D-06] Two colliding exact plane waves do create scalar particles (Dorca & Verdaguer 1993, Nucl. Phys. B 403, 770; 1994, PRD 50, 2631). The "in" vacuum contains "out" particles numbering in proportion to the inverse frequency, with a thermal-like spectrum at a temperature inversely proportional to the focusing time. Scope: a strong-field, non-perturbative colliding-wave spacetime that focuses into a Killing-Cauchy horizon, with a massless scalar. It shows that going from one wave to two non-collinear waves changes the answer, but it is not a weak-field check of B&O. B&O cite it only as an "other limit". (refs: RT-14 verified; RC-06 duplicate, unverifiable on re-read) peer-reviewed; ACCESS abstract.
- [D-07] KMOC / waveforms from amplitudes: the classical radiation of a source is a coherent state, and the radiated-field operator is R = Int d^4y J(y) [G_ret - G_adv](x - y), the Pauli-Jordan combination. "In the far future, where the observer measures the wavetrain emitted from the scattering event, G_adv will vanish." Applying this to B&O's eq. 5 (a state defined on the whole time axis therefore carries an incoming, advanced part at early times) is the researcher's inference, and the checker calls it an analogy. (refs: RT-15) peer-reviewed (Cristofoli, Gonzo, Kosower, O'Connell 2022, PRD 106, 056007).
- [D-08] Waveforms are in-in expectation values <in|a_out|in> and can be written through retarded products; "the waveform depends only on physics within its past light cone" (Caron-Huot, Giroux, Hannesdottir, Mizera, JHEP 01 (2024) 139). The researcher's gloss, that B&O's in-out pair probability from a coherent state is a different quantity, is labelled medium confidence. (refs: RT-16) peer-reviewed.
- [D-09] Graviton radiation from a classical source is a coherent state with Poissonian statistics. For minimally coupled scalar sources, the leading deviation comes from the six-point tree amplitude with two emitted gravitons (Britto, Gonzo, Jehu, JHEP 03 (2022) 214). Kanno, Soda & Taniguchi (PRL 136, 061404 (2026)) find that the coherent state reproduces classical GWs from binary black holes at leading order, with a squeezing parameter of about 1e-4 for GW150914. Both support B&O's starting state (eq. 3) as the source's radiated state; neither treats it as an in-state for scattering. (refs: RT-17, RF-17) peer-reviewed; RF-17 ACCESS abstract.
- [D-10] The Gertsenshtein effect (graviton <-> photon conversion) needs an external field: a single graviton converts "through a region filled with a constant magnetic field". It is linear in the GW amplitude and in the background field. A parametric-resonance variant (arXiv:2205.08767, a preprint) instead needs a plasma with m_plasma^2 < (1 - c_s^2) omega^2/4. Neither is vacuum g g -> gamma gamma, and B&O agree ("this is different if one considers decays in a medium"). The scaling with B^2 L^2 is unsourced. (refs: RT-20, RC-12) peer-reviewed (Palessandro, CQG 41, 215011); ACCESS abstract (Palessandro).
- [D-11] Sawyer 2020 (PRL 124, 101301) uses the same O(G^2) g g -> gamma gamma amplitudes. He argues that "in dense clouds of pure gravitons there are collective effects utilizing these same amplitudes that under the right circumstances can lead to copious production of photons", via a "quantum break" at a time T_B (of order (8 pi G n)^-1 log N in the arXiv text), in a multi-beam initial state. He also states: "Coherent many body systems of gravitons, as in a wave detectable by LIGO, are essentially classical fields". B&O cite Sawyer but have not been reconciled with him in print. (refs: RC-08 corrected, RF-18) peer-reviewed; full-text.
- [D-12] Flauger & Weinberg 2019 (PRD 99, 123030): GW absorption by ionized gas in clusters is nearly cancelled by stimulated emission, so nanohertz GWs are not blocked. This is an analogy for B&O's stimulated factor, not a test of g g -> gamma gamma. (refs: RC-11) peer-reviewed; ACCESS abstract.
- [D-13] Jones, Singleton et al. (IJMPD 24, 1544017 (2015); PRD 95, 065010 (2017)) published the earliest claim of GW attenuation through g g -> gamma gamma / production by a plane GW, without coherent-state N^2 language. Their own conclusion is that "the conversion ... is significant only close to the source of the gravitational waves where h+ is large", with decay length Lambda proportional to h^-4 (numbers in D-63). B&O do not cite them, yet claim their calculation "supersedes previous semiclassical calculations". The 2017 method is disputed (D-67). (refs: RC-05, RT-11) peer-reviewed.
- [D-14] Ground-based radio astronomy is blocked below about 10 MHz by the plasma frequency of the ionosphere's F layer. The lowest-frequency cosmological radio instrument being built is LuSEE-Night (lunar far side, 0.1-50 MHz, planned arrival 2027). The band comes from an unopened paper (arXiv:2407.07173), and the date is a plan. (refs: RE-08) peer-reviewed (Astron. J.) / abstract.
- [D-15] Voyager 1 in-situ plasma: n_e about 0.002 cm^-3 in the outer heliosphere, rising to about 0.1 cm^-3 in the interstellar medium; plasma oscillations at about 2.6 kHz correspond to about 0.08 cm^-3 (Gurnett et al. 2013, Science 341, 1489, author copy). (refs: RE-09) peer-reviewed; hardware, one spacecraft, one crossing.
- [D-16] A CMB spectral distortion constrains only the fractional energy released: mu = 1.4 Delta E/E for injection at 1e4 < z < 1e7 (Kogut et al. 2011, JCAP 07 (2011) 025). The y relation Delta E/E = 4y used by the run is standard but unsourced here. (refs: RE-03) peer-reviewed.
- [D-17] Status of spectral-distortion missions (BISOU proceedings, EPJ Web Conf. 2024): the PIXIE and PRISTINE proposals "have not been successful"; ESA Voyage 2050 selected spectral distortions as a theme with no mission fixed; BISOU is a CNES Phase-0 balloon study aiming at least at the Compton y-distortion. No 2025-2026 status update was found. (refs: RE-06) peer-reviewed proceedings; the timeline rests on a 2024 paper.
- [D-18] No published constraint on GW damping by photon-pair emission was found. The nearest tests (standard-siren comparisons with GW170817 and GW190521, Mastrogiovanni et al. 2021) constrain a different, beyond-GR friction parameterisation. Such damping would be degenerate with luminosity distance. (refs: RE-16) peer-reviewed; ACCESS abstract; a negative search result.
- [D-19] Unverified: the DEMETER three-axis search-coil magnetometer covers 1 Hz-20 kHz (Seran et al. 2005). Its noise floor was not retrieved. (refs: RE-13) ACCESS abstract; checker could not confirm, so unverified.

### 1b. Run calculations (status: secondary/analysis; the checkers verified the numbers against the logs, nothing is peer-reviewed)

- [D-20] Helicity selection for tree-level g g -> gamma gamma, with physical helicities: only 4 of the 16 helicity amplitudes are non-zero, all with OPPOSITE incoming graviton helicities and opposite photon helicities, with |M| = 8 pi G t^2/s or 8 pi G u^2/s. Two same-helicity gravitons give sigma(R,R) = 1.9e-30 (roundoff), against sigma(R,L) = (4/5) pi G^2 s = 10.053 at s = 4 (natural units, G = 1). Hence B&O's four non-zero eq. 6 labels match a common-axis spin-projection labelling, not physical helicities of both pairs, and a coherent state of a single circular polarisation has zero tree-level fusion amplitude. Route 2 (crossed Bjerrum-Bohr) gives the unpolarised sigma = 2 pi G^2 s/5 = 5.0265, equal to (1/2) sigma(R,L). The checker notes that route 2 confirms only the normalisation; the helicity pattern rests on the toolkit code (consistent with D-02). (refs: RT-09) [calc: runs/2026-10-13-gravitational-wave-decay-photon-pairs/calc/theory_helicity_selection.py]
- [D-21] B&O's top-hat normalisation Theta^2 = 2 pi^2 N/(omega_s sigma_omega) is reproduced exactly (ratio 1.000000) only for f_s constant over the full 4 pi sr in both helicities. One helicity, or a hemisphere, is off by a factor 2; a 0.1 rad beam by a factor 400.3. B&O's SM S6 integrates the pair momentum over 0 <= |K| <= 2 omega_s, i.e. opening angles up to pi. Inference (the checker calls it likely, not proven): eq. 8 describes an isotropic, unpolarised shell in k-space, not a collimated outgoing wave. For opening angle psi, s/(4 omega_s^2) = 0.5 at pi/2, 2.5e-3 at 0.1 rad and 2.5e-7 at 1e-3 rad. (refs: RT-18) [calc: runs/2026-10-13-gravitational-wave-decay-photon-pairs/calc/theory_shell_normalisation.py]
- [D-22] B&O's merger luminosity, sized from their printed eqs. 8-12 and inputs (150 Hz, E_rel = 2 M_sun c^2, t_m = 5 ms). The vacuum (unstimulated) yield is of order 1e-4 photons/s, i.e. about 1e-6 photons per merger. B&O's quoted "~1e6 s^-1" is reproduced to within a factor 5-10 only after multiplying by the CMB stimulation factor (1 + 2 f_gamma) = 7.6e8 at 150 Hz. So essentially all of the quoted merger luminosity is that factor. B&O's validity condition tau << 1/sigma_omega fails by about 1e6 without the factor. Numbers are in D-34 to D-37. (refs: RQ-12, RF-06, RE-12) [calc: quantitative_inputs.py, frontier_sanity.py, engineering_detectability.py]
- [D-23] Observability of the merger photons: they come out at the GW frequency (150 Hz = 6.2e-13 eV). A 150 Hz EM wave is evanescent where n_e > 2.8e-4 cm^-3, so it cannot propagate through the outer heliosphere (f_p about 400 Hz), the local ISM (about 2.5 kHz) or the ionosphere (about 9 MHz). Every cosmological-background instrument's lowest frequency is 2e7-2e8 times higher (FIRAS about 12 GHz, ARCADE 2 3 GHz, PIXIE 30 GHz); even LuSEE-Night (0.1 MHz) is about 670 times higher. At face value, B&O's ~1e6 photons/s at 410 Mpc gives 4.9e-77 W/m^2 at Earth. B&O themselves say photons below kHz "are immediately absorbed by the interstellar medium". (refs: RE-09, RE-11, RQ-16, RE-01, RE-05, RE-07, RE-08, RF-07) [calc]
- [D-24] The stochastic-background channel is far out of reach. B&O's eq. 14 at the BBN-type rho_GW bound gives about 6e-110 photons m^-3 s^-1, injecting over a Hubble time a fraction 1e-110 (at 1e-13 eV) to 1e-87 (at 10 GeV) of rho_CMB. That is 80 or more orders of magnitude below the FIRAS-allowed Delta E/E of about 6e-5, even if eq. 14 were right. (refs: RQ-11, RE-04, RQ-09) [calc]

## 3. Contested or conflicting

- [D-67] Plane-wave production at collinear momenta: Garriga & Verdaguer's Bogoliubov coefficient beta is proportional to delta(k_v + l_v). It vanishes for massive quanta but can be non-zero for massless quanta exactly collinear with the wave, and Jones, McDougall & Singleton (PRD 95, 065010) claim massless production on this basis. Against this, the Green-function / DeWitt-Schwinger route (Gibbons; Khusnutdinov, scalar and vector) gives none. Khusnutdinov calls it "an unresolved issue" and notes that any such photons are aligned with the wave and "leave with the sandwich". This concerns a measure-zero collinear set of modes, not B&O's non-collinear fusion. (refs: RT-11, RC-04, RC-05, RF-16)
- [D-68] Chakraborty et al. 2024 (PRD 110, L021701, abstract only): scalar quanta "may indeed be produced in a perturbative manner" in flat space perturbed by a linear GW, a formulation "by no means restricted to monochromatic plane waves". This is in apparent tension with the plane-wave no-go (D-03), but whether their set-up escapes Gibbons' assumptions is unchecked, and no rate is given. Low confidence. (refs: RC-13)
- [D-69] B&O v1 vs v2 on the nature of the effect. v1: "a quantum process forbidden classically and semi-classically"; W_gamma "vanishes for hbar -> 0". v2: "admits a semiclassical description once the metric is solved to second order"; with a photon background "all the hbar disappear, and our calculation corresponds to a classical process". v2 also keeps "the quadratic coupling of photons forbids it at the classical level from pure vacuum". The v2 comment line says "results unchanged". The run's reading (RC-15, RT-21, inference) is that "classical" can only mean stimulated amplification of a pre-existing photon field, with the vacuum part quantum. Bao et al. 2026 (preprint, abstract) argue that "all nonclassical effects are suppressed by extra powers of the weak coupling". (refs: RF-01, RC-01, RT-04, RT-05, RC-15, RF-14)

## 4. Constraints: theorems, bounds, no-go results

- [D-77] Kinematics of massless 1 -> 2 (textbook kinematics; B&O agree). Assumptions: on-shell massless particles, flat space, energy-momentum conservation. A massless particle can decay to two massless particles only for exactly collinear momenta (measure zero), where s = 0. B&O: "g -> gamma gamma is forbidden by the graviton being massless", g -> g gamma gamma "has a vanishing rate", "emptyset -> g g gamma gamma violates energy-momentum conservation", and diagrams with an on-shell intermediate GW graviton need collinear incoming momenta, where "helicity conservation ... cannot be satisfied" and "the amplitude also vanishes at s = 0". So the only channel is 2 -> 2 fusion inside one coherent state, needing non-collinear momenta, with s = (k1 + k2)^2 > 0. Correction to RT-19's wording: |M| <= 8 pi G s, so the tree amplitude does vanish as s -> 0. No quotable textbook page was retrieved. (refs: RT-19, RF-03, RC-02)
- [D-78] Plane-wave no-particle-production (D-03). Assumptions: exact plane-fronted (Brinkmann/Rosen) wave of infinite transverse extent, sandwich or not; fixed c-number metric with no back-reaction and no graviton statistics; free quantum test field (Gibbons: massive scalar; Khusnutdinov: massless scalar and Maxwell); Green-function / DeWitt-Schwinger method. Domain: single plane waves only. It does not cover finite-aperture packets, spherical waves, crossed waves, or the second-order metric sourced by the wave's own stress (absent for an exact plane wave; that last point is an inference, RC-03/RC-10). Open edge: the collinear Bogoliubov dispute (D-67). B&O accept this no-go (RT-04, RC-02). (refs: RT-10, RC-03, RC-04, RF-16)
- [D-79] Deser 1975: exact gravitational (and EM, Yang-Mills) plane waves are not corrected by vacuum polarisation at any loop order, provided no cosmological term is induced. The domain is exact plane waves and the wave's own effective action. (refs: RT-12)
- [D-80] Time-like support for pair production (Redi & Tesi 2026, preprint; full text). Particle production from a classical inhomogeneous metric background is set by the source's stress-tensor two-point function at time-like momentum (q^mu q_mu > 0), while GW emission is set by the light-like region (q^2 = 0). "null-like tensor and vector perturbations do not produce particles"; on sub-horizon scales, production "practically reduces to gravitational scattering". Assumptions: classical background, test fields, cosmological (early-Universe) setting, sub-horizon. Applying this to B&O (a freely propagating on-shell wave makes no pairs; production tracks near-source or crossing-wave off-shell components) is the researchers' inference. B&O cite Redi & Tesi as an "other limit". (refs: RC-07, RF-15)
- [D-81] Threshold structure of near-source pair creation (Franchino-Vinas, Varacalli, Zanusso 2026, preprint). The O(curvature^2) pair probability carries Theta(-4 m^2 - p^2), i.e. support only at time-like p with p0 = 2 n omega (harmonic n of the orbital frequency); "the n = 0 term does not contribute, even for massless fields". Pairs are localised in the orbital plane, while GW power peaks along the rotation axis. Assumptions: weak field, point-mass non-relativistic binary, spin 0, 1/2 or 1 test fields; the binary number quoted (D-64) is for a massless scalar with the O(1) form factor stripped. (refs: RF-13, RC-14)
- [D-82] Tree-level helicity selection (run calc D-20, with D-02 for pure gravity). g g -> gamma gamma is non-zero only for opposite physical graviton helicities. Assumptions: tree level, flat space, physical helicities along each momentum; the Einstein-Maxwell extension of the Ward-identity argument is inferred, and the pattern rests on toolkit code plus an unpolarised normalisation check. Consequence: zero tree-level fusion within a purely circularly polarised coherent state. (refs: RT-09, RT-08)
- [D-83] Causality / in-in structure: physical radiated fields are in-in expectation values that depend only on the past light cone (D-08); for a source's radiation the advanced part of the Pauli-Jordan field vanishes only in the far future (D-07). Assumptions: KMOC framework, scattering setting; extending it to B&O's source-with-Pauli-Jordan state is an analogy (checker). (refs: RT-15, RT-16)
- [D-84] B&O's own validity condition: eq. 8 is "a probability per unit time for tau << delta t ~ 1/sigma_omega", with 2 pi delta(0) -> T, T ~ 1/sigma_omega "provided 1/sigma_omega >> tau" (SM SB). For their merger inputs, tau ~ 1e4 s against 1/sigma_omega ~ 7 ms (D-34), so the condition fails by about 1e6 unless the (1 + 2 f_gamma) ~ 7.6e8 factor is applied (tau -> ~1e-5 s). The leftover delta is a finite overlap of four profile functions of order 1/sigma_omega, so the O(1) coefficient depends on the profile. That is the researcher's reading at medium confidence; no textbook page on 2 pi delta(0) -> T for wave packets was retrieved. (refs: RT-06, RF-04, RC-09, RQ-12)
- [D-85] BBN / N_eff bound on rho_GW (D-49, D-50). Inputs: N_eff from Planck 2018 (+BAO); radiation-density relation with Maggiore 2000's coefficient (h0^2 rho_gamma/rho_c = 2.481e-5). Domain: only GWs already present at T ~ MeV; "It does not apply to any stochastic background produced later, like backgrounds of astrophysical origin". B&O use it as their rho_GW for eq. 14. (refs: RQ-08, RQ-07, RF-08)
- [D-86] Spectral-distortion bounds constrain integrated energy injection, not a spectral line at the injected frequency: mu = 1.4 Delta E/E for 1e4 < z < 1e7 (D-16); B&O consider injection only after z ~ 3e6 (thermalised before). Inputs: FIRAS (1996 values in B&O; the mu limit is superseded, D-43); the y relation is unsourced. The run did not check whether B&O's injected photon spectrum and propagation (plasma, cascade) are right for these bounds. (refs: RE-03, RE-02, RQ-04)
- [D-87] Plasma cutoff: an EM wave of frequency f does not propagate where n_e > eps0 m_e (2 pi f)^2/e^2 (cold, unmagnetised plasma); 150 Hz -> 2.8e-4 cm^-3. Inputs: Voyager densities (D-15), mean cosmic n_e (Omega_b h^2 = 0.0224, Y = 0.245, fully ionised), assumed ISM 0.03 cm^-3, ionosphere 1e12 m^-3. The ionosphere is opaque below about 10 MHz (D-14). (refs: RE-09, RQ-16, RE-08)
- [D-88] Infrared-safety / transparency (analogy only): Bose-enhanced emission and absorption in a GW background "compensate each other to all orders", leaving inclusive decay rates "essentially unchanged" (Ai, Ellis, Pradler, preprint, v2 "corrected and improved interpretation"); Flauger-Weinberg near-cancellation (D-12). The domain is matter decays and absorption by soft gravitons, not g g -> gamma gamma. (refs: RC-11, RF-11)
