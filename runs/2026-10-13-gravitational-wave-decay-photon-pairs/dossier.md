## 1. Established

### 1a. Physics established in the peer-reviewed literature (verified unless noted)
- [D-01] Tree-level graviton-photon amplitudes. In the massless limit, the only physical non-vanishing gamma g -> gamma g helicity amplitudes are D(++;++) = D(--;--) = 8 pi G s^2/t and D(--;++) = D(++;--) = 8 pi G u^2/t. The unpolarised cross section is dsigma/dt = 2 pi G^2 (s^4 + u^4)/(s^2 t^2), a sum of squares with no cross term, and it agrees with Skobelev (1975). Crossing s <-> t gives the g g -> gamma gamma magnitudes 8 pi G t^2/s and 8 pi G u^2/s, which are the entries in B&O eq. 6. The crossing step is the researcher's (standard) inference. Caveat from the check: the paper's list also contains a longitudinal D(00;++) = 8 pi G su/t, which the paper argues is unphysical in the massless limit. (refs: RT-15, RT-03, RT-12) peer-reviewed
- [D-02] Coherent states of massless bosons are the classical limit of radiation fields. Classical wave observables split into those with incoming waves (a classical wave in the in-state) and those with outgoing waves (waveforms). (refs: RT-13) peer-reviewed. The researcher's inference that S_source|0> taken as an in-state is therefore not automatically the outgoing retarded field is NOT stated in the source; see section 3.
- [D-03] Expectation values of radiation fields (waveforms) are inclusive observables with relaxed time-ordering. They are computed with retarded products on a Schwinger-Keldysh contour and are distinct from time-ordered S-matrix elements. This is the formal basis for the in-out vs in-in distinction. The statement that the two agree at first order is the researcher's inference and is unquoted. (refs: RT-14) peer-reviewed
- [D-04] Particle creation by gravitational waves is established where two plane waves collide head-on (Dorca-Verdaguer). That is an exact, strong-field colliding-wave spacetime: the waves focus into a Killing-Cauchy horizon, and the interaction region is locally isometric to the inside of a Schwarzschild horizon. The analysis uses wave-packet mode quantisation, so creation is localised. This is not two weak coherent packets in flat space. (refs: RT-19 [verified, gr-qc/9403015], RC-09 [abstract of NPB 403, 770, unverifiable]) peer-reviewed
- [D-05] The Gertsenshtein effect (GW -> single photon) needs an external background field B. It is a 1 -> 1 conversion, quenched by the effective photon mass above ~1 PeV in the Milky Way field. It is not a vacuum decay and is no precedent for decay into photon pairs. (refs: RC-13; the Palessandro quote is unchecked) peer-reviewed
- [D-06] Sawyer (2020): the lowest-order g g -> gamma gamma cross sections are O(G^2), "too small to be of any interest", but collective effects in dense clouds of pure gravitons "can lead to copious production of photons". The arXiv comment notes that the helicity amplitudes were redefined with no effect on later equations. (refs: RF-12) peer-reviewed (the PRL reference was not checked), abstract only
- [D-07] Free electromagnetic waves are evanescent below the local electron plasma frequency, f_pe = 8980 sqrt(n_e / cm^-3) Hz. Voyager 1 measured 2.6 kHz plasma oscillations, i.e. n_e ~ 0.08 cm^-3 (range 0.06-0.08 cm^-3), in the very local interstellar medium. The ionosphere is opaque below a few MHz. (refs: RE-04, RE-05, RQ-10) peer-reviewed (Gurnett 2013); RE-05's source is a preprint (Snowmass white paper)
- [D-08] COBE/FIRAS spectral-distortion limits: |y| < 15e-6 and |mu| < 9e-5 (95% CL); T_CMB = 2.728 +- 0.004 K (95% CL). These are the limits B&O use, and they remain the only measurement. Whether any later spectrometer has improved on them was not checked. (refs: RQ-06, RQ-07, RE-01) peer-reviewed
- [D-09] ARCADE-2 (3, 8, 10, 30 and 90 GHz) found an extragalactic radio excess, with both 3 GHz channels more than 5 sigma above the FIRAS CMB value. The band is 7-9 orders of magnitude above the Hz-kHz photons of the GW-fusion process. (refs: RE-03) peer-reviewed

### 1b. What B&O state (v2 preprint; the quotations are verified against the arXiv PDF, but these are claims of a non-refereed paper, not established physics)
- [D-10] Model. A classical source couples linearly to the graviton EFT. The coherent state D(f)|0> = S|0> is the state in which the dynamics is evolved ("coherent states as initial states"). Its field, <f|h|f> = sqrt(8 pi G) Int d^4y Delta_rad(x-y) T_s(y) (eq. 5), is called "the classical radiated field by the source", with Delta_rad named "the Pauli-Jordan function". The checker did not find the explicit "S = T exp[...]" form or the phrase "used as in-state" in the fetched text; that part is the researcher's reading. B&O do not use the word "retarded" (RC-04 check). (refs: RT-01, RC-04) preprint
- [D-11] Far from the source the field tends to an outgoing spherical packet h ~ cos(w_s(r-t))/r x exp(-(r-t)^2 sigma_w^2/2): a burst of duration dt ~ 1/sigma_w. It is then approximated by a narrow-band top-hat profile (the top-hat form was not grepped by the checker). The polarisation label is "suppressed from now on". (refs: RT-02, RC-04) preprint
- [D-12] Eq. 7 combines the eq. 6 amplitudes for an "unpolarized graviton state" into a single scalar (s^2 - 2tu)/s x delta_{mu1 mu2}, which equals (t^2 + u^2)/s on shell (s + t + u = 0). Reading this as an equal-sign coherent sum is the researcher's inference. (refs: RT-03, theory notes) preprint
- [D-13] SM S3: the diagram with an on-shell intermediate graviton (K^2 = s = 0) drops out. It needs collinear incoming momenta, where helicity conservation fails (+-2 +-2 != +-2), and the amplitude vanishes at s = 0. The other bilinears are "handled analogously". What remains is 2 -> 2 fusion of non-collinear gravitons from the same packet. (refs: RT-04) preprint
- [D-14] Rate step (SM S6, p. 10): "the remaining energy-conserving delta function is treated as 2 pi delta(0) -> T, the interaction time, so that P/T is the transition probability per unit time ... since T ~ 1/sigma_w, provided 1/sigma_w >> tau". T is the packet's own duration. B&O give no separate statement that the wave keeps fusing as it propagates. (refs: RT-05, RF-05) preprint
- [D-15] The narrow-band limit is used to drop a-dagger a terms: "suppressed because of energy conservation in the narrow-band limit sigma_w/w_s << 1" (footnote 2, p. 2). (refs: RC-14) preprint
- [D-16] Semiclassical equivalence (v2): the calculation "is equivalent to that of considering the creation of photons in a background h_c, once considered at second order (including ... the induced metric sourced by the energy-momentum tensor of the GWs)". It "supersedes previous semiclassical calculations". (refs: RT-07, RC-03, RF-03) preprint
- [D-17] B&O concede that the process is forbidden classically from pure vacuum by "the quadratic coupling of photons". They also concede that "no particle production occurs for an individual plane-wave GW" in semiclassical calculations [their refs 21-26]. (refs: RC-02, RF-03) preprint
- [D-18] B&O use the Feynman propagator in diagram 2 of Fig. 1, with G_F = G_ret - 2 pi i theta(-k0) delta(k^2). They assert that the extra term "vanishes when convoluted with the external coherent states". (refs: RF-03) preprint
- [D-19] Consequences as B&O state them. Binary photon luminosities "are very weak", comparable to the Hawking luminosity of a black hole of the binary's mass (eq. 13: W_H/W_gamma ~ 1e2-1e3 for GW150914; that figure was not re-grepped). The decay "happens in an extended region". Merger panel Fig. 2b gives L_gamma ~ 1e6 s^-1 "along the nominal frequency". Footnote 4 says frequencies below kHz "are immediately absorbed by the interstellar medium", and the flux is estimated "at the sphere of radius tau". The binning into fractional bandwidth eps "yields a lower bound ... as any residual coherence between bins is neglected". (refs: RF-06, RF-07, RE-08) preprint
- [D-20] B&O's acknowledgements thank P. A. Cano, J. Garriga ("comments on the semiclassical calculations") and N. Rodd ("comments on a previous version"). (refs: RF-17) preprint

## 3. Contested or conflicting
- [D-51] Massless particle production by an exact plane GW. Side A (none): the Green-function / DeWitt-Schwinger approach. Gibbons for scalars; Khusnutdinov 2026 for massless scalar and vector fields, by three methods; Garriga-Verdaguer's Pauli-Villars confirmation of vanishing <T_mu nu>. Side B (nonzero): the Bogolyubov-coefficient approach (Jones, McDougall, Singleton and co-workers) gives beta ~ delta(k_v + l_v), nonzero only for massless quanta exactly collinear with the GW. Khusnutdinov calls this "an unresolved issue". Any photons created stay in the sandwich and leave with it. The disputed sector is the exactly collinear one, where B&O's own S3 argument (D-13) says the amplitude vanishes (inference). (refs: RT-09, RT-11, RT-17, RT-18, RC-06, RC-07, RC-12, RF-14)
- [D-52] Perturbative production by linear, non-plane GW profiles. Chakraborty et al. (2024) show that scalar quanta "may indeed be produced in a perturbative manner" for any linear GW profile, including compact-binary signals. Plane-wave no-go results (D-56, D-57) concern exact plane waves. These are different regimes, and their reconciliation was not checked. This is a linear-in-h test-field process with no N^2 factor and is distinct from B&O's fusion. Its size for photons was not extracted. The clause in RC-12 calling Jones et al.'s result an "artefact" was dropped as unsupported. (refs: RF-15, RC-12)
- [D-53] Size of vacuum attenuation. Jones & Singleton (2015 GRF essay, IJMPD 24, 1544017) use an Unruh-DeWitt detector response to estimate a mean decay length <r> ~ 1e11 m (1e-5 ly) for a solar-mass-scale source at w ~ 1e2 s^-1, with Gamma growing linearly with r. They conclude that detecting GWs at large distances "may be hard or impossible". B&O's rates give path depletions of 1e-66 to 1e-57 (D-47). The researcher's inference is that the Jones-Singleton figure would conflict with the detection of GW150914 at 410 Mpc. B&O do not cite this essay. It uses a detector-response derivation (weakly supported). (refs: RC-11, D-47)
- [D-54] Quantum or classical origin of the N^2 effect, within B&O itself. v1 abstract: "a quantum process forbidden classically and semi-classically"; v1 body: W_gamma "vanishes for hbar -> 0". v2 instead says the effect "admits a semiclassical description once the metric is solved to second order" (D-16), and its eq. 11/12 power is linear in hbar, with "the quantumness ... from the energy of photons". In the stimulated-emission paragraph, "all the hbar disappear, and our calculation corresponds to a classical process". The v2 Discussion still says the effect "vanishes in the classical limit, but can be recovered semiclassically". The v1 interpretation is superseded by v2 ("results unchanged"). v2 carries both statements. Critique facet (inference): Maxwell's equations are linear and homogeneous in F on a fixed metric, so F = 0 stays a solution, and the photons come from quantised-field vacuum fluctuations in a second-order metric, not from a classical Einstein-Maxwell solution. (refs: RF-01, RF-02, RF-08, RT-16, RC-15)
- [D-71] (ID assigned out of sequence) Tensions specific to B&O's construction. Each is a researcher's inference, and none has a published rebuttal. The plane-wave constraints are in section 4.
  - (a) Field carried by the state. B&O call eq. 5 the "radiated field" and use the Pauli-Jordan function (the commutator function, retarded minus advanced, which is standard knowledge), yet describe it as an outgoing spherical packet. They use S|0> as the initial state. Cristofoli et al. distinguish incoming-wave from outgoing-wave observables (D-02), and Caron-Huot et al. put waveforms on in-in retarded products (D-03). Whether S|0> as an in-state carries the outgoing retarded field or a time-symmetric field is unresolved in the sources. No source sentence saying a coherent in-state S|0> carries a time-symmetric field was found. (refs: RT-01, RT-13, RT-14, RC-04)
  - (b) Coherent vs incoherent helicity sum. B&O eq. 7 gives an amplitude proportional to (t^2 + u^2)/s, which corresponds to adding the two final-helicity amplitudes with the same sign. The unpolarised cross section in Bjerrum-Bohr et al. adds squares, (t^4 + u^4)/s^2, with no cross term. Whether eq. 7 is a definite physical final state or a double count is open (hidden premise 2). (refs: RT-03, RT-15, theory notes)
  - (c) N-enhancement vs IR-safe inclusive rates. Ai, Ellis & Pradler (2025 preprint) resum soft-graviton real and virtual processes in a GW background. ## 4. Constraints: theorems, bounds, no-go results, each with its assumptions
- [D-55] Kinematics of massless 2-body processes. For two on-shell massless gravitons, s = 2 w1 w2 (1 - cos theta_12) >= 0, with s = 0 only for parallel momenta. A 1 -> 2 decay of a massless particle into massless particles is collinear-only, where the g -> gamma gamma amplitude vanishes. Any process must therefore be 2 -> 2 with non-collinear partners from the same coherent state. B&O's own SM S3 argument (D-13) excludes the on-shell intermediate-graviton channel. Assumptions: flat space, on-shell massless gravitons and photons, tree level, no medium (in a plasma the photon is not massless; see D-40). Sources: brief (known constraint), B&O SM (preprint), theory notes (unsourced kinematics). Researcher's inference: for a spherical wave the local angular spread at r >> lambda scales like lambda/r, so s -> 0 as the wave becomes locally plane (hidden premise 5). This is unsourced and unverified. (refs: RT-04, theory notes)
- [D-56] Gibbons (1975) no-go. A quantised MASSIVE scalar field on a classical c-number exact plane sandwich GW of arbitrary profile and polarisation has no particle production, an exactly computed Feynman propagator, and a vanishing <T_mu nu> under any reasonable regularisation. Assumptions: massive scalar test field, exact plane wave (infinite transverse extent, no focusing), fixed background, no graviton fluctuations, no second-order back-reaction. It does not by itself cover photons, spherical waves or second-order wave packets. The ending "propagate without hindrance" was not confirmed. (refs: RT-08 [ACCESS corrected to abstract], RC-05 [unverifiable]) peer-reviewed
- [D-57] Khusnutdinov (2026). Closed-form massless scalar and vector Green functions on an exact plane GW coincide with the DeWitt-Schwinger expansion, so there is no massless particle production. Assumptions: exact plane wave, test fields, Green-function definition of particles. The collinear Bogolyubov tension is noted (D-51). The journal reference EPJ Plus 141, 532 was not checked on the publisher page, so peer-review status is per INSPIRE/arXiv metadata. (refs: RT-09, RC-06, RF-14)
- [D-58] Deser (1975), "Plane waves do not polarize the vacuum". Per a catalog snippet only, gravitational plane waves are undistorted by vacuum polarisation to all loop orders if no cosmological term is induced. Assumptions: exact plane wave, vacuum-polarisation (effective action) effects, no induced cosmological term. UNVERIFIED (search-summary, low confidence). It concerns in-in <T> on a plane wave, not 2 -> 2 fusion of non-collinear waves. (refs: RT-20) ACCESS: search-summary
- [D-59] Garriga-Verdaguer (1991). For massless and massive scalars on exact plane GWs, the quantum scattering cross section equals the classical geodesic one, and the stress tensor between scattering states diverges where geodesics focus. Assumptions: scalar test field, fixed plane-wave background. (refs: RT-18, RC-08; abstract, unverifiable by checker)
- [D-60] Redi & Tesi (2026 preprint). The pair yield from a classical metric perturbation is N_dec = 2 Im Gamma_1PI = (c_J/(2560 pi)) Int d^4q/(2pi)^4 theta(q^2) W_{mu nu rho sigma}(q) W^{mu nu rho sigma}(-q). Equivalently, it is -(c_J/(30720 pi)) Int theta(q^2) h Pi h, with c_J = 4/3 (conformal scalar), 4 (Weyl fermion), 16 (gauge field). Support is time-like q^2 > 0 only: "null-like tensor and vector perturbations do not produce particles". GW emission is controlled by the same correlator on light-like support. Assumptions: conformal fields, linear in h in the amplitude (O(h^2) in the yield), sub-horizon flat-space limit, early-Universe inhomogeneity setting. Applying it to B&O's O(h^4), N^2 process is an inference that Redi-Tesi do not make. (refs: RT-10, RC-10, RF-13)
- [D-61] B&O's own validity conditions: narrow band sigma_w/w_s << 1 (D-15); tau << dt ~ 1/sigma_w (D-14, D-21); for chirps tau << min(1/(eps w_s), t_eps) (D-27). At face value these fail at the nominal GW150914 point (D-42) and at astrophysical inspiral frequencies, except possibly a narrow high-mass BBH corner (D-48). (refs: RQ-08, RQ-15, RF-04, RF-06)
- [D-62] Plasma cutoff. A photon of frequency nu propagates only where n_e < (nu/8980 Hz)^2 cm^-3, i.e. n_e < 2.8e-4 cm^-3 at 150 Hz. Merger-band photons cannot traverse the warm ISM, the VLISM, the solar wind or the ionosphere. B&O's footnote 4 concedes absorption below kHz. In the pre-recombination fully ionised plasma, a 150 Hz-today photon is evanescent for 1+z > ~1300. B&O's constraint passages use vacuum photon energies "in the average sense" with no plasma correction found. Assumptions: cold unmagnetised plasma dispersion; quoted mean densities. (refs: D-07, D-40, RE-08, RQ-10, RF-09)
- [D-63] Primordial GW energy bound (D-33). Assumptions: GWs present at BBN (cosmological origin); radiation-like extra energy density; N_eff from Planck+BAO. It does NOT apply to the astrophysical post-BBN merger background, for which the direct LVK and PTA limits apply (D-34, D-35). (refs: RQ-04, RQ-05)
- [D-64] Photofission and cascade bounds need injected or cascade photon energies >~ 2.2 MeV (D) or ~20 MeV (4He). For GW-frequency photons (energy h nu) that requires nu >~ 5e20-5e21 Hz, far above binary frequencies (kHz). B&O's Fig. 3 photofission bounds concern hypothetical high-frequency sources only (inference). The cascade spectrum is non-universal below E_gamma <~ 10/T_keV MeV (Poulin & Serpico). B&O require delta_D < 0.03. (refs: RQ-13, RF-09, D-37)
- [D-65] Spectral-distortion regimes as B&O use them: mu for 1e5 <~ z <~ 3e6, y for 1e3 <~ z <~ 1e5, thermalisation before z ~ 3e6. Bounds |mu| < 9e-5 and |y| < 1.5e-5 (FIRAS). Planned headroom is ~3e3 in mu and ~1e4 in y (D-36). (refs: RQ-07, RF-09, RE-01)
