## Strongest attack

The strongest published evidence against C6 is Sawyer, PRL 124, 101301 (2020), arXiv:1910.08835. B&O cite it as [16], and the dossier lists it as [D-06, D-68] with the note that its conditions "were not examined". Sawyer argues that the same g g -> gamma gamma amplitudes, applied to dense multi-directional graviton clouds, drive a collective, Bose-stimulated "quantum break". Its rate is set by the forward amplitude, so it scales as G n, not as the G^2 n^2 of cross section times density. This holds for nearly isotropic multi-beam distributions and "with whatever phase relations obtain among the components".

If that channel operated, it would undercut three parts of C6:
- the claim that eq. 14 is *the* rate for an isotropic background, with the kinetic rate as the whole story;
- the predictions that the rate "scales as rho_GW^2" and "is independent of phase coherence";
- the clause "coherence adds nothing".

Per graviton, it would exceed the kinetic rate by 1e83 to 1e106 [calc: runs/2026-10-13-gravitational-wave-decay-photon-pairs/calc/falsifier-C6-1_collective.py].

**The attack fails on its own assumptions.** Sawyer's instability needs a growth exponent of order ln N, which is about 210–250 here, built up before expansion and plasma dephasing act.
- The growth rate is mu = 16 pi G n (natural units). Its ratio to the Hubble rate obeys mu/H = 6 (rho_GW/rho_tot)(H/omega) at any epoch, an identity checked numerically to ratio 1.000000.
- For sub-horizon modes (H/omega << 1), the case where a particle or kinetic description applies at all, mu/H << 1 in every e-fold.
- With rho_GW/rho_tot <~ 0.04 (DeltaN_eff = 0.3), the exponent accumulated after horizon entry is <~ 6 x 0.04 ≈ 0.24, about 1e3 below ln N.
- Today mu/H0 runs from 3e-25 (DeltaN_eff-limited background at 25 Hz) to 8e-14 (all of that energy placed at 1e-10 Hz).
- In the mean IGM at 25 Hz the photon plasma detuning, omega_p^2/(2 omega) = 2.0 rad/s, exceeds mu = 6.8e-43 s^-1 by about 42 orders of magnitude.
- At PTA frequencies omega < omega_p, so no photons are produced at all.

So for every background that observations allow, the collective channel never develops, and the incoherent kinetic rate, eq. 14, is the operative rate. The claim that it is negligibly small holds under either mechanism: even Sawyer's break time is >= 1e33 s, against t_H = 4.4e17 s.

**Second line of attack.** C6 itself flags that its "three independent derivations" share the toolkit's cross sections. I re-derived the eq. 14 coefficient from a published input instead. I started from the unpolarised gravitational Compton cross section, dsigma/dt = 2 pi G^2 (s^4+u^4)/(s^2 t^2) [D-01, which states it "agrees with Skobelev (1975)"], and crossed the helicity-summed |M|^2 s <-> t. This gives sigma_unpol(g g -> gamma gamma) = (2 pi/5) G^2 s and Gamma/V = (8 pi/15) G^2 rho^2 exactly (natural units). Sawyer's eq. (2) also prints the same magnitudes, "i8πG T²/S" and "i8πG U²/S". The coefficient therefore stands on literature, not only on the toolkit.

**Third line of attack: the "only" clause.**
- Sawyer's mechanism itself needs non-parallel beams. His effective coupling contains 2(1 - cos theta_pq), "peaked in favor of opposing momenta and vanishing for parallel momenta".
- Dorca–Verdaguer's particle creation [D-04] is for colliding waves, which is again multi-directional.
- Garani et al. (JHEP 08 (2025) 037) find massless production fixed by the stress-tensor two-point function of metric inhomogeneities, which needs time-like Fourier support. This is consistent with the collinear no-go.
- The one published single-wave sustained claim, the Jones & Singleton attenuation (C1 variant b), is excluded by the GW150914 amplitude calibration [M-CONSTRAINTS-14].

I found no source that gives sustained vacuum production for a single direction of propagation.

## In principle vs. in practice

- **In principle:**
  - The kinetic rate is the leading sustained rate for an isotropic background only while Bose-stimulated collective growth (mu = 16 pi G n) is slower than expansion and medium dephasing.
  - A hypothetical background with rho_GW ~ rho_tot at horizon-scale frequencies, or a dense near-source cloud as Sawyer considers, falls outside that regime. There, "independent of phase coherence" and "scales as rho^2" are not guaranteed.
  - Sawyer's mechanism has no published follow-up confirming or refuting it [empiricist: "no follow-up or observation"].
- **In practice:** every observationally allowed background satisfies mu/H << 1, so eq. 14 is the operative rate and is negligible by >= 1e71 against FIRAS [M-CONSTRAINTS-03]. Nothing in C6's practical conclusion changes.

## Corrections to details

1. **"Coherence adds nothing" and the prediction "independent of phase coherence" are stated too generally.** A published collective channel (Sawyer 2020) depends on coherent, Bose-stimulated build-up and scales as G n ∝ rho/omega. It is inert for all allowed backgrounds: accumulated exponent <~ 6 Omega_GW << ln N ~ 200, and mu/H0 <= 8e-14 today. C6 should state that condition rather than an unconditional independence. The unverified two-cap ratios 0.982 and 0.931 [M-IDEALIZER-14] do not address this channel.
2. **"The cross sections are not re-derived outside the toolkit"** is no longer a gap. The unpolarised cross section and the 8 pi/15 coefficient follow from the published unpolarised Compton cross section [D-01] by crossing, and Sawyer's eq. (2) prints the same amplitude magnitudes.
3. **Sawyer's printed helicity list is incomplete.** It assigns T^2/S to (2,-2) and U^2/S to (2,2), which is not symmetric under T <-> U. It cannot be used to apportion helicity channels; his arXiv comment notes that the amplitudes were redefined. Only its magnitudes are used here. The eq. 14 coefficient needs only the helicity-summed |M|^2, which crossing fixes.
4. **"Three independent derivations agree to 1e-8"** shared one input. The crossing route above is the first that does not.

## What survives and remaining doubt

The core survives on both counts:
- Sustained production needs non-collinear, multi-directional momenta; every published production mechanism found, including Sawyer's, vanishes for parallel momenta.
- B&O's eq. 14 is, by their own phase-randomised P-representation, exactly the incoherent kinetic rate (8 pi/15) G^2 rho^2 (natural units).
- That rate is negligible.

Remaining doubts:
- **Sawyer's collective channel.** It would modify the rate law for dense clouds, but my calculation shows it cannot develop for any allowed background.
- **Squeezed primordial background.** A relic background is a two-mode squeezed state with <a_k a_-k> ≠ 0. I did not compute whether these anomalous correlators change eq. 14. They feed only photon pairs with zero total momentum, so an O(1) change to the sustained rate seems unlikely, but this is not shown.
- **Validity domain.** The plasma closes the channel wherever omega < omega_p, as C6 already notes.
