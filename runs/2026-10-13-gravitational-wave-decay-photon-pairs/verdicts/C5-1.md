# Verdict on C5 (refuter 1, angle: evidence)
claim: Near a real source, photon pairs come mainly from the source's own dynamical field (off-shell linear channel during merger-ringdown, remnant Hawking emission), not from N^2 graviton fusion.
status: final
verdict: survives
basis: calculation
narrowed: none

Units: natural units (hbar = c = 1, G explicit, signature -,+,+,+) for amplitudes and the coefficient k; SI for astrophysical numbers, as marked.

## Strongest attack

C5's weak point, which the candidate itself flags, is that its main sub-hypothesis (a) rests on a dimensional estimate, P_lin ~ k (hbar w/E_Pl)^2 N, with k uncomputed. C5 needs k >~ 3.2e-4 to beat B&O's fusion total for GW150914 [M-EXAMINER-09]. I attacked from three sides:
1. **Is there a linear channel at all?** Does any published result say that a source's own field makes massless pairs at linear order? Does the null-support rule kill it?
2. **Is k large enough?**
3. **Does the Hawking clause belong in the claim?** Hawking quanta are not pairs at infinity, are not GW-induced, and come from a stationary horizon rather than a "dynamical field".

None of the three defeats the core claim. Computing k shows the attack fails: k = 3.39e-2, about 106 times C5's threshold. The published evidence also supports a linear channel. The Hawking clause is mis-classified, but it does not carry the core claim. Details follow.

## Evidence

**1. The rule for the linear channel is published, and it does not exclude off-shell near-source fields.**
- Redi & Tesi, arXiv:2606.28297 (preprint; fetched with fetch_text.py), p. 13: "GWs are produced provided that the source has a non-zero anisotropic stress with time dependence on a null-like support. Particle production instead requires less: only time dependence of the source on a time-like support."
- Their eq. (6), p. 5: "Ndec≡2ImΓ 1PI = cJ 2560π Z d4q (2π)4θ(q2)Wµνρσ(q)Wµνρσ(−q)."
- p. 6: "Notice that null-like tensor and vector perturbations do not produce particles." This excludes only the on-shell (radiative) field. The source's near-zone field is off-shell.
- p. 1: "particle production from inhomogeneous metric backgrounds practically reduces to gravitational scattering". This is the source → virtual graviton → pair process. Their setting is cosmological, but the master formula is stated for a generic initial state (p. 13). Applying it to a binary is my inference.

**2. A published calculation of linear-order production by a binary exists, and it has C5's scaling.**
- Rahvar 2026, arXiv:2606.17178 v2 (preprint; abstract page and PDF via fetch_text.py), abstract: "Treating a massless scalar field coupled to the binary metric, we compute the particle flux and radiated energy to leading order in the metric perturbation $h_{\mu\nu}$ ... In this regime we find a characteristic non-thermal, power-law emission with $dE/dt \propto M^{10/3}\omega^{16/3}$".
- This is exactly the scaling of C5's P_lin ∝ G hbar w^2 L_GW/c^5 with L_GW ∝ M^(10/3) w^(10/3). It is reproduced in [calc: runs/2026-10-13-gravitational-wave-decay-photon-pairs/calc/falsifier-C5-1_linear_k.py], Step 5: exponents 3.333 and 5.333.
- Rahvar's normalisation, A ~ 1e-30 erg/s, implies k_scalar ≈ 14. That is about 5e3 times my conformal-scalar value of 2.8e-3. His setup is titled "Weak-Field Metric Perturbations in the Radiation Zone" (p. 2), and an on-shell field should produce nothing under Redi–Tesi's rule. I therefore cite Rahvar only for the existence and scaling of the channel, not for its size.
- Chakraborty et al. 2024, PRD 110, L021701 (arXiv abstract via lit_search): "we show that scalar particles may indeed be produced in a perturbative manner" by a linear GW profile [D-52].

**3. The coefficient k, computed** [calc: runs/2026-10-13-gravitational-wave-decay-photon-pairs/calc/falsifier-C5-1_linear_k.py, log beside it].
- **Step 1 (identity).** For a conserved T and h-bar = 16 pi G T/q^2, the linearised W.W* = (1/2)(16 pi G)^2 (T.T* - |T^mu_mu|^2/3). The ratio is 1.0000000000 at four random time-like q. Redi–Tesi's eq. (6) with c_J = 16 therefore gives N_lin = (8 pi/5) G^2 Int_{q0>0, q time-like} d^4q/(2pi)^4 (T.T* - |T|^2/3). This is the massive-spin-2 sum: the off-shell graviton "decays" to gamma gamma.
- **Step 2 (normalisation).** With the same conserved T, the graviton-number formula dN_GW/dt = (G w/pi) Int dOmega (T.T* - |T|^2/2) reproduces the quadrupole luminosity (32/5) G mu^2 a^4 Omega^6. The ratio is 1.000000.
- **Step 3 (k).** For a long-wavelength quadrupole source (|q| a << 1), k = (dN_lin/dt)/(G w^2 dN_GW/dt) = 3.385e-2. The value is the same for the rotating circular binary and the linear quadrupole, and it converged (nr = 12 and 24 give the same value to 7 digits).
- **Step 4 (GW150914).** P_lin = 3.1e-4 pairs (SI count, E = 2 Msun c^2, 150 Hz). B&O's in-model total is 2.9e-6 pairs, so the ratio is 107. Against C3's retarded-field fusion range (0.02–0.1 of B&O), the ratio is 1.1e3 to 5.4e3. C5's threshold k >~ 3.2e-4 is exceeded by a factor of 106. In the GW170817 100 Hz bin, P_BO/P_lin = 3.0e-8/k = 8.9e-7.
- **Step 6 (peak powers, SI).** At the GW150914 peak the linear-channel pair power is 3.1e-33 W (pair rate 3.2e-2 s^-1). B&O's unstimulated photon power is 2.8e-35 W. The remnant's Hawking photon power, at 341 times B&O's [M-CONSTRAINTS-11], is 9.5e-33 W.

**4. The attack on the Hawking clause is internal, not evidential.**
- The candidate itself says "Hawking emission of the remnant is a separate process, not GW-induced". Yet its heading lists Hawking emission under "the source's own dynamical field".
- Hawking emission comes from the stationary Kerr horizon. Its quanta reach infinity unpaired, with their partners behind the horizon (standard; no verbatim source fetched).
- Rahvar 2026, p. 8, finds for the inspiral: "So in the weak field approximation of particle production, the Hawking radiation dominates the flux from the particle production". That supports the claim's contrast with fusion, but it shows the two non-fusion contributions are of different kinds.

## Corrections to details

- **k is computed, not open.** For a long-wavelength quadrupole source and photons (c_J = 16), k = 3.39e-2 (natural units). The ratio P_BO/P_lin for GW150914 becomes 3.2e-4/k = 9.3e-3, so the linear channel exceeds B&O's own fusion total about 100-fold. This counts N_dec as pairs. If Redi–Tesi's N_dec counts particles instead, the margin halves to about 50, which changes nothing.
- **The "inspiral case fails" item in the evidence against is wrong.**
  - M-MECHANIST-10 checked the local phase-speed ratio q0/q_phi of a single rigidly rotating harmonic. That is not the Fourier support of the source.
  - A compact source radiating at w has T~(w, |q| = w) ≠ 0. Since T~ is smooth in |q| for |q| a << 1, it is also non-zero on the time-like region |q| < w, where T~_ij → -(w^2/2) Q~_ij.
  - The calculation gives the same k for the rotating circular binary as for the linear quadrupole (Step 3). Rahvar's inspiral calculation is a published case of this.
  - The linear channel therefore operates during the inspiral as well, not only during merger-ringdown. This widens C5's scope rather than narrowing it.
- **The Hawking clause should be reclassified.** Remnant Hawking emission is not "the source's own dynamical field" and does not produce photon pairs at infinity. It is a stationary, unpaired background that runs before and after the event: the progenitors emit it too, so it has no per-event total.
  - In photon power at the GW150914 peak it is about 3 times the linear channel (9.5e-33 W vs 3.1e-33 W).
  - It belongs in the count of "photons near the source", not in "pairs from the dynamical field".
- **Sub-hypothesis (c)** (merger-ringdown at O(h^2)) is subsumed: at linear order the full source T at time-like q already dominates.

## What survives and remaining doubt

The core claim stands: near a real compact-binary source, the leading photon-pair channel is linear in the source's off-shell field (amplitude O(G T), yield ∝ N), and it exceeds N^2 fusion by about 1e2 (against B&O's in-state total) to 1e3–5e3 (against the retarded-field estimate). This holds in principle.

Remaining doubts:
- k assumes linearised gravity and a long-wavelength source. At merger w a ~ 1 and the field is strong. A form factor or strong-field correction of O(1)–O(10) would not close a margin of at least 100, but a numerical-relativity evaluation of the Redi–Tesi functional has not been done.
- The cross term between the linear and fusion amplitudes is of order sqrt(P_lin P_fus), which is subleading.
- **In practice** none of these channels is observable. Photons at ~150–300 Hz lie below the interstellar plasma frequency (about 1.6 kHz) [M-MECHANIST-11], and a few tenths of a millipair per event is unobservable anyway. In an ISM environment, the ranking of channels is a statement about vacuum idealisations.
- The three supporting sources are preprints, apart from Chakraborty et al.
