## Strongest attack
C5's argument depends on one uncomputed number: the coefficient k in P_lin = k (hbar w/E_Pl)^2 N = k G w E_GW / c^5 (SI; in natural units k G w E_GW). The candidate itself says the claim fails if k << 3e-4 for GW150914 (math/examiner.md calls the scaling "verified arithmetic on an unverified scaling"). So the strongest physics attack is to compute k from first principles and see whether it falls below 3e-4. Two lesser attacks are also checked:
- that photons, being conformally coupled, get no pairs at linear order in h;
- that a quasi-circular inspiral has no time-like Fourier support (mechanist F10a).

The attack fails. In the long-wavelength (quadrupole) limit, k is computed exactly. It is 67/(630 pi) = 0.0339, about 107 times the threshold. Both lesser attacks also fail.

## Evidence
All in natural units (hbar = c = 1, Heaviside–Lorentz; g = eta + h, kappa^2 = 32 pi G) unless marked SI. [calc: runs/2026-10-13-gravitational-wave-decay-photon-pairs/calc/falsifier-C5-0_linear_k.py]

**1. The process exists for photons.**
- Take a traceless spin-2 metric perturbation at time-like q, evaluated in the rest frame of q.
- Its linearised Weyl tensor obeys W(q).W(-q) = 0.5 m^4 |h^TT|^2, which is non-zero (log Part 2: "[0.5 0.5 0.5 0.5]" for four random TT tensors).
- So Maxwell's conformal invariance does not forbid the channel. Only the conformal (trace) mode is inert.
- The Redi–Tesi rule [D-60] gives pairs from exactly this time-like Weyl content.

**2. Normalisation, by two independent routes that agree to 1e-15.**
- Both routes give P = (8 pi/5) G^2 Int d^4q/(2pi)^4 theta(q0) theta(-q^2) |T^TT(q)|^2, where T is the source's conserved stress tensor and h^TT = 16 pi G T^TT/m^2.
- Route A uses explicit Maxwell stress-tensor matrix elements. They give the width of a mass-m spin-2 state into gamma gamma as Gamma = kappa^2 m^3/(320 pi) (log: "ratio computed/expected = 0.9999999999999987"; this is the standard Randall–Sundrum graviton width m^3/(80 pi Lambda^2) with 1/Lambda = kappa/2). Writing the off-shell propagator as a spectral integral then gives A = 5.0265 = 8 pi/5.
- Route B uses Redi–Tesi with c_J = 16 [D-60], with theta(q^2) covering both signs of q0. It gives "A = 5.026548245743669, ratio B/A = 1.0000000000000013".

**3. k for a quadrupole source.**
- Set T^ij(w, q) ≈ S^ij for |q| <= w; this holds when |q| a <= w a = 2v << 1. Take T^0i and T^00 from conservation.
- Then |T^TT|^2 = T.T* - |tr T|^2/3. Its integral over the time-like ball |q| < w is 4 pi (67/315) w^3 |S|^2.
- In the same Fourier convention, P_GW = (G w^2/pi) Int dOmega Lambda(S) = (G w^2/pi)(8 pi/5)|S|^2. This was checked against 32/5 G mu^2 a^4 Omega^6 (log: "computed/(32/5 ...) = 0.9999999999999665").
- Hence k = Pdot_pairs/(G w P_GW) = 67/(630 pi) = 0.033852. The value is the same for a circular binary and a linear quadrupole (log: "k = 0.033852" for both), because the ball integral is rotation-invariant.

**4. Sizes.** Inputs are the M-EXAMINER-09 values: P_lin/k = 9.27e-3 and 1.87e-6; P_BO = 2.92e-6 and 5.60e-14.
- GW150914 (E = 2 Msun c^2, 150 Hz): P_lin = 3.1e-4 pairs against N^2 fusion 2.9e-6, so P_lin/P_BO = 107.
- GW170817 100 Hz bin: P_lin = 6.3e-8 against 5.6e-14, a ratio of 1.1e6.
- A profile-free bound (SI) compares B&O's fusion total, rewritten as (12 pi/25) G^2 w Int L^2 dt / c^10 [M-IDEALIZER-04], with P_lin = k G w Int L dt / c^5:
  - fusion/linear <= 44.6 L_peak/(c^5/G) = 0.044 for GW150914 (L_peak/L_P = 9.9e-4);
  - fusion would win only for a luminosity-weighted mean L > 0.022 c^5/G = 8.1e50 W, which is above any observed or plausible GW luminosity.
- So the linear channel dominates N^2 fusion for every astrophysical source, not just GW150914.

## Corrections to details
- **k is no longer uncomputed.** In the leading post-Minkowskian, quadrupole order it is k = 67/(630 pi) ≈ 0.034. C5's own survival test, k >~ 3e-4, is passed by a factor of ~107. The merger itself (v ~ c, |q| a ~ 1) is outside the long-wavelength approximation, so k there carries an O(1) uncertainty, not orders of magnitude.
- **"The inspiral case fails" (C5's evidence against, from mechanist F10a / M-MECHANIST-10) is wrong.**
  - The source's T(w, q) is analytic in q. It is non-zero on the light-cone sphere |q| = w, since that is the radiated GW.
  - So it cannot vanish inside the ball |q| < w. The interior carries the same multipole moments, and k above is v-independent at leading order.
  - M-MECHANIST-10 tests a local phase-velocity statement about one rigidly rotating harmonic (q0/q_phi = Omega r). That is not a Fourier-support statement about a compact source.
  - Consequence: the linear channel runs through the inspiral too, not only the merger-ringdown. An illustrative inspiral-only yield (assumed 1 Msun c^2 radiated near 50 Hz; SI) is 5.2e-5 pairs, 18 times B&O's whole-event fusion total.
  - The parenthetical "during merger-ringdown" is therefore too narrow. This broadens the claim rather than narrowing it.
- **"Remnant Hawking emission" does not belong in the mechanism list.**
  - It is thermal single-photon emission by a quasi-stationary horizon: the partner quanta are inside the horizon, not a photon pair outside. It is not caused by the source's dynamics, and the progenitors emitted it throughout their lives.
  - Its 341x luminosity ratio [M-CONSTRAINTS-11] is a fair comparison of photon output, but not of GW-induced pairs. The core claim rests on the linear channel alone, which suffices.
- **The prediction "yield scales as N, not N^2" stands.** P_lin = k G w E_GW / c^5 (SI) is linear in E_GW and contains no hbar. Pair number from this channel is hbar-independent.

## What survives and remaining doubt
The core claim survives and is strengthened. Near a real compact-binary source, photon pairs come from the source's own field through the linear-in-h off-shell graviton channel: P_lin ≈ 0.034 G w E_GW / c^5 (SI). This exceeds B&O's N^2 fusion total by ~1e2 for GW150914 and ~1e6 for a GW170817 inspiral bin. Fusion could compete only above about 0.02 c^5/G in luminosity.

Remaining doubts:
- The merger-ringdown phase lies outside the quadrupole and linearised approximation. An NR evaluation of the Redi–Tesi functional with the full time-like Weyl content (C5's decisive test (ii)) would fix k there to O(1), but cannot plausibly cut it by the factor 100 needed to reverse the ranking.
- Linear and fusion amplitudes are parts of the same time-like Weyl tensor and interfere. With the fusion amplitude ~10x smaller in modulus at GW150914, the interference correction is at most of order ±20%.
- All yields remain unobservably small (C4's conversion and plasma arguments apply equally).
