# Verdict on C3 (refuter 0, angle: physics)
claim: Eq. 8 is right arithmetic for the wrong state: the per-packet yield of a time-symmetric, imploding-then-re-expanding graviton shell, so a real source's outgoing wave yields substantially less.
status: final
verdict: weakened
basis: calculation
narrowed: B&O's eq. 8 is the yield of a free, time-symmetric (Pauli-Jordan) graviton shell, and the part a real source's wave makes in the far zone (kr >~ 2-5) is a small fraction of it. Whether a real source's total yield is smaller or larger is not established: in the same toy, a quadrupole (l = 2) retarded field gives more than the time-symmetric shell, and that excess comes from the near zone, r < 1/k.

## Strongest attack
C3's "substantially less" rests on one calculation done in the coherent field: the scalar monopole (l = 0) contact toy. It gives a retarded / time-symmetric ratio of 1/12 for a contact vertex and 0.020 for p = 2 [M-EXAMINER-03]. Its other two inputs are kinetic (local graviton-gas) estimates, and they count only the region beyond kr = 1 [M-IDEALIZER-08, M-MECHANIST-04].

Gravitational waves have no monopole or dipole radiation. Their lowest radiative multipole is l = 2. The retarded l = 2 field h_2^(1)(kr) has a near-zone part that grows like (kr)^-3 inside r < 1/k. The time-symmetric (Pauli-Jordan) field 2i j_2(kr), by contrast, is regular there. The monopole is the one case whose near zone is only 1/r, mild enough that the whole-space ratio stays finite and below 1.

I repeated the examiner's toy with angular structure. The setup is the same: contact vertex j = phi^2, massless pair, sum-frequency q0 = 2 w, the same late-time outgoing wave, natural units with w = 1. It reproduces 1/12 for l = 0. For l = 2 it **reverses the inequality**: the retarded source yields more than the time-symmetric shell, by a factor that scales as (ka)^-6 with source radius a. Even the retarded yield from beyond kr ~ 1 is 1.5-2 times the entire time-symmetric yield. It falls below that yield only beyond kr ~ 2 (0.06-0.16), and well below it by kr ~ 5 (0.003-0.03).

So C3's prediction, and its own decisive test ("a spin-2 in-in calculation with the retarded quadrupole source ... C3 predicts 0.02-0.1"), is contradicted by the l = 2 version of the very toy that supplies its evidence. The first half of the core claim stands: eq. 8 belongs to the time-symmetric state. The "so ... yields substantially less" holds only for the far-zone part of the wave.

## Corrections to details
- "Examiner toy: 1/12 ... (exact)" holds only for the monopole. For l = 2 the same toy gives a ratio above 1 for any source radius ka <~ 1.1, and about (ka)^-6 below that.
- The figures "0.014-0.024" and "0.069" beyond 1/k come from kinetic, local-stress-tensor estimates [M-MECHANIST-04; M-IDEALIZER-08]. The coherent l = 2 toy beyond kR ~ 1 gives 1.5-2 relative to the time-symmetric total, so these figures depend on the model at kr ~ 1. They agree with the coherent toy only from kr >~ 2-5 outward.
- The evidence-against item "an O(1) share could be made" at r < 1/w understates the problem. In the l = 2 toy the near zone dominates, by a factor (ka)^-6.

## What survives and remaining doubt
- These parts survive:
  - The state identity: the Pauli-Jordan field is a free solution, minus-advanced before the source and retarded after it [M-EXAMINER-01, M-MECHANIST-07].
  - Eq. 8 is the yield of that time-symmetric shell, made in the head-on in x out crossing region.
  - The far-zone yield of a real outgoing wave (kr >~ 2-5) is small and converges [M-EXAMINER-04/05; this calc's l = 2 beyond-R values].
  - B&O's number therefore cannot be credited to a propagating wave.
- What fails is the inference to a smaller real-source total. The quantity C3's decisive test names (the retarded quadrupole total) comes out larger in the l = 2 toy, and it is made in the near zone. That is where the free-graviton model fails and where C5's "source's own field" channel lives.
- Doubts about my own attack:
  - The toy is scalar with a contact vertex, not spin-2 Einstein-Maxwell.
  - In GR the near-zone l = 2 field is the Newtonian tidal potential, Riemann ~ GQ/r^5. Derivative couplings would sharpen the near-zone dominance, not remove it, but this was not computed.
  - The interior model is crude. Hole and regular continuation differ by about a factor 2.
  - So I do not claim that a real source yields more than eq. 8. I claim only that C3's "substantially less" for the real-source total is not supported, and that the evidence it rests on reverses for the physical multipole.
