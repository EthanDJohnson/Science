## Strongest attack

C4's distinguishing content is its magnitude: the real-source total is "of the order of B&O's per-packet total". Its own "if false" list names the alternative "another channel dominates (C5)", and the slate leaves that alternative open because the linear-channel coefficient k was "uncomputed" (math/examiner.md, "Not checkable": "F8's P_lin ~ k (hbar w/E_Pl)^2 N is a dimensional estimate with an uncomputed coefficient k"). C5 states the test: "C5 survives if k >~ 3e-4 for GW150914."

I computed k. The source's own metric perturbation, at linear order, has time-like Fourier support, so it makes photon pairs through the Redi–Tesi functional (c_J = 16 for photons). For a circular binary in the slow-motion limit, k = 0.034 (0.017 if Redi–Tesi's eq. 6 counts only q0 > 0). That is 60–110 times C5's threshold. Natural units are used throughout, and the dimensionless results use SI inputs:

- **Linear channel:** N_lin = k G E_GW w/c^5 per event. It is O(G^2) in the source.
- **B&O fusion total:** P_BO = (4/25)(G E w/c^5)(G E sigma/c^5). It is O(G^4) in the source, and is down by the further factor (4/25) G E sigma/c^5 / k.

| Case | N_lin (pairs) | P_BO (pairs) | P_BO / N_lin |
|---|---|---|---|
| GW150914, B&O inputs (E = 2 Msun c^2, 150 Hz, sigma = 200 rad/s) | 3.1e-4 (1.6e-4 for k/2) | 2.9e-6 | 9.3e-3 (1.9e-2) |
| GW150914, LVK E = 3 Msun c^2 | 4.7e-4 | 6.6e-6 | 1.4e-2 |
| GW170817, 100 Hz bin | 6.3e-8 | 5.6e-14 | 8.8e-7 |

So a real source's near-field photon-pair total is set by the linear channel. At the merger it is two orders of magnitude above B&O's per-packet total, and in inspiral bins six orders above it. Fusion contributes at most about 1–2% at GW150914.

The fusion share itself also undercuts "of the order of". In the verified toy calculations, the retarded (physical) field's whole-space fusion yield is 0.020 of the time-symmetric one for the graviton-like vertex |M|^2 ∝ s^2 (M-EXAMINER-03). The unverified pattern factors 0.23–0.56 multiply that further. The fusion piece is therefore likely 1e-2 to 1e-1 of B&O's total, while the full total is about 1e2 times it. Neither piece is "of the order of" B&O's number. The agreement C4 predicts ("within a factor ~3 of eq. 8 x T_eff") fails in both directions.

The attack does not touch the qualitative core:
- the total is finite;
- it is made near the source, so it is distance-independent;
- it is far too small to observe (about 1e-4 pairs per event, at 75–150 Hz, below every plasma cutoff).

That is why the verdict is "weakened", not "refuted".

## Corrections to details

- C4's prediction "the retarded in-in total per event lies within a factor ~3 of eq. 8 x T_eff" is wrong once all channels at leading order are counted: the total is about 1e2 times it at GW150914 and about 1e6 times it in inspiral bins. The fusion piece alone is likely 0.02 x (pattern factor) of it (M-EXAMINER-03).
- "It scales as (r_m/l)^2 (w/sigma), and for chirps it tracks w Int L_GW^2 dt". That law holds for the fusion piece only. The leading total tracks Int w dE_GW = Int w L_GW dt: it is linear in L, not quadratic.
- The Hawking comparison (B&O eq. 13, "very weak") is unchanged in kind.

## What survives and remaining doubt

**Survives:**
- finiteness and near-source localisation of the yield (M-EXAMINER-04);
- distance independence;
- unobservability, by a wide margin even at N ~ 5e-4 pairs per GW150914-like event, with photons at w/2 ~ 75–150 Hz evanescent in any plasma with n_e > 2.8e-4 cm^-3 (D-62).

**Doubts in my own attack:**
- k is computed at leading order in v. At GW150914's merger, wa ~ 2v ~ 1, so k carries an O(1) uncertainty there; the GW170817 bin (v ~ 0.16) is safe.
- An overall factor of 2 in the Redi–Tesi normalisation is unresolved (lambda = 0.5 against the 1 that strict eq. 6/eq. 39 consistency would require). It moves k between 0.017 and 0.068.
- The formula is flat-space and linear in h, while about half the merger signal comes from strong field.

None of these closes a gap of a factor 50–200 at the merger, or of 1e6 in the inspiral. Fusion would dominate only if strong-field effects suppressed the linear channel by more than 1e-2 while leaving the O(G^4) fusion term intact. No calculation suggests that.
