# Verdict on C2 (refuter 2, angle: scale)
claim: No vacuum decay: an outgoing gravitational wave's photon-pair production converges within a few wavelengths of its source, so there is no lifetime, no path depletion and nothing observable.
status: final
verdict: survives
basis: calculation
narrowed: none

## Strongest attack

From the scale angle, a null claim fails if some real configuration makes the far-zone yield grow with path length, or makes it large enough to matter. I tried five routes. Each is computed in [calc: runs/2026-10-13-gravitational-wave-decay-photon-pairs/calc/falsifier-C2-2_scale.py]; SI units unless marked.

1. **Drop the toys and use only the worst case.** C2's weakest support is that its R^-3 law comes from scalar toys and an unsourced Fresnel spread (C2's own "evidence against"). So I bounded the yield beyond R with no wave-optics input at all. The bound uses three ingredients:
   - the shell energy density falls as rho = E/(4 pi r^2 c T);
   - the kinetic rate per volume is hbar-free, K G^2 rho^2/c^7;
   - K takes its maximum: all gravitons split into two head-on, opposite-helicity beams (8 pi/5), times a pattern-peak factor of 2.5, so K = 4 pi.

   The result is P(>R) <= (G E/c^4)^2/(c T R). The head-on coefficient matches M-MECHANIST-03's closed form exactly (ratio 1, printed). The bound falls only as 1/R. So even with the angular suppression thrown away, dP/dr ∝ r^-2 and the yield converges. Geometric dilution of rho^2 alone guarantees this: no lifetime can arise from a single outgoing wave's self-fusion.
2. **Lensing re-crossing.** Lensing can re-create non-collinear, overlapping components far from the source. For bursts it cannot:
   - a galaxy lens's delay, 4GM/c^3 = 2.0e6 s, is far longer than T = 5 ms, so the image packets never overlap;
   - a stellar lens has w·4GM/c^3 = 0.019, which is the wave-optics regime with no separate images.

   For continuous sources the images do overlap, but at theta <= 1e-5 rad the rate is suppressed by 6e-22 relative to head-on.
3. **Static curvature of compact objects passed along the path.** Graviton plus the static field of a compact object gives a pair (a non-vacuum, Gertsenshtein-like background process). As a dimensional estimate with k = 1 it gives N(hbar w/E_P)^2 (r_s w/c)^2 x tau = 9.3e-3 x 8.6e-5 x 5.2e-23 ≈ 4e-29 pairs over 410 Mpc. This number is labelled dimensional; its coefficient is uncomputed.
4. **Cross-fusion with the ambient stochastic GW background.** This is the one route that gives a sustained rate in vacuum, so a formal lifetime. Per wave graviton the rate is Gamma = (16 pi/15) hbar G^2 w rho_bg/c^7.
   - At DeltaN_eff = 0.3 (rho_bg = 2.84e-15 J/m^3) and 150 Hz, the lifetime is 5.2e124 s, or 6.8e115 s with CMB stimulation (1 + 2f = 7.57e8).
   - The second figure matches M-CONSTRAINTS-03's tau_stim = 6.81e115 s, an independent cross-check.
   - The energy lost over a Hubble time is 6.4e-99.

   This is a multi-directional ensemble effect, which C2 explicitly leaves to C6. It does not touch the wave's own self-fusion.
5. **Required vs achievable.** The worst-case fraction of GW energy converted beyond R = lambda, at any distance and with stimulation, is 1.2e-75. A standard-siren path-loss test reaches about 1e-2, a shortfall of 8e72.

None of these defeats the core claim. The attack that comes closest (route 1) shows something else: "within a few wavelengths" is a toy-based localisation. The scale-robust statement is "converges at least as fast as 1/R".

## In principle vs. in practice

- **In principle:** self-fusion of a single outgoing wave converges for any angular spread, even a constant one (bound A; consistent with M-MECHANIST-04's condition a > -1/4). So no self-decay lifetime and no self-induced path depletion exist. A formal, sustained loss exists only through crossing other GWs (route 4), with a lifetime >= 6.8e115 s ≈ 1.6e98 Hubble times.
- **In practice:** every route's yield or energy fraction is short of any measurement by more than 70 orders of magnitude (route 5). Hz–kHz photons are also forbidden in the ISM, solar wind and ionosphere [D-62]. "Nothing observable" stands.

## Corrections to details

- **"Within a few wavelengths" is toy-dependent.** It rests on the R^-3 toys (M-CONSTRAINTS-08, M-EXAMINER-04). The scale-robust statement is weaker: production beyond R is at most (G E/c^4)^2/(c T R), a 1/R tail. In that worst case the bound at R = lambda (2.9e-6 pairs) equals B&O's whole per-packet total (2.9e-6, M-IDEALIZER-09), and 10% of it would lie beyond 10 lambda. A physical outgoing wave cannot be head-on, so the toys' localisation within ~lambda is plausible, but "converges" is what is established without them.
- **"No lifetime" needs its scope stated.** For a wave crossing the ambient stochastic background there is a formal, unobservable lifetime >= 6.8e115 s (stimulated, DeltaN_eff = 0.3; 5.2e124 s unstimulated). C2's scope bullet excludes this (C6), but the heading sentence does not.
- **The idealizer tail figures quoted in C2 differ slightly from the checked row.** C2 quotes 7.5e-10, 1.8e-24 and 2.9e-66 pairs; M-IDEALIZER-09 prints 8.1e-10, 1.94e-24 and 3.2e-66 (the slate says these come from the corrected asymptote). The difference is O(10%) and immaterial.

## What survives and remaining doubt

The core survives. A single outgoing GW's own pair production has a finite total. No sustained rate, lifetime or path depletion arises from its self-fusion, and no consequence is observable by any margin. The robust form of the convergence rests only on 1/r^2 dilution of rho^2, and it is independent of the disputed angular-spread law.

Remaining doubt:
- **Where the yield is made.** That it is made within ~lambda depends on the unperformed spin-2 in-in calculation (C2's decisive test).
- **Memory and tails.** Their contributions are not computed. Any such component still has energy density falling as 1/r^2 outside the source, so bound A caps it as well, unless it carries energy not counted in E.
- **Route 3.** The static-field channel is a dimensional estimate with an uncomputed coefficient. At about 1e-29 pairs it would need a coefficient around 1e28 to matter for the count, and even then it would be a background-field process, not vacuum decay.
