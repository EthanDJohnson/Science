# Verdict on C1 (refuter 0, angle: physics)
claim: A freely propagating gravitational wave decays into photon pairs in vacuum at B&O's eq. 8 rate, sustained along its path, giving a finite lifetime and cumulative path depletion.
status: final
verdict: refuted

Units: natural units (hbar = c = 1) in Part A and C of the calculation; SI for GW150914 numbers (s, J, m, pairs). B&O = Blas & Oller, arXiv:2607.24930 v2. "Pairs" = photon pairs.

## Strongest attack

**B&O's own model gives a finite total for all time, and makes it within a few 1/sigma_w of the focus. A rate that is sustained along the path is ruled out by the very S-matrix element that eq. 8 comes from.**

1. **The total is finite.** Eq. 8 comes from an in-out S-matrix amplitude, which integrates over all of spacetime (t from -inf to +inf, and every point along the path out to infinity).
   - In the narrow-band model the pair amplitude depends on the pair energy K0 only through C(K0) = Int f(a) f(K0 - a) da (B&O S6).
   - The total is J_f = Int |C|^2 dK0, which is finite for any finite-bandwidth packet: 2 sigma^3/3 for a top hat (natural units).
   - B&O's step "2 pi delta(0) -> T" just names this finite number T_eff = 2 pi J_f / I_f^4 = 4 pi/(3 sigma) [calc, Part A: 83.77 vs 4 pi/(3 x 0.05) = 83.78].
   - A process that kept going along the path would make the in-out probability grow as R x T without bound. B&O's does not.
2. **Production is confined in time.** K0 is conjugate to the time at which the pair is made. By the convolution theorem, FT[C](t) = a(t)^2, so the production profile in time is |a(t)|^4. Parseval holds: J_f = (1/2 pi) Int |a|^4 dt, which agrees with the direct convolution to 3e-4.
   - Top hat: the fraction made at |t| > T is 3/(pi (sigma T)^3). Numerically it is 9.385e-7 at sigma T = 100 against 9.549e-7 analytic, and 9.43e-10 at 1000 against 9.55e-10. These are identical for sigma/w = 0.05 and 0.02.
   - Gaussian: the fraction is erfc(sqrt2 sigma T) = 1.5e-23 at sigma T = 5.
   - So the model's own production rises and falls with the packet's focus and lasts about 1/sigma_w. It is not a decay rate.
3. **GW150914 at B&O's inputs** (SI: E = 2 Msun c^2, f = 150 Hz, sigma_w = 200 rad/s):
   - R = 1.398e-4 pairs/s, T_eff = 0.0209 s, total P = 2.93e-6 pairs.
   - The sustained reading over 410 Mpc (t_prop = 4.22e16 s) gives 5.90e12 pairs, an over-count of 2.0e18.
   - Pairs that the model itself makes at |t| > t_prop: 2.93e-6 x 1.59e-57 = 4.7e-63.
   - Energy fraction removed over the packet's whole history: 1.6e-84. The candidate's "path depletion" is 3.3e-66, so its depletion is not just unobservable: inside the model it is 18 orders of magnitude larger than the model allows.
4. **The sustained count is not Lorentz invariant.** Boost along the propagation direction. Along the null ray, Delta t' / Delta t = w'/w = sigma'/sigma = k = gamma (1 - beta) (sympy, verified), and N is invariant.
   - A sustained count R Delta t, with R = 3 G^2 N^2 w^3 sigma^2/(25 pi), scales as **k^6**. The number of photon pairs made between two events is frame-independent, so for a freely propagating wave, with no source or medium to pick a frame, the count must be zero.
   - B&O's total P ~ G^2 N^2 w^3 sigma scales as k^4. So eq. 8 holds only in the packet's own centre-of-momentum frame. That frame exists because B&O's state has zero net momentum (an isotropic shell that focuses), not because it is a wave propagating in one direction.
5. **B&O's own validity condition contradicts their reading.** Their S6 rests on T ~ 1/sigma_w, "provided 1/σω≫τ". Yet S7 integrates the same rate to t ~ tau_h = 1.3e82 s, so that N_gamma(tau_h) = N_0. At GW150914, tau sigma_w = 7.2e5, so the condition fails [M-DIALECTICIAN-01].

Independent physics routes in the math checks reach the same conclusion for a realistic outgoing wave:
- an exact plane wave has Fourier support only on q^2 = 0, so it yields nothing [M-EXAMINER-06, verified];
- in the geometric-optics limit Gamma(x) = (2 pi/5) G^2 T_mn T^mn vanishes for collinear (null) stress [M-IDEALIZER-06, verified];
- the retarded outgoing yield beyond R falls as (wR)^-3 (R^-2 for a sharp window), never as R^+1 [M-EXAMINER-04, M-CONSTRAINTS-08, M-IDEALIZER-07/08, verified].

The candidate's own decisive test needs d ln P(>R)/d ln R = +1, against -3 (or at most -2) in every toy.

Variant (b) (Jones–Singleton, decay length ~1e11 m) fails on observation as well. GW150914, seen at 410 Mpc within 11.29% amplitude calibration, needs an e-folding length > 5.3e25 m. The variant predicts an amplitude factor exp(-6.3e13) [M-CONSTRAINTS-14, verified].

## In principle vs. in practice

- **In principle:** refuted. Inside B&O's own in-out model, production is a finite per-packet total made within about 1/sigma_w of the packet's focus. A sustained count along a null path is excluded by Lorentz covariance, since it scales as k^6 under a boost along the ray. For a real outgoing wave, the yield made beyond R falls as R^-3, not +R.
- **In practice:** even read at face value, the claimed depletion (3.3e-66 over 410 Mpc) is unobservable. The large-attenuation variant (b) is excluded by GW150914's detection at the GR amplitude.

## Corrections to details

- B&O's GW150914 inputs have sigma_w/w = 0.21, which is outside the narrow-band model (the toolkit refuses sigma/w > 0.1). The GW150914 numbers above are therefore B&O's formula evaluated at their inputs, not a controlled model result.
- The candidate's "evidence against" quotes the collinear factor correctly as pi/5 (M-DIALECTICIAN-10). DIALECTICIAN-E's 2 pi/5 is the uncorrected form.
- My time-profile argument uses the narrow-band factorisation (M evaluated at K0 = 2w). Corrections of order sigma/w change the shape of |a(t)|^4 but not the fact that the total is finite.

## What survives and remaining doubt

Nothing of the core claim survives. Two things do stand, but neither is part of the claim:
- the within-model arithmetic of eq. 8 (3/(25 pi) and the rest);
- the existence of tree-level g g -> gamma gamma for non-collinear gravitons.

Both belong to the per-packet or near-source reading (C3–C5) and to the multi-directional ensemble (C6), not to a sustained vacuum decay.

Remaining doubt:
- No spin-2 in-in calculation of the retarded field of a real source has been published. The R^-3 law beyond R comes from scalar and kinetic toys.
- Nonlinear memory and tails have not been computed.

That doubt cannot rescue C1, for two reasons:
- the finite-total and Lorentz-scaling arguments act on B&O's own expression, whatever the far-zone exponent;
- any exponent below -1 already converges.
