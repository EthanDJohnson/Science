# Verdict on C5 (refuter 2, angle: scale)
claim: Near a real source, photon pairs come mainly from the source's own dynamical field (off-shell linear channel during merger-ringdown, remnant Hawking emission), not from N^2 graviton fusion.
status: final
verdict: survives
basis: calculation
narrowed: none

## Strongest attack

C5's argument depends on one number it never computed: the linear-channel coefficient k in P_lin = k (hbar w/E_Pl)^2 N_GW. Fusion leads unless k >~ 3.2e-4 for GW150914 (candidates.md; arithmetic in M-EXAMINER-09). The obvious scale attack is that a phase-space or coupling suppression pushes k below that threshold. Mechanist F10a, carried into C5's own "evidence against", even says k ≈ 0 for an inspiral because its near-zone support is space-like. So I computed k directly and did not argue it dimensionally.

The process is tree level, first order in G for the amplitude:

source T_mu_nu → its own off-shell field h~ = 16 pi G T~/K^2 at time-like K → photon pair through L = -(1/2) h_mu_nu T_EM^mu_nu.

I used the toolkit's tested Born spectral routine, `qft_production.born_metric_general`. Its photon spin-2 density is rho2 = s^2/(40 pi), and the self-test checks it against explicit vertices and against Redi & Tesi's c_J = 16 [D-60].

- **Source.** A Gaussian-windowed mass quadrupole, either rotating (the circular-binary pattern) or linear, with sigma/w ≈ 0.1. Conservation K_mu T^{mu nu} = 0 is built in.
- **Finite size.** A form factor exp(-k^2 R^2/2) at v = wR = 0 to 0.8 stands in for the merger's size.
- **GW count.** The same I~_ij(w) is fed into the quadrupole formula to get N_GW.

The attack fails. k is about 100 times larger than C5 needs.

## Evidence

[calc: runs/2026-10-13-gravitational-wave-decay-photon-pairs/calc/falsifier-C5-2_linear_k.py] (log beside it). Natural units (hbar = c = 1, G = w0 = 1); the SI conversions are marked.

**The coefficient k.**
- Point quadrupole, full 4D integral: k = 0.0357 for both the circular and the linear pattern. Doubling the grid changes it by about 5e-10.
- Finite size: k = 0.0339 at v = 0.3, 0.0310 at v = 0.5 and 0.0251 at v = 0.8.
- Semi-analytic check: 1/(6 pi) = 0.053 narrow-band, or 0.056 with the window. This keeps the k → 0 spin-2 weight across the whole time-like cone, so it is an upper estimate. The 4D result, which includes how the spin-2 projection depends on |k|/K0, is lower by about 2/3.
- Against C5's threshold: k/k_required = 78 to 112 (log: "k/k_required = 112 ... 78").

**GW150914 in SI.** The formula is P_lin = k G E w / c^5, using (hbar w/E_Pl)^2 N = G E w/c^5.
- B&O inputs (E = 2 Msun c^2, f = 150 Hz): (hbar w/E_Pl)^2 N = 9.29e-3. So P_lin = 2.9e-4 pairs (v = 0.5) to 3.3e-4 pairs (point source).
- E = 3 Msun c^2 at 250 Hz: P_lin = 7.2e-4 to 8.3e-4 pairs.
- C4's fusion totals are 7.0e-7 to 1.4e-5 pairs, so P_lin/P_fusion is 21 to 411 at B&O's inputs.

**Why the margin holds.** Any compact time-dependent source that radiates GWs has a smooth T~(w, k) across the light cone. That is because the light-cone value is exactly what produces the GWs. The same quadrupole amplitude therefore fills the time-like region |k| < w, and the pair yield follows:
- P_lin ~ G^2 Int w^7 |I~|^2, against N_GW ~ G Int w^5 |I~|^2.
- So P_lin/N_GW ~ G w^2. This is the examiner's scaling, now with a computed coefficient.
- Fusion is higher order in G: P_BO/P_lin = c_tot G E sigma/(k c^5) = 3.2e-4/k ≈ 1e-2 for GW150914.

**Literature.** A literature search (`lit_search.py`) found no published photon-yield calculation for this channel that disagrees with mine. Chakraborty et al. 2024, PRD 110 L021701 (abstract, scalars only): "scalar particles may indeed be produced in a perturbative manner". They extract no photon number.

## In principle vs. in practice

- **In principle.** The linear channel beats fusion by roughly 20 to 400 times for a GW150914-like merger. For lower-luminosity sources the gap is larger still, since P_BO/P_lin ∝ G E sigma/c^5: about 3e-8/k ≈ 1e-6 for the GW170817 100 Hz bin.
- **In practice.** Neither channel is observable. Both make ~100 Hz photons that cannot cross ionised gas [D-62]. That does not bear on C5's claim, which is about which channel dominates.

## Corrections to details

1. **k is not "uncomputed".** It is k ≈ 0.025 to 0.036 for a quadrupole source, or 0.053 as the point-source, small-k upper estimate. C5's survival condition "k >~ 3e-4" is met by a factor of about 100.
2. **"The inspiral case fails" is wrong for the linear channel.** M-MECHANIST-10 verified only a local phase-velocity statement, q0/q_phi = Omega r, for one rigidly rotating harmonic. The space-time Fourier transform of a compact rotating source is nonzero for |k| < 2 Omega. The rotating ("circular") pattern in my calculation gives the same k = 0.036 as the linear one.
   - So the linear channel works in the inspiral as well, not only at merger-ringdown.
   - Because P_lin ∝ Int w dE_GW, the merger dominates the event total. The core claim is unaffected, but "during merger-ringdown" is a needless restriction.
   - It also means the GW170817 inspiral ratio (3.0e-8/k) is not undercut.
3. **Hawking emission is misfiled.** It is listed as the "source's own dynamical field", but it comes from a stationary remnant and is not GW-induced, as C5's own evidence-against admits. It emits thermal single photons, not pairs.
   - It has no natural "per event" window. M-CONSTRAINTS-11's 341 is a power ratio, and the components radiate before the merger too.
   - The ranking should rest on sub-hypothesis (a). That sub-hypothesis now carries the claim by itself.
4. **The coefficient comes from first-order Born theory with a quadrupole source.** In GW150914's strong field, the merger's true T~ (or, for black holes, the effective source from nonlinear and horizon structure) changes k by an O(1) factor, not a factor of 100.

## What survives and remaining doubt

C5's core claim survives, now resting on a computed number instead of a dimensional estimate. For a GW150914-like event:
- the source's off-shell linear channel gives about 3e-4 to 8e-4 photon pairs;
- N^2 fusion gives 7e-7 to 1.4e-5 pairs.

Remaining doubts:
- **The source model.** The calculation uses a point-quadrupole EFT source with a Gaussian form factor, not numerical-relativity h_mu_nu. Strong-field effects, or the black holes' lack of a matter T_mu_nu, would have to suppress the time-like support by more than about 20 to 100 times to reverse the ranking, and I know of no mechanism that does.
- **Interference.** I did not compute interference between the linear and fusion amplitudes. It is higher order in G and cannot affect which channel leads.
- **The decisive test.** Running NR h_mu_nu through the Redi–Tesi kernel is still the clean confirmation. The scale argument against C5 does not hold.
