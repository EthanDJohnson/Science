# Verdict on C8 (refuter 2, angle: scale)
claim: Gravitational waves convert into ultralight-dark-matter scalar pairs at astrophysically relevant rates through Bose-enhanced resonant fusion, as B&O's eqs. 16-18 state for supermassive binaries.
status: final
verdict: refuted
basis: calculation
narrowed: none

## Strongest attack

Required versus achievable. B&O's resonant rate (eq. 17) holds only for a graviton packet whose spectral width is σ_ω ≈ m_DM v²/2. B&O state this condition in the same sentence as eq. 17 (v2 p. 5, quoted in analyses/empiricist.md F1: "m_DM ≤ ω_s ≲ m_DM(1 + v²/2) ... with σ_ω ≈ m_DM v²/2"). Eq. 18 then inserts N0 = E_rel/ω_s, the graviton number of a whole merger. No real binary can supply that number in that band.

1. **B&O's own merger packet is 4.2e5 times too broad.** B&O's merger input has fractional bandwidth σ_ω/ω_s = 200/(2π·150) = 0.212, while eq. 17 needs v²/2 = 5.0e-7 at v = 1e-3 c. Eqs. 17 and 18 therefore combine a packet that violates the resonance condition with a rate that assumes it. This is an internal inconsistency inside eqs. 16–18 [calc: runs/2026-10-13-gravitational-wave-decay-photon-pairs/calc/falsifier-C8-2_scale.py, §7].
2. **The required coherence time is missed by 11 orders of magnitude.** The resonance needs a packet coherent for 1/σ = 1.32e12 s (4.2e4 yr) at m = 1e-21 eV. A Newtonian equal-mass chirp at the resonant frequency spends only 12.1 s in the band, so achievable/required = 9.2e-12. The ratio is the same at every mass from 1e-22 to 2e-20 eV (§2). This repeats M-EMPIRICIST-03 and M-CONSTRAINTS-12 (both verified) by an independent script.
3. **Corrected for what a real binary supplies, the power falls from 3.8e13 W·x to at most about 2 W·x, or 3.5e2 W·x under the most generous correction (SI, x = DM density over the cosmic mean).** Three corrections, each applied to B&O's own eq. 17 (§3, §7):
   - **duty cycle:** the face-value rate applies only for the 12 s in band. This gives 3.5e2 W·x, or ≤ 4.6e14 J·x per merger, which is 4.6e-42·x of E_rel. It is the most generous reading, because it still assumes all N0 gravitons sit in the band.
   - **in-band graviton number:** only 2.3e-7 of E_rel lies in the band, so (N_band/N0)² = 5.1e-14 and the power is 1.95 W·x.
   - **best case over every binary mass:** I scanned chirp masses from 1e1 to 1e11 Msun. N_eff is the number of gravitons in band within one coherence time, and the binary must still be before ISCO. The maximum is **1.6 W·x_MW at Mc = 7.5e9 Msun**. Lighter binaries do stay in band longer than 1/σ, but they put far fewer gravitons into it: 4e-10 W·x at Mc = 1e7 Msun, 2e-24 W·x at 1e3 Msun.

   The corrected power is 1e11 to 2e13 times below B&O's eq. 18 (3.8e13 W·x at 1e-21 eV).
4. **Even the densest plausible ULDM does not make the corrected rates "stellar".** I estimated soliton peak densities from Schive et al. 2014 eqs. (3) and (6), for an isolated soliton. At m = 1e-21 eV they give x = 6.8e10, 3.2e13 and 6.8e14 for halos of 1e12, 1e14 and 1e15 Msun.
   - At face value this does reach 3 to 70 Lsun in cluster-scale halos, which is the only setting where B&O's "stellar" remark holds.
   - After the in-band correction it is 1.6e-13 to 3.5e-12 Lsun.
   - Even under the generous duty-cycle correction it is 3.5e2 W × 6.8e14 = 2.4e17 W = 6e-10 Lsun.

   A 1 Lsun output needs x = 2e26 after the in-band correction. A 10% amplitude effect on the GW, the only possible observable, needs x = 7e48 (in-band) or 4e35 (face) (§6). The bound Ω_φ ≤ 5.4e-37·x·Ω_GW means no cosmological relevance either.
5. **Geometry adds further suppression (order-of-magnitude, labelled).**
   - **Where the kinematics allow it.** The resonant kinematics need graviton pairs within 2v = 2e-3 rad of head-on (M-MECHANIST-08, M-IDEALIZER-12, both verified). Such pairs exist only at r ≲ ħc/m = λ/2π = 1.97e14 m. That is 1.22 times the binary separation, which here equals r_ISCO = 1.61e14 m (§4). So the "conversion" happens inside the binary's own near zone, not in a propagating wave.
   - **Bose enhancement smeared out.** A production region of size R ≲ ħ/(mc) gives final-state momenta spread over ≥ ħ/(2R) ≈ 0.5 mc. The occupied ULDM modes have |p| ≤ 1e-3 mc, so only about (2v)³ ≈ 8e-9 of final states can be Bose-enhanced (§5).

   This is an estimate, not a derivation, and the verdict does not rest on it.

## In principle vs. in practice

- **In principle:** tree-level g g → φφ through gravity exists, and Bose enhancement of a final state with high occupation is standard physics. If an ideal graviton packet had fractional width ≤ 5e-7 around ω = m, lasted ≥ 1/σ (4e4 yr at 1e-21 eV) and contained many head-on pairs, then eq. 17 would apply. None of the analyses rules this out as a formal statement about such a packet.
- **In practice:** no astrophysical source supplies that packet.
  - A merger is 4.2e5 times too broad in fractional bandwidth.
  - A chirp crosses the band 1e11 times too fast.
  - The best binary of any mass gives ≤ 1.6 W·x_MW at 1e-21 eV (≤ ~2e2 W·x at 1e-22 eV, since W ∝ m⁻² at a fixed in-band fraction).
  - Even soliton densities lift this only to ≤ ~1e-11 Lsun.
  - The scalars couple only gravitationally, so no instrument could see them. The induced GW depletion is ≤ 1e-40 of E_rel per unit x, against PTA amplitude errors of order 10%.

  So the rates are not astrophysically relevant, on any reading.

## Corrections to details

- **Mass range:** the fiducial 1e-22 to 1e-21 eV is disfavoured by Lyman-α (Rogers & Peiris 2021, cited in candidates.md). This does not change the scale verdict: the shortfall factor 9.2e-12 is the same at every mass.
- **Scalar count:** B&O's "~1e54 s⁻¹" is 2.4e53 s⁻¹ at E_rel = 2/65 Mc², within B&O's stated order.
- **The "1/16" coefficient** in the brief is not B&O's printed form, which is 1/128 (empiricist F1).
- **The face-value "stellar" remark** is not wrong as arithmetic for cluster-scale solitons (3 to 70 Lsun at x ~ 3e13 to 7e14). It fails only once the resonance condition is imposed.

## What survives and remaining doubt

What survives is a formal statement: B&O's algebra from eq. 17 to eq. 18 is right as printed, and g g → φφ is a real tree-level channel. The candidate's core claim, "astrophysically relevant rates ... for supermassive binaries", does not survive.

Doubts:
- **Eq. 17 itself** (its v⁶ power and 1/128) is unverified (M-EMPIRICIST-07). The verdict does not depend on them, because every correction is a ratio applied to eq. 17 as printed.
- **The in-band N² premise** is a physical inference. The duty-cycle correction, which does not use it, still gives a factor 1e-11.
- **Fully relativistic ULDM** (v ~ 0.3c, as in a gravitational atom around the binary with α = 0.14) lies outside eq. 17's v ≪ 1 regime. The candidate does not claim it, and it would need a separate calculation, which is a superradiance-type problem rather than B&O's mechanism.
- **The decisive test** in candidates.md, a time-dependent Bose-enhanced rate with the chirp's spectral density resolved, has not been run in full. My best-case scan over binary mass is its scale-level stand-in.
