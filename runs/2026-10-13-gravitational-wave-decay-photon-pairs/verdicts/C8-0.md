## Strongest attack

C8's 1e14 W x_MW comes from eq. 17. That equation applies only inside a resonance window, and B&O's own window conditions cannot be met by any real supermassive binary. Once a source is fed in that does meet them, the Bose-enhanced power is between 1e-22 W and about 2 W per unit x_MW. A solar luminosity would then need a dark-matter density that puts the binary's near zone inside its own horizon. This holds whether eq. 17's coefficient is right or not. All numbers below are at m = 1e-21 eV and v = 1e-3 c; units are natural (ħ = c = 1, eV) for eqs. 16–18 and SI for astrophysical quantities.

1. **Spectral window (time in band).** Eq. 17 holds for m ≤ ω ≲ m(1 + v²/2), with σ_ω ≈ m v²/2 = 7.60e-13 rad/s. So the graviton packet must stay inside a fractional band of 5e-7 for 1/σ = 1.32e12 s (4.2e4 yr).
   - The equal-mass binary whose ISCO frequency is resonant has M = 1.82e10 Msun. It chirps through that band in t_in = 12.1 s, so t_in σ = 9.2e-12.
   - If eq. 17 is fed only the gravitons that are actually in band (N_band = 1.41e89 rather than N0 = 6.24e95, a suppression of 5.1e-14), W_φ = 1.95 W x_MW (SI).
   - These reproduce the empiricist and constraints lenses' numbers by an independent script ([M-EMPIRICIST-03], [M-CONSTRAINTS-12]).

2. **A source that does satisfy the window is negligible (new).** To stay in band for 1/σ, a binary needs f/ḟ ≥ 4/(m v⁴) = 2.63e18 s, which is 6 times the age of the Universe.
   - At f_GW = 2.42e-7 Hz this forces M_c ≤ 1.9e3 Msun and L_GW ≤ 1.6e26 W, with 1.3e78 gravitons per coherence cell.
   - Eq. 17/18 at face value then gives W_φ = 1.6e-22 W x_MW. At x = 1e5 that is 1.6e-17 W, or 1e-43 of the source's own GW luminosity.
   - Under this counting the graviton number per coherence cell grows monotonically with M_c (∝ M_c^{10/3} below the window limit, ∝ M_c^{5/3} above it). So no binary between these two cases does better than the ~2 W x_MW of the in-band ISCO chirp.

3. **"Astrophysically relevant" needs impossible densities (new).** B&O's Discussion claims "stellar" luminosities in galactic-centre solitons. To reach 1 Lsun:
   - with the in-band feed: x = 2.0e26, i.e. ρ = 0.44 kg/m³ (2.5e20 GeV/cm³). A uniform ball at that density is inside its own horizon for radii above 1.9e13 m, which is smaller than the near-zone radius ħc/m = 2.0e14 m, where (point 4) any production must happen;
   - with the window-respecting feed: x = 2.3e48 (5e21 kg/m³, horizon radius 175 m).
   - Only B&O's printed eq. 18 avoids this (x = 1.0e13, ρ = 1.3e7 GeV/cm³). It does so by assigning all N0 = 6.2e95 merger gravitons to a 5e-7 band that a chirp crosses in 12 s.

4. **Kinematics: the outgoing wave has no head-on gravitons.**
   - The resonance needs graviton pairs within π − θ ≤ 2v = 2.0e-3 rad of head-on ([M-MECHANIST-08], [M-IDEALIZER-12], reproduced).
   - A retarded multipole field is exactly h_l^(1)(kr) e^{-ikr} = polynomial in 1/(kr). For l = 2 this is (i x² − 3x − 3i)/x³ (sympy, also l = 3). It contains no incoming e^{-ikr} component at any radius, so beyond the near zone (r ≳ c/ω = 2e14 m) there are no head-on pairs.
   - In a scalar toy, the Q ≈ 0 weight of the 2ω part of h² is bounded (|S_ret| ≤ 4π/k, oscillating with R) for an outgoing wave. For a standing (Pauli–Jordan-like) wave it grows linearly in R out to the ULDM coherence length 1/(2mv). That makes the rate ratio standing/outgoing ≥ 6.2e4 ≈ 1/(16 v²).
   - B&O's N0² over the whole packet therefore counts head-on content that the retarded field of a real binary does not have. This is an extra suppression of at least ~v² for a resonant volume of size 1/(mv) (scalar toy, labelled).

5. **Even taken at face value, eq. 18 is not astrophysically relevant.** W_φ = 3.8e13 W x_MW (validated here) is 1.1e-31 of a merger's peak L_GW at x = 1e5 ([M-EMPIRICIST-04]). The products are 1e-21 eV scalars with only gravitational couplings, so the only observable is GW depletion at the 1e-32 level.

Together these defeat the core claim. The "astrophysically relevant rates" exist only when B&O's resonance formula is evaluated outside its own validity window, on a time-symmetric field that a real source does not radiate.

- The brief's "1/16" coefficient is not in B&O v2. As an aside, eq. 17 rewritten for a steady source (N0 = L/(ħωσ)) does give W = G² m² v² N_φ L²/16 in natural units, which may be where a "1/16" arises.

## What survives and remaining doubt

- Tree-level g g → φφ is a real process, and scalar pairs near rest can be produced in principle by the near-zone or non-radiative part of a source's field. Bose enhancement by a large ULDM occupation is legitimate for final states inside the occupied phase space. None of that is in question.
- What fails is the rate, and its attribution to a freely propagating GW.
- Not addressed here: binary-driven ionisation and resonances of ULDM clouds bound to the black holes ("gravitational atoms"). That is a first-order tidal effect of the orbit, not GW → φφ fusion, and is outside C8.
- Remaining doubt: no full in-in calculation of φφ production by the retarded field of a quadrupole source within an ULDM medium exists. Such a calculation would have to come in many orders of magnitude above eq. 17 evaluated with physical inputs to revive C8, and nothing in the kinematics suggests that.
