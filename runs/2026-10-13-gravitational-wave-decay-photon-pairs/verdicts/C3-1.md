narrowed: Eq. 8 is the in-out pair yield of B&O's free, time-symmetric (Pauli-Jordan) coherent state, generated by its incoming-times-outgoing overlap, not the yield of an outgoing wave; the part of a real source's retarded field beyond about 1.5–2 lambda/2pi does yield substantially less (about 0.02–0.3 of it), but whether the real source's total is smaller is not established, because its quadrupolar near zone, where real binaries sit, can yield more.

## Strongest attack
C3 has two halves. The first ("eq. 8 belongs to a time-symmetric state") holds up. B&O's own text confirms it (see Evidence, quotes 1–2), and nothing published contradicts it: INSPIRE and arXiv show no comment, reply or erratum (lit_search, 2026-10-13: "0 citations").

The second half ("so a real source's outgoing wave yields substantially less") rests entirely on scalar toys. The decisive one is the examiner's l = 0 monopole, where retarded/time-symmetric = 1/12 for p = 0 and 0.020 for p = 2 [M-EXAMINER-03]. A real GW source radiates at l = 2. Its retarded field has a near zone that grows as r^-3, while the time-symmetric (j_2) field is regular at the origin.

I applied the examiner's own method (source j = psi^2, yield = Int theta(q^2)(q^2)^p |J(q)|^2) to the l = 2 field. I compared the retarded field h_2(kr) with the time-symmetric field 2 j_2(kr), which has the same outgoing part, and excised the region r < a that a real source's interior occupies. I first validated the code on l = 0, where it reproduces 0.0839 (exact value 1/12) and 0.0203 (exact 0.0200).

For l = 2 the retarded/time-symmetric ratio is not a fixed "substantially less". It depends steeply on the source size, roughly as (ka)^-6:

Real sources sit at ka < 1 (SI inputs from M-EXAMINER-08):
- **GW150914 at 150 Hz:** the separation is 3.39e5 m against c/w = 3.18e5 m, so k·separation = 1.07 and each body sits at kr of about 0.5.
- **GW170817 at 100 Hz:** k·separation = 0.32, so each body sits at kr of about 0.16.

At those sizes the same toy that C3 cites gives a retarded yield 2 to more than 1e4 times the time-symmetric one, not 0.02–0.1. C3's prediction "a retarded-source in-in total at O(G^2) is about 0.02–0.1 x (eq. 8 x T_eff)" (C3, predictions), and the GW150914 figure of "<~ 3e-7 pairs" built on it, are therefore unsupported: in the multipole that matters, the same method points the other way.

The run's own idealizer proxy already showed this, and C3 quoted only the part beyond kr = 1. For the m = 2 outgoing field, M-IDEALIZER-07 gives a local fusion density f = 1302 at x = kr = 1, against 7.9e-4 for the standing wave. The table "R(>1) = 0.069" excludes exactly that region. C3's own "evidence against" concedes that the r < 1/w region "could make an O(1) share". The calculation shows the share can be dominant, not O(1).

1. **B&O treat the coherent state as an initial state.** B&O v2, p. 1 (fetch_text.py on arxiv.org/pdf/2607.24930v2): "the evolution of the GW has to be studied with these coherent states as initial states, which, as we will see, implies their decay into light particles".
2. **The state carries the Pauli-Jordan field, evaluated as a background.** B&O v2, p. 2: "hc µν =⟨f| ˆhµν|f⟩= √ 8πG Z d4y∆ rad(x−y)µναβTαβ s ,(5) is the classical radiated field by the source, and ∆rad(x− y)µναβ is the Pauli-Jordan function". On the same page: "the calculation we will perform is equivalent to that of considering the creation of photons in a back- groundh c".
   - The Pauli-Jordan function is G_ret - G_adv. As a free field, the background is therefore an incoming shell at t < 0 and the outgoing wave at t > 0 [M-EXAMINER-01, M-MECHANIST-07, both verified].
   - B&O describe only the late-time limit: "far from the source, the classical field of Eq. (5) tends asymptotically to the outgoing spherical wave packet" (p. 2).
   - This supports C3's first half directly, from B&O's own text rather than from a reading of it, which closes the gap D-71a left open.
3. **The time-symmetric yield is cross-term yield.** M-EXAMINER-02 and M-EXAMINER-05 (verified) show that the self-terms give zero time-like support, so the whole of the time-symmetric yield comes from the incoming-times-outgoing overlap. This stands.
4. **The quadrupole calculation** (table above). Method, normalisation and validation are given in the script's docstring and log. Natural units (c = 1, k = w = 1); all quantities are dimensionless ratios.
5. **No published spin-2 retarded-source computation exists.** lit_search found nothing ("particle production classical gravitational wave localized source retarded field": 0 results), and B&O have 0 citations. Neither side of the "less" claim has published support.

## In principle vs. in practice
- **In principle:** the state identity is right, so eq. 8 is not the yield of an outgoing wave. "Less" holds for the field beyond about 1.5–2 lambda/2pi. The total from the retarded field depends on the near zone and the source size, which the free-graviton model does not describe. In the scalar l = 2 toy, that total is larger once ka < about 1.2.
- **In practice:** both C3 and the alternatives give totals of order 1e-6 pairs per event, or even 1e-6 x 1e4 for compact inspirals. Either way the yield is unobservable (M-CONSTRAINTS-06: W_gamma/L_GW of about 4e-85). The ordering does not change any observational conclusion.

## Corrections to details
- C3's predicted ratio of 0.02–0.1, and "<~ 3e-7 pairs" for GW150914, hold only for the field beyond kr of about 1.5–2. Including the l = 2 near zone outside a source of size ka = 0.5–1, the scalar toy gives 2–115 times the time-symmetric yield, not 0.02–0.1.
- The "1/12 (exact)" and "0.020" ratios are specific to l = 0. For l = 2 with the source excised at ka = 3 they happen to be similar (0.097 and 0.021), but they rise steeply as ka falls.
- "Right arithmetic" needs the qualifier C3 itself carries: B&O's eq. 7 real-sign sum is the azimuthal maximum for a single axis [M-MECHANIST-02]. The coefficient 3/(25 pi) reproduces only within that convention [M-IDEALIZER-01].

## What survives and remaining doubt
- **Survives:**
  - B&O's eq. 8 belongs to a free, time-symmetric Pauli-Jordan state used as an initial state. This is now backed by B&O's own wording.
  - Its yield comes from incoming-times-outgoing overlap.
  - Its "rate" is a per-packet total divided by T_eff.
  - A purely outgoing wave far from the source (kr >~ 2) yields far less, with the yield beyond R falling as R^-3.
- **Lost:** the claim that a real source's outgoing wave yields "substantially less" in total. The l = 2 near zone can make the retarded total larger.
- **Remaining doubt about this attack:**
  - Both my toy and C3's are scalar fields with an ad hoc vertex (q^2)^p. Spin-2 structure, gauge, and the Weyl coupling of Maxwell fields could change the near-zone weighting.
  - The excision W(r) = 1 - exp(-(r/a)^8) is a crude stand-in for the source. A real binary's near zone also contains higher multipoles and the Newtonian monopole, which overlaps with C5.
  - For GW150914, the region at ka of about 1 is strong-field, so no flat-space computation settles it.
  - So the attack establishes that "less" is not shown. It does not establish that the real total is larger. That is why the verdict is weakened rather than refuted.
