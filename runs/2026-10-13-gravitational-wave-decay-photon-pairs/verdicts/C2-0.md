claim: No vacuum decay: an outgoing gravitational wave's photon-pair production converges within a few wavelengths of its source, so there is no lifetime, no path depletion and nothing observable.
status: final
verdict: weakened

narrowed: The photon-pair production an outgoing GW makes on its own (self-fusion, including its curvature tails) converges within a few wavelengths of the source, so it has no self-induced lifetime or path depletion; the only sustained depletion of a real wave comes from crossing the ambient GW background from other sources, with a graviton lifetime of ~5e124 to 2e128 s, and nothing is observable either way.

## Strongest attack

The physics angle cannot overturn the convergence of a wave's *self*-production. It does break the heading's absolute "no lifetime, no path depletion" for any *real* outgoing wave. A real wave never travels through spacetime that is empty of other gravitons. It crosses the stochastic GW background (SGWB) made by every other source, which arrives from all directions. Those collisions are not collinear: for a background graviton at angle theta, s = 2 w w_b (1 - cos theta) is greater than 0. So the tree-level g g -> gamma gamma rate (the same cross-section C2's own kinematics bullet uses) is non-zero, and it is **constant per unit path length**. It does not fall as R^-4. This is still a vacuum process: the background is made of vacuum GW solutions, and no matter is involved.

Per test graviton of energy w in an isotropic background of energy density rho_b (natural units):

Gamma_1 = n_b <sigma (1 - cos theta)> = (16 pi/15) G^2 w rho_b

In SI this is (16 pi/15) G^2 (hbar w) rho_E / c^7, in s^-1. It does not depend on the background's frequency. [calc: runs/2026-10-13-gravitational-wave-decay-photon-pairs/calc/falsifier-C2-0_ambient_crossing.py]

What this means:
- **C2's own prediction fails.** The candidate predicts that "the in-in yield of a retarded outgoing packet beyond R falls faster than R^-1 ... and saturates at a near-source total". That is false for any real packet beyond R_x ~ 0.5–4 pc (about 1e10 wavelengths), where production per unit length switches from the self term (falling as R^-4) to the flat cross term.
- **The cross term dominates the far path.** Beyond 410 Mpc it exceeds C2's own self-production figure (2.9e-66 pairs) by 2e32 to 1e36.
- **The decisive test needs a correction.** C2 and C1 propose to settle the question with "the sign of the slope" of P(>R). That test, applied to any real wave, would find the sustained ambient term.
- **The core does not fall.** The term is a different process from C1's eq. 8 rate. It is 1e-27 to 1e-24 of B&O's near-source total, and it leaves "nothing observable" intact.

The candidate's scope bullet ("does not deny ... production in multi-directional ensembles (C6)") partly anticipates this, but its heading still says "no lifetime, no path depletion". The narrowing above is the honest form.

## Corrections to details
- **"No lifetime" should read "no self-induced lifetime".** A graviton in a real outgoing wave has a finite lifetime of ~1e124.7 to 1e128.3 s against ambient-background fusion (table above).
- **The prediction "saturates at a near-source total" holds only out to R_x ~ 0.5–4 pc.** Beyond that, cumulative production grows linearly with R at ~1e-33 pairs per 410 Mpc (PTA-level background). The decisive slope test must subtract this ambient term, or be posed for an isolated wave.
- **"Path depletion of a single wave is zero at this order" (dialectician F7 (iv), as carried into C2) is wrong for a wave in the real universe.** It is ~1e-111 over 410 Mpc, which is non-zero but immaterial.
- **The quoted R^-3 exponent depends on the toy.** It belongs to smooth windows with an s^2 weight (M-EXAMINER-04, M-CONSTRAINTS-08). The dialectician's (lambda/2 pi r)^5 is a different spread model. Only convergence itself is robust, and C2's core needs only that.

## What survives and remaining doubt
- **What survives.**
  - The physics of the core: a single outgoing wave's own fusion has time-like support only at s ~ 4 w/R and converges within a few wavelengths. Curvature tails converge faster still.
  - There is no eq. 8-type sustained self-decay.
  - Every observable channel falls short by enormous factors, and the ambient cross term adds ~1e-33 pairs per GW150914 burst.
- **What is narrowed.** Only the absolute "no lifetime, no path depletion", which holds for an isolated wave but not for a real one in the GW background.
- **Remaining doubt.**
  - Self-convergence for spin-2 still rests on scalar toys plus the kinematic argument. No spin-2 in-in retarded calculation with memory exists. My tail estimate is a scaling argument, not a computation.
  - Nonlinear memory gravitons are collinear with the wave and of energy <~ hbar sigma. Kinematically they add no time-like support beyond the diffraction term, but this is not computed.
  - The ambient-crossing numbers ignore redshift evolution of rho_b and the plasma cutoff for Hz–kHz photons (D-62), which would remove the photons in most of the path anyway.
