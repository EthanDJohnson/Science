# Verdict on C3 (refuter 2, angle: scale)
claim: Eq. 8 is right arithmetic for the wrong state: the per-packet yield of a time-symmetric, imploding-then-re-expanding graviton shell, so a real source's outgoing wave yields substantially less.

## Strongest attack
The scale attack goes after the two places where C3's "substantially less" factor (0.02–0.1) was obtained outside the regime of a real source:

1. **Bandwidth.** The decisive toy ratios (1/12 and 0.020, M-EXAMINER-03) are narrow-band limits, checked at sigma/w = 0.01. GW150914 has sigma/w = 200/(2 pi x 150) = 0.21 (B&O's own inputs), and a merger is close to a single-cycle burst. A broader band shrinks the region r < c/sigma where the time-symmetric shell's head-on incoming x outgoing cross term operates. That could push the ratio toward O(1).
2. **Source size and multipole.** Every outgoing-wave number C3 cites is either a monopole toy or a kinetic proxy cut at kr = 1. A real source is a quadrupole of finite radius a (ka ~ 2v/c, about 1 at merger and much less than 1 in an inspiral). Its retarded field has a near zone (h_2(kr) ~ (kr)^-3) that the time-symmetric (Pauli–Jordan) field, which is regular at r = 0, does not have. If the near-zone part of the retarded yield were large, a real source would not yield "less".

I computed both, in a scalar toy where j = phi^2, the pair rate is Int d^4q theta(q^2) (q^2)^p abs(J)^2, and p = 2 mimics graviton fusion's abs(M)^2 ~ s^2 (natural units, w = k = 1, dimensionless). The comparison holds the late-time outgoing wave fixed, and hence N.

Results:
- **Bandwidth does not rescue eq. 8.** The ratio rises only slowly with sigma/w.
- **Multipole and source size** split the answer. The circular-binary pattern makes C3 stronger. The linear-quadrupole pattern shows that the near zone can dominate a source's total. That bears on the total yield (C4/C5), not on the outgoing wave, which is what C3's heading is about.

So the attack does not defeat the core claim. It corrects some supporting numbers, listed below.

## Evidence
- **Bandwidth** [calc: runs/2026-10-13-gravitational-wave-decay-photon-pairs/calc/falsifier-C3-2_bandwidth.py]. Monopole toy with a Gaussian envelope, sum-frequency (fusion) part only. Own closed forms:
  - J_ret = (4 pi/q) i artanh(q/q0) F(q0 - 2)/4;
  - J_sym = -(4 pi/q)(pi/4) F(q0 - 2) erf(q/2 sigma).

  Checks: J_sym matched brute-force quadrature (-115.16676 both ways). The narrow-band limits reproduce 1/12 and 0.0200 (M-EXAMINER-03).

  | sigma/w | ret/sym, p = 0 | ret/sym, p = 2 |
  |---|---|---|
  | 0.01 | 0.0840 | 0.0203 |
  | 0.10 | 0.0906 | 0.0235 |
  | 0.21 (GW150914) | 0.1001 | 0.0282 |
  | 0.30 | 0.1096 | 0.0327 |
  | 0.50 | 0.1373 | 0.0428 |
  | 1.00 | 0.2217 | 0.0615 |

  At sigma/w >~ 0.5 the split between sum and difference frequencies becomes ambiguous, so treat the last rows as indicative only. Even a single-cycle burst stays below 0.07 at p = 2.

- **Quadrupole and source size** [calc: runs/2026-10-13-gravitational-wave-decay-photon-pairs/calc/falsifier-C3-2_quadrupole.py]. Monochromatic fields psi_ret = h_l^(1)(kr) Y_lm and psi_sym = 2 j_l(kr) Y_lm, which have the same outgoing part. psi^2 is decomposed into Y_L by Gaunt coefficients. The radial Fourier integrals use contour rotation, and the region r < a is removed with the smooth window of M-EXAMINER-04 (D = a/8).
  - **Validation.** The monopole gives ret/sym = 0.0830 (p = 0) and 0.0200 (p = 2). The smooth-window monopole fractions match M-EXAMINER-04/05:
    - retarded beyond kr = 1 is 0.617 of its total, and beyond kr = 2 it is 0.220 (examiner: 0.60, 0.21);
    - time-symmetric beyond kr = 1 is 0.656, and beyond kr = 2 it is 0.216 (examiner: 0.65, 0.21).
  - **Circular binary pattern (l = 2, m = 2), p = 2.** Over the whole space, including the near zone down to r = 0, ret/sym = 2.87e-3. Beyond kr = 1, 2 and 3 it is 3.3e-3, 4.8e-3 and 6.5e-3 of the time-symmetric total. These rise slightly with the cut, which is window leakage on a tiny number. The near zone adds nothing here, because psi^2 is pure L = 4 and j_4(qr) ~ (qr)^4 suppresses small r.
  - **Linear quadrupole (l = 2, m = 0), p = 2.** Retarded yield beyond ka as a fraction of the whole time-symmetric total:

    | ka | retarded yield beyond ka |
    |---|---|
    | 0.3 | 1.17e3 |
    | 0.5 | 60 |
    | 1 | 1.32 |
    | 2 | 0.053 |
    | 3 | 0.014 |

    The yield diverges as (ka)^-6: from ka = 0.3 to 0.1 it grows by 692, against 3^6 = 729. The cause is the L = 0 and L = 2 parts of psi^2, which reach r^-6 in the near zone.
- **In SI for GW150914.** B&O's per-packet total is 2.9e-6 pairs (M-IDEALIZER-09). The outgoing wave beyond kr ~ 2, i.e. r >~ 640 km, then yields about 1e-8 to 1.5e-7 pairs, depending on the pattern (0.003 to 0.05).

## In principle vs. in practice
- **In principle.** The core holds in every toy I could test at a real source's scale:
  - the outgoing wave of a retarded source, beyond about two reduced wavelengths, yields 0.003–0.05 of the time-symmetric shell at the same N;
  - at GW150914's actual bandwidth the ratio is 0.028 (monopole), which is still well below 1.

  The state identification (Pauli–Jordan, an imploding-then-re-expanding free field) is a verified identity (M-EXAMINER-01). Any isotropic free coherent state, such as the shell in which the idealizer reproduces 4/25 (M-IDEALIZER-01), is necessarily incoming at early times.
- **In practice.** Nothing observable depends on the factor. B&O's number is already a fraction 9e-85 of E_GW (M-IDEALIZER-09), and multiplying it by 0.003–0.06 changes no consequence.

## Corrections to details
- C3's prediction band "0.02–0.1 x (eq. 8 x T_eff), before pattern factors" is too narrow on the low side. For the circular-binary pattern, the one the brief's reference sources actually have, the scalar toy gives 2.9e-3 over the whole space, about seven times below the band's floor. The "less" is larger than C3 states for real binaries.
- C3's "beyond kr = 1" figures depend on the pattern: 0.014–0.024 (mechanist) and 0.069 (idealizer). Beyond kr = 1, my calculation gives:
  - 3.3e-3 for the circular pattern;
  - 1.2e-2 for the monopole;
  - 1.32 for the linear quadrupole. Between kr = 1 and 2 the retarded linear field is near-zone dominated and out-yields B&O's whole shell.

  The ratio is uniformly small only beyond kr ~ 2–3 (at most 0.053). So the boundary of "the outgoing wave" should be set at r >~ 2–3 lambda/2pi, not at lambda/2pi.
- C3's evidence-against entry, "an O(1) share could be made at r < 1/w", understates the open problem for non-circular patterns. For an m = 0 quadrupole the retarded near-zone yield grows as (ka)^-6 and can exceed eq. 8 x T_eff by orders of magnitude for ka < 1. This is a statement about the source's own near field (C4/C5 territory), not about the outgoing wave, and the free-field toy is not trustworthy there.
- The monotone rise of the ratio with bandwidth (0.020 to 0.028 at sigma/w = 0.21) should be quoted for GW150914 in place of the narrow-band 0.020.

## What survives and remaining doubt
- **What survives.** The core survives the scale attack. B&O's eq. 8 corresponds to a time-symmetric shell. Holding the late-time outgoing wave and N fixed, the retarded outgoing wave of a real source yields far less: 0.003 for a circular pattern, about 0.02–0.03 for a monopole at GW150914's bandwidth, and about 0.05 beyond 2 lambda/2pi for a linear quadrupole.
- **Remaining doubt.**
  - All of this is scalar. No spin-2 in-in calculation with a retarded quadrupole source exists; it is C3's own decisive test. In harmonic gauge the spatial h_ij of a compact source is a monopole-type retarded field per component, which suggests the monopole value of about 0.02, but that is an argument, not a calculation.
  - The finite-band quadrupole case was not computed. Only the monochromatic quadrupole case and the finite-band monopole case were.
  - The total a real source makes, including its near zone and the strong-field interior, is not settled by any of this and may exceed eq. 8 x T_eff for some patterns. That affects C4 and C5, not the outgoing-wave statement that C3 makes.
