## Strongest attack
I compared the rate C1 needs with the most the wave can supply. C1 needs an r-independent pair rate R = 3 G^2 E^2 w sigma^2/(25 pi c^10) (SI form of eq. 8), sustained at every radius the wave reaches. For GW150914 inputs that is R = 1.40e-4 pairs/s. The question is what the outgoing wave's own field can supply at radius r.

1. **The local rate has a ceiling that falls with r.** Graviton fusion is a 2 -> 2 process. Its local rate density for any distribution of on-shell gravitons is Gamma = (2 pi/5) G^2 T_mn T^mn. This form is verified in M-IDEALIZER-05/06, M-CONSTRAINTS-01 and M-DIALECTICIAN-09.
   - T_mn T^mn = rho^2 <(1 - n.n')^2>, and <(1 - n.n')^2> = 1 - 2|<n>|^2 + Tr(M^2) with Tr M = 1. So it is at most 2, reached by two head-on beams.
   - Hence Gamma <= (4 pi/5) G^2 rho^2/c^7 (SI) for every possible direction distribution. A radially outgoing wave is the opposite extreme: nearly collinear, with Gamma close to 0.
2. **The energy density thins as r^-2.** B&O state (p. 2) that far from the source the field "tends asymptotically to the outgoing spherical wave packet", with amplitude proportional to 1/r. Its energy E sits in a shell of thickness l >= c/sigma, so rho = E/(4 pi r^2 l).
   - The achievable rate is therefore Ndot(r) <= Gamma_max x 4 pi r^2 l = G^2 E^2 sigma/(5 c^8 r^2). That is r^-2 even with head-on kinematics everywhere, which the outgoing wave does not have.
   - In closed form, Ndot_max/R = (5 pi/3) c^2/(r^2 w sigma). The ceiling drops below R at r* = 1.58e6 m, about 0.79 wavelengths [calc].
3. **The numbers.** The achievable ceiling as a fraction of the required R is:
   - 1.1e-10 at 1 AU;
   - 2.2e-12 at B&O's own sphere of radius c tau;
   - 1.6e-38 at 410 Mpc.

   The pairs that can be made anywhere beyond one wavelength total at most N_max(>lambda) = 5.8e-7. That is 0.2 of B&O's per-packet total P = 2.9e-6, and it falls as 1/r. C1's sustained count R x t_prop = 5.9e12 pairs exceeds this ceiling by 1.0e19 [calc].
4. **C1 fails its own decisive test.** C1's decisive test needs d ln P(>R)/d ln R = +1. The ceiling, computed under the most favourable kinematics, gives at most -1. Realistic outgoing kinematics (theta ~ c/(w r)) give -5. The coherent scalar toys give -3 [M-CONSTRAINTS-08, M-EXAMINER-04, M-IDEALIZER-07/08]. No admissible graviton distribution reaches the sign C1 needs. So a constant-rate decay, a finite lifetime tau_h and cumulative path depletion cannot be supplied by the wave's own field.
5. **The sustained reading also contradicts B&O's own derivation.** That derivation sets T ~ 1/sigma, the packet's own duration (S6, p. 10). It declares eq. 8 a rate only "for tau << delta t ~ 1/sigma_w" (p. 3). At the GW150914 point tau sigma = 7.2e5 [M-DIALECTICIAN-01], so the condition fails by nearly six orders of magnitude. Extending the rate over t_prop = 4.2e16 s goes beyond the stated validity by t_prop sigma ~ 8e18.

### Checking my own assumptions
- **(i) Coherence.** The ceiling uses the kinetic, geometric-optics rate. Coherence does not escape it:
  - eq. 8 itself scales as N^2, as the kinetic n^2 V does;
  - the toolkit's two-beam coherent in-in yield matches the kinetic rate times the overlap volume (ratio 1.002, as listed in tools.md; M-DIALECTICIAN marks it as not independently checked);
  - the fully coherent Fourier toys fall faster than my ceiling, not slower;
  - the margin at 410 Mpc is 1e38, far beyond any O(1) or O(w/sigma) = 4.7 coherence or shape factor.
- **(ii) Beaming.** Beaming into a solid angle 0.1 x 4 pi raises the ceiling tenfold and moves r* to 5.0e6 m. The r^-2 law is unchanged.
- **(iii) Shell thickness.** l >= c/sigma is the bandwidth limit. A chirp only thickens the shell. A broadband l ~ lambda raises the ceiling by w/sigma = 4.7.
- **(iv) Regime.** The bound is used only for r >> lambda, where the field is radiative and on-shell. The near zone (r <~ lambda) is excluded: that is where C4 and C5 place the finite yield.

## In principle vs. in practice
- **In principle** (ideal vacuum, ideal source): refuted. Energy conservation (rho ~ r^-2) and the 2 -> 2 kinematic ceiling cap the achievable rate at r >> lambda at G^2 E^2 sigma/(5 c^8 r^2). That is below R beyond about 0.8 wavelengths and falls as r^-2. A constant rate, a lifetime tau_h = 1.3e82 s and linear path depletion are not available from the wave's own field. B&O's "N_gamma(tau_h) = N_0" (S7, p. 10), complete conversion, is therefore unreachable. The same holds for the high-frequency corner where B&O say tau_h is shorter than the age of the universe, because the ceiling's r^-2 law does not depend on w.
- **In practice:** worse still.
  - Fusion at 150 Hz is kinematically forbidden at every angle in warm ISM and VLISM plasma: f_p = 1.56 and 2.54 kHz exceed 150 Hz, and two gravitons give s <= 4 w^2 < 4 w_p^2.
  - In the mean IGM, fusion stops beyond r_max ~ 4e8 m [M-CONSTRAINTS-09].
  - The large-attenuation variant (b) is excluded outright by the GW150914 detection [M-CONSTRAINTS-14].

## Corrections to details
- Variant (b)'s "Gamma growing linearly in r" is incompatible with the ceiling above, which falls as r^-2, quite apart from the observational exclusion.
- The plasma label in my own script prints "OPEN (head-on only)" for the mean IGM. The verified statement is narrower: open for crossing angles >= 3.22 deg [M-CONSTRAINTS-09]. This does not affect any number used here.
- C1's evidence-for line "Size (a) is contradicted by no observation" is true but has no force. The size-(a) depletion is unobservable whether or not the mechanism exists, so it cannot support the mechanism.

## What survives and remaining doubt
- **What survives:** tree-level g g -> gamma gamma is non-zero for non-collinear gravitons, and B&O's arithmetic for eq. 8 inside their model is right. Read as a per-packet quantity, eq. 8 times T_eff (about 3e-6 pairs for GW150914) has the same order as my ceiling on everything made beyond one wavelength (5.8e-7). That fits a finite, near-source, distance-independent total (C3 and C4), not a decay.
- **Remaining doubt:** the ceiling assumes the local rate of a classical radiative field obeys the kinetic (2 pi/5) G^2 T T bound to within an O(1) coherence factor at r >> lambda. A full spin-2 in-in calculation with the source in the Hamiltonian has not been done; it is C1's own decisive test. For C1 to survive, that calculation would have to beat this bound by a factor growing as r^2, about 1e38 at 410 Mpc. Nothing in second-order perturbation theory supplies such a factor.
