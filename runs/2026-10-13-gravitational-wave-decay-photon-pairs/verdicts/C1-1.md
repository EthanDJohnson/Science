# Verdict on C1 (refuter 1, angle: evidence)
claim: A freely propagating gravitational wave decays into photon pairs in vacuum at B&O's eq. 8 rate, sustained along its path, giving a finite lifetime and cumulative path depletion.
status: final
verdict: refuted
basis: internal-inconsistency
narrowed: none

Units: eq. 8 is in natural units (hbar = c = 1); every number below is converted to SI (pairs/s, s, m) as marked. Inputs are B&O's GW150914 values: E_rel = 2 Msun c^2, f = 150 Hz, sigma_w = 200 rad/s, D = 410 Mpc [D-27].

## Strongest attack

B&O's own derivation defines eq. 8 as a per-packet total divided by the packet's duration. It does not define a rate that persists along the path. The "sustained decay" reading (tau_h, S7, the S12 propagation-sphere integral) contradicts the step that produced the number.

1. **B&O fix T as the packet duration, not the propagation time.** In SM S6 the energy delta function is replaced by "2πδ(0)→T, the interaction time", with "T∼1/σ ω" (quoted below). With a finite packet, that delta function is smeared by the packet profile. The in→out probability, which already integrates over all times from -inf to +inf and so over the wave's whole propagation, is therefore finite: P = R x T_eff, with T_eff = 4 pi/(3 sigma) for a top-hat band [M-DIALECTICIAN-02, verified]. For GW150914 that is P = 2.93e-6 pairs in total (SI) [calc below]. The S-matrix has already counted the whole history of the wave. Reading R as a rate sustained for t_prop = 4.22e16 s instead gives 5.9e12 pairs, which is 2.0e18 times the model's own all-time total.

2. **B&O's own validity condition fails at their reference point.** They require "1/σω≫τ, the reaction time" (S6). For GW150914, tau = 1/(2R) = 3.58e3 s against 1/sigma = 5e-3 s. The condition is violated by tau sigma = 7.2e5 (SI) [calc below; M-DIALECTICIAN-01, verified]. On the paper's own terms, the "probability per unit time" is defined only for t <~ 1/sigma. That is far too short to emit even one photon, and much too short to build a lifetime tau_h = 1.3e82 s or any path depletion.

3. **The long-duration limit sends the rate to zero.** A truly sustained wave, i.e. a long, nearly monochromatic train, is the limit sigma -> 0. There eq. 8 gives R ∝ sigma^2 -> 0, and the total ∝ sigma -> 0. At fixed E: sigma = 200, 20 and 2 rad/s give R = 1.4e-4, 1.4e-6 and 1.4e-8 pairs/s (SI) [calc below]. The formula itself says that nothing is produced once the wave approaches a long plane-like train. This fits B&O's own admission, and the published no-go results, that a single plane wave produces nothing.

4. **Independent literature and verified calculations say the same for the far zone.**
   - Redi & Tesi show that pair production needs time-like Fourier support and that "null-like tensor and vector perturbations do not produce particles".
   - Khusnutdinov finds no massless (scalar or vector) production on an exact plane GW. Even the disputed collinear Bogolyubov photons "would remain there and leave with the sandwich". That sector is where the g g -> gamma gamma amplitude vanishes anyway [D-13].
   - This run's verified math checks find that the yield beyond radius R of an outgoing (retarded) wave converges as (w R)^-3: slope -2.995 at w R = 320 [M-CONSTRAINTS-08], -2.9 to -3.0 [M-EXAMINER-04], exact asymptote 0.0632 (kr)^-3 [M-IDEALIZER-07/08]. C1 needs a slope of +1.
   - The local kinetic rate (2 pi/5) G^2 T_mn T^mn vanishes for the null (locally plane) stress of an outgoing wave [M-IDEALIZER-06, M-CONSTRAINTS-13, both verified].
   - Purely outgoing self-terms have zero time-like Fourier amplitude [M-EXAMINER-02, verified].

5. **The large-attenuation variant (b) is excluded by observation.**
   - Jones & Singleton's decay length of ~1e11 m would attenuate the GW150914 amplitude by exp(-6.3e13) over 410 Mpc.
   - LIGO's <= 11.29% amplitude calibration [D-39] requires an energy e-folding length >= 5.3e25 m (SI) [calc below; M-CONSTRAINTS-14, verified].

## In principle vs. in practice

- **In principle:** refuted. Within B&O's own formalism the all-time pair number of a finite packet is finite and per-packet, at 2.9e-6 pairs for GW150914 (SI). A rate R sustained over the path contradicts the S6 identification T ~ 1/sigma and B&O's own validity condition, which fails by 7.2e5. The verified far-zone calculations give a yield that converges as R^-3, not one that grows as R^+1.
- **In practice:** size (a) cannot be tested by observation. Its claimed depletion over 410 Mpc is 3.3e-66 (vacuum) or 2.5e-57 (CMB-stimulated), against ~1e-1 calibration. So observation neither supports it nor rules it out. The refutation rests on internal consistency and calculation. Variant (b) is refuted by observation, since GW150914 was detected.

## Corrections to details

- The collinear coefficient in C1's evidence-against list is pi/5, not 2 pi/5, for an unpolarised gas [M-DIALECTICIAN-10, refuted coefficient]. This is immaterial.
- The 2 pi delta(0) -> T step is not "unjustified" in itself. It is a standard top-hat bookkeeping step whose correct product is the per-packet total P = R x T_eff [M-DIALECTICIAN-02]. The error is only in reading R as sustained.

## What survives and remaining doubt

- **What survives:**
  - B&O's arithmetic within their model (3/(25 pi), eqs. 9–12) [M-IDEALIZER-01/03].
  - The non-zero tree-level g g -> gamma gamma amplitude for non-collinear gravitons [D-01].
  - A finite per-packet total of order eq. 8 x T_eff, made near the source. That is C3/C4, not C1.
- **Remaining doubt:**
  - No spin-2 in-in (Schwinger–Keldysh) calculation with a retarded quadrupole source has been published. The far-zone R^-3 law rests on scalar toys and kinetic proxies [D-55; math scope notes].
  - Second-order memory and tail terms are not computed. They could leave a small residual far-zone yield.
  - Neither caveat rescues C1 as stated. Any such residual would not run at the eq. 8 rate, because eq. 8 is itself the per-packet total over 1/sigma. The internal-inconsistency argument above does not depend on the toys.
