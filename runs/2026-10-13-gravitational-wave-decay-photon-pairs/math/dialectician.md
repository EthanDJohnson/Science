## Refuted
- M-DIALECTICIAN-10 (F10, last sentence; quoted in K1 as "F10's theta^4" in DIALECTICIAN-E): F10 reads "For nearly collinear momenta the local rate factor sigma (1 - cos theta) falls as (2 pi/5) G^2 w^2 theta^4". This follows a sentence about the unpolarised gas, whose sigma is (2 pi/5) G^2 s. With s = 2 w^2 (1 - cos th) ~ w^2 th^2 and 1 - cos th ~ th^2/2, the unpolarised factor is (pi/5) G^2 w^2 th^4. The value 2 pi/5 holds only for an opposite-helicity (RL or LR) pair, whose sigma is (4 pi/5) G^2 s. The lens's own printout (1.2545e-4 at th = 0.1) is the RL value. Corrected form: sigma_unpol (1 - cos th) = (pi/5) G^2 w^2 th^4 + O(th^6); for an RL pair, (2 pi/5) G^2 w^2 th^4. The refutation is of an O(1) coefficient only. The th^4 collinear suppression, which is what F7, K1 and candidates A and E rely on, stands. Eq. 14 (M-09) is unaffected, because its derivation uses the correct unpolarised sigma. No candidate answer changes.
