## Refuted
None. Every checked claim agrees with an independent derivation. One numerical caveat (M-EXAMINER-04): the R^-3 fall-off of the retarded yield beyond R holds for smooth windows with width ∝ R. A sharper window leaks, giving local slope -2.55 at D = R/40, and a sharp cut tends to R^-2. The yield beyond R converges either way, so F3, F6, F9 and [EXAMINER-B] stand. Exponents quoted as "-3" or "R^-4 per path length" are window-defined.
## Unverified
- M-EXAMINER-12: the pattern coefficients 0.31, 0.56, 0.23 and the pure-circular zero are toolkit outputs that this check did not re-derive. They need the helicity-resolved coherent amplitude with physical phases, which the mechanist's math check owns. [EXAMINER-A] cites them only as supporting context (F5); its core rests on M-01 to M-05, all verified.
- Not checkable as mathematics: F8's P_lin ~ k (hbar w/E_Pl)^2 N is a dimensional estimate with an uncomputed coefficient k. Only the arithmetic built on it was checked (M-EXAMINER-09). So [EXAMINER-D]'s ratio is "verified arithmetic on an unverified scaling".
- Scope of M-03 to M-05: they verify the scalar monopole toy (j = phi^2, vertex (q^2)^p). Whether its numbers carry over to spin-2 quadrupole fields is a physics assumption that this check cannot settle (lens Assumptions, first item).

- M-EXAMINER-06: the distributional Fourier transform of F(t - z) on R^4 is supported on {q0 = q_z, q_x = q_y = 0}, a subset of the null cone q0^2 = |q|^2.
