## Refuted
- M-MECHANIST-02 (finding 2; M1 'Amplitude' row; premise-2 ledger entry; the eq. 7 part of MECHANIST-B): the claim is that for two linearly polarised gravitons the cross term is +-2 t^2 u^2 cos(4 chi), "does not depend on photon azimuth", so that aligned polarisations reproduce B&O's real-sign (t^2+u^2)^2/(2 s^4) and its integral 14/15 (7/6 of the incoherent 4/5), with anti-aligned at 5/7 of B&O. This is wrong. The two opposite-helicity channels carry J_z = +4 and -4 along the collision axis, so their interference into any fixed photon final state goes as e^(+-8 i phi) in the photon azimuth. My own KLT amplitudes confirm this to 2e-15. For fixed lab polarisations the rate is therefore modulated as cos(8 phi + const). At theta = 1.1 and aligned orientation it runs from 0.1104 to 0.18173 (B&O's value is only the maximum), and its azimuthal mean is 0.14230 = (t^4+u^4)/(2 s^4) for every relative orientation. Corrected form: after the photon-angle integral, the two channels do not interfere. A linearly polarised pair gives half the opposite-helicity value at any orientation, i.e. 2/5 per (8 pi G s)^2, which is 6/7 of B&O's 7/15 per collision. The toolkit's own docstring states the same: "the two opposite-helicity channels do not interfere in the total cross section". The lens's flat-in-phi result must come from polarisation frames tied to the photon azimuth. What still stands: the integrals 4/5, 14/15, 2/3 and the ratios 7/6, 5/7, 12/7 as arithmetic, the pure (+2,-2) value 12/7 x B&O, the same-helicity zero, so the range [0, 12/7] x B&O per collision axis, and the verdict that hidden premise 2 fails. That verdict gets stronger: no fixed polarisation choice realises B&O's real-sign total for a single collision axis. Not addressed: coherent summation over different collision axes in B&O's full multi-direction state (idealizer's domain).

## Formalizable
- M-MECHANIST-02: if A_+(alpha) = e^(4 i alpha) A_+(0) and A_-(alpha) = e^(-4 i alpha) A_-(0) for complex constants, then the integral over alpha in [0, 2 pi] of abs(a A_+(alpha) + b A_-(alpha))^2 equals 2 pi (abs(a)^2 abs(A_+(0))^2 + abs(b)^2 abs(A_-(0))^2). Also the polynomial integrals over c in [-1, 1]: (1-c)^4 + (1+c)^4 -> 64/5, ((1-c)^2 + (1+c)^2)^2 -> 224/15, ((1-c)^2 - (1+c)^2)^2 -> 32/3 (that is, 4/5, 14/15, 2/3 after the 1/16).
- M-MECHANIST-04: for a in R, the integral of r^(-2-4a) over [R, infinity) with R > 0 converges if and only if a > -1/4, and then equals R^(-1-4a)/(1+4a).
- M-MECHANIST-07: for f in C^2(R), phi(t, r) = (f(t-r) - f(t+r))/r satisfies (1/r) d_r^2 (r phi) = d_t^2 phi for r > 0, and its limit as r -> 0 is -2 f'(t).
- M-MECHANIST-08: for 0 < v <= pi, arccos(1/(1 + v^2/2)) <= v (since 1/(1 + v^2/2) >= 1 - v^2/2 >= cos v).
- M-MECHANIST-09: for null k1 = w(1, n1), k2 = w(1, n2) with unit n1, n2 and the Minkowski form, (k1 + k2)^2 = 2 w^2 (1 - n1.n2) >= 0 and (k1 - k2)^2 = -2 w^2 (1 - n1.n2) <= 0.
