## Refuted

## Unverified
- None among the 14 claims listed. Scope notes:
  - The cross sections sigma(RL) = (4/5) pi G^2 s and sigma(RR) = 0 are toolkit inputs and were not re-derived here; the source and other checks cover them. M-CONSTRAINTS-01 depends on them.
  - M-CONSTRAINTS-08 verifies the (w R)^-3 law inside a scalar-monopole toy with an s^2 rate weight. The control run shows that the exponent is -(p+1) for weight s^p, so applying it to real GWs relies on sigma ~ s times flux ~ s, which is physics, not math. The lens's suggestion of an in-in calculation for a spherical GW is not done anywhere.
  - Several inputs are the lens's stated assumptions and were used, not tested: eq. 19's integrand as extracted, 0.136 c^3/(GM), the Page-type Hawking luminosity, and the IGM and halo densities.
  - The lens's 32-point energy-condition scan (F14) was not re-run.
