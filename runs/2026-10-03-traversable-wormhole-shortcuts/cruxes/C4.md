Units: SI unless marked; "geometric" means G = c = 1. T_thru is the earliest arrival through the throat, T_ext the exterior light-travel time, Δ the clock lag of one mouth, b₀ the throat radius, v the crossing speed.

## Response to the verdicts

The three verdicts agree on the core: two say `survives` (C4-0, C4-1), one says `weakened` (C4-2). All three accept that GR "solved in reverse" with labelled exotic matter admits static geometries with any designed T_thru/T_ext, and that the transit table (M-EXAMINER-02, -03) stands. Their attacks fall on the stability of particular matter models, on creation, and on the wording of the matter clause. I take them point by point.

### 1. Ghost-scalar instability kills the slow crossing of sub-hypothesis (a): conceded

- **The attack (all three).** The massless ghost-scalar Ellis throat has one unstable mode with e-folding time τ = 0.846 b₀/c (González, Guzmán & Sarbach 2009, Table I, quoted full-text in C4-0 and C4-1). A crossing of length πb₀ at speed v uses N = πc/(0.846 v) e-folds, independent of b₀.
- **My check** [calc: runs/2026-10-03-traversable-wormhole-shortcuts/calc/crux-C4_stability_windows.py]: N = 1.113×10⁵ at v = 10 km/s, 74 at 0.05c, 37 at 0.1c, 4.1 at 0.9c. Against a seed budget ln(1/δ) of 58 to 80 e-folds (C4-0's log), the minimum speed is 0.046c to 0.064c. C4-1, with a different crossing length (2.364 c/v e-folds), puts the threshold near 0.03c. So the threshold is about 0.03c to 0.07c, whichever convention is used.
- **Concession.** C4's sub-hypothesis (a) as worded ("a slow crossing (10 km/s) needs only b₀ ≥ 505 m ... or 4.5 km ... 0.34 or 3.1 M_☉ ... 0.16 s or 1.4 s") is **wrong for a phantom scalar**. Those figures are tide-only. With a ghost scalar a human crossing must be at v ≳ 0.05c through b₀ ≈ 6.8×10⁶ to 7.0×10⁶ m, needing about 4.6×10³ to 4.8×10³ M_☉ of negative mass (C4-0, C4-2 logs). The 505 m and 4.5 km figures survive only for a linearly stable source (Bronnikov et al. 2013's phantom fluid plus radial field, or a β₀² < 0 thin shell).
- **What it does not take.** The core is about shortcuts for "a signal or payload". C4's own headline case, the b₀ = 1 m Ellis throat with T_thru = 6.64×10⁻⁸ s, uses 23.5 e-folds observer-to-observer, against 58 to 80 available (C4-0 and C4-1 both compute this). A light signal or a fast payload passes, and the ratio T_thru/T_ext = 2.1×10⁻¹⁵ at 1 ly is untouched. Shinkai & Hayward 2002 (full-text, quoted in C4-1) say a fast traveller "may go through the wormhole and exit safely ... if the speed is high enough".

### 2. The throat cannot be left unattended: conceded as a qualifier

- C4-1 and C4-2 find the unattended ghost-scalar lifetime is 67.8 to 74.9 b₀/c from Planck-scale seeding, or about 96 to 128 e-folds under CMB seeding (2.3×10⁻⁷ to 3.6×10⁻⁷ s at b₀ = 1 m).
- **Concession.** In the ghost-scalar model, a C4 shortcut is a one-shot transient: built within about 70 b₀/c of use, or held by tuned ghost pulses "for a limited time" (Shinkai & Hayward), and closed by the payload's own passage. "A standing shortcut" is not shown for the ghost scalar.
- **Rebuttal on scope.** The matter clause of C4 is "labelled phantom or modified-gravity matter", not "a ghost scalar". The verdicts themselves supply a linearly stable labelled source for the same Ellis metric: Bronnikov, Lipatova, Novikov & Shatskiy 2013, "linearly stable under both spherically symmetric perturbations and axial perturbations of arbitrary multipolarity" (abstract, quoted in C4-0 and C4-2). Poisson–Visser shells are linearly stable for β₀² < 0 at a₀ > 3M [D-14, Q-30]. C4's own "evidence against" called these sound speeds "superluminal or imaginary"; C4-2 correctly notes that Poisson–Visser treat β₀² as a free equation-of-state parameter of exotic matter, so this route is a live stable window, and I withdraw the framing of it as evidence against.

### 3. Modified-gravity support is thinner than the wording suggests: conceded

- C4-0, C4-1 and C4-2 cite Cuyubamba, Konoplya & Zhidenko 2018: the Kanti–Kleihaus–Kunz EdGB wormhole is "unstable against small perturbations for any values of its parameters". C4-1 cites Mironov, Rubakov & Volkova 2019: static spherical Horndeski wormholes "always have either ghosts or gradient instabilities"; beyond-Horndeski analysis is "incomplete".
- **Concession.** No stable modified-gravity shortcut is on record. "Or modified-gravity matter" stays only as an existence clause (static solutions); it is not a stability route. The core now rests on the phantom-fluid and thin-shell sources alone.

### 4. Phantom fields need a Lorentz-violating cutoff of about 3 MeV (C4-1): conceded as a cost, not a refutation

- Cline, Jeon & Moore 2004 (abstract, quoted in C4-1): ghosts interacting at least gravitationally need Λ < 3 MeV, and a Lorentz-conserving cutoff is "completely excluded". C4-1's power-counting inference (medium confidence) puts controllable phantom-scalar throats above about 5.3×10⁷ m.
- **Response.** This bound is on a *quantised* ghost in an effective field theory. C4 is explicitly a classical, labelled-matter claim, and C4-0 already notes that "a quantised ghost has an unstable vacuum ... those are costs of the label". I concede that the label is not innocent: any attempt to promote C4's phantom to a quantum field inherits a Lorentz-violating cutoff, and on C4-1's reading small throats sit outside its control. That pushes C4 further from admissible physics; it does not remove the classical solutions.

### 5. Creation needs an acausal spacetime (C4-2, Geroch 1967): conceded

- Geroch (abstract via lit_search, quoted in C4-2): topology change of spacelike sections occurs "if and only if the model is acausal". C4 already listed Tipler 1977 (singularities on topology change) as evidence against.
- **Concession.** C4's "design" means reshaping a wormhole that is *supplied* (primordial, or by labelled quantum topology change), or building it inside a region that is already acausal. C4 is an existence claim about spacetimes, so this narrows the route, not the existence.

### 6. "Any designed ratio" holds only while the mouth clocks stay synchronised (C4-2): partly rebutted

- C4-2: a 30 km/s relative mouth speed drifts the clocks at 5.0×10⁻⁹ s/s, reaching Δ = T_ext for 1 ly after about 2.0×10⁸ yr (3.7×10⁶ yr at 220 km/s). My script reproduces these: 1.997×10⁸ yr and 3.714×10⁶ yr [calc: crux-C4_stability_windows.py].
- **Rebuttal.** For a designer shortcut, C7's shortcut threshold Δ_s = T_thru − d/c is negative (−3.16×10⁷ s at 1 ly), so the geometry is a shortcut at every Δ below Δ_CTC = T_thru + d/c = 3.16×10⁷ s. The shortcut ratio is not lost by drift; what drift brings, after 10⁶ to 10⁸ yr, is a time machine. Mouths held at rest in equal potentials keep Δ = 0 in principle. So the drift is an extra consequence (C7's corollary), not a limit on "any designed T_thru/T_ext".

### 7. Polar and nonlinear stability, one-sided embedding (doubts raised by all three)

- Not rebutted. Bronnikov et al. cover spherical and axial modes; polar modes are only "predicted". No source evolves a two-mouth, one-ambient-space phantom throat (C4-0, C4-1), and none treats Visser's cube dynamically (C4-2). M-DECOMPOSER-05 already marks the one-sided embedding as approximate.

### Net position

I accept C4-2's narrowing as the honest core:

> In principle, classical exotic-matter shortcuts of any designed T_thru/T_ext exist as GR solutions whose labelled exotic matter is tuned for at least linear stability (Bronnikov et al.'s phantom fluid plus radial field; thin shells with β₀² < 0). With the canonical ghost scalar they exist only as transients crossed at v ≳ 0.03c to 0.07c. EdGB and Horndeski supports are unstable. The wormhole must be supplied, not created, because classical topology change needs an acausal spacetime.

Lost: sub-hypothesis (a)'s slow 10 km/s crossing and its 0.34 and 3.1 M_☉ budgets for a ghost scalar; modified gravity as a stability route; any implication that the label is free of quantum costs. Kept: the transit table, the energy-condition table, and the existence of designed ratios down to 2.1×10⁻¹⁵ at 1 ly (and lower at larger d) for signals and fast payloads.

## Deciding observation

C4 is an in-principle claim conditioned on labelled matter, so most rivals differ from it on *whether that matter, or an admissible substitute, can exist*, not on the geometry. One test per rival:

### C4 against C1 (the null)
- **Observation.** A Galactic microlensing detection of the Ellis-lens signature: magnification below Schwarzschild, with "gutters" of about 4% just outside the Einstein-ring crossing [D-22, Q-42]. A positive, confirmed Ellis-shaped light curve would show that a zero-ADM-mass, NEC-violating throat exists in nature, which C1's "none stays open without negative null energy ... none could pass 1 kg in practice" can absorb only if the throat is quantum-supported. Its absence leaves C1 untouched and C4 a pure in-principle statement. Limit: a lens shows a throat exists, not that its mouths are one-sided or that T_thru < T_ext; a second mouth would still have to be found (the S2-orbit and pulsar tests [D-23] are the only sourced route).
- **Who and precision.** Galactic microlensing surveys, sensitive to throat radii of 100 to 10⁷ km, at about 4% photometry around the ring crossing; source radii near 10⁶ km smear smaller lenses [Q-42]. Current SDSS bounds are n < 10⁻⁴ h³ Mpc⁻³ for throats of 10 to 10⁴ pc [Q-40].
- **When.** Instruments are operating (TRL 6–9 [F-19]); all searches so far are null. No date for a discriminating result is known.
- **Theory equivalent.** A curved-space QEI (Fewster–Smith type) evaluated on the Ellis throat (C4's own stated test). If it admits a macroscopic throat for some admissible field, C4's matter clause becomes unnecessary and C1's practice leg loses its 1.6×10⁶² single-field excess (M-CONSTRAINTS-07); if it tightens the flat-space band bound (≤ 1.84×10⁻²¹ m at r₀ = 1 m), C1 holds and C4 stays labelled-only.

### C4 against C2 (long quantum-supported wormholes)
- The two are compatible; they differ in what nature supplies. **The decider is the lensing signature of any wormhole mouth found:** C2 predicts near-extremal, magnetically charged black-hole mouths with positive mass and no Ellis gutters [D-22, C2 predictions]; C4 predicts zero-ADM-mass Ellis lenses (or negative-mass shell lenses) with about 4% gutters. Same surveys as above; plus, for C2, primordial magnetically charged black-hole searches (Bai et al. 2020, cited in C2). No date known.

### C4 against C3 (achronal ANEC fails for quantum fields)
- **Calculation.** Back-react the Urban–Olum stress tensor self-consistently, or compute the curved-space QEI on an Ellis throat. If an admissible quantum field self-consistently violates ANEC on a complete achronal throat geodesic *and* at throat-scale band thickness, C3 delivers C4's geometries without labelled matter (C3 wins; C4 becomes a special case). If violations stay confined to fixed backgrounds or Planck-thin bands [D-27, M-CONSTRAINTS-08], C3's route has no source and C4's classical label is the only route to a shortcut.
- **Who and precision.** Semiclassical theorists; the sign of ∫T_kk dλ on a complete achronal geodesic in a solution of G = 8π⟨T⟩, and the band thickness relative to b₀. No date known.

### C4 against C6 (narrower ban for misaligned mouths)
- **Calculation.** Achronality of complete through-throat null geodesics for a finite-mouth, curved-exterior, mirror-glued Visser thin-shell or Ellis one-sided handle (the extension of M-DECOMPOSER-08 that C6 names as cheapest). If no complete achronal geodesic exists, C6's loophole applies to C4's own geometries, and a C4-type shortcut could in principle be supported by admissible quantum fields without meeting the achronal ANEC, which weakens C4's need for the label. If an achronal geodesic is restored (C4's "evidence against" already finds the radial geodesic achronal in two-ended versions, M-CONSTRAINTS-14), C4 stays labelled-only and C6's loophole closes for these geometries.
- **Who and precision.** Mathematical-relativity theorists; a yes/no on existence of a complete achronal through-geodesic, with the near-grazing segment lengths (about d/ε², per C6) computed. No date known.

### C4 against C7 (shortcut status set by Δ)
- For C4's designer throats C7's window is degenerate: Δ_s = T_thru − d/c is negative (−3.27×10⁻⁶ s at 1 km, −499 s at 1 AU, −3.16×10⁷ s at 1 ly for the b₀ = 1 m Ellis throat), so they are shortcuts at Δ = 0 and become time machines at Δ_CTC = +3.40×10⁻⁶ s, +499 s, +3.16×10⁷ s [calc: crux-C4_stability_windows.py]. C7's claim that "one barrier governs both cruxes" therefore says that, under the self-consistent conjecture, C4's geometries are already past the barrier.
- **Decider.** A numerical evolution of a two-mouth, one-ambient-space, stably supported (phantom-fluid or β₀² < 0 shell) shortcut with one mouth in a deeper potential, run until Δ approaches Δ_CTC. If the throat stays intact and only vacuum polarisation of a test field grows near Δ_CTC (Kim–Thorne's weak divergence), C4 holds as a shortcut and the barrier is at Δ_CTC, against C7's thesis that the barrier sits at shortcut onset. If the solution loses stationarity or its support fails as Δ grows, C7's single-barrier reading wins. Numerical-relativity groups with Kain-type spherical codes [D-13]; the precision needed is the sign of the throat-radius drift over a drift time of 3.7×10⁶ to 2.0×10⁸ yr (scaled down in a simulation by choosing small d). No date known.

### C4 against C8 (two-sided bookkeeping)
- Compatible; they address disjoint constructions. **Decider:** C8's own test, chaotic sparse-SYK teleportation at N ≥ 20 with the classical channel cut. Fidelity dropping to zero confirms C8 and leaves C4 untouched, because C4's shortcuts are one-sided and use no coupling channel; any delivery beyond the coupling channel would falsify C8 but say nothing about classical phantom throats. The only observation that bears on both is the existence of *any* throat that beats the exterior without a channel, which is the lensing test against C1. Hardware groups; error per gate about 10⁻⁵; N ≈ 20 plausibly in 5 to 10 yr (C8's unsourced engineer estimate).

### Single most decisive item
A confirmed Ellis-type microlensing event (about 4% gutters, zero-ADM-mass light curve) would separate C4 from C1 and C2 at once; a curved-space QEI on the Ellis throat would separate C4 from C1 and C3 in theory. Neither is scheduled.
