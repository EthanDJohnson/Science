Units: years and light-years with c = 1 ly/yr unless marked SI. T_thru is the throat transit time, d the mouth separation (d/c its exterior light time), Δ the lag of mouth B's clock behind exterior-synchronised time, Δ_s = T_thru − d/c, Δ_CTC = T_thru + d/c. R ∈ SO(3) maps a ray's entry direction at A to its exit direction at B, n̂ is its axis, e the separation direction and α the angle between them.

## Response to the verdicts

**Verdict tally.** Two verdicts say `weakened` (C7-0 and C7-1, both on the same point) and one says `survives` (C7-2). All three accept the kinematic core: the shortcut threshold Δ_s and the time-machine threshold Δ_CTC, and the one-way window 2d/c wide between them. C7-0 re-checked them on 20,000 random cases with 0 mismatches. No verdict disputes that shortcut status depends on Δ.

### 1. "The barrier acts at Δ_a = T_thru − (d/c)|e·n̂|, not at Δ_s, for misaligned mouths" (C7-0, C7-1). Conceded in general; rebutted in part for the published constructions.

- **Conceded.** The conjecture acts only once a complete achronal throat geodesic exists. In the point-mouth handle model (M-DECOMPOSER-08 corrected form, brute-forced 24/24 by C7-0) that happens at Δ_a ∈ [Δ_s, T_thru]. So C7's sentence "under the conjecture, protection must act at Δ_s" is true only for aligned gluing (n̂ ∥ e). For a generic gluing a one-way-shortcut band (d/c)(1 − cos α) wide escapes the conjecture. That band is up to 1000 yr at d = 1000 ly and 3000 yr at d = ℓ = 3000 ly (C7-1's calc; my calc reproduces both) [calc: runs/2026-10-03-traversable-wormhole-shortcuts/calc/crux-C7_alignment.py]. I adopt the narrowed wording: the barrier acts at Δ_a = T_thru − (d/c)|e·n̂| ≤ T_thru, which equals Δ_s for aligned mouths.
- **Partly rebutted: symmetry makes the published constructions aligned.**
  - *The argument.* MMP's static configuration is two opposite magnetic charges on one axis. Its field-line identification is therefore symmetric under rotations about the separation axis e [D-06]. If the whole spacetime has that symmetry, R must commute with every rotation Q about e. In SO(3), the only elements that commute with all rotations about e are the rotations about e themselves. So n̂ = e and α = 0, and the barrier sits exactly at Δ_s.
  - *Checks.* This is a standard group fact; the calc only illustrates it.
    - The midplane mirror (R = diag(1, −1, −1)) and a 40° twist about e both commute with Q_e and have axis angle 0°.
    - A half-turn about an axis perpendicular to e has commutator norm 1.89, so it is not axisymmetric.
    - Among 20,000 random rotations, every one whose axis is more than 1° from e fails to commute: the smallest commutator norm is 0.0048 [calc: crux-C7_alignment.py].
  - *Agreement with the refuters.* C7-1 ("the symmetric field-line geometry suggests a mirror-plane identification, which would be 'aligned'") and C7-0 (the mirror and point-reflection constructions are aligned) say the same.
  - *What breaks the symmetry.* Only something that breaks the axial symmetry opens C6's band, for example MM's orbital co-rotation, whose axis is perpendicular to e (C7-0's remaining doubt).
  - *The band is quadratic in the misalignment.* It is (d/c)(1 − cos α) ≈ (d/c)α²/2. At d = 1000 ly the band is 0.152 yr at α = 1° and 15.2 yr at α = 10°. A 1 yr band needs α = 2.56° [calc: crux-C7_alignment.py].
  - *Unshown.* Nobody has computed how large α is for an orbiting MM pair.
  - *Net.* The "aligned" case is the symmetric default, not a special one. A Haar-random rotation (C7-0's mean band of 505 yr) is the wrong prior for a solution built from an axisymmetric magnetic configuration. **This rebuttal is a symmetry argument, not a computation of MMP's actual throat map.**
- **Edge case conceded.** For the non-orientable translation gluing (R = −I), no through-geodesic is achronal anywhere in the window (C7-0). On that route only Graham–Olum's Theorem 3 acts, at Δ_CTC. I accept it as an edge case outside orientable 3-space, as C7-0 treats it.

### 2. "One barrier governs both cruxes" is conditional on the conjecture (C7-0 logical asymmetry; C7-1 point 2). Conceded.

- **The implication runs one way only.** The kinematics are unconditional: every mouth-offset route from a long wormhole to a CTC passes through the shortcut window first. Hence a shortcut ban implies chronology protection for such routes, but chronology protection does not imply a shortcut ban.
- **If the conjecture fails (C3), the cruxes separate.** The window opens, and Hawking/Klinkhammer-type protection could still act at Δ_CTC (C7-1 quotes Klinkhammer 1992: "at the Cauchy horizon ('chronology horizon')").
- **C7 already said this.** Its P2 and its rival sub-hypothesis (ii) say as much. The heading's "one barrier governs both cruxes" should read: "if the self-consistent achronal ANEC holds, the barrier that bans shortcuts also pre-empts the chronology horizon, by at least d/c for every orientable gluing and by exactly 2d/c for aligned gluing".
- **What remains of the claim.** Graham–Olum's Theorem 3 blocks CTCs by a separate theorem, which C7-1 notes. The surviving content is the ordering: under the conjecture the shortcut-type barrier acts first, at Δ_a ≤ T_thru < Δ_CTC (C7-1's check line: "Δ_a ≤ T_thru < Δ_CTC for every axis angle: PASS").

### 3. "d should be the exterior light time T_ext, including Shapiro delay" (C7-0). Conceded formally; negligible numerically.

## Deciding observation

There is one master calculation, and it separates C7 from most rivals by its outcome. The calculation is a **self-consistent MMP (or FGM 2019) solution with a clock offset.**
- *Set-up.* Take MMP's lowest-Landau-level 2D reduction with the twisted identification (t, x) ~ (t + Δ, x + L), or a mouth pair at asymmetric redshift, and scan Δ from 0 to Δ_CTC.
- *Quantities to compute:*
  - the existence and stationarity of the solution;
  - T_thru(Δ);
  - ANEC on the on-axis complete throat geodesic.
- *Who could make it.* Semiclassical theorists working in MMP's framework [D-06, Q-10]. Sadhukhan's linear-stability programme [F-01] is the closest existing work.
- *Precision.* The location of the existence boundary in Δ, to better than d/c (1000 yr for MM at d = 1000 ly), so that Δ_s, T_thru and Δ_CTC can be told apart.
- *When.* No date is known; no source in the dossier treats long wormholes with a time offset (C7-1).

**vs C1 (null).** C1's no-shortcut leg reads "T_thru/T_ext ≥ 1" as a property of the static solutions. Its own synchronisation-free test, Δ_B ≤ 0 on one clock at B, already includes the offset.
- *If C7(i) is right:* the master calculation finds solutions ceasing, or T_thru(Δ) growing so that T_thru − Δ ≥ T_ext, at or before Δ_a. C1 then holds, but as a dynamical statement.
- *If a stationary solution exists with Δ_a < Δ < Δ_CTC and ANEC < 0 on the achronal on-axis geodesic:* C1's leg (i) fails, and C7's P2 is confirmed.
- *Precision:* the sign of T_ext − (T_thru − Δ) at the existence boundary.

**vs C2 (long Casimir wormholes "never shortcuts").** The same calculation decides between them.
- *If MMP exists at Δ > Δ_s:* C2's "never" is false, because the same object is a one-way shortcut.
- *If the long equilibrium is lost first:* C2's "never" survives, but as an existence statement in Δ, not a geometric one. The toy models predict this, at 0.25 to 0.56 Δ_s (M-MECHANIST-06) or Δ/l₀ = 0.1495 (M-DIALECTICIAN-07).
- *An observational handle.* Bai et al.'s Andromeda-field searches for magnetically charged black holes (C2's test) cannot decide this: they see mouths, not clock offsets.

**vs C3 (the achronal ANEC fails).**
- *For C3 and against C7(i):* a self-consistent long wormhole that crosses Δ_a intact, with ANEC < 0 on a complete achronal geodesic and no CTC. This is C7's P2, and it would be the first self-consistent counterexample that Kontou [D-27] says is missing.
- *For C7(i) and against C3 on this route:* failure at or before Δ_a.
- *Precision:* the sign of ∫T_kk dλ on the on-axis geodesic in a solution of G = 8π⟨T⟩, evaluated inside the window.
- *Advantage over C3's own test:* it starts from a known self-consistent solution, unlike C3's Urban–Olum back-reaction.

**vs C4 (classical phantom shortcuts).** C7's corollary predicts that any such shortcut with mouths at unequal potentials drifts toward Δ_CTC: 2.3×10⁴ yr for a 1 AU throat with one mouth on Earth's surface (M-MECHANIST-04). Phantom matter is not bound by the quantum conjecture, so only a Hawking-type barrier at Δ_CTC can stop the drift.
- *Deciding calculation:* a 3D numerical-relativity evolution of a phantom-stabilised Ellis throat with one mouth held in a potential well, tracking T_thru − Δ toward −d/c.
- *For C4 plus C7's corollary (classical shortcuts are time machines in waiting):* a compactly generated Cauchy horizon forms.
- *For C7's corollary that no long-lived natural short throats exist:* the throat collapses before Δ_CTC, as ghost throats do under weak pulses [D-13].
- *Who:* numerical-relativity groups (Kain-type spherical codes [D-13] would need extending to 3D).
- *Precision:* Δ resolved to better than d/c (499 s at 1 AU).
- *When:* no date known.

**vs C6 (misaligned shortcuts escape the ban).** This is the crux that the two `weakened` verdicts opened.
- *Deciding calculation:* the throat ray map R of the actual MMP solution, and of an orbiting MM pair. Trace null geodesics entering mouth A off-axis, record the exit direction at B, and read off the axis n̂.

- *Who:* mathematical-relativity theorists, extending M-DECOMPOSER-08 to finite mouths in a curved exterior, which is C6's own cheapest test.
