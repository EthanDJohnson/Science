Units: SI unless marked; "geometric" means G = c = 1 (lengths in m). l_P = 1.616×10⁻³⁵ m. l_* = √N·l_P is the species cutoff length for N light fields. f is the Ford–Roman sampling ratio (dimensionless).

## Response to the verdicts

All three verdicts return `weakened`, and they agree on the narrowed core. I accept that narrowing. C3 cannot be defended in its indicative form ("the conjecture *fails*", "a back-reacted spacetime *can* violate"). It can be defended in a conditional, open-question form:

> **C3 (as defended).** The self-consistent achronal ANEC is unproven, and every existing proof stops short of a throat. So a short, quantum-supported one-sided shortcut is not excluded by theorem. If a back-reacted violation on a complete achronal throat geodesic exists, a short throat is a large shortcut (T_thru/T_ext down to 2.1×10⁻¹⁵ at 1 m and 1 ly). It is a time machine once the mouth clock lag reaches about d/c, unless chronology protection acts at the Cauchy horizon. No such solution has been exhibited. On single-scale estimates it would sit within about 10^1.7 to 10^3.7 of the species cutoff in length.

### Concessions (what C3 loses)

1. **"Fails" becomes "unproven".** No self-consistent violation is known [D-27]. Kontou 2024 says "No counterexamples to the self-consistent achronal ANEC have been found in the context of semiclassical gravity" (C3-1, full-text quote, p. 24). C3's own evidence-against already said this. The heading claim overstated it. **Conceded in full.**
2. **Urban–Olum 2010 is not evidence for the self-consistent clause.**
   - Its full text says the stress tensor was computed "in a given background without attempting to impose self-consistency", and that "self-consistency may be enough to enforce the energy conditions" (C3-0 and C3-2, p. 10, full-text).
   - Its spacetime is globally conformal to Minkowski. It therefore has Minkowski light cones and trivial topology, so it can hold neither a throat nor a shortcut (C3-1, attack 1).
   - This also closes RC-03 against C3: the source is now full-text checked, and it supports only "the *non*-self-consistent version fails". **Conceded.**
3. **Ishibashi–Maeda–Mefford count against shortcuts, not for them.**
   - The boundary metric is a fixed source.
   - The violation is anomaly-driven.
   - Their converse theorem says that a boundary which is not causally proper implies a bulk naked singularity (C3-1, abstract quote).
   
   In their holographic setting a shortcut costs cosmic censorship. **Conceded.** I withdraw this source as support.
4. **Positive-frequency Dirac fields [D-12]** violate ANEC only on fixed backgrounds, and the same author finds no self-consistent two-ended solution. **Conceded:** this cuts against sub-hypothesis (a), not for it.
5. **Wall 2010 (sub-hypothesis c).** Wall says the ANEC theorems fail once gravitons are quantised. In the same abstract he proposes a "shear-inclusive" ANEC "proven under an additional assumption" (C3-1). So what is lost is one proof route, not the condition. **Correction accepted.** Sub-hypothesis (c) stays "not excluded, unsupported".
6. **Sub-hypothesis (b), the FGM 2018 quotient, is weak.**
   - M-CONSTRAINTS-17 verifies the algebra only, and the comparison path is a modelling choice.
   - FGM 2018 build their construction to respect topological censorship: "That curve must be achronal, else the two boundaries would be causally connected" (C3-1, p. 30).
   - It is AdS₃, not 4D asymptotically flat.
   
   **Conceded as evidence.** I keep it only as a place where a calculation could be done (see the C8 test below).
7. **The "because" is not the only route to a shortcut.**
   - M-DECOMPOSER-08 (refuted lens item) shows that a static, misaligned one-sided shortcut need not carry a complete achronal throat geodesic. So the conjecture's failure is not necessary for a shortcut, which is C6's point (all three verdicts).
   - I also concede the converse half of C3-0 attack 3: a violation somewhere is not sufficient. The violation must reach about 1/(8a) on the throat geodesic, not merely be negative.
   - **But the time-machine leg does need the failure.** Every mouth-motion route to a CTC passes through an epoch with an achronal throat geodesic (M-DECOMPOSER-08, "what survives"; acknowledged by C3-1 and C3-2). So the conjecture is the binding constraint on time machines, not on shortcuts. C3's distinctive content is therefore the time-machine leg and *aligned* short shortcuts.
8. **"Hence time machines" needs chronology protection to fail as well.** Kay–Radzikowski–Wald is PROVED within its assumptions [K-12] and does not use the achronal ANEC. **Partly conceded.**
   - *What I keep.* The dossier says KRW is interpreted "as support only": its result is that the linear-field QFT description breaks down at base points of a compactly generated Cauchy horizon [K-12, D-C4]. That is a statement that semiclassical gravity stops being the right description there. It is not a proof that the horizon does not form.
   - *Other sources point the same way.* Kim–Thorne find the divergence "extremely weak", and Visser finds that a Roman ring makes the vacuum polarisation "arbitrarily small". Both are abstract-level and are carried in candidates.md under C7.
   - *Corrected wording.* "Shortcuts are possible, and time machines are not excluded by the achronal ANEC. Whether they form is then decided at the Cauchy horizon, in a regime outside semiclassical control." C3-2 is right that semiclassical gravity cannot *exhibit* a time machine.

### Rebuttals (what C3 keeps)

1. **The two scale attacks disagree by more than 3 orders in throat size, and only the generous one bounds what is allowed.** [calc: runs/2026-10-03-traversable-wormhole-shortcuts/calc/crux-C3_scales.py]
   - *C3-0's estimate.* It assumes a "typical" Casimir-like state varying on the curvature scale, |G⟨T_kk⟩| ≲ αN l_P²/R⁴. It gets R_max/l_sp = 0.0168 to 1.047, which is curvature of 0.91 to 3.6×10³ in cutoff units. That is an estimate of what ordinary states give, not a bound.
   - *C3-2's ceiling.* It uses the state-independent Ford–Roman QI, the most a free-field state is *allowed* to give. It gets a_max/l_* = 4.9×10³ (f = 0.01) or 48.9 (f = 0.1), which is curvature of 4.2×10⁻⁸ or 4.2×10⁻⁴ in cutoff units.
   - *The gap.* The QI ceiling is 4.7×10³ times larger in throat size than C3-0's most generous case. To reach it, a state needs α·η = 3.0×10⁶ (f = 0.01) in C3-0's parametrisation, which is 2.2×10⁷ to 8.5×10¹⁰ times C3-0's coefficients.
   - *So C3-0 overstates.* Its "at or below the semiclassical cutoff" describes typical states. The QI does not forbid a throat whose curvature is 4×10⁻⁸ in cutoff units. That is a numerically small expansion parameter, though not a parametric one, as C3-2 rightly says.
   - *What this keeps.* The in-principle question stays open even at a single scale, without appealing to the unexhibited hierarchical (MMP-type) or thin-band constructions that both C3-0 and C3-2 already leave open.
   - *What it does not keep.* It does not rescue any macroscopic claim. With Standard-Model N ≈ 10², a_max = 7.9×10⁻³¹ m (C3-2 §3). A 1 m throat needs ≳ 1.6×10⁶² species (C3-2 §2) or a 10⁻²¹ m band. **The in-practice concession is total.**
2. **The existing proofs exclude exactly the region C3 lives in.**
   - Kontou–Olum 2015 prove achronal ANEC only on backgrounds obeying the null convergence condition, which a throat violates (R_kk = 8πT_kk < 0). C3-1 itself states this limit.
   - Rosso 2020 covers AdS₂×S^{d−2}, dS and AdS (C1, abstract); the flat-space proofs cover Minkowski [K-07d]; Wald–Yurtsever cover 2D, or 4D with a bifurcate Killing horizon [K-07b].
   - Flanagan–Wald 1996 (C3-0) is second-order perturbation theory about flat space.
   
   None of these covers a non-perturbative, multiply connected, back-reacted throat. So the narrowed claim, "not excluded by theorem", is not a technicality. It describes the actual edge of the proofs.
3. **The geometric consequences stand as computed.** No verdict disputes these, and C3-1 confirms the d/c lag as the T_thru → 0 limit of Δ ≥ T_thru + T_ext (M-CONSTRAINTS-13).
   - T_thru/T_ext = 2.1×10⁻¹⁵ for a 1 m throat between mouths 1 ly apart (M-EXAMINER-02).
   - The time-machine lag Δ_CTC → d/c is 3.34×10⁻⁹ s at 1 m, 499 s at 1 AU and 3.16×10⁷ s (1 yr) at 1 ly [calc: crux-C3_scales.py].
   - C3-2's caveat stands: the Ellis ratio treats a two-sided throat as one-sided (M-DECOMPOSER-05). I accept it as illustration only.

### Net position
C3 survives as **the conditional, open branch of Q1**: shortcuts and, unless the Cauchy horizon intervenes, time machines are not excluded, because the self-consistent achronal ANEC is a conjecture whose proofs do not reach throats. Everything else is lost:
- no self-consistent violation is known;
- every cited violation is fixed-background;
- Standard-Model throats are ≤ 7.9×10⁻³¹ m;
- the time-machine leg leaves semiclassical control.

## Deciding observation

**C3's core test.** One calculation decides C3 against C1, and in a sharpened form against C7. Construct a self-consistent solution of G_μν = 8π⟨T_μν⟩ and evaluate the sign of ∫T_kk dλ on a complete *achronal* null geodesic through the throat.
- *Most tractable instance:* FGM 2019 at second order in back-reaction. Does t_min − d go negative? C1's test, read with the opposite sign.
- *Who:* semiclassical-gravity theorists using FGM's perturbative methods [D-07].
- *Precision needed:* the sign only, against corrections of the same order as the "logs".
- *When:* no date is known [D-C3, section 6: "no 2023–2026 proof or refutation"].

**Per rival:**

- **vs C1 (null).**
  - *The test:* the core test above. C1 and C3 differ only on the in-principle half of the no-shortcut leg. C3 concedes C1's legs (ii) and (iii): negative null energy is required, and nothing is payload-capable in practice.
  - *Favours C3:* t_min < d at second order, or any self-consistent ANEC < 0 on a complete achronal geodesic.
  - *Favours C1:* t_min − d ≥ 0 at second order, or a proof of the self-consistent achronal ANEC that covers spacetimes violating the null convergence condition, extending Kontou–Olum 2015 beyond its NCC assumption.
  - *Who:* semiclassical-gravity theorists, or mathematical relativists for the proof. *When:* no date known.

- **vs C2 (long Casimir wormholes, never shortcuts).**
  - *Whether they conflict:* they need not conflict. They separate only on whether "long" is forced or merely chosen. C2 explains MMP's length by the conjecture, through chronal throat lines.
  - *The test:* the MMP energy E = r_e³/(G_N l²) − q/(8l) [Q-10]. Does a stationary self-consistent MMP-family solution exist with mouth separation d > πℓ, i.e. T_thru < T_ext, so that the throat line becomes achronal?
  - *Favours C3:* such a solution exists and is stationary. That is a self-consistent achronal violation.
  - *Favours C2's "never":* the equilibrium is lost first, or the throat lengthens.
  - *Who:* MMP-framework theorists (lowest-Landau-level fermions).
  - *Precision needed:* existence or non-existence, with the ANEC sign on the throat line.
  - *When:* no date known.

- **vs C4 (classical phantom shortcuts).** Both allow shortcuts. They differ on whether *admissible quantum fields* can supply the support.
  - *The test:* a curved-space QEI of Fewster–Smith type evaluated on the Ellis throat [C4's own decisive test].
  - *Favours C3:* the QEI permits |∫T_kk dλ| ≥ 1/(8a) (geometric) at a ≫ l_*, i.e. it relaxes the flat-space ceiling a_max/l_* = 4.9×10³ (f = 0.01) [calc: crux-C3_scales.py, C3-2 §3].
  - *Favours C4:* only labelled phantom or modified-gravity matter supports throats.
  - *Who:* QEI theorists (Fewster, Kontou groups) or numerical-relativity groups.
  - *Precision needed:* the bound's coefficient to within the factor of about 10³–10⁷ that separates C3-0's typical estimate from C3-2's ceiling.
  - *When:* no date known.

- **vs C6 (narrower ban).** C6 says misaligned shortcuts escape even if the conjecture holds.
  - *The test:* C6's own cheapest calculation. For finite mouths in a curved (Schwarzschild-like) exterior with rotated or mirror gluing, does every shortcut configuration carry a complete achronal through-throat null geodesic?
  - *Favours C3:* yes, an achronal through-line always exists. Then C6's loophole closes, and every shortcut needs C3's failure.
  - *Favours C6:* no, at least one misaligned shortcut has no achronal through-line. Then C3's "because" is redundant for shortcuts, and C3 keeps only the time-machine leg.
  - *Who:* mathematical relativists.
  - *Precision needed:* the existence of an achronal line, including the near-grazing rays of achronal length about d/ε² that C6 flags as uncomputed.
  - *When:* no date known; this is the cheapest calculation on this list.

- **vs C7 (clock offset Δ).** C7 is the sharpest discriminator for C3's time-machine leg.
  - *The test:* solve MMP or FGM 2019 self-consistently with the twisted identification (t, x) ~ (t + Δ, x + L), and ask whether stationary solutions exist inside the window Δ_s < Δ < Δ_CTC. At MM scale, with ℓ = d = 3×10³ ly, the window runs from Δ_s = 6.4×10³ yr to Δ_CTC = 1.24×10⁴ yr and is 6.0×10³ yr wide [calc: crux-C3_scales.py].
  - *Favours C3:* a solution survives intact inside the window. By C7's own P2, the conjecture is then falsified without a CTC, which is exactly C3's claim.
  - *Favours C7(i) and against C3:* the solution loses stationarity, or T_thru lengthens, before Δ_s (C7's P1).
  - *Caveat:* the 2D toy already points against C3, since the long equilibrium is lost at Δ/l₀ = 0.1495 (M-DIALECTICIAN-07).
  - *Who:* semiclassical theorists in MMP's lowest-Landau-level framework.
  - *Precision needed:* existence or non-existence across the window.
  - *When:* no date known.

- **vs C8 (two-sided bookkeeping).** They are largely compatible. C8 covers coupled two-sided protocols; C3 needs a coupling-free one-sided spacetime. They separate only through C3's sub-hypothesis (b).
  - *The test:* compute FGM 2018's first-order horizon shift ΔV in the coupling-free Z₂-quotient and compare it with the shortcut threshold |ΔV| > 2ℓe^{−πr₊/ℓ}. The threshold is 0.42ℓ, 0.086ℓ, 3.7×10⁻³ℓ and 1.6×10⁻⁴ℓ at r₊/ℓ = 0.5, 1, 2 and 3 [calc: crux-C3_scales.py; M-CONSTRAINTS-17 verified as algebra only].
  - *Favours C3(b):* the shift exceeds the threshold along a comparison path that FGM's own topological-censorship argument does not already exclude. Information then beats the coupling-free light time with no channel, which C8's "never beat the channel" cannot absorb.
  - *Favours C8 (and C3 loses (b)):* the shift stays below the threshold, or the comparison path is excluded, as FGM 2018's "must be achronal" sentence suggests.
  - *The quantum-hardware tests do not bear on C3* (C8 decisive test).
  - *Who:* AdS₃ semiclassical theorists.
  - *Precision needed:* ΔV to O(1) relative accuracy near the threshold.
  - *When:* no date known.
