Units: SI unless marked; "natural" means ħ = c = 1. T_thru is the earliest arrival through the throat, T_ext the exterior light time, Δ the mouth clock offset, Δ_s = T_thru − d/c and Δ_CTC = T_thru + d/c.

**Defended core after concessions.** Long 4D wormholes held open by quantum-field Casimir energy on chronal null lines (MMP, FGM 2019, and MM with its labelled RS II plus dark-U(1) sector) are traversable in principle. Every computed static, unshifted solution has T_thru > T_ext, but no 4D theorem guarantees this. With Standard-Model fields they carry low-energy, many-quantum signals: total energy in transit per window ≤ |E_min| (MeV to GeV at r_e = 1/TeV, more for smaller admissible r_e). That is about 9 to 2.6×10⁴ electrons, or up to Nq/16 ≈ 10¹³ to 10¹⁵ gap-scale quanta, per window, renewed every πℓ/c. Never a payload. The SM mouths sit less than 1/TeV ≈ 2×10⁻¹⁹ m apart.

## Response to the verdicts

All three verdicts return `weakened`, on the same clause. None attacks "traversable in principle" or the support mechanism. I concede the clause and two qualifications to "never".

**1. "Only single quanta below about 10⁸ eV pass" / "qubit-scale" (C2-0, C2-1, C2-2): conceded in full.**
- The inference in C2 (a) treated the binding energy as the energy of one quantum. It is a cap on the total energy in the throat at one time.
- MMP state the ratio themselves: the binding energy "is larger than the gap by a factor of q" (arXiv:1807.04726 p. 19, quoted in C2-0, C2-1 and C2-2). MM give the capacity as a rate, "of the order of ∼ c2 qubits per 𝓁" (arXiv:2008.06618 p. 7, quoted in C2-1).
- I re-derived the identity the verdicts use, from MMP's energy function (Q-10). E(l) = A/l² − B/l has l* = 2A/B and E_min = −B²/(4A), so |E_min|·l* = B/2. With B = Nq/8 (natural units) this is |E_min|·ℓ = Nq/16 [calc: runs/2026-10-03-traversable-wormhole-shortcuts/calc/crux-C2_thresholds.py].
- So "qubit-scale" is lost. What survives is the energy statement. Per window, at r_e = 1/TeV, the throat holds:
  - 2.6×10¹³ to 1.4×10¹⁵ gap-scale quanta [calc: runs/2026-10-03-traversable-wormhole-shortcuts/calc/falsifier-C2-0_capacity.py; falsifier-C2-1_capacity.py; falsifier-C2-2_capacity.py];
  - or, counting only electrons that can enter from a broken-phase exterior, about 8.8 to 2.55×10⁴, renewed every πℓ/c = 0.22 to 12 ns (C2-1 and C2-2: 7×10⁸ to 1.2×10¹⁴ electrons/s).
- One caveat I keep, which C2-2 also flags: the sustained rate assumes the energy leaves and the throat relaxes on about πℓ/c. MMP's p. 19 statement covers single passage. MM warn that infalling matter which "loses energy accumulates" [D-08]. The per-window count (≥ 9 electrons) does not depend on this, and it already exceeds "single quanta".

**2. The payload leg (all three): stands, and the verdicts confirm it.** 1 kg·c² exceeds |E_min| by 10²⁵·⁶ to 10²⁹·¹ at r_e = 1/TeV (C2-0 log). In the hardest-binding admissible case it still exceeds |E_min| by 2.5×10¹⁶ (C2-2 scan, r_e = 10⁻³⁰ m, |E_min| = 3.6 J).

**3. "r_e = 1/TeV is the boundary case, not typical" (C2-2): conceded.**
- MMP need r_e ≪ d < 1/TeV (p. 27, quoted in C2-2).
- At fixed g, the verified M-ENGINEER-01 form |E_min| = g²ħc/(256π r_e) grows as 1/r_e. So the SM figures in C2 (a) (4.5 MeV to 13 GeV, 0.3 to 17 mK, 2.7×10⁸ kg mouths) are the weakest-binding, coldest, heaviest corner.
- Smaller admissible throats bind up to 2.25×10¹⁹ eV (r_e = 10⁻³⁰ m, C2-2). This strengthens the many-quanta reading and leaves the no-payload reading intact.

**4. "ℓ is not a proper throat length; 2.4 ns is through-throat, not exterior" (C2-2): conceded as a labelling error.**
- ℓ is the AdS₂ parameter setting the exterior-time transit πℓ/c. C2 already says this for MM ("long is temporal"), and it applies equally to the SM row.
- The exterior distance is d < 1.97×10⁻¹⁹ m, so T_ext ≤ 6.6×10⁻²⁸ s and T_thru/T_ext ≥ 3.4×10¹⁷ to 1.8×10¹⁹ [calc: runs/2026-10-03-traversable-wormhole-shortcuts/calc/crux-C2_thresholds.py]. This agrees with C2-0 and C2-2.

**5. The mouth separation is omitted from (a) (C2-0, C2-2): conceded.** The SM-MMP row is a sub-picometre scattering object (d < 1/TeV, or < 1/m_e = 3.86×10⁻¹³ m per MMP p. 27), not a link between distant points. This weakens its practical value and strengthens "not a shortcut".

**6. "Never shortcuts" in 4D is computed, not a theorem (C2-1): conceded.**
- FGM write that "it is difficult to use arguments about causal curves connecting distant points to rigorously bound wormhole transit times" in 4D (fn. 8). They place t_min ≥ d in D ≥ 5 (p. 18) (quoted in C2-1).
- C2's "never" therefore rests on the solutions on record, which all have T_thru > T_ext (MM π𝓁 > d, verified as M-EXAMINER-04; MMP transit/d > 2 [Q-12]; FGM t_min = d + logs [Q-13]), plus the authors' GSL argument.
- I also concede C2-1's correction: Q-13's "approaching 1 from above but not crossing it" overstates FGM's 4D claim. The sign of t_min − T_ext at log order, with T_ext including the exterior Shapiro delay, is not stated in what was read. This is exactly the C3 crux below.

**7. "Never" vs mouth clock offsets (C2-0, C2-1, C2-2): partly conceded.**
- C2's own evidence-against already says "never" holds only while Δ ≈ 0, and that the 2D toys lose the long equilibrium before Δ_s (M-DIALECTICIAN-07; M-MECHANIST-06). So "never" is a dynamical expectation, not a proof.
- One rebuttal-by-calculation narrows C7's threat for the SM row. With d ≤ 1/TeV the shortcut window is 2d/c ≤ 1.3×10⁻²⁷ s, against Δ_s = 0.22 to 12 ns. That is a fraction 1.1×10⁻¹⁹ to 6×10⁻¹⁸ of Δ_s [calc: runs/2026-10-03-traversable-wormhole-shortcuts/calc/crux-C2_thresholds.py]. So for SM-MMP, "one-way shortcut" and "time machine" coincide to about 18 decimal places. Any offset large enough to make a shortcut also makes a CTC, so the question falls wholly to chronology protection.
- C2-2 adds that a sustained velocity difference cannot be kept within d < 1/TeV between equal-mass mouths. For MM, by contrast, the window is wide: 6.0×10³ yr at d = ℓ, with Δ_s = 6.42×10³ yr (same calc; matches C7). So MM is where "never" is really at risk.

**8. Stability and "eternal" (C2-1, C2-0 remaining doubt): no rebuttal, already listed.**
- The stability evidence is preprints only [F-01, D-C5]. FGM 2019 is exponentially fragile [D-07].
- C2-1's flag on MMP p. 24 ("lifetime ... small compared to the time to go through the wormhole") is judged by C2-1 itself to be a likely extraction slip (lifetime ∝ d³/(q² l_p²) ≳ q⁴ l_p ≫ πℓ). I do not rely on it either way. It is unconfirmed, and it bears on the C1 crux below.

**9. "4D" label on MM (C2-0): conceded.** MM's working version is an RS II braneworld (a five-dimensional bulk) with a 4D effective description. C2 (c) already labels it speculative.

## Deciding observation

**Against C1 (null; its strong variant says no traversable wormhole is realisable at all).**
- C2 and C1's three legs are compatible, since C2 concedes no shortcut and no payload. What separates them is DECOMPOSER-B's claim that the long constructions are not viable even in principle.
- *Test:* a complete linear stability analysis of MMP in every perturbation sector, with the fermion response (including the anomaly trace) kept, beyond Sadhukhan's O(α) one-sector result [F-01]. It should be followed by a nonlinear check against Bilotta's "unstable to large fluctuations" [D-C5].
- *Reading:* a mode whose e-folding time is shorter than the transit time πℓ/c favours C1. That is 0.22 to 12 ns for the SM rows, and 9.42×10³ yr for MM (same calc). No such mode keeps C2.
- *Who and precision:* semiclassical theorists, extending Sadhukhan's lowest-Landau-level framework. Growth rates are needed to O(1) relative to 1/(πℓ/c).
- *When:* the Sadhukhan preprints are Sep and Oct 2026. No date is known for a complete analysis.

**Against C3 (achronal ANEC fails; shortcuts possible).**
- *Test:* a second-order (back-reacted) computation of FGM 2019's minimum transit time, with T_ext including the exterior 4D Shapiro delay that FGM fn. 8 flags as IR-log-divergent.
- *Reading:* t_min − T_ext ≥ 0 favours C2. t_min − T_ext < 0 in a self-consistent solution is a Casimir-supported 4D shortcut, which favours C3 and falsifies C2's "never".
- *Who and precision:* semiclassical theorists using FGM's methods [D-07]. The sign must be resolved at the "logs" of t_min = d + logs [Q-13]; my unsourced reading is that these are Shapiro-type terms of order GM ln(d/GM) (geometric units).
- *When:* no date is known.

**Against C4 (classical exotic-matter shortcuts of any design).**
- *Test:* a curved-space QEI (Fewster–Smith type) evaluated on the Ellis/Morris–Thorne throat itself, instead of the flat-space Ford–Roman extrapolation.
- *Reading:* if the curved-space bound still confines admissible negative energy to bands about 1.84×10⁻²¹ m thick at r₀ = 1 m (M-CONSTRAINTS-08), C2 wins: the only admissible route is long, chronal Casimir support. If it allows throat-scale bands of quantum-field negative energy, C4's shortcuts gain an admissible source. The bound must loosen by about 21 orders of magnitude in band width to matter.
- *Who:* mathematical-physics QEI groups. C4's other test, numerical evolution of a stabilised phantom throat with a payload pulse (Kain-type spherical codes [D-13]), separates the two only for labelled phantom matter.
- *When:* no date is known.

**Against C6 (misaligned one-sided shortcuts escape the ban).**
- *Why it bites:* C2's real content is that Casimir support delivers only long wormholes. Wrapping null loops are always chronal (my argument, not sourced: one lap returns to the same spatial point a positive time later, which a resting observer also reaches), so Casimir support does not by itself force T_thru > T_ext. A misaligned short throat would escape the achronal-ANEC argument, as C6 says.
- *Test:* a self-consistent MMP-type (lowest-Landau-level Casimir) solution with rotated or mirror mouth gluing and πℓ < d.
- *Reading:* if one exists, C6 wins and C2's restriction to long wormholes was contingent. If the energy balance forces πℓ > d whatever the gluing, C2 wins. For example, the field-line loop length enters the Casimir term, so larger d weakens the support; the dossier's unchecked RC-17 "πℓ/d → 1 for d ≪ d₀" note points to this.
- *Precision:* the sign of πℓ − d at the solution.
- *Cheaper precursor:* C6's finite-mouth curved-exterior achronality calculation.
- *Who:* semiclassical and mathematical-relativity theorists. No date is known.

**Against C7 (shortcut status set by the clock offset Δ).**
- *Test:* solve MMP or MM self-consistently with the twisted identification (t, x) ~ (t + Δ, x + L), or with an asymmetric mouth redshift, across Δ_s ≤ Δ < Δ_CTC.
- *Reading:* the solution ceasing to exist or lengthening before Δ_s favours C2's "never". Surviving intact into the window favours C7, and also falsifies the self-consistent achronal ANEC.
- *Precision:* existence of solutions across the window. MM is the informative case: window 6.0×10³ yr wide at d = ℓ, Δ_s = 6.42×10³ yr. For SM-MMP the window is ≤ 1.3×10⁻²⁷ s, so it cannot separate the candidates [calc: runs/2026-10-03-traversable-wormhole-shortcuts/calc/crux-C2_thresholds.py].
- *Who:* semiclassical theorists. *When:* no date is known.

**Against C8 (two-sided wormholes are teleportation bookkeeping).**
- *What separates them:* whether a coupling-channel bound transfers to the one-sided, self-supporting constructions.
- *Test:* a calculation in MMP (or in a single-system quotient/twisted-loop simulator protocol) of the quanta that pass the throat per window, compared with the classical bits carried by any exterior channel during the transit.
- *Reading:* C8's MSY-type bound, transferred, predicts quanta ≲ const × bits exchanged [K-15]. C2, as narrowed, predicts Nq/16 ≈ 10¹³ to 10¹⁵ gap quanta per window, or MM's c₂ ≈ 7×10⁷² qubits per ℓ (about 7×10⁶¹ qubits/s; same calc), with no message traffic outside, since the support is vacuum Casimir energy. A capacity that collapses to O(exchanged bits) favours C8's reading; capacity at order Nq/16 with zero exchanged bits favours C2.
- *Who:* holography theorists; quantum-hardware groups for the simulator version [D-16, D-17, Q-39]. The simulator needs about 10⁻⁵ error per gate (C8 estimate, unsourced).
- *When:* no date is known for the theory. The engineer's unsourced estimate for N ≈ 20 hardware is 5 to 10 yr.

**Single most decisive for C2:** the back-reacted MMP/MM solution with a clock offset or asymmetric redshift (the C7 test). It is the only calculation that could falsify "never shortcuts" for the very constructions C2 defends. The FGM second-order sign is the second.
