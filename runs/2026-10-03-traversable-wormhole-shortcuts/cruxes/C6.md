## Response to the verdicts

All three verdicts return `weakened` and agree on one point: the misalignment mechanism is an artefact of point mouths. I concede that point in full. What C6 keeps is narrower: a logical gap, a mouth-scale marginal band, and the separate polyhedral sub-hypothesis.

**1. "Misaligned shortcuts escape" holds only at a = 0 (C6-0, C6-1, C6-2). Conceded.**
- C6's misalignment argument rested only on M-IDEALIZER-01/02 and M-DECOMPOSER-08. Both use point mouths with a rigid direction map n → On. M-DECOMPOSER-08 itself lists "point mouths, a flat exterior and no back-reaction" as its limits, and C6 listed "toy models only" as evidence against.
- The three verdicts used three different finite-mouth models and reached the same result:
  - C6-0, smooth Ellis lensing: the winding Δφ(b) = 2kK(k) runs from 0 to ∞, so some through-ray leaves parallel to its entry direction for every gluing.
  - C6-1, thin shell and tube: the hit point or the incidence angle can be chosen so the ray realigns.
  - C6-2: a flat-exterior minimiser argument.
- Every counterexample C6 cited becomes a complete achronal through-line once a > 0:
  - for L = 0.5d with 10° or 180° rotation, and for mirror gluing, the advance is +49.9 b0 at d = 100 b0 [calc: runs/2026-10-03-traversable-wormhole-shortcuts/calc/falsifier-C6-0_finite_mouth_lines.py];
  - the asymptotic margins are −0.50d to −0.60d [calc: runs/2026-10-03-traversable-wormhole-shortcuts/calc/falsifier-C6-1_thinshell_realign.py; runs/2026-10-03-traversable-wormhole-shortcuts/calc/falsifier-C6-1_tube_realign.py];
  - the line thresholds are L* = 1.16d to 1.20d at a = 0.1d [calc: runs/2026-10-03-traversable-wormhole-shortcuts/calc/falsifier-C6-2_finite_mouth_line.py].
- I re-checked C6-2's hemisphere step independently on 400 random gluings G ∈ O(3), 200 of each determinant sign. Each one has an entry normal n with n·u ≤ 0 and (Gn)·u ≥ 0. The largest best-case excess a·u·(n − Gn) is −0.109a, which is ≤ 0. So the path through the handle is never longer than s + t + L, and every far-pair minimiser threads the throat when L < d [calc: runs/2026-10-03-traversable-wormhole-shortcuts/calc/crux-C6_residual_window.py].
- For the reflection gluings (+I, and diag(1, 1, −1)), only n ⊥ u qualifies, and the excess is exactly 0. The argument still holds there, with equality.
- I also reproduced C6-1's thin-shell 180° threshold √(d² + 4a²): 1.00020d at a = 0.01d and 1.00499d at a = 0.05d. Both are above d (same calc).
- C6's own "if it is wrong" condition was "finite-mouth geometry restores a complete achronal through-line". The verdicts meet it for a flat exterior. C6's prediction that "finite mouths narrow the loophole by O(a/d) but do not close it" is wrong: finite mouths close the misalignment loophole.

**2. The "standard Visser orientation" label for O = −I (C6-1). Conceded.**
- By the decomposer's convention, an orientable gluing is −R with R ∈ SO(3). O = −I has det −1, so the mirror handle is non-orientable. The "standard" label was unsupported.
- This does not matter for the verdict, because the mirror case also acquires a through-line at finite a in both C6-0's and C6-2's models.

**3. The near-grazing "d/ε²" route (C6-0, C6-1, C6-2). Conceded.**
- The restoring rays are deep, not grazing:
  - b_π = 0.79 b0 at L = 0 (C6-0);
  - cos i ≥ 0.93 (C6-1);
  - entry n·u = −0.91 (C6-2).
- That test is dropped.

**4. Graham–Olum's simple-connectedness step is not needed when the advance is positive (C6-0, C6-2). Partly conceded.**
- C6-2 quotes Lemma 1: in a generic spacetime obeying the conjecture, "there are no complete, achronal null geodesics" [Graham & Olum 2007 p. 3, as quoted in C6-2]. The lemma is local to the geodesic and does not assume simple connectedness. So once a through-line exists, the ban applies whatever the topology.
- This moves the open step. It is no longer "the theorem assumes simple connectedness". It is now "does a complete through-line exist in a curved, back-reacted, possibly non-static one-sided geometry?"
- What I keep:
  - C6-0 and C6-2 both say they applied, but did not prove for these geometries, the limit-curve and Arzelà–Ascoli line-existence step;
  - both say a curved exterior was not computed.
- C6-1 agrees that "no theorem extends the shortcut ban to multiply connected (one-sided) spacetimes". So C1's Q1 leg still rests on the conjecture plus an unproved step. That step is now much better supported, but it is not a theorem.

**5. The residual band (C6-0, C6-2). Conceded as real but marginal, and mostly not caused by misalignment.**
- C6-0's window is δ(0) = 1.39738 b0 for an Ellis flare, falling as π²b0²/(2L) for long throats.
- C6-2's band is about 1.5a/c of time advantage, and it is shared with aligned mouths, which have width (π − 2)a. Misalignment adds at most 0.36a on the a = 0.1d sample.
- In SI, these windows are [calc: runs/2026-10-03-traversable-wormhole-shortcuts/calc/crux-C6_residual_window.py]:

  | Mouth | Ellis δ(0)/c | about 1.5a/c | Part due to misalignment (≤ 0.36a/c) |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | SM-MMP, r_e = 2×10⁻¹⁹ m | 9.3×10⁻²⁸ s | 1.0×10⁻²⁷ s | 2.4×10⁻²⁸ s |
  | 1 m throat | 4.7×10⁻⁹ s | 5.0×10⁻⁹ s | 1.2×10⁻⁹ s |
  | MM, r_e = 1.5×10⁷ m | 7.0×10⁻² s | 7.5×10⁻² s | 1.8×10⁻² s |

  - The MM window is 2.2×10⁻⁹ of d = 1 ly.
- Following C6-0, I accept that a shortcut this marginal cannot be told apart from the ambiguity in where "near the mouth" begins. So the band is not a physically meaningful escape. It is a definitional fringe that aligned mouths share.

**6. What I do not concede.**
- **(a) The logical gap.** All three verdicts keep it: no published theorem derives a complete achronal through-line for general one-sided (multiply connected), curved, back-reacted or non-static spacetimes.
  - C6-0 notes that exterior mass shifts the threshold by O(M), which is O(b0) for near-extremal mouths. This is relevant because the only quantum-supported one-sided constructions (MMP, FGM 2019) have near-extremal black-hole mouths with photon spheres.
  - None of the verdicts computed a photon-sphere exterior, so whether the δ band widens there is open for either sign (C6-1 says the same).
- **(b) Sub-hypothesis (b), polyhedral face rays.** C6-0 and C6-2 both say it is untouched.
  - The face rays carry ANEC exactly 0 [Visser 1989, as quoted in candidates.md C4c] and are null-non-generic, so Lemma 1's generic condition fails on them.
  - C6-2's minimiser argument would, for a flat-faced handle, pick the straight face ray itself. That is an achronal complete line with ANEC = 0, which the conjecture (ANEC ≥ 0) allows.
  - This is the one surviving route where the conjecture plus the line argument does not forbid a one-sided shortcut. It still needs edge line mass of −c²/(8G) = −1.68×10²⁶ kg/m (SI) [M-CONSTRAINTS-15], whose quantum support is unknown. I keep it at low confidence, as C6 already labelled it.
- **(c) Non-orientable or mirror sectors.** C6-1 notes that the thin-shell translation gluing G = +I realigns only at grazing incidence. That case rests on labelled mirror-sector or pin-structure physics (C6-1's E3 and E4). It is a speculative remnant, and I carry it only as such.

**Net position after the verdicts.** C6 now reads: "Granting the conjecture, the one-sided shortcut ban rests on a line-existence step that is proved nowhere for curved, back-reacted or non-static geometries. Finite mouths close the misalignment loophole in flat exteriors, leaving only a mouth-scale band (≤ about 1.5a/c, shared with aligned mouths) and the non-generic polyhedral face-ray route." The heading's mechanism ("misaligned one-sided shortcuts escape it") is withdrawn.

## Deciding observation

All the deciding items below are theory calculations. Mathematical-relativity and semiclassical theorists could do them. The dossier gives no date for any of them.

**Main test, common to all rivals: the curved-exterior line-existence calculation.**
- *What.* For a static one-sided handle whose mouths are near-extremal Reissner–Nordström-like (MMP/FGM-type exteriors, with photon spheres), use arbitrary O(3) gluing. Compute in the optical metric whether a complete achronal through-throat null line exists for every L < d − δ, and find δ.
- *Methods.* Extend C6-0's Ellis lensing map and C6-2's minimiser code.
- *Precision.* δ to O(a), say ±0.1a.
- *Reading.*
  - If δ stays O(a) (≲ a few r_e), C6 shrinks to the polyhedral remnant, and C1's Q1 leg needs only the conjecture.
  - If photon-sphere winding opens a band with δ ≫ a, or one that grows with d, C6's gap is physical.

**C1 (null).**
- The two are compatible. C6 only qualifies C1's in-principle Q1 leg.
- *Decider.* The main test, plus a proof of the line-existence lemma for static multiply connected spacetimes with complete optical metric. The proof would turn C6-2's "applied, not proved" Arzelà–Ascoli step into a theorem.
- *Reading.* A proof with δ = O(a) favours C1: the Q1 leg rests on the conjecture alone. A failure of the proof favours C6.
- *Precision.* A yes/no theorem, and δ to O(a).

**C2 (long Casimir wormholes, never shortcuts).**
- *Decider.* Again the main test, applied to the MMP geometry with rotated gluing and L pushed below d.
- *Reading.*
  - A through-line forming at L < d − O(r_e) means the conjecture alone forces quantum-supported one-sided throats to stay long, which is C2's premise. This favours C2 and removes C6's gap.
  - A self-consistent MMP-type solution with L < d and no achronal through-line favours C6.
- *Precision.* L resolved to about r_e, with r_e ≲ 2×10⁻¹⁹ m (SI) for SM-MMP [Q-11]. The SM-MMP throat itself is 0.23 m to 1.1 m long (candidates.md C2a), so this is a relative precision of about 10⁻¹⁸ in L.

**C3 (the conjecture fails).**
- C6 grants the conjecture, so the two split on where a short one-sided semiclassical solution, if found, gets past the ban.
- *Decider.* In any such solution, find the complete achronal through-throat line and compute ∫T_kk dλ along it.
- *Reading.*
  - A line exists with ∫T_kk dλ < 0: the conjecture is falsified, which favours C3.
  - No line exists, or the line has ∫T_kk dλ = 0 (a polyhedral face ray): the ban was narrower than stated, which favours C6.
- *Precision.* The sign of ∫T_kk dλ, and whether the line exists.

**C4 (classical exotic shortcuts).**
- The overlap is the polyhedral face-ray route (C4c and C6b).
- *Decider.* A Visser-cube handle with finite-thickness (smoothed) edges and a small positive-energy test payload on a face ray. Ask two things:
  - Does the face-ray minimiser stay achronal with ANEC ≥ 0? Payload focusing would make it generic, and then Lemma 1 bans it.
  - Can admissible quantum fields supply the edge tension −1.68×10²⁶ kg/m (SI) within QEI bounds?
- *Reading.*
  - Achronal and QEI-admissible: C6(b) survives as a conjecture-compatible shortcut. C4 survives only with phantom matter otherwise.
  - Payload focusing makes the face ray chronal and hands minimality to a negative-ANEC ray: C6(b) falls, and only C4's phantom route (outside the conjecture) remains.
- *Who.* Numerical-relativity groups with Kain-type codes [D-13].
- *Precision.* The sign of the margin to O(payload mass), and the edge stress against the QEI to order of magnitude.

**C7 (clock offset).**
- The verdicts help C7. With finite mouths, the achronal-line threshold coincides with the shortcut threshold Δ_s = T_thru − d/c to within δ ≈ O(a/c) for every orientation. That removes C7's caveat that "achronal epoch is not shortcut epoch for misaligned mouths".
- *Decider.* The main test, repeated with a clock offset Δ, gives the achronal-line threshold Δ_line.
- *Reading.*
  - |Δ_line − Δ_s| ≤ O(a/c) for all gluings means C7's "protection must act at Δ_s" holds universally, and C6 adds nothing there.
  - A finite gap ≫ a/c means C6's narrower ban matters for C7's window.
- *Precision.* Δ to about 0.1a/c. That is about 5×10⁻³ s for MM's r_e = 1.5×10⁷ m (SI, from 1.5a/c = 7.5×10⁻² s [calc: crux-C6_residual_window.py], scaled).

**C8 (two-sided bookkeeping).**
- There is no direct competition. C8 covers two-sided and coupled constructions, while C6 covers one-sided handles under the conjecture, and both can hold.
- *Nearest discriminating calculation.* Apply the finite-mouth line argument to the FGM 2018 Z₂ quotient [D-05], a one-boundary, multiply connected construction with no coupling channel. Ask whether a complete achronal line through the quotient exists once |ΔV| > 2ℓe^{−πr₊/ℓ} [M-CONSTRAINTS-17].
- *Reading.*
  - If it exists, the conjecture caps the quotient's transit, and C8's "never beats the channel" extends to the channel-free one-sided case.
  - If it does not, that construction is a concrete instance of C6's gap.
- *Precision.* The sign of t* − πℓ at the threshold (boundary time).
