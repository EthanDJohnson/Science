# Traversable wormholes: shortcuts, negative energy, and what holds them open: report
*Run 2026-10-03-traversable-wormhole-shortcuts · 2026-10-08 · depth deep · question: "Can a traversable wormhole carry a payload, or even a signal, between two distant points sooner than light travelling between them through the surrounding space, and can one be held open at all without negative energy? Weigh the classical Morris–Thorne and thin-shell wormholes against the constructions proposed since 2017 that use quantum effects or ordinary matter: Gao–Jafferis–Wall, Maldacena–Qi, Maldacena–Milekhin–Popov, Fu–Grado-White–Marolf, Maldacena–Milekhin's "humanly traversable" wormholes, and the Einstein–Dirac–Maxwell solutions of Blázquez-Salcedo, Knoll and Radu, with their critiques. For each, check by calculation which energy conditions it violates, including the averaged null energy condition along the throat, and whether the path through it is a true shortcut or longer than the path outside. Treat the self-consistent achronal averaged null energy condition, and chronology protection against wormhole time machines, as central cruxes. For whatever survives, what would it take to create or widen one enough to pass a payload, in mass, energy and technology, and what is the nearest experiment or observation that could test any part of it, including the 2022 "wormhole on a quantum processor" and astronomical searches for natural wormholes?"*

## Bottom line
No. In established physics no known traversable wormhole delivers a signal, let alone a payload, before light travelling around it, and every throat needs negative null energy: that second part is a theorem of general relativity, and the newer "ordinary matter" designs take their negative energy from the quantum vacuum of ordinary fields. Those designs are real solutions, but deliberately long: the human-scale one takes 9,400 years seen from outside against 0.16 seconds for the traveller. Shortcuts exist only on paper, with hypothetical phantom matter. Nothing passes a kilogram within a century; the cheapest real-universe design needs magnetic monopoles, never observed, and fields 10³⁴ times the laboratory record. The 2022 "wormhole on a chip" tested a teleportation signature, not spacetime. Confidence about 0.9.

## Why, candidate by candidate

**C1 (null).** For: pointwise NEC violation at a throat is a theorem [K-02]; the radial ANEC of any static two-ended throat is strictly negative in GR whatever the matter (M-CONSTRAINTS-02, verified); every published self-consistent one-sided solution is long (MM T_thru/T_ext = π at d = ℓ, M-EXAMINER-04; MMP > 2; FGM 2019 d + logs), and the two-sided ones deliver nothing without their coupling [D-01]. In practice SM-MMP is 24.2–29.1 orders short of 1 kg binding energy and 34.2 orders short in field, with zero magnetic charge demonstrated (lens-engineer_cost_table.py.log; M-ENGINEER-05/06/09). Against: the in-principle no-shortcut leg rests on the self-consistent achronal ANEC conjecture plus a line-existence step that is not a theorem; M-DECOMPOSER-08 (refuted) broke the lenses' point-mouth bridge, which the three C6 refuters then restored for finite mouths in flat exteriors, so the step is well supported but unproved. All three refuters narrowed C1 because the brief counts labelled phantom matter as admissible: a phantom Ellis throat passes a light-speed signal with 28 e-folds to spare before its unstable mode grows (falsifier-C1-0_ellis_lifetime.py.log). The advocate conceded this and dropped the "nothing realisable at all" variant. Senses of "negative energy": pointwise, forced; ANEC on some null line, forced for asymptotically flat globally hyperbolic wormholes [K-01]; achronal ANEC, satisfied only by long wormholes; WEC, forced through the NEC, though a static observer can see ρ > 0 (M-CONSTRAINTS-03). Decisive test: the sign of t_min − T_ext for FGM 2019 at second order, including the 4D Shapiro logs.

**C8 (two-sided bookkeeping).** For: GJW state nothing crosses when decoupled and that the construction "cannot be used to violate causality"; the message must be inserted 4.6R–138R before the coupling (M-EXAMINER-08); MSY's bound is "a few bits", parametric [K-15]; both sides of the Sycamore dispute agree no spacetime was made [D-C1]; C8-0 computed that every run at N ≤ 100 sits at Schwarzian coupling ≤ 0.3, far from a semiclassical bulk. Against: "teleportation" is wrong for Maldacena–Qi, an always-on quantum coupling with no classical step; the geometry fixes a transfer time (J/μ)^{1/3} shorter than bare hopping (Plugge et al.; MQ p. 28), but inside the coupling's entangling capacity by 15 to 6×10⁴ (falsifier-C8-0). The advocate conceded the wording and adopted the "whole coupled Hamiltonian" benchmark. Decisive test: two-copy chaotic sparse SYK at N ≥ 50 with μ → 0 and LOCC controls; needs gate error 3.5×10⁻⁶–2.3×10⁻⁵ against ~10⁻³–2×10⁻³ demonstrated (Granet et al. 2025).

**C6 (narrower ban).** For: Graham–Olum's theorem assumes simple connectedness (full-text, p. 5); no published theorem derives a complete achronal through-line for curved, back-reacted one-sided geometries. Against: three independent finite-mouth calculations (Ellis lensing; thin shell and tube; flat-exterior minimiser) find a complete achronal through-throat null line for every gluing whenever L < d, with line thresholds L* = 1.16–1.20 d at a = 0.1d (falsifier-C6-2_finite_mouth_line.py.log); the point-mouth escape is an artefact of a conical mouth. The advocate withdrew the heading mechanism. What survives: a mouth-scale band ≤ 1.5a/c shared with aligned mouths (0.075 s for MM) and the polyhedral face-ray route (ANEC exactly 0, non-generic; low confidence). Decisive test: the same line calculation in a near-extremal Reissner–Nordström exterior with photon spheres.

**C7 (clock offset).** For: Δ_s and Δ_CTC verified six times plus 20,000 random cases; for MM at d = 1000 ly, 8.4×10³ and 1.04×10⁴ yr; a gentle β ≤ 0.03 circuit respects MM's Ω ≪ 1/ℓ condition and reaches Δ_s in 10⁷–10⁸ yr, 18 orders inside MM's 3×10²⁶ yr coalescence time (C7-2). Against: the barrier coincides with Δ_s only for aligned gluing (axial symmetry makes the static pair aligned, a symmetry argument, not a computed throat map); "one barrier governs both cruxes" holds only if the conjecture holds; the Sgr A* ISCO and 0.9c routes violate MM's own conditions; M-MECHANIST-05 (refuted) shows the negative null energy on the closing ray shrinks, not grows, toward the threshold. Decisive test: a self-consistent MMP solution with twisted identification (t, x) ~ (t + Δ, x + L) scanned across the window.

**C4 (classical phantom shortcuts).** For: transit and energy-condition tables verified (M-EXAMINER-02/03, M-CONSTRAINTS-01/05/15); T_thru/T_ext = 2.1×10⁻¹⁵ at 1 m and 1 ly; a light-speed crossing uses 23.5 e-folds of the ghost mode against 58–80 available; Bronnikov et al. 2013 give a linearly stable phantom-fluid Ellis throat; Poisson–Visser shells are stable for β₀² < 0. **C2 (long Casimir wormholes).** For: MMP is a peer-reviewed self-consistent perturbative solution whose energy minimum was reproduced three times; its ANEC-violating loops are chronal (−πc/(3L) per loop, M-CONSTRAINTS-12); flat-space QIs do not apply (sampling time exceeds l_B by 3.3×10⁵–2.8×10¹⁸, M-CONSTRAINTS-10); MMP's SM row has T_thru/T_ext ≥ 1.7×10¹¹ (falsifier-C2-0). **C3 (conjecture fails).** For: it is a conjecture [K-07a]; Kontou–Olum 2015 prove achronal ANEC only on NCC-obeying backgrounds, which throats violate; Wall's route fails with quantised gravitons. ## Assumptions every surviving answer shares
- Semiclassical gravity, G_μν = 8π⟨T_μν⟩, is the right description at throat scale; quantum-gravity topology change or primordial wormholes are not supplied for free.
- The published static solutions have mouth clock offset Δ = 0 (fixed by symmetry), and point- or finite-mouth flat-exterior models capture achronality; no curved-exterior, back-reacted line calculation exists.
- Flat-space QEIs extrapolate to curved throats with sampling ratio f ≈ 0.01; f = 0.1 moves size bounds by 100.
- MMP's energy function and its N-species scaling, with the lenses' readings g = 0.06 or e and N_eff = 1 or 54 (a factor ~25 spread in |E_min|).
- The demonstrated anchors: Casimir 0.27 J/m³ at 0.2 µm, 1200 T, 38 pK, 592 EJ/yr, gate error 4.2×10⁻³ (2022) or 2.2×10⁻³ (2025).
If a stable phantom field, a lab QEI violation, or macroscopic magnetic charge turns out real, the in-principle ranking changes; the practice verdict does not.

## What would change this verdict
1. **FGM 2019 at second order** (semiclassical theorists; no date): t_min − T_ext < 0 would raise C3 to ~0.3 or more and remove C1's in-principle leg; ≥ 0 would push C3 below 0.1.
2. **Self-consistent MMP or MM with a clock offset** (MMP-framework theorists; no date): a stationary solution inside the 2d/c window falsifies the conjecture without a CTC (C7's P2, C3 up); loss of the solution before Δ_s turns C1's and C2's "never" into dynamical results.
3. **Negative-energy sources**: a curved-space QEI on the Ellis throat (QEI groups) or a time-resolved laboratory sub-vacuum energy density in J/m³ against the Fewster–Eveson bound (none exists); and primordial magnetic black-hole searches with Andromeda fields (Bai et al. 2020), the only observational class aimed at MMP-type mouths. Any macroscopic magnetic charge halves the practice-leg margin of C1 in kind, not in orders.
