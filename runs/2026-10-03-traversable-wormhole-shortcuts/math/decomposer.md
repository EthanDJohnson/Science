## Refuted
- M-DECOMPOSER-08: **"one-sided shortcut ⇒ complete achronal null geodesic through the throat" (L6) fails when the two mouths are rotated relative to each other.** The lens's step "no curve in another class (exterior, or multiple passes) beats it" is the one that fails. Counterexample: in the toy model, take a static throat (s = 0) of length L = 0.5d and mouths rotated by any nonzero angle (10° or 180°) about an axis perpendicular to the separation e. Then T_thru/T_ext = 0.5, so it is a shortcut. Yet every complete null geodesic through the throat is chronal, with best margin −0.5d. The reason is that a straight line through the throat must keep its direction (Ru = u) or the exterior beats it at large affine distance. The only such directions lie along the rotation axis n̂. For far-apart points along n̂, the throat saves only d(e·n̂) − τ.
  - **Corrected form (toy model).** A complete achronal throat-crossing null geodesic exists iff −d < τ ≤ d·max|e·n̂|, the maximum taken over directions n̂ fixed by R. For aligned mouths (R = I, or an axis along e) this reduces to the lens's −d < τ ≤ d. For a zero-length thin shell (τ = 0) it holds marginally whatever R is.
  - **What survives.** The "shortcut stage = achronal stage" identification in F5 fails for misaligned mouths, so a static, finite-length, misaligned one-sided shortcut with d|e·n̂| < L < d escapes achronal ANEC on its throat geodesics. Even so, every mouth-motion route to a CTC still passes through an epoch with an achronal throat geodesic (−d < τ ≤ 0 always qualifies; checked at R = 180°, τ = −0.5d), so F5's conclusion that achronal ANEC would act before a CTC forms survives in the toy model.
  - **Dependents.** Finding 4 (status "derived"), L6, Q1.4a, and the "if and only if" in [DECOMPOSER-A]: "one-sided shortcuts … are excluded if and only if self-consistent achronal ANEC holds" holds only for aligned mouths or throat lengths below d·max|e·n̂|. [DECOMPOSER-C]'s merger of the two cruxes and [DECOMPOSER-F]'s elimination need restating with the corrected threshold, though their conclusion about conversion holds. The load-bearing paragraph "If L5 holds (and L6 holds), no admissible wormhole is a shortcut" inherits the gap.
  - **Caveats.** The model has point mouths, a flat exterior and no back-reaction, and the "none" result rests on a direction grid plus the analytic fixed-direction argument. Whether self-consistent physics allows such a configuration is not a math question.
- M-DECOMPOSER-09: under the scaling MM use (and Q-52 uses), the MT-like criterion "1 g over 2 m" (L14) gives r_e ≳ 1.35×10⁸ m, a factor √80 ≈ 8.9 above MM's 1.5×10⁷ m. It does not give a factor of about 4.5 or 6.8×10⁷ m. The 6.8×10⁷ m figure (factor √20 ≈ 4.5) belongs to a 0.5 m body at 1 g. This affects Q3.1's throat-size anchor and L14's "if it fails" column. It changes no candidate's status, but the engineer's cost anchor under the MT criterion should use 1.35×10⁸ m (scaling only; MT's own equations, which carry γ² factors, were not opened).

## Formalizable
- M-DECOMPOSER-01: for reals L, d > 0 and s: (L + s < d) ∧ (L + s + d > 0) holds for every s in the open interval (−(L+d), −(L−d)) of length 2d, and the set {−s : L + s + d ≤ 0} lies strictly above {−s : L + s < d}'s lower end; i.e. (L+d) − (L−d) = 2d > 0.
- M-DECOMPOSER-08: for e, u unit vectors in ℝ³, R ∈ SO(3), d > 0, τ ∈ ℝ: inf over Λp, Λq ≥ 0 of |de + Λp u + Λq Ru| − Λp − Λq − τ is ≥ 0 iff Ru = u and τ ≤ d(e·u). Hence sup over u of that infimum is ≥ 0 iff τ ≤ d·max{e·u : Ru = u, |u| = 1}.
- M-DECOMPOSER-10: for unit u, v ∈ ℝ³ and Λp, Λq ≥ 0: |de + Λp u + Λq v| ≤ d + Λp + Λq (triangle inequality), so τ > d implies the margin is negative.
- M-DECOMPOSER-02: (k+1)/(k−1) is decreasing on k > 1 with limit 1 as k → ∞.
## Notes
- M-DECOMPOSER-04: the 1.7×10⁶ yr (0.1c) and 1.5×10⁴ yr (0.9c) trips and their energies buy the 8425 yr shortcut lag, not the CTC. The CTC lag (1.04×10⁴ yr) needs 2.08×10⁶ yr at 0.1c, 1.85×10⁴ yr at 0.9c, and 2.08×10⁴ yr parked at α = 0.5. At 0.1c the mouth covers 1.7×10⁵ ly, far more than d = 1000 ly, so the motion must be a circuit or an oscillation that keeps the separation near d. The lag formula holds for any constant-speed path, so the scale estimate stands.
- M-DECOMPOSER-05: the Ellis throat is two-sided; treating its mouths as sitting 1 km to 1 ly apart in one ambient space is the lens's idealisation, and the ratios depend on T_ext = d/c (using (d−2R)/c changes the 1 km value by 2%).
