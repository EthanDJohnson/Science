Notes on scope:
- **M-01, M-05, M-08:** the formulas follow from MMP eq. 5.30–5.31, which are quoted (Q-10), not re-derived from the MMP solution. Two inputs are the lens's own, as it says: the N-species extension (Casimir term ×N, so \|E_min\| ∝ N²) and the reading g = 0.06. The algebra is exact under those assumptions.
- **M-05:** the "1 kg needs 25.6 more orders" gap uses the (g = 0.06, N = 54) case. Under the lens's own sensitivity case (g = 0.3, N = 54, \|E_min\| = 5.23×10⁻⁸ J) the gap is 24.2 orders. At N = 1 and g = 0.06 it is 29.1 orders. ENGINEER-A's "at least 25 orders" is therefore at least 24 orders across the lens's stated range. The conclusion does not change.
- **M-08:** the lens's 3.5-order difference from MM's N_f > 10⁵² is accounted for: 2.9 orders from the 256π factor, 0.18 from r_e = 1.5×10⁷ m against 10⁷ m, and the rest from rounding MM's figure.
- **M-09:** this checks only that the Schwinger-form exponent reduces exactly to S_BH at B = B_h, and the numbers under that form. That this form approximates Garfinkle–Strominger's instanton action is the lens's assumption; it was not checked against their paper.
- **M-10:** for a traveller falling from rest at infinity, the exact head-on blueshift at the throat is 2γ², not γ². With 2γ² and the mean CMB photon energy of about 2.7k_BT, the required ambient temperature is about 5×10⁻²² K rather than 2.9×10⁻²¹ K, about 0.7 orders lower. The cooling time grows by about ln(5.4)/H_Λ ≈ 3×10¹⁰ yr. These are order-one factors and leave the conclusion unchanged. The 1 eV tolerance is the lens's criterion.
- **M-12:** the q/μ < 1 regime and BKR's charge normalisation are quoted (Q-19). The lens's range covers both Gaussian and Heaviside–Lorentz conventions; which one BKR use was not checked.
- **M-13:** the gate model 2kN²n_T is the lens's unsourced estimate. Only its arithmetic and the independent-error fidelity model are checked.
- **Not checked, because they are quoted and not derived:** the MMP equations and validity condition, the Ford–Roman band formula (Q-21), γ, ℓ and E_bin for MM (Q-02), the echo delay πℓ/c (Q-06), the S2 sensitivities (Q-43), Kain's throat radii, the Casimir smallest gap, and the 1200 T, 38 pK, 45.5 T and 592 EJ anchors.

## Formalizable
- M-ENGINEER-01: for r, q, G > 0, f(ℓ) = r³/(Gℓ²) − q/(8ℓ) on ℓ > 0 has a unique critical point ℓ* = 16r³/(Gq). It is the global minimum, and f(ℓ*) = −Gq²/(256r³). Also, for N ≥ 1 and 0 < g²N ≤ 1: N²g²/(256πr) ≤ N/(256πr).
- M-ENGINEER-02: for b₀ > 0, 2∫_{b₀}^{∞} (−b₀²/(4πr⁴))·4πr² dr = −2b₀.
- M-ENGINEER-11: for G, M, d, ε₀ > 0 with Q² = 4πε₀GM², G(GM²/d² + Q²/(4πε₀d²))/c⁴ = 2(GM/(c²d))².
- M-ENGINEER-13: for n ≥ 1, 1 − 2^{−1/n} ≤ (ln 2)/n, and n(1 − 2^{−1/n}) → ln 2 as n → ∞.
- M-ENGINEER-14: for M > 0, f(r) = (1 − M/r)² on r > M: f/r² has its unique critical point (a maximum) at r = 2M, where r/√f = 4M. For f = 1 − 2M/r on r > 2M, the critical point is r = 3M and r/√f = 3√3M.
- M-ENGINEER-10: for F ∈ (0, 1], e ≥ 1 and ε > 0, with u = (e/F, √((e²/F − 1)/H)) and k = (ε/F, −ε/√(FH)): −g(u, k) = (eε/F)(1 + √(1 − F/e²)). As F → 0 with e = 1, F·(−g(u, k))/ε → 2.
