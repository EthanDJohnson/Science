## Unverified
- None of the eight claims is unverified. Two caveats on verified claims:
  - M-DIALECTICIAN-03: Finding 6 speaks of "the B→A throat null geodesic". In the idealised model, the throat segment is achronal exactly in Δ_s < Δ < Δ_CTC. The complete geodesic is achronal across the whole window only if it enters B from the side facing away from A and leaves A facing away from B, along the mouth axis. A straight off-axis geodesic at angle θ stays chronal for Δ − Δ_s < d(1 − cos θ) (at 60°, 2.05 is chronal and 2.5 achronal, with T = 3 and d = 1). A geodesic whose exit direction differs from its entry direction stays chronal throughout. The conclusion of Finding 6 needs only one achronal complete geodesic through the throat, and the on-axis one supplies it, so the claim stands as used. It is a property of the idealised model: point mouths, flat exterior, no Shapiro delay, and an identification that permits the on-axis geodesic. The model does not show that MM's actual mouth geometry has such a geodesic. Below Δ_s the throat segment is itself chronal, so no geodesic through the throat is achronal there. That confirms the lower edge of the window.
  - M-DIALECTICIAN-07: the toy result is checked as algebra only. Whether MMP's lowest-Landau-level sector behaves as a 2D CFT on a twisted loop of length l, with r_e fixed, is the lens's labelled assumption and is not mathematics. One check in the log (lim f as x → δ⁺ = −∞) came back UNDECIDED because the checker cannot compare with infinity. SymPy returned −∞, and the grid check on f′ > 0 settles the monotonicity.
## Formalizable
- M-DIALECTICIAN-01: for reals T > d > 0 and Δ ≥ 0: T − Δ < d ⇔ Δ > T − d; T − Δ + d < 0 ⇔ Δ > T + d; and (T + d) − (T − d) = 2d.
- M-DIALECTICIAN-06: for r, G, q > 0, E(l) = r³/(G l²) − q/(8l) on l > 0 has a unique minimiser l₀ = 16r³/(Gq), with E(l₀) = −Gq²/(256 r³) = −(r/G)(r/l₀)².
- M-DIALECTICIAN-07: for f_δ(x) = x⁻² − x[(x−δ)⁻² + (x+δ)⁻²] on x > δ, there is δ_c ∈ (0.1494, 0.1496) such that f_δ has a strict local minimum for 0 ≤ δ < δ_c and f′_δ > 0 on (δ, ∞) for δ > δ_c.
- M-DIALECTICIAN-04 (finite-dimensional part): for the 2×2 boost Λ with velocity Δ/L and T′ = ρ′·I₂ (covariant components) with ρ′ = −πc/(6(L² − Δ²)), the pullback evaluated on (½, ∓½) equals −πc/(12(L ∓ Δ)²).
- M-DIALECTICIAN-03 (discrete form): in the weighted digraph with nodes {B, A}, edge weights w(B→A) = min(T − Δ, d) and w(A→B) = min(T + Δ, d), throat points s ∈ [0, T] and the stated entry and exit costs, no pair of throat points (s₁ < s₂) has a path cost below s₂ − s₁, and no pair (s₂ → s₁) has one below s₁ − s₂, if and only if T − d ≤ Δ ≤ T + d.
