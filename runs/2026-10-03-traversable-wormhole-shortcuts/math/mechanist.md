## Refuted
- M-MECHANIST-05 (finding 9; O4 item 4; the "support grows along the closing direction" reading in MECHANIST-B and O3): in the twisted 2D loop the null direction that closes at Δ = L is the one whose identification shift in its own null coordinate is L + Δ, so T_kk along it is −πc/(3(L + Δ)²). It weakens as Δ grows (factor (L/(L + Δ))² = 0.31 at Δ_sc for MM at D/c = 10³ yr, 0.43 at 3×10³ yr, 0.25 at 1 yr), and the ANEC per lap along the closing null curve stays finite, −πc/(12L), at Δ = L. The (L − Δ)⁻² enhancement (27, 4.3, 2.2×10⁷) and the divergence at Δ = L are in T_kk along the opposite, non-closing null direction, which is the stress of radiation co-moving with the closing rays. The divergence of the energy density at Δ = L (factor κ) stands, so finding 10's κ, which is the average of the two components, is not affected by the mislabelling. What fails is the claim that the negative null energy on the would-be achronal (closing) geodesic grows toward the shortcut and CTC thresholds. In the toy it shrinks.
- M-MECHANIST-06 (finding 10; MECHANIST-B's "folds at 0.25–0.5 Δ_sc"): within the lens's own toy model, the long branch for D/c = 3×10³ yr survives to Δ = 3.62×10³ yr. At Δ = 3.26×10³ yr the roots are still 1.67×10³ and 6.63×10³ yr. So the fold is at 0.56 Δ_sc, not 3.3×10³ yr = 0.52 Δ_sc. Corrected range: 0.25–0.56 Δ_sc. The other two cases check out. The qualitative reading (the fold comes before the no-feedback shortcut threshold) is unchanged. The model's physics (l_eq ∝ 1/Casimir coefficient, with κ's L-dependence ignored when minimising) was not checked. It is the lens's labelled assumption, and it uses the energy-density enhancement κ, which M-MECHANIST-05 confirms.

- M-MECHANIST-01: for reals T_w ≥ 0, d > 0, Δ, take the directed multigraph on {A, B} with edge weights w(B→A) ∈ {T_w − Δ, d} and w(A→B) ∈ {T_w + Δ, d}. Some closed walk has total weight ≤ 0 iff |Δ| ≥ T_w + d. Also T_w − Δ < d iff Δ > T_w − d.
- M-MECHANIST-05: for a symmetric 2×2 tensor T' = ρ·diag(1, 1) with ρ = −πc/(6(L² − Δ²)), and the Lorentz map Λ with β = Δ/L (0 ≤ Δ < L), (Λ⁻¹k)ᵀ T' (Λ⁻¹k) = −πc/(3(L ± Δ)²) for k = (1, ±1), and the k = (1, 0) contraction divided by −πc/(6L²) equals L²(L² + Δ²)/((L − Δ)²(L + Δ)²).
