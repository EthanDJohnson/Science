## Refuted
- M-IDEALIZER-07: the lens's own bound b₀ ≥ γv√(ξ/a_max) grows without limit as v → c, so ultra-relativistic travel through an Ellis throat does not give 1.5×10⁷ m. That figure (and 1.35×10⁸ m for 2 m at 1 g) is the bound at γv = c, i.e. v = c/√2. At MM's crossing γ ≈ 2×10¹² an Ellis-type throat would need b₀ ≥ 3.0×10¹⁹ m. Corrected form: MM's 1.5×10⁷ m comes from a different geometry. The AdS₂×S² throat (equal radii) has no transverse tide, and its longitudinal tide c²ξ/r_e² does not change under a boost, so r_e ≥ c√(ξ/a_max) at any crossing speed. Dependents:
  - F6: the sentence "Ultra-relativistic travel reproduces MM's 1.5×10⁷ m" and the claim that the redshift-driven ultra-relativistic crossing is what makes a quantum long throat large.
  - The scaling-law row "1.35×10⁸ m (v → c, 2 m at 1 g)".
  - IDEALIZER-E: the phrase "Any human-traversable throat crossed at relativistic speed has r ≳ c√(ξ/a_max) … the cost floor is universal". The number survives for MM-type throats, and as a lower bound for Ellis throats with γv ≥ c, but for a different reason than the lens gives. It is not universal: Ellis throats need far more at high γ and far less at low speed (505 m at 10 km/s).
