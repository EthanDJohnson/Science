# Analysis: mechanist (causes and mechanisms)

## Method applied
For each construction and for the owned worked calculation (time-machine conversion), write the causal chain (process -> what it acts on -> what sustains it -> what ends it), classify the mechanism by kind and governing theory, evidence each link with [D-..] or mark it assumed, and look for mechanisms the dossier misses.

## Findings
1. **MTY 1988 conversion is a claim about any maintained traversable wormhole, not about shortcuts.** [new: Morris, Thorne & Yurtsever 1988, PRL 61, 1446, doi:10.1103/PhysRevLett.61.1446, abstract (INSPIRE API), "It is argued that, if the laws of physics permit an advanced civilization to create and maintain a wormhole in space for interstellar travel, then that wormhole can be converted into a time machine with which causality might be violatable."] The same abstract ties the question to "whether field theory enforces an averaged version of the weak energy condition". Status: peer-reviewed, "argued", not proved.
2. **Kim & Thorne 1991: the vacuum-polarisation divergence at the chronology (Cauchy) horizon is real but weak in the semiclassical calculation; whether it stops CTCs is two competing conjectures.** [new: Kim & Thorne 1991, PRD 43, 3929, doi:10.1103/PhysRevD.43.3929, abstract (INSPIRE API), "The renormalized stress-energy tensor is found to diverge as one approaches the Cauchy horizon.\nHowever, the divergence is extremely weak: so weak, that as seen in the rest frame of one of the wormhole mouths the vacuum polarization's gravity distorts the spacetime metric near the mouth by only δgμνVP∼(lP/D)(lP/Δt), where Δt is the proper time until one reaches the Cauchy horizon and D is the distance between the..."] and "Hawking, in response to this, has conjectured that the spacetime near the Cauchy horizon remains classical until DΔt (which in a certain sense is frame invariant) gets as small as ∼lP2, and correspondingly until δgμνVP∼1, and that, as a result, the vacuum-polarization divergence will prevent the formation of closed timelike curves." So the decisive regime (D·Δt ~ l_P²) is quantum-gravitational on both readings: chronology protection is unsettled by semiclassical physics, consistent with [D-C4], [K-12].
3. **Graham & Olum 2007's wormhole theorem does not cover one-sided wormholes; their own counterexample is a long one-sided throat.** [new: Graham & Olum 2007, PRD 76, 064001, arXiv:0705.3193 (arXiv preprint text), full-text, p. 5: "We must also restrict ourselves to simply-connected spacetimes, which means that the wormholes we rule out are only those which connect one asymptotically flat region to another, not those which connect a region to itself."] and p. 5 to 6: "Let M be a static spacetime with a single asymptotically flat region and a wormhole connecting the region to itself, and suppose the throat of the wormhole is longer than the distance between the mouths on the outside. Any causal path through the wormhole emerges in the future of the place where it entered, and thus is not achronal. We can still find fastest paths through the wormhole, but they are chronal." So the conjecture constrains a one-sided wormhole only through (i) its CTC theorems and (ii) any complete achronal null geodesic that threads the throat. This sharpens [K-07a] and [D-27].
4. **Graham–Olum's no-time-machine theorems transfer Tipler's and Hawking's theorems to the self-consistent achronal ANEC.** [new: Graham & Olum 2007, arXiv:0705.3193, full-text, p. 6: "Theorem 3 (No construction of time machines — Hawking version) Let M, g be an asymptotically flat, globally hyperbolic spacetime satisfying self-consistent achronal ANEC and the generic condition, with a partial Cauchy surface S. Then M, g cannot have a compactly generated Cauchy horizon H+(S)."] The mechanism: the generators of a compactly generated Cauchy horizon are complete achronal null geodesics, and the conjecture forbids ANEC violation on them. So under the conjecture, mouth-motion conversion fails for long and short wormholes alike; without it, only Kim–Thorne vs Hawking (finding 2) remains.
5. **Roman ring: semiclassical chronology protection can be evaded by distributing the time shift over many wormholes.** [new: Visser 1997, PRD 55, 5212, arXiv:gr-qc/9702043, abstract (arXiv page): "no subset of wormholes is near to chronology violation, though the combined system can be arbitrarily close to chronology violation. I show that (with enough wormholes in the ring) the gravitational vacuum polarization (the expectation value of the quantum stress-energy tensor) can be made arbitrarily small."] This is a mechanism the dossier lacks; it targets the vacuum-polarisation (Hawking/Kim–Thorne) version of protection, not the achronal-ANEC version (the closed generator of the ring is still achronal, my reading).
6. **Two thresholds, not one (owned calculation).** With mouth B's clock lagging the exterior-synchronised clock of A by Δ and a throat traversal time T_w (mouth proper time), the B→A throat arrival time is T_w − Δ, so the wormhole becomes a one-way shortcut at Δ_sc = T_w − D/c and contains a closed null curve at Δ_ctc = T_w + D/c (exterior-synchronised time, any units). A non-shortcut wormhole is therefore not protected from conversion: it needs a larger time offset, by T_w. MM worked case (T_w = πℓ/c ≈ 9.4×10³ yr, [Q-06]): D/c = 10³ yr gives Δ_sc = 8.4×10³ yr and Δ_ctc = 1.04×10⁴ yr; D/c = 3×10³ yr gives 6.4×10³ and 1.24×10⁴ yr. A short throat (T_w → 0) is a shortcut already at Δ = 0, and Δ_ctc = D/c: 3.3 ns at 1 m, 499 s at 1 AU, 1 yr at 1 ly (SI). 7. **Mouth-motion cost (owned calculation).** Lag rate 1 − 1/γ: 5.0×10⁻³ at v = 0.1c, 0.564 at 0.9c. Short throat at D = 1 ly: 200 yr of motion at 0.1c, 1.8 yr at 0.9c. 8. **Gravitational conversion (owned calculation; a natural mechanism the dossier lacks).** Lag rate 1 − √(1 − 3φ) on a circular orbit (φ = GM/rc², Schwarzschild): 0.293 at the Sgr A* ISCO; 3.1×10⁻⁵ at 0.01 pc from Sgr A*; 7.0×10⁻¹⁰ static on Earth's surface. This is a mechanistic consequence (assumed: mouths survive and stay bound), not a sourced result.
9. **2D toy: the support itself diverges as the offset approaches closure.** For a c = 1 2D CFT on a loop of light-length L = T_w + D/c with twisted identification (t, x) ~ (t + Δ, x + L), boosting the rest-frame Casimir tensor gives T_kk = −πc/(3(L − Δ)²) along the direction that closes and −πc/(3(L + Δ)²) along the other (ħ = c = 1; Δ → 0 recovers −πc/(3L²) for both). [calc: same file, symbolic] This is the MMP support mechanism in miniature: MMP's ANEC-violating null lines wrap the field-line circle [D-06]. At the no-feedback shortcut threshold the closing-direction enhancement (L/(L − Δ))² is 27 for MM at D/c = 10³ yr, 4.3 at 3×10³ yr and 2.2×10⁷ at 1 yr. So the Casimir support grows, not shrinks, as mouth motion approaches the shortcut and CTC; it becomes divergent (negative) only at Δ = L, the CTC threshold. Assumed: the quantum state follows adiabatically (the boosted Casimir vacuum), and the 2D loop model represents MMP's lowest-Landau-level fermions.
10. **Toy feedback: the long equilibrium folds before the shortcut threshold.** Feeding the enhancement κ = L²(L² + Δ²)/((L − Δ)²(L + Δ)²) into MMP's energetics, where the equilibrium throat length scales as l_eq = 16r_e³/(G_N q), inversely with the Casimir coefficient [Q-10], gives T_w(Δ) = T_w0/κ. The long (non-shortcut) branch then disappears at Δ ≈ 2.3×10³ yr for D/c = 100 yr, 2.7×10³ yr for D/c = 10³ yr and 3.3×10³ yr for D/c = 3×10³ yr. That is 0.25, 0.32 and 0.52 of the no-feedback shortcut threshold (T_w0 = 9.4×10³ yr). [calc: runs/2026-10-03-traversable-wormhole-shortcuts/calc/lens-mechanist_timemachine.py, sections E and F] Read it only as a sign: in the toy, a mouth time offset strengthens the support, shortens the throat and moves it toward the shortcut, a positive feedback. If the full MMP equations agree, the self-consistent achronal ANEC requires something outside the toy to intervene (mouth collapse, merger, or the solution ceasing to exist) at an offset of order the throat time. This is low confidence: the κ dependence of l_eq on L is ignored when minimising, and the 3D field-line geometry is replaced by a 2D loop.
11. **No construction in the dossier holds a throat open without a negative null-energy source; they differ only in what supplies it.** The support classes are: exotic classical matter (Ellis/phantom [D-13], [D-15]; thin-shell σ < 0 [D-14], [Q-29]); quantum vacuum (Casimir-type) negative null energy, either driven by a boundary coupling (GJW, MQ [D-01], [D-04]) or topological (FGM 2018 quotient [D-05], MMP/MM field-line circle [D-06], [D-08], FGM 2019 Hartle–Hawking state [D-07]); and classical Dirac fields whose energy is not bounded below (BKR/EDM [D-09]–[D-12]). Hochberg–Visser [K-02] forces pointwise NEC violation at any throat, so the mechanist's question is only which physical process supplies it and what limits it.
12. **What ends each mechanism (the termination links) is better evidenced than what sustains it.** Positive-energy infall closes ghost-scalar throats [D-13]; MM accumulates infalling matter toward collapse and needs a refrigerator [D-08]; FGM 2019 is exponentially fragile and the mouths merge on ∼d^{3/2} (G = c = 1) [D-07]; GJW is open for less than a Planck time [D-03], [Q-16]; EDM wormholes collapse into black holes [D-11]; thin shells beyond a₀ = 3M need β₀² < 0 [D-14]. Only MMP has a claimed eternal configuration, and its stability rests on preprints [D-C5], [F-01].
13. **The mundane mechanism behind "shortcut" claims is clock synchronisation.** By finding 6, T_thru(B→A)/T_ext = (T_w − Δ)/(D/c): the verdict depends on the mouth clock offset Δ as much as on the throat geometry. Any reported T_thru/T_ext must state Δ (the brief's "synchronised through the exterior" fixes Δ only for mouths that were never moved or held in unequal potentials).
14. **The mundane mechanism behind the 2022 "wormhole" signal is size winding in a learned, fully commuting Hamiltonian** [D-C1], [Q-37]. There is no gravitational back-reaction and no negative null energy in a spacetime sense [D-16]. It tests the boundary-side teleportation mechanism of GJW/MSY, not the support mechanism of any 4D construction. Classification: quantum simulation with a contested model artefact.

**Does the throat geodesic become chronal?** It works the other way round. For Δ < T_w − D/c the through-throat null geodesics are chronal, as in Graham–Olum's long-throat example (finding 3). For T_w − D/c < Δ < T_w + D/c the B→A throat path is the fastest path, so a complete null geodesic through it can be achronal. A generic complete achronal geodesic must violate ANEC, so the self-consistent conjecture forbids this window (my inference from the lemma Graham–Olum use; consistent with [D-27] "seems to prohibit shortcut wormholes" and [D-06]). At Δ = T_w + D/c the closed null generator of the chronology horizon is achronal, and Theorem 3 applies (finding 4). So under the conjecture the operative barrier for a long wormhole is Δ_sc, a time 2D/c before any Cauchy horizon. There, the semiclassical stress tensor in my 2D toy is finite, merely enhanced by a factor of a few to 10⁷ (finding 9).
