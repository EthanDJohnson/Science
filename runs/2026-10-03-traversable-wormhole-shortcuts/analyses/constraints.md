# Analysis: constraints (Kant: conditions any valid answer must satisfy)
status: final

## Method applied
Audit every constraint that applies (pointwise NEC/WEC/SEC/DEC; ANEC and achronal ANEC; quantum inequalities; topology censorship; conservation, stability; chronology), compute what each wormhole construction requires against each, and classify each as violates / strained / consistent, stating the assumptions under which each constraint applies. Owner of the brief's energy-condition table (rho + p along the throat, radial ANEC, achronality).

## Findings
- F0 (setup). The dossier gives the structural facts this lens audits against: generic throat NEC violation [K-02], topological censorship [K-01], self-consistent achronal ANEC as conjecture [K-07a, D-C3], long wormholes escaping it because their throat geodesics are chronal [K-07f, D-27], QI bounds with their flat-space/free-field assumptions [K-08, K-09], and chronology protection as conjecture plus a test-field theorem [K-12].
- F1 (Ellis throat, recomputed). At the throat of the Φ = 0 Ellis–Bronnikov wormhole: ρ = p_l = −1/(8π b0²) = −3.979×10⁻² m⁻², p_t = +3.979×10⁻² m⁻² (b0 = 1 m, geometric), so ρ + p_l = −1/(4π b0²) = −9.63×10⁴² J/m³ (SI) and ρ + p_l + 2p_t = 0 exactly. ρ + p_l < 0 at every one of 2001 sampled points on l ∈ [−50, 50] m. The radial ANEC is −0.12500 m⁻¹ = −E/(8b0) by direct integration and by parts (agree to 1e-8), and is unchanged to 6 digits for cutoffs |l| < 100, 150, 1000, 1500 m (convergence). SI: −1.513×10⁴³ J/m² per unit E. A gr_tensors scan across the throat region (l ∈ [−10, 10] m, 41 and 61 points) finds NEC, WEC, SEC and DEC violated at every point, Hawking–Ellis type I; curvature radius at the throat 1.000 m (= b0), Kretschmann 12.000 m⁻⁴; precision_check on the radial T_kk shows no sign flip (relative error 0). Reproduces [D-15] and [Q-24, Q-25]. [calc: runs/2026-10-03-traversable-wormhole-shortcuts/calc/lens-constraints_ec_table.py]
- F2 (redshift does not rescue the radial ANEC). With a bounded redshift Φ = −b0/r, the radial ANEC is −0.19798 m⁻¹ (direct and by parts agree), more negative than Φ = 0. The by-parts identity I = −(E/4π)∫e^{−Φ}(r′/r)² dl, with vanishing boundary terms whenever Φ is bounded and r′/r → 0 at both ends, makes the radial ANEC strictly negative for every static, spherically symmetric, horizon-free wormhole joining two asymptotically flat ends, whatever the matter model (wormhole_tools derivation, checked numerically here). This covers the static EDM solutions (BKR, Konoplya–Zhidenko, Kain's static set) as well as Morris–Thorne. [calc: same]
- F3 (static observers can see positive energy; "negative energy" then appears only for moving observers). For b = r0(1 + α(1 − r0/r)), α = 0.5, Φ = 0 (finite ADM mass M = 0.75 r0): ρ > 0 at r = 1, 1.5, 3, 10 m, but ρ + p_r < 0 at all of them (−1.99×10⁻² m⁻² at the throat). A radially moving observer at the throat measures γ²(ρ + v²p_r) < 0 for v > √α = 0.7071c. So the throat's WEC violation is forced (through the NEC), but whether a given observer sees negative energy density depends on its speed; radial ANEC −0.0946 m⁻¹ (direct = by parts). [calc: same]
- F4 (the ANEC deficit can be made small, never zero). For the slow-flare family r = b0 + √(l² + L²) − L (Φ = 0, b0 = 1 m): radial ANEC = −0.125, −0.0727, −0.0304, −0.01064, −0.00348 m⁻¹ for L/b0 = 1, 10, 10², 10³, 10⁴ (direct = by parts at every L), matching my asymptotic estimate I ≈ −(√2/4)/√(b0 L) = −0.00354 m⁻¹ at L = 10⁴ m. Throat ρ + p_l = −1/(4π b0 L) exactly as predicted; static ρ at the throat turns positive for L > 2b0. Magnitudes of pointwise and averaged violation can be made small by a slow flare (consistent with Visser–Kar–Dadhich [K-21]), but the sign is fixed (F2). [calc: same]
- F5 (thin shells, recomputed). Flat-exterior thin shell (M → 0), a = 1 m: σ = −1/(2πa) = −1.926×10⁴³ J/m², P = +9.63×10⁴² N/m, shell mass −2.693×10²⁷ kg (= −2a geometric ≈ −1.4 M_Jup), shell ANEC −1.926×10⁴³ J/m² per unit E; a = 1 km scales as 1/a (−2.69×10³⁰ kg). Schwarzschild exterior, M = 1 M_☉ (1476.6 m): a = 2.5M, 3M, 4M, 10 km give σ = −2.33, −2.51, −2.31, −1.62 ×10³⁹ J/m² and m_s = −4.45×10³⁰, −6.89×10³⁰, −1.12×10³¹, −2.26×10³¹ kg; all four 4D energy conditions fail at every radius (σ < 0 fails radial crossings), while the 2+1 surface NEC holds for a ≤ 3M (σ + P = +9.6×10⁻⁶ m⁻¹ at 2.5M, 0 at 3M) and fails above. - F6 (thin-shell stability, recomputed). Linear radial stability requires β² = dP/dσ > 3.5 at a = 2.5M (sound speed above c), and β² < −1.75 at 4M, < −0.79 at a = 10 km, < −0.5 for the flat-exterior shell; at a = 3M the critical β² diverges (sign flip). Either superluminal or imaginary sound speed: "somewhat perverse" as Poisson & Visser say [D-14, Q-30]. Classification: stability is strained for every Schwarzschild thin-shell wormhole unless the shell matter has an exotic equation of state. [calc: same]
- F7 (QI single-scale throat bound, recomputed). A static observer at a Φ = 0 throat is geodesic and sees constant ρ = −1/(8π b0²), so its Lorentzian average is ρ itself (toolkit check: lorentzian_average of a constant returns it). With sampling time τ0 = f × (curvature radius) = f b0 (curvature radius = b0 from F1): Ford–Roman gives b0 ≤ √(3/4π) l_P/f² = 4.89×10³ l_P = 7.9×10⁻³² m at f = 0.01, and Fewster–Eveson's Lorentzian bound gives 1.83×10³ l_P = 3.0×10⁻³² m. At b0 = 1 m the required density (−4.8×10⁴² J/m³) exceeds the single-field Ford–Roman bound at τ0 = 3.3×10⁻¹¹ s (−3.0×10⁻²⁰ J/m³) by 1.6×10⁶²; at b0 = 1.5×10⁷ m (MM tidal size) by 3.6×10⁷⁶. Reproduces [Q-20] (researcher's 5.0×10³ l_P). Assumptions: free massless minimally coupled scalar; flat-space QI with τ0 ≪ curvature radius and boundary distances [K-08, K-09]. - F8 (QI thin band, recomputed). With static density ∼ −1/(8π r0 Δ) in a band of thickness Δ and τ0 = fΔ: Δ ≤ (3 r0 l_P²/(4π f⁴))^{1/3} = 1.84×10⁻²¹ m (1.1×10¹⁴ l_P) for r0 = 1 m, 4.5×10⁻¹⁹ m for r0 = 1.5×10⁷ m, 3.9×10⁻¹⁶ m for r0 = 1 ly (f = 0.01). The dossier's form gives 1.48×10⁻²¹, 3.7×10⁻¹⁹, 3.1×10⁻¹⁶ m: same scaling, constants differ by 1.24. Reproduces [Q-21]. [calc: same]
- F9 (species-count reading of the QI; my estimate). If N free fields each saturate the single-field bound, a throat of radius b0 needs N ≥ 4.19×10⁻⁸ (b0/l_P)² (f = 0.01): 1.6×10⁶² fields for 1 m, 3.6×10⁷⁶ for 1.5×10⁷ m. With a Bekenstein-like species bound N < (M_pl/TeV)² ∼ 10³² [K-22], the largest QI-compatible free-field throat is b0 ≲ 7.9×10⁻¹⁶ m. This is the QI-side analogue of MM's own N_f > 10⁵² obstruction [Q-03] and points the same way. Assumes the bound scales linearly with species and that the single-scale geometry applies. [calc: same]
- F10 (where the QI does not apply: Casimir-supported throats). For MMP/MM throats the 4D fermions sit in the lowest Landau level. The magnetic length l_B = r_e√(2/q), with the extremal magnetic Reissner–Nordström relation r_e = √π q l_P/g (Heaviside–Lorentz, ħ = c = 1), is 5.3×10⁻¹⁴ m for r_e = 1.5×10⁷ m and g = e = 0.3028, and 6.2×10⁻²⁷ m for r_e = 2×10⁻¹⁹ m (the Standard Model MMP size). The QI sampling time τ0 = 0.01 r_e exceeds l_B by 10¹⁸ to 10⁶ in every case, and the negative energy is a Casimir energy on a closed circle of field lines, which a flat-space QI does not bound at sampling times longer than the circle (GJW say their negative energy "is similar to the Casimir eﬀect, since the int eraction between the boundaries implies that the radial direction is eﬀectively a compact circle" [new: Gao, Jafferis & Wall, arXiv:1608.05687 (JHEP 2017), full-text]). So F7–F9 do not constrain MMP, MM, FGM or GJW: their assumptions fail there; this is not a violation. [calc: same]
- F11 (D-C7 resolved). The same relation gives q = 1.59×10⁴¹ Dirac units for an r_e = 1.5×10⁷ m mouth with g = e, matching RE-15's 1.6×10⁴¹ [Q-09]. RQ-17's r_e = √(πq) l_P/g₄ puts the square root in the wrong place: MMP state r_e ∼ q [D-06], and their πℓ ∝ q² [Q-12] needs r_e linear in q (via ℓ = 16 r_e³/(G q) [Q-10]). So the q ∼ 10⁸² estimate should be dropped. [calc: same]
- F12 (GJW, quoted not recomputed). The GJW one-loop T_UU (eqs. 3.8–3.16) is an Appell-F1 integral that I did not re-evaluate. Quoted: "We ﬁnd an analytic expression for ´ dU TU U, which is negative for all 0 < ∆ < 1", and for ∆ = 0, "´ dU TU U = 0". On achronality, the coupling works by "changing the commutation relations along null geodesics through th e wormhole and making them no longer achronal" [new: Gao, Jafferis & Wall, arXiv:1608.05687v3, full-text]. So the GJW throat geodesic is chronal in the full coupled system, by the authors' own statement, and the ANEC violation it carries is compatible with the self-consistent achronal ANEC.
- F13 (Maldacena–Qi integral reproduced in JT gravity). Global AdS₂, ds² = (−dt² + ds²)/sin²s, with 8πG T_ab = −∇_a∇_bφ + g_ab(□φ − φ) (sign fixed by dilaton focusing). Check: the vacuum black hole φ = cos t/sin s gives T = 0 exactly. The static eternal-wormhole dilaton for traceless matter is φ = φ0[1 + (π/2 − s)cot s] (minimum φ0 at the throat s = π/2). It needs T_kk = −φ0 sin⁴s/(4πG) < 0 at every point of the boundary-to-boundary null ray (k^t = 1 at the throat), so the NEC fails pointwise everywhere along the ray. The complete-ray ANEC is −φ0/(8G) = −φ_r/(4πG) = −2φ_r/(8πG) with φ_r = πφ0/2 (closed form = numeric quadrature, −0.125000 at φ0 = G = 1). This equals Maldacena–Qi's "∫dX⁺T₊₊ = −2φ_r" [D-04] once φ is normalised with 1/(8πG). A finite negative ANEC holds the wormhole open eternally; the ray links the two boundaries in global time π, which the boundary coupling (instantaneous L–R interaction) already connects, so it is chronal in the coupled system. - F14 (MMP energy reproduced). E(l) = r_e³/(G l²) − q/(8l) has its minimum at l = 16 r_e³/(G q), where E_min = −G q²/(256 r_e³) and d²E/dl² = G³q⁴/(32768 r_e⁹) > 0 (ħ = c = 1). Reproduces [Q-10]. A flat-circle 2D Casimir estimate (c = q species, light-length πl) gives E0 = −q/(6l), the same order as MMP's −q/(8l) (ratio 4/3; geometry and boundary-condition factors not reproduced). Along the field-line circle, the ANEC per loop is −πc/(3L), so a complete null line wrapping it n times has ANEC −nπc/(3L) → −∞. It returns to its own spatial point, so it is chronal, as MMP state [D-06]. [calc: same]
- F15 (achronality margins). For a static one-sided wormhole with throat light-time T_thru and mouth separation T_ext, the throat-crossing null geodesic is chronal whenever T_thru > T_ext: a timelike curve through the exterior links the entry and exit events. It can be achronal only when T_thru < T_ext, i.e. only if it is a shortcut. MM's worked example: πℓ/c = 9.42×10³ yr against T_ext = 1, 10², 10³, 2990 ly, giving T_thru/T_ext = 9425, 94.2, 9.42, 3.15; chronal in every case. FGM 2019: t_min − d = "logs" > 0, chronal by a logarithmic margin [Q-13]. A classical one-sided throat of 2 m light-length between mouths 1 km apart would be achronal (−3.3×10⁻⁶ s margin): that is precisely the configuration the self-consistent achronal ANEC forbids. [calc: same]
- F16 (two-ended static throats: the radial geodesic is achronal). In a static two-ended wormhole such as Ellis (Φ = 0), the optical metric is dl² + r²dΩ². Between points on opposite sides of the same radial line, the radial path through the throat has optical length |l₂ − l₁| and any path with angular motion is longer, so the radial null geodesic is the fastest causal curve, and no two of its points are timelike related. It is achronal, with ANEC < 0 (F1, F2). Under the self-consistent achronal ANEC, any semiclassical two-ended asymptotically flat traversable wormhole is therefore excluded. That covers Ellis/Morris–Thorne built from quantum fields, and the static EDM solutions if read as semiclassical. Graham & Olum: the conjecture requires "that there is no self-consistent space-time in semiclassical gravity in which ANEC is violated on a complete, {\em achronal} null geodesic... it is sufficient to rule out wormholes and closed timelike curves" [new: Graham & Olum 2007, arXiv:0705.3193 (PRD 76, 064001), abstract]. Their v2 "qualify conditions on theorem 1" (same page).
- F17 (polyhedral wormholes: ANEC along the throat geodesic need not be negative). Visser 1989: on a flat face "the two principal radii of curvature are inﬁnite and the stress energy is zero", and a traveller "will feel no tidal forces and see no matter, exo tic or otherwise" [new: Visser 1989, PRD 39, 3182 (arXiv:0809.0907), full-text]. Hence along a face-centred crossing ray T_kk ≡ 0 and the ANEC is exactly 0 (satisfied). The exotic matter sits on the edges: around each edge of a cube wormhole the total angle is 2 × 3π/2 = 3π, a conical excess of π, giving line mass μ = −c²/(8G) = −1.68×10²⁶ kg/m per edge (my derivation via the conical relation δ = 8πGμ/c²; corners not computed). A 1 m cube carries −2.0×10²⁷ kg in its edges, comparable to the −1.35×10²⁷ kg of a spherical thin shell of radius 0.5 m. Topological censorship [K-01] is evaded through the edge rays. The face ray has R_kk = 0 and vanishing Weyl curvature, so it fails the null generic condition that the Gao–Wald and focusing arguments use [K-03]. Any positive-energy traveller on that ray restores genericity. My inference, low confidence: whether a one-sided polyhedral shortcut slips past Graham–Olum's theorem through this non-genericity is not settled by any source here. - F18 (classical creation). Tipler 1977: "if the Einstein equations (and the weak energy condition) hold on the “topology-changing” space-time considered by Geroch, then topology change cannot occur... if topology change occurs within a finite region, then this change of topology must be accompanied by singularities" [new: Tipler 1977, Annals Phys. 108, 1, abstract via INSPIRE]. Because every throat already violates the WEC (F1–F5), the first clause does not by itself forbid creation. The second clause does constrain it: classical creation of a one-sided wormhole within a finite region requires a singularity. Creating a classical wormhole therefore needs a singularity, CTCs (Geroch's theorem, not retrieved [D §4 note]) or quantum topology change (labelled speculative in the brief). Pair creation of connected magnetic black holes (Garfinkle–Strominger) is a quantum route outside these classical theorems; no rate is in the dossier.

  - Standard-Model-scale MMP (r_e = 2×10⁻¹⁹ m, g = e, N_f = 1): q = 2.1×10¹⁵; throat length ℓ = 16 r_e³/(l_P² q) = 0.23 m (external transit πℓ/c = 2.4 ns); E_min = −1.8×10⁻¹¹ J = −113 MeV; each extremal mouth 2.7×10⁸ kg; required temperature < 9.9×10⁻³ K. With N_f = 54 [Q-11], the Casimir term, and with it ℓ and |E_min|, grows by about 54 (my reading).
  - Payload back-reaction (energy conservation): payload energy must stay below |E_bin|, otherwise the throat closes [D-13, D-08]. SM-MMP gives |E_bin|/(1 kg·c²) = 2.0×10⁻²⁸, so only single quanta below about 10⁸ eV can pass. The MM worked example gives 5.0×10⁹ for 1 kg and 7.2×10⁷ for 70 kg, so energy is not its binding constraint; its speculative sector is.

- F20 (chronology, as a constraint). With mouth clocks offset by Δ (exit at B occurs Δ earlier than throat transit alone implies), a closed causal curve through throat and exterior exists when Δ > T_thru + T_ext. For MM this is Δ > 9.42×10³ yr + d/c; for a classical shortcut with T_thru ≪ T_ext it reduces to Δ > T_ext ≈ d/c (the Morris–Thorne–Yurtsever case). So a long wormhole is not immune to conversion; it needs a larger offset. Under the self-consistent achronal ANEC, which Graham–Olum say also rules out CTCs (F16), the conversion fails for long and short wormholes alike: the chronology horizon's generators are achronal null geodesics. Independently, Kay–Radzikowski–Wald prove that linear test fields break down at the base points of a compactly generated Cauchy horizon [K-12]; this is support, not proof, of Hawking's conjecture [D-C4]. The mechanist owns the mouth-speed numbers.
