# Analysis: idealizer (Plato)
status: final

## Method applied
Build the most idealized models that keep the question's essence (a zero-thickness handle joining two points of flat space; an Ellis/thin-shell throat as the "classical" limit; a Casimir-balanced long throat as the "quantum" limit; a shock-shift model for payload back-reaction), solve them in closed form with scripts, express the answers as scaling laws, then relax the idealizations one at a time.

## Findings

F1. **Idealized one-sided handle (point mouths in flat space, throat light-time L, mouth separation d, gluing map O on directions).** Shortcut for observers at the mouths iff L < d (T_thru/T_ext = L/d). A complete radial null geodesic through the throat is achronal iff (i) the gluing is aligned along it (O n = n) and (ii) L ≤ D·n̂. Confirmed numerically for L/d from 0 to 10 and ray angles 0–120 degrees: for aligned gluing along D̂ the switch from achronal to chronal happens exactly at L/d = 1 (margin −0.01 m at L/d = 0.99, +0.01 m at 1.01, d = 1 m); off-axis rays switch at L = d cos ψ [calc: runs/2026-10-03-traversable-wormhole-shortcuts/calc/lens-idealizer_handle.py]. So in the aligned case "shortcut" and "achronal throat geodesic" are the same condition, and a self-consistent achronal ANEC [K-07a] forbids exactly the shortcut regime while leaving long throats (L > d) free; this is the content of MMP's statement that short wormholes are excluded [D-06] and of Kontou's "long wormholes escape" [D-27].

F2. **The gluing caveat.** For the mirror gluing (O = −I, the standard Visser cut-and-paste orientation) no complete radial null geodesic through the throat is achronal even at L = 0, the maximal shortcut (margin +799 m with d = 1 m: the ray re-emerges heading back the way it came). For a rotated gluing only rays along the rotation axis with D·n̂ ≥ L can be achronal (0/200 and 2/200 random rays achronal) [calc: runs/2026-10-03-traversable-wormhole-shortcuts/calc/lens-idealizer_handle.py]. So in the idealized model the achronal ANEC by itself does not exclude every one-sided shortcut; the exclusion of shortcuts with "unaligned" gluing needs a further step (MMP's covering-space argument [D-06], FGM's GSL argument [D-07], or forbidding the time machine the shortcut can be turned into). With finite mouths, near-grazing rays give achronal segments of length ∼ d/ε² (ε the grazing angle) but, in this idealization, no complete achronal line that crosses the throat (a qualitative estimate, not computed).

F3. **Time-machine threshold in the same model.** With a clock offset Δ between the mouths, a closed causal loop (throat A→B, exterior B→A) exists iff Δ ≥ d + L. A long throat does not protect against conversion in principle; it only raises the needed offset. For an MM-like throat (πℓ ≈ 9.4×10³ ly [Q-06]) with d = 10³ ly, Δ ≥ 1.04×10⁴ yr, which a twin-paradox mouth trip at 0.99c needs about 1.2×10⁴ yr of exterior time to accumulate (at 0.5c, about 7.8×10⁴ yr) [calc: runs/2026-10-03-traversable-wormhole-shortcuts/calc/lens-idealizer_handle.py]. (The mechanist lens owns the full conversion; this is my own independent number.)

F4. **Graham–Olum restrict their wormhole exclusion to simply connected spacetimes, and say so.** [new: Graham & Olum 2007, PRD 76, 064001 (arXiv:0705.3193v2), full-text, "We must also restrict ourselves to simply-connected spacetimes, which means that the wormholes we rule out are only those which connect one asymptotically flat region to another, not those which connect a region to itself."] They give the long one-sided wormhole as the counterexample: [new: same, full-text, "Any causal path through the wormhole emerges in the future of the place where it entered, and thus is not achronal. We can still find fastest paths through the wormhole, but they are chronal."] This matches F1 (L > d ⇒ chronal) and shows F2's point is not an artefact of my toy: the published theorem does not cover one-sided shortcuts. Their exclusion of one-sided shortcuts runs through their other result (no CTCs) only if the shortcut is turned into a time machine, or through arguments outside the theorem (covering space, GSL).

F5. **Universal mass scale of a throat.** For any throat of areal radius r the geometric mass scale is r c²/G (1.347×10²⁷ kg for r = 1 m, SI). For a classical exotic throat this scale is itself the negative mass: Ellis ∫ρ dV over both sheets = −r c²/G exactly, and a flat-space (M = 0) thin-shell wormhole has shell mass 4πa²σ = −2a, i.e. −2a c²/G (−2.69×10²⁷ kg at a = 1 m) [calc: runs/2026-10-03-traversable-wormhole-shortcuts/calc/lens-idealizer_cost.py]; agrees with [Q-26] and [Q-28]. The Ellis ANEC integral reproduced −0.125 /m at b₀ = 1 m (−E/(8b₀)) [D-15]. Scaling: |M_exotic| ∝ r, independent of shape to a factor of about 2.

F6. **Tidal size limit depends on crossing speed: b₀ ≥ γv √(ξ/a_max).** The transverse tidal curvature at the Ellis throat is R^θ_lθl = −1/b₀² (from gr_tensors), and R^θ_tθt = 0, so a radial traveller at local speed v feels γ²v²ξ/b₀² [calc: runs/2026-10-03-traversable-wormhole-shortcuts/calc/lens-idealizer_cost.py]. Ultra-relativistic travel reproduces MM's 1.5×10⁷ m (0.5 m, 20 g) [Q-01] and gives 1.35×10⁸ m for 2 m at 1 g (the brief's MT-like criterion; [Q-52] used 0.5 m and got 6.8×10⁷ m). At 10 km/s the same human criteria need only b₀ ≥ 505 m (MM criterion) or 4.5 km (MT-like), i.e. 0.34 M_☉ or 3.1 M_☉ of exotic negative mass. Crossing time ≈ π√(ξ/a_max) ≈ 0.16 s or 1.4 s, independent of v when v ≪ c. A classical static throat can be crossed slowly; a quantum-supported long throat cannot, because its redshift makes the crossing ultra-relativistic (γ ∼ 2×10¹² [Q-02]).

F7. **Casimir-balanced long throat: |E_neg| = M (r_e/ℓ)².** Minimising the MMP energy E(ℓ) = r_e³/(Gℓ²) − Nq/(8ℓ) (ħ = c = 1; [Q-10], with N flavours assumed to multiply the Casimir term) gives ℓ* = 16r_e³/(GNq) and E_min = −GN²q²/(256 r_e³); symbolically |E_min|/(r_e/G) − (r_e/ℓ*)² = 0 [calc: runs/2026-10-03-traversable-wormhole-shortcuts/calc/lens-idealizer_cost.py]. So the negative energy that holds the throat open is the mouth mass times the square of (mouth radius / throat length). With MM's γ = ℓ/r_e ∼ 2×10¹² and M_e = 2.02×10³⁴ kg this gives 2.5×10⁻²⁵ and 5.05×10⁹ kg, which match MM's own |E_bin|/M_e ≈ 2.5×10⁻²⁵ [Q-05] and E_bin ∼ −5×10⁹ kg [Q-02]. The one-line model reproduces the published worked example.

F8. **Payload cap and the separation window.** Taking a payload as closing the throat once its positive energy exceeds the negative-energy budget (m ≲ |E_neg|; MM's ship-mass condition [Q-03], qualitative collapse in [D-13]) and combining with F7: ℓ ≤ r_e √(M/m). Long (non-shortcut, achronal-ANEC-safe) wormholes need πℓ > d, so a consistent mouth separation is d < π r_e √(M/m). For a 70 kg human at r_e = 1.5×10⁷ m: ℓ_max ≈ 2.7×10⁷ ly, d < 8.5×10⁷ ly, exterior transit up to 8.5×10⁷ yr against a proper time ≈ 0.157 s; for 1 kg, d < 7×10⁸ ly [calc: runs/2026-10-03-traversable-wormhole-shortcuts/calc/lens-idealizer_cost.py]. Payload back-reaction is not what limits the long-throat design; the species count and the dark sector are.

F9. **Species requirement in the same model is ≫ 10³².** With a charge normalisation κ = r_e²/(Gq²) = π/g² and g²N < 1, the payload condition gives N ≳ 256π m r_e/(ħ/c) ≈ 3×10⁵⁵ for m = 10³ kg, r_e = 1.5×10⁷ m, against MM's quoted N_f > 10⁵² [Q-03] and the UV-cutoff ceiling 10³² [K-22] [calc: runs/2026-10-03-traversable-wormhole-shortcuts/calc/lens-idealizer_cost.py]. The normalisation is mine, so it is good only to a few orders of magnitude, but the conclusion (the 4D free-fermion version fails by 20 or more orders) is robust to it.

F10. **Quantum-inequality relaxation reproduces Ford–Roman.** Requiring the throat density c⁴/(8πG r₀²) to respect the flat-space QI over a sampling length f r₀ gives r₀ ≤ √(3/4π) l_P/f² (0.49 l_P at f = 1; 4.9×10³ l_P at f = 0.01), matching [Q-20]. A thin band of width w gives w ≤ (3r₀/(4πf⁴l_P))^{1/3} l_P, matching the form of [Q-21] to within a factor of about 1.5 [calc: runs/2026-10-03-traversable-wormhole-shortcuts/calc/lens-idealizer_qi_twosided.py]. So for a free field the classical Ellis/thin-shell row survives only as a Planck-scale throat or as a macroscopic throat whose negative energy sits in a sub-femtometre band of enormous density.

F11. **Two-sided channel model.** If the coupling that makes GJW/MQ traversable acts at boundary time t₀, the message cannot exit before t₀ plus the time for the coupling's information to reach the right side, so the wormhole's lead over the non-gravitational channel is ≤ 0 by construction [D-01, D-02]. To carry a payload of energy mc² as quanta that fit a throat of size R needs N ∼ mcR/ħ quanta (2.8×10⁴² for 1 kg at R = 1 m), and MSY's parametric bound ties the quanta to the bits sent through the classical channel [Q-17] [calc: runs/2026-10-03-traversable-wormhole-shortcuts/calc/lens-idealizer_qi_twosided.py]. The two-sided constructions are teleportation with geometric bookkeeping, not shortcuts.

F12. **The classical covering-space argument does not apply to quantum-supported one-sided wormholes, and the GSL route has the same topological gap.** MMP: [new: Maldacena, Milekhin & Popov, arXiv:1807.04726, full-text, "In classical physics the equations are local, so we can go the covering space, where it joins two different universes. In this case, the wormhole becomes short, since the two different universes are infinitely far, so it should be forbidden by causality. ... Our solution will use Casimir energy, which depends on the actual topology of space, so we cannot go to the covering space."] Wall's GSL-based proof: [new: Wall 2010, arXiv:0910.5751, full-text, "If as the result of nontrivial topology the I+ and I− lie in the same asymptotic region, as in the case of the RP 3 geon, the horizon generators will no longer be achronal."] FGM 2019 concede the transit-time bound is not rigorous: [new: Fu, Grado-White & Marolf, arXiv:1908.03273, full-text, "it is difficult to use arguments about causal curves connecting distant points to rigorously bound wormhole transit times."] Together with F2 and F4: for one-sided, quantum-supported wormholes, "no shortcut" is a well-motivated expectation, not a consequence of any proved theorem or of the stated conjecture alone.

### Where reality departs from the models most
1. **Topology dependence of quantum negative energy.** Casimir-type support exists only because of the non-contractible loop [D-05, D-06]. In M1 the gluing and the loop are free inputs. In reality they are fixed by the field configuration, and that decides whether a complete achronal throat line exists (F1/F2), and blocks the covering-space argument (F12). This departure *helps* the shortcut loophole on paper (IDEALIZER-B) but *hurts* any concrete design, because nobody has a self-consistent short one-sided quantum solution.
2. **Stability and dynamics.** Every model is static. The real objects are unstable or fragile (F above). This hurts every surviving construction.
3. **The "classical exotic matter" input of M2** is the least physical element: the matter it needs violates the QIs that free fields obey (F10), and ghost fields are unstable (D-13). This hurts the classical rows decisively.

## What would change my mind
- A self-consistent semiclassical (quantum-field-supported) one-sided solution with T_thru < T_ext, or a demonstration that some loophole gluing (F2) can actually be realised with Casimir support: this would upgrade IDEALIZER-B to a live shortcut and weaken A.
- A proof of the quantum focusing conjecture, or of a GSL argument for causal horizons in multiply connected spacetimes, that excludes all one-sided shortcuts: this would close B's gap and make A theorem-level, conditional only on that proof's assumptions.
- A published MMP/MM normalisation showing that the Casimir term does not scale with N as I assumed: this changes F9's number but not its conclusion unless it moves the species count by more than 20 orders.
- A laboratory violation of a Fewster–Eveson-type QEI for a real field [D-C6]: this would reopen the QFT route for C.

## Assumptions I relied on
- Point mouths and flat exterior in M1; the conclusion about achronality is a far-field (x, y → ∞) statement and is checked numerically only on a finite grid (to 200 times the system size).
- Only radial through-geodesics; non-radial or multiply-traversing geodesics are not checked in M1.
- MMP's energy function [Q-10] is exact within its regime, and N flavours multiply its Casimir term (my assumption).
- Payload closure at m ≈ |E_neg| (MM's own condition, [Q-03]) treated as a linear superposition of null energy.
- Ford–Roman's flat-space QI applied in curved space at sampling length f × size, f = 0.01 (as [K-08] does).
- Tidal limit from the transverse Riemann component at an Ellis throat (Φ = 0); for Φ ≠ 0 a radial component adds.
- Achronal-ANEC and GSL statements are taken from Graham–Olum, MMP, Wall and FGM as quoted; I did not re-derive their proofs.
