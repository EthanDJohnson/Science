claim: Two-sided wormholes (GJW, Maldacena-Qi) and quantum-processor analogues are teleportation with geometric bookkeeping: they never beat their coupling channel, and Sycamore-type runs test size winding, not spacetime.
status: final

narrowed: No two-sided construction (GJW, MSY, Maldacena–Qi) or processor analogue delivers anything without, ahead of, or in excess of its boundary coupling, and Sycamore-type runs test size winding rather than spacetime; but only the measured-coupling GJW/MSY variant is teleportation in the LOCC sense, while GJW-as-written, Maldacena–Qi and the Sycamore circuit run on a direct quantum coupling whose geometric description carries real dynamical content (for example the μ^{2/3} transfer rate), not mere bookkeeping.

## Strongest attack
The attack targets the first predicate, "are teleportation with geometric bookkeeping", as the heading applies it to Maldacena–Qi and to GJW itself. The other two clauses survive it.

1. **GJW's own text says its wormhole needs a *quantum* channel, and that the classical, measured version does not give a traversable wormhole.** GJW propose a teleportation *interpretation*. But they state that, unlike standard teleportation, "it is essential that the channel between the left CFT, A, and the right CFT, B, is a quantum one". Projecting onto O_L eigenstates "can never lead to a traversable wormhole". When the measurement is instantaneous and classical, "the traversable window will be very small (and not well described by a semiclassical spacetime)". So the LOCC protocol C8 has in mind ("fidelity drops to zero when the classical L→R step is cut") is the limit in which the geometry disappears. It is not the geometric case. MSY recover a standard teleportation protocol only through "a minor variation" (measuring O_R), and then "only the left density matrix" is unchanged.
2. **Maldacena–Qi has no classical step at all.** The coupling is a permanent Hamiltonian term between the two boundaries, S_int = g Σ∫du O_L O_R. The wormhole is the ground state of the coupled system. Calling this "teleportation" stretches the word: no message is measured and no bits are sent.
3. **The geometry predicts transfer faster than the bare coupling would give, so it is not only bookkeeping.** In the Maldacena–Qi model, Plugge, Lantagne-Hurtubise and Franz (PRL 124, 221601) find the revival frequency ω_re ∼ μ^{2/3}. That is "much larger than the naive guess ω_re ∼ μ, indicating that the chaotic SYK interactions assist the transmission of information". At μ = 10⁻² J the wormhole route is about 7× faster than single-bilinear hopping, and at μ = 10⁻⁴ J about 34× faster [calc: runs/2026-10-03-traversable-wormhole-shortcuts/calc/falsifier-C8-1_channel.py].

**What the attack does not do.** It does not show any construction beating its coupling channel. Without the coupling nothing passes: GJW, D-01. MSY's back-reaction bound holds. The μ^{2/3} speed-up is a property of the coupled boundary dynamics. It is not a route around the coupling. A crude speed limit from the full coupling norm (about Nμ/2 for N bilinears) is respected whenever N ≥ π μ^{−1/3}: N ≥ 15 at μ = 10⁻², and N ≥ 68 at μ = 10⁻⁴ (same calc). The large-N regime where Maldacena–Qi is controlled meets this easily. This bound is heuristic, not a theorem.

## In principle vs. in practice
- **In principle:**
  - No two-sided construction delivers a signal without its coupling, ahead of it, or in excess of it. Nothing found in GJW, MSY, Maldacena–Qi, Freivogel et al. or Plugge et al. contradicts this, and GJW and MSY assert it.
  - The coupling is an instantaneous boundary interaction, or classical bits in the MSY variant, so "earlier than the channel" cannot be beaten. The live content is the information bound, and MSY call it parametric.
- **In practice:**
  - Processor runs (7 to 9 qubits on Sycamore; N = 8 for Byun et al.) are far from the large-N regime where any bulk description is controlled.
  - They test size winding and teleportation fidelity. No one on either side of D-C1 claims a spacetime was made.

## Corrections to details
- **Prediction "fidelity drops to zero when the classical L→R step is cut."** The Sycamore and Maldacena–Qi protocols have no classical step: the coupling e^{iμV} is a quantum gate across the two halves. The right control is μ → 0, or replacing the coupling with a measure-and-send LOCC version. By GJW's own statement, that version removes the semiclassical wormhole.
- **"Teleported qubits never exceed the classical bits the coupling carries."** For GJW-as-written and Maldacena–Qi, the bound is in bits *or qubits* exchanged through a quantum coupling (MSY: "the number of bits or qubits necessary to open the wormhole"). It is not in classical bits.
- **"The message emerges only after the coupling acts."** This is right for GJW/MSY. For Maldacena–Qi the coupling acts at all times, so the clause is empty there.
- **The 2.8×10⁴² quanta for a 1 kg payload** is confirmed (2.843×10⁴²). It counts quanta of wavelength about R carrying energy mc². It is not a bit count.
- **"Bookkeeping" understates the bulk description.** It predicts transfer-rate scaling (μ^{2/3}), Shapiro-type delays and the scrambling-time window that generic teleportation does not. These come from the same boundary dynamics, so the geometry adds description, not capability.

## What survives and remaining doubt
- **What survives:**
  - the operative claim: no two-sided wormhole or processor analogue beats, bypasses or out-carries its coupling channel (GJW: "cannot be used to violate causality"; MSY: cannot send more than was transferred to set up the coupling);
  - the claim that Sycamore-type runs test size winding, not spacetime, which both sides of D-C1 support.
- **What is narrowed:** "are teleportation". This holds literally only for the measured MSY variant. GJW-as-written, Maldacena–Qi and the on-chip coupling are directly quantum-coupled systems, and their geometric description carries dynamical content.
- **Remaining doubt:**
  - The MSY information bound is parametric, not sharp. No paper found derives a sharp "qubits ≤ ½ bits" result for the gravitational regime.
  - Freivogel et al.'s horizon-area quanta count for large BTZ horizons was not compared quantitatively against the coupling's bit or qubit content.
  - The full-coupling speed limit in the calc is a heuristic estimate.
- **Why weakened rather than survives:** the heading applies "teleportation" to Maldacena–Qi by name, and GJW's own text rejects the classical-channel reading for the geometric regime. The narrowing therefore touches the heading. It is not only a supporting detail.
