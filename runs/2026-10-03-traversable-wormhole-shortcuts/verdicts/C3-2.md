# Verdict on C3 (refuter 2, angle: scale)
claim: Shortcuts, and hence time machines, are possible in principle because the self-consistent achronal ANEC fails: a back-reacted semiclassical spacetime can violate ANEC on a complete achronal throat geodesic.
status: final
verdict: weakened
basis: calculation
narrowed: The self-consistent achronal ANEC is an unproven conjecture, so no theorem yet excludes a shortcut throat. But no back-reacted violation has been exhibited. Any violation supplied by free quantum fields that is big enough to hold a throat open must either keep the throat within about 5×10³ (f = 0.01) of the species cutoff length √N·l_P, or squeeze the negative energy into a band about 10⁻²¹ m thick. So a shortcut is "possible in principle" only conditionally: on a solution nobody has constructed and, for a payload-sized throat, on ≳10⁶⁰ labelled dark species. The "hence time machines" step cannot be shown inside semiclassical gravity at all.

Units: SI unless marked; geometric means G = c = 1 (lengths in m). l_P = 1.616×10⁻³⁵ m. f is the Ford–Roman sampling ratio (sampling time over the local length scale).

## Strongest attack

The core claim is an existence claim: a self-consistent solution of G_μν = 8π⟨T_μν⟩ with ∫T_kk dλ < 0 on a complete achronal throat geodesic. The candidate supports it only with (i) "the conjecture is unproven" and (ii) violations on fixed backgrounds. Neither is a self-consistent violation, and its own evidence section concedes "No self-consistent violation is known" [D-27]. "Unproven" does not mean "fails". Three scale calculations show why the jump from fixed-background violations to a back-reacted throat is not a formality.

1. **Size of the required violation.** Holding an Ellis throat of radius a open needs ∫T_kk dλ = −1/(8a) (geometric) on the radial geodesic. That is −c⁴/(8Ga) = −1.5×10⁴³ J/m² at a = 1 m [calc: runs/2026-10-03-traversable-wormhole-shortcuts/calc/falsifier-C3-2_scale.py, §1, numeric quadrature agrees with the analytic −0.125 m⁻¹]. The deficit is classical, of order c⁴/G. Quantum violations are of order ħ.

2. **Free fields can supply it only near the cutoff, whatever N is.** One free field, sampled at the throat scale under the flat-space Ford–Roman QI, has to be multiplied by N_min = 4.19×10⁻⁸ (a/l_P)² species (f = 0.01) or 4.19×10⁻⁴ (a/l_P)² (f = 0.1) [calc §2; reproduces calc/lens-constraints_qi.py §3]. With N species the gravitational cutoff length is l_* = √N·l_P (Dvali: "black hole physics imposes an upper bound on the mass of the species equal to M_{Planck}/\sqrt{N}", arXiv:0710.4344, abstract via lit_search). Combining the two removes N: a_max/l_* = 4.9×10³ (f = 0.01) or 49 (f = 0.1), so the throat curvature is always ≥ 4×10⁻⁸ (or ≥ 4×10⁻⁴) in cutoff units [calc §3]. A self-sustained single-scale throat is therefore never parametrically semiclassical. It sits at most 3.7 orders of magnitude in length (1.7 orders at f = 0.1) above the scale where the semiclassical equation the candidate invokes stops being controlled. In absolute terms:
   - a_max = 7.9×10⁻³¹ m for Standard-Model-like N = 100;
   - 7.9×10⁻¹⁶ m (sub-proton) even for N = 10³², the largest count Dvali's TeV-scale bound allows.

   Ford–Roman's thin-band escape is the only route out, and it is extreme. At a = 1 m it needs |ρ| = 2.6×10⁶³ J/m³ in a band of width w ≤ 1.8×10⁻²¹ m, holding −6.7×10²⁶ kg of negative mass-energy [calc §6; matches the run's M-IDEALIZER-11]. No quantum state is known that does this, and Ford–Roman call macroscopic wormholes "very improbable" [K-08].

3. **The candidate's lead counterexample cannot back-react without trans-Planckian quanta.** Urban–Olum's violation comes from vacuum plus two-particle admixtures, and the violation grows "as the momentum grows in magnitude and is constrained within a cone which increasingly narrows around the direction of the null geodesic" (Urban & Olum 2010, arXiv:0910.5925, p. 10, fetch_text). Producing O(1) curvature over a length L self-consistently takes an energy of about c⁴L/G within L: 1.2×10⁴⁴ J (1.3×10²⁷ kg) at L = 1 m [calc §4]. Carried by O(1) quanta, each has a reduced wavelength l_P²/L = 2.6×10⁻⁷⁰ m = 1.6×10⁻³⁵ l_P, which is trans-Planckian and outside semiclassical gravity. Carried instead by quanta of wavelength L, it takes (L/l_P)² = 3.8×10⁶⁹ of them, and the state is then nearly classical. The negative part is then still capped by the state-independent QI bound of about ħc/L⁴ per field (point 2), while the positive energy grows with the number of quanta. Urban & Olum say themselves that they computed "the stress tensor in a given background without attempting to impose self-consistency" (p. 10). They also note that for the earlier anomalous violation, self-consistency makes it "too small to lead to the curvature necessary to produce the violation" (p. 3).

4. **"Hence time machines" does not follow inside the framework.** Kay, Radzikowski & Wald prove that a linear-field QFT stress tensor breaks down at the base points of a compactly generated Cauchy horizon [D-C4]. So semiclassical gravity cannot exhibit a wormhole time machine even if the achronal ANEC fails somewhere. The run's own toy model also points the other way: on the closing null ray the negative T_kk weakens toward the CTC threshold, as −πc/(3(L + Δ)²) with a finite ANEC per lap of −πc/(12L) (M-MECHANIST-05, refuted lens item; math/mechanist/M-MECHANIST-05.py.log).

## In principle vs. in practice
- **In principle.** Not refuted. The conjecture is unproven [K-07a], Wall's GSL derivation fails once gravitons are quantised [K-07c], and the thin-band configuration passes the flat-space QI (§6). So a self-consistent achronal violation is not excluded by any theorem or by my scale arithmetic. Free fields at a single scale do not exclude it either, provided enough species exist: the size cap is a_max ≈ 4.9×10³·√N·l_P. But the claim as written asserts the violation as fact ("can violate"), and no such solution exists. The scale analysis also makes "in principle" depend on labelled-speculative physics:
  - a throat of radius 0.1 to 1 m, wide enough for a 1 kg object or a human, needs N ≳ 10⁶⁰ to 10⁶² light free species at f = 0.01 (§2), against N ≈ 10² for the Standard Model;
  - or an unexhibited thin-band state.

  The time-machine half cannot be established within semiclassical gravity (Kay–Radzikowski–Wald).
- **In practice.** Out of reach. With Standard-Model fields the self-sustained throat is ≤ 7.9×10⁻³¹ m (§3), about 4.9×10⁴ l_P. That is a qubit-scale object at best, with a payload ceiling of order 10⁻³ kg-equivalent, meaningless at that size. Even 10³² species give a sub-proton throat. The thin band needs 10⁶³ J/m³. Laboratory Casimir densities are about 4×10⁴ J/m³ at 10 nm (M-IDEALIZER-11), a gap of about 59 orders of magnitude. TRL 0.

## Corrections to details
- "Hence time machines": the inference needs a stage at which a complete achronal throat geodesic exists. M-DECOMPOSER-08 shows that a static, misaligned one-sided shortcut can have no such geodesic. So the candidate's "because" is not the only route to a shortcut (this cuts against neither leg's conclusion, only the stated causal link). Conversely, the shortcut→CTC step still passes through an achronal stage (M-DECOMPOSER-08, "what survives"), so the time-machine leg does need the failure.
- Sub-hypothesis (b), the FGM 2018 quotient, is an AdS₃/BTZ two-sided construction. Its "shortcut" comparison path is the lens's modelling choice (M-CONSTRAINTS-17 verified the algebra only). It is not evidence of a 4D asymptotically flat shortcut.
- The 1 m Ellis T_thru/T_ext = 2.1×10⁻¹⁵ treats a two-sided throat as one-sided (M-DECOMPOSER-05 caveat). As an illustration this is fine, but the scale results above put a self-sustained 1 m throat at N ≳ 1.6×10⁶² species.

## What survives and remaining doubt
- **What survives:** the conditional. The self-consistent achronal ANEC is a conjecture, and if a back-reacted violation on a complete achronal throat geodesic exists, short one-sided shortcuts follow geometrically.
- **What does not survive:**
  - the indicative claim that it fails;
  - any implication of macroscopic shortcuts with known field content;
  - "hence time machines" as something semiclassical physics could show.
- **Doubts about my own attack:**
  - The QI argument uses flat-space free-field QEIs, with an unfixed sampling ratio f (0.01 to 0.1 moves the cap by a factor of 100 in a). Interacting fields have no proven QEI.
  - The species-cutoff reading assumes N light, gravitationally coupled fields.
  - The thin-band route is not excluded by anything I computed, only unexhibited.
  - The Urban–Olum back-reaction estimate (§4) is order-of-magnitude. It shows that the obvious ways of sourcing the curvature fail, not that every state fails.

  For these reasons the verdict is weakened rather than refuted.
