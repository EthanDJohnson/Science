## Strongest attack

The best physics attack goes after the Maldacena–Qi (MQ) leg of "they never beat their coupling channel". In the coupled-SYK realisation of MQ, signals cross the wormhole far faster than the bare coupling alone would carry them. Plugge, Lantagne-Hurtubise & Franz (PRL 124, 221601; arXiv:2003.03914) find a revival frequency ω_re ∼ μ^{2/3} (units J = ħ = 1), "much larger than the naive guess ωre∼µ, indicating that the chaotic SYK interactions assist the transmission of information between the two sides of the wormhole". At μ = 10⁻⁴ J that is a factor of 21.5. If this counted as "beating the coupling channel", the bookkeeping reading would fail: the geometry (the strongly coupled dynamics it encodes) would be doing work the channel cannot.

The attack fails, for three reasons I checked:
1. **Not without the channel.** At μ = 0 the L–R transmission amplitude vanishes identically. GJW and MQ both say the bulk passage exists only because of the direct coupling. MQ: "This does not violate any causality constraint since we are adding direct interactions between the two sides" (arXiv:1804.00491, p. 53).
2. **Not earlier than the channel.** The MQ coupling iμΣψ_Lψ_R is an equal-time nonlocal interaction. Its causal latency is zero, so no bulk passage, which takes t_re ∼ π/μ^{2/3}, can arrive before it.
3. **Not above the channel's capacity.** The relevant benchmark is not the naive single-mode rate μ. It is the most a Hamiltonian coupling of norm ‖H_int‖ ≤ Nμ/2 can do. The small-incremental-entangling (SIE) theorem bounds the entangling rate of any coupling by c‖H_int‖ log d, whatever the local Hamiltonians H_L and H_R are. The qubit-transmission rate is at most the entangling rate. The MQ transfer rate, (N/2)μ^{2/3}/π qubits per unit 1/J, stays 15 to 6×10⁴ times below that bound over N = 100 to 10⁴ and μ = 10⁻² to 10⁻⁴ J. Analytically, bound/rate = cπNμ^{1/3}/2, so the bound would bite only for μ < 3×10⁻⁸ J at N = 100, deep inside a regime MQ never uses. The strongly coupled dynamics uses more of the coupling's capacity. It does not exceed it. [calc: runs/2026-10-03-traversable-wormhole-shortcuts/calc/falsifier-C8-0_channel_and_regime.py]

A second attack goes after "Sycamore-type runs test size winding, not spacetime". If chaotic runs at N = 20 to 100 sat inside the semiclassical JT/Schwarzian regime, then by holographic duality they would arguably test (a dual of) spacetime, and the dichotomy would fail. The calculation gives the opposite result. The Schwarzian action is S = −(Nα_S/J)∫{f,τ}dτ (Maldacena–Stanford 2016, eq. 4.176). Gravity is weakly coupled only if βJ ≫ 1 and Nα_S/(βJ) ≫ 1. The Sycamore learned model (N = 7) gives Nα_S/(βJ) ≈ 0.01 to 0.03, and Byun et al.'s N = 8 (β = 3, J = √2) gives 0.013 to 0.029. The proposed N = 20 and N = 100 give 0.03 to 0.06 and 0.14 to 0.31. Requiring βJ ≥ 3 together with a Schwarzian coupling ≥ 10 needs N ≳ 1.9×10³ to 4.3×10³, depending on α_S. So every Sycamore-type run, proposed ones included, is far from a semiclassical bulk. This strengthens C8's second clause.

## In principle vs. in practice
- **In principle:** in GJW and MQ, nothing is delivered earlier than the boundary coupling or without it. This is a structural feature of the constructions, not a resource limit. MQ's μ^{2/3} speed-up stays inside the coupling Hamiltonian's entangling capacity. No calculation here or in the run's math checks breaks this.
- **In practice:** quantum-processor runs at every demonstrated and proposed size (N = 7 to 100) sit at a Schwarzian coupling Nα_S/(βJ) ≲ 0.3. A semiclassical bulk needs N of order 10³ to 10⁴ Majoranas, beyond even C8's own N ≥ 20 decisive test. What those runs can probe is size winding and operator-generic coupling-sign asymmetry in the boundary system, as C8 says.

## Corrections to details
- **"Teleportation" is the wrong label for Maldacena–Qi.** MQ is a coherent, eternal Hamiltonian coupling (state transfer), not LOCC teleportation. No classical bits are exchanged. So the K-15/MSY bound "quanta ≲ const × bits exchanged", and the prediction "teleported qubits never exceed the classical bits the coupling carries", apply to GJW/MSY-type one-shot couplings only. For MQ the right statement is that the transfer rate is bounded by the coupling Hamiltonian's capacity (SIE). The core claim is unaffected.
- **"Bookkeeping" undersells the dynamics.** The bulk description is not just relabelling the bare coupling. The strongly coupled dynamics speeds transmission up by μ^{-1/3} (×10 at μ = 10⁻³ J, ×21.5 at 10⁻⁴ J) over the naive coupling rate. The geometry still adds no channel. Both descriptions are of one coupled process, which is C8's point.
- **The decisive test does not reach the gravity regime.** C8's decisive test (chaotic sparse SYK at N ≥ 20) cannot reach the semiclassical gravity regime: it needs N ≳ 2×10³ to 4×10³ for βJ ≥ 3 with a Schwarzian coupling ≥ 10. It can still decide sub-hypotheses (a) and (b) about size winding. It should not be described as approaching a test of spacetime.
- **"Open for less than a Planck time".** Q-16 is a proper-time statement from Freivogel et al. in the BTZ probe regime. It should be stated with that scope.

## What survives and remaining doubt
The core survives the physics attack. Two-sided constructions deliver nothing earlier than or without their coupling channel. The MQ speed-up (ω ∼ μ^{2/3} against μ) does not exceed the coupling's own capacity. Sycamore-type runs, including the proposed N = 20 to 100, lie far outside the semiclassical-bulk window, so they test boundary signatures such as size winding, not spacetime.

Remaining doubt:
- The SIE constant c and α_S(q = 4) are bracketed, not read from source. The conclusions hold across both brackets with one to four orders of margin.
- Sparse SYK may have a somewhat different α_S than dense SYK. It would need to be larger by about 10² to open a window at N = 100, and nothing in the dossier suggests that.
- Whether a large-N boundary simulation "tests spacetime" via duality is partly interpretive. The calculation shows only that no run in reach is in the regime where the question arises.
