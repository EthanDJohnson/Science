# Verdict on C1 (refuter 2, angle: scale)
claim: No viable option: no admissible traversable wormhole beats light through the exterior or its coupling channel, none stays open without negative null energy, and none could pass 1 kg in practice.
status: final
verdict: weakened
basis: cited-evidence
narrowed: Within GR plus semiclassical quantum fields (no labelled phantom matter or modified gravity), no known traversable wormhole beats light through the exterior or its coupling channel and none stays open without negative null energy; under any admissible physics, none could pass 1 kg within about 100 years.

Units: SI unless marked; "geometric" means G = c = 1.

## Strongest attack

The scale angle tests required against achievable, including stability. C1's practice leg (iii) holds by margins of 24 orders or more. My calculation and the verified engineer checks agree on this, so the attack does not land there. It lands on the two **in-principle** legs, which C1 states over "admissible" physics. The brief defines that scope as "consistent with established physics plus labelled extensions, with unlimited resources", and it lists phantom fields and modified gravity among the allowed labelled extensions.

1. **Leg (i), no shortcut. The "unstable" premise does not hold in general.** C1 sets aside the C4 designer shortcuts on the grounds that they "need achronal-ANEC violation or labelled phantom matter and are unstable". The instability evidence it relies on (D-13, D-14) covers ghost-scalar Ellis–Bronnikov throats and Poisson–Visser thin shells. It does not cover every labelled-exotic source. Bronnikov, Lipatova, Novikov & Shatskiy 2013 (Grav. Cosmol. 19, 269, peer-reviewed) take the *same Ellis metric* with a different labelled-exotic source: a negative-density perfect fluid plus a radial electric or magnetic field. They find it linearly stable under spherical perturbations and under axial perturbations of every multipole. A static Ellis throat is a shortcut once its mouths sit far apart in one exterior, so C1 cannot dismiss "labelled phantom" shortcuts as unstable. My calculation gives a 1 kg-capable throat (b₀ = 0.1 m) with T_thru/T_ext ≈ 1.6×10⁻¹¹ at 1 AU and 2.5×10⁻¹⁶ at 1 ly. The payload perturbs the throat by only G m/(c² b₀) = 7.4×10⁻²⁷, so back-reaction from 1 kg is negligible in principle. "No admissible traversable wormhole beats light" therefore holds only if admissibility excludes the brief's labelled phantom sources, or if a one-sided version of the Bronnikov model turns out unstable. Nobody has shown either.

2. **Leg (ii), no support without negative null energy. It is silent on labelled modified gravity.** Kanti, Kleihaus & Kunz (PRL 107, 271101, 2011; PRD 85, 044007, 2012) build 4D traversable wormholes in dilatonic Einstein–Gauss–Bonnet gravity "without needing any form of exotic matter", and a subset is linearly stable against radial perturbations. The required NEC violation is real but geometric: it is "realized via the presence of an effective energy-momentum tensor generated by the quadratic-in-curvature Gauss-Bonnet term". C1's leg (ii) is argued entirely within GR. Its sources are K-01, K-02, the static-throat ANEC calculation, and the Casimir support of the post-2016 constructions, and it never addresses modified gravity. Read with "negative null energy" meaning the energy of any matter or field, leg (ii) fails for labelled EGB. Read with it meaning the effective (curvature) stress tensor, leg (ii) becomes a restatement of the flare-out condition. It is true, but it no longer says anything about energy.

3. **Leg (i) also depends on an unproved step, by C1's own admission.** The in-principle no-shortcut leg for quantum-field throats rests on the self-consistent achronal ANEC, which is a conjecture [K-07a]. It also rests on the bridge "one-sided shortcut ⇒ complete achronal throat geodesic", and the math check refuted that bridge for misaligned mouths (M-DECOMPOSER-08, refuted). Even inside GR plus quantum fields, then, the leg is supported as "no known" and not proved as "no admissible". The narrowed core says "no known" for this reason.

Taken together: the practice leg stands in full. The in-principle legs stand only for GR plus semiclassical quantum fields, and there only as an expectation (no known counterexample), not as a theorem. The core claim's "no admissible" is too broad for the brief's own definition of admissible.

## In principle vs. in practice

- **In principle**, with unlimited resources and the brief's labelled extensions:
  - *Shortcuts.* A labelled negative-density fluid gives an Ellis-metric throat that is linearly stable in the two-sided model (Bronnikov et al. 2013). The same throat placed one-sided is a shortcut with T_thru/T_ext as small as desired, and a 1 kg payload is a 10⁻²⁷ perturbation. Its non-spherical polar stability is only "predicted", and nobody has analysed the one-sided embedding.
  - *Support without exotic matter.* Labelled dilatonic EGB gravity gives a radially stable traversable throat with no exotic matter.
  - *Within GR plus semiclassical quantum fields.* Here C1's in-principle legs stand as expectations. They need the self-consistent achronal ANEC (a conjecture) together with a bridge to one-sided shortcuts, and that bridge is refuted for misaligned mouths.
- **In practice** (about 100 years), C1 survives with large margins:
  - *SM-MMP row:* 24.2 to 29.1 orders short of 1 kg binding; 34.2 orders short in pair-creation field; no magnetic charge ever observed.
  - *Labelled-phantom row:* no phantom fluid known; 35.9 to 45.2 orders above demonstrated Casimir energy density; |exotic mass| 2.7×10²⁶ kg even at b₀ = 0.1 m.
  - *EGB row:* the theory itself is speculative, and no formation route is known.
  - *MM human-scale row:* 10⁴ M_☉ mouths, species count 12 to 23 orders above the cap, and 10⁻²² K environments (M-ENGINEER-08, -10).
  - Nothing in the dossier or in my search shrinks any of these gaps to within reach of 100-year technology.

## Corrections to details

- *Formation-energy gap.* C1 predicts that "the in-practice gaps stay above about 5 orders for every row". The SM-MMP formation-energy gap is 10^4.91 world-energy-years against 1 yr, just under 5. The binding (24 or more orders) and field (34.2 orders) gaps are what bind, so the prediction holds if it is read as the controlling gap per row.
- *The "unstable" clause.* C1 calls the C4 shortcut geometries "unstable". That holds for ghost-scalar throats [D-13] and for thin shells with ordinary sound speeds [D-14]. It does not hold for every labelled-exotic source with the same metric (Bronnikov et al. 2013).
- *"Every static, spherical, horizon-free two-ended throat: the radial ANEC is strictly negative, whatever the matter".* This is correct as a statement about the Einstein tensor (M-CONSTRAINTS-02). In modified gravity it constrains the effective stress tensor, not the matter, so "whatever the matter" should read "in GR".
- *Human-row exotic mass.* My human-row |exotic mass| of 1.2×10³¹ kg at b₀ = 4.5 km uses the 2b₀c²/G measure. M-IDEALIZER-06's 3.1 M_☉ uses the coefficient-1 measure. The two differ by the known factor (M-ENGINEER-02, M-IDEALIZER-04), and neither changes any conclusion.

## What survives and remaining doubt

**What survives.**
- The practice leg survives in full. No admissible construction passes 1 kg within about 100 years; the smallest controlling gap is about 24 orders.
- The in-principle legs survive only inside GR plus semiclassical quantum fields, and only as "no known counterexample". They rest on the self-consistent achronal ANEC conjecture and on an unproved one-sided bridge.

**Remaining doubt.** The case for narrowing rather than leaving C1 intact depends on three things:
- reading the brief's "admissible" to include labelled phantom and modified-gravity sources, as the brief's own "In principle" definition says;
- Bronnikov et al.'s linear stability carrying over to a one-sided embedding, which nobody has computed;
- for the EGB throat, whether "negative null energy" means matter energy or effective curvature. Read the second way, leg (ii) is a restatement of flare-out and not a statement about energy.

I chose weakened over survives because the heading says "no admissible", and the brief counts these labelled sources as admissible. I chose weakened over refuted because C1's own construction-by-construction case (the post-2016 rows, the practice leg) is untouched, and the counterexamples need labelled speculative matter or gravity, with stability shown only at linear order or radially.
