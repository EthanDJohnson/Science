# Verdict on C7 (refuter 1, angle: evidence)
claim: A wormhole's shortcut status depends on its mouths' clock offset: long wormholes become one-way shortcuts at offset T_thru - d before time machines at T_thru + d, so one barrier governs both cruxes.
status: final
verdict: weakened
basis: calculation
narrowed: Under the self-consistent achronal ANEC, every mouth-offset route from a long wormhole to a time machine is blocked before Δ_CTC = T_thru + d/c. The block falls exactly at the shortcut threshold Δ_s = T_thru − d/c only for mouths whose relative rotation fixes the separation axis (aligned gluing). For misaligned mouths it falls later, at T_thru − (d/c)·max|e·n̂| ≤ T_thru, so a one-way-shortcut stage up to d/c long is left ungoverned by the conjecture. If the conjecture fails, the two cruxes separate again.

Units: years and light-years with c = 1 ly/yr unless marked SI. T_thru is the throat transit time, d the mouth separation, Δ the lag of B's clock behind exterior-synchronised time. The other symbols are those of candidates.md.

## Strongest attack

The kinematic half of the heading is not in dispute. The shortcut threshold Δ_s = T_thru − d/c and the time-machine threshold Δ_CTC = T_thru + d/c have been verified six times, and the published sources are consistent with them (see Evidence). The weak point is the inference marked "so": that because the shortcut threshold comes first, **one barrier** (the self-consistent achronal ANEC, acting at Δ_s) governs both the shortcut crux and the chronology crux. That inference needs the shortcut epoch and the epoch with an achronal throat geodesic to coincide. The run's own math check refutes that coincidence.

1. **The shortcut epoch is not the achronal epoch for misaligned mouths (calculation, run math check).** M-DECOMPOSER-08 is marked **refuted**. Its corrected form says a complete achronal throat-crossing null geodesic exists iff −d < τ ≤ d·max|e·n̂|. Here τ = T_thru − Δ, and the maximum runs over the directions n̂ that the relative mouth rotation leaves fixed. The barrier therefore first acts at Δ_a = T_thru − d·|cos α|, where α is the angle between the rotation axis and the separation. Δ_a can lie anywhere from Δ_s up to T_thru. For MM's parameters (T_thru = πℓ = 9425 yr):
   - at d = 1000 ly, a mouth rotation about an axis perpendicular to the separation leaves a one-way-shortcut span of 1000 yr (half the 2000 yr window) in which no throat geodesic is achronal;
   - at d = ℓ the same span is 3000 yr of a 6000 yr window
   [calc: runs/2026-10-03-traversable-wormhole-shortcuts/calc/falsifier-C7-1_misaligned_window.py].

   In that span the long wormhole *is* a one-way shortcut, yet the conjecture says nothing about it. So the first threshold in C7's heading is not where "the barrier" acts in general.

2. **The cited literature treats chronology protection as a separate barrier at the chronology horizon, not at a shortcut threshold.** The standard protection mechanism acts at Δ_CTC: Klinkhammer 1992 places it "at the Cauchy horizon ('chronology horizon') where closed timelike curves first try to form". Graham–Olum's own time-machine theorem (Theorem 3) is also a statement about the Cauchy horizon, and it needs the generic condition. The shortcut ban needs neither. Both rest on the one conjecture, but they enter by different routes and at different epochs. If the conjecture fails (C3), Hawking-type protection could still act at Δ_CTC while the window Δ_s < Δ < Δ_CTC opens. The two cruxes are then governed separately. "One barrier governs both" is therefore a conditional statement, true only if the conjecture holds, and not a structural fact. C7 itself lists vacuum polarisation at Δ_CTC as rival (ii), which concedes this.

3. **What the attack does not achieve.** Even for misaligned mouths, Δ_a ≤ T_thru < Δ_CTC, so under the conjecture the barrier still acts before any CTC forms. The calculation's check line reads "Δ_a ≤ T_thru < Δ_CTC for every axis angle (barrier precedes CTC): PASS". This agrees with M-DECOMPOSER-08's "what survives": "−d < τ ≤ 0 always qualifies". The chronology half of "one barrier governs both cruxes" therefore survives (for orientable gluings); the shortcut half survives only for aligned mouths. That is a narrowing, not a refutation.

## In principle vs. in practice

- **In principle.** The thresholds are correct kinematics. Under the conjecture, conversion to a CTC is blocked before Δ_CTC for every orientable gluing (narrowed form above). The block at Δ_s holds only for aligned mouths.
- **In practice.** Some of the offset-accumulation routes C7 lists conflict with MM's own stability requirement. MM require the mouths' rotation frequency to be "much smaller than the energy gap 1/𝓁 … [so] that the throat is unaffected by the radiation and Unruh quanta" (arXiv:2008.06618 App. B, fetch_text). Applying this to a single moving mouth is my extrapolation of a condition MM state for the binary's mutual rotation. With ℓ = 3000 ly (gap c/ℓ = 1.06×10⁻¹¹ s⁻¹, SI):
  - a mouth parked on the Sgr A* ISCO rotates at Ω·ℓ/c = 3.0×10⁸;
  - a 0.9c circuit of radius 500 ly gives Ω·ℓ/c = 5.4;
  - a circuit at R = 1000 ly that respects Ω·ℓ/c = 0.01 needs v = 3.3×10⁻³ c and 1.5×10⁹ yr to reach Δ_s
  [calc: same file].

  So the fast routes probably destroy the throat before the window opens (C7's rival iii). The slow route is allowed in principle, but at timescales where coalescence and environment must be checked.

## Corrections to details

- **The listed accumulation routes.** The 0.9c route (about 1.5×10⁴ yr) and the Sgr A* ISCO route (2.9×10⁴ yr) break MM's Ω ≪ 1/ℓ throat condition by factors of 5.4 and 3×10⁸ respectively, under the extrapolation stated above. A route that respects the condition takes about 1.5×10⁹ yr at R = 1000 ly.
- **The "Δ = 0 is only a reading" caveat.** It understates the support for Δ = 0 in the published constructions. MM's Appendix B stabilises the pair by mutual Kepler rotation ("stabilizing the wormhole mouths by rotation"), and for equal mouths that symmetry fixes Δ = 0. Converting the wormhole needs an external, symmetry-breaking agent, which C7's "if false" clause already anticipates.
- **"So under the conjecture, protection must act at Δ_s".** This should read "at Δ_s for aligned mouths, and at T_thru − d·max|e·n̂| ≤ T_thru otherwise".

## What survives and remaining doubt

- **What survives:**
  - shortcut status depends on Δ;
  - long wormholes pass a one-way-shortcut stage 2d/c before the CTC threshold;
  - under the self-consistent achronal ANEC, every orientable mouth-offset route to a CTC is blocked before Δ_CTC, either through an achronal throat geodesic or, at the latest, by Graham–Olum's Theorem 3.
- **What is lost:**
  - the identification of the barrier's onset with the shortcut threshold, which holds only for aligned mouths;
  - the claim that one barrier governs both cruxes unconditionally. If the conjecture fails, the shortcut window opens while Hawking-type protection might still act at Δ_CTC.
- **Remaining doubt:**
  - all the achronality results are point-mouth, flat-exterior toy models;
  - nobody has computed which gluing MMP's or MM's actual mouths realise. The symmetric field-line geometry suggests a mirror-plane identification, which would be "aligned" along the axis, but I did not verify this;
  - for orientation-preserving (non-orientable-handle) gluings, M-IDEALIZER-02's "no achronal through-ray at any L" would extend to every offset below Δ_CTC, leaving only Theorem 3 at the Cauchy horizon. I did not check whether such gluings count as admissible.
