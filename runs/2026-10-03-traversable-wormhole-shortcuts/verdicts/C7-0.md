# Verdict on C7 (refuter 0, angle: physics)
claim: A wormhole's shortcut status depends on its mouths' clock offset: long wormholes become one-way shortcuts at offset T_thru - d before time machines at T_thru + d, so one barrier governs both cruxes.
status: final
verdict: weakened
basis: calculation
narrowed: In the static point-mouth model, the clock offset Δ sets both statuses (one-way shortcut for Δ > T_thru − d/c, closed causal loop for Δ ≥ T_thru + d/c), and the self-consistent achronal ANEC, if true, blocks every orientable mouth-motion route to a CTC no later than Δ = T_thru, at least d/c before Δ_CTC. That barrier coincides with the shortcut threshold only when the gluing's rotation axis lies along the mouth separation. For a generic orientable gluing, one-way shortcuts in a band d(1 − |e·n̂|)/c wide escape it, and if the conjecture fails the two cruxes come apart.

## Strongest attack
The kinematic half of the claim is right, and I could not break it. The thresholds Δ_s = T_thru − d/c and Δ_CTC = T_thru + d/c have been checked six times in math/. I re-checked them on 20,000 random cases by finding the minimum-weight cycle and found 0 mismatches. In the window, the reverse direction takes T_thru + Δ > d/c, so the shortcut is one-way.

The attack is on the inference "so one barrier governs both cruxes". The argument spells this out as "under the conjecture, protection must act at Δ_s, 2d/c before Hawking's chronology horizon." The conjecture (self-consistent achronal ANEC) only constrains a wormhole once a **complete achronal null geodesic** runs through the throat. In the point-mouth handle, that happens at the offset where τ = T_thru − Δ ≤ d·max{e·u : Ru = u}, where R maps a ray's entry direction to its exit direction (M-DECOMPOSER-08). I checked that formula by brute force: 24/24 cases agree.

What fixes R is a physics question, and I settled it by calculation:
- Glue mouth A's sphere to mouth B's by the map r ↦ Mr. A ray entering A along u leaves B along −Mu, so R = −M.
- The handle is orientable only if M reverses orientation (det M = −1). Then det R = +1, so R is in SO(3), and every orientable gluing has exactly one fixed axis n̂ (200/200 random cases).
- The barrier therefore acts at **Δ_a = T_thru − d|e·n̂|/c**, not at Δ_s.
  - Δ_a = Δ_s only when n̂ is parallel to the separation e. The two symmetric constructions do satisfy this: the mirror across the midplane gives R = diag(1, −1, −1) with axis e, and the point reflection gives R = I.
  - For any other orientable gluing, Δ_s < Δ_a ≤ T_thru. In that case one-way shortcuts in [Δ_s, Δ_a) thread no complete achronal null geodesic. The conjecture is silent about them, so it does not govern the shortcut crux there.
- With MM's numbers (T_thru = πℓ/c = 9424.8 yr, d = 1000 ly), the unconstrained shortcut band is:

| Axis angle to e | Band width |
|---|---|
| 0° | 0 yr |
| 30° | 134 yr |
| 60° | 500 yr |
| 90° | 1000 yr (half the 2000 yr window) |

- For a rotation drawn at random (uniform over all rotations), the mean band is about 505 yr. Only 0.38% of axes lie within 5° of e.
- The other half of the claim survives. Because Δ_a ≤ T_thru, the conjecture still acts at least d/c (1000 yr for MM) before Δ_CTC for every orientable gluing. So "the conjecture pre-empts chronology protection" stands, but "the conjecture acts exactly at the shortcut threshold" does not.
- Edge case: the non-orientable translation gluing (M = +I, so R = −I) has no fixed direction. With it, no complete straight through-geodesic is achronal anywhere in the window (best margin ≈ −2×10⁷ d on the 10⁷ d grid). Along that route, only Graham–Olum's Cauchy-horizon theorem would act, which happens at Δ_CTC itself. This is the case idealizer's "mirror" gluing m = −n describes (M-IDEALIZER-02). It is non-orientable in 3D, so I treat it as an edge case only.

**Logical asymmetry.** The candidate's own P2 says that a solution entering the window intact "falsifies the self-consistent achronal ANEC without any CTC forming". Its sub-hypothesis (ii) has vacuum polarisation acting only at Δ_CTC. So the barrier is "one" in a single direction only: the conjecture implies chronology protection (as Graham–Olum's Theorem 3 already says for compactly generated Cauchy horizons, quoted in candidates.md C6). Chronology protection does not imply the shortcut ban. If the conjecture fails, a separate barrier is still needed at Δ_CTC. "One barrier governs both cruxes" is therefore conditional on the conjecture being true, and even then only for aligned gluing.

## In principle vs. in practice
- **In principle:** the Δ-dependence of shortcut status and the ordering Δ_s < Δ_CTC are sound kinematics. The conjecture's barrier lies somewhere in [Δ_s, T_thru], and where depends on the gluing. It never lies above T_thru for orientable handles.
- **In practice:** reaching even Δ_s for an MM wormhole takes about 10⁴ to 10⁶ yr of relativistic mouth motion, or parking a mouth deep in a potential well (M-DECOMPOSER-04, M-MECHANIST-03/04 verified). The narrowing changes the conjecture's threshold by at most d/c (1000 yr for MM). It does not change any feasibility conclusion.

## Corrections to details
- "d" in the thresholds should be the exterior light time T_ext, which includes Shapiro delay near the mouths. With magnetically charged black-hole mouths (MM), T_ext > d/c. The window is 2T_ext, not exactly 2d/c (M-DIALECTICIAN-01 notes "Shapiro delay ignored").
- The 0.1c accumulation figure (1.7×10⁶ yr) has the mouth covering about 1.7×10⁵ ly. The motion must be a circuit that keeps the separation near d (M-DECOMPOSER-04 note). Under straight-line recession, the growing d alone would make the wormhole a geometric shortcut long before Δ reaches Δ_s.
- "Protection must act at Δ_s" should read "at Δ_a = T_thru − d|e·n̂|/c ∈ [Δ_s, T_thru]" for orientable gluings.

## What survives and remaining doubt
- **What survives:**
  - the reframe that shortcut status is a property of the geometry plus the mouths' clock offset, not of the geometry alone;
  - the one-way window, 2T_ext wide;
  - the point that MM's footnote ("long wormholes cannot be converted into time machines") holds only dynamically;
  - the point that the conjecture, if true, pre-empts Hawking's chronology horizon by at least d/c for every orientable mouth-motion route.
- **What fails is the exact identification of the two barriers.** For aligned gluing (the symmetric mirror and point-reflection constructions) they coincide at Δ_s. For generic gluing they do not. And if the conjecture is false, they separate entirely.
- **Remaining doubt:**
  - all of this is a toy model: point mouths, flat exterior, constant R, no back-reaction;
  - which R the MM or MMP throats actually realise (for example, whether the orbital co-rotation sets n̂ along the orbital axis, which is perpendicular to e) is not stated in the sources and was not computed;
  - finite mouths and a curved exterior could add or remove near-grazing achronal geodesics, as C6's decisive test notes.
