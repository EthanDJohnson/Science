## Strongest attack
The scale attack asks whether a real long wormhole can be carried to the offset Δ_s = T_thru − d/c at all. If it cannot, "one barrier governs both cruxes" is empty: the barrier would never be reached, and rival (iii), that the wormhole dies first, would take over. Maldacena–Milekhin (MM) is the only long wormhole with numbers, so I tested each route the candidate uses to build up the offset against three things: MM's own conditions for not disrupting the throat, MM's own lifetime, and the energy and force each route needs.

- **What MM requires.** The mouths may move only with "an angular velocity Ω ≪ 1/𝓁 that is smaller than the energy gap, so that we do not have a disruptive effect inside the wormhole". The same gap condition is what protects the throat from Unruh quanta (App. B). In SI units this means Ωℓ/c ≪ 1 and a proper acceleration a ≪ 2πc²/ℓ = 0.020 m/s².
- **Three of the candidate's four routes fail this test.**
  - *Parking at the Sgr A\* ISCO* violates MM's condition by 8 orders of magnitude: Ωℓ/c = 3.0×10⁸. Gravitational-wave radiation reaction makes the mouth plunge about 2.6 days after it reaches r = 6GM/c². Holding it there for 2.9×10⁴ yr would mean replacing 1.5×10⁵⁶ J of radiated energy, about 8×10⁴ times the mouth's own rest energy.
  - *Circling at 0.9c* within d = 1000 ly gives Ωℓ/c = 2.7, and an acceleration twice the Unruh-gap value. The throat is disrupted.
  - *Circling at 0.1c* at 10³ ly gives Ωℓ/c = 0.3, which is marginal rather than "≪ 1".
- **The energy cost is understated.** The candidate quotes the kinetic energy, 9.1×10⁴⁸ J. The real cost of a circling route is the centripetal impulse, F·t ≈ 2(cΔ_s/R)·Mc/c. That impulse cannot come from gravity: it would need a central mass of 6.4×10¹³ M_☉ (at 0.1c) or 6.4×10¹¹ M_☉ (at 0.01c) inside 10³ ly. Delivered by a photon drive it costs about 17 Mc² ≈ 3×10⁵² J at any speed, which is 3×10³ times the quoted figure.

The attack still fails against the core claim, for two reasons.

- **A gentle route stays inside MM's own conditions.** Circling at β = 0.01 to 0.03 with R = d = 10³ ly gives Ωℓ/c = 0.03 to 0.09, and accelerations 5×10⁻⁵ to 4×10⁻⁴ of the Unruh-gap value. It reaches Δ_s in 1.7×10⁸ to 1.9×10⁷ yr.
- **MM's own lifetime is far longer.** Their Keplerian coalescence formula, T ≈ (3/4)d³/r_e², gives 3.7×10¹⁶ yr at d = 0.5 ly (their "about 10¹⁶ years") and 3×10²⁶ yr at d = 10³ ly. The dark-U(1) radiation from forced circling removes only about 10⁻¹⁵ of Mc².

So for MM, coalescence (rival iii) does not stop the conversion. With unlimited resources, the window from Δ_s to Δ_CTC can in principle be reached under MM's own rules. The threshold ordering itself is kinematics, verified six times (M-DIALECTICIAN-01, M-MECHANIST-01, M-DECOMPOSER-01 and others), and scale cannot touch it.

## In principle vs. in practice
- **In principle (unlimited resources):**
  - *MM.* The core survives. A slowly circling MM mouth (β ≲ 0.03) meets MM's own no-disruption conditions and reaches Δ_s in about 10⁷ to 10⁸ yr. That is 18 orders of magnitude inside its coalescence lifetime. The barrier at Δ_s is therefore reachable, so "one barrier governs both cruxes" has real content and is not empty.
  - *FGM 2019.* Building up the offset dynamically does not work. FGM is traversable "only at sufficiently early times" and is exponentially fragile, so any mouth acceleration is a "larger perturbation". FGM can enter the window only if the offset is built into its initial data. The kinematic threshold still applies to FGM.
- **In practice (about 100 years):** none of this is achievable.
  - Each mouth is about 2×10³⁴ kg (10⁴ M_☉). Moving one costs at least about 10⁴⁹ J of kinetic energy, plus about 3×10⁵² J of reaction-drive energy for any circling route of radius 10³ ly.
  - The MM wormhole cannot survive in our environment in the first place: the paper's refrigerator and 10⁻²⁶ eV conditions (dossier D-08).
  - The gap from current technology exceeds 20 orders of magnitude in energy.
  - So the reframe changes how the in-principle question is posed, not the practical answer.

## Corrections to details
- **Sgr A\* ISCO parking** ("2.9×10⁴ yr to the shortcut, 3.6×10⁴ yr to the CTC") is not a valid route for an MM mouth. Ωℓ/c = 3×10⁸ violates MM's condition Ω ≪ 1/ℓ. The mouth plunges about 2.6 days after reaching r = 6GM/c² (quadrupole estimate). Holding it in place would mean replacing about 8×10⁴ Mc² of gravitational-wave energy. The time-dilation arithmetic (M-MECHANIST-04) is correct; the route is not.
- **Mouth motion at 0.9c, "about 1.5×10⁴ yr"**:
  - It cannot be done by circling within d = 10³ ly, where Ωℓ/c = 2.7 and the acceleration is 2× the Unruh-gap value.
  - It must be a straight-line trip with gentle turnarounds. One reversal at a = 0.1 × 2πc²/ℓ takes about 2.0×10⁴ yr of coordinate time and overshoots by about 6×10³ ly, so the total time is at least 2× the quoted figure.
  - A photon-rocket mass ratio of e^{4·artanh 0.9} ≈ 360 for the full trip is my estimate, not a computed log line.
- **Mouth motion at 0.1c** at 10³ ly is marginal (Ωℓ/c = 0.3). MM's "≪" condition is met comfortably only at β ≲ 0.03, which takes about 2×10⁷ to 2×10⁸ yr.
- **Energy figures.** The "9.1×10⁴⁸ J (51 M_☉c²)" is kinetic energy only. For circling routes the dominant cost is the centripetal impulse:
  - by photon drive it is about 3×10⁵² J ≈ 17 Mc² at R = 10³ ly, roughly independent of β;
  - by gravity it is impossible, since it would need 10¹¹ to 10¹³ M_☉ inside 10³ ly.
- **"Circling at 10³ ly: needs 1.9×10³⁰ N from an external agent."** The number is correct. That agent cannot be a gravitating mass (see the previous item), so it must be a reaction drive or field coupling on the scale given there.
- **Rival (iii) "coalescence"** is ruled out for MM by MM's own Appendix B: the lifetime is about 10¹⁶ yr at d = 0.5 ly and 3×10²⁶ yr at d = 10³ ly. This strengthens C7's point that MM's footnote holds "only dynamically". With a lifetime that long, any sustained redshift asymmetry ε ≳ 3×10⁻²³ would cross Δ_s, so the footnote needs Δ to be held at essentially zero.

## What survives and remaining doubt
- **What survives:** the core. Shortcut status depends on the clock offset. Δ_s = T_thru − d/c comes 2d/c before Δ_CTC. Under the self-consistent achronal ANEC, the barrier must act at Δ_s and so also covers chronology. For MM, Δ_s can in principle be reached within MM's own stability conditions and lifetime, so the barrier is not empty.
- **Remaining doubts:**
  - My disruption criteria (Ωℓ/c ≪ 1, a ≪ 2πc²/ℓ) are order-of-magnitude readings of MM's own statements, not a computed throat response. Whether a circling mouth's tidal or field perturbations break the RS II dark-sector solution at β ≈ 0.01 was not calculated.
  - The circling geometry treats d as fixed at R. The candidate's own toy model (M-MECHANIST-06, M-DIALECTICIAN-07) loses the long equilibrium at 0.25 to 0.56 Δ_s, so the wormhole that reaches Δ_s may not be the original MM solution. That is the candidate's decisive test, not a scale failure.
  - For FGM, the offset cannot be built up by moving the mouths.
