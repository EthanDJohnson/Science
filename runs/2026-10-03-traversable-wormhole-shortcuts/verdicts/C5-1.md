## Strongest attack

The claim needs two things: a valid, two-ended, traversable EDM solution, and no exotic matter. The literature, including the papers that support the claim, gives neither.

1. **Each two-ended EDM solution in print has a published defect that its own authors or a direct critique state.**
   - *Symmetric BKR 2021.* These solutions are not solutions. The matching conditions fail at the throat, which leaves a charged shell and a spurious Dirac source there (Danielson, Satishchandran, Wald & Weinbaum, PRD 104, 124055).
   - *Asymmetric solutions (Konoplya–Zhidenko 2022; Kain, PRD 108, 084010).* These are negative-frequency solutions used with the classical stress tensor. Weinbaum 2026 says "a physical interpretation of these classical Dirac solutions does not exist".
   - *Kain's asymmetric static solutions under evolution.* Evolved in time, they form black holes and trap every null ray that crosses the throat: "Einstein-Dirac-Maxwell wormholes are not traversable" (Kain, PRD 108, 044019).
   - *Dzhunushaliev et al. 2026 (EPJC 86, 240).* This is the one paper the candidate lists as unread support, and I opened it here. Its regular solutions exist only for spinor frequency −1 < Ω̄ < Ω̄_crit ≈ −ē, so every one is negative-frequency. The authors attribute the negative masses to "the negative energy density of the spinor field". They also say they expect their configurations to be unstable, as Kain's were.
   - *Positive-frequency (physical) sources.* Weinbaum's numerical search finds only partial wormholes, which "cannot be continued to a second asymptotically flat end".
2. **"Without exotic matter" contradicts the brief's own definition.**
   - The brief defines exotic matter as "matter that violates the NEC classically".
   - Every static two-ended asymptotically flat throat needs a negative radial NEC integral. The run's math check M-CONSTRAINTS-02 proved this, and Weinbaum p. 4 states it.
   - Kain's abstract confirms that the asymmetric EDM static solutions "violate the null energy condition".
   - The classical Dirac field is therefore exotic matter in the brief's sense. The phrase "no exotic matter" only relabels which field supplies the NEC violation; it does not remove the violation.

Either point alone defeats the core claim. Together they leave no published EDM configuration that is a valid solution, has a physically interpretable source, is two-ended, stays traversable under evolution, and is free of NEC violation.

## In principle vs. in practice

- **In principle.** No published EDM configuration meets all of the claim's conditions.
  - BKR are not solutions.
  - KZ, Kain QFT and Dzhunushaliev et al. use negative-frequency modes with the classical stress tensor, which Weinbaum calls physically uninterpretable.
  - Kain's evolved asymmetric solutions are not traversable.
  - The only physically meaningful (positive-frequency) search finds only one-ended partial wormholes.
  - Even a valid solution would need NEC-violating matter, which is exotic by the brief's own definition.
  - What remains is an open loophole, not a result:
    - Weinbaum's search is numerical, restricted to a definite-frequency, definite-parity spherical ansatz, and covers neutral Einstein–Dirac.
    - He argues that including the self-generated Maxwell field for a single particle is itself inconsistent. If that holds, it removes the "–Maxwell" ingredient rather than leaving a gap for it.
- **In practice.** Worse still.
  - Kain's throats are 75 to 498 Planck lengths, about 10⁻³³ m (SI) [D-11, Q-18].
  - The q/μ < 1 condition needs fermions near the Planck mass. No known charged fermion qualifies (dossier M-ENGINEER-12).

## Corrections to details

- **Weinbaum's scope.** The candidate's sub-hypothesis (c) and the "Who" line credit Weinbaum with a Maxwell-coupled analysis. His positive-frequency search covers the neutral Einstein–Dirac system: p. 9 says "we will restrict our attention to normalizable, positive-frequency, neutral, classical Dirac fields". A Weinbaum-type search of the Maxwell-coupled branch has not been done. So the candidate's decisive test is correctly framed, but its basis is narrower than written.
- **Dzhunushaliev et al. 2026 is evidence against, not for.** The candidate lists it as "for" evidence [D-C2], read from the abstract only. The full text shows that all its regular solutions are negative-frequency (−1 < Ω̄ < Ω̄_crit ≤ 0), that the authors invoke negative spinor energy density, and that they themselves expect instability. It therefore falls under Weinbaum's negative-frequency objection and the dialectician's P4 prediction ("any two-ended solution uses negative-frequency … modes, or collapses").
- **Kain PRD 108, 084010 is the QFT paper.** Weinbaum's ref [14] is Kain's "EDM wormholes in quantum field theory" (PRD 108, 084010), not the evolution paper (PRD 108, 044019). The dossier's line that Weinbaum "undercuts … Kain 2023" should point to 084010.

## What survives and remaining doubt

- **What survives.**
  - Classical charged Dirac fields can supply NEC violation; Weinbaum p. 29 gives an explicit T_kk = −1/(2π) example on a fixed background.
  - Static EDM throats exist as formal ODE solutions if negative-frequency classical spinors are allowed.
  - Neither of these is the claim, which requires a traversable wormhole held open without exotic matter.
- **Remaining doubt.**
  - No theorem forbids a positive-frequency, Maxwell-coupled, two-ended, dynamically stable EDM wormhole. The evidence against it is numerical and ansatz-limited.
  - I did not open the BKR reply (arXiv:2108.12187) beyond its abstract opening. No BKR or KZ response to Kain or Weinbaum was found.
  - Even if such a solution turned up, it would still violate the NEC, so the "without exotic matter" half of the claim fails by definition whatever the numerics show.
