# Verdict on C3 (refuter 1, angle: evidence)
claim: Shortcuts, and hence time machines, are possible in principle because the self-consistent achronal ANEC fails: a back-reacted semiclassical spacetime can violate ANEC on a complete achronal throat geodesic.
status: final
verdict: weakened
basis: cited-evidence
narrowed: The self-consistent achronal ANEC is an unproven conjecture, so no theorem yet excludes shortcuts or time machines in semiclassical gravity; but no back-reacted spacetime that violates ANEC on a complete achronal geodesic is known, and none of the violations C3 cites is self-consistent or sits on a throat.

## Strongest attack
C3's core is an existence claim: the conjecture "fails", so a back-reacted spacetime can violate ANEC on a complete achronal throat geodesic. It offers three pieces of evidence for this. Read in full, each is a fixed-background or test-field result, which is exactly the class the self-consistent conjecture was written to exclude. Two of the three sources undercut C3 in their own text.

1. **Urban & Olum 2010 is a fixed, conformally flat background, and it cannot hold a throat or a shortcut.**
   - The spacetime is chosen as Ω²η (a conformal rescaling of Minkowski), not solved from G = 8π⟨T⟩.
   - A spacetime globally conformal to Minkowski has Minkowski's light cones and R⁴ topology. So it has no throat, and nothing in it can outrun Minkowski light. The violation exists, but in a setting where a shortcut is impossible by construction.
   - The authors say both violations "could be softened by some transverse averaging".
   - Kontou 2024 classes such violations as eliminable: "Those can be eliminated either by averaging over transverse directions or imposing self-consistency."
2. **Ishibashi, Maeda & Mefford 2019 also use a fixed background, and their result argues against shortcuts.**
   - The boundary metric is set by a freely chosen conformal factor. In the even-dimensional case the violation is "due to the conformal anomaly".
   - The boundary is "causally proper": the fastest null geodesics lie on the boundary.
   - They prove the converse: if causal properness fails, "there must be a naked singularity in the bulk". In their holographic setting, a boundary shortcut costs cosmic censorship.
3. **The Dirac-field ANEC violations [D-12] are on fixed wormhole backgrounds.** The dossier records that Weinbaum "finds no self-consistent solution" (dossier line 262).
4. **The best back-reacted 4D construction ends at the bound and does not cross it.**
   - FGM 2019 (asymptotically flat, back-reacted) find t_min transit = d + logs. The abstract calls d the value that "would be the theoretical minimum" (c = 1, d = mouth separation in m).
   - FGM 2018 use topological censorship to argue that the complete null curves in their construction are achronal. Their wormholes are built to respect the condition, not to break it.
   - C3's sub-hypothesis (b) depends on a comparison path that the math check flags as a modelling choice (M-CONSTRAINTS-17, verified "the algebra only").
5. **The beyond-semiclassical escape (sub-hypothesis c) is quoted without its remedy.**
   - Wall 2010 does say the ANEC-based theorems fail once the linearised graviton is quantised.
   - In the same abstract, Wall proposes a "shear-inclusive" generalisation that "is proven under an additional assumption".
   - So what is shown is the loss of one proof route, not a violation.
6. **The literature's current status is the opposite of C3's premise.** Kontou 2024: "No counterexamples to the self-consistent achronal ANEC have been found in the context of semiclassical gravity".
7. **The inference is invalid.**
   - Graham–Olum show the condition is sufficient to rule out CTCs and wormholes. If it fails, the no-go is lost, but that does not establish possibility.
   - A shortcut would still need a back-reacted negative-energy source at throat scale, and the free-field quantum inequalities constrain that source (C4, K-09).
   - On the shortcut leg, the "because" is not even needed. M-DECOMPOSER-08 (refuted claim) shows that misaligned one-sided shortcuts have no complete achronal throat geodesic, so they evade the conjecture without its failing (C6's point).
   - On the time-machine leg, the dependence is real. The same check finds that every mouth-motion route to a CTC passes through an epoch with an achronal throat geodesic.

What I cannot do is prove the conjecture. Wall's proof is conditional on the GSL plus auxiliary assumptions, and Kontou–Olum 2015 prove ANEC on achronal geodesics only for backgrounds obeying the null convergence condition, which a throat violates (R_kk = 8πT_kk < 0 there). So C3's open-question form survives. Its claim that the condition fails does not.

## Evidence
- Urban & Olum 2010, arXiv:0910.5925 (fetch_text, PDF p. 10): "It appears that both of the above violations could be softened by some transverse aver- aging. ... the violation in Sec. III grows as r → 0, where r parameterizes the width of the deviation from flatness." Abstract: "Since all geodesics in conformally flat spacetimes are achronal, the achronal averaged null energy condition is likewise violated."
- Kontou 2024, arXiv:2405.05963 (fetch_text, p. 13): "there are violations that occur if the geodesic is not integrated in the transverse direction over at least a few Planck lengths. Examples are included in Ref. [6], where violations in spacetimes conformal to Minkowski were considered. Those can be eliminated either by averaging over transverse directions or imposing self-consistency." p. 24: "No counterexamples to the self-consistent achronal ANEC have been found in the context of semiclassical gravity, and it has been shown [6] that it is sufficient to prevent causality violations."
- Ishibashi, Maeda & Mefford 2019, arXiv:1903.11806 (abstract, fetch_text): "The conformal boundary of our bubble solution is asymptotically flat and is causally proper in the sense that a "fastest null geodesics" connecting any two points on the boundary must lie entirely on the boundary. We show that conversely, if the spacetime fails to have this causally proper nature, then there must be a naked singularity in the bulk." PDF p. 4: "we have shown that the achronal ANEC can be violated in the boundary CFT theory with even dimension, due to the conformal anomaly."
- Wall 2010, arXiv:0910.5751 (abstract): "It is pointed out that these theorems fail once the linearized graviton field is quantized, because then the renormalized shear squared term in the Raychaudhuri equation can be negative. A "shear-inclusive" generalization of the ANEC is proposed to remedy this, and is proven under an additional assumption about perturbations to horizons in classical general relativity."
- Fu, Grado-White & Marolf 2019, arXiv:1908.03273 (abstract): "a wormhole with mouths separated by a distance $d$ becomes traversable with a minimum transit time $t_{\text{min transit}} = d + \text{logs}$ ... and approaches the value that, at least in higher dimensions, would be the theoretical minimum."
- Fu, Grado-White & Marolf 2018, arXiv:1807.07917 (PDF p. 30): "That curve must be achronal, else the two bound- aries would be causally connected (violating topological censorship [4, 5])."
- Graham & Olum 2007, arXiv:0705.3193 (abstract): "there is a weaker condition, which is free of known violations, requiring only that there is no self-consistent space-time in semiclassical gravity in which ANEC is violated on a complete, achronal null geodesic."
- Kontou & Olum 2015, arXiv:1507.00297 (abstract): "We then use this inequality to prove ANEC on achronal geodesics in a curved background that obeys the null convergence condition." (Limit: this does not cover throats, which violate the null convergence condition.)
- Math checks: M-DECOMPOSER-08 (refuted: shortcut does not imply an achronal throat geodesic for misaligned mouths; CTC routes still pass through an achronal epoch); M-CONSTRAINTS-17 (FGM 2018 threshold verified as algebra only; comparison path is a modelling choice); M-CONSTRAINTS-13 (CTC iff Δ ≥ T_thru + T_ext).

## In principle vs. in practice
- **In principle.**
  - The question is open: the conjecture is unproven. Its proof routes are Wall's GSL argument (conditional) and NCC-background or flat-space theorems, none of which covers a back-reacted throat.
  - C3 is right that no theorem closes the door.
  - C3 is not right that the door is open. No example passes through it, and every cited "violation" belongs to Kontou's fixed-background or Planck-localised classes.
- **In practice.**
  - No self-consistent construction exists. The one back-reacted asymptotically flat construction, FGM 2019, reaches t_min = d + logs, which is not a shortcut.
  - Even if the conjecture failed, the shortcut would still need a throat-scale negative-energy source that the free-field quantum inequalities confine (C4).

## Corrections to details
- "Wall's GSL derivation fails once linearised gravitons are quantised": Wall says the downstream theorems fail and offers a shear-inclusive ANEC, proven under an extra assumption. Citing the failure without the remedy overstates it.
- Urban–Olum is listed as a "known achronal-ANEC violation" supporting C3. The violation is real, but it is in a fixed spacetime globally conformal to Minkowski. That spacetime has Minkowski causal structure and trivial topology, so it can contain neither a throat nor a shortcut. It is not evidence for a violation on a "throat geodesic".
- Ishibashi et al. is likewise a fixed boundary metric with an anomaly-driven violation. The paper's converse theorem (a boundary that is not causally proper implies a bulk naked singularity) counts against shortcuts, not for them.
- "It becomes a time machine at a clock lag of only d/c" is the T_thru → 0 limit of the condition Δ ≥ T_thru + T_ext (M-CONSTRAINTS-13). It holds at the stated precision for a short throat.
- The shortcut leg does not need the conjecture to fail. Misaligned one-sided shortcuts evade it (M-DECOMPOSER-08). Only the time-machine leg depends on the failure.

## What survives and remaining doubt
- **What survives:** the self-consistent achronal ANEC is a conjecture. If a self-consistent violation on a complete achronal throat geodesic were found, short shortcuts and time machines (at a clock lag near d/c) would no longer be excluded by it.
- **What does not survive:** the claim that the condition "fails". No cited source shows it, and the sources C3 relies on say their violations are fixed-background, anomaly-driven or removable by self-consistency or transverse averaging.
- **Remaining doubt:**
  - Wall's proof assumes the GSL, extra-strong cosmic censorship and horizon persistence, and breaks for quantised gravitons. Sub-hypothesis (c) is therefore not excluded, only unsupported.
  - Second-order FGM 2019 (C3's decisive test) has not been done.
  - I did not read the Graham–Olum body or the full Weinbaum Dirac paper.
