# Verdict on C6 (refuter 1, angle: evidence)
claim: Even granting the self-consistent achronal ANEC, the shortcut ban is narrower than stated: it forbids only shortcuts threaded by a complete achronal throat geodesic, so misaligned one-sided shortcuts escape it.
status: final
verdict: weakened
basis: calculation
narrowed: Granting the conjecture, no theorem extends the shortcut ban to multiply connected (one-sided) spacetimes, because Graham–Olum's topological censorship assumes simple connectedness; but mouth misalignment is not an escape route: once the mouths have finite size, the misaligned static shortcuts used as counterexamples are threaded by complete achronal throat-crossing null lines, so any escape must come from curved-exterior or back-reaction effects that nobody has computed.

## Strongest attack
C6 has two clauses.

The first, that the ban reaches only shortcuts threaded by a complete achronal throat geodesic, is what the literature says. Graham & Olum say it themselves (E1).

The second, that **misaligned** one-sided shortcuts therefore escape, rests entirely on two point-mouth toy models (M-DECOMPOSER-08, M-IDEALIZER-01/02). Both give the throat one fixed direction map, u → R u (or n → O n), which is the map for radial rays only. A mouth of any finite radius a also carries non-radial through-rays, and their exit direction depends on where they hit. The "straight line that keeps its direction", which the decomposer's check showed an achronal through-line needs, can then be found by choosing the hit point. I checked two finite-mouth versions of C6's own counterexample: a static shortcut with throat light-time 0.5d, mouths rotated 10°, 90° or 180° about an axis perpendicular to the separation, flat exterior, c = 1.

1. **Thin-shell mouths, zero-length shell with a fixed delay τ = 0.5d.** A null ray crossing the shell keeps its tangential components and the size of its normal component. So a ray hitting A at outward normal n leaves B along G·reflect_n(v). For any G with G⁻¹v ≠ v, the hit point n ∝ (G⁻¹v − v) sends it out parallel to v.
   - For every orientable gluing tested (G = −R, det G = −1), at a = 0.05d and a = 0.01d, a realigned complete through-throat null line exists. Its asymptotic margin is −0.50 d to −0.60 d.
   - Over all point pairs checked (exterior, one-pass and multi-pass routes), the supremum margin is 0, attained by the line itself. **The line is achronal.**
   - The threshold becomes τ ≤ d + 2a|n·v|, or √(d² + 4a²) for the 180° case. Both are ≥ d, so **every** static shortcut with finite orientable thin-shell mouths has such a line.
   - The one exception is the translation gluing G = +I: there the ray realigns only at grazing incidence. [calc: runs/2026-10-03-traversable-wormhole-shortcuts/calc/falsifier-C6-1_thinshell_realign.py]
2. **A throat of real proper length L = 0.5d** (tube S²(a) × [0, L]). A ray with incidence angle i sweeps an angle L·tan i / a around the throat. A slightly oblique near-radial ray can therefore carry its exit point to wherever the gluing needs it.
   - Newton's method finds realigned rays for the 10°, 90° and 180° rotations, with residual ≤ 3.7×10⁻¹³, and also for the translation ("mirror") gluing.
   - In all these cases τ_eff = L/cos i lies between 0.500 d and 0.538 d, and the asymptotic margin between −0.52 d and −0.60 d. That margin is the quantity the decomposer's check found to be the supremum. [calc: runs/2026-10-03-traversable-wormhole-shortcuts/calc/falsifier-C6-1_tube_realign.py]

So "misaligned ⇒ no achronal throat line" is an artefact of the singular limit a → 0. Throwing away the non-radial rays throws away the realigned line.

There is also an internal inconsistency. C6 cites the idealizer's "mirror gluing (O = −I, the standard Visser cut-and-paste orientation)". By the decomposer's own convention, orientable gluings are −R with R ∈ SO(3). O = −I has det −1, so the "mirror" handle is **non-orientable**. A frame check agrees: the ray's frame (x, y, z) comes out as (−x, y, z).

This narrows C6 rather than refuting it, for three reasons:
- the first clause stands, since no theorem covers one-sided handles;
- the models have a flat exterior, idealised throats and no back-reaction;
- the tube case has only its asymptotic margin computed.

The surviving content is the logical gap, not the misalignment loophole.

## Evidence
- **E1. Graham & Olum 2007, arXiv:0705.3193, p. 5 (ACCESS: full-text).**
  - "We must also restrict ourselves to simply-connected spacetimes, which means that the wormholes we rule out are only those which connect one asymptotically flat region to another, not those which connect a region to itself."
  - Their counterexample is a **long** throat: "suppose the throat of the wormhole is longer than the distance between the mouths on the outside ... We can still find fastest paths through the wormhole, but they are chronal. This can happen because the timelike connections between points on such a path are not in the same homotopy class as the path itself." (pp. 5–6.)
  - No chronal *shortcut* example is given.
- **E2. Kontou & Olum 2013, arXiv:1212.2290, abstract (lit_search).** "Exotic spacetimes, such as those allow wormholes or the construction of time machines are possible in general relativity only if ANEC is violated along achronal geodesics." This is the broad form that E1 shows is unproved for one-sided handles, which supports C6's first clause.
- **E3. Dokuchaev & Eroshenko 2014, arXiv:1308.0896, abstract (ACCESS: full-text abstract page).** "If any particle (or classical object) is traversing through the non-orientable wormhole, it turns into a corresponding mirror particle and vice versa." This bears on the thin-shell G = +I case, the only one left without a realigned ray in model 1.
- **E4. Lemos et al. 2021, arXiv:2009.04071, abstract (ACCESS: full-text abstract page).** "theoretical arguments that combine universality of physical experiments with local arrow of time, CP violation and CPT invariance are offered to support the choosing of time- and space-orientable spacetime manifolds."
  - Against this, Chamblin et al. 1997 (gr-qc/9607079, lit_search abstract) write that "Many of these instantons admit the pin structure which corresponds to the fermions actually observed in nature".
  - So orientability is a physical expectation, not a theorem.
- **E5. Thin-shell calculation** (falsifier-C6-1_thinshell_realign.py.log). Flat exterior, c = 1, d = 1, τ = 0.5d.
  - a = 0.05d: "R = 10 deg about z (perp to e) | det G = -1 ... asymptotic margin tau - (Q0-P0).v = -0.59951 d ... sup margin = +0.00000 d ... ACHRONAL complete throat line exists".
  - "R = 180 deg ... achronal for tau <= 1.00491 d ... ACHRONAL".
  - a = 0.01d: "R = 180 deg ... achronal for tau <= 1.00019 d", which matches the analytic √(d² + 4a²) = 1.00020 d.
  - "non-orientable translation gluing G = +I ... no direction v admits a realigned through-ray (v_out = v) except grazing incidence".
  - Method:
    - the one-pass arrival time is minimised over a 12 000-point sphere grid, refined by pattern search to 10⁻¹¹ rad;
    - exterior times use the straight-line distance, a lower bound;
    - two or more passes are bounded below by 2τ plus the distances to the spheres.
- **E6. Tube calculation** (falsifier-C6-1_tube_realign.py.log), L = 0.5d.
  - "a = 0.05 d, R = 180 deg about z (perp e): realigned ray |v_out - v| = 3.7e-15; tau_eff = 0.5170 d (cos i = 0.9672) ... asymptotic margin = -0.5797 d".
  - "a = 0.05 d, non-orientable translation gluing G = +I ...: tau_eff = 0.5170 d ... asymptotic margin = -0.5797 d".
  - "a = 0.01 d, R = 90 deg ...: tau_eff = 0.5020 d ... asymptotic margin = -0.5179 d".
  - The full pair check was not repeated for the tube.
- **E7. Math check M-DECOMPOSER-08 (math/decomposer.md).** Its model is "point mouths ... relative mouth rotation R (orientable gluing −R)", and its own caveat reads "The model has point mouths". E5 and E6 show that this caveat carries the counterexample.

## In principle vs. in practice
- **In principle.** The logical gap remains: granting the conjecture, no theorem forbids one-sided shortcuts. But "misaligned shortcuts" do not fill it. In both finite-mouth flat-exterior models, misaligned static shortcuts are threaded by achronal through-lines, which places them inside the ban's reach.
- **What is still open:**
  - (i) curved exteriors, photon-sphere lensing and back-reaction, which could add delay of the order of the mouth size and might reopen a thin band just below T_thru = T_ext; nobody has computed this for either sign;
  - (ii) the thin-shell translation gluing, which is non-orientable and rests on a labelled mirror sector or pin-structure physics.
- **In practice.** Unchanged, as C6 concedes. No self-consistent one-sided quantum shortcut solution exists in any orientation.

## Corrections to details
- "Mirror gluing (O = −I, the standard Visser cut-and-paste orientation)": by the decomposer's convention (orientable ⇔ R ∈ SO(3)), O = −I gives a non-orientable handle. The "standard" label is unsupported.
- The prediction "finite mouths narrow the loophole by O(a/d) but do not close it" is wrong in both flat-exterior finite-mouth models. Any a > 0 closes it for the tested misalignments: the threshold rises from d·max|e·n̂| to at least about d.
- The decomposer's counterexample ("L = 0.5d and mouths rotated by 10° or 180° ... every throat-crossing null geodesic is chronal") holds only at a = 0.
- The "near-grazing d/ε²" escape C6 mentions is not the generic situation. Realigned rays are near-radial (cos i ≥ 0.93 in the tube model) or obliquely incident at a finite angle (thin shell).

## What survives and remaining doubt
- **Survives:** granting the self-consistent achronal ANEC, the ban is proved only where a complete achronal null line threads the handle (E1). Its extension to one-sided shortcuts is an expectation, not a theorem.
- **Does not survive:** "misaligned one-sided shortcuts escape it" as a class.
- **Remaining doubt:**
  - the models have a flat exterior and idealised throats (a zero-length shell, a flat tube);
  - the tube case has only the asymptotic margin;
  - a curved-exterior, smooth-throat computation with back-reaction could still find a narrow escape band;
  - this is why the verdict is weakened rather than refuted.
