# Verdict on C6 (refuter 0, angle: physics)
claim: Even granting the self-consistent achronal ANEC, the shortcut ban is narrower than stated: it forbids only shortcuts threaded by a complete achronal throat geodesic, so misaligned one-sided shortcuts escape it.
status: final
verdict: weakened
basis: calculation
narrowed: Even granting the conjecture, no general theorem yet extends the ban to multiply connected spacetimes, but misalignment is not itself an escape: once the mouths are smooth and of finite size, every misaligned one-sided shortcut whose time advance d − L exceeds a mouth-scale margin δ (at most 1.40 b0/c for thin Ellis-type mouths, ≈ π²b0²/(2Lc) for long throats) is threaded by a complete achronal throat null geodesic carrying negative ANEC, so only marginal shortcuts inside that window remain unbanned.

## Strongest attack
C6's "misaligned shortcuts escape" rests entirely on point-mouth toy models (M-IDEALIZER-02, M-DECOMPOSER-08). In those, the gluing map O carries an incoming direction n rigidly to an outgoing O n. A complete through-ray then has to satisfy O n = n, or the exterior beats it at large affine distance. That rigidity comes from the singular point mouth. A smooth mouth of finite radius b0 lenses: a through-ray with impact parameter b < b0 winds by Δφ(b) = 2(b/b0)K(b/b0) (geometric units, Ellis flare). Δφ runs continuously from 0 at b = 0 to ∞ as b → b0. So for any gluing orientation and any incoming direction e, some through-ray leaves B parallel to e: the winding needed is the angle between e and O⁻¹e, and that is never more than π. The only cost is an extra transit time τ(b) − L, bounded by δ = τ(b_π) − L.

The asymptotic advance of such a line over the exterior route is adv(e) = e·D − τ(b_req(e)). If adv > 0, the limit-curve argument applies. Take the generators of ∂J⁺(A − S e) that reach B + S e. Each is achronal and crosses the throat, and as S → ∞ they converge to an inextendible achronal null geodesic through the throat: a null line. It is the globally fastest route, not just the fastest within one homotopy class, so Graham–Olum's simple-connectedness step is never needed.

I evaluated adv for every counterexample the candidate cites (10° and 180° rotations about an axis perpendicular to D, mirror O = −I, L = 0, 0.5d and 0.9d, d = 20 to 1000 b0). Every case has an achronal through-line. The decomposer's own example (L = 0.5d, rotated 10° or 180°) has advance +49.9 b0 at d = 100 b0, where the point-mouth model gave "every throat-crossing null geodesic chronal, best margin −0.5d" (M-DECOMPOSER-08). The line carries strictly negative ANEC, so the conjecture forbids it. Misalignment escapes only when d − L < δ(L), a window of at most 1.40 b0, and much smaller for long throats.

It is closed whenever adv > 0, given global hyperbolicity (a static wormhole with a complete optical metric) and the standard limit-curve lemma.
- Internal check against the point-mouth results. Setting b0 → 0 sends δ → 0 but also removes the lensing family: Δφ is pinned at 0, so the line exists only when O e = e. The point-mouth "escape" therefore exists only at b0 = 0, where the smooth Ellis-type mouth has degenerated to a singular one, and it does not survive for any finite smooth mouth with d − L > δ.

## In principle vs. in practice
- **In principle.** In a smooth finite-mouth model, the misalignment loophole shrinks from "every misaligned static shortcut with d·max|e·n̂| < L < d" (M-DECOMPOSER-08) to marginal shortcuts with d − L < δ ≤ 1.40 b0, for any orientation. Aligned mouths have no window at this resolution. Two gaps remain open.
  - No general theorem covers arbitrary curved exteriors. Exterior mass shifts the threshold by O(M), which is O(b0) for near-extremal mouths.
  - C6's sub-hypothesis (b), polyhedral face rays with ANEC exactly 0, is untouched by this attack. Those rays are achronal but do not violate the conjecture, so they are a different escape from the misalignment claim in the heading.
- **In practice.** Nothing changes. No self-consistent short one-sided solution exists (C6's own "evidence against"). The residual window is 2.2×10⁻⁹ of d for MM-scale mouths 1 ly apart, and a shortcut that marginal is indistinguishable from the mouth-size ambiguity in defining "near the mouth": δ(0) = 1.40 b0 against the 2 b0 difference between centre and facing-surface separations.

## Corrections to details
- The candidate's own counterexample does not escape once the mouths are smooth: "a static throat with L = 0.5d and mouths rotated by 10° or 180° is a shortcut … yet every throat-crossing null geodesic is chronal". With finite mouths, a through-line with advance ≈ +49.9 b0 exists at d = 100 b0.
- The same holds for "Mirror gluing (O = −I) gives no achronal complete through-ray even at L = 0". The ray at b = 0.793 b0 winds by π and is achronal, with advance d − 1.40 b0.
- The prediction "finite mouths narrow the loophole by O(a/d) but do not close it" understates the effect. Finite mouths make the loophole independent of orientation and confine it to an absolute window δ ≤ 1.40 b0 in d − L. The relative window vanishes as b0/d → 0 for short throats and as b0²/(L d) for long ones.
- The "near-grazing rays … about d/ε²" route in the decisive test is not needed. The restoring rays are deep through-rays (b_π = 0.79 b0 at L = 0; 0.03 b0 at L = 100 b0).
- The Graham–Olum quote is accurate, but the paper's own reason for simple connectedness is a long-throat counterexample (p. 5–6). It does not suggest a short one-sided escape.

## What survives and remaining doubt
- **What survives.** The logical point survives: the conjecture bans a shortcut only through a complete achronal null geodesic with negative ANEC, and no published theorem derives such a geodesic for multiply connected spacetimes in general. A thin window of marginal shortcuts (d − L ≲ δ, mouth-scale) also survives, along with the separate ANEC = 0 polyhedral route.
- **Doubts about my model.**
  - Ellis flares are inserted into one flat exterior. Their 1/r² tails and mutual influence shift times by O(b0²/d).
  - The model has no back-reaction and no exterior mass.
  - Existence of the line rests on the standard limit-curve lemma (achronality is closed under limits in strongly causal spacetimes), not on an explicit pairwise check of every pair of points on the specific ray.
  - The window δ is computed for one smooth flare family. Other profiles change its O(1) coefficient, not its mouth-scale size.
- **Verdict.** Because the general curved-exterior theorem is still missing, I narrow rather than refute. The heading's "misaligned one-sided shortcuts escape it" fails as stated for any smooth finite-mouth geometry outside the δ window.
