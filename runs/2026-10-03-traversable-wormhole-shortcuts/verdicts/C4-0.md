# Verdict on C4 (refuter 0, angle: physics)
claim: Classical exotic-matter wormholes (Morris-Thorne/Ellis, Visser thin-shell and polyhedral) give shortcuts of any designed T_thru/T_ext in principle, given labelled phantom or modified-gravity matter.
status: final
verdict: survives
basis: calculation
narrowed: none

Units: SI unless marked; "geometric" means G = c = 1.

## Strongest attack
The strongest physics attack on "in principle" is dynamical. The one concrete Lagrangian matter model that C4's sub-hypothesis (a) names, a massless phantom (ghost) scalar supporting the Ellis throat, is linearly unstable. It has exactly one growing mode with e-folding time τ_u = 0.846 r_throat/c (González, Guzmán & Sarbach 2009, Table I, γ₁ = 0). A static geometry that is torn apart in about a microsecond would not be a usable shortcut. So the question is whether the instability is fast enough to close the throat before a crossing ends. I computed this for signals and for payloads [calc: runs/2026-10-03-traversable-wormhole-shortcuts/calc/falsifier-C4-0_ghost_lifetime.py].

**Result: the attack fails against the core claim and succeeds against one supporting clause.**

- **Signals.** In a b₀ = 1 m Ellis throat, τ_u = 2.82×10⁻⁹ s.
  - A light signal crosses the flare region |l| < 3b₀ in 2.0×10⁻⁸ s, which is 7.1 e-folds.
  - It goes from the 10 m observer at one mouth to the 10 m observer at the other in 6.64×10⁻⁸ s, which is 23.5 e-folds.
  - Even when the instability is seeded at the quantum floor (δ ~ l_P/b₀ = 1.6×10⁻³⁵), the throat survives about 80 e-folds, 2.26×10⁻⁷ s or 68 b₀/c.
  - Seeded by a 70 kg payload's own field (δ = Gm/(c²b₀) = 5.2×10⁻²⁶), it survives 58 e-folds, 1.64×10⁻⁷ s.
  - A 1 eV photon seed gives 145 e-folds.
  - So the throat outlives a signal or relativistic crossing by a factor of 2 to 10.
- **The ratio.** T_thru/T_ext is set by b₀ through T_thru and by d through T_ext, so it can still be designed freely: 2.03×10⁻² at 1 km, 1.33×10⁻¹⁰ at 1 AU and 2.10×10⁻¹⁵ at 1 ly, reproducing M-EXAMINER-02.
- **Slow payloads.** C4's sub-hypothesis (a) has a 10 km/s crossing of a b₀ = 505 m or 4.5 km phantom-scalar throat lasting 0.16 s or 1.4 s. That crossing spans 1.11×10⁵ e-folds of the unstable mode. The quantum-seeded lifetime is only 1.23×10⁻⁴ s (505 m) or 1.13×10⁻³ s (4.5 km), and the 70 kg self-seeded lifetime is 9.2×10⁻⁵ s or 8.5×10⁻⁴ s. The crossing is about 1.3×10³ times longer than the throat lives.
- **Minimum speed.** The condition πb₀/v < T·ln(1/δ)·b₀/c gives v_min = π/(T ln(1/δ)) c = 0.032c to 0.067c, nearly independent of b₀.
- **A human through a ghost-scalar throat.** Combined self-consistently with the transverse-tide bound b₀ ≥ γv√(ξ/a), at 1 g over 2 m, the human needs v ≈ 0.050c and b₀ ≈ 6.8×10⁶ m. That is |M| ~ b₀c²/G ≈ 9.2×10³³ kg ≈ 4.6×10³ M_☉ of negative mass, not 3.1 M_☉.

**Why this does not reach the core.**
1. *The core claim is about shortcuts.* The brief defines a shortcut as T_thru < T_ext for "a signal or payload", and a signal (one quantum) suffices. Signals and payloads at ≳ 0.05c pass well inside the instability time. So shortcuts of any designed ratio exist even in the least stable labelled model.
2. *The claim's matter clause is "labelled phantom or modified-gravity matter", not "a phantom scalar".* With a labelled phantom perfect fluid plus a radial field, the same Ellis metric has been shown to be linearly stable. Bronnikov, Lipatova, Novikov & Shatskiy 2013 (quoted below) find that, "for a certain class of fluid equations of state", the model is "linearly stable under both spherically symmetric perturbations and axial perturbations of arbitrary multipolarity".
3. *Thin shells can be stabilised by choosing their equation of state* (Poisson–Visser region, M-CONSTRAINTS-06 verified), and an exotic equation of state is part of the labelled extension.
4. *GR "solved in reverse" puts no lower bound on T_thru/T_ext.* T_thru is fixed by the throat and T_ext by the mouth separation d.

Two other physics attacks were checked and do not apply.
- **Gao–Wald time delay and topological censorship.** Both assume the NEC or ANEC, and phantom matter violates them by hypothesis. The quantum inequalities and the self-consistent achronal ANEC constrain quantum fields, not the classical labelled phantom matter the claim stipulates. The candidate itself lists these as practical and admissibility costs, not in-principle bars.
- **No math check refutes a number C4 relies on.** The refuted items are M-DECOMPOSER-08 and -09, M-MECHANIST-05 and -06, and M-IDEALIZER-07. M-DECOMPOSER-08 concerns achronality of the throat geodesic in misaligned one-sided shortcuts, which if anything widens C4's room.

## In principle vs. in practice
- **In principle (GR plus labelled phantom or modified-gravity matter): survives.**
  - Any T_thru/T_ext down to ~10⁻¹⁵ at 1 ly, and lower for larger d, is reachable by geometry choice.
  - Signals cross well inside even the fastest known instability time.
  - Stable labelled phantom-fluid supports exist for the Ellis metric at linear order.
  - The idealisation that remains is the one-sided embedding (M-DECOMPOSER-05). The computed metrics are two-sided, and the two-mouth, one-ambient-space version is an approximate construction, not an exact solution. Since "solve in reverse" lets the labelled matter absorb the mismatch, this is not a refutation.
- **In practice (about 100 yr): not viable.**
  - Negative mass of order b₀c²/G ≈ 1.35×10²⁷ kg per metre of throat is needed.
  - If the support is a ghost scalar, a human crossing needs about 4.6×10³ M_☉ of negative mass and v ≈ 0.05c.
  - The 38 to 57 order gap to demonstrated negative energy (M-ENGINEER-02 to -04) stands.
  - No phantom matter has been observed.

## Corrections to details
1. **Sub-hypothesis (a): a phantom scalar does not support a slow (10 km/s) crossing.**
   - The 0.16 s and 1.4 s crossings last 1.1×10⁵ e-folds of the unstable mode, about 10³ times the quantum-seeded lifetime of the 505 m and 4.5 km throats.
   - With a ghost scalar the crossing must be ≳ 0.03c to 0.07c. A human then needs b₀ ≈ 6.8×10⁶ m and about 4.6×10³ M_☉ of negative mass, not 0.34 or 3.1 M_☉.
   - The 0.34 and 3.1 M_☉, 10 km/s figures hold only for a stabilised phantom source, such as the Bronnikov et al. 2013 fluid plus radial field, or a thin shell with a designed equation of state.
2. **The C4 argument and the "if not" prediction lean on [D-13] ("ghost throats collapse … under a weak positive pulse").** This bars a slow payload in the scalar model only. It does not bar signals or fast payloads. On the timescale given in González et al.'s own abstract, the throat survives 50 to 120 b₀/c after seeding.
3. **"Modified-gravity matter" should not be cited as a stabilising route without qualification.** The one widely cited stable EdGB wormhole claim was disputed by Cuyubamba, Konoplya & Zhidenko 2018. Existence in principle is unaffected; stability in that sector is not established.

## What survives and remaining doubt
**The core claim survives.** Granting labelled classical phantom matter (or an exotic thin-shell equation of state), GR admits one-sided wormhole geometries whose throat transit beats the exterior light-travel time by any designed factor. Signals pass well inside even the ghost-scalar instability time, and linearly stable phantom-fluid Ellis models exist.

Remaining doubts:
- **Stability is linear only.** Bronnikov et al. cover spherical and axial modes; polar modes are only "predicted". Nobody has evolved a two-mouth, one-ambient-space configuration.
- **The labelled extension may itself be inconsistent.** A quantised ghost has an unstable vacuum. Those are costs of the label, and the claim stipulates the label.
- **The claim is close to tautological.** Its strength comes from "given labelled phantom matter". It says nothing about admissible (quantum) sources, which the quantum inequalities confine to Planck-thin bands (M-CONSTRAINTS-07, -08).
