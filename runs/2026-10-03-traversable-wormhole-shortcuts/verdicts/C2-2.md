narrowed: Long 4D wormholes held open by quantum-field Casimir energy on chronal null lines (MMP, FGM 2019, MM) are traversable in principle but never shortcuts; with Standard-Model fields they carry particle-level signals only: from about 10 up to about 10¹³ massive quanta in flight per transit, a total energy in flight of 4.5 MeV to about 3.6 J, but never anything near a 1 kg payload.

## Strongest attack
The scale attack hits the last clause, "Standard-Model fields pass only qubit-scale signals". The argument puts it as "only single quanta below about 10⁸ eV pass". That clause is wrong in kind and wrong in size.

1. **The binding energy is a budget for many quanta, not a cap on one.** MMP say so themselves: the binding energy "is larger than the gap by a factor of q". From the verified energy function (M-ENGINEER-01), |E_min|/(ħc/ℓ) = Nq/16 exactly. That gives:
   - 2.6×10¹³ (g = 0.06, N = 1);
   - 1.3×10¹⁴ (g = e, N = 1);
   - 1.4×10¹⁵ (g = 0.06, N = 54).

   These are gap-scale quanta that can sit in the throat at once, carried on q×N ≈ 4×10¹⁴ to 2×10¹⁶ parallel lowest-Landau-level channels.
2. **The conservative count still gives many quanta per transit.** Suppose every signal quantum must leave the configuration as a real electron, so E ≥ m_e c². Even then the budget holds 8.8, 223 or 2.6×10⁴ electrons in flight per transit, in the three readings above.
3. **The channel can be reused.** MMP say the waves "come back out the other black hole after a time of order ℓ". Reusing the channel every πℓ/c gives at least 7×10⁸, 9×10¹⁰ or 1×10¹⁴ electrons per second. At one bit per electron that is a sustained classical or quantum channel, not one qubit.
4. **C2's figures sit at the edge of MMP's validity window.** The Standard-Model numbers in C2 all use r_e = 1/TeV. MMP's Standard-Model embedding needs r_e ≪ d < 1/TeV (MMP p. 27), so r_e = 1/TeV is the boundary case, not a typical one. Since |E_min| ∝ 1/r_e at fixed g, smaller wormholes inside the window bind harder:
   - 1.1 GeV at r_e = 2×10⁻²⁰ m (g = e);
   - 2.3×10¹¹ eV at 10⁻²² m;
   - 2.3×10¹⁹ eV (3.6 J) at 10⁻³⁰ m, where q is still about 10⁴ ≫ 1 and ℓ ≫ d.

   So the per-transit budget runs from about 10⁸ eV up to about 10¹⁹ eV, with up to 4×10¹³ electrons in flight. "Below about 10⁸ eV" is the weakest-binding corner, not a cap.

What does survive: even the hardest-binding Standard-Model case is still 1 kg c²/|E_min| ≈ 2.5×10¹⁶ short of a 1 kg payload, and 4.9×10²⁷ short at the r_e = 1/TeV corner. So "signals, never payloads" holds. "Qubit-scale" does not.

I found no scale attack that defeats "traversable in principle" or "never shortcuts". See below.

## In principle vs. in practice
- **In principle.** C2's "traversable in principle" stands.
  - The MMP energy minimum has been reproduced three times (M-CONSTRAINTS-12, M-ENGINEER-01, M-DIALECTICIAN-06).
  - MMP state the wormhole is "traversable for waves".
  - The Standard-Model version needs magnetic flux q ≈ 10³ to 10¹⁵ Dirac units, which is a labelled extension because no magnetic charge has been seen.
  - It also needs an environment colder than about 10 mK at the r_e = 1/TeV corner.
  - Nothing at scale rules it out. The capacity is particle-level signals at up to about 10¹⁴ s⁻¹, never kilograms. MM 2020 (speculative RS II plus a dark U(1)) is the only route to a human payload.
- **In practice.** No. These gaps all come from verified lens numbers:
  - two Standard-Model mouths cost 4.8×10²⁵ J of mass-energy, about 8×10⁴ years of world primary energy (M-ENGINEER-07);
  - the horizon field is 1.8×10³⁷ T against 1200 T demonstrated, 34 orders (M-ENGINEER-06/09);
  - zero monopoles have been demonstrated, against 4×10¹⁴ flux units needed (M-ENGINEER-05);
  - the Schwinger-form pair-creation exponent at 1200 T is about 7×10⁶⁶ (M-ENGINEER-09).

  Even a working Standard-Model wormhole would join points less than 1/TeV ≈ 2×10⁻¹⁹ m apart (MMP p. 27). It is a scattering object with a nanosecond-scale delay, not a link between distant places.

## Corrections to details
- **"Only single quanta below about 10⁸ eV pass."** Wrong. The budget is total energy in flight, and it exceeds the gap quantum by Nq/16 ≈ 10¹³ to 10¹⁵, as MMP state ("larger than the gap by a factor of q"). Counting only exiting electrons, it is about 9 to 2.6×10⁴ per transit at r_e = 1/TeV, and up to about 4×10¹³ inside the window.
- **r_e = 1/TeV lies outside MMP's window.** MMP need r_e ≪ d < 1/TeV. The Standard-Model figures (q, ℓ, |E_min| = 4.5 MeV to 13 GeV, T < 0.3 to 17 mK, 2.7×10⁸ kg mouths) are therefore the boundary case. Admissible smaller wormholes have larger |E_min| ∝ 1/r_e, shorter ℓ ∝ r_e², lighter mouths, and warmer allowed environments.
- **"Throat length 2.1 cm to 1.1 m" and "external transit 2.4 ns".**
  - ℓ is the AdS₂ parameter that sets the transit time πℓ/c, measured in exterior time. It is not a proper spatial length. C2 itself makes this point for MM, under "long is temporal".
  - The 2.4 ns figure is the time through the throat, not an exterior transit time. The exterior distance is d < 2×10⁻¹⁹ m, so T_thru/T_ext ≳ 10¹⁸.
- **Mouth motion (scale check on "never shortcuts").** At r_e = 1/TeV the clock offset needed is at most πℓ/c, 0.2 to 12 ns. One mouth moving at 1 km/s for 40 to 2100 s would build that up (calc section 3).
  - However, MMP require d < 1/TeV between equal-mass mouths. A sustained velocity difference therefore cannot be maintained without leaving the construction's validity window.
  - So cost does not protect "never shortcuts" for MMP, and neither does it threaten it. The question is dynamical, as C2's own evidence-against already says (M-DIALECTICIAN-07, M-MECHANIST-06).

## What survives and remaining doubt
- **What survives.**
  - "Traversable in principle": the MMP and FGM 2019 rows, and MM with its labelled speculative sector.
  - "Never shortcuts": T_thru ≫ T_ext for MMP (by ≳ 10¹⁸ in the Standard-Model case), d + logs for FGM 2019, and π for MM at d = ℓ.
  - "No payload with Standard-Model fields": 1 kg is 2.5×10¹⁶ to 1.3×10²⁹ times |E_min|.
- **What does not survive.** "Only qubit-scale" / "single quanta" understates the signal capacity by a factor of about 10 to 10¹³ quanta per transit, and ignores repeated use.
- **Remaining doubts.**
  - The steady-throughput numbers assume that energy which has passed through leaves the throat, and that the throat relaxes on a time of about πℓ/c. MMP's p. 19 statement covers single passage, not sustained flux. MM warn that matter which "loses energy accumulates".
  - Electron exit assumes a broken-phase exterior beyond d.
  - Stability rests on preprints (F-01).
  - If sustained flux turns out to destabilise the throat, the per-transit figures (about 10 to 4×10¹³ quanta) still beat "single quanta".
