## Strongest attack
The physics attack targets the last clause of the heading, "Standard-Model fields pass only qubit-scale signals". The argument behind it in candidates.md is "|E_min| = 4.5 MeV to 13 GeV ... So only single quanta below about 10⁸ eV pass." That inference is wrong. The back-reaction limit caps the **total** energy in the throat at one time, not the number of quanta. MMP give the ratio themselves:

- MMP (arXiv:1807.04726 p. 19, fetch_text): "the throat energy gap is (5.21), Egap∼ 1/(q2lp). However, if we send too much energy into the throat we run the risk of making a near extremal black hole. This happens when we send an energy that is larger than the binding energy of the throat (5.31) which is of order 1 /(qlp). While this is larger than the gap by a factor of q, it is still a relatively small energy."
- The same page: "We can say that the wormhole is traversable for waves, but not for very small and energetic objects."

I checked this exactly from MMP eq. 5.31, the form the lenses verified (M-CONSTRAINTS-12, M-ENGINEER-01). In natural units, |E_min|·ℓ = qN/16. So the throat can hold about q/16 quanta at the gap scale ħc/ℓ, and each can carry at least one qubit. For the lenses' SM readings that is 2.6×10¹³ (engineer, N = 1), 1.3×10¹⁴ (constraints) and 1.4×10¹⁵ (engineer, N = 54) quanta per transit. Taking E_q = 2πħc/ℓ instead gives 4.1×10¹² to 2.2×10¹⁴. A chiral 2D fermion gas with c = q/2 at E = |E_min| has entropy √6·πq/24 = 0.32q nats, or 1.9×10¹⁴ to 1.4×10¹⁵ bits. The attack has a weak point, which I checked. A sender in our broken-phase vacuum cannot launch gap-scale (about 10⁻⁶ eV) charged quanta, because every charged SM fermion is massive outside the region where electroweak symmetry is restored. Low-frequency neutral waves are mostly reflected (Q-51). The conservative carrier is therefore an electron, costing at least m_ec² = 0.511 MeV of throat energy. Even with that floor the throat holds about 8.75 electrons (engineer, N = 1), 229 (constraints) or 2.55×10⁴ (engineer, N = 54) at once. Each electron can be placed in one of about 2q lowest-Landau-level channels. That gives at most 433, 1.2×10⁴ and 1.3×10⁶ bits per transit. The transits last 2.3 to 12 ns, so the sustained rate is up to 3.6×10¹⁰, 5.1×10¹² and 5.7×10¹⁵ bit/s. The upper ends assume ideal encoding in the channel label. In every reading the capacity is many quanta, not a single qubit. It is not unlimited either. "Qubit-scale" is therefore wrong as a statement about information. It is right only as a statement about energy: everything is MeV to GeV, with no payload.

The second physics finding sharpens what "traversable" buys in the SM case. MMP put the SM mouths almost on top of each other: "the overall size of the conﬁguration, namely the distance between the two wormhole mouths, can be taken to be smaller than the electroweak scale to ensure that the fermions can be approximated as massless" (p. 5). On p. 27 they also allow "a conﬁguration where the distance d is smaller than the Compton wavelength of the electron: 1/me". So T_ext ≤ ħ/(m_ec²) = 1.29×10⁻²¹ s, against T_thru = πℓ/c = 2.3 to 12 ns. That gives T_thru/T_ext ≥ 1.7×10¹¹ to 9.3×10¹² (d = 1/m_e) and 3.4×10¹⁷ to 1.8×10¹⁹ (d = 1/TeV) [calc: same file]. This supports "never shortcuts" overwhelmingly. It also means the SM row joins two points less than a picometre apart, not two distant points.

## In principle vs. in practice
- **In principle.**
  - *Not a shortcut.* This survives for every static configuration on record. MM: T_thru/T_ext = π at d = ℓ (verified M-EXAMINER-04). SM-MMP: at least 1.7×10¹¹ (this file). FGM 2019: t_min = d + logs (quoted, first order).
  - *Traversable.* This survives too: for waves and quanta (MMP p. 19) and, under MM's labelled RS II plus dark-U(1) sector, for a human.
  - *Capacity.* SM-MMP passes many-quantum signals. The bit count per transit lies between about 4×10² (electron carriers, engineer N = 1) and about 10¹⁵ (gap-scale quanta, N = 54). No payload passes: 1 kg exceeds the cap by 25.6 to 29.1 orders.
- **In practice.**
  - The candidate's practice leg stands: magnetic charge has never been observed, and there is no formation route.
  - The SM mouths sit within about 4×10⁻¹³ m of each other, so even a working SM-MMP pair is no communication link between distant points.

## Corrections to details
- "So only single quanta below about 10⁸ eV pass" is wrong. The cap is on total energy in transit, so the throat holds 8.75 to 2.55×10⁴ electrons, or up to about q/16 ≈ 10¹³ to 10¹⁵ gap-scale quanta, at once. MMP state the factor-of-q ratio themselves.
- Sub-hypothesis (a) omits the mouth separation. MMP require d below 1/TeV, or below 1/m_e = 3.86×10⁻¹³ m, so that the fermions stay massless. The SM row is therefore a sub-picometre "wormhole", with T_thru/T_ext ≥ 1.7×10¹¹.
- Calling MM "4D" needs its label. MM's working version needs an RS II braneworld (a five-dimensional bulk) plus a dark U(1). C2 does label it as speculative in (c), but the heading's "4D" covers only its effective description.
- Constraints' SM-MMP |E_min| is 117 MeV at r_e = ħc/TeV, against 112.5 MeV at r_e = 2×10⁻¹⁹ m. This is rounding only.

## What survives and remaining doubt
- **What survives.**
  - The support claim survives: quantum-field negative null energy on chronal circles, outside the reach of the flat-space QIs and the achronal-ANEC conjecture. So does "not a shortcut" in the static solutions.
  - "SM fields pass no payload" survives with a margin of 25 to 29 orders.
  - What falls is the literal "qubit-scale" or "single quanta" capacity, which understates the information capacity by 2.6 to 15 orders in bits per transit.
- **Remaining doubt.**
  - **"Never".** The heading's "never shortcuts" rests on Δ ≈ 0. With an accumulated mouth clock offset, the threshold Δ_s = T_thru − d/c makes any long wormhole a one-way shortcut (verified six times, see C7). Only 2D toy models (M-DIALECTICIAN-07, M-MECHANIST-06) suggest the MMP equilibrium disappears first. I did not count this against the core, because the toys point the candidate's way, but "never" is a dynamical claim no one has proved.
  - **"Traversable in principle".** This rests on stability evidence that is preprints only (Sadhukhan 2026, one sector, O(α); Bilotta's "unstable to large fluctuations") and on FGM 2019's exponential fragility.
  - **Electron-carrier estimate.** The count assumes unit transmission for charged fermions in the lowest partial wave (Freivogel et al. 2026, preprint, Q-51). It also assumes full use of the roughly 2q channel labels. If coupling from the broken-phase exterior is suppressed, the floor falls toward the 8.75-electron end, which is still not a single qubit.
