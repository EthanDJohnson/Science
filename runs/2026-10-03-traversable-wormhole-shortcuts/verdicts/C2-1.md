# Verdict on C2 (refuter 1, angle: evidence)
claim: Long 4D wormholes held open by quantum-field Casimir energy on chronal null lines (MMP, FGM 2019, MM) are traversable in principle but never shortcuts; Standard-Model fields pass only qubit-scale signals.
status: final
verdict: weakened
basis: cited-evidence
narrowed: Long 4D Casimir-supported wormholes (MMP, FGM 2019, MM) are traversable in principle and every published solution has T_thru > T_ext, though no 4D theorem forbids a shortcut. A Standard-Model MMP pair passes no payload. It does pass a continuous stream of low-energy quanta, capped at about |E_min| (MeV to GeV) per transit window: up to roughly 10¹³–10¹⁵ gap-scale quanta per window, not single qubits.

## Strongest attack
The last clause fails on the constructions' own papers. C2's argument says "only single quanta below about 10⁸ eV pass" (sub-hypothesis (a), taken from CONSTRAINTS F19). That reading treats the binding energy |E_min| as the energy of one quantum. MMP and MM both say the opposite.
- **The binding energy exceeds the throat's energy gap by a factor q.** MMP say so directly (quote below). So the throat can hold up to about q gap-scale quanta at once without forming a horizon.
- **MM give the capacity as a rate, not a total:** about c₂ qubits per ℓ.
- **The wormholes are eternal.** MMP is "eternally traversable" in FGM's words, so the total information that can pass grows without limit over time.

Using the run's own SM-MMP rows:
- **Gap-scale quanta per window:** 2.5×10¹³ to 1.4×10¹⁵.
- **Using only the lightest charged SM fermion that can enter from the exterior** (the electron, 0.511 MeV), each window still carries 9 to 2.5×10⁴ quanta. At the 2.4–11.5 ns transit time that is about 10⁹–10¹⁴ electrons/s.

Neither figure is "qubit-scale" in the sense the candidate's argument uses ("single quanta").

The "never shortcuts" leg survives the literature check, but in a narrower form than "never". FGM, the authors of one of C2's three examples, write that in 4D the Shapiro-delay infrared divergence makes it "difficult ... to rigorously bound wormhole transit times". They place the bound t_min ≥ d only in D ≥ 5. So "never shortcuts" in 4D rests on the computed solutions (MMP Fig. 7; MM's "π𝓁 > d"; FGM's t_min = d + logs), plus the GSL argument the authors invoke. It is not a 4D theorem.

## Evidence

However, if we send too much energy into the throat we run the risk of making a near extremal black hole. This happens when we send an energy that is larger than the binding energy of the throat (5.31) which is of order 1 /(qlp). While this is larger than the gap by a factor of q, it is still a relatively small energy." The same page adds: "We can say that the wormhole is traversable for waves". It names "the charged fermion ﬁelds themselves" as the probes.

The constraint that the energy is smaller than|Ebin| in (2.13) implies that the total number of quanta cannot exceed a number ∼ c2. ... However, in our case we can talk about the information per unit time (namely of the order of ∼ c2 qubits per 𝓁), rather than total transferable amount." The same page: "The binding energy (2.13) is larger by a factor of c2" than the gap.

## In principle vs. in practice
- **In principle.**
  - *Traversable, not a shortcut.* The literature supports both points: MMP's abstract calls it "a long wormhole that does not lead to causality violations in the ambient space"; MM give "π𝓁 > d"; FGM give t_min = d + logs. MM add (footnote, p. 2) "This also implies that they can not be converted into time machines [4]". That is the authors' assertion, citing Morris–Thorne–Yurtsever, not a theorem.
  - *The 4D caveat.* In 4D the no-shortcut status is a property of each computed solution, not a theorem-backed bound (FGM fn. 8).
  - *Mouth motion.* Clock offsets from mouth motion (C7) remain an untested route. C2's own evidence-against reports that the equilibrium is lost before the shortcut threshold in a 2D toy, which supports "never" only in that toy.
- **In practice.**
  - *SM-MMP.* It needs macroscopic magnetic charge that has never been observed [D-25]. Its capacity is a stream of sub-|E_min| quanta, not "qubit-scale".
  - *MM.* It needs a labelled speculative sector. For MM, too, the capacity is a rate, of order c₂ qubits per ℓ.
  - *Payload verdict.* No 1 kg payload in any SM row: |E_bin|/(1 kg·c²) = 2×10⁻²⁸ [CONSTRAINTS F19]. On this the candidate is right.

## Corrections to details
- **"Only single quanta below about 10⁸ eV pass" (sub-hypothesis (a)) is wrong.** |E_min| is the cap on the total energy in the throat per window, not on one quantum's energy. Per MMP p. 19 and MM p. 7 the cap admits about q (or c₂) gap-scale quanta, and the channel is a continuous rate because MMP is eternal.
- **FGM's no-shortcut status in 4D is computed, not bounded.** "Approaching 1 from above but not crossing it" (Q-13, as used in C1/C2) overstates what FGM claim for 4D. They say the bound holds in D ≥ 5, and that rigorous 4D transit bounds are hard to obtain (fn. 8). The sign of t_min − T_ext at sub-leading (log) order, with T_ext including the exterior Shapiro delay, is not stated in what I read.
- **Graham–Olum's long-wormhole remark is used correctly.** C2 cites it for chronal fastest paths through long wormholes, which is what it shows; it does not prove "never" for one-sided 4D constructions.
- **A wording trap for anyone citing MMP's lifetime.** MMP p. 24 reads "the total lifetime is ﬁnite Tlifetime∝ d3 q2l2 p (6.54) which is small compared to the time to go through the wormhole". Taken literally, the orbit-supported pair would decay before a signal crossed. With the d ≳ q² l_p range quoted on p. 23 and πℓ ∼ q² l_p, a lifetime ∝ d³/(q² l_p²) instead comes out ≳ q⁴ l_p ≫ πℓ. So the sentence looks like a slip, and I do not use it as an attack. The extracted formula is garbled, so this reading is unconfirmed.

## What survives and remaining doubt
- **What survives.** Long, chronal, Casimir-supported 4D wormholes (MMP, FGM 2019, MM) are traversable in principle per peer-reviewed solutions. None computes T_thru < T_ext, and the SM row passes no payload. That is the narrowed core above.
- **What is lost.** The "qubit-scale" clause is lost in its literal, single-quantum reading. It survives only as "low-energy quantum signals with total energy per transit window below |E_min|".
- **Remaining doubt.**
  - *Coupling in from far away.* Whether gap-scale (μeV) quanta can actually be injected into a 2×10⁻¹⁹ m SM mouth is unclear. The SM exterior has no massless charged fermions, and low-frequency waves are mostly reflected [D-C5, Freivogel et al. 2026, preprint]. The realistic per-window count may therefore be the electron-limited 9 to 2.5×10⁴ rather than 10¹³ or more. That is still a multi-quantum, continuous channel.
  - *Stability.* MMP's stability evidence remains preprint-level [F-01]. FGM's traversability is exponentially fragile [D-07].
  - *The 4D no-shortcut claim has no theorem behind it* (FGM fn. 8).
