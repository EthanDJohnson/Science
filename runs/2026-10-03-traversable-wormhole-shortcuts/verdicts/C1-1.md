# Verdict on C1 (refuter 1, angle: evidence)
claim: No viable option: no admissible traversable wormhole beats light through the exterior or its coupling channel, none stays open without negative null energy, and none could pass 1 kg in practice.
status: final
verdict: weakened
basis: cited-evidence
narrowed: Within established physics (4D GR, QFT on curved spacetime, semiclassical gravity), no known traversable wormhole beats light through the exterior or its coupling channel, every one needs negative null energy (pointwise NEC violation, and ANEC violation for asymptotically flat globally hyperbolic ones), and none could pass 1 kg within about 100 years; but the in-principle exclusion of all shortcuts is a conjecture-dependent expectation, and it does not extend to the brief's labelled extensions (phantom or modified-gravity sources), where shortcut geometries exist and their instability is no longer settled.

## Strongest attack

C1 is a universal "no" over "admissible" physics. The brief defines admissible physics to include labelled speculative extensions ("phantom fields", "modified gravity ... whose effective stress tensor violates the NEC"). It also defines "in principle" as "established physics plus labelled extensions, with unlimited resources". C1's in-principle legs reach that whole domain in only two places, and the literature undercuts both.

1. **Leg (i) disposes of the labelled shortcut geometries (C4) with one clause: they "are unstable".** That clause is no longer an established result.
   - Rotation stabilisation of the Ellis–Bronnikov throat (GR plus a phantom scalar, the C4 sub-hypothesis (a) geometry). Azad et al. (PRD 109, 124051, 2024) find the static unstable mode "becomes less unstable with increasing rotation, until it reaches zero and then disappears". Their PLB letter calls this "favoring a viable traversable wormhole".
   - Beyond-Horndeski scalar–tensor wormholes. These are free of ghost and gradient instabilities: Franciolini et al. (JHEP 2019), and Mironov, Rubakov & Volkova (PRD 107, 104061, 2023), who give a Lagrangian whose wormhole "complies with all stability constraints for the high energy modes".
   - Neither result is a full stability proof. The rotation result is a second-order slow-rotation expansion, and the beyond-Horndeski result leaves "slow" tachyonic modes unconstrained. Both are two-sided solutions, so using them as shortcuts needs the same one-sided idealisation C4 uses (M-DECOMPOSER-05).
   - Even so, "are unstable" is what C1 uses to remove the labelled-extension shortcuts from "admissible", and it is now an open question rather than a result. So C1's leg (i), stated over all admissible physics, rests on an open question.

2. **Leg (ii), "none stays open without negative null energy", is argued only from GR theorems.** These are [K-01] topological censorship and [K-02] Hochberg–Visser flare-out, and both assume Einstein's equations with the matter stress tensor as source. In labelled modified gravity the matter need not carry negative null energy at all.
   - Bronnikov & Galiakhmetov (Einstein–Cartan, 2015) give exact, static, asymptotically flat traversable wormholes "with an arbitrary throat radius". Their sources are two scalar fields: "Both scalar fields are canonical (that is, satisfy the weak energy condition)".
   - The NEC violation sits in the torsion (geometric) sector, not in any matter field. Read as "matter with negative null energy", leg (ii) is false under labelled Einstein–Cartan gravity. Read as "an effective total stress tensor with T_kk < 0", it holds there only by definition.
   - The one earlier counterexample of this kind that was claimed to be stable has since been withdrawn. Cuyubamba, Konoplya & Zhidenko (2018) show that the Kanti–Kleihaus–Kunz Einstein–dilaton–Gauss–Bonnet wormhole "is unstable against small perturbations for any values of its parameters". So this attack narrows leg (ii); it does not supply a stable counterexample.

3. **Even inside established physics, the in-principle no-shortcut leg is an expectation, not a result.**
   - It needs the self-consistent achronal ANEC, which is a conjecture [K-07a]. The only proof C1 cites, Rosso 2020, covers fixed backgrounds (AdS₂×S^{d−2}, dS, AdS), not self-consistent spacetimes. Rosso's abstract: "We prove the achronal averaged null energy condition for general quantum field theories in the near horizon geometry of spherical extremal black holes (i.e. AdS₂×S^{d−2}), de Sitter and anti-de Sitter."
   - It also needs a bridge from the conjecture to one-sided shortcuts. Graham–Olum's theorem assumes simple connectedness, and the run's math check **refuted** the lenses' bridge "one-sided shortcut ⇒ complete achronal throat geodesic" (M-DECOMPOSER-08): a static throat with L = 0.5d and mouths rotated by 10° or 180° is a shortcut (T_thru/T_ext = 0.5) with every throat-crossing null geodesic chronal.
   - C1 lists both points under "evidence against", but its heading still states the exclusion as fact. A refuted step supports nothing, so the in-principle Q1 leg can be asserted only as "no known construction", not as "no admissible wormhole".

The in-practice leg (iii) and the two-sided "coupling channel" clause survive every attack I found.

## Evidence

- **Azad et al. 2024, PRD 109, 124051, arXiv:2403.08387** (ACCESS: abstract, via fetch_text): "The calculations show that the unstable mode present in the static case becomes less unstable with increasing rotation, until it reaches zero and then disappears. This indicates that wormhole solutions may become linearly mode stable at sufficiently fast rotation."
- **Azad et al. 2024, PLB 848, 138349, arXiv:2301.05243** (ACCESS: abstract): "We find indications that simple wormhole solutions such as Ellis-Bronnikov in General Relativity can be stabilized by rotation, thus favoring a viable traversable wormhole."
- **Blázquez-Salcedo et al., PLB 875 (2026) 140335, arXiv:2510.11406** (ACCESS: abstract): "We show that the instability remains in the presence of charge, but exhibits a very fast decrease to zero. ... By analogy, we conjecture an analogous behavior for rotating chargeless wormholes." This tempers the Azad result: the stabilisation is still conjectural.
- **Franciolini et al., JHEP 01 (2019) 221, arXiv:1811.05481** (ACCESS: abstract): "we show that scalar-tensor theories of "beyond Horndeski" type can have wormhole solutions that are free of ghost and gradient instabilities. Such solutions are instead forbidden within the more restrictive "Horndeski" class of theories."
- **Mironov, Rubakov & Volkova, PRD 107, 104061 (2023), arXiv:2212.05969** (ACCESS: abstract): "'slow' tachyonic instabilities remain unconstrained. We give an example of beyond Horndeski Lagrangian admitting a wormhole solution which complies with all stability constraints for the high energy modes."
- **Bronnikov & Galiakhmetov, Grav. Cosmol. 21, 283 (2015), arXiv:1508.01114** (ACCESS: abstract): "A family of exact static, spherically symmetric wormhole solutions with an arbitrary throat radius, with flat or AdS asymptotic behavior, has been obtained with sources in the form of two noninteracting scalar fields with nonzero potentials. Both scalar fields are canonical (that is, satisfy the weak energy condition), one is minimally and the other nonminimally coupled to gravity, and the latter is a source of torsion."
- **Cuyubamba, Konoplya & Zhidenko, PRD 98, 044040 (2018), arXiv:1804.11170** (ACCESS: abstract), cutting the other way: "more detailed analysis of perturbations shows that the Kanti-Kleihaus-Kunz wormhole is unstable against small perturbations for any values of its parameters."
- **Rosso 2020, arXiv:2005.06476** (ACCESS: abstract), quoted under attack 3 above: a fixed-background proof.
- **Math check M-DECOMPOSER-08** (refuted; log math/decomposer/M-DECOMPOSER-08.py.log), as summarised in math/decomposer.md: "'one-sided shortcut ⇒ complete achronal null geodesic through the throat' (L6) fails when the two mouths are rotated relative to each other ... T_thru/T_ext = 0.5, so it is a shortcut. Yet every complete null geodesic through the throat is chronal, with best margin −0.5d."

## In principle vs. in practice

- **In principle, established physics only.**
  - *Q2 leg: stands.* Pointwise NEC violation at a throat and ANEC violation in asymptotically flat, globally hyperbolic spacetimes are theorems of GR [K-01, K-02]. The post-2016 constructions all use Casimir-type negative null energy [D-01, D-04 to D-08].
  - *Q1 leg: an expectation.* No known self-consistent construction is a shortcut, but the exclusion rests on the self-consistent achronal ANEC conjecture plus an unproved step to one-sided spacetimes; the lenses' version of that step was refuted for misaligned mouths (M-DECOMPOSER-08).
- **In principle, with the brief's labelled extensions.**
  - *Shortcuts.* Shortcut geometries exist (C4), and the instability C1 invokes is contested: rotation may stabilise Ellis–Bronnikov, and beyond Horndeski evades ghost and gradient instabilities.
  - *Support without negative-energy matter.* In Einstein–Cartan, WEC-satisfying canonical scalars hold the throat open; the null-energy violation is geometric.
  - So C1's "no admissible ..." overreaches here, though no fully stable, one-sided, payload-tested labelled shortcut has been shown either.
- **In practice (about 100 years): C1 stands.** None of the literature above lowers any cost.
  - Phantom or torsion-supported throats need exotic or new-physics sources of order c²/G ≈ 1.35×10²⁷ kg per metre of throat radius (SI; C4's verified number).
  - The cheapest established-physics row (SM-MMP) needs magnetic charge, which has never been observed.

## Corrections to details

- "Achronal ANEC is proved for general QFTs in AdS₂×S^{d−2}" is accurate, but the proof is for quantum fields on a fixed, exact AdS₂×S^{d−2} background. MMP/MM throats are only approximately AdS₂×S², so "the MMP/MM throat cannot carry such a violation on its own" holds to the extent that the test-field approximation holds. C1 should label it a test-field result, not a self-consistent one.
- "Shortcut geometries ... are unstable" should read "the static ghost-scalar ones have one unstable radial mode [D-13]. Rotation may remove it (Azad et al. 2024; slow-rotation expansion only), and beyond-Horndeski versions avoid ghost and gradient instabilities, with slow tachyonic modes unchecked."
- Leg (ii) should be scoped to Einstein's equations: "none stays open without negative null energy in the total (effective) stress tensor". Matter itself can satisfy the WEC in labelled Einstein–Cartan solutions.

## What survives and remaining doubt

- **What survives.** Within established physics, C1's core stands as "no known construction":
  - no known wormhole is a shortcut or beats its coupling channel;
  - none is held open without negative null energy;
  - none could pass 1 kg in practice, by 24 or more orders of magnitude on the cheapest row.
  This is the narrowed core.
- **What is lost.** The universal "no admissible" form, across the brief's labelled extensions and as an in-principle theorem.
- **Why weakened rather than refuted.** No source I found exhibits a stable, one-sided, admissible shortcut, or a self-consistent achronal-ANEC violation. The rotation stabilisation is "indications" from a perturbative expansion, the beyond-Horndeski stability is partial, and the Einstein–Cartan wormholes have no stability analysis in the abstract I read.
- **What would sharpen it.** A full (non-perturbative-in-rotation) stability analysis of rapidly rotating Ellis–Bronnikov throats would decide whether C1's instability clause can be restored for the phantom row. A one-sided embedding with a payload test would decide the shortcut question in that row.
