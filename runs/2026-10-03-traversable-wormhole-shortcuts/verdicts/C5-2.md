# Verdict on C5 (refuter 2, angle: scale)
claim: Einstein-Dirac-Maxwell fields hold an asymptotically flat traversable wormhole open without exotic matter.
status: final
verdict: weakened
basis: calculation
narrowed: At most, a semiclassical two-fermion EDM model with a hypothetical near-Planck-mass charged fermion (q/μ < 1) has static throats of about 10² to 10³ Planck lengths. None of these has been shown to stay traversable under evolution, none works with any known fermion, and none can be widened toward signal-plus-payload scale.

## Strongest attack
The scale attack does not prove that no EDM wormhole exists. It shows that every route from the published solutions to a wormhole of any useful size is blocked. Three of the blocks follow from the model's own scaling laws.

1. **q/μ < 1 is scale-invariant, and every known charged fermion misses it by 15.8 to 21.9 orders.**
   - BKR's scaling transformation divides μ and q by the same λ. The ratio q/μ (Planck units) therefore cannot be changed by resizing a solution.
   - BKR report that "all solutions constructed so far have Qe/M > 1 and q/µ < 1".
   - The electron has q/μ = 2.04×10²¹ (Gaussian) to 7.24×10²¹ (Heaviside–Lorentz). A unit-charge fermion would need m > 1.04×10¹⁸ GeV (Gaussian) to 3.70×10¹⁸ GeV (HL).
   - That is 15.8 orders above the top quark and 13.9 orders above the LHC's √s = 13.6 TeV [calc: runs/2026-10-03-traversable-wormhole-shortcuts/calc/falsifier-C5-2_edm_scale.py]. This agrees with M-ENGINEER-12 (verified).
   - So no EDM wormhole in the known parameter domain can be built from Standard-Model fields. The claim needs a labelled hypothetical particle: a charged fermion near the Planck mass whose gravity beats its electrostatics.
2. **The throat is pinned near the Planck scale, and Pauli exclusion blocks widening.**
   - BKR: Q_N → λ²Q_N when r₀ → λr₀, and the one-particle condition fixes Q_N = 1 per spinor.
   - Kain's QFT treatment states it directly: "R0/ℓP = 1/√N̄nj … solutions are only valid for a single wormhole throat radius" (eq. 64). Each mode holds one particle.
   - Scaling the published Kain throats (75.28 to 498.4 l_P = 1.2 to 8.1×10⁻³³ m, SI) up to a 2 m throat needs λ = 2.5×10³² to 1.6×10³³. That means 6×10⁶⁴ to 3×10⁶⁶ fermions per spinor mode, which Pauli forbids.
   - A proton-radius throat (10⁻¹⁵ m) still needs 1.5×10³⁴ to 6.8×10³⁵ per mode [calc: same file].
   - The only way to get many fermions is to fill many distinct modes. Andréasson & Blomqvist (2025) find that Einstein–Dirac solutions with κ fermions become "remarkably similar" to classical Einstein–Vlasov solutions "even for a comparatively small number of particles".
   - Vlasov matter satisfies the NEC identically: T_kk = ∫ f (p·k)² dvol ≥ 0 for any distribution f ≥ 0 (signature −+++).
   - Flare-out at a static throat needs NEC violation there (Hochberg–Visser, [D-10a]). So the classical many-fermion limit removes the very property that holds the throat open.
   - This last step is an inference: Andréasson–Blomqvist study solitons, not wormholes.
3. **Payload gaps, granted the solution exists** [calc: same file]:
   - a 1 kg payload's own Schwarzschild radius (1.49×10⁻²⁷ m) is 1.8×10⁵ times the largest Kain throat;
   - the payload's mass is 10⁸·¹ times the two fermions' rest mass (2μ = 8.7×10⁻⁹ kg);
   - a 2 m human is 10³²·⁴ times the throat.
4. **Cited dynamical evidence settles traversability for the branches that were tested.**
   - Kain 2023 evolved the asymmetric static solutions. In every case black holes form, and null rays through the throat are trapped.
   - Danielson et al. show the symmetric BKR solutions are not EDM solutions.
   - Weinbaum 2026 finds only partial wormholes for positive-frequency sources.
   - Neither Weinbaum's result nor Kain's is a theorem. Dzhunushaliev et al. 2026 remains unevaluated. This is why the verdict is weakened rather than refuted.

## In principle vs. in practice
- **In principle** (labelled extension: a charged fermion near the Planck mass with q/μ < 1):
  - Static two-fermion configurations with throats of 1.2 to 8.1×10⁻³³ m exist in the asymmetric branch (Kain, Table I). The one asymmetric branch that was evolved, Kain's, collapses to black holes and traps signals.
  - No configuration is known to be both two-ended with positive frequency and dynamically traversable.
  - Even granted one, Pauli and the scaling law pin the throat at 10² to 10³ l_P. A 1 kg or human payload is excluded by 5 to 32 orders.
  - With Standard-Model fermions there is no member of the known family at all: the gap is 15.8 to 21.9 orders in q/μ, and resizing cannot close it.
- **In practice** (about 100 years): no.
  - The required particle is at least 13.9 orders above collider reach. There is no known way to assemble a Planck-mass, two-particle singlet bound state around a throat.
  - The object's light-crossing time is about 4×10⁻⁴² to 3×10⁻⁴¹ s (SI).

## Corrections to details
- The candidate's range for the required fermion mass, "7×10¹⁷ to 4×10¹⁸ GeV", is correct as M-ENGINEER-12 states it. The lower end (6.95×10¹⁷ GeV) is for a charge-2/3 (top-like) fermion in Gaussian units. For a unit-charge fermion the lower end is 1.04×10¹⁸ GeV.
- "A Planck-size throat would not be traversable [D-27]" is too strong as a size argument for a *signal*:
  - a quantum whose wavelength is 2πR₀ carries only 0.5% to 3.3% of the two fermions' rest energy, so the static back-reaction of one quantum is small [calc: same file];
  - what blocks signal passage is the dynamical collapse Kain found, not the throat's size;
  - size does exclude payloads.
- The candidate counts the Dirac field as non-exotic "in the brief's classical sense". The brief defines exotic matter as classical NEC violation, and Kain's static solutions violate the NEC, as does every static throat by flare-out. The brief does list classical Dirac fields as a separate category, so this is a labelling dispute, not a refutation. But "without exotic matter" holds only under that separate category.

## What survives and remaining doubt
- What survives is the narrowed core: Planck-scale, hypothetical-particle, two-fermion static configurations whose traversability is unshown.
- Remaining doubt:
  - Weinbaum's obstruction is numerical on a restricted ansatz, and Kain's covers only the parameters he evolved.
  - Dzhunushaliev et al. 2026 (abstract only) has not been evolved.
  - A many-mode EDM wormhole that keeps NEC violation at large κ has not been ruled out by theorem. The Einstein–Vlasov limit argument above is an inference from soliton studies.
- If a positive-frequency, two-ended solution were found and evolved without forming a horizon, the in-principle core would recover. The scale gaps in q/μ and particle number would remain.
