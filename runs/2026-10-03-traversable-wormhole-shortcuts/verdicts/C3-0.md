# Verdict on C3 (refuter 0, angle: physics)
claim: Shortcuts, and hence time machines, are possible in principle because the self-consistent achronal ANEC fails: a back-reacted semiclassical spacetime can violate ANEC on a complete achronal throat geodesic.
status: final
verdict: weakened
basis: calculation
narrowed: The self-consistent achronal ANEC is an unproven conjecture, so a short (shortcut) throat is not excluded by any theorem; but no back-reacted violation is known, and on single-scale semiclassical estimates any violation large enough to hold a short throat open sits at or below the semiclassical cutoff l_P√N, so "possible in principle" holds only as "not ruled out", and the step to time machines still meets chronology-protection results that do not rest on the conjecture.

## Strongest attack

The core claim is an existence claim: the conjecture *fails*, because a back-reacted semiclassical spacetime *can* carry negative ANEC on a complete achronal throat geodesic. Three attacks, in order of strength.

1. **Self-consistency calculation: a short throat held open by quantum fields sits at the semiclassical cutoff.** [calc: runs/2026-10-03-traversable-wormhole-shortcuts/calc/falsifier-C3-0_selfconsistency.py]
   - *Required.* For an Ellis-type short throat, r = √(l² + b₀²), the exact radial requirement is 8π∫T_kk dλ = −πE/b₀, so ∫T_kk dλ = −E/(8b₀) (geometric, G = c = 1). This was recomputed symbolically, and numerically (∫(r′/r)²dl = 1.570796326795 = π/2 at b₀ = 1 m). It agrees with M-CONSTRAINTS-01/02.
   - *Available.* N quantum fields whose state varies only on the curvature scale R give |G⟨T_kk⟩| ≲ αN l_P²/R⁴ (geometric), over an affine extent ηR.
   - *Self-consistency.* G = 8π⟨T⟩ then needs 8παηN l_P²/R³ ≥ π/R, that is R ≤ √(8αηN) l_P.
   - *Result.* Measured against the species length l_sp = √N l_P, below which semiclassical gravity with N species is not controlled: R_max/l_sp = 0.017 (α = 1/(2880π²), η = 1) up to 1.05 (α = π²/720, the electromagnetic plate Casimir coefficient, with η = 10, deliberately generous).
   - *What follows.* For every N, a self-consistent single-scale short throat lies at or below the scale where the semiclassical framework fails. That framework is the one the conjecture, and C3's claim, are stated in.
   - *Examples.* N = 1 gives R_max = 1.05 l_P = 1.7×10⁻³⁵ m. A 1 m throat needs N ≥ 3.5×10⁶⁹ species, and then l_sp = 0.955 m ≈ R.
2. **The cited violations are, by their authors' own account, not self-consistent.** C3's lead evidence is Urban–Olum 2010. Its own text says its examples were computed "without attempting to impose self-consistency" and that "self-consistency may be enough to enforce the energy conditions". Its "as large as desired" is a statement about the quantum state on a fixed conformally flat background, not about a solution of G = 8π⟨T⟩.
   - Flanagan–Wald 1996 imposed the semiclassical equation perturbatively, to second order about flat space. They found the transverse-averaged ANEC strictly positive, and concluded that semiclassical wormholes "cannot be macroscopic but must be 'Planck scale'". That is the same conclusion as attack 1, reached independently.
   - Ishibashi–Maeda–Mefford compute a CFT on a fixed boundary metric: in AdS/CFT the boundary metric is a non-dynamical source, so this is again a background, not back-reacted, violation. Their boundary is also "causally proper", so the violation gives no shortcut.
   - Weinbaum's Dirac violation [D-12] is on fixed backgrounds, and the same author finds no self-consistent two-ended solution.
   - So none of C3's evidence instantiates the claim's key clause, "a back-reacted semiclassical spacetime".
3. **The two "hence" links are non sequiturs (internal inconsistency).**
   - *Failure does not imply a shortcut.* A violation somewhere does not support a throat. Attack 1 shows that the violation must reach about 1/R in magnitude at the throat, not merely be negative. Ishibashi et al. show a violation with no shortcut at all.
   - *The ban does not cover every shortcut.* M-DECOMPOSER-08, refuted in math/decomposer.md, shows that a one-sided shortcut need not carry a complete achronal throat geodesic. The conjecture's failure is therefore neither necessary nor sufficient for a shortcut. The word "because" in the claim overstates the logical role of the conjecture.
   - *Shortcut does not imply time machine.* A failed conjecture removes only Graham–Olum's Theorem 3 ban. The Kay–Radzikowski–Wald result is PROVED within its assumptions (linear QFT, compactly generated Cauchy horizon, F-locality) [K-12], and does not use the achronal ANEC. So "hence time machines" does not follow from the conjecture's failure; at most chronology protection is left at conjecture status (C7's sub-hypothesis ii).

## In principle vs. in practice
- **In principle.** Not refuted, because the conjecture is unproven and nothing here proves it.
  - *What the calculation covers.* The scaling assumes a single curvature scale R along the geodesic and states with no structure below R. Those are the standard Ford–Roman and Flanagan–Wald regimes.
  - *The escape route.* A hierarchical construction (MMP-type: r_e ≪ ℓ) evades the single-scale estimate. But every such construction on record is long, with chronal throat geodesics (M-CONSTRAINTS-13, M-DECOMPOSER-10 verified), so it supplies no achronal violation.
  - *What remains open.* Whether a hierarchical short throat exists is exactly what C3 would need, and nobody has exhibited one.
  - *What follows.* The in-principle claim survives only as "not ruled out", not as "possible because it fails".
- **In practice.** Nil.
  - The self-consistent throat sits at R ≲ l_P√N. At N = 1 that is 1.7×10⁻³⁵ m; a 1 m throat needs 3.5×10⁶⁹ field species, against MM's own estimate of at most about 10³² species with a TeV cutoff [Q-03].
  - At R ≈ l_sp, a signal quantum that fits needs E ≥ ħc/R. For N = 1 that is 2×10⁹ J, comparable to the throat's own mass-energy (E_sig/(Mc²) = 1/N = 1).

## Corrections to details
- *Urban–Olum is now checked.* It is cited as evidence that the conjecture "fails". Its full text (now checked, closing RC-03 for this point) disclaims self-consistency, so it supports only "the non-self-consistent version fails". The candidate's own "evidence against" concedes this, but its heading claim does not.
- *Positive-frequency Dirac fields [D-12]* are fixed-background violations. The same source finds no self-consistent two-ended solution, so they cut against sub-hypothesis (a), not for it.
- *Sub-hypothesis (b), the FGM 2018 quotient.* M-CONSTRAINTS-17 verifies only the algebra, and the comparison path (half the boundary circle) is the lens's own modelling choice. FGM 2018 is an AdS₃ quotient, outside 4D asymptotically flat physics. It is not evidence of a back-reacted achronal violation in our setting.
- *"Hence time machines."* This needs the further failure of chronology protection, for which Kay–Radzikowski–Wald is a proved obstruction within its assumptions [K-12].

## What survives and remaining doubt
- **What survives.** The self-consistent achronal ANEC is a conjecture, and nothing proves it in curved 4D spacetime with dynamical gravity (Wall's GSL route fails once gravitons are quantised [K-07c]). So a shortcut is not excluded by theorem.
- **What does not.** The positive claim that the conjecture *fails*, and that this makes shortcuts and time machines possible, has no instance. Its cited sources disclaim self-consistency. A generic semiclassical estimate, and the perturbative result of Flanagan–Wald, push any self-consistent throat-supporting violation to the semiclassical cutoff.
- **Remaining doubt.**
  - The scaling bound is an order-of-magnitude estimate, not a theorem. α is state-dependent: Urban–Olum's states evade simple bounds; the absence of a null-geodesic QEI in 4D (recalled from Fewster–Roman, not source-checked in this run) leaves room for that.
  - A hierarchical (multi-scale) short throat has not been excluded.
  - A non-perturbative large-N semiclassical solution could change this.
- **Why weakened, not refuted.** The core is narrowed rather than refuted because an open conjecture cannot be shown to hold by this attack.
