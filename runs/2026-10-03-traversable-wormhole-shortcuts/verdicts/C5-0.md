## Strongest attack

The claim fails on its own key term. The brief defines exotic matter as "matter that violates the NEC classically" (brief.md, line 38). The candidate's Scope line says C5 answers "yes" "in the brief's classical sense". But in Einstein–Dirac–Maxwell (EDM) theory, the Dirac sector has to violate the NEC at the throat of every static, flaring-out wormhole. This follows from the Einstein equations alone. It holds for any number of fermions and any mass, charge or frequency, whether the solution is symmetric (BKR) or asymmetric (Konoplya–Zhidenko, Dzhunushaliev et al.).

1. **The Einstein tensor at a throat.** Take ds² = −e^{2Φ(l)}dt² + dl² + r(l)²dΩ² (G = c = 1, lengths in metres) and the radial null vector k. For arbitrary Φ(l) and r(l), gr_tensors gives G_kk = 2Φ′r′/r − 2r″/r. At a throat r′ = 0 and r″ > 0 (flare-out), so 8πT_kk = −2r″/r₀ < 0 [calc: runs/2026-10-03-traversable-wormhole-shortcuts/calc/falsifier-C5-0_edm_nec.py].
2. **The Maxwell part contributes nothing to the radial NEC.** The most general static, spherically symmetric Maxwell field is a radial electric field E(l) plus a magnetic monopole P. For it, T^EM_kk = 0 exactly along radial null rays. Its energy density, E²/8π + P²/(8πr⁴), is non-negative, and so is T^EM_kk along tangential null rays. All of the NEC violation therefore sits in the Dirac sector: 8πT^Dirac_kk(throat) = −2r″/r₀ < 0. Under the brief's definition, the Dirac field is exotic matter.
3. **The ANEC is violated too, not just the pointwise NEC.** Along the complete radial null geodesic, the identity verified in M-CONSTRAINTS-02 gives ∫T_kk dλ = −(E/4π)∫e^{−Φ}(r′/r)² dl < 0 for any static throat that is asymptotically flat at both ends. Because T^EM_kk = 0 along this ray, the whole negative integral comes from the Dirac field. I checked this on an asymmetric sample throat with different Φ and r asymptotics at the two ends. The direct quadrature gives −0.117639 m⁻¹ (|l| < 200 m) and −0.117639 m⁻¹ (|l| < 400 m), against −0.117639 m⁻¹ from the by-parts form, so it converges. M-CONSTRAINTS-14 shows this radial geodesic is achronal for any Φ(l).
4. **The proponents concede the point.** BKR state that NEC violation is essential to their solution (quote below). Their "without exotic matter" means "without a phantom or non-standard Lagrangian", which is a different claim from the brief's sense.

The second leg of the claim, "hold ... open ... traversable", also fails for every known solution:
- the symmetric BKR configurations are not solutions of the EDM equations (Danielson et al.);
- the asymmetric smooth ones collapse to black holes that trap every null ray crossing the throat (Kain 2023);
- with positive-frequency Dirac sources, no two-ended solution has been found (Weinbaum 2026, numerical rather than a theorem).

## In principle vs. in practice

- **In principle.**
  - Without exotic matter in the brief's sense: impossible. The theorem in steps 1 to 3 above, together with topological censorship, forces the Dirac sector to violate the NEC and ANEC.
  - In the weaker sense, a non-phantom Lagrangian whose classical Dirac energy is unbounded below: static solutions exist on paper (Konoplya–Zhidenko, asymmetric). None has been shown to stay traversable:
    - Kain's evolutions collapse;
    - Weinbaum finds no positive-frequency two-ended solution;
    - the symmetric ones are not solutions.
- **In practice.** Kain's static throats are R₀ = 75–498 l_P = 1.2×10⁻³³ to 8.1×10⁻³³ m. They need a fermion of μ̄ = 0.2 m_P = 4.35×10⁻⁹ kg = 4.8×10²¹ m_e (M-DIALECTICIAN-08; calc/lens-dialectician_edm_scale.py.log). The condition q/μ < 1 needs a fermion of 7×10¹⁷ to 4×10¹⁸ GeV (M-ENGINEER-12). No known particle comes within 15 orders of magnitude, and a throat of Planck size passes nothing.

## Corrections to details

- The dossier's citation "Kain 2023 (PRD 108, 044019)" is correct for the evolution paper "Are Einstein-Dirac-Maxwell wormholes traversable?" (arXiv:2305.11217). A different Kain 2023 paper, PRD 108 084010 ("EDM wormholes in quantum field theory"), should not be confused with it.
- Sub-hypothesis (c) is listed as evidence *for* C5, but it cuts against it. Weinbaum's ANEC violation by Dirac fields on fixed backgrounds shows that the Dirac field is the exotic ingredient. It does not show that the field avoids being exotic.

## What survives and remaining doubt

- **What survives.** A narrower statement, which is not C5's core: EDM theory has static, asymptotically flat, two-ended solutions in which the required NEC/ANEC violation comes from a classical Dirac field with a standard Lagrangian (energy unbounded below) instead of a phantom field. Their dynamical traversability is unshown, and the evidence so far is against it.
- **Remaining doubt.**
  - Kain's collapse result and Weinbaum's obstruction are numerical, on restricted ansätze, and do not exclude every asymmetric or non-definite-parity branch.
  - The Dzhunushaliev et al. 2026 solutions have not been evolved.
  - None of this rescues the "without exotic matter" leg. That leg fails by the calculation above for any static throat, and by topological censorship for any asymptotically flat traversable one.
