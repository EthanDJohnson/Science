# Dossier: Negative-energy options for an Alcubierre drive
(SMOKE-TEST FIXTURE: hand-written summaries of well-known results, not checked by the pipeline. Use for testing agent mechanics only.)

## 1. Established
- [D-01] Alcubierre metric ds^2 = -dt^2 + (dx - v f(r_s) dt)^2 + dy^2 + dz^2 requires Eulerian energy density rho = -(c^4/8 pi G) v^2 (y^2+z^2)/(4 r_s^2) (df/dr_s)^2 < 0 in the bubble wall. (Alcubierre, Class. Quantum Grav. 11, L73, 1994) peer-reviewed
- [D-02] Generic warp drives of the Natario class violate the null energy condition. (Santiago, Schuster, Visser, Phys. Rev. D 105, 064038, 2022) peer-reviewed
- [D-03] Casimir energy density between ideal parallel plates separated by a: rho = -pi^2 hbar c / (720 a^4). Casimir force measured to the percent level (Lamoreaux 1997; Bressi et al. 2002 for parallel plates). peer-reviewed
- [D-04] Quantum inequalities (Ford & Roman) bound time-averaged negative energy density seen by inertial observers; for a massless scalar in 4D Minkowski with Lorentzian sampling time tau0: <rho> >= -3 hbar / (32 pi^2 c^3 tau0^4). peer-reviewed

## 3. Contested or conflicting
- [D-06] Positive-energy warp solutions: Lentz (2021) claimed superluminal positive-energy solitons; others (e.g. Fell & Heisenberg 2021; Bobrick & Martire 2021) construct positive-energy but subluminal shells, and the superluminal claims are disputed.

## 4. Constraints
- [D-07] Superluminal bubbles have a horizon at the front: the interior is causally disconnected from the front wall, so the ship cannot create or steer the bubble (Krasnikov 1998; Everett & Roman 1997).
- [D-08] Semiclassical instability: renormalized stress-energy grows exponentially at the front horizon of superluminal warp drives (Finazzi, Liberati, Barcelo, Phys. Rev. D 79, 124017, 2009).

## 5. Frontier and speculative
- [D-09] Van Den Broeck (1999) geometry reduces the total energy to a few solar masses of negative energy by shrinking the external bubble to microscopic size.
