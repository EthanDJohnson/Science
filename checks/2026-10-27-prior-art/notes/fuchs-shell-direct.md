## Verdict
angle_verdict = partly-anticipated: Fuchs et al. themselves state, qualitatively, that a shift upper limit exists, that the interior light-timing is a delay not an advance, that the shell needs hoop stress with |p| < energy density, and that the signature is a 7.6 ns light-timing difference described as frame dragging/circulation. None of the three papers gives the quantitative cap (beta_crit, compactness or thickness scaling), the superluminal/advance thresholds, the gauge-plus-holonomy reading, P_ADM=0, or the stress-ratio/graphene comparison. Barzegar et al. state the shift is a coordinate choice (one sentence).
