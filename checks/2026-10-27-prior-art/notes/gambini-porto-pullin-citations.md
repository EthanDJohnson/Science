## CONCLUSION (angle: citations)
- INSPIRE lists 86 citations of hep-th/0406260 (matches my curl count); Semantic Scholar 81 with 24 extracted contexts. No Comment/Reply/Erratum paper found in the citation list, by title search (INSPIRE, arXiv rate-limited), or by 2 WebSearches.
- No third-party citer points out (a) the (8 pi)^-2 vs (8 pi)^-4 inconsistency, (b) that complete decoherence is reached only within an unresolvably small interval before Tmax, or (c) that the rate depends on the clock's design horizon Tmax (the closest: Butterfield 2015 recounts Salecker-Wigner delta T ∝ sqrt(T/M) and Ng-van Dam delta T ∝ T^(1/3), i.e. accuracy depends on the interval to be timed, but does not tie it to GPP's BH Tmax).
- Real critiques of the same paper/claim exist but on other grounds: Okon-Sudarsky 2016 (BH claim: the puzzle is merely moved from fundamental to clock time), Anastopoulos-Hu 2008 (conditional-probability construction not a probability measure), Anastopoulos et al 2022 (classical-noise/GR symmetry; Markovian master-equation non-unitarity unrelated to BH non-unitarity), Butterfield 2015 (clock bound heuristic and controversial).
- Partial anticipation of point (2) exists only from the authors themselves (gr-qc/0501027, 2005).
- Verdict: partly-anticipated (authors' own 2005 admission), third-party prior art for the specific points: none found.
