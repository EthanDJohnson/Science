## FINAL SUMMARY (2026-10-27)
- Graham-Olum 2007 has 130 INSPIRE citers (all enumerated by default sort, most-recent sort, and date ranges). None of the titles/abstracts mention mouth rotation/misalignment/orientation; INSPIRE free-text queries and two web searches for a rotated/misaligned-mouth escape returned nothing on point.
- WHAT IS ALREADY PUBLISHED (partly anticipates our framing, not our result):
  (a) GO07 itself (p.5) restricts Theorem 1 to simply connected spacetimes, says wormholes "which connect a region to itself" are not covered, and gives a long-throat counterexample (credit: L. Ford). The word "shortcut" never occurs in GO07. So "GO07 argue a one-sided shortcut implies an achronal through-line" over-reads GO07; later papers (MMP 2018, Kontou 2024, Freivogel et al 2026, Kanai et al 2026) attribute the one-sided shortcut ban to GO07.
  (b) Freivogel et al 2606.12528 fn 3 and Kontou 2405.05963 p.20 restate the simple-connectedness limit; MMP fn 2 (remark by A. Wall) and Freivogel p.6 give the covering-space reduction of a one-sided wormhole to a two-region one and say it works classically but fails semiclassically (Casimir energy depends on topology). Witten 2020 p.68-69 states the cover reduction of a 'shortcut' for the classical NEC.
  (c) Gao-Wald 2000 fn 1 (Galloway, private communication) + Morales-Santillan 2022: a causal curve between far points passing through a compact region K implies a null line (inextendible achronal null geodesic), by a direct construction; K' uncontrolled; NEC-level; not stated to pass through the throat; no wormholes/mouths discussed. This is the closest published statement to our 'line-existence step' and means 'no published theorem ... for curved, back-reacted one-sided geometries' should be softened to 'no published theorem with controlled constants / for the self-consistent achronal ANEC / for finite mouths'.
- WHAT WE DID NOT FIND: any paper that tests, shows, or explicitly leaves open whether rotated/misaligned/twisted mouth identifications escape the achronal-line argument. Closest: Feng-Lemos-Matzner 2105.03433 p.2 restricts to identifications 'without twisting' (for a flat-exterior portal/dihedral wormhole, but for a collision problem, not for energy conditions); Schein-Aichelburg-Israel gr-qc/9602053 fixes one axisymmetric gluing (inner poles identified) for a two-Schwarzschild-mouth wormhole and does not vary it; MMP/Freivogel 'rotation' = orbital motion of the mouths, not gluing orientation.
