# Prior-art check: wormhole-mouth-rotation (angle: topical)

- Finding: One-sided wormhole shortcuts: rotating or misaligning the mouths does not escape the achronal-null-line argument (Graham-Olum 2007, PRD 76, 064001, arXiv:0705.3193). Three finite-mouth models find a complete achronal through-throat null line for every gluing when L < d; L* about 1.16-1.20 d at a = 0.1 d. Point-mouth escape is an artefact of conical mouths. Survivor: mouth-scale band <= 1.5a/c.

## GO07 itself (verified text, fetch_text.py)
- p.5: "We must also restrict ourselves to simply-connected spacetimes, which means that the wormholes we rule out are only those which connect one asymptotically flat region to another, not those which connect a region to itself." VERIFIED
- p.5: "One can see the necessity of simple connectedness (or some other additional assumption) by considering the following example. Let M be a static spacetime with a single asymptotically flat region and a wormhole connecting the region to itself, and suppose the throat of the wormhole is longer than the distance between the mouths on the outside. Any causal path through the wormhole emerges in the future of the place where it entered, and thus is not achronal." (footnote 2: "We thank Larry Ford for pointing out this counterexample.") VERIFIED
- p.6: "We can still find fastest paths through the wormhole, but they are chronal. This can happen because the timelike connections between points on such a path are not in the same homotopy class as the path itself." VERIFIED
- Consequence for our wording: GO07 never uses the word "shortcut" and does NOT assert that a short (L<d) one-sided wormhole implies a complete achronal through-line; it says the theorem only rules out wormholes connecting different asymptotic regions and gives a long-throat counterexample for same-region wormholes. Our "GO07 argue that a wormhole shortcut implies ..." is therefore an extension of GO07, not a quote. The simple-connectedness statement is confirmed.

## Works found
### W1 Maldacena, Milekhin, Popov, "Traversable wormholes in four dimensions", arXiv:1807.04726 (CQG 40 155016, 2023). Status: peer-reviewed. Relation: background-physics / partly-anticipates (line-existence step only); NOT about mouth orientation.
- Verified (fetch_text, pdf p.3-4): "Traversable short wormholes that join very distant points in space, and would lead to causality violation, are not allowed by the Einstein equations combined with the achronal average null energy condition [1, 2, 3, 4]." [refs: [3] Gao-Wald gr-qc/0007021, [4] Graham-Olum; [1],[2] FSW-type topological censorship refs incl hep-th/9912119]. "However, long traversable wormholes are in principle allowed. By 'long' we mean that it takes longer to go through the wormhole than through the ambient space."
- Verified footnote 2 (p.4): "The fact that long wormholes are not allowed in classical physics can be understood as follows. In classical physics the equations are local, so we can go the covering space, where it joins two different universes. In this case, the wormhole becomes short, since the two different universes are infinitely far, so it should be forbidden by causality. We thank A. Wall for this remark. Our solution will use Casimir energy, which depends on the actual topology of space, so we cannot go to the covering space."
- Bearing: (i) the field states the short-one-sided-wormhole ban as a consequence of achronal ANEC without a proof of the line-existence step beyond citing GO07 etc. -> supports our "unproved step" framing; (ii) Wall's covering-space remark is a published route that gets around simple connectedness for classical (local) matter, and it is orientation-blind (gluing orientation enters only through the cover). It does NOT cover semiclassical/Casimir matter (they say so). So it partly anticipates the "line-existence step" for the classical case; says nothing on mouth rotation.

## SUMMARY (topical angle)
- No prior treatment of rotated/misaligned mouths in the shortcut/achronal-ANEC argument found in INSPIRE (0 hits for the targeted queries), arXiv search (partly unavailable), WebSearch, or the full text of ~20 papers grepped for orientation/rotation/misalign/mouth/shortcut. The closest explicit mention: Frolov-Krtous-Zelnikov (PRD 108 024034, 2023) note "an ambiguity in the choice of positions and orientations of the discs" in a one-space ring wormhole and then pick the simplest case; no causal/ANEC consequence is drawn.
- The simple-connectedness caveat in our finding is NOT new: Graham-Olum say it themselves (p.5, with Ford's long-throat counterexample), and Freivogel-Fumagalli-Tomasevic 2026 fn.3 repeats it. Also GO07 never uses "shortcut" and does not claim a short same-region wormhole implies an achronal line; the field (MMP 2018, Kontou 2024, FGM 2019, FFT 2026) states the short-wormhole ban as following from achronal ANEC anyway. MMP fn.2 (credit A. Wall) gives a covering-space argument for the classical case and says it fails for Casimir/topology-dependent stress energy. FFT 2026 says the same about locality.
- Face-ray/polyhedral route (ANEC integral zero on rays through flat faces) is Visser 1989 (two-sided gluing), background to our "survivor".
- Not accessible: Visser's book, Krasnikov 2018 book, Everett-Roman 2012 book (catalogue records only), Kim-Thorne and MTY full text, Frolov-Novikov 1990.
