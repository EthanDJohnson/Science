## SUMMARY (citations angle)
- INSPIRE lists 96 citers (S2 101; Crossref 75 per sibling note) -- not "a few hundred". Scanned all 96 titles and abstracts; opened text of ~28 (Krasnikov, VdB gr-qc/9906050, Lobo-Visser, Finazzi-Liberati-Barcelo, Bobrick-Martire, Santiago-Schuster-Visser, Alcubierre-Lobo, Lobo review, Barzegar x2, Helmerich, Sajeendran, Fell-Heisenberg, Clark-Hiscock-Larson, Hart, Ridgely, Gonzalez-Diaz, Santos-Pereira thesis, Krasnikov 2006, Roman 2005, others).
- NO citer says that the printed QI numbers (w = 0.349, Delta~/72.5 = 1.4e-34 m, -6.6e93, -9.2e94) belong to the Planck-scale parameters, nor that Delta~/72.5 = 1.4e-17 m with eq.(7) of v5, nor that the femtometre version violates the QI by ~1e33.
- Closest: Krasnikov 2003 fn 8 (profile 'violates the quantum inequality (e.g. in w = 0.98)' and costs ~1e36 more exotic matter -- the 1e36 matches the corrected v5 E_II,- = 1.4e30 kg vs his ~1e-3 g). Bare assertion, no calculation, no parameters, no mention of the v1-v4/v5 change. My side calc shows a local-sampling-time application reproduces such a violation at w = 0.98 for BOTH parameter sets (so it is probably unrelated to the parameter swap, but I cannot confirm what he did).
- Two independent readers (Bobrick-Martire 2021, Finazzi et al 2009) take the PUBLISHED numbers to be the femtometre/solar-mass version; they repeat or assume the QI is satisfied and do not recompute it.
- Author's own follow-up gr-qc/9906050 (v4 May 2000) keeps 'QI is satisfied' and 'structure with sizes only a few orders of magnitude above the Planck scale'.
- Caveat for the finding itself (my side calc): the 'QI satisfied at v1-v4 parameters' result holds only for VdB's global tau0 = 0.1 r_c,min; a local tau0 = 0.1 r_c(w) test fails at both parameter sets by many orders of magnitude (e.g. ~2e65 at w = 0.98).
