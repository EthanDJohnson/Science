- Arithmetic (mine): with omega = 1/(8 pi M), M=(Tmax/tP)^(1/3), sigma = tP (tP/(Tmax-T))^(1/3): omega^2 sigma (Tmax-T) = (8pi)^-2. So the single-8pi frequency reproduces PRL eq.(5)'s printed coefficient; PRL eq.(4)'s (8pi)^2 gives (8pi)^-4. The IJMPD paper is the authors' own evidence for which factor is intended (single 8pi in the earlier paper; (8pi)^2 repeated in 2005 eq.12 and 2007 eq.19).
- Different calculation (sub-optimal clock, timescale comparison), so 'checks-the-same-paper-differently' is the right class; it does not state the (4)/(5) mismatch.

### 6. Gambini & Pullin 'Discrete space-time', gr-qc/0505023 -- fetch_text.py grep (pdf, 16 pp)
- VERIFIED verbatim (p.14, eq.62): "|rho12(Tmax)| ~ |rho12(0)| (MPlanck/MBH)^(2/3) ...
